# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers, students, and careful readers use HadithCritic to examine hadith transmission, early Islamic history, Quranic authority, and source criticism. They need a reliable index into long-form studies and supporting research material.

## Product Purpose

HadithCritic is an independent digital scholarly publication and research archive. It presents evidence-led studies, citations, diagrams, and source comparison without diluting the underlying research.

## Brand Personality

Exact, archival, independent. The site should feel like a carefully typeset critical edition, calm enough for sustained reading and clear enough for source discovery.

## Anti-references

Avoid AI-startup styling, generic SaaS dashboards, decorative glassmorphism, nested card grids, loud agency animation, and developer-tool visual language.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve research claims and source material exactly.
2. Make long-form reading and discovery the primary interaction.
3. Use motion to orient, reveal structure, and clarify state.
4. Prefer catalogue, ledger, and critical-edition patterns over generic cards.
5. Make scholarly access work with keyboard, reduced motion, and no client JavaScript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Provide visible focus states, semantic navigation and landmarks, keyboard-operable controls, reduced-motion alternatives, and descriptive image text.
