# The Hadith of The Uncurable Illness

\
حدثنا أبو هاشم سمعت كثير بن محمد بن كثير بن رفاعة يقول : جاء رجل إلى عبد الملك بن سعيد بن أبجر فجس بطنه فقال : بك داء لا يبرأ . قال : ما هو ؟ قال : الدبيلة . قال فتحول الرجل فقال : الله الله الله ربي لا أشرك به شيئا اللهم إني أتوجه إليك بنبيك محمد نبي الرحمة صلى الله عليه وسلم تسليما يا محمد إني أتوجه بك إلى ربك وربي يرحمني مما بي . قال فجس بطنه فقال : قد برئت ما بك علة .

Abu Hashim said: I heard Kathir ibn Muhammad ibn Kathir ibn Rifa'ah say: A man came to Abdul Malik ibn Sa'id ibn Abjar and he examined his stomach: You have a disease that will not go away. He said: “What is it?” he said: The empyema. The man turned around and said: O Allah, Allah, Allah, my Lord, with whom I have nothing in common, <mark style="color:red;">**O Allah, I turn to you by your Prophet Muhammad, the Prophet of Mercy, peace be upon him, O Muhammad, I turn to you to your Lord and my Lord to have mercy on me. He said he felt his abdomen and said: “You are cleared, there is nothing wrong with you**</mark>.

كذلك قوله: (ونُقل عن أحمد بن حنبل في منسك المروذي التوسل بالنبي صلى الله عليه وسلم في الدعاء).

He also said: “<mark style="color:red;">**It is reported from Ahmad ibn Hanbal in al-Marwadhi's hermitage that the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) can be invoked in supplication**</mark>.

* it was narrated from <mark style="color:yellow;">**Ahmad bin Hanbal**</mark> in Mansik Al-Marwadhi to seek intercession through the Prophet, may Allah’s prayers and peace be upon him, in supplication
* <mark style="color:yellow;">**Ibn Abi Al-Dunya**</mark> in the book (<mark style="color:purple;">**Mujabi Al-Du’aa’**</mark>)
