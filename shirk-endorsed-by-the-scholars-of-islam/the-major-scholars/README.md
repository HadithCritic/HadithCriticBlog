# The Major Scholars

This section documents the endorsements and historical practice of **Tawassul**, **Tashaffuʿ**, and **Istighāthah** by prominent Sunni scholars across Islamic history, spanning from the classical period through late antiquity and modernity.

Each scholar page contains the scholar's bio, death date (AH/CE), explicit quotes affirming the practice, Arabic citations, and high-resolution scans of the original manuscripts and printed reference texts.
