# Fiqh Compass correction handling procedure

Status: **draft operating procedure; not yet live-operations verified**.

This procedure supports the public correction form. It does not authorize
changing a source claim or score without evidence, and it does not imply that
an expert reviewer or an accountable inbox owner has been appointed.

## Intake boundaries

- Accept factual/source corrections, question wording concerns, edition or
  locator issues, translation/transliteration notes, profile metadata reports,
  accessibility/technical defects, and requests for additional coverage.
- Quiz answers are not requested or needed. Do not collect, infer, or attach
  browser-local answers to a correction record.
- Keep the submitter's optional email separate from the scholarly assessment.
  Do not publish an email address or other personal detail in a release note.
- Until the Web3Forms account retention and recipient settings are checked,
  treat third-party delivery, access, and deletion behavior as unverified.
  Do not promise a retention period or confidential handling beyond what has
  been confirmed.

## Triage and review

For each delivered report, create a local ticket identifier and record only the
category, affected item/release, report summary, received date, evidence
locators, decision state, reviewer role, and decision rationale. Keep submitted
contact details in the service inbox; do not copy them into research exports.

Use these states:

1. **Received** — delivery confirmed by the configured inbox.
2. **Triaged** — type, affected release, urgency, and likely evidence needs
   identified.
3. **Pending evidence/review** — source, edition, attribution, rights, or
   specialist review is incomplete. Published scores remain unchanged or
   suppressed where the uncertainty matters.
4. **Accepted** — the source and proposed correction have been checked; update
   the research record, relevant export, and change log together.
5. **Declined** — retain a concise reason and the evidence checked; do not
   present disagreement as a factual error.
6. **Duplicate / out of scope** — link to the existing ticket or explain the
   scope boundary without importing unverified claims.

For textual reports, verify title-page metadata, edition, printed page or
manuscript folio, exact Arabic wording, surrounding context, authorial layer,
and translation alignment. Catalog metadata or a search-result excerpt is a
lead, not passage-level verification. For legal-position or scoring disputes,
preserve competing readings and require qualified human review before changing
a public interpretation or score. An automated model may help locate evidence
but is not a reviewer and cannot approve a change.

## Release and response

Accepted changes must update the source record and versioned public release
together. Record old and new values, evidence locators, decision rationale,
review role, date, and the release identifier; keep private contact data out of
the public change log. Test the changed route and scoring behavior before
publishing. If a change could affect an existing result, describe the impact
and provide the release/change identifier.

Do not promise a response deadline. Reply only when the optional address is
useful and the recipient account is verified. Before relying on this process
for a public beta, verify the Web3Forms access key's recipient, spam controls,
retention setting, authorized inbox access, deletion process, and successful
delivery using a clearly marked test submission that contains no personal or
quiz data. Assign an accountable editor and backup before opening the workflow
to public reports.
