# Hadith criticism: works not in the local library

Compiled 2026-09-29 for the atlas at `/research/`. These are studies and sources that matter for hadith criticism and that are not in `Desktop\org\05 Scholarly Library and OCR` in any form: not as a file, not as a chapter inside a local edited volume.

## How this list was built

1. Every candidate was checked against the library's file list, its bibliography catalog, and the tables of contents of the local edited volumes. Anything that is already local as a chapter was dropped. For example, Juynboll's "Some Isnād-Analytical Methods", Cook's "Eschatology and the Dating of Traditions", Robson's "The Isnād in Muslim Tradition", Speight on the will of Saʿd and Motzki on the Muṣannaf of ʿAbd al-Razzāq are all chapters of the local *Ḥadīth: Origins and Developments* (Motzki, ed.). Motzki's Zuhrī study and "Whither Ḥadīth Studies?" are chapters of the local *Analysing Muslim Traditions*.
2. **Cited by** is the number of the 800 local text transcripts that contain the work's exact title string. It measures how central a missing work is to the library's own scholarship. A blank means the title is too generic to count reliably. Scanned works read as text under-count.
3. **Check** column:
   - `ok` means the work, author, venue and year were confirmed against Crossref or against a bibliography printed in a local work. Page ranges were confirmed only where Crossref or a local bibliography printed them, so treat the rest as unchecked until the atlas's usual title-page pass.
   - `confirm` means the work itself could not be matched and the whole citation is from memory. Check it before it goes into `inputs/seeds.json`.
4. Nothing here is a grade of a work or a scholar. Themes follow the atlas.

Priority: **P1** is foundational and widely cited by the library's own works, **P2** is strong and specific, **P3** is useful context.

## Bibliographic notes

- Motzki's German original of the Zuhrī study, "Der Fiqh des az-Zuhrī: die Quellenproblematik" (*Der Islam* 68, 1991), is not local, but the English version is chapter 1 of *Analysing Muslim Traditions*, so it is omitted.
- Robson's "The Isnād in Muslim Tradition" appears in the literature as both 1953 and 1953-54 (*Transactions of the Glasgow University Oriental Society* 15). The local volume prints 1953.
- Crossref dates several reprints as 2016 to 2024 (Routledge reissues of the Variorum and Ashgate volumes). The years above are original publication years.
