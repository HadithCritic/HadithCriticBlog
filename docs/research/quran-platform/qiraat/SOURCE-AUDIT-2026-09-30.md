## Record structure and conventions

The source-native authoring records are farsh batch items. A position is a `feature` keyed to a sura and verse, with a source-spelled lemma, scope, Qurʾānic word anchors, and distinct reading values/forms. A `claim` links one or more features to a book, volume, printed page, exact evidence excerpt, named authorities, form span, value, and basis (`listing`, `report`, `rejected`, `disputed`, or `permitted`). Evidence is accepted only when it is found on the cited cached source page; named spans must be inside the quotation or use a page-qualified context reference.

`authorities.json` distinguishes the ten qāriʾs from their twenty riwāya transmitters. Naming a qāriʾ resolves to both transmitters unless the source names one transmitter specifically. Groups are used only where the cited source defines them. “The rest” is computed only against the source book's declared reader set. Unresolved attribution is retained as unresolved rather than guessed. The display data resolves claims independently per source and keeps reported, disputed, rejected, and permitted readings distinct.

Routes below the twenty standard transmitters are stored separately in checked route batches. Quoted route material that does not support a precise form/reader mapping stays in `second-witness/route-detail.json` or appears as a source quotation; it is not promoted into a reading claim by name matching alone. Verse anchoring is a candidate link to the Cairo text and retains its status; the source's verse and spelling are not silently rewritten.

- *at-Taysīr*: the Sura 1 pilot and pp. 72–84 have partial coverage. Open work includes one p. 72 scope question, two p. 73 passages (the unassignable regional-route clause and an opaque cross-reference), the p. 74 recurrence scope of `هزوا`, p. 75's named exception locations and al-Ḥijr cross-reference, the p. 76 al-Yazīdī route split, and p. 77's incomplete locators for `عما تعملون` and `ومن يطوع`. Pages 78–84 have been read for farsh, with unresolved p. 78–79 uṣūl and p. 81–82 locator/route clauses retained in the coverage ledger. Pages 85–228 and earlier relevant chapters remain to be read and accounted for; repeated readings still require source-specific claims. Accordingly, this audit does not add claims from machine-only matches, English translations, or ambiguous source passages. Existing verified claims and route data were left intact. A later extraction pass can promote a candidate only after it has a source-exact excerpt, specific page, supported location, form, and explicit reader/transmitter attribution, then passes the existing verifier and duplicate checks.
