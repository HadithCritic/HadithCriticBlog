# Article 82–85 quotation verification

This audit records source-language pairings that still need edition-level collation after the article repairs. Arabic added to the article components was retrieved from the cited witness or a repository reproducing that witness; it was not back-translated from English.

## Article 82

- **Sālim b. Dhakwān, *The Epistle of Sālim Ibn Dhakwān*, 141–142, §128.** The card uses the Crone–Zimmermann English translation. The work was composed in Arabic, so this is a translated primary source rather than an English-original quotation. The corresponding Arabic passage needs collation against the edition before adding it to the card.
- **Al-Rabīʿ b. Ḥabīb, *Musnad*, no. 45; al-Kudamī, *al-Istiqāma*, 2:119; al-Kindī, *al-Muṣannaf*, vol. 7, pt. 10, 332–333, 349; al-Mizzī, *Tahdhīb al-Kamāl*, 13:542–543, no. 3027.** The English cards are preserved, but their Arabic source text has not yet been collated with the cited editions.

## Article 83

- No English-only primary-source cards were found in the component audit. Hadith cards pair the Arabic and English passages.

## Article 84

- **ʿAbd al-Razzāq, *al-Muṣannaf*, no. 21058; al-Bazzār, *al-Musnad*, no. 3647; Ibn Abī Ḥātim, *al-ʿIlal*, no. 379, 2:291.** The Arabic and English pairings are now present in separate bilingual source blocks. The Arabic was checked against digital transcriptions of each numbered report; the Ibn Abī Ḥātim passages preserve separate transmitted-report and critical-commentary functions.
- **Ibn ʿAdī, *al-Kāmil fī Ḍuʿafāʾ al-Rijāl*, entry Ismāʿīl b. Muslim al-Makkī.** The ʿAmr b. ʿAlī pupil-list and evaluation passage is now displayed bilingually and linked to the HadithWeb transcription. The translation includes the narrator-critical qualification in the Arabic rather than truncating the passage to the pupil list.

## Article 85

- No English-only primary-source cards were found in the component audit. Hadith cards pair the Arabic and English passages.

## Retrieval limitations

- **Al-Bukhārī 1287–1288 endpoints:** the HadithDB numbered endpoints returned HTTP 410 during retrieval. Article 85 now presents the complete continuous funeral account available at al-Bukhārī 1286 and retains the 1286–1288 range in the discussion/reference. The unavailable endpoints were not treated as independently verified texts.
- **Article 82 translated quotations:** the Arabic source passages for Sālim b. Dhakwān and the cited al-Rabīʿ b. Ḥabīb, al-Kudamī, al-Kindī, and al-Mizzī extracts still require collation against their cited editions before Arabic is added. Existing English translations are retained; no Arabic was reconstructed.
