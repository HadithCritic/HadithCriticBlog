# Verse groupings in al-Zamakhsharī’s al-Kashshāf

Every passage of al-Kashshāf as the printed edition divides it: the verses quoted together and then commented on as one unit. A range such as 35:5–7 means the three verses are quoted together and discussed together, so 35:6 has no commentary of its own outside that passage.

**Source.** al-Zamakhsharī (d. 538 AH), *al-Kashshāf ʿan ḥaqāʾiq ghawāmiḍ al-tanzīl*, ed. Muṣṭafā Ḥusayn Aḥmad (Dār al-Rayyān li-l-Turāth, Cairo, and Dār al-Kitāb al-ʿArabī, Beirut; printing completed 1362 AH / 1947 CE), 4 vols., Shamela book 23627, read from `al-kashshaf-23627.json`.

**Method.** A passage opens where the edition prints a heading in square brackets, for example `[سورة فاطر (35) : الآيات 5 الى 7]`, and runs to the next heading. The ranges below are the ones printed in those headings, unchanged. The headings are the editor’s, but they follow the verses al-Zamakhsharī quotes together before he comments, and they are the same divisions the site uses for its al-Kashshāf passages. The "Edition" column gives the volume and page where the heading is printed.

**Totals.** 2979 passages across 114 surahs: 1407 cover two or more verses and 1572 cover a single verse. The longest covers 22 verses.

## Irregularities in the printed headings

The headings run in order through each surah with the exceptions below. Each is also noted beside its passage.

- **Headings with no commentary.** Three headings print the verses and nothing else, and the edition then prints a second heading over the same verses with the commentary: 14:32–33 (then 32–34), 21:101 (then 101–103) and 24:62 (printed twice). Only the heading that carries the commentary is listed.
- **Shared verses.** Some headings begin with the verse the previous heading ended on: 14:12, 27:60, 35:35, 39:33, 44:34 and 80:32. Both ranges are listed as printed.
- **Re-quoted ranges.** Three headings re-quote verses already treated, and the commentary under them covers only the last verses: 24:24–26 (commentary on 26), 38:38–47 (on 45–47) and 41:41–48 (on 47–48). The printed range is listed.
- **Verses outside every heading.** 7:108, 26:190–191, 26:224, 29:50, 44:48–50, 54:8 and 80:42. Of these, 7:108, 44:48–50 and 54:8 are discussed in the commentary of the passage before them, and 26:224 at the start of the passage after it. No commentary on 26:190–191, 29:50 or 80:42 was found at their place in this text.
