surah: 99
title: Sūrat al-Zalzala (99)
revelation: Medinan; it is also said Meccan. Eight verses. [Revealed after al-Nisāʾ (4).]

::: passage 99:1-8 | 4:783-785

{L|Severely quaked|zilzālahā} read with *kasra* on the *zāy* and with *fatḥa* (*zalzālahā*); the form with *kasra* is a verbal noun and the form with *fatḥa* a noun. Among the patterns there is no *faʿlāl* with *fatḥa* except in doubled roots.

{Q} What does *zilzālahā* (“its quaking”), with annexation, mean? {A} It means the quaking it is due in wisdom and by God’s will, which is the violent quaking with none beyond it. Like it is your saying “Honor the pious man with his honoring, and humble the wicked man with his humbling,” meaning the honoring and humbling each is due. Or: all its quaking, and the whole of what is possible of it.

{L|Its loads|athqālahā} *al-athqāl* is the plural of *thiql*, which is household goods, as in “*And they carry your loads*” (Q 16:7). He made the buried things in its belly its burdens.

{L|The human will wonder|wa-qāla l-insānu} “*What is happening?*” (*mā lahā*, “what is wrong with it”), that it quakes with this violent quaking and casts out what is in its belly? That is at the second blast, when it quakes and casts out its dead alive; and they say that {p|784}because of the dreadful matter that dazzles them, as they say “*Who resurrected us from our death?*” (Q 36:52). It is also said: this is the saying of the unbeliever, because he did not believe in the raising; but the believer says “*This is what the Most Gracious has promised. The messengers were right*” (Q 36:52).

{Q} What is the meaning of the earth’s telling and of revealing to it? {A} It is a figure for God Most High’s bringing about in it states that stand in the place of telling with the tongue, so that whoever says *mā lahā* (“what is wrong with it?”) looks at those states and knows why it quaked and why it cast out the dead, and that this is what the prophets used to warn of and caution against. It is also said: God makes it speak in reality, and it tells what was done on it of good and evil. It is related from the Messenger of God ﷺ: it bears witness against everyone to what he did on its back.

{Q} What puts *idhā* and *yawmaʾidhin* in the accusative? {A} *Yawmaʾidhin* is a substitute for *idhā*, and what governs both is *tuḥaddithu* (“tells”). *Idhā* may also be governed by an implied verb, and *yawmaʾidhin* by *tuḥaddithu*.

{Q} Where are the two objects of *tuḥaddithu*? {A} The first is left out, and the second is “*its news*.” Its original form is “it tells creation its news,” but the purpose is to mention its telling of the news, not to mention creation, to make the Day great.

{Q} What is the *bāʾ* in His saying *bi-anna rabbaka* attached to? {A} To *tuḥaddithu*. Its meaning: it tells its news because your Lord revealed to it and commanded it to tell. The meaning may also be: on that day it tells, by telling that your Lord revealed to it, its news; telling that your Lord revealed to it being itself a telling of its news, as you say, “You gave me every counsel, in that you counseled me in religion.” *Bi-anna rabbaka* may also be a substitute for “*its news*,” as if it were said: on that day it tells its news, that your Lord revealed to it; because you say both *ḥaddathtuhu kadhā* and *ḥaddathtuhu bi-kadhā*. *Awḥā lahā* is in the sense of *awḥā ilayhā* (“revealed to it”), and it is a figure, as in His saying “*we simply say to it, ‘Be,’ and it is*” (Q 16:40). He said: “*He revealed to it to settle, and it settled*.” Ibn Masʿūd read *tunabbiʾu akhbārahā*, and Saʿīd ibn Jubayr *tunbiʾu*, without doubling.

{L|The people will issue|yaṣduru l-nāsu} they come forth from their places of exit, the graves, to the Standing Place, “*from every direction*” (*ashtātan*, “scattered”): some white-faced and secure, some black-faced and afraid. Or they come forth from the Standing Place scattered, the ways of Paradise and the Fire parting them, “*to be shown*” the repayment of their deeds. In the reading of the Prophet ﷺ: *li-yaraw*, with *fatḥa*. Ibn ʿAbbās and Zayd ibn ʿAlī read *yurahu*, with *ḍamma*. It is told that a Bedouin put “*of good will see it*” after what follows it, and he was told, “You have put first what comes last.” He said: {p|785}“*Take the hollow of Harshā or its back, for both sides of Harshā are a road for them*.”

{L|An atom’s weight|dharra} *al-dharra* is the small ant. It is also said that *al-dharr* is the motes seen in a sunbeam.

{Q} The good deeds of the unbeliever are made void by unbelief, and the evil deeds of the believer are pardoned through his avoiding the great sins. So what is the meaning of repayment for atom’s weights of good and evil? {A} The meaning is: whoever does an atom’s weight of good, of the party of the blessed; and whoever does an atom’s weight of evil, of the party of the wretched; because it comes after His saying “*the people will issue from every direction*.”

{From|the Messenger of God ﷺ} “*Whoever recites Sūrat idhā zulzilat four times is like one who has recited the whole Qurʾān*.”
