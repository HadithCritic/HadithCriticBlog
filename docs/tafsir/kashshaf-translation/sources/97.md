surah: 97
title: Sūrat al-Qadr (97)
revelation: Meccan; it is also said Medinan. Five verses. [Revealed after ʿAbasa (80).]

::: passage 97:1-5 | 4:780-781

He made the Qurʾān great in three ways. The first: He ascribed its sending down to Himself and made it His alone, no one else’s. The second: He brought it as a pronoun and not as a name stated outright, bearing witness to its renown and that it needs no pointing out. The third: raising the worth of the time in which it was sent down. It is related that it was sent down all at once on the Night of Decree, from the Preserved Tablet to the lowest heaven, and Gabriel dictated it to the scribes; then he brought it down to the Messenger of God ﷺ in portions over twenty-three years. From al-Shaʿbī: the meaning is, We began its sending down on the Night of Decree.

They differ over its time. Most of them hold that it is in the month of Ramaḍān, in the last ten nights, on the odd ones; and the most common view is that it is the seventh of them. Perhaps the reason for hiding it is that whoever wants it should keep many nights alive seeking to fall upon it, so that his worship grows and his reward is doubled; and so that people, were it made known, would not rely on gaining the merit in it and neglect the other nights. The meaning of the Night of Decree is the night of decreeing affairs and settling them, from the saying of God Most High “*every matter of wisdom is clarified*” (*fīhā yufraqu kullu amrin ḥakīm*, Q 44:4). It is also said that it was so named for its weight and its honor above all other nights.

{L|How awesome is the Night of Destiny!|wa-mā adrāka mā laylatu l-qadr} He means: your knowing has not reached the full measure of its merit and the height of its worth. Then He made that plain: that it is better than a thousand months. The reason its merit rises so high is the benefits for religion found in it that He mentioned: the coming down of the angels and the Spirit, and the making distinct of every wise affair. On the singling out of this span, it is mentioned that the Messenger of God ﷺ spoke of a man of the Children of Israel who wore his armor in the way of God for a thousand months. The believers wondered at it, and their own works seemed small to them, so they were given a night better than the span of that fighter. It is also said that in times past a man was not called a worshipper until he had worshipped God for a thousand months; so they were given {p|781}a night which, if they keep it alive, gives them more right to be called worshippers than those worshippers had.

{L|Descend|tanazzalu} to the lowest heaven; it is also said, to the earth. “*And the Spirit*”: Gabriel. It is also said: a kind of angel whom the angels see only on that night. “*To carry out every command*” (*min kulli amr*, “for every affair”): that is, they come down for the sake of every affair God has decreed for that year until the next. *Min kulli mriʾin* is also read, that is, for the sake of every person. It is said that they meet no believing man or woman on that night without greeting them.

{L|Peaceful it is|salāmun hiya} it is nothing but safety: that is, God decrees nothing in it but safety and good, while on other nights He decrees both trial and safety. Or: it is nothing but greeting, for how often they greet the believers. *Maṭlaʿ* is read with *fatḥa* on the *lām*, and with *kasra* (*maṭliʿ*).

{From|the Messenger of God ﷺ} “*Whoever recites Sūrat al-Qadr is given a reward like that of one who fasted Ramaḍān and kept the Night of Decree alive*.”
