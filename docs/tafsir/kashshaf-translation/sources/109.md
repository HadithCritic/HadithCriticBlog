surah: 109
title: Sūrat al-Kāfirūn (109)
revelation: Meccan. Six verses. [Revealed after al-Māʿūn (107).] This sūra and Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ are called al-muqashqishatān, the two that clear one of hypocrisy.

::: passage 109:1-6 | 4:808-809

Those addressed are particular unbelievers of whom God knew that they would not believe. It is related that a party of Quraysh said, “*Muḥammad, come, follow our religion and we will follow yours: you worship our gods for a year and we worship your God for a year*.” He said, “*God forbid that I should associate anything else with God*.” They said, “*Then touch some of our gods, and we will believe you and worship your God*.” So it was revealed. In the morning he went to the Sacred Mosque, where the leading men of Quraysh were, stood over them and recited it to them, and they gave up hope.

{L|I do not worship|lā aʿbudu} worship in the future is meant, because *lā* comes only before an imperfect verb with a future sense, as *mā* comes only before an imperfect with a present sense. Do you not see that *lan* is an emphatic form of what *lā* negates? {p|809}Al-Khalīl said of *lan* that it is originally *lā an*. The meaning: I will not do in the future what you ask of me, which is to worship your gods, and you will not do in it what I ask of you, which is to worship my God.

{L|Nor will I ever worship what you worship|wa-lā ana ʿābidun mā ʿabadtum} that is, I was never, in the past, a worshipper of what you worshipped in it. He means: no worship of an idol was ever known from me in the Jāhiliyya, so how can it be hoped for from me in Islam? “*Nor will you ever worship what I worship*”: that is, you have not worshipped at any time what I now worship.

{Q} Then why was it not said *mā ʿabadtu* (“what I worshipped”), as it was said *mā ʿabadtum* (“what you worshipped”)? {A} Because they were worshipping idols before the mission, and he was not worshipping God Most High at that time.

{Q} Why does it use *mā* (“what”) and not *man* (“whom”)? {A} Because the attribute is meant, as if He said: I do not worship falsehood, and you do not worship the truth. It is also said that *mā* forms a verbal noun, that is: I do not worship as you worship, and you do not worship as I worship.

{L|To you is your religion, and to me is my religion|lakum dīnukum wa-liya dīn} to you your associating partners with God, and to me my belief in His oneness. The meaning: I am a prophet sent to you to call you to the truth and to salvation; so if you will not accept it from me or follow me, then leave me be, and do not call me to associate partners with God.

{From|the Messenger of God ﷺ} “*Whoever recites Sūrat al-Kāfirūn, it is as though he had recited a quarter of the Qurʾān; the rebel devils keep far from him, he is cleared of associating partners with God, and he is spared the greatest terror*.”
