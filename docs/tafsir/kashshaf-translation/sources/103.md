surah: 103
title: Sūrat al-ʿAṣr (103)
revelation: Meccan. Three verses. [Revealed after al-Sharḥ (94).]

::: passage 103:1-3 | 4:793-794

He swears by the ʿAṣr prayer for its excellence. The proof is the saying of God Most High “*especially the middle prayer*” (Q 2:238), [read] “*the ʿAṣr prayer*” in the codex of {p|794}Ḥafṣa, and his saying ﵊: “*Whoever misses the ʿAṣr prayer, it is as though he had been robbed of his family and his property*.” Also, the duty of performing it is harder, because people are caught up in their trading and earning at the end of the day and busy with their livelihoods. Or He swears by the late afternoon as He swore by the forenoon, because both hold signs of His power. Or He swears by time, for the many kinds of wonders in its passing.

*Al-insān* (“man”) stands for the whole kind. *Al-khusr* is loss (*khusrān*), as *al-kufr* is said for *al-kufrān*. The meaning: people are at a loss in their trading, except the righteous alone, because they bought the Hereafter with this world, and so profited and found happiness, while everyone else traded the other way, and so fell into loss and misery.

{L|And exhort one another to uphold the truth|wa-tawāṣaw bi-l-ḥaqq} to the firm matter that cannot be denied, which is all that is good: belief in the oneness of God and obedience to Him, following His books and His messengers, renouncing this world and longing for the Hereafter.

{L|And exhort one another to be steadfast|wa-tawāṣaw bi-l-ṣabr} patience in keeping from sins, in doing acts of obedience, and under what God tests His servants with.

{From|the Messenger of God ﷺ} “*Whoever recites Sūrat wa-l-ʿAṣr, God will forgive him, and he will be among those who counseled one another to the truth and counseled one another to patience*.”
