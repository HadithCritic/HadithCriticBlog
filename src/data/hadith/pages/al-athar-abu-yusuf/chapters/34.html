Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 192
English
Isnad

Yusuf narrated from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Alqamah b. Marthad, from Ibn Buraydah, from his father, ؓ,

Matn

who said When the Prophet, ﷺ, sent out an army or a detachment, he would advise its leader to fear God in his own conduct and to treat those with him well. Then he would say: "March forth in the cause of God, in the name of God. Fight those who disbelieve in God. Do not embezzle, do not betray, do not kill a child, and do not mutilate. When you meet your enemy from the polytheists, call them to Islam. If they accept, then accept it from them and refrain from fighting them. Then call them to migrate from their land to the abode of the emigrants. If they refuse, inform them that they will be like the Bedouin Muslims—God’s ruling applies to them as it does to the believers, but they will have no share in the spoils of war or booty. If they refuse, then call them to pay the jizyah. If they accept, then accept it from them and refrain from fighting them. If they do not accept, then fight them. If you besiege the people of a fortress, do not grant them the covenant of God or the covenant of His Messenger, but grant them your own covenant and the covenant of your fathers. If you violate your covenant, it is less severe than violating God’s covenant. If they ask you to let them surrender to God’s judgment, do not do so. Instead, let them surrender to your judgment, then decide their fate as you see fit."

English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father, from Rawh b. Musafir, from Muqatil b. Hayyan, from Muslim b. Haysam, from al-Nu'man b. al-Muqarrin al-Muzani, ؓ,

Matn

who said Whenever the Messenger of God, ﷺ, sent a commander over an army or a detachment, he would advise him personally to fear God and to treat well those Muslims with him. Then he would say to them: "March forth in the name of God and in the path of God. Fight those who disbelieve in God. Do not embezzle, do not betray, do not mutilate, and do not kill children. When you meet your enemy from the polytheists, call them to three matters—invite them to Islam. If they accept, then refrain from fighting them and accept from them. Then call them to migrate from their land to the land of the emigrants, and inform them that if they do so, they will have what the emigrants have, and upon them will be what is upon the emigrants. If they enter Islam but choose to remain in their land, inform them that they are like the Bedouin Muslims—God’s ruling applies to them as it applies to the Muslims, but they will have no share in the spoils of war or booty unless they fight alongside you. If they refuse, then call them to pay the jizyah. If they accept, then refrain from fighting them and accept it from them. If they refuse, then seek God’s help against them and fight them. If you besiege the people of a fortress and they wish for you to grant them the protection of God and the protection of His Messenger, do not grant them the protection of God or the protection of His Messenger. Instead, grant them your protection and the protection of your companions, for if you violate your protection or that of your companions, it is better for you than violating the protection of God and His Messenger. If you besiege the people of a fortress and they wish to surrender to God’s judgment, do not grant them God’s judgment. Instead, grant them your judgment and the judgment of your companions, for you do not know whether you will judge among them according to God’s judgment or not." Muqatil said: "I examined the lands of Khurasan that were conquered during the time of 'Umar and 'Uthman, ؓ, and I did not find in any of them the protection of God or the protection of His Messenger, but only the protection of the Imam and his companions among the Muslims."

Isnad

الْمُشْرِكِينَ فَادْعُهُمْ إِلَى ثَلَاثِ خِصَالٍ أَوْ ثَلَاثِ خِلَالٍ: ادْعُهُمْ إِلَى الْإِسْلَامِ، فَإِنْ قَبِلُوا فَكُفُّوا عَنْهُمْ، وَاقْبَلْ مِنْهُمْ، وَادْعُهُمْ إِلَى التَّحَوُّلِ مِنْ دَارِهِمْ إِلَى دَارِ الْمُهَاجِرِينَ، وَأَخْبِرْهُمْ أَنَّهُمْ إِنْ فَعَلُوا لَهُمْ مَا لِلْمُهَاجِرِينَ، وَأَنَّ عَلَيْهِمْ مَا عَلَى الْمُهَاجِرِينَ، وَإِنْ دَخَلُوا فِي الْإِسْلَامِ وَاخْتَارُوا that he said When you see the gardens of Paradise, graze therein. Indeed, they are not the gatherings of storytellers, but rather the gatherings of the people of jurisprudence.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنِ الْهَيْثَمِ، عَنِ ابْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رضي الله عنه

Matn

أَنَّهُ قَالَ «إِذَا رَأَيْتُمْ رِيَاضَ الْجَنَّةِ فَارْتَعُوا، أَمَا إِنَّهَا لَيْسَتْ بِمَجَالِسِ الْقُصَّاصِ، وَلَكِنَّهَا مَجَالِسُ أَهْلِ الْفِقْهِ»

English
Isnad

Yusuf narrated from his father from Abu Hanifah who said

Matn

that it had reached him that Umar b. al-Khattab, ؓ, said: “If I had appointed Uthman, he would have placed the family of Abu Muayt on the people’s shoulders. By God, if I had done so, he would have done it; and if he did, they would soon march against him and cut off his head.” They asked, “What about Ali?” Umar said, “He is a weak, unimpressive man.” They asked, “Talhah?” He said, “That man is conceited.” They asked, “Al-Zubayr?” He said, “He is not suited to it.” They asked, “Sa'd?” He said, “He is a horseman and archer.” They asked, “Abd al-Rahman b. Awf?” He said, “He is very tight-fisted. This office suits only someone who gives without extravagance and withholds without being miserly.”

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ،

Matn

قَالَ بَلَغَنِي أَنَّ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ رضي الله عنه قَالَ: «لَوْ وَلَّيْتُهَا عُثْمَانَ لَحَمَلَ آلَ أَبِي مُعَيْطٍ عَلَى رِقَابِ النَّاسِ، وَاللَّهِ لَوْ فَعَلْتُ لَفَعَلَ، وَلَوْ فَعَلَ لَأَوْشَكُوا أَنْ يَسِيرُوا إِلَيْهِ حَتَّى يَجُزُّوا رَأْسَهُ» ، فَقَالُوا: عَلِيٌّ؟ قَالَ: رَجُلٌ قُعْدُدٌ، قَالُوا: طَلْحَةُ؟ قَالَ: ذَاكَ رَجُلٌ فِيهِ بَأْوٌ، قَالُوا: الزُّبَيْرُ؟ قَالَ: لَيْسَ هُنَاكَ، قَالُوا: سَعْدٌ؟ قَالَ: صَاحِبُ فَرَسٍ وَقَوْسٍ، فَقَالُوا: عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ عَوْفٍ؟ قَالَ: ذَاكَ فِيهِ إِمْسَاكٌ شَدِيدٌ، وَلَا يَصْلُحُ لِهَذَا الْأَمْرِ إِلَّا مُعْطٍ فِي غَيْرِ سَرَفٍ، وَمُمْسِكٌ فِي غَيْرِ تَقْتِيرٍ "
