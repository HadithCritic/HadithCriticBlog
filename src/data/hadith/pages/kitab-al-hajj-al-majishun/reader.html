The Qurʾānic foundation of pilgrimage

ENGLISHTranslation

'Abd al-'Aziz narrated to us, saying: God, majestic be His praise, said: {The pilgrimage is in well-known months. Whoever undertakes the pilgrimage in them, then there is to be no sexual relations, no wickedness, and no quarrelling during the pilgrimage. Whatever good you do, God knows it. And take provision, for the best provision is God-consciousness (taqwa); and fear Me, O people of understanding.} And God said: {And proclaim the pilgrimage among the people; they will come to you on foot and on every lean mount, coming from every deep mountain pass.} And He said: {And complete the pilgrimage and the 'umra for God. But if you are prevented…} And He said: {And pilgrimage to the House is a duty owed to God by the people — for whoever is able to find a way to it.} And “ability,” according to what has reached us, is a mount and provisions.

العربيةالنصّ

حدثنا عبد العزيز قال: قال الله جل ثناؤه: {الحج أشهرٌ معلوماتٌ فمن فرض فيهن الحج فلا رفث ولا فسوق ولا جدال في الحج وما تفعلوا من خيرٍ يعلمه الله وتزودوا فإن خير الزاد التقوى واتقون يا أولي الألباب} . وقال الله: {وأذن في الناس بالحج يأتوك رجالاً وعلى كل ضامرٍ يأتين من كل فجٍ عميق} . وقال: {وأتموا الحج والعمرة لله فإن أحصرتم} . وقال: {ولله على الناس حج البيت من استطاع إليه سبيلاً} . والاستطاعة، فيما بلغنا، مركبٌ وزادٌ.

The obligation of ḥajj and garments of iḥrām

ENGLISHReading text

Pilgrimage is an obligation upon every Muslim, once in a lifetime. The Messenger of God ﷺ performed the pilgrimage, showed the people their rites (manasik), and taught them what is permitted to them in their pilgrimage (hajj) and 'umra and what is forbidden to them. The Messenger of God divested himself [of sewn garments] and commanded divesting (tajrid), and he forbade the wearing of shirts, trousers, hooded cloaks (burnus), turbans, leather socks (khuff), and caps. Hunting and prohibited acts

ENGLISHReading text

The muhrim (the person in ihram) must not hunt, nor order hunting, nor point out game, nor kill any animal except what the Messenger of God ﷺ permitted; for it has reached us that he said: Five vicious creatures: there is no blame on the muhrim for killing them: the scorpion, the mouse, the biting dog (al-kalb al-'aqur), the crow, the kite, and the snake; one doubts about killing them. As for the biting dog, its interpretation in our view is: every predatory animal that attacks people — the lion, the leopard, the wolf, and the like; and among the things that indicated this to us is that a dog that is not biting carries no blame for the muhrim in killing it, because it is tame and domestic — so how much more the biting among them; so we understood that he meant only what attacks among wild animals, and that is similar to the vicious creatures. The muhrim must not delouse his head or his garment, but he may scratch his head lightly,

ولا يصطاد المحرم ولا يأمر بصيدٍ ولا يدل عليه، ولا يقتل من الدواب شيئاً إلا ما أرخص فيه رسول الله ﷺ؛ فإنه بلغنا أنه قال: خمسٌ فواسق ليس على المحرم جناحٌ في قتلهن: العقرب، الفأرة، والكلب العقور، والغراب، والحدأة، والحية؛ يشك أحدٌ في قتلها. وأما الكلب العقور فإن تأويله في رأينا: كل ما عدا على الناس من السباع الأسد والنمر والذئب وما أشبهه؛ ومما دلنا على ذلك أن الكلب الذي ليس بعقورٍ ليس في قتله جناحٌ على المحرم لأنه أنيسٌ أهلي، فكيف بالعقور منها، فعرفنا أنه إنما أراد ما عدا من الوحش، فذلك شبه الفواسق. والمحرم لا يفلي رأسه ولا ثوبه، ويحك رأسه حكاً خفيفاً

The talbiya and first circumambulation

ENGLISHReading text

As for the community in the past, they did not abandon the talbiya until they went in the afternoon to the standing place (mawqif), and then they abandoned it until they had completed their pilgrimage. Al-Fadl b. 'Abbas and Usama b. Zayd rode behind the Messenger of God ﷺ, according to what has reached us, on the night of al-Muzdalifa and the morning of Jam': Usama rode behind him from 'Arafa to al-Muzdalifa, and it is reported of him that he did not cease reciting the talbiya until he came to al-Muzdalifa; and al-Fadl rode behind him from al-Muzdalifa until he cast pebbles at the Jamra, and it is reported of him that he did not cease reciting the talbiya until he cast pebbles at the Jamra; and they claimed that Ibn Mas'ud acted in accordance with this; and the imams today hold to this. When the muhrim arrives in Mecca, he begins with the circumambulation (tawaf) and touching the Corner (al-rukn), if he is able — though that is not obligatory upon him; he walks briskly (yakhubbu) for three circuits and walks [normally] for four;

العربيةالنصّ

فأما الجماعة فيما مضى فكانوا لا يتركون التلبية حتى يروحوا إلى الموقف، ثم يتركوها حتى يقضوا حجهم. وكان الفضل بن عباس وأسامة بن زيد ردفي رسول الله ﷺ، فيما بلغنا، ليلة المزدلفة وغداة جمعٍ، ردفه أسامة من عرفة إلى المزدلفة، فذكر عنه أنه لم يزل يلبي حتى جاء المزدلفة، وردفه الفضل من المزدلفة حتى رمى الجمرة، فذكر عنه أنه لم يزل يلبي حتى رمى الجمرة وزعموا أن ابن مسعود كان يعمل بذلك؛ والأئمة اليوم على ذلك. فإذا قدم المحرم مكة بدأ بالطواف واستلام الركن إن استطاع، وليس ذلك بواجبٍ عليه، يخب ثلاثة أطوافٍ ويمشي أربعة؛

ENGLISHReading text

The imams in the time of the Banu Marwan used to pray in it; but Ibn 'Umar, who was more reliable and more knowledgeable, God willing, used to say: He did not pray in it; he only performed ablution and prayed at al-Muzdalifa; and Ibn 'Umar used to pray at al-Muzdalifa.
