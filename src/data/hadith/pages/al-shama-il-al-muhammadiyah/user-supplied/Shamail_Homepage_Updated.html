About the edition.

Source. Dar al-Gharb al-Islami, Beirut, 1st ed. (2000), ed. Mahir Yasin al-Fahl, reviewed by Bashshar Awwad. Alifta uses it for hadith numbering and volume/page numbering.

Editorial policy. The manuscript-style source transcription and reading-friendly matn are kept separately. Sixteen entries in this dataset have no independent matn and refer instead to other transmissions; those are displayed as references rather than reconstructed quotations.

Translation status. The publication JSON marks the English renderings as retranslated and not yet reviewed. The design preserves that status in the source details rather than asserting that the wording has been critically authenticated.
