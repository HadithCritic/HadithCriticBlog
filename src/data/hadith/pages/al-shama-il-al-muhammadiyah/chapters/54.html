Hadith385/ 14
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 211
English
Isnad

Abū ʿAmmār al-Ḥusayn b. Ḥurayth, Qutayba b. Saʿīd and more than one narrated to us; they said: Sufyān b. ʿUyayna narrated to us, from al-Zuhrī, from Anas b. Mālik, who said:

Matn

The last glance I cast at the Messenger of God ﷺ was when he lifted the curtain on Monday, and I looked at his face, which was like a leaf of the Muṣḥaf. The people were behind Abū Bakr, and he signalled to the people to stay in place, with Abū Bakr leading them in prayer, and he let the drape fall. The Messenger of God ﷺ died at the end of that day.

Isnad

Qutayba narrated to us; he said: al-Layth narrated to us, from Ibn al-Hād, from Mūsā b. Sarjis, from al-Qāsim b. Muḥammad, from ʿĀʾisha, that she said:

Matn

Isnad

Abū Kurayb Muḥammad b. al-ʿAlāʾ narrated to us; he said: Abū Muʿāwiya narrated to us, from ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Abī Bakr — that is, Ibn al-Mulaykī — from Ibn Abī Mulayka, from ʿĀʾisha, who said:

Matn

When the Messenger of God ﷺ was taken, they differed over his burial. Abū Bakr said: “I heard something from the Messenger of God ﷺ that I have not forgotten. He said: ‘God does not take a prophet except in the place where he loves to be buried.’ Bury him in the place of his bed.”

العربية

حدثنا أبو كريب محمد بن العلاء ، قال : حدثنا أبو معاوية عن عبد الرحمن بن أبي بكر وهو ابن المليكي ، عن ابن أبي مليكة ، عن عائشة ، قالت : لما قبض رسول الله - صلى الله عليه وسلم - اختلفوا في دفنه ؛ فقال أبو بكر : سمعت من رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم شيئا ما نسيته ، قال : « ما قبض الله نبيا إلا في الموضع الذي يحب أن يدفن فيه » ؛ ادفنوه في موضع فراشه .

Isnad

Bishr b. Hilāl al-Ṣawwāf al-Baṣrī narrated to us; he said: Jaʿfar b. Sulaymān narrated to us, from Thābit, from Anas, who said:

On the day the Messenger of God ﷺ entered Medina, everything in it was illuminated; and on the day he died, everything in it grew dark! We had not yet shaken the dust from our hands — we were still in the midst of burying him ﷺ — when we found our hearts changed.

English
Isnad

Naṣr b. ʿAlī al-Jahḍamī narrated to us; he said: ʿAbd Allāh b. Dāwūd narrated to us; he said: Salama b. Nubayṭ narrated to us, from Nuʿaym b. Abī Hind, from Nubayṭ b. Sharīṭ, from Sālim b. ʿUbayd, who was a Companion, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ fainted during his illness, then recovered, and said: “Has the time of prayer come?” They said: “Yes.” He said: “Tell Bilāl to call the adhān, and tell Abū Bakr to lead the people in prayer.” Then he fainted again and recovered and said: “Has the time of prayer come?” They said: “Yes.” He said: “Tell Bilāl to call the adhān, and tell Abū Bakr to lead the people in prayer!” ʿĀʾisha said: “My father is a man easily moved to tears; when he stands in that place he weeps and cannot continue; so if only you would command someone else!” Then he fainted again and recovered and said: “Tell Bilāl to call the adhān, and tell Abū Bakr to lead the people in prayer; for you are the companions of Joseph!” So Bilāl was told and called the adhān, and Abū Bakr was told and led the people in prayer. Then the Messenger of God ﷺ found some relief and said: “Look for someone for me to lean on!” Barīra came, with another man, and he leaned on them. When Abū Bakr saw him, he went to step back, but he signalled to him to stay in his place until Abū Bakr completed his prayer. Then the Messenger of God ﷺ was taken, and ʿUmar said: “By God, I will not hear anyone say that the Messenger of God ﷺ has been taken without striking him with this sword of mine!” The people were unlettered, with no prophet before him among them, so the people held back. They said: “Sālim, go to the Companion of the Messenger of God ﷺ and call him!” I came to Abū Bakr while he was in the mosque, and I came to him weeping in bewilderment. When he saw me he said: “Has the Messenger of God ﷺ been taken?” I said: “ʿUmar says: I will not hear anyone say that the Messenger of God ﷺ has been taken without striking him with this sword of mine.” He said to me: “Go!” and I went with him. He came, and the people had gone in to the Messenger of God ﷺ. He said: “O people, make way for me!” and they made way for him. He came until he bent over him and touched him, and said: {Indeed you will die, and indeed they will die}. Then they said: “Companion of the Messenger of God ﷺ, has the Messenger of God ﷺ been taken?” He said: “Yes.” So they knew he had spoken truthfully. They said: “Companion of the Messenger of God ﷺ, will prayer be offered over the Messenger of God?” He said: “Yes.” They said: “How?” He said: “A group will enter, say the takbīr, pray and make supplication, then leave; then another group will enter, say the takbīr, pray and make supplication, then leave, until all the people have entered.” They said: “Companion of the Messenger of God ﷺ, will the Messenger of God ﷺ be buried?” He said: “Yes.” They said: “Where?” He said: “In the place where God took his soul, for God did not take his soul except in a good place.” So they knew he had spoken truthfully. Then he commanded that his paternal kinsmen wash him. And the Emigrants gathered to consult, and said: “Let us go to our brothers of the Anṣār and include them with us in this matter!” The Anṣār said: “A leader from us and a leader from you.” ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb said: “Who has the like of these three: {the second of two when they were in the cave, when he said to his companion: Do not grieve; indeed God is with us}? Who are the two?” Then he stretched out his hand and pledged allegiance to him, and the people pledged allegiance to him, a good and beautiful pledge.
