English
Isnad

Muḥammad b. Bashshār narrated to us; he said: Abū Dāwūd al-Ṭayālisī narrated to us; he said: Hammām narrated to us, from Qatāda; he said:

Matn

I said to Anas b. Mālik: “How was the sandal of the Messenger of God ﷺ?” He said: “Each had two straps (qibālān).”

Isnad

Isḥāq b. Mūsā al-Anṣārī narrated to us; he said: Maʿn narrated to us; he said: Mālik narrated to us; he said: Saʿīd b. Abī Saʿīd al-Maqburī narrated to us, from ʿUbayd b. Jurayj, that he said to Ibn ʿUmar:
