Hadith330/ 13
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 185
English
Isnad

Aḥmad b. Manīʿ, Saʿīd b. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-Makhzūmī and more than one narrated to us; they said: Sufyān b. ʿUyayna narrated to us, from al-Zuhrī, from ʿUbayd God, from Ibn ʿAbbās, from ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Do not exaggerate in praising me as the Christians exaggerated about the son of Mary. I am only a servant, so say: ‘the servant of God and His Messenger.’”

العربية

حدثنا أحمد بن منيع وسعيد بن عبد الرحمن المخزومي وغير واحد قالوا : حدثنا سفيان بن عيينة عن الزهري ، عن عبيد الله ، عن ابن عباس ، عن عمر بن الخطاب قال : قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : لا تطروني كما أطرت النصارى ابن مريم ، إنما أنا عبد فقولوا : عبد الله ورسوله »

English
Isnad

Isḥāq b. Manṣūr narrated to us; he said: ʿAbd al-Razzāq narrated to us; he said: Maʿmar narrated to us, from Thābit al-Bunānī and ʿĀṣim al-Aḥwal, from Anas b. Mālik,

Matn

that a tailor invited the Messenger of God ﷺ and set before him tharīd with gourd (dubbāʾ) on it. He said: “The Messenger of God ﷺ would pick out the gourd, and he loved gourd.” Thābit said: I heard Anas say: “Since then no food has been made for me in which I could have gourd put but that I had it put in.”

English
Isnad

Muḥammad b. Ismāʿīl narrated to us; he said: ʿAbd Allāh b. Ṣāliḥ narrated to us; he said: Muʿāwiya b. Ṣāliḥ narrated to us, from Yaḥyā b. Saʿīd, from ʿAmra, who said:

Matn

ʿĀʾisha was asked: “What did the Messenger of God ﷺ do in his house?” She said: “He was a human being among human beings: he would delouse his garment, milk his sheep, and serve himself.”
