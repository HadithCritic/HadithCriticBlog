Leaving Friday prayer

The shaykh, imam, scholar, the unique one (al-awhad), the great chief (al-sadr al-kabir), Taj al-Din Abu l-Yumn Zayd b. al-Hasan b. 03 / Friday and the Day of Increase

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

And by his chain of transmission, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Reported text · Matn

“Jibril came to me with a mirror in which there was a black spot. I said: What is this, O Jibril? He said: This is Friday; you and your people have been favored with it, and its name with your Lord is the Day of Increase (yawm al-mazid). He said: I said: And what is this spot? He said: The Hour will be established on it. I said: And what is the Day of Increase? He said: Your Lord, Blessed and Exalted, has made in Paradise a spacious valley in which are hills of musk; and when it is the day of Friday, the Lord, Blessed and Exalted, descends, and surrounds His assembly with pulpits of light, and the prophets sit upon them; and those pulpits are surrounded by pulpits of gold, crowned with rubies and chrysolite, and the truthful (siddiqun) and the martyrs sit upon them; and the people of the Gardens of Eden descend. They remain so, and God says: I am your Lord; I have been true to My promise to you; so ask Me and I shall give you. They say: We ask You for Your good pleasure (ridwan). He says: I am pleased with you, and with Me there is more. And there is no day more beloved to them than the day of Friday.”

05 / The roasted bird and ʿAlī

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

And by it he said:

Reported text · Matn

A roasted bird was given as a gift to the Messenger of God ﷺ. When it was placed before him he said: “O God, bring me the one most beloved of Your creation to You to eat with me of the bird.” He said: I said to myself: O God, make him a man of the Ansar. He said: Then 'Ali ﵇ came and knocked at the door softly. I said: Who is this? He said: 'Ali. I said: The Messenger of God ﷺ is occupied with something; so he left. 75 / Al-Ḥārith asks about faith

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

And by it, from al-Harith, who said:

Reported text · Matn

I came to 'Ali ﵇ and said: O Commander of the Believers, what is faith? Explain it to me. He said: “O Harith, faith is a thing on four pillars: on patience, certainty, justice, and jihad. He said: I said: On what is patience built? He said: Patience is built on four branches: on longing (shawq), apprehension (shafaq), asceticism (zuhd), and anticipation (tarqqub); so whoever longs for Paradise turns away from base desires, and whoever is apprehensive of the Fire turns back from the forbidden things, and whoever is ascetic in this world treats afflictions lightly, and whoever keeps watch for death hastens to good deeds. He said: I said: On what is certainty built? He said: On four branches: on the insight of sagacity, the recitation of wisdom, the admonition of lessons, and the practice (sunna) of the ancients.
