A letter of counsel

May God protect you, my brother, with that by which He protects the people of faith; may He shield you from what is disliked, grant you success in good deeds, and complete His favors upon you in all matters; and may He gather us and you in the Abode of Peace,

03 / A TRANSMITTED LETTER

The virtues of Mecca

Sanctity, sacred history, and the merits ascribed to the city

Source pp. 18–22
EDITION PAGE18
ENGLISH

These verses, my brother, God Most High sent down all of them about Mecca in particular, and He did not send them down about any other land. Then, my brother, I shall go on to give you what has come from the Prophet ﷺ in reports on the virtues of Mecca, the virtues of its people, and of whoever lives beside it.

Know, my brother, that the Messenger of God ﷺ, when he left Mecca, stopped at al-Ḥazwara, faced the Kaʿba, and said: "By God, I know that you are the most beloved of God's lands to me, and that you are the most beloved of God's earth to God, Mighty and Majestic, and that you are the best spot on the face of the earth and the most beloved of it to God Most High; and had your people not driven me out of you, I would not have left." And he ﷺ said in another hadith: "The best land on the face of the earth, and the most beloved of it to God Most High, is Mecca."

A transmitted letter, not an anthology of isnāds

The surviving text is attributed to al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī as advice to an ascetic living in Mecca who intended to move to Yemen. Its opening supplies later chains and a report of the letter’s occasion.
