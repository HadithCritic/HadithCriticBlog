English
Isnad

Yahya said:

Matn

And the greens (al-khudar), in our usage, are: the fresh produce (al-ritab), the fragrant herbs (al-rayahin), the vegetables (al-buqul), and the fruits, such as pear (kummathra), quince (safarjal), peach (khawkh), apple (tuffah), fig (tin), plum (ijjas), apricot (mishmish), pomegranate (rumman), cucumber (khiyar), qiththa', nabq, broad beans (baqilla), carrots (jazar), banana (mawz), muql, walnut, almond, watermelon (bittikh), and the like.

Isnad

Yahya said: Hasan b. Salih said:

Matn

Sadaqa is on wheat, barley, dates [p. 143] and raisins; and he said: This is what we have heard, that the Messenger of God ﷺ made sadaqa obligatory on it. And Hasan did not consider that there was sadaqa on anything other than that, whether sorghum (dhura) or anything else.

العربية
Isnad

The Arabic of this report is shown undivided.

Matn

قَالَ يَحْيَى، قَالَ حَسَنُ بْنُ صَالِحٍ: الصَّدَقَةُ فِي الْحِنْطَةِ وَالشَّعِيرِ وَالتَّمْرِ ⦗ص: 143⦘ وَالزَّبِيبِ، وَقَالَ: هَذَا الَّذِي سَمِعْنَا أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم " فَرَضَ فِيهِ الصَّدَقَةَ، وَلَمْ يَرَ حَسَنٌ فِيمَا سِوَى ذَلِكَ صَدَقَةً مِنْ ذُرَةٍ وَلَا غَيْرِهَا".

Isnad

Isma'il informed us. He said: al-Hasan narrated to us. He said: Yahya narrated to us. He said: 'Abd al-Salam b. Harb narrated to us, from 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib, who said:

Matn

Musa b. al-Mughira wished to take [sadaqa] from the greens of the land of Musa b. Talha, and Musa b. Talha said to him: There is nothing on greens; and he related it from the Messenger of God ﷺ. He said: So they wrote about that to al-Hajjaj, and al-Hajjaj wrote: Musa b. Talha knows better than Musa b. al-Mughira.

العربية
Isnad

Matn

أَرَادَ مُوسَى بْنُ الْمُغِيرَةِ أَنْ يَأْخُذَ مِنْ خُضَرِ أَرْضِ مُوسَى بْنِ طَلْحَةَ، فَقَالَ لَهُ مُوسَى بْنُ طَلْحَةَ: إِنَّهُ لَيْسَ فِي الْخُضَرِ شَيْءٌ، وَرَوَاهُ عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم. قَالَ: فَكَتَبُوا بِذَلِكَ إِلَى الْحَجَّاجِ، فَكَتَبَ الْحَجَّاجُ: أَنَّ مُوسَى بْنَ طَلْحَةَ أَعْلَمُ مِنْ مُوسَى بْنِ الْمُغِيرَةِ
