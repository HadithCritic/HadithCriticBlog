English
Isnad

The shaykh Abu 'Abd Allah al-Husayn b. 'Ali b. Ahmad b. al-Busri—may God grant him success—informed us. He said: Abu Muhammad 'Abd Allah b. Yahya b. 'Abd al-Jabbar al-Sukkari informed us, by recitation to him in Dhu al-Hijja of the year 415. He said: Abu 'Ali Isma'il b. Muhammad b. Isma'il al-Saffar informed us, by recitation to him. He said: Abu Muhammad al-Hasan b. 'Ali b. 'Affan narrated to us. He said: Yahya b. Adam narrated to us. He said: I asked Sharik about His saying: “And give its due on the day of its harvest” [Q. 6:141]. He said:

Matn

The tenth and half of the tenth.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا الشَّيْخُ أَبُو عَبْدِ اللَّهِ الْحُسَيْنُ بْنُ عَلِيِّ بْنِ أَحْمَدَ بْنِ الْبُسْرِيِّ أَحْسَنَ اللَّهُ تَوْفِيقَهُ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنَا أَبُو مُحَمَّدٍ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ يَحْيَى بْنِ عَبْدِ الْجَبَّارِ السُّكَّرِيُّ، قِرَاءَةً عَلَيْهِ فِي ذِي الْحِجَّةِ مِنْ سَنَةِ خَمْسَ عَشْرَةَ وَأَرْبَعِمِائََةٍ. قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنَا أَبُو عَلِيٍّ إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ إِسْمَاعِيلَ الصَّفَّارُ، قِرَاءَةً عَلَيْهِ قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مُحَمَّدٍ الْحَسَنُ بْنُ عَلِيِّ بْنِ عَفَّانَ قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ آدَمَ قَالَ: سَأَلْتُ شَرِيكًا عَنْ قَوْلِهِ:

Matn

The tenth and half of the tenth abrogated it—he said: and sometimes he said: the tenth and half of the tenth. And I said to Hafs: In what way does the meaning differ? He was silent. I said to him: Did you hear him mention Miqsam in it? He said: No.

Isnad

Isma'il informed us. He said: al-Hasan narrated to us. He said: Yahya narrated to us. He said: [p. 123] Sufyan b. 'Uyayna narrated to us, from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid, regarding His saying: “And give its due on the day of its harvest” [Q. 6:141], who said:

Matn

At the crop, you give a handful (qabs) from it, and it is like this—and he indicated with the tips of his fingers, as though taking with them—and at the cutting (siram) you give a grasp (qabd), and it is like this—and he indicated with his palm, as though gripping with it—he means: you give a handful, and you leave them to follow the traces of the cutters. Sufyan said: You let the poor follow the harvesters; whatever the sickle leaves, they take.

Isnad

Isma'il informed us. He said: al-Hasan narrated to us. He said: Yahya narrated to us. He said: Fudayl b. 'Iyad narrated to us, from Mansur, from Mujahid, regarding His saying: “And give its due on the day of its harvest” [Q. 6:141], who said:

Matn

When he harvests and the poor are present, he scoops for them from the ears; when he threshes and they are present, he gives them from the ears; and when he knows its measure, he sets aside its zakat. And at the picking of palms, if they are present, some of the stalks (thafariq) and of the palm are thrown to them; and when he knows its measure, he sets aside its zakat.
