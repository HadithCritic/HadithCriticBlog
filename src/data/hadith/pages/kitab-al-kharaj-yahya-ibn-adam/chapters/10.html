English
Isnad

Isma'il informed us. He said: al-Hasan narrated to us. He said: Yahya narrated to us. He said: Abu Bakr b. 'Ayyash narrated to us, from 'Asim b. Abi al-Najud, from Abu Wa'il, from Masruq, from Mu'adh b. Jabal, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ sent me to Yemen and ordered me to take the tenth from what the sky waters and what is watered rain-fed (ba'lan), and half of the tenth from what is watered by water-wheels (dawali).

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ عَيَّاشٍ، عَنْ عَاصِمِ بْنِ أَبِي النَّجُودِ، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، عَنْ مَسْرُوقٍ، عَنْ مُعَاذِ بْنِ جَبَلٍ، قَالَ:

Matn

" بَعَثَنِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلَى الْيَمَنِ، وَأَمَرَنِي أَنْ آخُذَ مِمَّا سَقَتِ السَّمَاءُ، وَمَا سُقِيَ بَعْلًا الْعُشْرَ وَمَا سُقِيَ بِالدَّوَالِيِّ نِصْفَ الْعُشْرِ "

Isnad

Isma'il informed us. He said: al-Hasan narrated to us. He said: Yahya narrated to us. He said: Jarir b. 'Abd al-Hamid al-Dabbi narrated to us, from Mansur, from al-Hakam, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ wrote to Mu'adh in Yemen: On what the sky waters or what is watered by ghayl [flowing streams], the tenth; and what is watered by the bucket (gharb), half of the tenth.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا جَرِيرُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الْحَمِيدِ الضَّبِّيُّ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنِ الْحَكَمِ، قَالَ: كَتَبَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلَى مُعَاذٍ بِالْيَمَنِ: "

Matn

فِيمَا سَقَتِ السَّمَاءُ ، أَوْ سُقِيَ غَيْلًا الْعُشْرُ، وَمَا سُقِيَ بِالْغَرْبِ، فَنِصْفُ الْعُشْرِ "

English
Isnad

Yahya said: And I asked Abu Iyas, who said:

Matn

The ba'l, the 'athariyy, and the 'adhy is that which is watered by sky water. Yahya said: And if the land is such that part of it is watered by free flow and part of it by the bucket (gharb), and in all of it five wasqs come out, then it is assessed proportionally: what was watered by free flow, the tenth; and what was watered by the bucket, half of the tenth. And the 'athariyy is what is sown by clouds and rain specifically, not watered except by the rain that falls on it; that is the 'athariyy. And the ba'l is those vines whose roots have gone down into the earth to the water, so they do not need watering for five or six years, and can bear being left unwatered; this is the ba'l. And the sayl is the water of the wadi when it flows. As for the ghayl, it is a flow smaller than the great flood: when a little flows with clear water, that is the ghayl; and the 'adhy is rain water. [p. 120]

English
Isnad

Yahya said:

Matn

Between Mecca and Yemen there are places where they sow twice a year. They said: We sow when the Pleiades set, and they harvest it and finish from it after five months or so; then they sow at the rising of Mirzam al-Jawza', which is al-Shi'ra [Sirius]. And they sow 'alas, which is a wheat, small grains in its husks, with two grains in each husk; and they sow al-mayiya, which is also a small-grained wheat; and they sow sult, which is a barley, except that it is white and small and has no husks, and some of it is green; and they sow dhura, which is a grain like wheat, except that it is eaten as rice is eaten, and some of them bake it as rice is baked also.
