About the edition.

Edition. ed. ʿAbd Allāh ʿUmar al-Bārūdī, Muʾassasat al-Kitāb al-Thaqāfiyya and Dār al-Janān, Beirut, 1st ed. 1408/1988. Text, headings, page numbers and report numbers follow this edition as given in the Shamela library (book 13071).

Headings. The edition numbers 166 headings: 125 open with bāb, 4 with kitāb, and 37 name their subject without either word. Each chapter here is one of these headings, in the edition's order and with its Arabic wording.

Subject groups. The 28 subject groups in the index (Purification, Prayer and so on) are an editorial aid for finding a chapter. They are not part of the work, and only where a group begins at one of the edition's kitāb headings does it follow the book's own division.

Numbering. Reports carry the edition's numbers, 1 to 1193, which skip some numbers. A number printed twice is marked as the edition marks it: 101/2, 754/2, 794 (م), 827 (م), 855 (م).

English translation. The English is a retranslation that has not yet been reviewed. For 221 reports, a note beneath the report records a part of the Arabic that the English leaves out or another point still open. The supplied English is not divided into isnad and matn, so it appears as one passage beside the divided Arabic. Reports 29 and 516 were missing from the earlier source; they were added from the edition with a new translation, which divides isnad and matn.

Transmitters. Names are shown as the source gives them.
