English
Isnad

Ya'qub b. Ibrahim al-Dawraqi narrated to us; he said: Hushaym b. Bashir narrated to us; Mansur informed us, from al-Hasan, from Hittan b. 'Abd Allah al-Raqashi, from 'Ubada b. al-Samit, ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

Take from me, take from me! God has made a way for them. The previously married (thayyib): one hundred lashes and then stoning; and the unmarried with the unmarried: one hundred lashes and banishment for a year.

English
Isnad

Ibn al-Muqri' narrated to us; he said: Sufyan narrated to us, from al-Zuhri, from 'Ubayd God, from Abu Hurayra,

Matn

Zayd b. Khalid, and Shibl, who said: We were with the Prophet ﷺ when a man came and said: I adjure you by God, judge between us by the Book of God. His opponent stood up—and he was more knowledgeable than him—and said: He is right; judge between us by the Book of God, and permit me (to speak). He said: Speak. He said: My son was a hired laborer for this man, and he committed adultery with his wife. I was told that my son must be stoned, so I ransomed him with one hundred sheep and a servant. Then I asked men of knowledge, and they informed me that my son must receive one hundred lashes and a year's banishment, and that this man's wife must be stoned. He said: I will judge between you by the Book of God: the one hundred sheep and the servant are to be returned, and your son receives one hundred lashes and a year's banishment. And go in the morning, O Unays, to this man's wife, and if she confesses, stone her. She confessed, so he stoned her.

English
Isnad

Ibn al-Muqri' narrated to us; he said: Sufyan narrated to us, from al-Zuhri, from 'Ubayd God, from Ibn 'Abbas, ؓ, who said:

Matn

'Umar, ؓ, said: I fear that a long time may pass for the people until someone says: “We do not find stoning in the Book of God,” and so they go astray by abandoning an obligation that God revealed. Behold, stoning is a right upon whoever commits adultery when he is muhsan, and the proof is established, or there is pregnancy, or confession. Behold, the Messenger of God ﷺ stoned, and we stoned with him.

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us; he said: Ma'mar informed us, from al-Zuhri, from Abu Salama b. 'Abd al-Rahman, from Jabir b. 'Abd Allah, ؓ:

Matn

that a man of Aslam came to the Prophet ﷺ and confessed to him that he had committed adultery. Then he confessed again, and he turned away from him; then he confessed again, and he turned away from him; until he had testified against himself four times. The Prophet ﷺ said: Are you insane? He said: No. He said: Are you muhsan? He said: Yes. So the Prophet ﷺ gave the order concerning him, and he was stoned at the prayer ground. When the stones afflicted him, he fled, but he was caught and stoned until he died. The Prophet ﷺ spoke well of him, but did not pray over him.

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 304
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya and Ahmad b. Yusuf al-Sulami narrated to us; they both said: 'Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us; he said: Ibn Jurayj informed us; he said: Abu l-Zubayr informed me that 'Abd al-Rahman b. Samit, nephew of Abu Hurayra, informed him

Matn

that he heard Abu Hurayra, ؓ, say: The Aslami came to the Prophet of God ﷺ and testified against himself four times that he had had unlawful relations with a woman, and each time he turned away from him. On the fifth time he turned to him and said: Did you have intercourse with her? He said: Yes. He said: Until that of yours disappeared into that of hers, as the kohl stick disappears into the kohl container and the rope into the well? He said: Yes; I did with her what a man lawfully does with his wife, but unlawfully. He said: What do you want by this statement? He said: I want you to purify me. So the Prophet ﷺ gave the order concerning him, and he was stoned. Then the Prophet ﷺ heard two of his Companions, one of whom was saying to the other: Look at this man whom God had concealed, yet his own soul did not leave him alone until he was stoned like a dog. The Prophet ﷺ was silent about them. Then he walked on for a while until he passed the carcass of a donkey with its leg raised. He said: Where are so-and-so and so-and-so? They said: Here we are—and al-Sulami said: here we two are—O Messenger of God! He said: Get down and eat from the carcass of this donkey. They said: O Prophet of God, may God forgive you, who would eat from this? He said: What you two just took from the honor of your brother is worse than eating carrion. By Him in whose hand is my soul, he is now in the rivers of Paradise, plunging into them. And al-Sulami said: wading about in them.

Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us; he said: Ma'mar informed us, from Yahya b. Abi Kathir, from Abu Qilaba, from Abu l-Muhallab, from 'Imran b. Husayn, ؓ:

Matn

that a woman of Juhayna confessed to adultery before the Prophet ﷺ and said: I am pregnant. The Prophet ﷺ called her guardian and said: Treat her well, and when she gives birth, inform me. He did so. Then the Prophet ﷺ gave the order concerning her, and her garments were tied tightly about her. Then he ordered that she be stoned, and she was stoned, and then he prayed over her. 'Umar said: O Messenger of God, you stoned her and then you pray over her? He said: She has repented with a repentance that, if it were divided among seventy of the people of Medina, would suffice them. Have you found anything better than that she gave herself freely for God?

Sulayman b. Dawud al-Qazzaz narrated to us; he said: Abu Dawud narrated to us; he said: Za'ida narrated to us, from al-Suddi, from Sa'd b. 'Ubayda, from Abu 'Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, who said:

Matn

'Ali, ؓ, addressed us and said: O people, carry out the hudud on your slaves, whether they are muhsan or not. A slave girl of the Messenger of God ﷺ committed adultery, and he commanded me to flog her. I came to her and found that she had only recently given birth, and I feared that if I flogged her she would die—or he said: that I would kill her. So I met the Prophet ﷺ and mentioned that to him, and he said: You did well.

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Abu Salih narrated to us; he said: al-Layth narrated to me; he said: Yunus narrated to me, from Ibn Shihab; he said: Abu Umama b. Sahl b. Hunayf informed me that some of the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ from the Ansar informed him:

Matn

that one of their men fell ill until he became emaciated, and his skin clung to his bone. A slave girl of one of them came in, and he was drawn to her and had relations with her. When men of his people entered to visit him, he told them of that and said: Seek a ruling for me from the Messenger of God ﷺ, for I have had relations with a slave girl who came in to me. They mentioned that to the Messenger of God ﷺ and said: O Messenger of God, we have not seen anyone afflicted with such hardship as he is in; if we carried him to you his bones would break apart; he is nothing but skin over bone. So the Messenger of God ﷺ commanded them to take one hundred palm stalks and strike him with them in one blow.

Matn

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Amr b. Hammad b. Talha narrated to us; he said:

Matn

Asbat—meaning Ibn Nasr—narrated to us, from Simak, from 'Alqama b. Wa'il, from his father Wa'il b. Hujr: that a woman was assaulted by a man in the darkness before dawn as she was making her way unwillingly to the mosque—Ibn Yahya said: struggling against him—so she called for help from a man who passed by her, and her assailant fled. Then a group of people passed by her, and she called to them for help, and they caught up with the one she had called upon, while the other had gone ahead of them and escaped. They brought him, leading him to her, and he said: I am the one who helped you, and the other has gone. They brought him to the Messenger of God ﷺ, and she told him that he had assaulted her, and the people told him that they had caught him running. He said: I was helping her against her assailant, and these people caught up with me and seized me. She said: He lies; he is the one who assaulted me. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: Take him away and stone him. He said: Then a man from among the people stood up and said: Do not stone him; stone me. I am the one who did the act to her. And he confessed. So three were gathered before the Messenger of God ﷺ: the one who assaulted her, the one who helped her, and the woman. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: As for you (the woman), God has forgiven you. And he spoke kindly to the one who had helped her. 'Umar said: Stone the one who confessed to adultery. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: No, he has repented to God.
