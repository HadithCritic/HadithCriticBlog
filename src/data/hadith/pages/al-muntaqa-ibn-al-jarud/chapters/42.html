English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya and Ahmad b. Yusuf narrated to us; both said: ‘Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, from Mansur, from Mujahid, from Abu ‘Ayyash al-Zuraqi (ؓ), who said:

Matn

We were with the Prophet (ﷺ) at ‘Usfan. He said: The polytheists faced us, led by Khalid b. al-Walid, and they were between us and the qibla. He said: The Prophet (ﷺ) led us in the Zuhr prayer, and they said: “They were in a state in which we could have caught them off guard!” Then they said: “There will now come upon them a prayer that is dearer to them than their own children and themselves.” He said: So Jibril descended with this verse between Zuhr and ‘Asr: {And when you are among them and lead them in prayer…}. He said: Then the prayer time came, and he commanded them — Ibn Yahya said: the Prophet (ﷺ) — and both said: they took up their weapons, and we formed two rows behind him. He said: Then he bowed and we all bowed together; then he rose and we all rose together; then the Prophet (ﷺ) prostrated with the row nearest to him, while the others stood guarding them. When they had prostrated and stood up, the others sat and prostrated in their place; then these advanced to the places of those, and those came to the places of these. He said: Then he bowed and they all bowed together; then he rose and they all rose together; then the Prophet (ﷺ) prostrated together with the row nearest to him, while the others stood guarding them. He said: When they sat, the others sat and prostrated; then he gave the salam to them and departed. The Messenger of God (ﷺ) prayed it twice: once at ‘Usfan, and once in the land of Banu Sulaym.

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us; he said: Ma'mar informed us, from al-Zuhri, from Salim, from Ibn 'Umar, ؓ, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ prayed the fear prayer with one of the two groups for one rak'a, while the other group faced the enemy. Then they departed and stood in the place of their companions, facing the enemy, and those others came and the Prophet ﷺ prayed one rak'a with them. Then the Prophet gave the salutation, and then this group completed one rak'a and that group completed one rak'a.

English
Isnad

Abu 'Abd Allah Hammad b. al-Hasan b. 'Anbasa al-Warraq narrated to us; he said: Rawh narrated to us; he said: Malik narrated to us, from Nafi':

Matn

that Ibn 'Umar, ؓ, when asked about the fear prayer, would say: The imam steps forward with a group of the people, and the imam prays one rak'a with them, while another group of them stands between him and the enemy, not having prayed. When those with him have prayed one rak'a, they move back to the place of those who have not prayed, without giving the salutation, and those who have not prayed come forward and pray one rak'a with him. Then the imam departs, having prayed two rak'as, and each of the two groups stands and prays one rak'a by themselves after the imam has departed, so that each of the two groups has prayed two rak'as. And if the fear is more severe than that, they pray on foot, standing upon their feet, or riding, facing the qibla or not facing it.

العربية

Matn

أن ابن عمر رضي الله عنهما كان إذا سئل عن صلاة الخوف ، قال : يتقدم الإمام وطائفة من الناس ، فيصلي بهم الإمام ركعة ، ويكون طائفة منهم بينه وبين العدو لم يصلوا ، فإذا صلى الذين معه ركعة استأخروا مكان الذين لم يصلوا ، ولا يسلموا ويتقدم الذين لم يصلوا فيصلوا معه ركعة ثم ينصرف الإمام وقد صلى ركعتين ، فيقوم كل واحد من الطائفتين فيصلون لأنفسهم ركعة بعد أن ينصرف الإمام ، فيكون كل واحدة من الطائفتين قد صلوا ركعتين ، وإن كان خوفا أشد من ذلك صلوا رجالا قياما على أقدامهم ، أو ركبانا مستقبلي القبلة وغير مستقبليها

English
Isnad

Bahr b. Nasr narrated to us, from Ibn Wahb, from Malik, from Yazid b. Ruman, from Salih b. Khawwat, from one who

Matn

prayed the fear prayer with the Messenger of God ﷺ on the day of Dhat al-Riqa': that one group lined up with him, and another group lined up facing the enemy. He prayed one rak'a with those who were with him, then remained standing while they completed (the prayer) by themselves. Then they departed and lined up facing the enemy, and the other group came, and he prayed with them the rak'a that remained of his prayer, then remained sitting until they completed by themselves, then gave the salutation.

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Isma'il b. Salim narrated to me; he said: Rawh b. 'Ubada said: Shu'ba and Malik b. Anas narrated to us, from Yahya b. Sa'id, from al-Qasim b. Muhammad, from Salih b. Khawwat, from Sahl b. Abi Hathma:

Matn

that he said regarding the fear prayer: One group stands in front of the imam and one group behind him. He prays with those behind him one rak'a with two prostrations, then sits in his place until they complete one rak'a with two prostrations. Then they move to the position of their companions, and their companions move to the place of these, and he prays with them one rak'a with two prostrations, then sits in his place until they pray one rak'a with two prostrations, then gives the salutation.
