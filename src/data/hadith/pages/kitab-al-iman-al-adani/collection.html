About the edition.

Edition. al-Dār al-Salafiyya, Kuwait, 1st ed., 1407 AH

Editor. Ḥamad b. Ḥamdī al-Jābirī al-Ḥarbī

Kind of work. Topical book (kitāb)

Structure. A short book in chapters (bāb). Most chapters hold one or two reports; each chapter heading is a chapter here, with its reports under it.

English translation. The English is the supplied translation, which follows the source record by record. Notes that the translation attaches to a report appear beneath it, apart from the Arabic and English text.

Arabic text. The Arabic is the text of the source page records, shown as supplied. A report’s printed number stands in its label instead of the text, and page markers are carried over from the source.

Isnad and matn. The English chain and words are divided as the translation gives them. The Arabic is divided at the same point by a fixed rule that does not alter the text; where the rule finds no clean division, the Arabic is shown whole.

Transmitters. Transmission paths list the names the translation gives for each chain, in English only. No transmitter is identified beyond the wording of the source.

Dating. The date shown is the death year of Ibn Abī ʿUmar al-ʿAdanī, 243 AH, not a verified date of composition.

Coverage. 79 entries in 56 sections, in the source order.
