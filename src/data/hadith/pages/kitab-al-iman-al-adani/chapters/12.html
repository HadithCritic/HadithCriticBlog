English
Isnad

Yahya b. 'Isa narrated to us. He said: al-A'mash said: It was narrated to us from 'Amr b. Murra, from al-Mughira b. Sa'd al-Akhram, from his father or his uncle—al-A'mash was unsure—who said:

Matn

I came to the Messenger of God ﷺ wishing to ask him, and I approached him, and some of his Companions shouted at me. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Leave him, for he has some need in coming.” So I took hold of the camel’s halter and said: O Messenger of God, direct me to a deed that will bring me near to Paradise and keep me far from the Fire. He said: “If you have been brief…” and he was silent for a while, then raised his head to the sky and said: “You worship God and associate nothing with Him, establish the prayer, give the zakat, fast Ramadan, and love for people what you love to be done to you; and what you dislike to be done to you, leave people from it. Let go of the camel’s halter.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ عِيسَى،

Matn

قَالَ الْأَعْمَشُ: حُدِّثْنَا عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ مُرَّةَ، عَنِ الْمُغِيرَةِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ الْأَخْرَمِ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، أَوْ عَنْ عَمِّهِ - شَكَّ الْأَعْمَشُ - قَالَ: أَتَيْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أُرِيدُ أَنْ أَسْأَلَهُ، فَاسْتَقْبَلْتُهُ، فَصَاحَ بِي نَاسٌ مِنْ أَصْحَابِهِ، فقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ: «دَعُوهُ فَأَرَبٌ مَا جَاءَ بِهِ» فَأَخَذْتُ بِزِمَامِ النَّاقَةِ فقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، دُلَّنِي عَلَى عَمَلٍ يُقَرِّبُنِي مِنَ الْجَنَّةِ، وَيُبَاعِدُنِي مِنَ النَّارِ، قَالَ: «إِنْ كُنْتَ أَوْجَزْتَ» فَسَكَتَ سَاعَةً، ثُمَّ رَفَعَ رَأْسَهُ إِلَى السَّمَاءِ فقَالَ: «تَعْبُدُ اللَّهَ وَلَا تُشْرِكُ بِهِ شَيْئًا، وَتُقِيمُ الصَّلَاةَ، وَتُؤْتِي الزَّكَاةَ، وَتَصُومُ رَمَضَانَ، وَتُحِبُّ لِلنَّاسِ مَا تُحِبُّ أَنْ يُؤْتَى إِلَيْكَ، وَمَا كَرِهْتَ أَنْ يُؤْتَى إِلَيْكَ فَدَعِ النَّاسَ مِنْهُ خَلِّ عَنْ زِمَامِ النَّاقَةِ»

Isnad

Sufyan narrated to us, from Su'ayr b. al-Khims al-Tamimi, from Habib b. Abi Thabit, from Ibn 'Umar, who said: The Prophet ﷺ said:

Matn

“Islam is built on five: the testimony that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is the Messenger of God, the establishment of prayer, the giving of zakat, the fast of the month of Ramadan, and the pilgrimage to the House.”

English
Isnad

Sufyan narrated to us, from al-Sari b. Isma'il, from al-Sha'bi, who said: 'Ali said:

Matn

“Take from me these five words, for by God, if you were to ride the mounts until you wore them out, you would not find their like: Let no servant hope for anything but his Lord, and let him fear nothing but his sin; let him not be ashamed, when asked about what he does not know, to say ‘I do not know’; and let him not be ashamed to learn when he does not know; and patience is to faith as the head is to the body—there is no good in a body that has no head.”

English
Isnad

Sufyan b. 'Uyayna narrated to us. He said: Abu Hamza al-Thumali informed us. He said:

Matn

'Abd Allah b. al-Ahtam entered upon 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz and said: O Commander of the Faithful, would you like me to praise you? He said: “No.” He said: Would you like me to admonish you? He said: Yes. So he praised God and extolled Him, and said: “To proceed: God, mighty and majestic, in His majesty, His greatness and His power, created creation, being free of need of their obedience and secure against their disobedience. People at that time differed in opinion and standing, and the Arabs were in the worst of those stations: the people of the saddle-sore (ahl al-dabar), the people of camel hair (ahl al-wabar), the people of stone (ahl al-hajar) and the people of settled habitation (ahl al-hadar); the good things of the world and the ease of its living were gathered up to the exclusion of them. They did not ask God in congregation and did not recite a book; their living were blind and impure, and their dead were in the Fire, together with what cannot be counted of things to be shunned and turned away from. Then, when God willed to send to them their Prophet ﷺ and to spread His mercy among them, He raised among them a messenger from among themselves, grievous to him is what you suffer, anxious over you, to the believers compassionate and merciful: Muhammad ﷺ. Yet that did not stop them from wounding him in his body, giving him nicknames in place of his name, and driving him out of his home, while he had with him an admonition from his Lord: no step forward except by His command, and no departure except by His leave. He had taken the rope of protection (dhimma) from the Most High, and they had forced him into the belly of a cave, where he hid himself. Then, when he was commanded to resolve and was charged with jihad, his hesitation gave way (isbatarra lawthuhu) to God’s command, and he kept steadfastly to what God commanded him—conveying His message and striving against the one who turns away—until God took him, having discharged what he owed of His right. Then Abu Bakr rose after him, took up his sunna, called to his path and proceeded on his course, when the Arabs apostatized against him, or those of them who apostatized, and they were keen to establish the prayer but not to give the zakat. He refused to accept from them anything except what the Messenger of God ﷺ had accepted from them in his lifetime. So he drew the swords from their scabbards [p. 87], kindled the fire in its flames, and set the people of truth upon the shoulders of the people of falsehood; he did not cease cutting their limbs and watering the earth with their blood, until he brought them back into the door from which they had gone out and made them acknowledge what they had shunned. Then God took him to Himself, on the way of his Prophet; may God have mercy on him and forgive him.”

Translator note

This is an address delivered to 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz, not a hadith of the Prophet. Several rare expressions (ahl al-dabar, isbatarra lawthuhu, and the phrase on the ‘rope of protection’) are obscure; the rendering is tentative in these places. The source has an opening quotation mark before ‘To proceed’ and a closing one at the end.

English
Isnad

Sufyan narrated to us, from al-Zuhri, who said:

Matn

Abu Bakr was told: Accept from them that they not pay the zakat. He said: If they withheld from me anything of what they had affirmed to the Messenger of God, I would fight them over it. It was said to Abu Bakr: Did the Messenger of God ﷺ not say: “I have been commanded to fight people until they say: There is no god but God; and when they say it, they protect from me their blood and their wealth, except by its right, and their reckoning is with God”? Abu Bakr said: This is part of its right. If they withheld from me anything of what they gave to the Messenger of God, I would fight them over it. Do not separate between what God has joined. Sufyan said: “Meaning the prayer and the zakat.”

Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ:

Matn

قِيلَ لِأَبِي بَكْرٍ: اقْبَلْ مِنْهُمْ أَنْ لَا يُؤَدُّوا الزَّكَاةَ، فقَالَ: لَوْ مَنَعُونِي شَيْئًا مِمَّا أَقَرُّوا بِهِ لِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ، لَقَاتَلْتُهُمْ عَلَيْهِ، فَقِيلَ لِأَبِي بَكْرٍ: أَلَيْسَ قَدْ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم " أُمِرْتُ أَنْ أُقَاتِلَ النَّاسَ حَتَّى يَقُولُوا: لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، فَإِذَا قَالُوهَا عَصَمُوا مِنِّي دِمَاءَهُمْ وأمْوَالَهُمْ إِلَّا بِحَقِّهَا وَحِسَابُهُمْ عَلَى اللَّهِ؟ " فقَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ: هَذَا مِنْ حَقِّهَا، لَوْ مَنَعُونِي شَيْئًا مِمَّا أَعْطَوْا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، لَقَاتَلْتُهُمْ عَلَيْهِ، لَا تُفَرِّقُوا بَيْنَ مَا جَمَعَ اللَّهُ قَالَ سُفْيَانُ: «يَعْنِي الصَّلَاةَ وَالزَّكَاةَ»
