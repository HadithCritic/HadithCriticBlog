Narration36/ 6
Qirāʾāt al-Nabī · p. 87
English
Isnad

Abu 'Umara told us → Isma'il b. 'Ulayya → Rawh b. al-Qasim → 'Abd Allah b. Muhammad b. 'Aqil → al-Rubayyi' bint Mu'awwidh.

Matn

She said: Ibn 'Abbas came to me and asked me about the report I had related from the Messenger of God — that she saw the Prophet perform ablution and wash his feet. Ibn 'Abbas said: "People insist on washing, but I find nothing in God's Book except wiping."

Matn

[37] Abu Tumayla Yahya b. Wadih told us → 'Abd Allah b. al-Mubarak → Yunus b. Yazid → Ibn Shihab al-Zuhri → Anas b. Malik. The Messenger of God recited {And We ordained for them therein that a life is for a life} [5:45] — with the first noun in the accusative — (and the eye for the eye) in the nominative, to the end of the verse. Muhammad b. 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz told us → Abu Bakr 'Abd Allah b. Muhammad b. Ibrahim → Mu'awiya b. Hisham → Nusayr al-Ta'i → al-Salt al-Dahhan → Hamiya b. Ri'ab.

Matn

He said: I asked Salman about this verse, {That is because among them are priests (qissisin) and monks} [5:82]. He said: "Leave the qissisin in their hermitages and ruins! The Messenger of God taught it to me as: {That is because among them are siddiqin (men of truth) and monks}."

Narration41/ 6
Qirāʾāt al-Nabī · p. 91
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Abd al-'Aziz told us → Ahmad b. Ibrahim → 'Abd al-Samad b. 'Abd al-Warith → Malik b. Mighwal → 'Ali b. Mudrik → Abu 'Amir al-Ash'ari.

Matn

He said: A man was killed among them at Awtas, and the Prophet said to him, "O Abu 'Amir, would you not have changed it?" He then recited this verse: {O you who believe, you are responsible for yourselves; he who has gone astray cannot harm you when you are guided} [5:105]. The Messenger of God grew angry and said: "Where have you gone? It is only: O you who believe, you are responsible for yourselves; whoever has gone astray from among the disbelievers cannot harm you when you are guided."

العربية
Isnad

Isnad

al-Kisa'i told us → several people told me → Muhammad b. Sa'id → 'Ubada b. Nusayy → 'Abd al-Rahman b. Ghanm.

Matn

He said: We mentioned in the presence of Mu'adh {Is your Lord able…} [5:112]. He said: "The Messenger of God taught it to me many times, saying: (hal tastati'u rabbaka)" — with t ("Can you ask your Lord…").
