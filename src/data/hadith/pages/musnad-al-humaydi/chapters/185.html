English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us, from Sa'id ibn Abi 'Aruba and Yahya b. Subayh, from Qatada, from al-Nadr b. Anas, from Bashir b. Nahik, from Abu Hurayra, who traced it back to the Prophet ﷺ, who said:

Matn

“Any slave owned jointly by two men, if one of them emancipates his share and is wealthy, the slave’s value is assessed against him. If he has no wealth, the slave is made to work toward [the price of] his freedom without being subjected to hardship.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ أَبِي عَرُوبَةَ، وَيَحْيَى بْنِ صُبَيْحٍ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنِ النَّضْرِ بْنِ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ بَشِيرِ بْنِ نَهِيكٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، يَرْفَعُهُ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم، قَالَ:

Matn

«أَيُّمَا عَبْدٍ كَانَ بَيْنَ رَجُلَيْنِ فَأَعْتَقَ أَحَدُهُمَا نَصِيبَهُ، فَإِنْ كَانَ مُوسِرًا قُوِّمَ عَلَيْهِ، فَإِنْ لَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ مَالٌ اسْتُسْعَى الْعَبْدُ غَيْرَ مَشْقُوقٍ عَلَيْهِ»

Al-Humaydi narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us; al-Zuhri narrated to us; 'Anbasa b. Sa'id ibn al-'As informed me, from Abu Hurayra, who said:

Matn

“I came to the Messenger of God ﷺ and his Companions at Khaybar after they had conquered it, and I asked the Messenger of God ﷺ to assign me a share of the spoils.” One of the sons of Sa'id ibn al-'As said: “Messenger of God, do not assign him a share.” I said: “Messenger of God, this man killed Ibn Qawqal.” The son of Sa'id said: “How astonishing that a little hyrax, come down to us from Qadum Da'n [as written in the source], reproaches me for killing a Muslim man whom God honored by my hand, while not disgracing me at his!” Sufyan said: “I do not know whether he assigned him a share or did not assign him one.”

Isnad

Matn

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us; al-Zuhri narrated to us that he heard 'Abd al-Rahman al-A'raj say: I heard Abu Hurayra say:

Matn

They claim Abu Hurayra narrates too many reports from the Messenger of God ﷺ. God is the One before whom the reckoning will be! I was a poor man accompanying the Messenger of God ﷺ for enough to fill my stomach. The Ansar were occupied tending their property, and the Emigrants were occupied trading in the markets. I attended a gathering where the Messenger of God ﷺ was speaking. He said: “Who will spread his cloak until I finish speaking, then gather it to himself? He will never forget anything he hears from me.” I spread the mantle I was wearing until the Prophet ﷺ finished speaking, then gathered it to myself. By the One who sent him with the truth, I have forgotten nothing I heard from him since. Sufyan said: al-Mas'udi said: Another man stood up and spread his cloak, and the Prophet ﷺ said: “The young man of Daws has preceded you in this.”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

يَزْعُمُونَ أَنَّ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ يُكْثِرُ الْحَدِيثَ عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، وَاللَّهِ الْمُوعِدِ، إِنِّي كُنْتُ امْرَأً مِسْكِينًا أَصْحَبُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَلَى مَلْءِ بَطْنِي، وَكَانَتِ الْأَنْصَارُ يَشْغَلُهُمُ الْقِيَامُ عَلَى أَمْوَالِهِمْ، وَكَانَ الْمُهَاجِرُونَ يَشْغَلُهُمُ الصَّفْقُ بِالْأَسْواقِ، وَإِنِّي شَهِدْتُ مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَجْلِسًا وَهُوَ يَتَكَلَّمُ، فَقَالَ: «مَنْ يَبْسُطُ رِدَاءَهُ حَتَّى أَقْضِيَ مَقَالَتِي، ثُمَّ يَقْبِضَهُ إِلَيْهِ، فَلَا يَنْسَى شَيْئًا سَمِعَهُ مِنِّي 0؟» ، فَبَسَطْتُ بُرْدَةً كَانَتْ عَلَيَّ حَتَّى إِذَا قَضَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَقَالَتَهُ قَبَضَّتُهَا إِلَيَّ، فَوَالَّذِي بَعَثَهُ بِالْحَقِّ مَا نَسِيتُ شَيْئًا بَعْدُ سَمِعْتُهُ مِنْهُ، قَالَ سُفْيَانُ: قَالَ الْمَسْعُودِيُّ: وَقَامَ آخَرُ فَبَسَطَ رِدَاءَهُ، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «سَبَقَكَ بِهَا الْغُلَامُ الدَّوْسِيُّ»

Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us; Suhayl b. Abi Salih narrated to us, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, who said:

Matn

They asked: O Messenger of God, will we see our Lord on the Day of Resurrection? He said: “Do you have difficulty seeing the sun at noon when there is no cloud?” They said: No. He said: “Do you have difficulty seeing the moon on the night when it is full, with no cloud?” They said: No. “By the One in whose hand is my soul, you will have no more difficulty seeing your Lord than you do seeing either of them. He will meet a servant and say: ‘So-and-so, did I not honor you, make you a leader, marry you, place horses and camels at your service, and leave you to govern and take a quarter share?’ He will say: ‘Yes, my Lord.’ He will ask: ‘Did you think you would meet Me?’ He will say: ‘No.’ He will say: ‘Then I shall forget you as you forgot Me.’ He will meet a second and say the same, and the second will answer likewise. Then He will meet a third, who will say: ‘I believed in You, Your Book, and Your Messenger, and I prayed, fasted, and gave charity,’ praising himself as much as possible. He will say: ‘Then stay here.’ Then He will say: ‘Shall We not summon Our witness against you?’ The man will wonder who could testify against him. His mouth will be sealed and his thigh commanded: ‘Speak!’ His thigh, flesh and bones will speak of his deeds, so that he has no excuse against himself. That is the hypocrite with whom God is angry. Then a caller will announce: ‘Let every community follow what it worshipped besides God.’ Those who worshipped devils and crosses will follow them into Hell. We believers will remain, and our Lord will come to us, He being our Lord who will reward us. He will ask: ‘What are these people waiting for?’ They will say: ‘We are God’s believing servants. We believe in God, associate nothing with Him, and this is our station until our Lord comes to us—our Lord who will reward us.’ Then they will set out until they reach the bridge, where there are hooks of fire that seize people. Then intercession will be permitted, with the plea: ‘O God, grant safety! O God, grant safety!’ Once they have crossed the bridge, anyone who spent a pair of possessions from his wealth in God’s path will be called by every gatekeeper of Paradise: ‘O servant of God, O Muslim, there is good here; come!’” Abu Bakr said: O Messenger of God, such a servant has no loss if he leaves one gate and enters another. The Prophet ﷺ struck him gently with his hand and said: “By Him in whose hand is Muhammad’s soul, I hope you will be one of them.”
