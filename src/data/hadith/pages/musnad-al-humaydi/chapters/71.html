Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 437
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: Sulayman b. Suhaym, freedman of Ibn 'Abbas, told us: Ibrahim b. 'Abd Allah b. Ma'bad b. 'Abbas informed me, from his father, from Ibn 'Abbas, who said:

Matn

“The Messenger of God ﷺ lifted the curtain while the people were in rows behind Abu Bakr, and said: ‘Nothing remains of the glad tidings of prophethood except the righteous dream, which a Muslim sees or which is shown to him. Beware: I have been forbidden to recite (the Qur'an) while bowing or prostrating. As for bowing, glorify the Lord in it; and as for prostration, strive in supplication, for it is most likely to be answered for you.’”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ سُحَيْمٍ مَوْلَى ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَعْبَدِ بْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ قَالَ:

Matn

" كَشَفَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم السِّتَارَةَ وَالنَّاسُ صُفُوفٌ خَلْفَ أَبِي بَكْرٍ فَقَالَ: إِنَّهُ لَمْ يَبْقَ مِنْ مُبَشِّرَاتِ النُّبُوَّةِ إِلَّا الرُّؤْيَا الصَّالِحَةُ يَرَاهَا الْمُسْلِمُ أَوْ تُرَى لَهُ، أَلَا إِنِّي نُهِيتُ أَنْ أَقْرَأَ رَاكِعًا أَوْ سَاجِدًا، فَأَمَّا الرُّكُوعُ فَعَظِّمُوا الرَّبَ فِيهِ، وَأَمَّا السُّجُودُ فَاجْتَهِدُوا فِي الدُّعَاءِ فَقَمِنٌ أَنْ يُسْتَجَابَ لَكُمْ

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: 'Amr b. Dinar told us: 'Ata' b. Abi Rabah informed me, from Ibn 'Abbas, that

Matn

the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “When one of you eats, let him not wipe his hands until he licks them or has them licked.” [p. 438] Sufyan said: 'Amr b. Qays said to him: “O Abu Muhammad, 'Ata' only told us this from Jabir.” 'Amr said: “By God, I heard it from 'Ata' narrating it from Ibn 'Abbas before Jabir came to us in Mecca.” Sufyan said: “'Amr and 'Ata' met Jabir, God willing, in a year in which he stayed (in Mecca).”

Matn

«إِذَا أَكَلَ أَحَدُكُمْ فَلَا يَمْسَحْ يَدَيْهِ حَتَّى يَلْعَقَهَا أَوْ يُلْعِقَهَا» ⦗ص: 438⦘ قَالَ سُفْيَانُ: فَقَالَ لَهُ عَمْرُو بْنُ قَيْسٍ: يَا أَبَا مُحَمَّدٍ إِنَّمَا حَدَّثَنَاهُ عَطَاءٌ عَنْ جَابِرٍ فَقَالَ عَمْرٌو: وَاللَّهِ لَقَدْ سَمِعْتُهُ مِنْ عَطَاءٍ يُحَدِّثُهُ عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ قَبْلَ أَنْ يَقْدَمَ عَلَيْنَا جَابِرٌ مَكَّةَ قَالَ سُفْيَانُ: وَإِنَّمَا لَقِيَ عَمْرٌو وَعَطَاءٌ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى جَابِرًا فِي سَنَةٍ جَاوَرَ فِيهَا

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: 'Ata' told us, from Ibn 'Abbas, that

Matn

the Prophet ﷺ passed by a dead sheep belonging to a freedwoman of Maymuna, which had been given to her as charity. The Prophet ﷺ said: “What harm would it be for its owners if they took its hide, tanned it and benefited from it?” They said: “O Messenger of God, it is carrion.” He said: “Only eating it has been prohibited.” Sufyan would sometimes mention Maymuna in it and sometimes not, so we mention it such-and-such way.

Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا عَطَاءٌ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ

Matn

أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَرَّ بِشَاةٍ لِمَوْلَاةٍ لِمَيْمُونَةَ قَدْ أُعْطِيَتْهَا مِنَ الصَّدَقَةِ مَيِّتَةً قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «مَا عَلَى أَهْلِ هَذِهِ لَوْ أَخَذُوا إِهَابَهَا فَدَبَغُوهُ وَانْتَفَعُوا بِهِ» فَقَالُوا يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنَّهَا مَيْتَةٌ فَقَالَ «إِنَّمَا حُرِّمَ أَكْلُهَا» وَكَانَ سُفْيَانُ رُبَّمَا ذَكَرَ فِيهِ مَيْمُونَةَ وَرُبَّمَا لَمْ يَذْكُرْهُ فَنَحْنُ نَذْكُرُ كَذَا وَكَذَا

Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 438
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: 'Amr b. Dinar told us, from 'Ata', and Ibn Jurayj told us, from 'Ata', from Ibn 'Abbas. 'Amr said:

Matn

“The Messenger of God ﷺ delayed the 'Isha' prayer one night,” and 'Umar went out and called: “The prayer, O Messenger of God! The women and children have fallen asleep.” Ibn Jurayj said: The Messenger of God ﷺ delayed 'Isha' one night, then the Messenger of God ﷺ came out [p. 439] with his head dripping water, saying: “This is its proper time, were it not that I would burden the believers; I would not pray except at this hour.” Ibn Jurayj said: He came out wiping the water from his side, saying: “It is the proper time, were it not that I would burden my community.” Sufyan would sometimes narrate this hadith and merge it from Ibn 'Abbas, from 'Amr and Ibn Jurayj, without mentioning the chain wording; but when he said ‘told us’, ‘I heard’ or ‘informed us’, he reported this from one and that from the other.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا عَمْرُو بْنُ دِينَارٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءٍ وَحَدَّثَنَاهُ ابْنُ جُرَيْجٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ

Matn

قَالَ عَمْرٌو: «أَعْتَمَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ذَاتَ لَيْلَةٍ بِالْعِشَاءِ» فَخَرَجَ عُمَرُ فَنَادَى فَقَالَ الصَّلَاةَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ قَدْ رَقَدَ النِّسَاءُ وَالْوِلْدَانُ وَقَالَ ابْنُ جُرَيْجٍ: أَخَّرَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ذَاتَ لَيْلَةٍ بِالْعِشَاءِ فَخَرَجَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ⦗ص: 439⦘ وَرَأْسُهُ يَقْطُرُ مَاءً وَهُوَ يَقُولُ: «إِنَّهُ لَلْوَقْتُ لَوْلَا أَنْ أَشُقَّ عَلَى الْمُؤْمِنِينَ مَا صَلَّيْتُ إِلَّا هَذِهِ السَّاعَةَ» قَالَ ابْنُ جُرَيْجٍ فَخَرَجَ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَهُوَ يَمْسَحُ الْمَاءَ عَنْ شِقَّهِ وَهُوَ يَقُولُ «إِنَّهُ الْوَقْتُ لَوْلَا أَنْ أَشُقَّ عَلَى أُمَّتِي» وَكَانَ سُفْيَانُ رُبَّمَا حَدَّثَ بِهَذَا الْحَدِيثِ فَأَدْرَجَهُ عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ عَنْ عَمْرٍو وَابْنِ جُرَيْجٍ مَا يَذْكُرُ فِيهِ الْخَبَرَ فَإِذَا قَالَ فِيهِ حَدَّثَنَا وَسَمِعْتُ أَوْ سَمِعْتُ أَوْ أَخْبَرَنَا أَخْبَرَ بِهَذَا عَلَى هَذَا وَهَذَا عَلَى هَذَا

Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: Ibn Tawus told us, from his father [p.

Matn

440], from Ibn 'Abbas, who said: “The Prophet ﷺ was commanded to prostrate on seven: on his hands, his knees, the tips of his toes, and his forehead; and he forbade — God willing — gathering up the hair and clothes.” Sufyan said: Ibn Tawus showed us: he put his hand on his forehead and passed it until it reached the tip of his nose; and my father used to count this as one.

Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 440
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: Sulayman al-Ahwal, maternal uncle of Ibn Abi Najih, told us: I heard Tawus say:

Matn

“When the Prophet ﷺ rose at night to pray tahajjud, he would say: ‘O God, to You belongs all praise; You are the Light of the heavens and the earth and whoever is in them. To You belongs all praise; You are the Sustainer of the heavens and the earth and whoever is in them. To You belongs all praise; You are the King of the heavens and the earth and whoever is in them. To You belongs all praise; You are the Truth, Your promise is true, meeting You is true, Paradise is true, the Fire is true, the Hour is true, Muhammad is true, and the prophets are true. O God, to You I have submitted, in You I have believed, upon You I have relied, to You I have turned in repentance, by You I have disputed, and to You I have referred judgment. So forgive me what I have sent ahead and what I have left behind, what I have concealed and what [p. 441] I have made public. You are the Advancer and You are the Delayer; there is no god but You’” — or he said: “there is no god other than You” (Sufyan was unsure). 'Abd al-Karim added: “and there is no power and no strength except through You,” which Sulaym did not say.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا سُلَيْمَانُ الْأَحْوَلُ خَالُ ابْنِ أَبِي نَجِيحٍ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ طَاوُسًا يَقُولُ:

Matn

«كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِذَا قَامَ مِنَ اللَّيْلِ يَتَهَجَّدُ قَالَ اللَّهُمَّ لَكَ الْحَمْدُ أَنْتَ نُورُ السَّمَوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ وَمَنْ فِيهِنَّ، وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ أَنْتَ قَيِّمُ السَّمَوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ وَمَنْ فِيهِنَّ، وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ أَنْتَ مَلِكُ السَّمَوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ وَمَنْ فِيهِنَّ، وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ أَنْتَ الْحَقُّ، وَوَعْدُكَ الْحَقُّ، وَلِقَاؤُكَ حَقٌّ، وَالْجَنَّةُ حَقٌّ، وَالنَّارُ حَقٌّ، وَالسَّاعَةُ حَقٌّ، وَمُحَمَّدٌ حَقٌّ، وَالنَّبِيُّونَ حَقٌّ، اللَّهُمَّ لَكَ أَسْلَمْتُ، وَبِكَ آمَنْتُ، وَعَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ أَنَبْتُ، وَبِكَ خَاصَمْتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ حَاكَمْتُ، فَاغْفِرْ لِي مَا قَدَّمْتُ، وَمَا أَخَّرْتُ، وَمَا أَسْرَرْتُ وَمَا ⦗ص: 441⦘ أَعْلَنْتُ، أَنْتَ الْمُقَدِّمُ، وَأَنْتَ الْمُؤَخِّرُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ» أَوْ قَالَ: «لَا إِلَهَ غَيْرُكَ» شَكَّ سُفْيَانُ وَزَادَ فِيهِ عَبْدُ الْكَرِيمِ «وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِكَ» وَلَمْ يَقُلْهَا سُلَيْمٌ

Narration504/ 9
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 441
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Rahman, freedman of the family of Talha, told us: Kurayb Abu Rishdin told us: I heard Ibn 'Abbas say:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ left the house of Juwayriyya after praying the dawn prayer — her name had been Barra, and he renamed her Juwayriyya, disliking it to be said that he left from Barra's. Ibn 'Abbas said: He left her at the time of the dawn prayer, then returned to her after the day had advanced while she was sitting in her place of prayer. He said to her: “Have you remained in this place of yours?” She said: “Yes.” The Prophet ﷺ said: “After leaving you I said four words, three times, which if weighed against all that you have said, would outweigh it: ‘Glory be to God and with His praise, as many as the number of His creation, as much as His good pleasure, the weight of His Throne, and the ink of His words.’”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ مَوْلَى آلِ طَلْحَةَ قَالَ: ثنا كُرَيْبٌ أَبُو رِشْدِينَ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ يَقُولُ:

Matn

خَرَجَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَنْ بَيْتِ جُوَيْرِيَةَ حِينَ صَلَّى الصُّبْحَ وَكَانِ اسْمُهَا بَرَّةُ فَسَمَّاهَا جُوَيْرِيَةَ كَرِهَ أَنْ يُقَالَ خَرَجَ مِنْ عِنْدِ بَرَّةَ قَالَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ فَخَرَجَ مِنْ عِنْدِهَا حِينَ صَلَّى الصُّبْحَ ثُمَّ رَجَعَ إِلَيْهَا بَعْدَمَا تَعَالَى النَّهَارُ وَهِيَ جَالِسَةٌ فِي مُصَلَّاهَا فَقَالَ لَهَا: «لَمْ تَزَالِي فِي مَجْلِسِكِ هَذَا» قَالَتْ: نَعَمْ فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لَقَدْ قُلْتُ بَعْدَكَ أَرْبَعَ كَلِمَاتٍ ثَلَاثَ مَرَّاتٍ لَوْ وُزِنَّ بِجَمِيعِ مَا قُلْتِ لَوَزَنَتْهُنَّ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ عَدَدَ خَلْقِهِ، وَرِضَى نَفْسِهِ، وَزِنَةَ عَرْشِهِ، وَمِدَادَ كَلِمَاتِهِ»
