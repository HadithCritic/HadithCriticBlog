Narration320/ 10
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 321
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Hisham b. 'Urwa narrated to us that he heard his father say:

Matn

Asma bint Abi Bakr al-Siddiq informed me, saying: My mother came to me, desirous [of my kindness], in the time of the treaty of Quraysh, and I asked the Messenger of God (ﷺ): Shall I maintain ties with her? He said: “Yes.” Sufyan said: And about her was revealed: “God does not forbid you from those who did not fight you” [Q 60:8] — the verse.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا هِشَامُ بْنُ عُرْوَةَ أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ أَبَاهُ يَقُولُ:

Matn

أَخْبَرَتْنِي أَسْمَاءُ بِنْتُ أَبِي بَكْرٍ الصِّدِّيقِ قَالَتْ: أَتَتْنِي أُمِّي رَاغِبَةً فِي عَهْدِ قُرَيْشٍ فَسَأَلْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَصِلُهَا؟ قَالَ «نَعَمْ» قَالَ سُفْيَانُ وَفِيهَا نَزَلَتْ {لَا يَنْهَاكُمُ اللَّهُ عَنِ الَّذِينَ لَمْ يُقَاتِلُوكُمْ} [الممتحنة: 8] الْآيَةَ

Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Hisham b. 'Urwa narrated to us that he heard his wife Fatima bint al-Mundhir narrating from her grandmother Asma, who said: The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said:

Matn

“One who claims to have received what he has not received is like one who wears two garments of falsehood.”

Narration322/ 10
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 322
English
Isnad

Muhammad informed us, saying: Bishr narrated to us, saying: Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Hisham b. 'Urwa narrated to us

Matn

that he heard his wife Fatima bint al-Mundhir narrating that she heard Asma bint Abi Bakr say: A woman asked the Messenger of God (ﷺ) about menstrual blood that gets on a garment. The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “Scrape it, then rub it with water, then sprinkle it with water, and pray in it.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مُحَمَّدٌ قَالَ: ثنا بِشْرٌ قَالَ: ثنا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا هِشَامُ بْنُ عُرْوَةَ

Matn

أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ امْرَأَتَهُ فَاطِمَةَ بِنْتَ الْمُنْذِرِ تُحَدِّثُ أَنَّهَا سَمِعَتْ أَسْمَاءَ بِنْتَ أَبِي بَكْرٍ تَقُولُ: إِنَّ امْرَأَةً سَأَلَتْ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ دَمِ الْحَيْضِ يُصِيبُ الثَّوْبَ؟ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «حُتِّيهِ ثُمَّ اقْرُصِيهِ بِالْمَاءِ ثُمَّ رُشِّيهِ بِالْمَاءِ وَصَلِّي فِيهِ»

Narration325/ 10
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 323
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: al-Walid b. Kathir narrated to us, from Ibn Tadrus, from Asma bint Abi Bakr, who said:

Matn

When “Perish the hands of Abu Lahab” [Q 111:1] was revealed, the one-eyed Umm Jamil bint Harb came, wailing, with a stone pestle (fihr) in her hand, saying: “Mudhammam [‘the Reviled’] we have refused, his religion we have despised, and his command we have disobeyed.” The Messenger of God (ﷺ) was sitting in the mosque, and then he recited Qur’an, with Abu Bakr beside him. When Abu Bakr saw her he said: O Messenger of God, she is coming, and I fear she will see you. The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “She will not see me,” and he recited Qur’an by which he sought protection, as he said, and he recited: “And when you recite the Qur’an, We place between you and those who do not believe in the Hereafter a concealing partition” [Q 17:45]. She came until she stood over Abu Bakr and did not see the Messenger of God (ﷺ), and said: O Abu Bakr, I was told that your companion satirized me. He said: No, by the Lord of this House, he did not satirize you. He said: She turned away, saying: “Quraysh know that I am the daughter of its chief.” [p. 324] He said: Al-Walid said in his hadith — or someone else said: Umm Jamil stumbled while circumambulating the House in her wrap (mirt) and said: “Perished is Mudhammam.” Umm Hakim, daughter of 'Abd al-Muttalib, said: “I am chaste, so I am not spoken to, and reserved, so I am not taught; and both of us, of the cousin-clan of Quraysh, [I] am the more knowing in the end.” (The last verse is obscure in the printed text.)

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا الْوَلِيدُ بْنُ كَثِيرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ تَدْرُسَ، عَنْ أَسْمَاءَ بِنْتِ أَبِي بَكْرٍ قَالَتْ:

Matn

لَمَّا نَزَلَتْ {تَبَّتْ يَدَا أَبِي لَهَبٍ} [المسد: 1] أَقْبَلَتِ الْعَوْرَاءُ أُمُّ جَمِيلٍ بِنْتُ حَرْبٍ وَلَهَا وَلْوَلَةٌ وَفِي يَدِهَا فِهْرٌ وَهِيَ تَقُولُ: مُذَمَّمٌ أَبَيْنَا، وَدِينَهُ قَلَيْنَا، وَأَمْرَهُ عَصَيْنَا، وَرَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم جَالِسٌ فِي الْمَسْجِدِ ثُمَّ قَرَأَ قُرْآنًا وَمَعَهُ أَبُو بَكْرٍ فَلَمَّا رَآهَا أَبُو بَكْرٍ قَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ قَدْ أَقْبَلَتْ وَأَنَا أَخَافُ أَنْ تَرَاكَ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «إِنَّهَا لَنْ تَرَانِي» وَقَرَأَ قُرْآنًا اعْتَصَمَ بِهِ، كَمَا قَالَ وَقَرَأَ {وَإِذَا قَرَأْتَ الْقُرْآنَ جَعَلْنَا بَيْنَكَ وَبَيْنَ الَّذِينَ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ بِالْآخِرَةِ حِجَابًا مَسْتُورًا} [الإسراء: 45] فَأَقْبَلَتْ حَتَّى وَقَفَتْ عَلَى أَبِي بَكْرٍ وَلَمْ تَرَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَتْ: يَا أَبَا بَكْرٍ إِنِّي أُخْبِرْتُ أَنَّ صَاحِبَكَ هَجَانِي فَقَالَ: لَا وَرَبِّ هَذَا الْبَيْتِ مَا هَجَاكِ قَالَ: فَوَلَّتْ وَهِيَ تَقُولُ: قَدْ عَلِمَتْ قُرَيْشٌ أَنِّي بِنْتُ سَيِّدِهَا ⦗ص: 324⦘ قَالَ فَقَالَ الْوَلِيدُ فِي حَدِيثِهِ أَوْ قَالَ غَيْرُهُ: تَعَثَّرَتْ أُمُّ جَمِيلٍ وَهِيَ تَطُوفُ بِالْبَيْتِ فِي مِرْطِهَا فَقَالَتْ: تَعِسٌ مُذَمَّمٌ فَقَالَتْ أُمُّ حَكِيمِ ابْنَةُ عَبْدِ الْمُطَّلِبِ: إِنِّي لَحَصَانٌ فَمَا أُكَلَّمُ، وَثَقَافٌ فَمَا أُعَلَّمُ، فَكِلْتَانَا مِنْ بَنِي الْعَمِّ قُرَيْشٍ بَعْدُ أَعْلَمُ

Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 324
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: al-Walid b. Kathir narrated to us, from Ibn Tadrus, from Asma bint Abi Bakr, that

Matn

they said to her: What was the worst that you saw the polytheists inflict on the Messenger of God (ﷺ)? She said: The polytheists were sitting in the mosque discussing the Messenger of God (ﷺ) and what he says about their gods. While they were so engaged, the Messenger of God (ﷺ) entered, and they rose to him; and whenever they asked him about [p. 325] anything he told them the truth. They said: Do you not say such-and-such? He said: “Indeed.” So they all seized hold of him. The cry for help reached Abu Bakr, and he was told: Rescue your companion. He went out from us — he had locks of hair (ghada’ir) — and entered the mosque saying: Woe to you! Would you kill a man for saying “My Lord is God,” when he has brought you clear proofs from your Lord? She said: So they left off the Messenger of God (ﷺ) and turned upon Abu Bakr. Abu Bakr returned to us, and he did not touch any of his locks but that it came away with him [in his hand], and he was saying: “Blessed are You, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا الْوَلِيدُ بْنُ كَثِيرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ تَدْرُسَ، عَنْ أَسْمَاءَ بِنْتِ أَبِي بَكْرٍ

Matn

أَنَّهُمْ قَالُوا لَهَا: مَا أَشَدَّ مَا رَأَيْتِ الْمُشْرِكِينَ بَلَغُوا مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَتْ: كَانَ الْمُشْرِكُونَ قَعَدُوا فِي الْمَسْجِدِ يَتَذَاكَرُونَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَمَا يَقُولُ فِي آلِهَتِهِمْ، فَبَيْنَمَا هُمْ كَذَلِكَ إِذْ دَخَلَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَامُوا إِلَيْهِ، وَكَانُوا إِذَا سَأَلُوا عَنْ ⦗ص: 325⦘ شَيْءٍ صَدَقَهُمْ، فَقَالُوا: أَلَسْتَ تَقُولُ كَذَا وَكَذَا فَقَالَ: «بَلَى» فَتَشَبَّثُوا بِهِ بِأَجْمَعِهِمْ فَأَتَى الصَّرِيخُ إِلَى أَبِي بَكْرٍ فَقِيلَ لَهُ: أَدْرِكْ صَاحِبَكَ فَخَرَجَ مِنْ عِنْدِنَا، وَإِنَّ لَهُ غَدَايِرَ فَدَخَلَ الْمَسْجِدَ وَهُوَ يَقُولُ: وَيْلَكُمْ أَتَقْتُلُونَ رَجُلًا أَنْ يَقُولَ رَبِّيَ اللَّهُ وَقَدْ جَاءَكُمْ بِالْبَيِّنَاتِ مِنْ رَبِّكُمْ، قَالَ: فَلَهَوْا عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَقْبَلُوا عَلَى أَبِي بَكْرٍ فَرَجَعَ إِلَيْنَا أَبُو بَكْرٍ فَجَعَلَ لَا يَمَسُّ شَيْئًا مِنْ غَدَايِرِهِ إِلَّا جَاءَ مَعَهُ وَهُوَ يَقُولُ: تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ

Narration328/ 10
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 325
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Abu al-Muhayyat narrated to us, from his mother, that

Matn

she said [p. 326]: When al-Hajjaj b. Yusuf killed 'Abd Allah b. al-Zubayr, al-Hajjaj came in to Asma bint Abi Bakr and said to her: Mother, the Commander of the Believers has commended you to me; do you have any need? She said: I have no need, and I am not a mother to you, but I am the mother of the one crucified at the head of the pass (al-thaniyya). But wait, and I will tell you what I heard from the Messenger of God (ﷺ), who said: “From Thaqif will come forth a great liar (kadhdhab) and a destroyer (mubir).” As for the liar, we have seen him — meaning al-Mukhtar — and as for the destroyer, it is you. Al-Hajjaj said: A destroyer of the hypocrites.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا أَبُو الْمُحَيَّاةِ، عَنْ أُمِّهِ

Matn

أَنَّهَا قَالَتْ ⦗ص: 326⦘ : لَمَّا قَتَلَ الْحَجَّاجُ بْنُ يُوسُفَ عَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ الزُّبَيْرِ دَخَلَ الْحَجَّاجُ عَلَى أَسْمَاءَ بِنْتِ أَبِي بَكْرٍ فَقَالَ لَهَا: يَا أُمَّهْ إِنَّ أَمِيرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ أَوْصَانِي بِكِ، فَهَلْ لَكِ مِنْ حَاجَةٍ؟ قَالَتْ: مَالِي مِنْ حَاجَةٍ وَلَسْتُ لَكَ بِأُمٍّ وَلَكِنِّي أُمُّ الْمَصْلُوبِ عَلَى رَأْسِ الثَّنِيَّةِ وَلَكِنِ انْتَظِرْ أُحَدِّثْكَ مَا سَمِعْتُ مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: «يَخْرُجُ مِنْ ثَقِيفٍ كَذَّابٌ وَمُبِيرٌ» فَأَمَّا الْكَذَّابُ فَقَدْ رَأَيْنَاهُ يَعْنِي الْمُخْتَارَ وَأَمَّا الْمُبِيرُ فَأَنْتَ فَقَالَ الْحَجَّاجُ: مُبِيرٌ لِلْمُنَافِقِينَ

Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 326
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: the brother of al-Zuhri narrated to us, saying: One who heard Asma bint Abi Bakr informed me; she said: The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said:

Matn

“O assembly of believing women, let none of you raise her head before the imam raises his head, because of the tightness of the men’s garments.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا أَخُو الزُّهْرِيِّ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي مَنْ سَمِعَ أَسْمَاءَ بِنْتَ أَبِي بَكْرٍ تَقُولُ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم:

Matn

«يَا مَعْشَرَ الْمُؤْمِنَاتِ لَا تَرْفَعَنَّ امْرَأَةٌ مِنْكُنَّ رَأْسَهَا قَبْلَ أَنْ يَرْفَعَ الْإِمَامُ رَأْسَهُ؛ مِنْ ضِيقِ ثِيَابِ الرِّجَالِ»
