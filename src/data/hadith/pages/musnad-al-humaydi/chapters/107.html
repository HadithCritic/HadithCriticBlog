Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 2, p. 56
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: al-Zuhri narrated to us: 'Ubayd Allah b. 'Abd Allah b. 'Utba informed me, from Zayd b. Khalid al-Juhani,

Matn

Abu Hurayra, and Shibl, who said: We were with the Prophet ﷺ when a man stood and said: “Messenger of God, I beseech you by God to judge between us according to God's Book.” His opponent stood—he had better knowledge of the law—and said: “Indeed, Messenger of God, judge between us according to God's Book, and give me permission to speak.” He said: “Speak.” The man said: “My son worked as a hired hand for this man and committed adultery with his wife. I was told my son must be stoned, so I ransomed him from the man with one hundred sheep and a servant. Then I asked men of knowledge, who told me my son must receive one hundred lashes and be banished for a year, and this man's wife must be stoned.” The Prophet ﷺ said: “By Him in whose hand is my soul, I shall judge between you according to God's Book. The hundred sheep and the servant are to be returned to you; your son is to receive one hundred lashes and be banished for a year. Unays, go tomorrow to this man's wife; if she confesses, stone her.” He went to her; she confessed, and he stoned her [p. 57]. Sufyan said: “Unays was a man of Aslam.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ: ثنا الزُّهْرِيُّ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي عُبَيْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُتْبَةَ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ خَالِدٍ الْجُهَنِيِّ، وَأَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، وَشِبْلٍ، قَالُوا:

Matn

كُنَّا عِنْدَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَقَامَ إِلَيْهِ رَجُلٌ، فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ أُنْشِدُكَ اللَّهَ إِلَّا قَضَيْتَ بَيْنَنَا بِكِتَابِ اللَّهِ، فَقَامَ خَصْمُهُ، وَكَانَ أَفْقَهُ مِنْهُ، فَقَالَ: أَجَلْ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ اقْضِ بَيْنَنَا بِكِتَابِ اللَّهِ، وَإِيذَنْ لِي فَلْأَقُلْ، قَالَ: «قُلْ» : قَالَ أَنَّ ابْنِي كَانَ عَسِيفًا عَلَى هَذَا، وَإِنَّهُ زَنَى بِامْرَأَتِهِ، فَأُخْبِرُتُ أَنَّ عَلَى ابْنِي الرَّجْمَ، فَافْتَدَيْتُ مِنْهُ بِمِائَةِ شَاةٍ وَخَادِمٍ، ثُمَّ سَأَلْتُ رِجَالًا مِنْ أَهْلِ الْعِلْمِ، فَأَخْبَرُونِي أَنَّ عَلَى ابْنِي جَلْدَ مِائَةٍ وَتَغْرِيبَ عَامٍ، وَأَنَّ عَلَى امْرَأَةِ هَذَا الرَّجْمَ، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ لَأَقْضِيَنَّ بَيْنَكُمَا بِكِتَابِ اللَّهِ، الْمِائَةُ شَاةٍ وَالْخَادِمُ رَدٌّ عَلَيْكَ، وَعَلَى ابْنِكَ جَلْدُ مِائَةٍ وَتَغْرِيبُ عَامٍ، وَاغْدُ يَا أُنَيْسُ عَلَى امْرَأَةِ هَذَا، فَإِنِ اعْتَرَفَتْ فَارْجُمْهَا» ، قَالَ: فَغَدَا عَلَيْهَا فَاعْتَرَفَتْ، فَرَجَمَهَا ⦗ص: 57⦘ . قَالَ سُفْيَانُ: «وَأُنَيْسٌ رَجُلٌ مِنْ أَسْلَمَ»

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: al-Zuhri narrated to us: 'Ubayd Allah b. 'Abd Allah b. 'Utba narrated to us

Matn

[p. 58], from Zayd b. Khalid, Abu Hurayra, and Shibl, who said: We were with the Prophet ﷺ when he was asked about a female slave who committed adultery before becoming married. The Prophet ﷺ said: “If one of your slave women commits adultery, flog her. If she repeats it, flog her. If she repeats it, flog her. On the third—or fourth—occasion, sell her, even for a rope of braided hair.”

Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 2, p. 58
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: Salih b. Kaysan narrated to us: 'Ubayd Allah b. 'Abd Allah b. 'Utba informed me, from Zayd b. Khalid al-Juhani, who said:

Matn

Rain fell at night in the time of the Messenger of God ﷺ. In the morning the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Did you not hear what your Lord said last night? He said: ‘Never do I bestow a blessing upon My servants without some of them waking up disbelieving in it, saying, We received rain because of such-and-such a star. Whoever believes in Me and praises Me for sending him rain believes in Me and disbelieves in the star. But whoever says, We received rain because of such-and-such a star, believes in the star and disbelieves in Me—or has denied My favor’” [p. 59]. Sufyan said: Ma'mar had first related this to us from Salih, and then we heard it from Salih directly.

Isnad

Matn

Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: Salih b. Kaysan narrated to us, from 'Ubayd Allah b. 'Abd Allah b. 'Utba. Sufyan said:

Matn

“I do not know whether [he related it from] Zayd b. Khalid or not.” He said: A man cursed a rooster in the Prophet's ﷺ presence, and the Prophet ﷺ said: “Do not curse the rooster, for it calls to prayer.”

Isnad

Matn

قَالَ سُفْيَانُ: لَا أَدْرِي زِيدُ بْنُ خَالِدٍ أَمْ لَا، قَالَ: سَبَّ رَجُلٌ دِيكًا عِنْدَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لَا تَسُبُّوا الدِّيكَ، فَإِنَّهُ يَدْعُو إِلَى الصَّلَاةِ»

Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: Yahya b. Sa'id narrated to us, from Muhammad b. Yahya b. Habban, from Abu 'Amra, from Zayd b. Khalid al-Juhani, who said:

Matn

We were with the Messenger of God ﷺ at Khaybar when a man of Ashja' died. The Prophet ﷺ did not pray over him but said: “Pray over your companion.” They searched his belongings and found some beads of the Jews, worth less than two dirhams.

Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 2, p. 61
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: Yahya b. Sa'id narrated to us, saying: I heard Yazid, the freedman of al-Munba'ith, say:

Matn

A man came to the Prophet ﷺ and asked him about a lost object. The Prophet ﷺ said: “Learn the appearance of its binding and container, then announce it for a year. If its owner recognizes it, [return it]; otherwise, mix it with your property.” He asked him about a stray sheep, and he replied: “It belongs to you, to your brother, or to the wolf.” He asked about a stray camel. The Prophet grew angry until his cheeks reddened and said: “What have you to do with it? It has its water supply and its feet; it reaches water and eats herbage until its owner comes for it.” Sufyan said: I was told that Rabi'a b. Abi 'Abd al-Rahman transmitted this with a chain reaching Zayd b. Khalid. I went to him and asked: “The report you relate from Yazid, the freedman of al-Munba'ith, concerning lost property and stray camels and sheep—is it from Zayd b. Khalid, from the Prophet ﷺ?” [p. 62]. He said: “Yes.” I had disliked his opinion, and so I had not asked him about it. Had he not supplied the chain, I would not have asked him for its isnad.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ: ثنا يَحْيَى بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ يَزِيدَ مَوْلَى الْمُنْبَعِثِ، يَقُولُ:

Matn

جَاءَ رَجُلٌ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَسَأَلَهُ عَنِ اللُّقَطَةِ، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «أَعْرِفْ عُفَاصَهَا وَوِعَاءَهَا ثُمَّ عَرِّفْهَا سَنَةً، فَإِنِ اعْتُرِفَتْ وَإِلَا فَاخْلِطْهَا بِمَالِكَ» قَالَ: وَسَأَلهُ عَنْ ضَالَّةِ الْغَنَمِ، فَقَالَ: «لَكَ أَوْ لِأَخِيكَ أَوْ لِلذِّئْبِ» وَسَأَلَهُ عَنْ ضَالَّةِ الْإِبِلِ، فَغَضِبَ حَتَّى احْمَرَّتْ وَجْنَتَاهُ، فَقَالَ: «مَا لَكَ وَلَهَا؟ مَعَهَا السِّقَاءُ وَالْحِذَاءُ، تَرِدُ الْمَاءَ وَتَأَكُلُ الْكَلَأَ، حَتَّى يَأْتِيَهَا رَبُّهَا» قَالَ سُفْيَانُ: " فَبَلَغَنِي أَنَّ رَبِيعَةَ بْنَ أَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ يُسْنِدُهُ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ خَالِدٍ، فَأَتَيْتُهُ، فَقُلْتُ لَهُ: الْحَدِيثُ الَّذِي تُحَدِّثُهُ عَنْ يَزِيدَ مَوْلَى الْمُنْبَعِثِ فِي اللُّقَطَةِ وَضَالَّةِ الْإِبِلِ وَالْغَنَمِ هُوَ عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ خَالِدٍ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم؟ ⦗ص: 62⦘ فَقَالَ: نَعَمْ، وَكُنْتُ أَكْرَهُهُ لِلرَّأْيِ، فَلِذَلِكَ لَمْ أَسْأَلْهُ عَنْهُ وَلَوْلَا أَنَّهُ أَسْنَدَهُ، مَا سَأَلْتُهُ عَنْ إِسْنَادِهِ "

Narration836/ 8
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 2, p. 62
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: Salim Abu al-Nadr narrated to us, from Busr b. Sa'id, who said:

Matn

Abu al-Juhaym sent me to ask Zayd b. Khalid al-Juhani what he had heard about someone passing in front of a person praying. He replied: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: “For one of you to stand [and wait] for forty is better than passing in front of someone praying” [p. 63]. He did not know whether he meant forty years, forty months, forty days, or forty hours.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ: ثنا سَالِمٌ أَبُو النَّضْرِ، عَنْ بُسْرِ بْنِ سَعِيدٍ، قَالَ:

Matn

أَرْسَلَنِي أَبُو الْجُهَيْمِ، سَأَلَ زَيْدَ بْنَ خَالِدٍ الْجُهَنِيَّ، مَا سَمِعْتَ فِي الَّذِي يَمُرُّ بَيْنَ يَدَيِ الْمُصَلِّي، فَقَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، يَقُولُ: «لَأَنْ يَمْكُثُ أَحَدُكُمْ أَرْبَعِينَ، خَيْرٌ لَهُ مِنْ أَنْ يَمُرَّ بَيْنَ يَدَيِ الْمُصَلِّي» ⦗ص: 63⦘ ، لَا يَدْرِي أَرْبَعِينَ سَنَةً، أَوْ أَرْبَعِينَ شَهْرًا، أَوْ أَرْبَعِينَ يَوْمًا، أَوْ أَرْبَعِينَ سَاعَةً

Narration837/ 8
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 2, p. 63
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: Ibn Abi Layla narrated to us, from 'Ata' b. Abi Rabah, from Zayd b. Khalid al-Juhani, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Whoever equips someone going on an expedition, or looks after his family in his absence, has himself participated in an expedition.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ: ثنا ابْنُ أَبِي لَيْلَى، عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ أَبِي رَبَاحٍ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ خَالِدٍ الْجُهَنِيِّ، قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم:

Matn

«مَنْ جَهَّزَ غَازِيًا أَوْ خَلَفَهُ فِي أَهْلِهِ، فَقَدْ غَزَا»
