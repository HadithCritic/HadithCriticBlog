Narration422/ 13
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 394
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: al-Zuhri narrated to us, saying: Abu Salama b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf informed me, saying:

Matn

I used to see dreams that made me shiver (u'ra), though I did not need to be wrapped up (uzammal). I came to Abu Qatada and complained of that to him, and he told me that he had heard the Messenger of God (ﷺ) say: “The good dream is from God, and the nightmare (hulm) is from Satan; so when one of you has a nightmare he dislikes, let him spit (yatfil) to his left three times and seek refuge in God from the evil of what he saw, for it will not harm him.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا الزُّهْرِيُّ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي أَبُو سَلَمَةَ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ عَوْفٍ قَالَ:

Matn

كُنْتُ أَرَى الرُّؤْيَا أُعْرَى مِنْهَا غَيْرَ أَنِّي لَا أُزَمَّلُ وَأَتَيْتُ أَبَا قَتَادَةَ فَشَكَوْتُ ذَلِكَ إِلَيْهِ فَحَدَّثَنِي أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: «الرُّؤْيَا مِنَ اللَّهِ، وَالْحُلْمُ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ فَإِذَا حَلُمَ أَحَدُكُمْ حُلْمًا يَكْرَهُهُ فَلْيَتْفُلْ عَنْ يَسَارِهِ ثَلَاثًا، وَيَسْتَعِذْ بِاللَّهِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا رَأَى فَإِنَّهُ لَنْ يَضُرَّهُ»

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: And four narrated it to us: Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Rahman, freedman of the family of Talha; 'Abd Rabbih and Yahya, the two sons of Sa'id; and Muhammad b. 'Amr b. 'Alqama — that they heard it from Abu Salama b. 'Abd al-Rahman,

Matn

narrating it from Abu Qatada, that the Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “The good dream is from God, and the nightmare is from Satan; so when one of you has a nightmare he dislikes, let him blow (yanfuth) to his left and seek refuge in God from the evil of what he saw, for it will not harm him.” [p. 395]

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ وَحَدَّثَنَاهُ أَرْبَعَةٌ مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ مَوْلَى آلِ طَلْحَةَ،

Matn

وَعَبْدُ رَبِّهِ، وَيَحْيَى ابْنَا سَعِيدٍ وَمُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَمْرِو بْنِ عَلْقَمَةَ أَنَّهُمْ سَمِعُوهُ مِنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ يُحَدِّثُهُ عَنْ أَبِي قَتَادَةَ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: «الرُّؤْيَا الصَّالِحَةُ مِنَ اللَّهِ، وَالْحُلْمُ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ فَإِذَا حَلُمَ أَحَدُكُمْ حُلْمًا يَكْرَهُهُ فَلْيَنْفُثْ عَنْ يَسَارِهِ، وَلْيَسْتَعِذْ بِاللَّهِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا رَأَى فَإِنَّهَا لَنْ تَضُرَّهُ» ⦗ص: 395⦘

Narration428/ 13
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 396
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Salih b. Kaysan narrated to us, saying: I heard Abu Muhammad say: I heard Abu Qatada say:

Matn

We went out with the Messenger of God (ﷺ) until, when we were at al-Qaha — and among us were those in ihram and those not in ihram — I suddenly saw my companions looking at something; I looked, and there was a wild donkey. I saddled my horse and mounted, and took my spear, and my whip fell. I said to my companions: Hand it to me — and they were in ihram — but they said: No, by God, we will not help you against it with anything. So I took my whip, then came upon the donkey from behind, while it was behind a small hill (akama), and I thrust at it with my spear and struck it down. I brought it to my companions; some of them said: Eat it, and some of them said: Do not eat it. The Prophet (ﷺ) was ahead of us, so I spurred my horse and caught up with him, and he said: “It is lawful, so eat it.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا صَالِحُ بْنُ كَيْسَانَ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ أَبَا مُحَمَّدٍ يَقُولُ: سَمِعْتُ أَبَا قَتَادَةَ يَقُولُ:

Matn

خَرَجْنَا مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم حَتَّى إِذَا كُنَّا بِالْقَاحَةِ وَمِنَّا الْمُحْرِمُ وَغَيْرُ الْمُحْرِمِ إِذْ بَصُرْتُ بِأصْحَابِي يَتَرَاءَونَ شَيْئًا فَنَظَرْتُ فَإِذَا أَنَا بِحِمَارِ وَحْشٍ فَأَسْرَجْتُ فَرَسِي فَرَكِبْتُ فَأَخَذْتُ رُمْحِي فَسَقَطَ سَوْطِي، فَقُلْتُ لِأَصْحَابِي نَاوِلُونِي وَكَانُوا مُحْرِمِينَ، فَقَالُوا: لَا وَاللَّهِ لَا نُعِينُكَ عَلَيْهِ بِشَيْءٍ فَتَنَاوَلْتُ سَوْطِي ثُمَّ أَتَيْتُ الْحِمَارَ مِنْ خَلْفِهِ وَهُوَ وَرَاءَ أَكَمَةٍ فَطَعَنْتُ بِرُمْحِي فَعَقَرْتُهُ فَأَتَيْتُ بِهِ أَصْحَابِي فَقَالَ بَعْضُهُمْ كُلُوهُ وَقَالَ بَعْضُهُمْ لَا تَأْكُلُوهُ وَكَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَمَامَنَا فَحَرَّكْتُ فَرَسِي فَأَدْرَكْتُهُ فَقَالَ «هُوَ حَلَالٌ فَكُلُوهُ»

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Muhammad b. 'Ajlan narrated to us, saying: Muhammad b. Qays informed me, from 'Abd Allah b. Abi Qatada [p.

Matn

397], from his father, who said: A man came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and said: O Messenger of God, what do you think: if I strike with this sword of mine in the path of God until I am killed, patient, hoping for reward, advancing and not retreating, will God expiate my sins for me? He said: “Yes.” Then he was silent for a while — I thought something was being revealed to him — and when the man turned away he said: “Come back; this is Jibril saying: Except if you have a debt.”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan said: Ishaq b. 'Abd Allah b. Abi Talha narrated to me, saying:

Matn

I heard a woman — I think she was the wife of 'Abd Allah b. Abi Qatada, Sufyan being unsure — [say] that Abu Qatada used to come to them and perform ablution at their house, and would tilt the vessel for the cat so that it could drink; so we asked him about its leftover, and he said: “The Messenger of God (ﷺ) informed us that it is not impure, and he said: ‘It is among those that go about among you, male and female.’”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي طَلْحَةَ قَالَ سَمِعْتُ امْرَأَةً أَظُنُّهَا امْرَأَةَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي قَتَادَةَ - يَشُكُّ سُفْيَانُ -
