Narration369/ 4
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 358
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Ibn Abi Husayn narrated to us, from Shahr b. Hawshab,

Matn

that he heard Asma bint Yazid b. Sakan say: The Messenger of God (ﷺ) spoke to us about the Dajjal and made his coming seem near. I said: O Messenger of God, I knead the dough for my family, and I do not think it will have risen before he comes out. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: “If he comes out while I am among you, I will be his adversary [in argument] (hajijuhu) on your behalf; and if he comes out after me, then God is my successor over every Muslim.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا ابْنُ أَبِي حُسَيْنٍ، عَنْ شَهْرِ بْنِ حَوْشَبٍ

Matn

أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ أَسْمَاءَ بِنْتَ يَزِيدَ بْنِ سَكَنٍ تَقُولُ: حَدَّثَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنِ الدَّجَّالِ فَقَرَّبَ أَمْرَهُ، فَقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنِّي لَأَعْجِنُ لِأَهْلِيَ الْعَجِينَ فَمَا أَظُنُّ أَنْ يَبْلُغَ حَتَّى يَخْرُجَ، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «إِنْ يَخْرُجْ وَأَنَا فِيكُمْ فَأَنَا حَجِيجُهُ دُونَكُمْ، وَإِنْ يَخْرُجْ بَعْدِي فَاللَّهُ خَلِيفَتِي عَلَى كُلِّ مُسْلِمٍ»

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Ibn Abi Husayn narrated to us, from Shahr b. Hawshab, from Asma bint Yazid b. Sakan, that he heard her

Matn

say: The Messenger of God (ﷺ) passed by me while I was among some women and greeted us, then said: “Beware of ingratitude to benefactors (kufr al-mun'imin).” I said: And what is ingratitude to benefactors? He said: “Perhaps one of you remains long unmarried (aymatuha) with her parents and grows old without marrying; then God, Mighty and Majestic [p. 359], provides her a husband and provides her through him wealth and children; then she gets angry one angry moment and denies it all, saying: I have never seen any good from you at any time.”

Narration371/ 4
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 359
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Ibn Abi al-Husayn narrated to us, from Shahr b. Hawshab, who said:

Matn

I came to Asma bint Yazid, and she brought near to me a basket (qina') with dates or fresh dates in it, and said: Eat. I said: I have no appetite for it. She cried out at me: Eat! For I am the one who adorned 'A’isha for the Messenger of God (ﷺ), and I brought her to him and seated her on his right. A vessel with milk in it was brought to the Prophet (ﷺ); he drank, then handed it to her; she lowered her head and was bashful. I said: Take it from the hand of the Messenger of God (ﷺ). So she took it and drank. Then he said to her: “Hand it to your companion (tirb).” I said: No, you drink, O Messenger of God, then hand it to me. He drank and then handed it to me, and I turned the vessel round to put my mouth on the place where his mouth had been. Then he said: “Give it to your friends.” They said: We do not want it. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: “Do not combine lying with hunger.” She said: Then the Messenger of God (ﷺ) noticed a gold bracelet on one of them and said: “Would you like God, Mighty and Majestic, to put around you in its place a bracelet of fire?” She said: We labored at it (fa-a'tawna 'alayhi) [the verb is as printed] until we removed it and threw it away, and to this very hour we do not know where it is. Then the Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “Is it not enough for one of you to make pearls (jumana) of silver, then take some saffron, dissolve it, and smear it over them, so that they look like gold?”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا ابْنُ أَبِي الْحُسَيْنِ، عَنْ شَهْرِ بْنِ حَوْشَبٍ قَالَ:

Matn

أَتَيْتُ أَسْمَاءَ بِنْتَ يَزِيدَ فَقَرَّبَتْ إِلِيَّ قِنَاعًا فِيهِ تَمْرٌ أَوْ رُطَبٌ، فَقَالَتْ: كُلْ، فَقُلْتُ: لَا أَشْتَهِيهِ، فَصَاحَتْ بِي كُلْ فَإِنِّي أَنَا الَّتِي قَيَّنْتُ عَائِشَةَ لِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَأَتَيْتُهُ بِهَا فَأَجْلَسْتُهَا عَنْ يَمِينِهِ، فَأَتَى النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِإِنَاءٍ فِيهِ لَبَنٌ فَشَرِبَ ثُمَّ نَاوَلَهَا وَطَأْطَأَتْ رَأْسَهَا وَاسْتَحْيَتْ، فَقُلْتُ: خُذِي مِنْ يَدِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَأَخَذَتْ فَشَرِبَتْ ثُمَّ قَالَ لَهَا «نَاوِلِي تِرْبَكِ» فَقُلْتُ: بَلْ أَنْتَ فَاشْرَبْ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ثُمَّ نَاوِلْنِي فَشَرِبَ ثُمَّ نَاوَلَنِي، فَأَدَرْتُ الْإِنَاءَ لِأَضَعَ فَمِي عَلَى مَوْضِعِ فِيهِ ثُمَّ قَالَ «أَعْطِي صَوَاحِبَاتِكِ» فَقُلْنَ: لَا نَشْتَهِيهِ فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لَا تَجْمَعْنَ كَذِبًا وَجُوعًا» قَالَتْ: فَأَبْصَرَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَلَى إِحْدَاهُنَّ سِوَارًا مِنْ ذَهَبٍ فَقَالَ: «أَتُحِبِّينَ أَنْ يُسْوِرَكِ اللَّهُ عز وجل مَكَانَهُ سِوَارًا مِنْ نَارٍ» قَالَتْ: فَأَعْتَوْنَا عَلَيْهِ حَتَّى نَزَعْنَاهُ فَرَمَيْنَا بِهِ فَمَا نَدْرِي أَيْنَ هُوَ حَتَّى السَّاعَةِ؟ ثُمَّ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «أَمَا يَكْفِي إِحْدَاكُنَّ أَنْ تَتَّخِذَ جُمَانًا مِنْ فِضَّةٍ ثُمَّ تَأْخُذَ شَيْئًا مِنْ زَعْفَرَانَ فَتُدِيفَهُ ثُمَّ تُلَطِّخَهُ عَلَيْهِ فَإِذَا هُوَ كَأَنَّهُ ذَهَبٌ»

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Ibn Abi al-Husayn narrated to us, from Shahr b. Hawshab,

Matn

that he heard Asma bint Yazid say: I pledged allegiance to the Messenger of God (ﷺ) along with some women, and he said: “Within what you are able and can bear.” We said: O Messenger of God, pledge us. He said: “I do not shake hands with you; I only take from you what God, Mighty and Majestic, took.”
