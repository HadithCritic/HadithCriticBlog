English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: al-Zuhri told us: I heard 'Isa b. Talha b. 'Ubayd Allah narrate from 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr b. al-'As that

Matn

a man asked the Messenger of God ﷺ: “O Messenger of God, I slaughtered before I threw (the pebbles).” He said: “Throw, and there is no harm.” Another said: “I shaved before I slaughtered.” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Slaughter, and there is no harm.” Sufyan was asked: “Is this among what you memorized from al-Zuhri?” He said: “Yes, as if I am hearing him, except that it is long and I memorized this part.” Bulayl said to him: “But 'Abd al-Rahman b. Mahdi narrates from you that you said you did not memorize it.” He said: “He is right; I did not memorize all of it, but as for this, I have mastered it.”

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: Hisham b. 'Urwa told us: my father informed me: I heard 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr b. al-'As say:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “God does not take away knowledge by snatching it from the hearts of men, but He takes it by taking away [p. 495] the scholars. When He leaves no scholar, people take ignorant leaders, who are asked and give rulings without knowledge, so they go astray and lead others astray.” 'Urwa said: Then I waited a year, then met 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr b. al-'As during tawaf, asked him about it, and he informed me of it.

Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib told us: my father informed me: I heard 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr b. al-'As say:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “There are two qualities that are easy, yet those who practice them are few, and no Muslim keeps to them without entering Paradise.” They said: “What are they, O Messenger of God?” He said: “You glorify (say subhan Allah) ten times after every prayer, magnify (Allahu akbar) ten times, and praise (alhamdulillah) ten times; and when you go to bed you glorify thirty-three, praise thirty-three, and magnify thirty-four.” Then Sufyan said: “One of them is thirty-four. So that is two hundred and fifty on the tongue and two thousand five hundred in the Scale.” 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr said: “I saw the Messenger of God ﷺ counting them on his hand.” Then he said: “Which of you does two thousand five hundred bad deeds in his day and night?” They said: “O Messenger of God, how then is it that they are not kept up?” He said: “Satan comes to one of you and says, ‘Remember such-and-such, remember such-and-such,’ until he gets up without having said them.” [p. 497] Sufyan said: “This was the first thing we asked 'Ata' about, and Ayyub told the people, when 'Ata' came to Basra, to go to him and ask him about this hadith.”
