The Shaykh, the Imam, the ascetic, the scholar, the hafiz Taqi al-Din Abu Muhammad 'Abd al-Ghani b. 'Abd al-Wahid b. 'Ali b. Surur al-Maqdisi — may God grant him success — narrated to us, he said: The jurist Abu al-Hasan Sa'd Allah b. Nasr al-Dajaji the preacher, and Abu al-Ma'ali Ahmad b. 'Abd al-Ghani b. Hanifa al-Bajisra'i narrated to us, both said: The Imam Abu Mansur Muhammad b. Ahmad b. 'Ali al-Muqri', known as al-Khayyat, narrated to us, he said: Abu Tahir 'Abd al-Ghaffar b. Ja'far al-Mu'addib narrated to us, [the text] being read to him while I listened, in the year 427, and he acknowledged it, he said: Abu 'Ali Muhammad b. Ahmad b. al-Hasan b. al-Sawwaf narrated to us, [the text] being read to him while I listened, he said: Abu 'Ali Bishr b. Musa narrated to us, he said:

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, he said: Sufyan narrated to us, he said: Muhammad b. 'Ajlan narrated to us

Matn

that he heard 'Iyad b. 'Abd Allah b. Sa'd b. Abi Sarh [p. 4] — that he heard Abu Sa'id al-Khudri say: The Messenger of God ﷺ liked these palm-stalks ('arajin), holding one in his hand and entering the mosque with it in his hand. He saw phlegm on the qibla wall of the mosque and scraped it off, then turned to the people, angry, and said: “Would any of you like to be spat in the face?” Then he said: “When a servant stands to pray, he is facing his Lord; so let him not spit in front of him, nor to his right, but let him spit to his left or beneath his left foot. And if an urge overcomes him while he is praying, let him spit into his garment, and say thus” — and Sufyan rubbed with his sleeve.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ: ثنا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَجْلَانَ،

Matn

أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ عِيَاضَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ سَعْدِ بْنِ أَبِي سَرْحٍ ⦗ص: 4⦘ ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ أَبَا سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيَّ، يَقُولُ: كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يُعْجِبُهُ هَذِهِ الْعَرَاجِينَ، يُمْسِكُهَا فِي يَدِهِ، وَيَدْخُلُ الْمَسْجِدَ وَهِيَ فِي يَدِهِ، فَرَأَى نُخَامَةً فِي قِبْلَةِ الْمَسْجِدِ، فَحَكَّهَا، ثُمَّ أَقْبَلَ عَلَى النَّاسِ مُغْضَبًا، فَقَالَ: «أَيُحِبُّ أَحَدُكُمْ أَنْ يُبْزَقَ فِي وَجْهِهِ» ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: «إِنَّ الْعَبْدَ إِذَا قَامَ إِلَى الصَّلَاةِ فَإِنَّمَا يُوَاجِهُ رَبَّهُ، فَلَا يَبْزُقْ بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ، وَلَا عَنْ يَمِينِهِ، وَلْيَبْزُقْ عَنْ يَسَارِهِ، أَوْ تَحْتَ قَدَمِهِ الْيُسْرَى، فَإِنْ عَجَّلَتْ بِهِ بَادِرَةٌ وَهُوَ يُصَلِّي، فَلْيَتْفُلْ فِي ثَوْبِهِ، وَلَيْقُلْ هَكَذَا» ، وَدَلَّكَ سُفْيَانُ بِكُمِّهِ

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, he said: Sufyan narrated to us, he said: Yazid b. Khusayfa narrated to us, he said:

Matn

I heard Busr b. Sa'id say: Abu Sa'id al-Khudri narrated to me, he said: I was in a circle in which Ubayy b. Ka'b was sitting, when Abu Musa al-Ash'ari came to us terrified — or he said: alarmed. We said: What is the matter with you? He said: 'Umar sent for me for some need and I came to him; I asked permission three times and was not permitted, so I returned. I said to him: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: “When one of you asks permission three times and is not permitted, let him return.” 'Umar said: You will bring proof for what you say, or I will do such and such to you. Ubayy b. Ka'b said to me: No one will stand with you but the youngest of the people. Sa'id [sic] said: I was the youngest of the people, so I went to 'Umar and told him that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “When one of you asks permission three times and is not permitted, let him return.”

Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, he said: Sufyan narrated to us, he said: Muhammad b. 'Ajlan narrated to us, he said: 'Iyad b. 'Abd Allah b. Sa'd b. Abi Sarh narrated to us, he said

Matn

[p. 11]: I saw Abu Sa'id al-Khudri come while Marwan b. al-Hakam was preaching on Friday. He stood and prayed two rak'as; the guards came to him to make him sit, but he refused to sit until he had prayed the two rak'as. When the prayer was over we went to him and said to him: O Abu Sa'id, they nearly did something to you. Abu Sa'id said: I would not abandon them for anything, after something I saw from the Messenger of God ﷺ. I saw the Messenger of God ﷺ when a man came while he was preaching on Friday, and he entered the mosque in a shabby state, and the Prophet ﷺ said to him: “Have you prayed?” He said: No. He said: “Then pray two rak'as.” Then the Messenger of God ﷺ urged the people to give charity, and the people threw down garments, and the Messenger of God ﷺ gave the man two garments of them. When the next Friday came, the man came while the Prophet ﷺ was preaching, and the Prophet ﷺ said: “Have you prayed two rak'as?” He said: No. He said: “Then pray two rak'as.” Then he urged the people to give charity, and they threw down garments, and the Messenger of God ﷺ gave the man two garments of them. When the next Friday came, the man came while the Prophet ﷺ was preaching, and the Prophet ﷺ said: “Have you prayed two rak'as?” He said: No. He said: “Then pray two rak'as.” Then he urged the people to give charity, and they threw down garments, and the man threw down one of his two garments; the Messenger of God ﷺ called out to him and said: “Take it,” and he took it. Then he said: “Look at this man. He came on that Friday in a shabby state, and I ordered the people to give charity, and they threw down garments, and I gave him two of them; and when this Friday came, I ordered the people to give charity, and he threw down one of his two garments.” Sufyan said: He means: “There is no charity except from surplus wealth ('an zahri ghina), and this man has no surplus beyond his garment.”
