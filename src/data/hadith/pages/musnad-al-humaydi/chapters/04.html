English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: al-Zuhri narrated to us:

Matn

Hasan and 'Abd Allah, sons of Muhammad b. 'Ali, informed me, from their father, that 'Ali, may God be pleased with him, said to Ibn 'Abbas, may God be pleased with them both: “The Messenger of God (ﷺ) forbade temporary marriage (nikah al-mut'a) and the meat of domestic donkeys at the time of Khaybar.” Sufyan said: He means that he forbade the meat of domestic donkeys at the time of Khaybar; he does not mean that the prohibition of temporary marriage was then.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ، ثنا سُفْيَانُ، ثنا الزُّهْرِيُّ، أَخْبَرَنِي حَسَنٌ،

Matn

وَعَبْدُ اللَّهِ ابْنَا مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ عَلِيٍّ عَنْ أَبِيهِمَا، أَنَّ عَلِيًّا رضي الله عنه قَالَ لِابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رضي الله عنهما: «إِنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم نَهَى عَنْ نِكَاحِ الْمُتْعَةِ، وَعَنْ لُحُومِ الْحُمُرِ الْأَهْلِيَّةِ زَمَنَ خَيْبَرَ» قَالَ سُفْيَانُ يَعْنِى أَنَّهُ نَهَى عَنْ لُحُومِ الْحُمُرِ الأَهْلِيَّةِ زَمَنَ خَيْبَرَ لَا يَعْنِى نِكَاحَ الْمُتْعَةِ

Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 171
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: 'Abd Allah b. Abi Najih narrated to us, from his father, who said: One who heard 'Ali, may God be pleased with him, informed me, saying:

Matn

I wanted to propose to the Messenger of God (ﷺ) for his daughter, then I remembered that I had nothing, but I remembered his kindness ('a’ida) and his generosity, so I proposed to her. He said to me: “Do you have anything to give her as dower?” I said: No. He said: “Where is your Hutami coat of mail that I gave you on such-and-such day?” I said: It is with me. He said: “Bring it.” I brought it and gave it to him, and he married her to me. When he brought her in to me he said: “Do nothing until I come to you both.” He came to us when we were under a cloak or velvet cover, and when we saw him we stirred. He said: “Stay where you are.” He called for a vessel with water, prayed over it, then sprinkled it on us. I said: O Messenger of God (ﷺ), is she dearer to you or am I? He said: “She is dearer to me than you, and you are more precious to me than she.” [p. 172] Abu 'Ali al-Sawwaf said: Ibrahim b. 'Abd Allah al-Basri narrated to us: Ibrahim b. Bashshar al-Ramadi narrated to us: Sufyan b. 'Uyayna narrated to us, from Ibn Abi Najih, from his father, who said: One who heard 'Ali b. Abi Talib on the pulpit of Kufa informed me, and he mentioned its meaning.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ، ثنا سُفْيَانُ، ثنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ أَبِي نَجِيحٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي مَنْ سَمِعَ عَلِيًّا رضي الله عنه يَقُولُ:

Matn

أَرَدْتُ أَنَ أَخْطُبَ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ابْنَتَهُ ثُمَّ ذَكَرْتُ أَنَّهُ لَا شَيْءَ لِي فَذَكَرْتُ عَائِدَتَهُ وَفَضْلَهُ فَخَطَبْتُهَا فَقَالَ لِي: «هَلْ عِنْدَكَ شَيْءٌ تُعْطِيهَا إِيَّاهُ؟» قُلْتُ: لَا، قَالَ «فَأَيْنَ دِرْعُكَ الْحُطَمِيَّةُ الَّتِي أَعْطَيْتُكَهَا يَوْمَ كَذَا وَكَذَا؟» قُلْتُ: هِيَ عِنْدِي، قَالَ «فَأْتِ بِهَا» قَالَ: فَجِئْتُ بِهَا فَأَعْطَيْتُهُ إِيَّاهَا فَزَوَّجَنِيهَا فَلَمَّا أَدْخَلَهَا عَلَيَّ قَالَ: «لَا تُحْدِثَا شَيْئًا حَتَّى آتِيكُمَا» فَجَاءَنَا وَعَلَيْنَا كِسَاءٌ أَوْ قَطِيفَةٌ، فَلَمَّا رَأَيْنَاهُ تَخَشْخَشْنَا فَقَالَ: «مَكَانَكُمَا» فَدَعَا بِإِنَاءٍ فِيهِ مَاءٌ فَدَعَا فِيهِ ثُمَّ رَشَّهُ عَلَيْنَا فَقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَهِيَ أَحَبُّ إِلَيْكَ أَمْ أَنَا؟ قَالَ: «هِيَ أَحَبُّ إِلِيَّ مِنْكَ، وَأَنْتَ أَعَزُّ عَلَيَّ مِنْهَا» ⦗ص: 172⦘ قَالَ أَبُو عَلِيٍّ الصَّوَّافُ: ثنا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ الْبَصْرِيُّ، ثنا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ الرَّمَادِيُّ، ثنا سُفْيَانُ بْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي نَجِيحٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي مَنْ سَمِعَ عَلِيَّ بْنَ أَبِي طَالِبٍ عَلَى مِنْبَرِ الْكُوفَةِ فَذَكَرَ مَعْنَاهُ

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: 'Amr b. Dinar narrated to us: 'Ata’ b. Abi Rabah informed me: I heard 'A’ish b. Anas say: I heard 'Ali b. Abi Talib on the pulpit of Kufa say:

Matn

“I used to find intense trouble from prostatic fluid (madhy)” and I wanted to ask the Messenger of God (ﷺ), but his daughter was with me and I was shy to ask him. So I ordered 'Ammar and he asked him, and he said: “Ablution is sufficient for it.”

Isnad

Matn

Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: Mutarrif b. Tarif narrated to us: I heard al-Sha'bi say: Abu Juhayfa informed me, saying: I said

Matn

to 'Ali b. Abi Talib: “Do you have anything from the Messenger of God (ﷺ) other than the Qur’an?” He said: “No, by Him who split the grain and created the soul, except for the understanding God gives a servant of His Book, or what is in the sheet (al-sahifa)” [p. 173]. I said: And what is in the sheet? He said: “The blood-money ('aql), the ransoming of captives, and that a Muslim is not to be killed for a disbeliever.”

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us: 'Ubayd Allah b. Abi Yazid informed me that he heard Mujahid say: I heard 'Abd al-Rahman b. Abi Layla narrating from 'Ali b. Abi Talib that

Matn

Fatima, daughter of the Messenger of God (ﷺ), came to the Messenger of God (ﷺ) “asking him for a servant.” He said: “Shall I not tell you what is better for you than that? Glorify God thirty-three times when you go to sleep, praise God thirty-three times, and magnify God thirty-four times.” Then Sufyan said: One of them is thirty-four. 'Ali said: I have not abandoned it since I heard it from the Messenger of God (ﷺ). They said to him: Not even the night of Siffin? He said: “Not even the night of Siffin.”

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us: 'Abd al-Malik b. Ibrahim narrated to us: Isma'il b. Muslim al-'Abdi narrated to me:

Matn

Abu Kathir narrated to us, saying: I was with my master 'Ali b. Abi Talib when he killed the people of al-Nahrawan, and the people felt disquiet in themselves over killing them. 'Ali said: O people, the Prophet of God (ﷺ) told me: “People will go out of the religion as the arrow goes out of the target, and they will never return to it. And the sign of that is that among them is a black man with a maimed arm, one of whose arms is like a woman’s breast with a nipple like a woman’s nipple.” [p. 183] He said: and I think he said: “around it are seven hairs. Look for him, for I do not see him except among them.” They found him at the edge of the river under the slain. He said: God and His Messenger have spoken the truth. And 'Ali was wearing an Arabian bow of his, poking with it at the man’s deformed arm (makhdaja). He said: The people rejoiced when they saw him and were glad, and what they had been feeling left them.
