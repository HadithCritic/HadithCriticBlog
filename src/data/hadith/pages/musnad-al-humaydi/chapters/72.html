Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: 'Amr told us: I heard 'Ata' narrate from Ibn 'Abbas, who said:

Matn

“The Messenger of God ﷺ only ran (ramal) around the House and between Safa and Marwa to show the polytheists his strength.”

Matn

«إِنَّمَا سَعَى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالْبَيْتِ وَبَيْنَ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ؛ لِيُرِي الْمُشْرِكِينَ قُوَّتَهُ»

“The Messenger of God ﷺ married while in ihram.” Abu al-Sha'tha' said: “Who do you think she was, 'Amr?” I said: “They claim it was Maymuna.” Abu al-Sha'tha' said: “So Ibn 'Abbas informed me: ‘The Prophet ﷺ married while in ihram.’”

Matn

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: Ibrahim b. 'Uqba, brother of Musa b. 'Uqba, told us: I heard Kurayb narrate

Matn

that he heard Ibn 'Abbas say: The Messenger of God ﷺ was returning, and when he was at al-Rawha' he met a caravan, greeted them and they returned his greeting. He said: “Who are the people?” They said: “The Muslims; who are you?” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “I am the Messenger of God.” A woman hurried to him and lifted a child of hers from her litter and said: “O Messenger of God, does this one have a hajj?” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Yes, and you have a reward.” Sufyan said: Ibn al-Munkadir first told it to us as a mursal narration, and it was said to him: “He only heard it from Ibrahim.” So I went to Ibrahim and asked him about it, and he told me of it and said: “I told it to Ibn al-Munkadir, and he took all of his family on hajj.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ عُقْبَةَ أَخُو مُوسَى بْنِ عُقْبَةَ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ كُرَيْبًا

Matn

يُحَدِّثُ أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ يَقُولُ: قَفَلَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَلَمَّا كَانَ بِالرَّوْحَاءِ لَقِي رَكْبًا فَسَلَّمَ عَلَيْهِمْ فَرَدُّوا عَلَيْهِ فَقَالَ: «مَنِ الْقَوْمُ» ؟ قَالُوا الْمُسْلِمُونَ فَمَنِ الْقَوْمُ؟ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «أَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ» فَفَزِعَتْ إِلَيْهِ امْرَأَةٌ فَرَفَعَتْ إِلَيْهِ صَبِيًّا لَهَا مِنْ مِحَفِّهِ فَقَالَتْ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ أَلِهَذَا حَجٌّ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «نَعَمْ، وَلَكِ أَجْرٌ» قَالَ سُفْيَانُ وَكَانَ ابْنُ الْمُنْكَدِرِ حَدَّثَنَاهُ أَوَّلًا مُرْسَلًافَقِيلَ لَهُإِنَّمَا سَمِعَهُ مِنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ فَأَتَيْتُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ فَسَأَلْتُهُ عَنْهُ فَحَدَّثَنِي بِهِ وَقَالَ حَدَّثْتُ بِهِ ابْنَ الْمُنْكَدِرِ فَحَجَّ بِأَهْلِهِ كُلِّهِمْ

Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: Ayyub told us, from 'Ikrima, who said:

Matn

When it reached Ibn 'Abbas that 'Ali had burned the apostates — meaning the heretics — Ibn 'Abbas said: “Had it been I, I would have killed them, because of the Messenger of God's ﷺ saying: ‘Whoever changes his religion, kill him’; and I would not have burned them, because of the Messenger of God's ﷺ saying: ‘It is not fitting for anyone to punish with God's punishment.’” Sufyan said: 'Ammar al-Duhni — who was in 'Amr b. Dinar's gathering while Ayyub was narrating this hadith — said: “'Ali did not burn them; he only dug pits for them and smoked them from them until he killed them.” 'Amr b. Dinar said: “Have you not heard their poet saying [poem, al-wafir meter]: ‘Let death cast me wherever it wishes, as long as it does not cast me into the two pits. When they bring firewood and fire, there death is paid in cash, not on credit.’”

Isnad

Al-Humaydi told us: Sufyan told us: al-Zuhri told us, from 'Ubayd Allah

Matn

— and in the last part of Sufyan's time he established Ibn 'Abbas in it — who said: A man came to the Prophet ﷺ on his way back from Uhud and said: “O Messenger of God, I saw a cloud shade dripping with ghee and honey, and I saw people scooping from it, some taking much and some little [p. 463]. And I saw a rope connecting to the sky; you took hold of it and God raised you; then a man after you took hold of it and rose; then another after him; then another after him, and it was cut off for him, then joined for him and he rose.” Abu Bakr said: “O Messenger of God, let me interpret it.” He said: “Interpret it.” He said: “The shade is Islam; what drips of ghee and honey while people scoop from it is the Qur'an, its sweetness and gentleness, some taking much and some little; and the rope connecting to the sky is the truth that you are upon: you took hold of it and God raised you, then another after him will take it and rise, then another after him, then another after him will take it and it will be cut off, then joined for him and he will rise. O Messenger of God, have I got it right?” He said: “You got part right and part wrong.” He said: “I implore you, O Messenger of God (tell me).” He said: “Do not swear, Abu Bakr.”
