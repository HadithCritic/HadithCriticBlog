English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us; 'Amr b. Dinar and Abu al-Zubayr narrated to us

Matn

that both heard Jabir b. 'Abd Allah say: “A man declared his slave to be freed after his death, having no other property. The Prophet ﷺ sold him, and Nu'aym b. al-Nahham bought him.” 'Amr b. Dinar said: Jabir said: “He was a Coptic slave who died last year during the rule of Ibn al-Zubayr.” Abu al-Zubayr added: “His name was Ya'qub the Copt.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ: ثنا عَمْرُو بْنُ دِينَارٍ، وَأَبُو الزُّبَيْرِ، أَنَّهُمَا سَمِعَا جَابِرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، يَقُولُ:

Matn

«دَبَّرَ رَجُلٌ غُلَامًا لَهُ لَيْسَ لَهُ مَالٌ غَيْرُهُ، فَبَاعَهُ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَاشْتَرَاهُ نُعَيْمُ بْنُ النَّحَّامِ» قَالَ عَمْرُو بْنُ دِينَارٍ: قَالَ جَابِرٌ: " عَبْدًا قِبْطِيًّا مَاتَ عَامَ الْأَوَّلِ فِي إِمَارَةِ ابْنِ الزُّبَيْرِ زَادَ أَبُو الزُّبَيْرِ: «اسْمُهُ يَعْقُوبُ الْقِبْطِيُّ»

Isnad

Matn

that both heard Jabir b. 'Abd Allah say: A man entered the mosque while the Prophet ﷺ stood on the pulpit delivering the Friday sermon. The Prophet ﷺ asked: “Have you prayed?” He said: No. He said: “Then pray two rak'as.” Sufyan said: Abu al-Zubayr identified the man in his report as Sulayk b. 'Amr al-Ghatafani.

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us; 'Amr b. Dinar narrated to us,

Matn

being asked in the presence of Ibn al-Munkadir, saying: I heard Jabir b. 'Abd Allah say: The Messenger of God ﷺ asked me: “Have you married, Jabir?” I said: Yes. He asked: “A virgin or a previously married woman?” I said: A previously married woman. He said: “Why not a young woman whom you could play with and who would play with you?” I said: O Messenger of God, my father was killed at Uhud and left nine daughters, who are my nine sisters. I did not want to bring them an inexperienced young woman like themselves, but rather a woman who would comb their hair and care for them. He said: “You have done well.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ: ثنا عَمْرُو بْنُ دِينَارٍ، قِيلَ تِلْقَاءَ ابْنِ الْمُنْكَدِرِ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ جَابِرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، يَقُولُ:

Matn

قَالَ لِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «أَنَكَحْتَ يَا جَابِرُ؟» ، قَالَ: نَعَمْ، قَالَ: «أَبِكْرٌ أَمْ ثَيِّبٌ؟» قُلْتُ: ثَيِّبٌ، قَالَ: «فَهَلَّا جَارِيَةً تُلَاعِبُكَ تُلَاعِبُهَا» ، قُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ قُتِلَ أَبِي يَوْمَ أُحُدٍ، وَتَرَكَ تِسْعَ بَنَاتٍ، وَكُنَّ لِي تِسْعَ أَخَواتٍ، فَلَمْ أُحِبَّ أَنْ أَجْمَعَ إِلَيْهِنَّ جَارِيَةً خَرْقَاءَ مِثْلَهُنَّ، وَلَكِنِ امْرَأَةٌ تُمَشِّطُهُنَّ، وَتَقُومُ عَلَيْهِنَّ، قَالَ: «أَصَبْتَ»

Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us; Muhammad b. al-Munkadir narrated to us that he heard Jabir b. 'Abd Allah say:

Matn

“I became ill, and the Messenger of God ﷺ and Abu Bakr came on foot to visit me. I lost consciousness. The Messenger of God ﷺ called for water, performed ablution, and poured it over me, and I came to. I said: O Messenger of God, how should I dispose of my property? What should I do with it? The Messenger of God ﷺ remained silent until the verse of inheritance was revealed.”

Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us; 'Amr b. Dinar narrated to us, saying: Muhammad b. 'Ali informed me, saying: I heard Jabir b. 'Abd Allah say:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ told me: “Jabir, if the revenue of Bahrain comes, I will give you this much, and this much, and this much,” gesturing three times. The Messenger of God ﷺ died before the Bahrain revenue arrived. It arrived during Abu Bakr’s caliphate, and Abu Bakr had a herald announce: “Anyone to whom the Prophet ﷺ owed a debt or made a promise should come forward.” Jabir said: I went to Abu Bakr and related the Messenger of God’s ﷺ promise. Abu Bakr scooped out a handful for me, then told me to count it. I counted five hundred. He said: “Take twice that amount as well.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ: ثنا عَمْرُو بْنُ دِينَارٍ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَلِيٍّ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ جَابِرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، يَقُولُ:

Matn

قَالَ لِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «يَا جَابِرُ لَوْ قَدْ جَاءَ مَالُ الْبَحْرَيْنِ لَأَعْطَيْتُكَ هَكَذَا، وَهَكَذَا، وَهَكَذَا» ، فَقُبِضَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَلَمْ يَأْتِ مَالُ الْبَحْرَيْنِ، وَأَتَى فِي خِلَافَةِ أَبِي بَكْرٍ، فَأَمَرَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ مُنَادِيًا فَنَادَى مَنْ كَانَ لَهُ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم دَيْنٌ أَوْ عِدَةٌ فَلْيَأْتِ، قَالَ جَابِرٌ: فَأَتَيْتُ أَبَا بَكْرٍ، فَقُلْتُ لَهُ: إِنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، قَالَ: «لَوْ قَدْ جَاءَ مَالُ الْبَحْرَيْنِ لَأَعْطَيْتُكَ هَكَذَا، وَهَكَذَا، وَهَكَذَا» فَحَثَى لِي أَبُو بَكْرٍ مَرَّةً، ثُمَّ قَالَ لِي: عُدَّهَا، فَعَدَدْتُهَا فَوَجَدْتُهَا خَمْسَمِائَةٍ، فَقَالَ خُذْ مِثْلَهَا مَرَّتَيْنِ

Sufyan said: Then I heard Ibn al-Munkadir narrate

Matn

that he heard Jabir b. 'Abd Allah tell the same account, except that he said: “He scooped out three handfuls for me.” Ibn al-Munkadir also added: Jabir said: Later I came to Abu Bakr and asked him to give me something, but he gave me nothing.
