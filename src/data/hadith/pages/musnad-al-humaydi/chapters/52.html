English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: al-Zuhri narrated to us, saying: 'Ata’ b. Yazid al-Laythi informed me that he heard Abu Ayyub al-Ansari say:

Matn

The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “It is not lawful for a Muslim to forsake his brother beyond three [days], the two meeting, and this one turning away and that one turning away; and the better of them is the one who begins with the greeting.” Sufyan said: Al-Zuhri had narrated to us before it the hadith of Anas, then followed it with this and said: And 'Ata’ b. Yazid informed me.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا الزُّهْرِيُّ قَالَ: وَأَخْبَرَنِي عَطَاءُ بْنُ يَزِيدَ اللَّيْثِيُّ أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ أَبَا أَيُّوبَ الْأَنْصَارِيَّ يَقُولُ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم:

Matn

«لَا يَحِلُّ لِمُسْلِمٍ أَنْ يَهْجُرَ أَخَاهُ فَوْقَ ثَلَاثٍ يَلْتَقِيَانِ فَيَصُدُّ هَذَا وَيَصُدُّ هَذَا وَخَيْرُهُمَا الَّذِي يَبْدَأُ بِالسَّلَامِ» قَالَ سُفْيَانُ كَانَ الزُّهْرِيُّ حَدَّثَنَا قَبْلَهُ حَدِيثَ أَنَسٍ ثُمَّ أتْبَعَهُ هَذَا فَقَالَ: وَأَخْبَرَنِي عَطَاءُ بْنُ يَزِيدَ

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: al-Zuhri narrated to us, saying: 'Ata’ b. Yazid al-Laythi informed me, from Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, who said: The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said:

Matn

“Do not face the qibla when relieving yourselves of stool or urine, and do not turn your backs to it, but face east or west.” Abu Ayyub said: We came to Syria and found latrines that had been built facing the qibla, so we turn aside and seek forgiveness from God, Mighty and Majestic. [p. 370] It was said to Sufyan: Nafi' b. 'Umar al-Jumahi does not give it a continuous chain. He said: But I memorize it and give it a chain as I told you. Then he said: The Meccans only took a book that Humayd al-A'raj brought from Syria, which had been written from al-Zuhri, and it came to Ibn Harhara; and the Meccans used to present that book to Ibn Shihab; as for us, we only used to hear from his mouth.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا الزُّهْرِيُّ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي عَطَاءُ بْنُ يَزِيدَ اللَّيْثِيُّ، عَنْ أَبِي أَيُّوبَ الْأَنْصَارِيِّ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم:

Matn

«لَا تَسْتَقْبِلُوا الْقِبْلَةَ بِغَائِطٍ وَلَا بَوْلٍ وَلَا تَسْتَدْبِرُوهَا وَلَكِنْ شَرِّقُوا أَوْ غَرِّبُوا» قَالَ أَبُو أَيُّوبَ فَقَدِمْنَا الشَّامَ فَوَجَدْنَا مَرَاحِيضَ بُنِيَتْ قِبَلَ الْقِبْلَةِ فَنَنْحَرِفُ وَنَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ عز وجل ⦗ص: 370⦘ ، فَقِيلَ لِسُفْيَانَ فَإِنَّ نَافِعَ بْنَ عُمَرَ الْجُمَحِيَّ لَا يُسْنِدُهُ، فَقَالَ: لَكِنِّي أَحْفَظُهُ وَأُسْنِدُهُ كَمَا قُلْتُ لَكَ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: إِنَّ الْمَكِّيِّينَ إِنَّمَا أَخَذُوا كِتَابًا جَاءَ بِهِ حُمَيْدٌ الْأَعْرَجُ مِنَ الشَّامِ قَدْ كُتِبَ عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ فَوَقَعَ إِلَى ابْنِ حَرْحَرَةَ وَكَانَ الْمَكِّيُّونَ يَعْرِضُونَ ذَلِكَ الْكِتَابَ عَلَى ابْنِ شِهَابٍ فَأَمَّا نَحْنُ فَإِنَّمَا كُنَّا نَسْمَعُ مِنْ فِيهِ

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Zayd b. Aslam narrated to us, saying: Ibrahim b. 'Abd Allah b. Hunayn informed me, from his father, who said:

Matn

Ibn 'Abbas and al-Miswar b. Makhrama disputed at al-'Arj about whether a person in ihram may wash his head. They sent me to Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, and I went to him and found him between the two posts of a well, bathing. When he saw me coming he gathered his garments to his chest, until I looked at him. I said: Your nephew Ibn 'Abbas sent me to you to ask how you saw the Messenger of God (ﷺ) wash his head while in ihram. He said: “With both his hands on his head; he moved them forward and back,” and he said: Like this. I returned to them and told them, and al-Miswar said to Ibn 'Abbas: I will never dispute with you again.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا زَيْدُ بْنُ أَسْلَمَ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ حُنَيْنٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ قَالَ:

Matn

امْتَرَأَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ وَالْمِسْوَرُ بْنُ مَخْرَمَةَ بِالْعَرَجِ فِي الْمُحْرِمِ يَغْسِلُ رَأْسَهُ؟ فَأَرْسَلُونِي إِلَى أَبِي أَيُّوبَ الْأَنْصَارِيِّ فَذَهَبْتُ إِلَيْهِ فَوَجَدْتُهُ بَيْنَ قَرْنَيِ الْبِئْرِ يَغْتَسِلُ، فَلَمَّا رَآنِي مُقْبِلًا جَمَعَ ثِيَابَهُ إِلَى صَدْرِهِ حَتَّى نَظَرْتُ إِلَيْهِ فَقُلْتُ: أَرْسَلَنِي إِلَيْكَ ابْنُ أَخِيكَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ أَسْأَلُكَ كَيْفَ رَأَيْتَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَغْسِلُ رَأْسَهُ وَهُوَ مُحْرِمٌ؟ فَقَالَ «بِيَدَيْهِ فِي رَأْسِهِ فَأَقْبَلَ بِهِمَا وَأَدْبَرَ» وَقَالَ: هَكَذَا فَرَجَعْتُ إِلَيْهِمَا فَأَخْبَرْتُهُمَا فَقَالَ الْمِسْوَرُ لِابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ لَا أُمَارِيكَ أَبَدًا

English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: Ibn Jurayj narrated to us, saying: I heard Abu Sa'd the blind narrating to 'Ata’ b. Abi Rabah, who said: Abu Ayyub set out to 'Uqba b. 'Amir, who was in Egypt, to ask him about a hadith that he had heard from the Messenger of God (ﷺ), of which no one was left who had heard it from the Messenger of God (ﷺ)

Matn

except himself and 'Uqba. When he arrived, he came to the house of Maslama b. Mukhallad al-Ansari, who was the governor of Egypt. [Maslama] was informed of him, so he hurried out to him and embraced him, then said: What brings you, Abu Ayyub? He said: A hadith that I heard from the Messenger of God (ﷺ), of which no one is left who heard it from the Messenger of God (ﷺ) except me and 'Uqba; so send someone to guide me to his house. He said: So he sent with him someone to guide him to the house of 'Uqba. 'Uqba was informed of him, so he hurried out to him and embraced him and said: What brings you, Abu Ayyub? He said: A hadith that I heard from the Messenger of God (ﷺ), of which no one is left who heard it except me and you, concerning the covering of a believer. 'Uqba said: Yes, I heard the Messenger of God (ﷺ) say: “Whoever covers a believer in this world over his disgrace, God will cover him on the Day of Resurrection.” Abu Ayyub said to him: You have spoken the truth. Then Abu Ayyub went back to his mount and rode it, returning to Medina, and the gift of Maslama b. Mukhallad did not catch up with him until al-'Arish in Egypt.

Matn

Narration389/ 9
Musnad al-Ḥumaydī · Vol. 1, p. 374
English
Isnad

Al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: 'Ubayda al-Dabbi narrated to us, from Ibrahim al-Nakha'i, from Sahm b. Minjab, from Qaza'a, from al-Qartha', from Abu Ayyub, that

Matn

when the sun passed the meridian the Messenger of God (ﷺ) would pray four [rak'as] and say: “The gates of heaven — or Paradise — are opened at the declining of the sun.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ قَالَ: ثنا سُفْيَانُ قَالَ: ثنا عُبَيْدَةُ الضَّبِّيُّ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ النَّخَعِيِّ، عَنْ سَهْمِ بْنِ مِنْجَابٍ، عَنْ قَزَعَةَ، عَنِ الْقَرْثَعِ، عَنْ أَبِي أَيُّوبَ

Matn

أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم كَانَ إِذَا زَالَتِ الشَّمْسُ يُصَلِّي أَرْبَعًا وَيَقُولُ: «إِنَّ أَبْوَابَ السَّمَاءِ تُفْتَحُ أَوِ الْجَنَّةِ عِنْدَ زَوَالِ الشَّمْسِ»
