Thumāma ibn Uthāl: captivity and conversion

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

We were also informed by the Shaykh, the hafiz Abu al-Khayr 'Abd al-Rahim al-Asfahani; narrated to us ⦗p. 32⦘ Abu Bakr Ahmad b. 'Ali al-Muqri', and Abu al-Faraj al-Bakrani, and a group; they said: 'Abd al-Razzaq b. 'Umar b. Musa narrated to us; Muhammad b. Ibrahim b. 'Ali narrated to us; ⦗p. 33⦘ Muhammad b. al-Hasan b. Qutayba and Muhammad b. Zabban b. Habib narrated to us — the two said: 'Isa b. Hammad Zu'ba narrated to us; al-Layth b. Sa'd narrated to us, from Sa'id al-Maqburi, that he heard Abu Hurayra ﵁ say:

Reported text · Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ ⦗p. 34⦘ sent cavalry toward Najd, and they came with a man from Banu Hanifa, called Thumama b. Uthal, the chief of the people of al-Yamama. They tied him to one of the pillars of the mosque. Then the Messenger of God ﷺ went out and said: «What do you have [to say], O Thumama b. Uthal?» He said: “I have good [to say], O Muhammad. If you kill me, you kill one who has blood; if you show favor, you show favor to one who is grateful; and if you want wealth, ask, and you will be given of it whatever you wish.” So the Messenger of God ﷺ left him until the day after tomorrow, and then said to him: «What do you have, O Thumama?» He said: “I have what I told you: if you show favor, you show favor to one who is grateful; if you kill, you kill one who has blood; and if you want wealth, ask, and you will be given of it whatever you wish.” Then the Messenger of God ﷺ said: «Release Thumama.» So he went off to a palm grove near the mosque, bathed, and then entered the mosque and said: “I bear witness that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is the Messenger of God. O Muhammad, by God, there was no face on earth more hateful to me than your face, and now your face has become the most beloved of all faces to me. By God, there was no religion more hateful to me than your religion, and now your religion has become the most beloved of all religions to me. By God, there was no land more hateful to me than your land, and now your land has become the most beloved of lands to me. Your cavalry seized me while I was intending the 'umra; so what do you think?” The Messenger of God ﷺ gave him glad tidings and commanded him to perform the 'umra. When he came to Mecca, someone said to him: “Have you become a Sabian (sabawta)?” He said: “No, but I have submitted (aslamtu) with Muhammad ﷺ, the Messenger of God. And no, by God, no wheat will come to you from al-Yamama until the Messenger of God ﷺ permits it.”

المتن

بَعَثَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ ⦗ص: 34⦘ وَسَلَّمَ خَيْلًا قِبَلَ نَجْدٍ، فَجَاءَتْ بِرَجُلٍ مِنْ بَنِي حَنِيفَةَ، يُقَالُ لَهُ: ثُمَامَةُ بْنُ أُثَالٍ، سَيِّدُ أَهْلِ الْيَمَامَةِ، فَرَبَطُوهُ بِسَارِيَةٍ مِنْ سَوَارِي الْمَسْجِدِ، فَخَرَجَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ، فَقَالَ: «مَا عِنْدَكَ يَا ثُمَامَةُ بْنَ أُثَالٍ؟» فَقَالَ: عِنْدِي يَا مُحَمَّدُ خَيْرٌ، إِنْ تَقْتُلْنِي تَقْتُلْ ذَا دَمٍ، وَإِنْ تُنْعِمْ تُنْعِمْ عَلَى شَاكِرٍ، وَإِنْ كُنْتَ تُرِيدُ الْمَالَ فَسَلْ تُعْطَ مِنْهُ مَا شِئْتَ. فَتَرَكَهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ حَتَّى كَانَ بَعْدَ الْغَدِ، قَالَ لَهُ: «مَا عِنْدَكَ يَا ثُمَامَةُ؟» قَالَ: عِنْدِي مَا قُلْتُ لَكَ: إِنْ تُنْعِمْ تُنْعِمْ عَلَى شَاكِرٍ، وَإِنْ تَقْتُلْ تَقْتُلْ ذَا دَمٍ، وَإِنْ كُنْتَ تُرِيدُ الْمَالَ فَسَلْ تُعْطَ مِنْهُ مَا شِئْتَ. فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: «أَطْلِقُوا ثُمَامَةَ» ، فَانْطَلَقَ إِلَى نَخْلٍ قَرِيبٍ مِنَ الْمَسْجِدِ، فَاغْتَسَلَ، ثُمَّ دَخَلَ الْمَسْجِدَ فَقَالَ: أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ، يَا مُحَمَّدُ، وَاللَّهِ مَا كَانَ عَلَى الْأَرْضِ وَجْهٌ أَبْغَضَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ وَجْهِكَ، فَقَدْ أَصْبَحَ وَجْهُكَ أَحَبَّ الْوُجُوهِ كُلِّهَا إِلَيَّ، وَاللَّهِ مَا كَانَ مِنْ دِينٍ أَبْغَضَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ دِينِكَ، فَقَدْ أَصْبَحَ دِينُكَ أَحَبَّ الدِّينِ كُلِّهِ إِلَيَّ، وَوَاللَّهِ مَا كَانَ مِنْ بَلَدٍ أَبْغَضَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ بَلَدِكَ، فَقَدْ أَصْبَحَ بَلَدُكَ أَحَبَّ الْبِلَادِ إِلَيَّ، وَإِنَّ خَيْلَكَ أَخَذَتْنِي وَأَنَا أُرِيدُ الْعُمْرَةَ، فَمَاذَا تَرَى؟ فَبَشَّرَهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ، وَأَمَرَهُ أَنْ يَعْتَمِرَ، فَلَمَّا قَدِمَ مَكَّةَ قَالَ لَهُ قَائِلٌ: صَبَوْتَ؟ قَالَ: لَا، وَلَكِنْ أَسْلَمْتُ مَعَ مُحَمَّدٍ ﷺ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، وَلَا وَاللَّهِ لَا يَأْتِيكُمْ مِنَ الْيَمَامَةِ حِنْطَةٌ حَتَّى يَأْذَنَ فِيهَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ.

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

Abu al-Fath informed us; Ahmad b. Yusuf narrated to us; Ahmad b. Ibrahim narrated to us; Ibn Bukayr narrated to us; he said: al-Layth narrated to me, from Khalid b. Yazid, from Sa'id ibn Abi Hilal, from Zayd b. Aslam, from 'Ata' b. Yasar, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, that

Reported text · Matn

he said: We said: “O Messenger of God, shall we see our Lord, the Mighty and Majestic?” He said: «Do you suffer any difficulty in seeing the sun when it is clear?» We said: “No.” He said: «Then do you suffer any difficulty in seeing the moon on the night of the full moon when it is clear?» We said: “No.” He said: «Then you will suffer no difficulty in seeing your Lord, the Mighty and Majestic, on that Day, except as you suffer difficulty in seeing those two.» Then he said: “A caller will call out: ‘Let every people go with what they used to worship.’ So the people of the cross will go with their cross, the people of the idols with their idols, and the people of every god with their god, until there remain those who used to worship God, the Mighty and Majestic, the righteous and the wicked, and remnants of the People of the Book. Then Jahannam will be brought forward, presenting itself as though it were a mirage. It will be said to the Jews: ‘What did you use to worship?’ They will say: ‘We used to worship 'Uzayr, the son of God.’ It will be said: ‘You have lied; God, the Mighty and Majestic, never had a consort or a child. So what do you want?’ ⦗p. 47⦘ They will say: ‘We want You to give us drink.’ It will be said: ‘Drink,’ and they will fall into Jahannam. Then it will be said to the Christians: ‘What did you use to worship?’ They will say: ‘We used to worship the Messiah, the son of God.’ It will be said: ‘You have lied; God never had a consort or a child. So what do you want?’ They will say: ‘We want You to give us drink.’ It will be said: ‘Drink,’ and they will fall, until there remain those who used to worship God, the Mighty and Majestic, the righteous and the wicked. It will be said to them: ‘What keeps you sitting here when the people have gone?’ They will say: ‘We parted from them when we were in greater need of them than we are today, and we heard a caller saying: “Let every people join what they used to worship,” and we are waiting for our Lord.’ Then the All-Compelling, the Mighty and Majestic, will come to them in a form other than the form in which they saw Him the first time, and He will say: ‘I am your Lord.’ They will say: ‘You are our Lord,’ and none will speak to Him except the prophets. He will say: ‘Is there a sign between you and Him that you recognize?’ They will say: ‘The shin.’ Then He will uncover His shin, and every believer will prostrate; and there will remain those who used to prostrate to God for show and repute. He will go to prostrate, but his back will become one single plate. Then the bridge will be brought and set across the midst of Jahannam.” We said: “O Messenger of God, what is the bridge?” He said: “A slippery place, a place of slipping, upon which are hooks, grappling-hooks, and broad thorns, having a curved thorn that is found in Najd, called al-Sa'dan. The believer will pass over it like the blink of an eye, like lightning, like the wind, and like the swift horses ⦗p. 48⦘ and riding-camels: one will be saved unharmed, one will be saved but scratched, and then one will be hurled into the Fire of Jahannam, until the last of them passes, dragged along. You are not more intense than we are in [seeking] the truth; it has become clear to you of the believers on that day, [in their appeal] to the All-Compelling, the Mighty and Majestic, when they see that they have been saved, concerning their brethren. They will say: ‘Our Lord, our brethren used to pray with us, fast with us, and act with us.’ Then God, the Mighty and Majestic, will say: ‘Go, and whoever you find in whose heart is a dinar’s weight of faith, bring him out.’ And God will forbid their forms to the Fire. So they will come to them, and some of them will have sunk into the Fire to their feet, and to the middle of their shins; and they will bring out those whom they recognize. Then they will return, and He will say: ‘Go, and whoever you find in whose heart is the weight of half a dinar, bring him out.’ So they will bring out those whom they recognize. Then they will return, and He will say: ‘Go, and whoever you find in whose heart is the weight of an atom of faith, bring him out.’ So they will bring out those whom they recognize.” Abu Sa'id said: “If you do not believe me, then recite the words of God, the Mighty and Majestic: {Indeed, God does not wrong [anyone] by the weight of an atom, and if it be a good deed, He multiplies it} [al-Nisa': 40].” “Then the prophets, the angels, and the believers will intercede, and the All-Compelling will say: ‘My intercession remains.’ Then He will take a handful from the Fire and bring out ⦗p. 49⦘ people who have been scorched, and they will be cast into a river at the mouths of Paradise, called al-Hayat [Life], and they will sprout on its two banks as the seed sprouts in the silt carried by the torrent — you have seen it beside the rock and beside the tree; what is toward the sun is greenish, and what is toward the shade is white. They will come out as though they were pearls, and seals will be placed on their necks, and they will enter Paradise. The people of Paradise will say: ‘These are the emancipated ones of the All-Merciful from the Fire; He admitted them to Paradise without any deed they had done and without any good they had put forward.’ It will be said to them: ‘Yours is what you have seen, and the like of it with it.’” ⦗p. 50⦘ Sa'id ibn Abi Hilal said: It has reached me that the bridge is thinner than a hair and sharper than a sword
