About the edition.

Edition. ʿĀdil b. Yūsuf al-ʿAzāzī and Aḥmad b. Farīd al-Mazīdī; Dār al-Waṭan, Riyadh; 1st ed., 1997. Two volumes.

Structure of the work. A musnad: the compiler's own headings name a Companion, and each heading is followed by that Companion's reports. The 281 headings and the narrations under them are kept in the order of the printed edition.

Numbering. Narrations keep the printed numbers 1 to 998. Number 258 is absent from the supplied translation and is not shown. Numbers 553, 754, 939 are not printed in their source records and are supplied from the sequence.

English translation. The English is the supplied translation, which names no translator. Its division into isnad and matn is the one the translation file records after its own review; narrations it flags as layered or as holding a second narration inside the story carry a source flag.

Arabic text. The Arabic of narrations 399 onward comes with the translation file. The Arabic of narrations 1 to 398 is taken from the Shamela text of the same edition, the source the translation names, matched record by record through the serial number. Printed numbers appear in the label instead of the text; page markers are carried over.

Isnad and matn. The Arabic is divided where its chain ends, by a fixed rule that does not alter the wording. A passage the rule cannot divide is shown whole and says so.

Transmitters. Each transmission path lists the names exactly as the isnad words them, read from the text between its transmission terms. English names are shown only where they line up one for one with the Arabic. No transmitter is identified beyond the wording of the source.
