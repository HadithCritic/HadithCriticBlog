English
Isnad

Isma'il Ibn 'Ulayya narrated to us, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Ishaq, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Mu'awiya, from Ibn Abi Dhubab, from Sahl b. Sa'd al-Sa'idi, who said:

Matn

«I never saw the Messenger of God ﷺ raising his hands high in supplication, on a pulpit or elsewhere; but I saw his hands level with his shoulders as he supplicated.»

Isnad

Sufyan b. 'Uyayna narrated to us, from al-Zuhri, who heard Sahl b. Sa'd al-Sa'idi say:

Matn

A man looked through a hole into the room of the Prophet ﷺ; he had with him a comb with which he was scratching his head. The Prophet ﷺ said: «If I knew you were looking, I would have stabbed your eyes with it. Asking permission has been made only because of sight.»

Musnad Ibn Abī Shayba · Vol. 1, p. 81
English
Isnad

Sufyan b. 'Uyayna narrated to us, from Abu Hazim, who said:

Matn

They came to Sahl b. Sa'd al-Sa'idi and said: From what is the pulpit of the Messenger of God ﷺ made? He said: No one of the people remains who knows it better than I. It is of tamarisk wood from al-Ghaba, and so-and-so, the client of so-and-so, made it for the Messenger of God ﷺ. The Messenger of God ﷺ used to lean on a palm trunk in the mosque and pray toward it when he preached. When he took the pulpit and went up on it, the trunk moaned. So the Messenger of God ﷺ came to it and found it – and in the hadith of Abu Hazim there is no “and found it” – that is, it grew still. Then he stood on the pulpit, faced the qibla and recited, then went down backward, raised his head and prostrated, then returned to the pulpit and did likewise.

العربية
Isnad

نا سُفْيَانُ بْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، قَالَ:

Matn

أَتَوْا سَهْلَ بْنِ سَعْدٍ السَّاعِدِيَّ، فَقَالُوا: مِنْ أَيِّ شَيْءٍ مِنْبَرُ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم؟ قَالَ: مَا بَقِيَ أَحَدٌ مِنَ النَّاسِ هُوَ أَعْلَمُ بِهِ مِنِّي، هُوَ مِنْ أَثْلِ الْغَابَةِ، وَعَمِلَهُ فُلَانٌ مَوْلَى فُلَانَةٍ، لِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، وَكَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم «يَسْتَنِدُ إِلَى جِذْعٍ فِي الْمَسْجِدِ يُصَلِّي إِلَيْهِ إِذَا خَطَبَ، فَلَمَّا اتَّخَذَ الْمِنْبَرَ صَعِدَ عَلَيْهِ حَنَّ الْجِذْعُ، فَأَتَاهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَوَجَدَهُ - وَلَيْسَ فِي حَدِيثِ أَبِي حَازِمٍ فَوَجَدَهُ - يَعْنِي سَكَنَ -، فَقَامَ عَلَى الْمِنْبَرِ فَاسْتَقْبَلَ فَقَرَأَ، ثُمَّ رَكَعَ الْقَهْقَرِيَّ فَرَفَعَ رَأْسَهُ فَسَجَدَ، ثُمَّ عَادَ إِلَى الْمِنْبَرِ ⦗ص: 82⦘ فَفَعَلَ مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ»

Waki' narrated to us, from Rabi'a b. 'Uthman; 'Imran b. Abi Anas narrated to us, from Sahl b. Sa'd, who said:

Matn

Two men disagreed in the time of the Prophet ﷺ about the mosque that was founded on piety. One of them said: It is the mosque of Medina. The other said: It is the mosque of Quba'. They came to the Prophet ﷺ and he said: «It is this mosque of mine.»

Matn

Isnad

'Umar: Ibn Abi Shayba narrated to us; Husayn b. 'Ali narrated to us, from Za'ida, from Abu Hazim, from Sahl b. Sa'd, who said:

Matn

A woman came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: O Messenger of God, I have given myself to you, so do with me what you wish. A young man said to him: O Messenger of God, if you have no need of her, marry her to me. He said: «Do you have anything to give her?» He said: I know of nothing. He said: «Go and look; perhaps you will find something, even an iron ring.» He went, and came back and said: I found nothing except this wrapper of mine. He said: “If you give her this wrapper of yours, nothing will remain on you. Do you recite anything of the Qur'an?” He said: Yes. He said: «Go; I have married you to her; teach her of the Qur'an.»

سَأَلْتُ سَهْلَ بْنَ سَعْدٍ، فَقُلْتُ: هَلْ أَكَلَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم النَّقِيَّ؟ فَقَالَ سَهْلٌ: «مَا رَأَى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِنْ حِينَ ابْتَعَثَهُ اللَّهُ حَتَّى قَبَضَهُ اللَّهُ» ، فَقُلْتُ: هَلْ كَانَ فِي عَهْدِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَنَاخِلُ؟ قَالَ: «مَا رَأَى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِنْخَلًا مِنْ حِينِ ابْتَعَثَهُ اللَّهُ حَتَّى قَبَضَهُ اللَّهُ» ، قُلْتُ: فَكَيْفَ كُنْتُمْ تَأْكُلُونَ شَعِيرًا غَيْرَ مَنْخُولٍ؟ قَالَ: «كُنَّا نَطْحَنُهُ فَنَنْفُخُهُ فَيَطِيرُ مَا طَارَ، وَمَا بَقِيَ ثَرَدْنَاهُ فَأَكَلْنَاهُ»

Isnad

Qutayba b. Sa'id narrated to us, from Ya'qub b. 'Abd al-Rahman, from Abu Hazim, from Sahl b. Sa'd al-Sa'idi, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

«A man works the deeds of the people of Paradise, as it appears to the people, while he is of the people of the Fire; and a man works the deeds of the people of the Fire, as it appears to the people, while he is of the people of Paradise.»

العربية

نا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ يَعْقُوبَ بْنَ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ السَّاعِدِيِّ، قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم:

English
Isnad

Qutayba b. Sa'id narrated to us; Ya'qub b. 'Abd al-Rahman narrated to us, from Abu Hazim, from Sahl b. Sa'd, who said:

Matn

«The Prophet's ﷺ front tooth (raba'iya) was broken on that day, and he was wounded in his face on that day by a missile that struck him, so that part of the helmet's mail entered his face, and the helmet was also broken on his head by a missile which one of the polytheists threw at him.»

العربية
Isnad

نا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، نا يَعْقُوبُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ،: قَالَ:

Matn

«كُسِرَتْ رَبَاعِيَةُ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَوْمَئِذٍ، وَجُرِحَ فِي وَجْهِهِ فِي ذَلِكَ الْيَوْمَ بِرَمْيَةٍ أَصَابَتْهُ، حَتَّى دَخَلَ بَعْضُ الْمِغْفَرِ فِي وَجْهِهِ، وَكُسِرَتِ الْبَيْضَةُ أَيْضًا عَلَى وَجْهِهِ بِرَأْسِهِ بِرَمْيَةٍ رَمَاهُ بِهَا بَعْضُ الْمُشْرِكِينَ»
