English
Isnad

'Abd al-Rahman b. Mahdi narrated to us, from Sufyan, from Habib b. Abi Thabit, from Mujahid, from Abu Ma'mar, from al-Miqdad b. 'Amr, who said:

Matn

'The Messenger of God ﷺ commanded us to throw dust into the faces of those who lavish praise.'

Musnad Ibn Abī Shayba · Vol. 1, p. 328
English
Isnad

Ghundar narrated to us, from Shu'ba, from Mansur, from Ibrahim, from Hammam b. al-Harith,

Matn

that a man began praising 'Uthman ؓ. Al-Miqdad knelt down—he was a large man—and began throwing pebbles into the man's face. 'Uthman asked him: 'What are you doing?' He replied: 'The Messenger of God ﷺ said: When you see those who lavish praise, throw dust in their faces.'

العربية
Isnad

نا غُنْدَرٌ، عَنْ شُعْبَةَ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ هَمَّامِ ⦗ص:

Matn

329⦘ بْنِ الْحَارِثِ، أَنَّ رَجُلًا جَعَلَ يَمْدَحُ عُثْمَانَ رضي الله عنه، فَعَمَدَ الْمِقْدَادُ فَجَثَى عَلَى رُكْبَتَيْهِ، قَالَ: - وَكَانَ رَجُلًا ضَخْمًا - فَجَعَلَ يَحْثُو فِي وَجْهِهِ الْحَصَاةَ، فَقَالَ لَهُ عُثْمَانُ: مَا شَأْنُكَ؟ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «إِذَا رَأَيْتُمُ الْمَدَّاحِينَ فَاحْثُوا فِي وُجُوهِهِمُ التُّرَابَ»

English
Isnad

Shababa b. Sawwar narrated to us, from Layth b. Sa'd, from Ibn Shihab, from 'Ata' b. Yazid al-Laythi, from 'Ubayd Allah b. 'Adi b. al-Khiyar, from al-Miqdad, who told him that he said:

Matn

'Messenger of God, suppose I encounter a man among the unbelievers who fights me and strikes one of my hands with his sword, cutting it off, then takes shelter from me behind a tree and says: I have submitted to God. May I kill him, Messenger of God, after he says that?' He ﷺ replied: 'Do not kill him.' I said: 'Messenger of God, he cut off my hand, then said it after cutting it off; may I kill him?' He said: 'Do not kill him. If you kill him, he will be in the position you were in before killing him, and you will be in the position he was in before saying the words he said.'

العربية
Isnad

نا شَبَابَةُ بْنُ سَوَّارٍ، عَنْ لَيْثِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ اللَّيْثِيِّ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَدِيِّ بْنِ الْخِيَارِ، عَنِ الْمِقْدَادِ، أَنَّهُ أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّهُ قَالَ:

Matn

يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ أَرَأَيْتَ إِنْ لَقِيتُ رَجُلًا مِنَ الْكُفَّارِ فَقَاتَلَنِي فَضَرَبَ إِحْدَى يَدَيَّ بِالسَّيْفِ فَقَطَعَنِي، ثُمَّ لَاذَ مِنِّي بِشَجَرَةٍ، فَقَالَ: أَسْلَمْتُ لِلَّهِ، أَفَأَقْتُلُهُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ بَعْدَ أَنْ قَالَهَا؟ قَالَ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لَا تَقْتُلْهُ» ، فَقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، إِنَّهُ قَطَعَ يَدِي، ثُمَّ قَالَ ذَلِكَ بَعْدَ ⦗ص: 330⦘ أَنْ قَطَعَهَا أَفَأَقْتُلُهُ؟ قَالَ: «لَا تَقْتُلْهُ، فَإِنْ قَتَلْتَهُ فَإِنَّهُ بِمَنْزِلَتِكَ قَبْلَ أَنْ تَقْتُلَهُ، وَأَنْتَ بِمَنْزِلَتِهِ قَبْلَ أَنْ يَقُولَ الْكَلِمَةَ الَّتِي قَالَهَا»

Musnad Ibn Abī Shayba · Vol. 1, p. 330
English
Isnad

Shababa b. Sawwar narrated to us; Sulayman b. al-Mughira narrated to us, from Thabit, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Abi Layla, from al-Miqdad, who said:

Matn

I came with two companions of mine. Exhaustion had taken away our hearing and sight. We offered ourselves for hospitality to the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ, but none of them accepted us. We came to the Prophet ﷺ, and he took us to his family. There were three goats there. The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Milk them and divide the milk among us.' We milked them and each person drank his share. He would come at night and give a greeting of peace that did not wake a sleeper but could be heard by someone awake. He would then go to the mosque and pray, and afterward come to his drink and drink it. One night Satan came to me after I had drunk my share and said: 'Muhammad visits the Ansar, who honor him and feed him. He has no need of this small drink.' I went to it and drank it. Once it had settled in my stomach and I knew there was no way to replace it, Satan filled me with regret, saying: 'Woe to you! What have you done? Have you drunk Muhammad's drink? He will come and not find it, and he will pray against you. You will perish and lose both this world and the Hereafter.' I had a small cloak: when I covered my feet with it, my head was exposed, and when I covered my head, my feet were exposed. Sleep would not come to me. My two companions, however, slept, for they had not done what I had done. The Prophet ﷺ came and gave the greeting as usual, went to the mosque and prayed, then came to his drink and uncovered it. Finding nothing there, he raised his head toward heaven. I thought: 'Now he will pray against me and I shall perish.' But he said: 'O God, feed the one who fed me, and give drink to the one who gave me drink.' I wrapped the cloak tightly around myself, took a knife, and went to the goats to slaughter the fattest for the Messenger of God ﷺ. But I found it with a full udder, and indeed all of them had full udders. I took a vessel belonging to Muhammad's family ﷺ, one they had not expected could be filled with milk. [I milked the goats.] He asked: 'Have you drunk your drink tonight?' I said: 'Messenger of God, drink.' He drank and passed it to me. When I knew the Prophet ﷺ had drunk his fill, and that I had obtained the benefit of his supplication, I laughed until I fell to the ground. The Prophet ﷺ said: 'One of your misdeeds, Miqdad?' I told him: 'Messenger of God, this and this happened to me, and I did such-and-such.' The Prophet ﷺ said: 'This is nothing but mercy from God. Why did you not inform me so that we could wake our two companions and they too could share in it?' I said: 'By Him who sent you with the truth, once I have received it and shared it with you, I do not mind who else among the people receives it.'

العربية
Isnad

نا شَبَابَةُ بْنُ سَوَّارٍ، نَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ الْمُغِيرَةِ، عَنْ ثَابِتٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ أَبِي لَيْلَى، عَنِ الْمِقْدَادِ، قَالَ:

Matn

أَقْبَلْتُ أَنَا وَصَاحِبَانِ لِي، وَقَدْ ذَهَبَتْ أَسْمَاعُنَا وَأَبْصَارُنَا مِنَ الْجَهْدِ، فَجَعَلْنَا نَعْرِضُ أَنْفُسَنَا عَلَى أَصْحَابِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَلَيْسَ أَحَدٌ مِنْهُمْ يَقْبَلُنَا، فَأَتَيْنَا النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَانْطَلَقَ بِنَا إِلَى أَهْلِهِ، فَإِذَا ثَلَاثَةُ أَعْنُزٍ، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «احْتَلِبُوا هَذَا اللَّبَنَ بَيْنَنَا» ، قَالَ: فَكُنَّا نَحْتَلِبُ فَشَرِبَ كُلُّ إِنْسَانٍ مِنَّا نَصِيبَهُ، قَالَ: فَيَجِيءُ مِنَ اللَّيْلِ فَيُسَلِّمُ تَسْلِيمًا لَا يُوقِظُ نَائِمًا، وَيُسْمِعُ الْيَقْظَانَ، قَالَ: ثُمَّ يَأْتِي الْمَسْجِدَ فَيُصَلِّي، ثُمَّ يَأْتِي شَرَابَهُ فَيَشْرَبُ، فَأَتَانِي الشَّيْطَانُ ذَاتَ لَيْلَةٍ، وَقَدْ شَرِبْتُ نَصِيبِي، فَقَالَ: مُحَمَّدٌ يَأْتِي الْأَنْصَارَ فَيُتْحِفُونَهُ، وَيُصِيبُ عِنْدَهُمْ، مَا بِهِ حَاجَةٌ إِلَى هَذِهِ الْجَرَعَةِ، فَأَتَيْتُهَا فَشَرِبْتُهَا، فَلَمَّا أَنْ وَغَلَتْ فِي بَطْنِي، وَعَلِمْتُ أَنَّهُ لَيْسَ إِلَيْهَا ⦗ص: 331⦘ سَبِيلٌ، قَالَ: نَدَّمَنِي الشَّيْطَانُ، فَقَالَ: وَيْحَكَ مَا صَنَعْتَ؟ أَشَرِبْتَ شَرَابَ مُحَمَّدٍ؟ فَيَجِيءُ فَلَا يَجِدُهُ فَيَدْعُو عَلَيْكَ فَتَهْلِكُ، فَتَذْهَبُ دُنْيَاكَ وَآخِرَتُكَ، وَعَلَيَّ شَمْلَةٌ، إِذَا وَضَعْتُهَا عَلَى قَدَمِي خَرَجَ رَأْسِي، وَإِذَا وَضَعْتُهَا عَلَى رَأْسِي خَرَجَ قَدَمَايَ، وَجَعَلَ لَا يَجِيئُنِي النَّوْمُ، وَأَمَّا صَاحِبَايَ، فَنَامَا وَلَمْ يَصْنَعَا مَا صَنَعْتُ، قَالَ: فَجَاءَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَسَلَّمَ كَمَا يُسَلِّمُ، ثُمَّ أَتَى الْمَسْجِدَ فَصَلَّى، ثُمَّ أَتَى شَرَابَهُ فَكَشَفَ عَنْهُ فَلَمْ يَجِدْ فِيهِ شَيْئًا، فَرَفَعَ رَأْسَهُ إِلَى السَّمَاءِ، فَقُلْتُ: الْآنَ يَدْعُو عَلَيَّ فَأَهْلَكُ، فَقَالَ: «اللَّهُمَّ أَطْعِمْ مَنْ أَطْعِمْنِي، وَأَسِقِ مَنْ سَقَانِي» ، قَالَ: فَعَمَدْتُ إِلَى الشَّمْلَةِ فَشَدَدْتُهَا عَلَيَّ وَأَخَذْتُ الشَّفْرَةَ فَانْطَلَقْتُ إِلَى الْأَعْنُزِ أَيُّهَا أَسْمَنُ فَأَذْبَحُهَا لِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَإِذَا هِيَ حَافِلَةٌ، وَإِذَا هُنَّ حُفَّلٌ كُلُّهُنَّ، فَعَمَدْتُ إِلَى إِنَاءٍ لِآلِ مُحَمَّدٍ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَا كَانُوا يَطْمَعُونَ أَنْ يَحْتَلِبُوا فِيهِ، قَالَ: أَشَرِبْتُمْ شَرَابَكُمُ اللَّيْلَةَ؟ قَالَ: قُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ اشْرَبْ فَشَرِبَ، ثُمَّ نَاوِلْنِي، فَلَمَّا عَرَفْتُ أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَدْ رُوِيَ، وَأَصَبْتُ دَعْوَتَهُ ضَحِكْتُ حَتَّى أُلْقِيتُ إِلَى الْأَرْضِ، قَالَ: فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «إِحْدَى سَوْآتِكَ يَا مِقْدَادُ» فَقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ كَانَ مِنْ أَمْرِي كَذَا وَكَذَا، وَفَعَلْتُ كَذَا، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «مَا هَذِهِ إِلَّا رَحْمَةٌ مِنَ اللَّهِ، أَفَلَا كُنْتَ آذَنْتَنِي، فَتُوقِظُ صَاحِبَيْنَا فَيُصِيبَانِ مِنْهَا» . قَالَ: فَقُلْتُ: وَالَّذِي بَعَثَكَ بِالْحَقِّ مَا أُبَالِي إِذَا أَصَبْتُهَا وَأَصَبْتُهَا مَعَكَ مَنْ أَصَابَهَا مِنَ النَّاسِ

Narration488/ 7
Musnad Ibn Abī Shayba · Vol. 1, p. 332
English
Isnad

Khalid b. Makhlad narrated to us; Musa b. Ya'qub al-Zam'i narrated to us, who said: My paternal aunt Qurayba bt. 'Abd Allah b. Wahb informed me, from her mother Karima bt. al-Miqdad b. 'Amr, from Duba'a bt. al-Zubayr—who was married to al-Miqdad b. 'Amr—who said:

Matn

In those days people would go to relieve themselves only once every two or three days, passing droppings like a camel. One day al-Miqdad went to relieve himself, came to Baqi' al-Gharqad, and entered a ruined building. While he was sitting there, a rat brought a dinar out of a hole. It kept bringing out dinars until it had brought out seventeen, then brought out the end of a red cloth. Al-Miqdad said: I rose and pulled it out, and found in it the value of eighteen [dinars]. I took it to the Messenger of God ﷺ and told him what had happened. He asked: 'Did your hand go into the hole?' I said: 'No, by Him who sent you with the truth.' He said: 'No charity is due on it. May God bless it for you.' Duba'a said: The last of it had not yet been spent before I saw bundles of silver in al-Miqdad's house.

العربية
Isnad

نا خَالِدُ بْنُ مَخْلَدٍ، نَا مُوسَى بْنُ يَعْقُوبَ الزَّمْعِيُّ، قَالَ:

Matn

أَخْبَرَتْنِي عَمَّتِي قُرَيْبَةُ بِنْتُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ وَهْبٍ، عَنْ أُمِّهَا كَرِيمَةَ بِنْتِ الْمِقْدَادِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو، عَنْ ضُبَاعَةَ بِنْتِ الزُّبَيْرِ، - وَكَانَتْ تَحْتَ الْمِقْدَادِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو - قَالَتْ: كَانَ النَّاسُ إِنَّمَا يَذْهَبُونَ لِحَاجَتِهِمْ فَرَطَ الْيَوْمَيْنِ وَالثَّلَاثَةِ فَيَبْعُرُونَ كَمَا تَبْعُرُ الْبَعِيرُ، فَلَمَّا كَانَ ذَاتَ يَوْمٍ خَرَجَ الْمِقْدَادُ لِحَاجَتِهِ حَتَّى أَتَى بَقِيعَ الْغَرْقَدِ، فَدَخَلَ خَرِبَةً لِحَاجَتِهِ، فَبَيْنَمَا هُوَ جَالِسٌ إِذْ أَخْرَجَ جُرَذٌ مِنْ حُجْرٍ دِينَارًا، فَلَمْ يَزَلْ يُخْرِجُ دِينَارًا حَتَّى أَخْرَجَ سَبْعَةَ عَشَرَ دِينَارًا ثُمَّ أَخْرَجَ طَرَفَ خِرْقَةٍ حَمْرَاءَ، قَالَ الْمِقْدَادُ: فَقُمْتُ فَأَخْرَجْتُهَا فَوَجَدْتُ فِيهَا قِيمَةَ ثَمَانِيَةَ عَشَرَ فَخَرَجْتُ بِهَا حَتَّى أَتَيْتُ بِهَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَأَخْبَرْتُهُ خَبَرَهَا، فَقَالَ: «هَلْ أَتْبَعْتَ يَدَكَ الْحُجْرَ؟» قُلْتُ: لَا وَالَّذِي بَعَثَكَ بِالْحَقِّ، قَالَ: «لَا صَدَقَةَ فِيهَا بَارَكَ اللَّهُ لَكَ فِيهَا» ، قَالَتْ ضُبَاعَةُ: فَمَا فَنِيَ آخِرُهَا حَتَّى رَأَيْتُ عَدَائِرَ الْوَرِقِ فِي بَيْتِ الْمِقْدَادِ

Musnad Ibn Abī Shayba · Vol. 1, p. 333
English
Isnad

Khalid b. Makhlad narrated to us, from Ibn Ya'qub al-Zam'i, who said: My paternal aunt Qurayba bt. 'Abd Allah b. Wahb b. Zam'a informed me, from her mother Karima bt. al-Miqdad, from Duba'a bt. al-Zubayr, from al-Miqdad b. al-Aswad, who said:

Matn

I said to the Prophet ﷺ: 'There is something I heard from you about which I am uncertain.' He said: 'When any of you is uncertain about a matter, let him ask me about it.' I said: 'Your statement about your wives: I hope that after me they will be among the truthful ones (al-siddiqin).' He asked: 'Who do you count as the truthful ones?' We said: 'Our children who die young.' He said: 'No; rather, the truthful ones are those who give charity (al-mutasaddiqun).'

Isnad

نا خَالِدُ بْنُ مَخْلَدٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ يَعْقُوبَ الزَّمْعِيِّ، قَالَ:

Matn

أَخْبَرَتْنِي عَمَّتِي قُرَيْبَةُ بِنْتُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ وَهْبِ بْنِ زَمْعَةَ، عَنْ أُمِّهَا كَرِيمَةَ بِنْتِ الْمِقْدَادِ، عَنْ ضُبَاعَةَ بِنْتِ الزُّبَيْرِ، عَنِ الْمِقْدَادِ بْنِ الْأَسْوَدِ، قَالَ: قُلْتُ لِلنَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم شَيْئًا سَمِعْتُهُ مِنْكَ شَكَكْتُ فِيهِ، قَالَ: «فَإِذَا شَكَّ أَحَدُكُمْ فِي الْأَمْرِ فَلْيَسْأَلْنِي عَنْهُ» ، قَالَ: قَوْلُكَ فِي أَزْوَاجِكَ: «إِنِّي لَأَرْجُو لَهُنَّ مِنْ بَعْدِي الصِّدِّيقِينَ» ، قَالَ: «وَمَنْ تَعُدُّونَ الصِّدِّيقِينَ؟» ، قَالَ: فَقُلْنَا: أَوْلَادَنَا الَّذِينَ يَهْلِكُونَ صِغَارًا، فَقَالَ: «لَا، وَلَكِنَّ الصِّدِّيقِينَ هُمُ الْمُتَصَدِّقُونَ»

Narration490/ 7
Musnad Ibn Abī Shayba · Vol. 1, p. 333
English
Isnad

'Ali b. Ishaq narrated to us, from 'Abd Allah b. al-Mubarak, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Yazid b. Jabir, who said: Sulaym b. 'Amir narrated to me, saying: Al-Miqdad, a Companion of the Prophet ﷺ, narrated to us: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say:

Matn

'On the Day of Resurrection, the sun will be brought close to the servants until it is a mile or two away.' He said: 'I do not know whether by mile he meant a measure of distance on the earth or the small rod used to apply kohl to the eye.' He said: 'The sun will melt them, and their sweat will be according to their deeds: for some it will reach their heels, for others their knees, for others their waists, and for others it will engulf them like a bridle.' I saw the Messenger of God ﷺ gesture toward his mouth, saying: 'It will engulf them like a bridle.'

العربية
Isnad

نا عَلِيُّ بْنُ إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مُبَارَكٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ بْنِ جَابِرٍ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنِي سُلَيْمُ بْنُ عَامِرٍ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا الْمِقْدَادُ صَاحِبُ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم

Matn

يَقُولُ ⦗ص: 334⦘ : «إِذَا كَانَ يَوْمُ الْقِيَامَةِ أُدْنِيَتِ الشَّمْسُ مِنَ الْعِبَادِ حَتَّى يَكُونَ قِيدَ مِيلٍ، أَوِ اثْنَيْنِ» ، قَالَ: لَا أَدْرِي الْمِيلَيْنِ، يَعْنِي مَسَافَةَ الْأَرْضِ أَوِ الْمِيلَ الَّذِي يُكْحَلُ بِهِ الْعَيْنُ، قَالَ: " فَتَصْهَرُهُمُ الشَّمْسُ، قَالَ: فَيَكُونُ فِي الْعَرَقِ قَدْرَ أَعْمَالِهِمْ، فَمِنْهُمْ مَنْ يَأْخُذُهُ إِلَى عَقِبِهِ، وَمِنْهُمْ مَنْ يَأْخُذُهُ إِلَى رُكْبَتَيْهِ، وَمِنْهُمْ مَنْ يَأْخُذُهُ إِلَى حِقْوَيْهِ، وَمِنْهُمْ مَنْ يُلْجِمُهُ إِلْجَامًا "، فَرَأَيْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يُشِيرُ بِيَدِهِ إِلَى فِيهِ، يَقُولُ: «يُلْجِمُهُ إِلْجَامًا»
