English
Isnad

'Umar narrated to us; Ibn Abi Shayba narrated to us; Abu Mu'awiya and Waki' narrated to us, from al-A'mash, from Ibrahim, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Yazid, who said:

Matn

They said to Salman: 'Your Prophet has taught you everything, even how to relieve yourselves.' He replied: 'Yes. He forbade us to face the qibla while defecating or urinating, to clean ourselves with the right hand, to clean ourselves with fewer than three stones, or to clean ourselves with dung or a bone.'

العربية
Isnad

عُمَرُ، نا ابْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، نا أَبُو مُعَاوِيَةَ، وَوَكِيعٌ، عَنِ الْأَعْمَشِ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ، قَالَ:

Matn

قَالُوا لِسَلْمَانَ: قَدْ عَلَّمَكُمْ نَبِيُّكُمْ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ حَتَّى الْخِرَاءَةَ، فَقَالَ: أَجَلْ، لَقَدْ «نَهَانَا أَنْ نَسْتَقْبِلَ الْقِبْلَةَ لِغَائِطٍ، أَوْ بَوْلٍ، أَوْ أَنْ نَسْتَنْجِيَ بِالْيَمِينِ، أَوْ نَسْتَنْجِيَ بِأَقَلَّ مِنْ ثَلَاثَةِ أَحْجَارٍ، أَوْ أَنْ نَسْتَنْجِيَ بِرَجِيعٍ أَوْ بِعَظْمٍ»

Isnad

Abu Usama narrated to us, from Mis'ar, from 'Umar b. Qays, from 'Amr b. Abi Qumra al-Kindi, from Salman, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

'I am a descendant of Adam. Whichever believing servant I have cursed or insulted without his deserving it, make it a blessing upon him.'

Isnad

«مِنْ وَلَدِ آدَمَ أَنَا، فَأَيُّمَا عَبْدٌ مُؤْمِنٌ لَعَنْتُهُ لَعْنَةً أَوْ سَبَبْتُهُ سَبَّةً فِي غَيْرِ كُنْهِهِ، فَاجْعَلْهَا عَلَيْهِ صَلَاةً»

Musnad Ibn Abī Shayba · Vol. 1, p. 301
English
Isnad

Ibn Fudayl narrated to us, from 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib, from Abu al-Bakhtari, who said:

Matn

When Salman went to fight the polytheists among the Persians, he said: 'Hold back and let me invite them as I used to hear the Messenger of God ﷺ invite them.' He came to them and said: 'I am a man from among you, and you can see my standing among these people. I invite you to Islam. If you become Muslims, you will have the same rights as us and bear the same obligations as us. If you refuse, pay the jizya with submission and while humbled. If you refuse, we shall fight you.' They said: 'We will not accept Islam, nor will we pay the jizya, but we will fight you.' He invited them in this way for three days, but they refused. Then he told the people: 'Advance against them.'

العربية
Isnad

نا ابْنُ فُضَيْلٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ السَّائِبِ، عَنْ أَبِي الْبَخْتَرِيِّ، قَالَ:

Matn

لَمَّا غَزَا سَلْمَانُ الْمُشْرِكِينَ مِنْ أَهْلِ فَارِسَ، فَقَالَ: كُفُّوا عَنِّي حَتَّى أَدْعُوَهُمْ كَمَا كُنْتُ أَسْمَعُ مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَدْعُوهُمْ، فَأَتَاهُمْ فَقَالَ: «إِنِّي رَجُلٌ مِنْكُمْ وَقَدْ تَرَوْنَ مَنْزِلَتِي مِنْ هَؤُلَاءِ الْقَوْمِ، وَأَنَا أَدْعُوكُمْ إِلَى الْإِسْلَامِ، فَإِنْ أَسْلَمْتُمْ فَلَكُمْ مِثْلُ الَّذِي لَنَا، وَعَلَيْكُمْ مِثْلَ الَّذِي عَلَيْنَا، وَإِنْ أَبَيْتُمْ فَأَعْطُوا الْجِزْيَةَ عَنْ يَدٍ وَأَنْتُمْ صَاغِرُونَ، فَإِنْ أَبَيْتُمْ قَاتَلْنَاكُمْ» ، قَالُوا: أَمَّا الْإِسْلَامُ فَلَا نُسْلِمُ، وَأَمَّا الْجِزْيَةُ فَلَا نُعْطِيهَا، وَأَمَّا الْقِتَالُ فَإِنَّا نُقَاتِلُكُمْ، فَدَعَاهُمْ كَذَلِكَ ثَلَاثَةَ أَيَّامٍ فَأَبَوْا عَلَيْهِ، فَقَالَ لِلنَّاسِ: اذْهَبُوا عَلَيْهِمْ

Isnad

Abu Usama narrated to us, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Yazid b. Jabir, who said: I heard al-Qasim b. Mukhaymira mention

Matn

that some people put Salman forward to lead them in prayer, but he refused, and they pushed him forward. After he had led them he asked: 'Are all of you content?' They said: 'Yes.' He said: 'Praise be to God. I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: Three people will have their prayers not accepted: a woman who leaves her home without his permission, a runaway slave, and a man who leads people in prayer when they dislike him.'

Matn

قَالَ ⦗ص: 302⦘ : سَمِعْتُ الْقَاسِمَ بْنَ مُخَيْمِرَةَ، يَذْكُرُ أَنَّ سَلْمَانَ، قَدَّمَهُ قَوْمٌ يُصَلِّي بِهِمْ فَأَبَى فَدَفَعُوهُ، فَلَمَّا صَلَّى بِهِمْ قَالَ: أَكُلُّكُمْ رَاضٍ، قَالُوا: نَعَمْ، قَالَ: الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ، إِنِّي سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: " ثَلَاثَةٌ لَا تُقْبَلُ صَلَاتُهُمُ: الْمَرْأَةُ تَخْرُجُ مِنْ بَيْتِهَا بِغَيْرِ إِذْنِهِ، وَالْعَبْدُ الْآبِقُ، وَالرَّجُلُ يَؤُمُّ النَّاسَ وَهُمْ لَهُ كَارِهُونَ "

'Ubayd Allah b. Musa narrated to us, from Isra'il, from Abu Ishaq, from Abu Qurra al-Kindi, from Salman, who said:

English
Isnad

Zayd b. al-Hubab narrated to us, from Musa b. 'Ubayda, who said: Muhammad b. Abi Mansur informed me, from Shurahbil al-Simt b. 'Abd Allah, from Salman,

Matn

who was in a detachment of Muslims. They suffered hardship and distress, so Salman said to the commander: 'Shall I tell you something I heard from the Messenger of God ﷺ that may help you with this detachment? I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: Whoever stands guard for a day and a night in the path of God, it is equal to fasting and praying for a month, without breaking the fast or leaving his post except for a need. Whoever dies while standing guard in the path of God will have his reward continue until God, Mighty and Majestic, judges between the people of Paradise and the people of the Fire.'

العربية
Isnad

نا زَيْدُ بْنُ الْحُبَابِ، عَنْ مُوسَى بْنِ عُبَيْدَةَ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ أَبِي مَنْصُورٍ، عَنْ شُرَحْبِيلَ السِّمْطِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنْ سَلْمَانَ، قَالَ:

Matn

كَانَ فِي جُنْدٍ مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ، فَأَصَابَهُمْ حَصْرٌ، وَضَرٌّ، فَقَالُ سَلْمَانُ لِأَمِيرِ الْجُنْدِ: أَلَا أُخْبِرُكَ بِمَا سَمِعْتُ مِنَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَكُونُ عَوْنًا لَكَ عَلَى هَذَا الْجُنْدِ سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: «مَنْ رَابَطَ يَوْمًا وَلَيْلَةً فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ، كَانَ عَدْلُ صِيَامِ شَهْرٍ وَصَلَاتِهِ لَا يُفْطِرُ، وَلَا يَنْصَرِفُ إِلَّا لِحَاجَةٍ، وَمَنْ مَاتَ مُرَابِطًا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ، جَرَى لَهُ أَجْرُهُ حَتَّى يَقْضِيَ اللَّهُ عز وجل بَيْنَ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ، وَبَيْنَ أَهْلِ النَّارِ»

Isnad

Qabisa b. 'Uqba narrated to us, from Hammad b. Salama, from 'Ali b. Zayd, from Abu 'Uthman, who said:

Matn

I was with Salman. He took a branch from a dry tree and said: 'I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: Whoever performs ablution and performs it well, his sins fall away as leaves fall from [a tree].' He then recited: {Establish prayer at the two ends of the day and in the early hours of the night} [Hud 11:114].

العربية
Isnad

نا قَبِيصَةُ بنُ عُقْبَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادِ بْنِ سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ عَلِيِّ بْنِ زَيْدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي عُثْمَانَ، قَالَ:

Matn

كُنْتُ مَعَ سَلْمَانَ فَأَخَذَ غُصْنًا مِنْ شَجَرَةٍ يَابِسَةٍ ⦗ص: 304⦘ ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: «مَنْ تَوَضَّأَ فَأَحْسَنَ الْوُضُوءَ، تَحَاتَّتْ خَطَايَاهُ كَمَا تَحَاتُّ مِنَ الْوَرِقِ» ، ثُمَّ تَلَا {وَأَقِمِ الصَّلَاةَ طَرَفَيِ النَّهَارِ وَزُلَفًا مِنَ اللَّيْلِ} [هود: 114]

'Ubayd Allah b. Musa narrated to us; Isra'il informed us, from Abu Ishaq, from Abu Qurra al-Kindi, from Salman, who said:

Matn

I was among the sons of the Persian cavalrymen (asawira) and attended a school. Two boys were with me. Whenever they returned from their teacher, they would visit a priest, so I went in with them. He said: 'Did I not forbid you to bring anyone with you?' I kept visiting him until he came to love me more than the other two. He said to me: 'When your family asks who kept you, say: My teacher; and when your teacher asks who kept you, say: My family.' Then he intended to move away. I said: 'I shall move with you,' and I went with him. We settled in a village, where a woman used to come to him. When his final hour came, he said: 'Salman, dig beside my head.' I dug and brought out a jar of dirhams. He said: 'Pour them on my chest.' I poured them on his chest, and he kept saying: 'Woe to me for hoarding them.' He died. I considered taking the dirhams, then remembered [his words] and left them. I notified the priests and monks, who attended. I told them he had left wealth. Young men of the village stood and said: 'This is our father's money,' and took it. I asked the monks to tell me of a learned man whom I could follow. They said: 'We know of no man on earth more learned than a man in Homs.' I went to him, met him, and told him the story. He asked: 'Have you come only in pursuit of knowledge?' I said: 'I have come only in pursuit of knowledge.' He said: 'I know of no man on earth more learned today than a man who comes to Jerusalem once a year. If you depart now, you will find his donkey.' I went and found his donkey at the gate of Jerusalem. I sat there, but he went away, and I did not see him until a year later. When he returned I said: 'Servant of God, what have you done to me?' He said: 'Are you still here?' I said: 'Yes.' He said: 'By God, I know of no man more learned today than a man who has set out for the land of Tayma'. If you leave now, you will meet him. He has three signs: he eats gifts but does not eat charity, and near the cartilage of his right shoulder is the seal of prophethood, like a pigeon's egg and the color of his skin.' I set out, going up and down across the land, until I passed some Bedouins who enslaved me and sold me, until a woman in Medina bought me. I heard them mentioning the Prophet ﷺ, though it was difficult [for me to go to him]. I said to her: 'Give me a day.' She agreed. I gathered firewood, sold it, prepared food, and brought the small amount to the Prophet ﷺ. I placed it before him. He asked: 'What is this?' I said: 'Charity.' He told his Companions to eat, but he did not eat. I said to myself: 'This is one of his signs.' After some time I asked my mistress for another day. She agreed. I collected and sold firewood for more than before and prepared food, bringing it to him. He asked: 'What is this?' I said: 'A gift.' He put his hand to it and said to his Companions: 'Partake, in the name of God.' I stood behind him. He lowered his cloak, and there was the seal of prophethood. I said: 'I bear witness that you are the Prophet of God.' He asked: 'Why is that?' I told him about the man, and then asked: 'Messenger of God, will he enter Paradise? For he told me that you were God's Prophet.' He replied: 'None shall enter Paradise except a Muslim soul.'

سَنَةٍ، إِنِ انْطَلَقْتَ الْآنَ وَجَدْتَ حِمَارَهُ، قَالَ: فَانْطَلَقْتُ فَإِذَا أَنَا بِحِمَارِهِ عَلَى بَابِ بَيْتِ الْمَقْدِسِ، فَجَلَسْتُ عِنْدَهُ وَانْطَلَقَ، فَلَمْ أَرَهُ حَتَّى الْحَوْلِ، فَجَاءَ، فَقُلْتُ لَهُ: يَا عَبْدَ اللَّهِ، مَا صَنَعْتَ بِي؟ قَالَ: وَإِنَّكَ لَهَاهُنَا؟ قُلْتُ: نَعَمْ. قَالَ: فَإِنِّي وَاللَّهِ مَا أَعْلَمُ الْيَوْمَ رَجُلًا أَعْلَمَ مِنْ ⦗ص: 313⦘ رَجُلٍ خَرَجَ بِأَرْضِ تَيْمَاءَ، وَإِنْ تَنْطَلِقِ الْآنَ تُوَافِقْهُ، وَفِيهِ ثَلَاثُ آيَاتٍ: يَأْكُلُ الْهَدِيَّةَ، وَلَا يَأْكُلُ الصَّدَقَةَ، وَعِنْدَ غُضْرُوفٍ فِي كَتِفِهِ الْيُمْنَى خَاتَمُ النُّبُوَّةِ، مِثْلُ بَيْضَةِ الْحَمَامَةِ، لَوْنُهَا لَوْنُ جِلْدِهِ، قَالَ: فَانْطَلَقْتُ تَرْفَعُنِي أَرْضٌ، وَتُخْفِضُنِي أُخْرَى، حَتَّى مَرَرْتُ بِقَوْمٍ مِنَ الْأَعْرَابِ فَاسْتَعْبَدُونِي، فَبَاعُونِي حَتَّى اشْتَرَتْنِي امْرَأَةٌ بِالْمَدِينَةِ، فَسَمِعْتُهُمْ يَذْكُرُونَ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَكَانَ عَزِيزًا، فَقُلْتُ لَهَا: هَبِي لِي يَوْمًا، قَالَتْ: نَعَمْ، فَانْطَلَقْتُ فَاحْتَطَبْتُ حَطَبًا فَبِعْتُهُ، وَصَنَعْتُ طَعَامًا، فَأَتَيْتُ بِهِ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَكَانَ يَسِيرًا، فَوَضَعْتُهُ بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ، فَقَالَ: «مَا هَذَا؟» قُلْتُ: صَدَقَةٌ، قَالَ: فَقَالَ لِأَصْحَابِهِ: كُلُوا، وَلَمْ يَأْكُلْ، قَالَ: قُلْتُ هَذَا مِنْ عَلَامَتِهِ. ثُمَّ مَكَثْتُ مَا شَاءَ اللَّهُ أَنْ أَمْكُثَ، ثُمَّ قُلْتُ لِمَوْلَاتِي: هَبِي لِي يَوْمًا، قَالَتْ: نَعَمْ، فَانْطَلَقْتُ فَاحْتَطَبْتُ حَطَبًا فَبِعْتُهُ بِأَكْثَرَ مِنْ ذَلِكَ، وَصَنَعْتُ بِهِ طَعَامًا، فَأَتَيْتُ بِهِ بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ، فَقَالَ: «مَا هَذَا؟» قُلْتُ: هَدِيَّةٌ، فَوَضَعَ يَدَهُ وَقَالَ لِأَصْحَابِهِ: «خُذُوا بِاسْمِ اللَّهِ» وَقُمْتُ خلفَهُ، فَوَضَعَ رِدَاءَهُ، فَإِذَا خَاتَمُ النُّبُوَّةِ، فَقُلْتُ: أَشْهَدُ أَنَّكَ نَبِيُّ اللَّهِ، قَالَ: «مَا ذَاكَ؟» فَحَدَّثْتُهُ عَنِ الرَّجُلِ، ثُمَّ قُلْتُ: أَيَدْخُلُ الْجَنَّةَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، فَإِنَّهُ حَدَّثَنِي أَنَّكَ نَبِيُّ اللَّهِ؟ قَالَ: «لَنْ يَدْخُلَ الْجَنَّةَ إِلَّا نَفْسٌ مَسْلَمَةٌ»

Narration469/ 21
Musnad Ibn Abī Shayba · Vol. 1, p. 313
English
Isnad

'Affan narrated to us; Hammad b. Salama narrated to us; 'Ali b. Zayd informed us, from Abu 'Uthman, from Salman, who said:

Matn

I made a manumission agreement with my owners to plant five hundred young palm trees for them; if they took root, I would be free. I mentioned it to the Prophet ﷺ, who said: 'When you wish to plant them, let me know.' I informed him, and the Messenger of God ﷺ planted them with his own hand, except one that I planted myself. All of them took root except the one I planted.

العربية
Isnad

نا عَفَّانُ، نَا حَمَّادُ بْنُ سَلَمَةَ، أَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ زَيْدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي ⦗ص: 314⦘ عُثْمَانَ، عَنْ سَلْمَانَ، قَالَ:

Matn

كَاتَبْتُ أَهْلِي عَلَى أَنْ أَغْرِسَ لَهُمْ خَمْسَمِائَةِ فَسِيلَةٍ، فَإِذَا عَلَقَتْ فَأَنَا حُرٌّ، قَالَ: فَذَكَرْتُ ذَلِكَ لِلنَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ: «إِذَا أَرَدْتَ أَنْ تَغْرِسَ فَآذِنِّي» ، فَآذَنْتُهُ فَغَرَسَهَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِيَدِهِ إِلَّا وَاحِدَةً، غَرَسْتُهَا بِيَدِي، فَعَلَقْنَ جَمِيعًا إِلَّا الْوَاحِدَةُ الَّتِي غَرَسْتُهَا

English
Isnad

'Affan narrated to us; Mu'tamir narrated to us, who said: I heard my father say: Abu 'Uthman narrated to us, from Salman,

Matn

that the Messenger of God ﷺ mentioned that God, Mighty and Majestic, 'created one hundred mercies; from them comes one mercy by which creation shows mercy to one another—or words to that effect—and ninety-nine [are reserved] for the Day of Resurrection.'

Matn

أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ذَكَرَ أَنَّ اللَّهَ جَلَّ وَعَزَّ " خَلَقَ مِائَةَ رَحْمَةٍ، مِنْهَا رَحْمَةٌ يَتَرَاحَمُ بِهَا الْخَلْقُ - أَوْ كَمَا قَالَ: - وَتِسْعٌ وَتَسْعَوْنَ لِيَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ "
