Narration1/ 13
Musnad Ibn Abī Shayba · Vol. 1, p. 27
English
Isnad

'Umar: Ibn Abi Shayba narrated to us; Waki' narrated to us, from Isra'il, from Jabir b. Samura, from Abu Ayyub ؓ, who said:

Matn

When the Prophet ﷺ was brought food and had eaten some of it, he would send it to Abu Ayyub. Food was brought and he did not eat any of it, and he said to the woman, “Do not hurry.” So I came to the Prophet ﷺ and mentioned that to him, and he said: «It contains this herb, and I dislike its smell.» Abu Ayyub ؓ said: «And I dislike what the Messenger of God ﷺ disliked.»

العربية
Isnad

عُمَرُ، نا ابْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، نا وَكِيعٌ، عَنْ إِسْرَائِيلَ، عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ سَمُرَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي أَيُّوبَ رضي الله عنه، قَالَ:

Matn

كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِذَا أُتِيَ بِطَعَامٍ فَأَصَابَ مِنْهُ شَيْئًا بَعَثَ بِهِ إِلَى أَبِي أَيُّوبَ، فَأُتِيَ بِطَعَامٍ فَلَمْ يُصِبْ مِنْهُ شَيْئًا، فَقَالَ لِلْمَرْأَةِ لَا تَعْجَلِي، فَأَتَيْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَذَكَرْتُ ذَلِكَ لَهُ، فَقَالَ: «إِنَّ فِيهِ هَذِهِ الْبَقْلَةَ وَإِنِّي أَكْرَهُ رِيحَهَا» . فَقَالَ أَبُو أَيُّوبَ رضي الله عنه: «وَأَنَا أَكْرَهُ مَا كَرِهَهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم»

Narration2/ 13
Musnad Ibn Abī Shayba · Vol. 1, p. 27
English
Isnad

'Umar: Ibn Abi Shayba narrated to us; Yazid b. Harun narrated to us, from Dawud b. Abi Hind, from al-Sha'bi, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Abi Layla, from Abu Ayyub, from the Messenger of God ﷺ, who said:

Matn

“Whoever says, ‘There is no god but God alone, without partner; His is the dominion and His is the praise; in His hand is the good, and He is powerful over all things,’ ten times, it will be for him like the equivalent of freeing ten slaves, or one slave.”

العربية
Isnad

عُمَرُ، نا ابْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، نا يَزِيدُ بْنُ هَارُونَ، عَنْ دَاوُدَ بْنِ أَبِي هِنْدَ، عَنِ الشَّعْبِيِّ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ أَبِي لَيْلَى، عَنْ أَبِي أَيُّوبَ، عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ:

Matn

" مَنْ قَالَ: لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ، وَلَهُ ⦗ص: 28⦘ الْحَمْدُ، بِيَدِهِ الْخَيْرُ، وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ عَشْرَ مَرَّاتٍ، كُنَّ لَهُ كَعِدْلِ عَشْرِ رِقَابٍ أَوْ رَقَبَةٍ "

English
Isnad

'Umar: Ibn Abi Shayba narrated to us; Yazid b. Harun narrated to us, from Abu Malik al-Ashja'i, from Musa b. Talha, from Abu Ayyub, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

«Muzayna, Juhayna, Ghifar, Aslam, Ashja', and whoever is of Banu Ka'b are allies to the exclusion of other people, and God and His Messenger are their ally.»

العربية
Isnad

عُمَرُ، نا ابْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، نا يَزِيدُ بْنُ هَارُونَ، عَنْ أَبِي مَالِكٍ الْأَشْجَعِيِّ، عَنْ مُوسَى بْنِ طَلْحَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي أَيُّوبَ، قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم:

Matn

«مُزَيْنَةُ، وَجُهَيْنَةُ، وَغِفَارٌ، وَأَسْلَمُ، وَأَشْجَعُ، وَمَنْ كَانَ مِنْ بَنِي كَعْبٍ مَوَالٍ دُونَ النَّاسِ، وَاللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُ مَوْلَاهُمْ»

English
Isnad

'Umar: Ibn Abi Shayba narrated to us; al-Muqri' b. 'Abd al-Rahman narrated to us, from Sa'id b. Abi Ayyub, who said: Shurahbil b. Sharik al-Ma'afiri narrated to us, from Abu 'Abd al-Rahman al-Hubuli, who said: I heard Abu Ayyub say: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say:

Matn

«A morning journey in the path of God, or an evening journey, is better than all that the sun rises and sets upon.»

العربية
Isnad

عُمَرُ، نا ابْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، نا الْمُقْرِئُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ سَعِيدٍ بْنِ أَبِي أَيُّوبَ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا شُرَحْبِيلُ بْنُ شَرِيكٍ الْمَعَافِرِيُّ، عَنْ أَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ الْحُبُلِيِّ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ أَبَا أَيُّوبَ، يَقُولُ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم

Matn

يَقُولُ ⦗ص: 29⦘ : «غَدْوَةٌ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ، أَوْ رَوْحَةٌ، خَيْرٌ مِمَّا طَلَعَتْ عَلَيْهِ الشَّمْسُ وَغَرَبَتْ»

Narration5/ 13
Musnad Ibn Abī Shayba · Vol. 1, p. 29
English
Isnad

'Umar: Ibn Abi Shayba narrated to us; 'Abd al-Rahim b. Sulayman narrated to us, from Muhammad b. Ishaq, from Bukayr b. 'Abd Allah b. al-Ashajj, from 'Ubayd b. Ta'la, who said:

Matn

We went on a campaign with 'Abd al-Rahman Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, a Companion of the Messenger of God ﷺ, while 'Abd al-Rahman b. Khalid was in command of the people. He ordered them to shoot arrows (at captives) until they were killed, bound. Abu Ayyub al-Ansari said: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ «forbid the binding of an animal (for shooting), and I would not like to have bound even a chicken.»

العربية
Isnad

عُمَرُ، نا ابْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، نا عَبْدُ الرَّحِيمِ بْنُ سُلَيْمَانَ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ بُكَيْرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ الْأَشَجِّ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ بْنِ تَعْلَى، قَالَ:

Matn

غَزَوْنَا مَعَ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ أَبِي أَيُّوبَ الْأَنْصَارِيِّ صَاحِبِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، وَعَلَى النَّاسِ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ خَالِدٍ، فَأَمَرَهُمْ بِالنَّبْلِ حَتَّى قُتِلُوا صَبْرًا، فَقَالَ أَبُو أَيُّوبَ الْأَنْصَارِيُّ: لَقَدْ سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «يَنْهَى عَنْ صَبْرِ الدَّابَّةِ، وَمَا أُحِبُّ أَنِّي صَبَّرْتُ دَجَاجَةً»

'Umar: Ibn Abi Shayba narrated to us; al-Husayn b. 'Ali al-Ju'fi narrated to us, from Za'ida, from Mansur, from Hilal b. Yasaf, from Rabi' b. Khuthaym, from 'Amr b. Maymun, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Abi Layla, from a woman of the Ansar, from Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

English
Isnad

Yunus b. Muhammad narrated to us; Layth b. Sa'd narrated to us, from Yazid b. Abi Habib, from Abu al-Khayr, from Abu Ruhm al-Sama'i, that Abu Ayyub narrated to him:

Matn

That the Prophet of God ﷺ stayed in his lower house while I was in the upper room. Water was spilled in the upper room, so I and Umm Ayyub stood with a velvet cloth, mopping up the water, fearing that the water would reach the Messenger of God ﷺ. I went down to the Prophet ﷺ, anxious, and said: O Messenger of God, it is not right that we should be above you; move to the upper room. The Prophet ﷺ ordered his belongings (moved), and his belongings were few. I said: O Messenger of God, you used to send us food, and I would look at it, and when I saw the trace of your fingers I would put my hand in it, until this food which you sent to me; I looked at it and did not see the trace of your fingers. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: «Yes; it contains onion, and I disliked to eat it because of the angel who comes to me; as for you, eat it.»

Matn

He said: “The one who cleans between in the ablution, that you clean between your fingers and your teeth; and the one who cleans (his teeth) after food.
