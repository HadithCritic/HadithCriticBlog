02 / Gentleness toward a difficult visitor

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

Abu Yahya narrated to us: Sufyan b. 'Uyayna narrated to us, from Ibn al-Munkadir, that he heard 'Urwa b. al-Zubayr say: 'A'isha narrated to us that

Reported text · Matn

a man asked permission to enter upon the Prophet ﷺ, and he said: “Let him in. What an evil man of the clan — or: what an evil son of the clan.” When he entered upon him, he spoke gently to him. 'A'isha said: “O Messenger of God, you said to him what you said, and when he entered, you spoke gently to him.” He said: “O 'A'isha, the worst of people in rank on the Day of Resurrection is the one whom people leave, or abandon, to guard against his obscenity.”

العربيةالنص الأصلي
الإسناد

ثَنَا أَبُو يَحْيَى: ثَنَا سُفَيْانُ بْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ الْمُنْكَدِرِ، سَمِعَ عُرْوَةَ بْنَ الزُّبَيْرِ، يَقُولُ حَدَّثَتْنَا عَائِشَةُ أَنَّ

المتن

رَجُلًا، اسْتَأْذَنَ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ ﷺ فَقَالَ: " ائْذَنُوا لَهُ فَبِئْسَ رَجُلُ الْعَشِيرِةِ أَوْ بِئْسَ ابْنُ الْعَشِيرَةِ، فَلَمَّا دَخَلَ عَلَيْهِ أَلَانَ لَهُ الْقَوْلَ، فَقَالَتْ عَائِشَةُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، قُلْتَ لَهُ الَّذِي قُلْتَ فَلَمَّا دَخَلَ أَلَنْتَ لَهُ الْقَوْلَ قَالَ: " يَا عَائِشَةُ إِنَّ " شَرَّ النَّاسِ مَنْزِلَةً يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ، مَنْ وَدَعَهُ أَوْ تَرَكَهُ النَّاسُ اتِّقَاءَ فُحْشِهِ "

06 / Following the imam in prayer

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

Sufyan narrated to us, from al-Zuhri, from Anas b. Malik, who said:

Reported text · Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ fell from a horse and his right side was scraped. We entered upon him to visit him, and the time of prayer came, so he prayed sitting, and we prayed sitting. When he had completed the prayer, he said: “The imam is appointed only to be followed. So when he says the takbir, say the takbir; when he bows, bow; when he rises, rise; when he says ‘God hears whoever praises Him,’ say ‘Our Lord, and to You belongs praise’; when he prostrates, prostrate; and when he prays sitting, pray sitting, all of you.”

الإسناد

حَدَّثَنَا سُفَيْانُ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ،

المتن

قَالَ سَقَطَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ مِنْ فَرَسٍ فَجُحِشَ شِقُّهُ الْأَيْمَنُ فَدَخَلْنَا عَلَيْهِ نَعُودُهُ فَحَضَرَتِ الصَّلَاةُ فَصَلَّى قَاعِدًا فَصَلَّيْنَا قُعُودًا ، فَلَمَّا قَضَى الصَّلَاةَ، قَالَ: " إِنَّمَا جُعِلَ الْإِمَامُ لِيُؤْتَمَّ بِهِ، فَإِذَا كَبَّرَ فَكَبِّرُوا، وَإِذَا رَكَعَ فَارْكَعُوا، وَإِذَا رَفَعَ فَارْفَعُوا، وَإِذَا قَالَ سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ، فَقُولُوا رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ، وَإِذَا سَجَدَ فَاسْجُدُوا، وَإِذَا صَلَّى قَاعِدًا فَصَلُّوا قُعُودًا أَجْمَعِينَ "

07 / ʿUmar on gifts to children

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

Sufyan b. 'Uyayna narrated to us, from al-Zuhri, from 'Urwa b. al-Zubayr, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Abd al-Qari, that

Reported text · Matn

'Umar b. al-Khattab ﵁ said: “What is the matter with people who give their children a gift (nuhla), and when one of them dies, he says, ‘My property is in my hands,’ and when he himself dies, he says, ‘I had given it to my child’? There is no gift except a gift that the child takes into possession, apart from the father; and if he dies, he inherits from him.”

الإسناد

المتن

Source & transmission

A fascicle transmitted through later readers.

The supplied collection is titled Ḥadīth Fascicle of Sufyān ibn ʿUyayna (جزء حديث سفيان بن عيينة). It consists of fifty numbered reports, with a long recensional opening before no. 1. The opening states that the material was read in the presence of Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī on 19 Shawwāl 850 AH. That statement documents the surviving line of transmission in this witness; it does not prove that the present fascicle is an autograph of Sufyān or that every wording was fixed in his lifetime.

The JSON's second heading, “Editor's introduction and study”, has no entries. No modern editor, edition publisher, or separate editorial introduction can therefore be identified from this file.
