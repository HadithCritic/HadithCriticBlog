Purification and madhy

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

Al-Layth informed us, from Hisham, from 'Urwa, from 'Ali b. Abi Talib ﵁, who said:

Reported text · Matn

I said to al-Miqdad: “Ask the Messenger of God ﷺ, for were it not that his daughter is my wife, I would have asked him about one of us who draws near to a woman and emits madhy [prostatic fluid], without having been able to control himself and without having touched her.” So al-Miqdad asked the Messenger of God ﷺ, and the Messenger of God ﷺ said: «When one of you emits madhy without having touched her, let him wash his penis and his testicles, then perform ablution and pray.»

03 / Ṣafiyya during the pilgrimage

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

Al-Layth informed us, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. al-Qasim, from al-Qasim b. Muhammad, from 'A'isha, that

Reported text · Matn

she said: “I mentioned to the Messenger of God ﷺ that Safiyya bint Huyayy, the wife of the Prophet ﷺ, had begun her menses during the days of Mina. He said: «Is she going to detain us?» They said: ‘She has already performed the ifada.’ He said: «Then in that case, no.»”

العربيةالنص الأصلي
الإسناد

أَخْبَرَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْن الْقَاسِمِ، عَنْ الْقَاسِمِ بْنِ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، أَنَّهَا قَالَتْ:

المتن

" ذَكَرْتُ لِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ أَنَّ صَفِيَّةَ بِنْتَ حُيَيٍّ زَوْجَ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ حَاضَتْ فِي أَيَّامِ مِنًى، فَقَالَ: «أَحَابِسَتُنَا هِيَ؟» فَقَالُوا: إِنَّهَا قَدْ أَفَاضَتْ . قَالَ: «فَلا إِذًا»

05 / Zayd ibn ʿAmr at the Kaʿba

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

Al-Layth informed us, from Hisham, from his father, from Asma' bint Abi Bakr, that

Reported text · Matn

she said: I saw Zayd b. 'Amr b. Nufayl standing, leaning his back against the Ka'ba, saying: “O company of Quraysh, by God, there is none among you upon the religion of Abraham but me. And he used to save the infant girl [who was to be] buried alive; he would say to a man who intended to kill his daughter: ‘Stop! Do not kill her; I will spare you the burden of her upkeep.’ Then he would take her, and when she had grown up, he would say to her father: ‘If you wish, I will hand her over to you, and if you wish, I will spare you the burden of her upkeep.’”

العربيةالنص الأصلي
الإسناد

أَخْبَرَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ هِشَامٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ أَسْمَاءَ ابْنَةِ أَبِي بَكْرٍ، أَنَّهَا قَالَتْ:

المتن

رَأَيْتُ زَيْدَ بْنَ عَمْرِو بْنِ نُفَيْلٍ قَائِمًا مُسْنِدًا ظَهْرَهُ إِلَى الْكَعْبَةِ، يَقُولُ: " يَا مَعْشَرَ قُرَيْشٍ، وَاللَّهِ مَا فِيكُمْ أَحَدٌ عَلَى دِينِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ غَيْرِي، وَكَان يُحْيِي الْمَوْءُودَةَ، يَقُولُ لِلرَّجُلِ إِذَا أَرَادَ أَنْ يَقْتُلَ ابْنَتَهُ: مَهْ لا تَقْتُلْهَا، أَنَا أَكْفِيكَ مُؤْنَتَهَا فَيَأْخُذُهَا، فَإِذَا تَرَعْرَعَتْ، قَالَ لأَبِيهَا: إِنْ شِئْتَ دَفَعْتُهَا إِلَيْكَ، وَإِنْ شِئْتَ كَفَيْتُكَ مُؤْنَتَهَا ".

06 / The account of sorcery

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

Al-Layth informed us, from Hisham, from his father, from 'A'isha ﵂, who said:

Reported text · Matn

“The Messenger of God ﷺ was bewitched, until he would imagine that he was doing something when he was not doing it. Then one day he made a supplication, and then said to me: ‘Did you realize that God, the Mighty and Majestic, has given me an answer concerning that in which is my cure? Two men came to me; one sat at my head and the other at my feet. One of them said to the other: “What is the man’s ailment?” The other said: “He is bewitched (matbub).” He said: “Who bewitched him?” — meaning: who cast a spell on him? He said: “Labid b. al-A'sam.” He said: “In what?” He said: “In a comb and the combed-out hair, and the casing of a male palm-spathe.” He said: “And where is it?” He said: “In Dhirwan.”’ So the Messenger of God ﷺ went out to it, then returned, and said to 'A'isha ﵂ when he returned: ‘Its palm trees look like the heads of devils, and its water like the dregs of henna.’ She said: I said: ‘Did you not extract it?’ He said: ‘No. As for me, God, the Mighty and Majestic, has healed me, and I feared that it would stir up evil among the people.’ Then I buried the well.”

العربيةالنص الأصلي
الإسناد

أَخْبَرَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ هِشَامٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ﵂، قَالَتْ:

المتن

" سُحِرَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ، حَتَّى كَانَ يُخَيَّلُ إِلَيْهِ أَنَّهُ يَعْمَلُ الشَّيْءَ وَمَا يَفْعَلُهُ، حَتَّى إِذَا كَانَ ذَاتَ يَوْمٍ دَعْا دُعَاءً، ثُمَّ قَالَ لِي: " أُشْعِرْتُ أَنَّ اللَّهَ عز وجل أَفْتَانِي بِمَا فِيهِ شِفَائِي، أَتَانِي رَجُلانِ فَقَعَدَ أَحَدُهُمَا عِنْدَ رَأْسِي وَالآخَرُ عِنْدَ رِجْلِي، فَقَالَ أَحَدُهُمَا لِلآخَرِ: مَا وَجَعُ الرَّجُلِ. فَقَالَ الآخَرُ: مَطْبُوبٌ. فَقَالَ: مَنْ طَبَّهُ. يَعْنِي: سَحَرَهُ؟ فَقَالَ: لَبِيدُ بْنُ الأَعْصَمِ. فَقَالَ: فِي مَاذَا؟ قَالَ: فِي مُشْطٍ وَمُشَاطَةٍ، وَجُفِّ طَلْعَةِ ذَكَرٍ. قَالَ: وَأَيْنَ هُوَ؟ قَالَ: فِي ذِرْوَانَ. فَخَرَجَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ إِلَيْهَا، ثُمَّ رَجَعَ، فَقَالَ لِعَائِشَةَ ﵂ حِينَ رَجَعَ: نَخْلُهَا كَأَنَّهُ رُءُوسُ الشَّيَاطِينِ، وَكَأَنَّ مَاءَهَا بِقَاعُ الْحِنَّاءِ، قَالَتْ: فَقُلْتُ: مَا اسْتَخْرَجْتُهُ؟ قَالَ: لا أَمَّا أَنَا فَقَدْ شَفَانِي اللَّهُ عز وجل، وَخَشِيتُ أَنْ يُثِيرَ ذَلِكَ عَلَى النَّاسِ شَرًّا، ثُمَّ دَفَنْتُ الْبِئْرَ "

07 / Perfuming before iḥrām

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

Al-Layth informed us, from Hisham, from 'Uthman b. 'Urwa, from 'Urwa, from 'A'isha ﵂, that

Reported text · Matn

she said: «I used to perfume the Messenger of God ﷺ at the time of his ihram with the best perfume I could find.»

العربيةالنص الأصلي
الإسناد

10 / Umm Ḥarām and the sea expedition

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

Al-Layth informed us, from Yahya b. Sa'id, from Muhammad b. Yahya b. Hayyan, from Anas b. Malik, from his maternal aunt Umm Haram bint Milhan, who said:

Reported text · Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ slept for a short while, then awoke and smiled. I said: “O Messenger of God, what made you laugh?” He said: «People from my community were presented to me, riding the back of this other sea like kings upon couches.» She said: “Then supplicate God to make me one of them.” So he supplicated for her. Then he slept a second time and did the same as before, and she said what she had said, and he answered her with the same answer as the first. She said: “Then supplicate God to make me one of them.” He said: «You are among the first.» He [Anas] said: So she went out with her husband 'Ubada b. al-Samit as a woman raider, at the first time the Muslims rode the sea, with Mu'awiya b. Abi Sufyan ﵂ [as in the source]. When they returned from their expedition, on their way back, they disembarked in al-Sham, and a riding animal was brought close to her so that she might ride it, but it threw her down and she died, may God have mercy on her ﵀.

The transmission frame

The prefatory item names a sequence of later transmitters and modes of reception, including samāʿ and ijāza. It contains explicit uncertainty in some reception statements and a damaged name/date segment. These details are retained as written.

It names Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī and a reading to Hājar bint Muḥammad al-Maqdisī dated 868 AH, and the chain proceeds through earlier transmitters including ʿĪsā ibn Ḥammād Zughba.
