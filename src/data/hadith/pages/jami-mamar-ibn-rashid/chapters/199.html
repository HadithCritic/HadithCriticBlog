English
Isnad

Ma'mar reported from al-Zuhri, from 'Urwa, from Marwan b. al-Hakam, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. al-Aswad, from Ubayy b. Ka'b, who said: “I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say, ‘

Matn

Some poetry contains wisdom.’”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ مَرْوَانَ بْنِ الْحَكَمِ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ الْأَسْوَدِ، عَنْ أُبَيِّ بْنِ كَعْبٍ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ يَقُولُ: «إِنَّ §

Matn

مِنَ الشِّعْرِ حِكْمَةً»

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 263
English
Isnad

Ma'mar reported from al-Zuhri, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Ka'b b. Malik, from his father: “He said to the Prophet ﷺ, ‘God has revealed what He revealed concerning poetry.’ He replied: ‘

Matn

A believer strives with his person and with his tongue. By Him in whose hand my soul is, it is as though they are shooting arrows at them with it.’”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ كَعْبِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، أَنَّهُ قَالَ لِلنَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ إِنَّ اللَّهَ قَدْ أَنْزَلَ فِي الشِّعْرِ مَا أَنْزَلَ، قَالَ: «إِنَّ §

الْمُؤْمِنَ يُجَاهِدُ بِنَفْسِهِ وَلِسَانِهِ، وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ لَكَأَنَّمَا يَرْمُونَ فِيهِمْ بِهِ نَضْحَ النَّبْلِ»

Entry20501/ 13
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 263
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Ayyub, from Ibn Sirin: “

Matn

While the Messenger of God ﷺ was besieging the people of Ta'if, he said to Ka'b b. Malik, who was beside him, ‘Well then,’ prompting him to recite some poetry. He recited a poem about them: ‘We have settled all our doubts about Tihama, and Khaybar too; then we gathered our swords. If those cutting blades could speak, they would say: Daws or Thaqif!’” The Prophet ﷺ said: “Such verses strike them more quickly than arrows.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنِ ابْنِ سِيرِينَ، قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ وَهُوَ مُحَاصِرٌ أَهْلَ الطَّائِفِ لِكَعْبِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ وَهُوَ إِلَى جَنْبِهِ هِيهِ، يَسْتَنْشِدُهُ فَأَنْشَدَهُ قَصِيدَةً فِيهِمْ يَقُولُ: [البحر الوافر] قَضَيْنَا مِنْ تِهَامَةَ كُلَّ رَيْبٍ ... وَخَيْبَرَ ثُمَّ أَجْمَعْنَا السُّيوفَا نُخَيِّرُهَا وَلَوْ نَطَقَتْ لَقَالَتْ ... قَوَاطِعُهُنَّ دَوْسًا أَوْ ثَقِيفَا فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «§لَهُنَّ أَسْرَعُ فِيهِمْ مِنْ وَقْعِ النَّبْلِ»

Entry20502/ 13
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 264
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Ayyub, from Ibn Sirin: “

Matn

'Abd Allah b. Rawaha, Ka'b b. Malik and Hassan b. Thabit came to the Prophet ﷺ and said, ‘Messenger of God, why not command 'Ali to answer those who lampoon you?’ They meant Abu Sufyan b. al-Harith, Ibn al-Ziba'ra and al-'As b. Wa'il. The Prophet ﷺ replied: ‘'Ali is not suited to that, but when people defend their Prophet with their swords, they are still more entitled to defend him with their tongues.’ Hassan said: ‘I needed nothing further than this in defending you. By God, I would not exchange it for power of speech across all the land from Busra to San'a'.’ Then he recited: ‘My tongue is a sharp sword without a flaw, and my sea cannot be muddied by buckets.’”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنِ ابْنِ سِيرِينَ، أَنَّ

Matn

عَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ رَوَاحَةَ، وَكَعْبَ بْنَ مَالِكٍ، وَحَسَّانَ بْنَ ثَابِتٍ، أَتَوُا النَّبِيَّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فَقَالُوا: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، لَوْ أَمَرْتَ عَلِيًّا يُجِيبُ هَؤُلَاءِ الَّذِينَ يَهْجُونَكَ وَهُمْ يَعْنُونَ أَبَا سُفْيَانَ بْنَ الْحَارِثِ، وَابْنَ الزِّبَعْرَى، وَالْعَاصَ بْنَ وَائِلٍ فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «إِنَّ عَلِيًّا لَيْسَ هُنَالِكَ، وَلَكِنَّ §الْقَوْمَ إِذَا نَصَرُوا نَبِيَّهُمْ بِأَسْيَافِهِمْ فَبِأَلْسنَتِهِمْ أَحَقُّ أَنْ يَنصْرُوهُ» ، فَقَالَ حَسَّانُ: مَا كُنْتُ لِأَنْتصِرَ مِنْكَ إِلَّا هَذَا، وَاللَّهِ مَا أُحِبُّ أَنَّ لِي بِهَا مَقُولًا مَا بَيْنَ بُصْرَى إِلَى صَنْعَاءَ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: [البحر الوافر] لِسَانِي صَارِمٌ لَا عَيْبَ فِيهِ ... وَبَحْرِي مَا تُكَدِّرُهُ الدِّلَاءُ

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 264
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Ibn Tawus, from his father, who said that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “It would be better for a man's

Matn

belly to be filled with pus than to be filled with poetry. If you hear someone reciting it, throw dust in his face.” Ma'mar added: “Yet I heard both al-Zuhri and Qatada reciting poetry, whereas al-Hasan did not do that.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ طَاوُسٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «§

Matn

لَأَنْ يَمْتَلِئَ جَوْفُ أَحَدِكُمْ قَيْحًا خَيْرٌ لَهُ مِنْ أَنْ يَمْتَلِئَ شِعْرًا، فَإِذَا سَمِعْتُمُوهُ يُنْشِدُ فَاحْثُوا فِي وَجْهِهِ التُّرَابَ» قَالَ مَعْمَرٌ: وَسَمِعْتُ الزُّهْرِيَّ، وَقَتَادَةَ «يُنْشِدَانِ الشِّعْرَ» ، قَالَ: «وَكَانَ الْحَسَنُ لَا يَفْعَلُ»

Entry20505/ 13
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 265
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Hisham b. 'Urwa, from his father: “

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ was traveling when one of the Emigrants dismounted and chanted rajaz verse at the others: ‘Neither a mudd nor half a mudd has nourished her; neither a few dates nor a loaf. Rather, the milk of autumn has nourished her: buttermilk, sour milk and fresh milk. Rather, the cracked colocynth nourished her, and a thin drink like the edge of a rough garment; she spends the night between a pen and a latrine.’” The Prophet ﷺ feared that trouble might arise between the men, so he instructed both to mount their animals. Ma'mar added: “Abu Hamza al-Thumali related a similar account, adding that the Prophet ﷺ turned his she-camel around and ordered both men to mount.” [Some expressions in the transmitted poem are uncertain.]

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ هِشَامِ بْنِ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، قَالَ:

Matn

كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فِي سَفَرٍ، فَنَزَلَ رَجُلٌ مِنَ الْمُهَاجِرِينَ فَرَجَزَ بِهِمْ، فَقَالَ: [البحر الرجز] لَمْ يَغْذُهَا مُدٌّ وَلَا نَصِيفُ ... وَلَا تُمَيْرَاتٌ وَلَا رَغِيفُ لَكِنْ غَذَاهَا اللَّبَنُ الْخَرِيفُ ... الْمَخْضُ والْقَارِصُ وَالصَّرِيفُ لَكِنْ غَذَاهَا الْحَنْظَلُ النَّقِيفُ ... وَمِذْقَةٌ كَطُرَّةِ الْخَنِيفِ تَبِيتُ بَيْنَ الزَّرْبِ وَالْكَنِيفِ قَالَ: «§فَخَافَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ أَنْ يَكُونَ بَيْنَهُمَا شَرٌّ، فَأَمَرَهُمَا فَرَكِبَا» ، وَحَدَّثَنِي أَبُو حَمْزَةَ الثُّمَالِيُّ بِنَحْوِ حَدِيثِ هِشَامٍ وَزَادَ فِيهِ: أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ عَطَفَ نَاقَتَهُ وَأَمَرَهُمَا فَرَكِبَا

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Yahya b. Sa'id, from Ibn al-Musayyib: “

Matn

I detest singing, but I like rajaz poetry.”

Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from Ibn al-Musayyib: “

Matn

Hassan recited poetry in the mosque. 'Umar passed him and gave him a disapproving look, saying, ‘In the mosque? In the mosque?’ Hassan replied, ‘By God, I recited it here in the company of one better than you.’ Fearing that Hassan would invoke the Messenger of God ﷺ against him, 'Umar let him continue and left.”

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Qatada: “

Matn

When Iblis was cast down, he asked, ‘My Lord, You have cursed him; what is his occupation?’ He said, ‘Sorcery.’ He asked, ‘What is his recitation?’ He said, ‘Poetry.’ ‘What is his scripture?’ ‘Tattooing.’ ‘What is his food?’ ‘Every carcass and whatever is slaughtered without God's name being mentioned.’ ‘What is his drink?’ ‘Every intoxicant.’ ‘Where is his residence?’ ‘The bathhouse.’ ‘Where is his place of assembly?’ ‘The markets.’ ‘What is his voice?’ ‘The flute.’ ‘What are his traps?’ ‘Women.’” [The dialogue's pronouns are presented as in the source.]

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَر، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، قَالَ: «§

Matn

لَمَّا أُهْبِطَ إِبْلِيسُ قَالَ: أَيْ رَبِّ، قَدْ لَعَنْتَهُ فَمَا عَمَلُهُ؟ قَالَ: السِّحْرُ، قَالَ: فَمَا قِرَاءَتُهُ؟ قَالَ: الشِّعْرُ، قَالَ: فَمَا كِتَابُهُ؟ قَالَ: الْوَشْمُ، قَالَ: فَمَا طَعَامُهُ؟ قَالَ: كُلُّ مَيْتَةٍ وَمَا لَمْ يُذْكَرِ اسْمُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ، قَالَ: فَمَا شَرَابُهُ؟ قَالَ: كُلُّ مُسْكِرٍ، قَالَ: فَأَيْنَ مَسْكَنُهُ؟ قَالَ: الْحَمَّامُ، قَالَ: فَأَيْنَ مَجْلِسُهُ؟ قَالَ: الْأَسْوَاقُ، قَالَ: فَمَا صَوْتُهُ؟ قَالَ: الْمِزْمَارُ، قَالَ: فَمَا مَصَايِدُهُ؟ قَالَ: النِّسَاءُ»
