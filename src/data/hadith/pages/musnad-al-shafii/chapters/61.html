Report1602/ 14
Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 371
English
Isnad

Muslim b. Khalid and Sa'id ibn Salim informed us, from Ibn Jurayj, from 'Ata', from Ibn 'Abbas, who said: al-Fadl b. 'Abbas informed me that

Matn

the Prophet ﷺ had him ride behind him from Jam' to Mina, and he did not cease reciting the talbiya until he threw the jamra. Sufyan informed us, from Muhammad b. Abi Harmala, from Kurayb, from Ibn 'Abbas, from al-Fadl, from the Prophet ﷺ, the like of it.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مُسْلِمُ بْنُ خَالِدٍ، وَسَعِيدُ بْنُ سَالِمٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ جُرَيْجٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي الْفَضْلُ بْنُ عَبَّاسٍ،

Matn

أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَرْدَفَهُ مِنْ جَمْعٍ إِلَى مِنًى، فَلَمْ يَزَلْ يُلَبِّي حَتَّى رَمَى الْجَمْرَةَ أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ أَبِي حَرْمَلَةَ، عَنْ كُرَيْبٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، عَنِ الْفَضْلِ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِثْلَهُ

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 371
English
Isnad

The trustworthy informant informed me, from Hammad b. Salama, from Ziyad, client (mawla) of Banu Makhzum — and he was trustworthy: that

Matn

a group of people in ihram killed game, and Ibn 'Umar said to them, “You owe compensation (jaza').” They said, “Does each one of us owe compensation, or do all of us together owe one compensation?” Ibn 'Umar said: “You are being deluded! Rather, all of you together owe one compensation.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنِي الثِّقَةُ، عَنْ حَمَّادِ بْنِ سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ زِيَادٍ، مَوْلَى بَنِي مَخْزُومٍ، وَكَانَ ثِقَةً،

Matn

أَنَّ قَوْمًا حُرُمًا أَصَابُوا صَيْدًا فَقَالَ لَهُمُ ابْنُ عُمَرَ: «عَلَيْكُمْ جَزَاءٌ» فَقَالُوا: عَلَى كُلِّ وَاحِدٍ مِنَّا جَزَاءٌ، أَوْ عَلَيْنَا كُلِّنَا جَزَاءٌ وَاحِدٌ؟ فَقَالَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ: «إِنَّهُ لَمُغَرَّرٌ بِكُمْ، بَلْ عَلَيْكُمْ كُلِّكُمْ جَزَاءٌ وَاحِدٌ»

English
Isnad

Muslim and Sa'id informed us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Bukayr b. 'Abd Allah, from al-Qasim, from Ibn 'Abbas: that

Matn

a man asked him about a muhrim who killed a locust. He said: “Let him give in charity a handful of food.” And Ibn 'Abbas said: “[Surely] he would take a handful of locusts! — but my opinion is according to that.” [The sense of the final clause is unclear in the text.]

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 372
English
Isnad

Sufyan informed us, from Ibn Abi Najih, from Maymun b. Abi Najih [sic], from Maymun b. Mihran, who said:

Matn

I sat with Ibn 'Abbas, and a man sat with him — I had never seen a man with longer hair. He said: “I entered ihram with this hair on me.” Ibn 'Abbas said: “Wrap up what is below the two ears of it.” He said: “I kissed a woman who is not my wife.” He said: “Your mouth has committed zina.” He said: “I saw a louse and threw it away.” He said: “That is a stray; it is not to be sought.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي نَجِيحٍ، عَنْ مَيْمُونِ بْنِ أَبِي نَجِيحٍ عَنْ مَيْمُونِ بْنِ مِهْرَانَ قَالَ:

Matn

جَلَسْتُ إِلَى ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ فَجَلَسَ إِلَيْهِ رَجُلٌ لَمْ أَرَ رَجُلًا أَطْوَلَ شَعْرًا مِنْهُ، فَقَالَ: أَحْرَمْتُ وَعَلَيَّ هَذَا الشَّعْرُ، فَقَالَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ: «اشْتَمِلْ عَلَى مَا دُونَ الْأُذُنَيْنِ مِنْهُ»، قَالَ: قَبَّلْتُ امْرَأَةً لَيْسَتْ بِامْرَأَتِي، قَالَ: «زَنَى فُوكَ»، قَالَ: رَأَيْتُ قَمْلَةً فَطَرَحْتُهَا، قَالَ: «تِلْكَ الضَّالَّةُ لَا تُبْتَغَى»

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 372
English
Isnad

'Abd Allah b. Mu'ammal al-'A'idhi informed us, from 'Umar b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. Muhaysin, from 'Ata' b. Abi Rabah, from Safiyya bint Shayba, who said: A daughter of Abu Tajrah, one of the women of Banu 'Abd al-Dar, informed me, saying:

Matn

I entered, with some women of Quraysh, the house of Abu Husayn to look at the Messenger of God ﷺ as he was running (yas'a) between al-Safa and al-Marwa, and I saw him running such that his waist-wrapper was swirling from the intensity of the running, so that I said: “I can see his knees.” And I heard him say: “Run, for God Almighty has prescribed the running upon you.” Al-Rabi' recited: “so that I would say.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُؤَمَّلٍ الْعَائِذِيُّ، عَنْ عُمَرَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ مُحَيْصِنٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ أَبِي رَبَاحٍ، عَنْ صَفِيَّةَ بِنْتِ شَيْبَةَ، قَالَتْ: أَخْبَرَتْنِي بِنْتُ أَبِي تَجْرَاةَ، إِحْدَى نِسَاءِ بَنِي عَبْدِ الدَّارِ قَالَتْ:

Matn

دَخَلْتُ مَعَ نِسْوَةٍ مِنْ قُرَيْشٍ دَارَ أَبِي حُسَيْنٍ نَنْظُرُ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَهُوَ يَسْعَى بَيْنَ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةِ، فَرَأَيْتُهُ يَسْعَى وَإِنَّ مِئْزَرَهُ لَيَدُورُ مِنْ شِدَّةِ السَّعْيِ حَتَّى لَأَقُولُ: إِنِّي لَأَرَى رُكْبَتَيْهِ، وَسَمِعْتُهُ يَقُولُ: «اسْعَوْا، فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ عز وجل كَتَبَ عَلَيْكُمُ السَّعْيَ» قَرَأَ الرَّبِيعُ: حَتَّى إِنِّي لَأَقُولُ

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 372
English
Isnad

The translation is shown whole: its chain opens the passage below.

Matn

Sufyan informed us, from Ibn Tawus, from his father. Al-Shafi'i ﵁ said: And Muslim informed us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Muhammad b. Qays b. Makhrama — one of them added to the other, and they agreed in meaning — that the Prophet ﷺ said: “The people of the Jahiliyya used to depart from 'Arafa before the sun set, and from al-Muzdalifa after the sun rose, and they would say: ‘Shine, Thabir, so that we may raid’ [p. 373]; so God Almighty delayed this one and advanced that one.” That is, He advanced [the departure from] al-Muzdalifa to before the sun rises, and delayed [the departure from] 'Arafa until the sun sets.

العربية
Isnad

Matn
