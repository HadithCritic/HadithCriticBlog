English
Isnad

Malik b. Anas informed us, from Abdullah b. Dinar, from Abdullah b. Umar ﵄, who said:

Matn

While the people were at Quba' in the dawn prayer, someone came to them and said: “Qur'an was revealed to the Messenger of God ﷺ tonight, and he has been commanded to face the Ka'bah, so face it.” Their faces were toward Syria, so they turned around toward the Ka'bah.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكُ بْنُ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ دِينَارٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ رضي الله عنهما قَالَ:

Matn

بَيْنَمَا النَّاسُ بِقُبَاءٍ فِي صَلَاةِ الصُّبْحِ إِذْ أَتَاهُمْ آتٍ فَقَالَ: إِنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَدْ أُنْزِلَ عَلَيْهِ اللَّيْلَةَ قُرْآنٌ، وَقَدْ أُمِرَ أَنْ يَسْتَقْبِلَ الْكَعْبَةَ فَاسْتَقْبِلُوهَا، وَكَانَتْ وُجُوهُهُمْ إِلَى الشَّامِ فَاسْتَدَارُوا إِلَى الْكَعْبَةِ

English
Isnad

Malik b. Anas informed us, from Nafi', that

Matn

Abdullah b. Umar ﵄, when asked about the fear prayer (salat al-khawf), would say: “The imam advances with one group,” and then relate the hadith. Ibn Umar said in the hadith: “If the fear is greater than that, they pray on foot or riding, facing the qiblah or not facing it.” Malik said: Nafi' said: I do not think Abdullah b. Umar mentioned that except from the Messenger of God ﷺ.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكُ بْنُ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ،

Matn

أَنَّ عَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ عُمَرَ رضي الله عنهما كَانَ إِذَا سُئِلَ عَنْ صَلَاةِ الْخَوْفِ قَالَ: يَتَقَدَّمُ الْإِمَامُ وَطَائِفَةٌ، ثُمَّ قَصَّ الْحَدِيثَ. وَقَالَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ فِي الْحَدِيثِ: فَإِنْ كَانَ خَوْفًا أَشَدَّ مِنْ ذَلِكَ صَلُّوا رِجَالًا وَرُكْبَانًا، مُسْتَقْبِلِي الْقِبْلَةَ وَغَيْرَ مُسْتَقْبِلِيهَا. قَالَ مَالِكٌ: قَالَ نَافِعٌ: لَا أَرَى عَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ عُمَرَ ذَكَرَ ذَلِكَ إِلَّا عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ

Isnad

Ibn Abi Fudayk informed us, from Ibn Abi Dhi'b, from al-Zuhri, from Salim, from his father; [transfer to a second chain] and Malik informed us, from Abdullah b. Dinar, from Abdullah b. Umar ﵄ ⦗p. 24⦘ that he said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ used to pray on his mount while travelling, in whatever direction it turned with him.

Isnad

Malik b. Anas informed us, from his uncle Abu Suhayl b. Malik, from his father, that he heard Talhah b. Ubaydullah ﵁ say:

Matn

A man came to the Messenger of God ﷺ asking about Islam. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Five prayers in the day and night.” He said: “Is there anything else required of me?” He said: “No, unless you volunteer.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكُ بْنُ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ عَمِّهِ أَبِي سُهَيْلِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ طَلْحَةَ بْنَ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ رضي الله عنه يَقُولُ:

Matn

جَاءَ رَجُلٌ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَإِذَا هُوَ يَسْأَلُ عَنِ الْإِسْلَامِ، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «خَمْسُ صَلَوَاتٍ فِي الْيَوْمِ وَاللَّيْلَةِ». فَقَالَ: هَلْ عَلَيَّ غَيْرُهَا؟ قَالَ: «لَا، إِلَّا أَنْ تَطَوَّعَ»

Isnad

Muslim b. Khalid and Abd al-Majid b. Abd al-Aziz b. Abi Rawwad informed us, from Ibn Jurayj: Abd al-Rahman b. Abdullah b. Abi Ammar informed me, from Abdullah b. Babah, from Ya'la b. Umayyah, who said:

Matn

I said to Umar b. al-Khattab: God Almighty said only: “...that you shorten the prayer, if you fear that those who disbelieve may put you to trial” [Q 4:101], and now people are safe. Umar ﵁ said: I was surprised by what surprised you, so I asked the Messenger of God ﷺ, and he said: “It is a charity that God Almighty has given to you in charity, so accept His charity.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مُسْلِمُ بْنُ خَالِدٍ، وَعَبْدُ الْمَجِيدِ بْنُ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ بْنِ أَبِي رَوَّادٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ جُرَيْجٍ، أَخْبَرَنِي عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي عَمَّارٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ بَابَاهُ، عَنْ يَعْلَى بْنِ أُمَيَّةَ قَالَ:

Matn

قُلْتُ لِعُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ: إِنَّمَا قَالَ اللَّهُ عز وجل: {أَنْ تَقْصُرُوا مِنَ الصَّلَاةِ إِنْ خِفْتُمْ أَنْ يَفْتِنَكُمُ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا} [النِّسَاء: 101]، فَقَدْ أَمِنَ النَّاسُ. فَقَالَ عُمَرُ رضي الله عنه: عَجِبْتُ مِمَّا عَجِبْتَ مِنْهُ، فَسَأَلْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ: «صَدَقَةٌ تَصَدَّقَ اللَّهُ عز وجل بِهَا عَلَيْكُمْ، فَاقْبَلُوا صَدَقَتَهُ»

English
Isnad

Muslim b. Khalid informed us, from Ibn Jurayj, who said: Abd al-Aziz b. Abd al-Malik b. Abi Mahdhurah informed me that Abdullah b. Muhayriz informed him, and he was an orphan in the care of Abu Mahdhurah ⦗p. 31⦘ when he prepared him for [travel to] Syria.

Matn

I said to Abu Mahdhurah: “Uncle, I am going to Syria and I fear I will be asked about your way of calling the adhan, so tell me, Abu Mahdhurah.” He said: Yes. I went out with a group, and we were on part of the road to Hunayn. The Messenger of God ﷺ was returning from Hunayn and met us on the road, and the muezzin of the Messenger of God ﷺ called the adhan for prayer in the presence of the Messenger of God ﷺ. We heard the voice of the muezzin while we were off to the side, so we shouted, imitating him and mocking him. The Prophet ﷺ heard and sent for us until we stood before him. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Which of you is the one whose voice I heard raised?” The whole group pointed to me, and they spoke truly. So he released them all and detained me. He said: “Stand and call the adhan for prayer.” I stood, and nothing was more hateful to me than the Prophet ﷺ or than what he commanded me to do. I stood before the Messenger of God ﷺ, and the Messenger of God ﷺ himself taught me the adhan, saying: “Say: ‘God is Greatest, God is Greatest; God is Greatest, God is Greatest; I bear witness that there is no god but God; I bear witness that there is no god but God; I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of God; I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of God.’” Then he said to me: “Go back and raise your voice.” Then he said: “Say: ‘I bear witness that there is no god but God; I bear witness that there is no god but God; I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of God; I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of God; come to prayer, come to prayer; come to success, come to success; God is Greatest, God is Greatest; there is no god but God.’” Then when I had finished the adhan he called me and gave me a pouch with some silver in it, then placed his hand on Abu Mahdhurah's forelock, passed it over his face, then between his breasts, then over his liver, until his hand reached Abu Mahdhurah's navel. Then the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “May God bless you and bless you [further].” I said: “O Messenger of God, appoint me to call the adhan in Mecca.” He said: “I have appointed you to it.” Everything of hatred I had felt toward the Messenger of God ﷺ went away, and all of it turned into love for the Messenger of God ﷺ. I came to Attab b. Asid ﵁, the governor of the Messenger of God ﷺ, and I called the adhan at the command of the Messenger of God ﷺ. Ibn Jurayj said: And those of the family of Abu Mahdhurah whom I met informed me of it along the lines ⦗p. 32⦘ of what Ibn Muhayriz informed me. Al-Shafi'i ﵁ said: I met Ibrahim b. Abd al-Aziz b. Abd al-Malik b. Abi Mahdhurah calling the adhan as Ibn Muhayriz described, and I heard him narrate from his father, from Ibn Muhayriz, from Abu Mahdhurah, from the Prophet ﷺ, the meaning of what Ibn Jurayj related.

Ibn Abi Fudayk informed us, from Ibn Abi Dhi'b, from al-Maqburi, from Abd al-Rahman b. Abi Sa'id al-Khudri, from Abu Sa'id ﵁, who said:

Matn

On the day of the Trench we were kept from praying until some time after sunset, deep into the night, until the danger had been averted for us. That is the saying of God Almighty: “And God sufficed the believers in the fighting, and God is Strong, Mighty” [Q 33:25]. The Messenger of God ﷺ called Bilal and commanded him, and he gave the iqamah for the noon prayer and he prayed it well, as he would pray it in its time; then he gave the iqamah for the afternoon prayer and prayed it likewise; then he gave the iqamah for the sunset prayer and prayed it likewise; then he gave the iqamah for the night prayer and prayed it likewise as well. He said: That was before there was revealed concerning the fear prayer: “...then on foot or riding” [Q 2:239].

أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ مِهْرَانَ، عَنْ عُمَارَةَ، عَنِ الْأَسْوَدِ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ قَالَ:

Matn

لَا يَجْعَلَنَّ أَحَدُكُمْ لِلشَّيْطَانِ مِنْ صَلَاتِهِ جُزْءًا، يَرَى أَنَّ حَتْمًا عَلَيْهِ أَنْ لَا يَنْفَتِلَ إِلَّا عَنْ يَمِينِهِ، فَلَقَدْ رَأَيْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَكْثَرَ مَا كَانَ يَنْصَرِفُ عَنْ يَسَارِهِ
