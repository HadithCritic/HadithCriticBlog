In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. A Biographical Note on al-Shafi'i ﵀. Praise be to God, Lord of the worlds, and may God send blessings upon Muhammad and upon all his family and companions. This is the book al-Musnad by al-Shafi'i. It is enough glory for al-Shafi'i that he is al-Shafi'i: none like this man has appeared among the scholars of Islam in understanding of the Book and the Sunnah, penetrating insight into both, and precision of derivation, together with strength of argument, light of insight, and creativity in establishing proof and silencing his opponent in debate. He was eloquent of tongue and clear in expression, at the highest summit of rhetoric. He acquired the refinement of the Bedouin and took knowledge and learning from the people of the settled lands, until he rose above every scholar before and after him. He emerged in the Hijaz, where the scholars were the reference for transmission and the Sunnah and were pillars of knowledge in the jurisprudence of the Qur'an, but many of them were not people of sharp debate and disputation, and they almost failed to hold their own against the People of Opinion (ahl al-ra'y). Then this young man came, debating and defending, knowing how to stand by his proof, how to bind the People of Opinion to the obligation of following the Sunnah, how to establish for them the authority of the single-narrator report (khabar al-wahid), how to set out for people the ways of understanding the Book according to the known eloquence and expression of the Arabs, how to guide them to the abrogating and abrogated in the Book and the Sunnah, and to reconciling what appears contradictory in them or in one of them, until the people of Mecca called him “Defender of Hadith.” His reports spread widely among the scholars of Islam in his time, and they would come to Mecca for the pilgrimage, debate him and take from him, in

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم نبذة عَن الشَّافِعِي رحمه الله الْحَمد لله رب الْعَالمين. وَصلى الله على مُحَمَّد وعَلى آله وَصَحبه أَجْمَعِينَ. هَذَا كتاب الْمسند للشَّافِعِيّ. وَكفى الشَّافِعِي فخرا أَنه الشَّافِعِي - ان هَذَا الرجل لم يظْهر مثله فِي عُلَمَاء الاسلام، فِي فقه الْكتاب وَالسّنة، ونفوذ النّظر فيهمَا ودقة الاستنباط. مَعَ قُوَّة الْعَارِضَة، وَنور البصيرة، والابداع فِي إِقَامَة الْحجَّة وإفحام مناظره. فصيح اللِّسَان، ناصع الْبَيَان، فِي الذرْوَة الْعليا من البلاغة. تأدب بأدب الْبَادِيَة، وَأخذ الْعُلُوم والمعارف عَن أهل الْحَضَر، حَتَّى سما عَن كل عَالم قبله وَبعده. نبغ فِي الْحجاز، وَكَانَ إِلَى علمائه مرجع الرِّوَايَة وَالسّنة، وَكَانُوا أساطين الْعلم فِي فقه الْقُرْآن، وَلم يكن الْكثير مِنْهُم أهل لسن وجدل، وكادوا يعجزون عَن مناظرة أهل الرَّأْي، فجَاء هَذَا الشَّاب يناظر وينافح، وَيعرف كَيفَ يقوم بحجته، وَكَيف يلْزم أهل الرَّأْي وجوب اتِّبَاع السّنة، وَكَيف يثبت لَهُم الْحجَّة فِي خبر الْوَاحِد، وَكَيف يفصل للنَّاس طرق فهم الْكتاب على مَا عرف من بَيَان الْعَرَب وفصاحتهم، وَكَيف يدلهم على النَّاسِخ والمنسوخ من الْكتاب وَالسّنة، وعَلى الْجمع بَين مَا ظَاهره التَّعَارُض فيهمَا أَو فِي أَحدهمَا، حَتَّى سَمَّاهُ أهل مَكَّة «نَاصِر الحَدِيث». وتواترت أخباره إِلَى عُلَمَاء الاسلام فِي عصره، فَكَانُوا يفدون إِلَى مَكَّة لِلْحَجِّ، يناظرونه وَيَأْخُذُونَ عَنهُ فِي

When we left him, a group of people of understanding of the Qur'an informed me that he was the most knowledgeable of people in his time of the meanings of the Qur'an and had been given understanding of it. Had I known him [earlier], I would have kept to him.” Dawud said: And I saw him regretting what he had missed of him. And Ahmad b. Hanbal says: “Were it not for al-Shafi'i, we would not have known the jurisprudence of hadith.” Then he entered Iraq, seat of the caliphate and capital of the state (1), and took from the People of Opinion their knowledge and their opinion, examined it, and debated and argued with them, gaining thereby greater insight into jurisprudence and greater support for the Sunnah, until the Meccan jurist Abu al-Walid Musa b. Abi al-Jarud said: “We and our companions among the people of Mecca used to say that al-Shafi'i took the books of Ibn Jurayj (2) from four people: from Muslim b. Khalid and Sa'id ibn Salim, both of them jurists; from Abd al-Majid b. Abd al-Aziz b. Abi Rawwad, who was the most knowledgeable of them regarding Ibn Jurayj; and from Abdullah b. al-Harith al-Makhzumi, who was among the most reliable. Leadership in jurisprudence in Medina came to rest with Malik b. Anas; he travelled to him, stayed with him and took from him. Leadership in jurisprudence in Iraq came to rest with Abu Hanifah; he took from his companion Muhammad b. al-Hasan a number of books, none of which he had not heard from him. Thus the knowledge of the People of Opinion and the knowledge of the People of Hadith came together in him; he worked with it, until he established the foundations and set down the rules, and both ally and opponent submitted to him. His affair became famous, his renown rose and his rank was elevated, until what came to pass came to pass.”

حَيَاة شُيُوخه، حَتَّى إِن أَحْمد بن حَنْبَل جلس مَعَه مرّة، فجَاء أحد إخوانه يعتب عَلَيْهِ أَن ترك مجْلِس ابْن عُيَيْنَة - شيخ الشَّافِعِي، وَيجْلس إِلَى هَذَا الاعرابي! فَقَالَ لَهُ أَحْمد: «اسْكُتْ، إِنَّك إِن فاتك حَدِيث بعلو وجدته بنزول، وَإِن فاتك عقل هَذَا أَخَاف أَن لَا تَجدهُ، مَا رَأَيْت أحدا أفقه فِي كتاب الله من هَذَا الْفَتى». وَحَتَّى يَقُول دَاوُد بن عَليّ الظَّاهِرِيّ الامام فِي كتاب مَنَاقِب الشَّافِعِي: " قَالَ لي إِسْحَق بن رَاهَوَيْه: ذهبت أَنا وَأحمد بن حَنْبَل إِلَى الشَّافِعِي بِمَكَّة فَسَأَلته عَن أَشْيَاء، فَوَجَدته فصيحا حسن الْأَدَب، فَلَمَّا فارقناه أعلمني جمَاعَة من أهل الْفَهم بِالْقُرْآنِ أَنه كَانَ أعلم النَّاس فِي زَمَانه بمعاني الْقُرْآن، وَأَنه قد أُوتِيَ فِيهِ فهما، فَلَو كنت عَرفته للزمته، قَالَ دَاوُد: ورأيته يتأسف على مَا فَاتَهُ مِنْهُ ". وَحَتَّى يَقُول أَحْمد بن حَنْبَل: «لَوْلَا الشَّافِعِي مَا عرفنَا فقه الحَدِيث». ثمَّ يدْخل الْعرَاق، دَار الْخلَافَة وعاصمة الدولة (1)، فَيَأْخُذ عَن أهل الرَّأْي علمهمْ ورأيهم، وَينظر فِيهِ، ويجادلهم ويحاجهم، يزْدَاد بذلك بصرا بالفقه، ونصرا للسّنة، حَتَّى يَقُول أَبُو الْوَلِيد الْمَكِّيّ الْفَقِيه مُوسَى بن أبي الْجَارُود: " كُنَّا نتحدث نَحن وأصحابنا من أهل مَكَّة أَن الشَّافِعِي أَخذ كتب ابْن جريح (2) عَن أَرْبَعَة أنفس: عَن مُسلم بن خَالِد، وَسَعِيد بن سَالم، وَهَذَانِ فقيهان، وَعَن عبد الْمجِيد بن الْعَزِيز بن أبي رواد، وَكَانَ أعلمهم بِابْن جريج، وَعَن عبد الله بن الْحَرْث المَخْزُومِي، وَكَانَ من الاثبات، وانتهت رياسة الْفِقْه بِالْمَدِينَةِ إِلَى مَالك بن أنس، رَحل إِلَيْهِ ولازمه وَأخذ عَنهُ، وانتهت رياسة الْفِقْه بالعراق إِلَى أبي حنيفَة، فَأخذ عَن صَاحبه مُحَمَّد بن الْحسن جملا لَيْسَ فِيهَا شئ إِلَّا وَقد سَمعه عَلَيْهِ، فَاجْتمع لَهُ علم أهل الرَّأْي وَعلم أهل الحَدِيث، فتصرف فِي ذَلِك، حَتَّى أصل الْأُصُول، وَقعد الْقَوَاعِد، وأذعن لَهُ الْمُوَافق والمخالف، واشتهر أمره. وَعلا ذكره، وارتفع قدره، حَتَّى صَار مِنْهُ مَا صَار ".

Then he entered Egypt in the year 199 and stayed there until he died, teaching people the Sunnah, the jurisprudence of the Sunnah and the Book, and debating and arguing with his opponents, most of whom were followers of his teacher Malik b. Anas and were fanatical in their attachment to his school. Al-Shafi'i dazzled them with his knowledge, his guidance and his intellect; they saw a man the like of whom eyes had not seen, so they kept to his circle, benefiting from him in the knowledge of the Book and the knowledge of hadith, and taking from him language, genealogies and poetry. At times he also taught them something of medicine, and they learned from him the etiquette of disputation and debate. He would write books in his own hand, and they would read to him what they copied from them, or he would dictate some of them to them. Most of them gave up what they had been fanatical about and learned from him independent reasoning (ijtihad) and the abandonment of blind imitation (taqlid). Thus al-Shafi'i filled the layers of the earth with knowledge. He died and was buried in Egypt, and his grave is known and famous to this day. He lived 54 years, was born in 150 in Gaza, and died on the night of Friday and was buried on Friday after the afternoon prayer, on the last day of Rajab in the year 204. May God have mercy on him.

The Imam Abu Abdullah Muhammad b. Idris al-Shafi'i ﵁ informed us: Malik b. Anas informed us, from Safwan b. Sulaym, from Sa'id ibn Salamah, a man of the family of Ibn al-Azraq, that al-Mughirah b. Abi Burdah, who is of Banu Abd al-Dar, informed him that he heard Abu Hurayrah ﵁ say:

Matn

A man asked the Messenger of God ﷺ and said: “O Messenger of God, we travel by sea and carry a little water with us; if we perform ablution with it we become thirsty. May we perform ablution with seawater?” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Its water is purifying, and its dead [animals] are lawful.”

Isnad

Sufyan narrated to us: al-Zuhri narrated to us: Abbad b. Tamim informed me, from his uncle Abdullah b. Zayd, who said:

Matn

A complaint was brought to the Prophet ﷺ about a man who imagines something [happening] during prayer. He said: “Let him not turn away [from his prayer] until he hears a sound or smells an odor.”

Matn

English
Isnad

Ibrahim b. Muhammad informed us, from Abu al-Huwayrith, from al-A'raj, from Ibn al-Simmah, who said:

Matn

I passed by the Prophet ﷺ while he was urinating and I greeted him, but he did not reply until he went to a wall and scraped it with a staff he had with him, then placed his hand on the wall and wiped his face and forearms, then returned my greeting. Abu al-Abbas al-Asamm ﵀ said: These two hadiths are not in the Book of Ablution, but I included them in it because this is their place. And at this point in the Book of Ablution, al-Shafi'i ﵁ said: Abu al-Huwayrith related, from al-A'raj, from Ibn al-Simmah, that the Messenger of God ﷺ urinated and performed tayammum. So I included the hadith in full for this reason.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي الْحُوَيْرِثِ، عَنِ الْأَعْرَجِ، عَنِ ابْنِ الصِّمَّةِ قَالَ:

Matn

مَرَرْتُ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَهُوَ يَبُولُ فَسَلَّمْتُ عَلَيْهِ، فَلَمْ يَرُدَّ عَلَيَّ حَتَّى قَامَ إِلَى جِدَارٍ فَحَتَّهُ بِعَصًا كَانَتْ مَعَهُ، ثُمَّ وَضَعَ يَدَهُ عَلَى الْجِدَارِ فَمَسَحَ وَجْهَهُ وَذِرَاعَيْهِ، ثُمَّ رَدَّ عَلَيَّ السَّلَامَ قَالَ أَبُو الْعَبَّاسِ الْأَصَمُّ رحمه الله: هَذَانِ الْحَدِيثَانِ لَيْسَا فِي كِتَابِ الْوُضُوءِ، وَلَكِنْ أَخْرَجْتُهُمَا فِيهِ لِأَنَّهُ مَوْضِعُهُ. وَفِي هَذَا الْمَوْضِعِ مِنْ كِتَابِ الْوُضُوءِ، قَالَ الشَّافِعِيُّ رضي الله عنه: وَرَوَى أَبُو الْحُوَيْرِثِ، عَنِ الْأَعْرَجِ، عَنِ ابْنِ الصِّمَّةِ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بَالَ فَتَيَمَّمَ، فَأَخْرَجْتُ الْحَدِيثَ بِتَمَامِهِ لِهَذِهِ الْعِلَّةِ

English
Isnad

Malik informed us, from Abu al-Nadr, freedman of Umar b. Ubaydullah, from Sulayman b. Yasar, from al-Miqdad b. al-Aswad, that

Matn

Ali b. Abi Talib ﵁ commanded him to ask the Messenger of God ﷺ about a man who draws near to his wife and prostatic fluid (madhy) comes out: what is required of him? Ali said: “The daughter of the Messenger of God ﷺ is with me, so I am shy to ask him.” Al-Miqdad said: I asked the Messenger of God ﷺ about that, and he said: “If one of you finds that, let him sprinkle his private part with water and perform ablution as he does for prayer.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ أَبِي النَّضْرِ مَوْلَى عُمَرَ بْنِ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ يَسَارٍ، عَنِ الْمِقْدَادِ بْنِ الْأَسْوَدِ،

Matn

أَنَّ عَلِيَّ بْنَ أَبِي طَالِبٍ رضي الله عنه أَمَرَهُ أَنْ يَسْأَلَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنِ الرَّجُلِ إِذَا دَنَا مِنْ أَهْلِهِ فَخَرَجَ مِنْهُ الْمَذْيُ، مَاذَا عَلَيْهِ؟ قَالَ عَلِيٌّ: فَإِنَّ عِنْدِي ابْنَةَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَأَنَا أَسْتَحْيِي أَنْ أَسْأَلَهُ. قَالَ الْمِقْدَادُ: فَسَأَلْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ ذَلِكَ، فَقَالَ: «إِذَا وَجَدَ أَحَدُكُمْ ذَلِكَ فَلْيَنْضَحْ فَرْجَهُ، وَلْيَتَوَضَّأْ وُضُوءَهُ لِلصَّلَاةِ»

Isnad

Malik informed us, from Abdullah b. Abi Bakr b. Muhammad b. Amr b. Hazm, that he heard Urwah b. al-Zubayr say:

Matn

I visited Marwan b. al-Hakam and we discussed what requires ablution. Marwan said: “And from touching the male organ, ablution is required.” Urwah said: “I did not know that.” Marwan said: Busrah bint Safwan informed me that she heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: “If one of you touches his male organ, let him perform ablution.”

Isnad

Matn

دَخَلْتُ عَلَى مَرْوَانَ بْنِ الْحَكَمِ فَتَذَاكَرْنَا مَا يَكُونُ مِنْهُ الْوُضُوءُ، فَقَالَ مَرْوَانُ: وَمِنْ مَسِّ الذَّكَرِ الْوُضُوءُ، فَقَالَ عُرْوَةُ: مَا عَلِمْتُ ذَلِكَ، فَقَالَ مَرْوَانُ: أَخْبَرَتْنِي بُسْرَةُ بِنْتُ صَفْوَانَ أَنَّهَا سَمِعَتْ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: «إِذَا مَسَّ أَحَدُكُمْ ذَكَرَهُ فَلْيَتَوَضَّأْ»

Matn

We had not waited long when the Prophet ﷺ came and said: “Have you eaten anything? Has anything been ordered for you?” We said: Yes. Soon the shepherd drove in his flock, and there was a lamb bleating. He said: “Hey, so-and-so, what has she given birth to?” He said: A female lamb. He said: “Then slaughter a sheep in its place for us.” Then he turned to me and said to me: “Do not think,” and he did not say “do not suppose,” “that we slaughtered it on your account. We have a hundred sheep and do not wish it to increase; so whenever the shepherd has a lamb born, we slaughter a sheep in its place.” I said: O Messenger of God, I have a wife with something in her tongue, meaning foul speech. He said: “Then divorce her.” I said: I have a child by her, and she has been my companion. He said: “Then command her,” meaning: admonish her, “and if there is good in her, she will accept. Isnad

Ibn Uyaynah informed us, from al-Zuhri, from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, from Abu Hurayrah, who said ⦗p. 21⦘:

Matn

A Bedouin entered the mosque and said: “O God, have mercy on me and Muhammad and do not have mercy with us on anyone else.” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “You have narrowed down something vast.” He said: It was not long before he urinated in a corner of the mosque, and the people hurried toward him, but the Prophet ﷺ stopped them, then ordered a large bucket of water, or a pail of water, and it was poured over it. Then the Prophet ﷺ said: “Teach, make things easy, and do not make things difficult.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا ابْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ الْمُسَيِّبِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ ⦗ص: 21⦘ :

Matn

دَخَلَ أَعْرَابِيٌّ الْمَسْجِدَ فَقَالَ: اللَّهُمَّ ارْحَمْنِي وَمُحَمَّدًا وَلَا تَرْحَمْ مَعَنَا أَحَدًا. فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لَقَدْ تَحَجَّرْتَ وَاسِعًا». قَالَ: فَمَا لَبِثَ أَنْ بَالَ فِي نَاحِيَةِ الْمَسْجِدِ، فَكَأَنَّهُمْ عَجِلُوا عَلَيْهِ، فَنَهَاهُمُ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم، ثُمَّ أَمَرَ بِذَنُوبٍ مِنْ مَاءٍ، أَوْ سَجْلٍ مِنْ مَاءٍ، فَأُهَرِيقَ عَلَيْهِ، ثُمَّ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «عَلِّمُوا، وَيَسِّرُوا وَلَا تُعَسِّرُوا»

Matn
