English
Isnad

'Abdullah b. al-Harith b. 'Abd al-Malik al-Makhzumi informed us, from Sayf b. Sulayman al-Makki, from Qays b. Sa'd, from 'Amr b. Dinar, from Ibn 'Abbas ﵄, that

Matn

the Messenger of God ﷺ gave judgment with the oath alongside the witness. 'Umar said: And in cases concerning property.

Isnad

'Abd al-'Aziz b. Muhammad informed us, from Rabi'a b. 'Abd al-Rahman, from Suhayl b. Abi Salih, from his father, from Abu Hurayra ﵁, that

Matn

the Messenger of God ﷺ gave judgment with the oath alongside the witness. 'Abd al-'Aziz said: “I mentioned that to Suhayl, and he said: Rabi'a informed me—and in my view he is trustworthy—that I had narrated it to him, though I do not remember it. 'Abd al-'Aziz said: Suhayl had been afflicted by an illness that took away part of his memory, and he forgot some of his hadith; afterwards Suhayl used to narrate it from Rabi'a, from himself, from his father.”

Matn

أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَضَى بِالْيَمِينِ مَعَ الشَّاهِدِ قَالَ عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ: " فَذَكَرْتُ ذَلِكَ لِسُهَيْلٍ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي رَبِيعَةُ، وَهُوَ عِنْدِي ثِقَةٌ، أَنِّي حَدَّثَتْهُ إِيَّاهُ وَلَا أَحْفَظُهُ. قَالَ عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ: وَقَدْ كَانَ أَصَابَ سُهَيْلًا عِلَّةٌ أَذْهَبَتْ بَعْضَ حِفْظِهِ، وَنَسِيَ بَعْضَ حَدِيثِهِ، وَكَانَ سُهَيْلٌ بَعْدُ يُحَدِّثُهُ عَنْ رَبِيعَةَ عَنْهُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ "

English
Isnad

Malik informed us, from Hisham b. 'Urwa, from his father, from Zaynab bint Abi Salama, from Umm Salama, the wife of the Prophet ﷺ, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“I am only a human being, and you bring your disputes to me. Perhaps some of you are more eloquent in his argument than others, so that I give judgment for him in accordance with [p. 151] what I hear from him. So whoever I judge for with something of his brother's right, let him not take it, for I am only cutting off for him a piece of the Fire.”

العربية
Isnad

Matn
