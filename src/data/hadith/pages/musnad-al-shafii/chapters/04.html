Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 52
English
Isnad

Malik informed us, from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayrah ﵁, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“By Him in whose hand is my soul, I have considered commanding that firewood be gathered, then commanding the prayer to be called, then commanding a man to lead the people, then going over to men [who stay behind] and burning their houses down on them. By Him in whose hand is my soul, if one of you knew that he would find a fat bone or two fine sheep's hooves, he would attend the night prayer.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ أَبِي الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الْأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رضي الله عنه، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ:

Matn

«وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ، لَقَدْ هَمَمْتُ أَنْ آمُرَ بِحَطَبٍ فَيحْتَطَبُ، ثُمَّ آمُرَ بِالصَّلَاةِ فَيُؤَذَّنَ بِهَا، ثُمَّ آمُرَ رَجُلًا فَيَؤُمَّ النَّاسَ، ثُمَّ أُخَالِفَ إِلَى رِجَالٍ فَأُحَرِّقَ عَلَيْهِمْ بُيُوتَهُمْ، وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ، لَوْ يَعْلَمُ أَحَدُكُمْ أَنَّهُ يَجِدُ عَظْمًا سَمِينًا أَوْ مِرْمَاتَيْنِ حَسَنَتَيْنِ لَشَهِدَ الْعِشَاءَ»

Matn

أَنَّ عِتْبَانَ بْنَ مَالِكٍ، كَانَ يَؤُمُّ قَوْمَهُ وَهُوَ أَعْمَى، وَأَنَّهُ قَالَ لِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: إِنَّهَا تَكُونُ الظُّلْمَةُ وَالْمَطَرُ وَالسَّيْلُ وَأَنَا رَجُلٌ ضَرِيرُ الْبَصَرِ، فَصَلِّ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ فِي بَيْتِي مَكَانًا أَتَّخِذُهُ مُصَلًّى، فَجَاءَهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ: «أَيْنَ تُحِبُّ أَنْ تُصَلِّيَ؟» فَأَشَارَ إِلَى مَكَانٍ مِنَ الْبَيْتِ، فَصَلَّى فِيهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ

Isnad

Malik informed us, from Abu Hazim b. Dinar, from Sahl b. Sa'd al-Sa'idi ﵁, that

Matn

the Messenger of God ﷺ went to Banu Amr b. Awf to reconcile them and the time for prayer came. The muezzin came to Abu Bakr ﵁ and said: “Will you lead the people in prayer so that I may give the iqamah?” He said: “Yes.” Abu Bakr prayed, then the Messenger of God ﷺ arrived while the people were in prayer. He made his way through until he stood in the row. The people clapped. He said: Abu Bakr never turned around in his prayer, but when the people clapped repeatedly he turned and saw the Messenger of God ﷺ, who signalled to him to stay in his place. Abu Bakr raised his hands and praised God for what the Messenger of God ﷺ ⦗p. 55⦘ had commanded him in that, then Abu Bakr stepped back and the Messenger of God ﷺ stepped forward and led the people in prayer. When he finished he said: “O Abu Bakr, what prevented you from staying firm when I commanded you?” Abu Bakr said: “It was not for the son of Abu Quhafah to pray in front of the Messenger of God ﷺ.” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “What is the matter that I saw you clapping so much? Whoever encounters something in his prayer, let him say ‘Subhan Allah,’ for when he says it, attention is turned to him; clapping is only for women.” Abu al-Abbas, meaning al-Asamm, said: I included this hadith in this place though it is a repeat, except that its wording differs, with additions and omissions.
