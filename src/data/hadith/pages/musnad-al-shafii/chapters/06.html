English
Isnad

Ibrahim b. Muhammad informed us: Abdullah b. Ata' b. Ibrahim, freedman of Safiyyah bint Abd al-Muttalib, narrated to me, from Urwah b. al-Zubayr, from A'ishah ﵂, from the Prophet ﷺ, that he said:

Matn

“The Fitr is the day you break your fast, and the Adha is the day you sacrifice.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عَطَاءِ بْنِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، مَوْلَى صَفِيَّةَ بِنْتِ عَبْدِ الْمُطَّلِبِ عَنْ عُرْوَةَ بْنِ الزُّبَيْرِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنَّهُ قَالَ:

Matn

«الْفِطْرُ يَوْمَ تُفْطِرُونَ، وَالْأَضْحَى يَوْمَ تُضَحُّونَ»

Isnad

Ibrahim b. Muhammad informed us: Ubaydullah b. Umar informed me, from Nafi', from Ibn Umar, that

Matn

he “used to go out to the prayer ground on the day of Fitr when the sun had risen, saying the takbir until he reached the prayer ground on the day of Eid, and then saying the takbir at the prayer ground, until, when the imam sat, he stopped the takbir.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، أَخْبَرَنِي عُبَيْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عُمَرَ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ،

Matn

أَنَّهُ «كَانَ يَغْدُو إِلَى الْمُصَلَّى يَوْمَ الْفِطْرِ إِذَا طَلَعَتِ الشَّمْسُ فَيُكَبِّرُ حَتَّى يَأْتِيَ الْمُصَلَّى يَوْمَ الْعِيدِ ثُمَّ يُكَبِّرَ بِالْمُصَلَّى، حَتَّى إِذَا جَلَسَ الْإِمَامُ تَرَكَ التَّكْبِيرَ»

Ibrahim b. Muhammad informed us: Adi b. Thabit informed me, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, from Ibn Abbas ﵄, who said:

Isnad

Malik informed us, from Zayd b. Aslam, from Ata' b. Yasar, from Abdullah b. Abbas ﵄, who said:

Matn

The sun was eclipsed, and the Messenger of God ﷺ prayed and the people with him. He stood for a long time, about [the length of] Surah al-Baqarah, he said. Then he bowed for a long time, then rose and stood for a long time, though less than the first standing; then he bowed for a long time, though less than the first bowing; then ⦗p. 78⦘ he rose and stood for a long time, though less than the first standing; then he bowed for a long time, though less than the first bowing; then he prostrated and finished, and the sun had cleared. He said: “The sun and the moon are two signs among the signs of God; they are not eclipsed for the death or life of anyone. When you see that, remember God.” They said: “O Messenger of God, we saw you reach out for something in your place, then we saw you draw back.” He said: “I saw, or I was shown, Paradise, and I reached for a cluster from it; had I taken it, you would have eaten from it for as long as the world endures. And I saw, or I was shown, the Fire, and I have never seen a sight like today's; and I saw that most of its people were women.” They said: “Why, O Messenger of God?” He said: “Because of their ingratitude.” It was said: “Do they disbelieve in God?” He said: “They are ungrateful to the spouse and ungrateful for kindness; if you were good to one of them for all time, then she saw something [she disliked] from you, she would say: ‘I have never seen any good from you.’”

Matn

“Medina is close to being struck by rain for forty nights such that no house of mud-brick will shelter its people.”
