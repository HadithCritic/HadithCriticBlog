Report1175/ 11
Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 260
English
Isnad

Ibn 'Uyayna informed us, from al-Zuhri, from Ibn al-Musayyab, that

Matn

the daughter of Muhammad b. Maslama was married to Rafi' b. Khadij, and he disliked something in her, either her age or something else, and wished to divorce her. She said: “Do not divorce me; keep me, and apportion to me as you see fit.” So God Almighty revealed: {And if a woman fears from her husband ill conduct (nushuz)} [al-Nisa': 128], to the end of the verse.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا ابْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنِ ابْنِ الْمُسَيِّبِ،

Matn

أَنَّ بِنْتَ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ مَسْلَمَةَ، كَانَتْ عِنْدَ رَافِعِ بْنِ خَدِيجٍ فَكَرِهَ مِنْهَا أَمْرًا، إِمَّا كِبْرًا أَوْ غَيْرَهُ، فَأَرَادَ طَلَاقَهَا فَقَالَتْ: «لَا تُطَلِّقْنِي، وَأَمْسِكْنِي، وَاقْسِمْ لِي مَا بَدَا لَكَ»، فَأَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ عز وجل: {وَإِنِ امْرَأَةٌ خَافَتْ مِنْ بَعْلِهَا نُشُوزًا} [النِّسَاء: 128] الْآيَةَ

Muslim b. Khalid informed us, from Ibn Jurayj, from 'Ata', from Ibn 'Abbas ﵄, that

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 260
English
Isnad

Malik informed us, from 'Abd Allah b. Abi Bakr b. Muhammad b. 'Amr b. Hazm, from 'Abd al-Malik b. Abi Bakr b. 'Abd al-Rahman, that when the Messenger of God ﷺ married Umm Salama and she woke up in the morning with him, he said:

Matn

“You are not of low standing with your family. If you wish, I will spend seven nights with you and seven with each of them; and if you wish, I will spend three with you and then rotate.” She said: “Three.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي بَكْرِ بْنِ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ عَمْرِو بْنِ حَزْمٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْمَلِكِ بْنِ أَبِي بَكْرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم حِينَ تَزَوَّجَ أُمَّ سَلَمَةَ وَأَصْبَحَتْ عِنْدَهُ قَالَ:

Matn

«لَيْسَ بِكِ عَلَى أَهْلِكِ هَوَانٌ، إِنْ شِئْتِ سَبَّعْتُ عِنْدَكِ وَسَبَّعْتُ عِنْدَهُنَّ، وَإِنْ شِئْتِ ثَلَّثْتُ عِنْدَكِ وَدُرْتُ». قَالَتْ: ثَلِّثْ

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 260
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Majid informed us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Habib b. Abi Thabit, that

Matn

'Abd al-Hamid b. 'Abd Allah b. Abi 'Amr and al-Qasim b. Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. al-Harith b. Hisham [p. 261] informed him that they heard Abu Bakr b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. al-Harith b. Hisham narrate from Umm Salama, who informed him that when she came to Medina she told them she was the daughter of Abu Umayya b. al-Mughira, but they did not believe her and said: “How the strangers lie!” until one of them was preparing for hajj and they said: “Will you write to your family?” She wrote with them and they returned to Medina. She said: “So they believed me, and I rose in their esteem. When I came out of my waiting period, the Messenger of God ﷺ came and proposed to me. I said to him: ‘A woman like me is not married: as for me, I have no child, I am jealous, and I have dependents.’ He said: ‘I am older than you; as for jealousy, God will take it away; and as for dependents, [they are] for God and His Messenger.’” So the Messenger of God ﷺ married her, and he would come to her and say: “Where is Zunab?” until 'Ammar b. Yasir came and took her away, saying: “This one keeps the Messenger of God ﷺ [from his wife],” and she used to nurse her. The Messenger of God ﷺ came and said: “Where is Zunab?” Qariba bint Abi Umayya, who happened to be with her, said: “'Ammar b. Yasir took her.” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “I will come to you tonight.” She said: I got up, put down my grinding mat, took out some grains of barley that were in a jar, and took out some fat, and cooked it into a porridge. She said: The Messenger of God ﷺ spent the night, and in the morning he said: “You have a claim to honor with your family. If you wish, I will spend seven [nights] with you, but if I spend seven I will spend seven with my [other] wives.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الْمَجِيدِ، عَنِ ابْنِ جُرَيْجٍ، عَنْ حَبِيبِ بْنِ أَبِي ثَابِتٍ،

Matn

أَنَّ عَبْدَ الْحَمِيدِ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي عَمْرٍو، وَالْقَاسِمَ بْنَ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ الْحَارِثِ بْنِ هِشَامٍ ⦗ص: 261⦘ ، أَخْبَرَاهُ أَنَّهُمَا، سَمِعَا أَبَا بَكْرِ بْنَ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ الْحَارِثِ بْنِ هِشَامٍ، يُحَدِّثُ عَنْ أُمِّ سَلَمَةَ، أَنَّهَا أَخْبَرَتْهُ أَنَّهَا، لَمَّا قَدِمَتِ الْمَدِينَةَ أَخْبَرْتَهُمْ أَنَّهَا ابْنَةُ أَبِي أُمَيَّةَ بْنِ الْمُغِيرَةِ، فَكَذَّبُوهَا وَقَالُوا: مَا أَكْذَبَ الْغَرَائِبَ، حَتَّى أَنْشَأَ إِنْسَانٌ مِنْهُمُ الْحَجَّ فَقَالُوا: أَتَكْتُبِينَ إِلَى أَهْلِكِ، فَكَتَبَتْ مَعَهُمْ فَرَجَعُوا إِلَى الْمَدِينَةِ، قَالَتْ: فَصَدَّقُونِي وَازْدَدْتُ عَلَيْهِمْ كَرَامَةً، فَلَمَّا حَلَلْتُ جَاءَنِي رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَخَطَبَنِي فَقُلْتُ لَهُ: مَا مَثَلِي نُكِحَ، أَمَّا أَنَا فَلَا وَلَدَ لِي وَأَنَا غَيُورٌ ذَاتُ عِيَالٍ، قَالَ: «أَنَا أَكْبَرُ مِنْكِ، وَأَمَّا الْغَيْرَةُ فَيُذْهِبُهَا اللَّهُ، وَأَمَّا الْعِيَالُ فَإِلَى اللَّهِ وَإِلَى رَسُولِهِ». فَتَزَوَّجَهَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَجَعَلَ يَأْتِيهَا وَيَقُولُ: «أَيْنَ زُنَابُ؟» حَتَّى جَاءَ عَمَّارُ بْنُ يَاسِرٍ فَاخْتَلَجَهَا وَقَالَ: هَذِهِ تَمْنَعُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، وَكَانَتْ تُرْضِعُهَا، فَجَاءَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ: «أَيْنَ زُنَابُ؟» فَقَالَتْ قَرِيبَةُ بِنْتُ أَبِي أُمَيَّةَ وَوَافَقَهَا عِنْدَهَا: أَخَذَهَا عَمَّارُ بْنُ يَاسِرٍ، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «إِنِّي آتِيكُمُ اللَّيْلَةَ». قَالَتْ: فَقُمْتُ فَوَضَعْتُ ثِفَالِي، وَأَخْرَجْتُ حَبَّاتٍ مِنْ شَعِيرٍ كَانَتْ فِي جَرٍّ، وَأَخْرَجْتُ شَحْمًا فَعَصَدْتُهُ، أَوْ صَعَدْتُهُ، قَالَتْ: فَبَاتَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَصْبَحَ فَقَالَ حِينَ أَصْبَحَ: «إِنَّ لَكِ عَلَى أَهْلِكِ كَرَامَةً، فَإِنْ شِئْتِ سَبَّعْتُ لَكِ، وَإِنْ أُسَبِّعْ أُسَبِّعْ لِنِسَائِي»

Isnad

My uncle Muhammad b. 'Ali b. Shafi' informed us, from Ibn Shihab, from 'Ubayd Allah b. 'Abd Allah, from 'A'isha, wife of the Prophet ﷺ, that she said:

Matn

“When the Messenger of God ﷺ intended a journey, he would draw lots among his wives, and whichever's lot came out, he would take her with him.”

Isnad

English
Isnad

Ibn 'Uyayna informed us, from al-Zuhri, from 'Ubayd Allah b. 'Abd Allah b. 'Umar, from Iyas b. 'Abd Allah b. Abi Dhubab, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Do not beat God's female servants.” He said: Then [p. 262] 'Umar b. al-Khattab ﵁ came to him and said: “O Messenger of God, women have become emboldened against their husbands, so permit beating them.” Many women then went around the family of Muhammad, all complaining about their husbands. The Prophet ﷺ said: “Seventy women have gone around the family of Muhammad, all complaining about their husbands; and those [husbands] are not the best of you.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا ابْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ، عَنْ إِيَاسَ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي ذُبَابٍ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم:

Matn

«لَا تَضْرِبُوا إِمَاءَ اللَّهِ». قَالَ: فَأَتَاهُ ⦗ص: 262⦘ عُمَرُ بْنُ الْخَطَّابِ رضي الله عنه فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، ذَئِرَ النِّسَاءُ عَلَى أَزْوَاجِهِنَّ، فَأَذِنِ فِي ضَرْبِهِنَّ، فَأَطَافَ بِآلِ مُحَمَّدٍ نِسَاءٌ كَثِيرٌ كُلُّهُنَّ يَشْكُونَ أَزْوَاجَهُنَّ، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لَقَدْ أَطَافَ بِآلِ مُحَمَّدٍ سَبْعُونَ امْرَأَةً كُلُّهُنَّ يَشْكِينَ أَزْوَاجَهُنَّ، وَلَا تَجِدُونَ أُولَئِكَ خِيَارَكُمْ»

English
Isnad

Al-Thaqafi informed us, from Ayyub, from Ibn Sirin, from 'Abida, that

Matn

he said regarding this verse {And if you fear a breach between the two, send an arbiter from his family and an arbiter from her family} [al-Nisa': 35]: A man and a woman came to 'Ali ﵁, each accompanied by a group of people. 'Ali ﵁ commanded them, and they sent an arbiter from his family and an arbiter from hers. Then he said to the two arbiters: “Do you know what is upon you? Upon you, if you see that you should bring them together, to bring them together; and if you see that you should separate them, to separate them.” He said: The woman said: “I am content with the Book of God, with what it places upon me and for me.” The man said: “As for separation, no.” 'Ali ﵁ said: “You have lied! By God, you will not leave until you acknowledge the same as she acknowledged.”

Isnad

Malik informed us, from Yahya b. Sa'id, from 'Amra, that

Matn

Habiba bint Sahl informed her that she was married to Thabit b. Qays b. Shammas, and that the Messenger of God ﷺ went out to the [p. 263] dawn prayer and found Habiba bint Sahl at his door in the darkness. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Who is this?” She said: “I am Habiba bint Sahl, O Messenger of God.” He said: “What is the matter?” She said: “Not me and not Thabit”—meaning her husband. When Thabit b. Qays came, the Messenger of God ﷺ said to him: “This is Habiba bint Sahl; she has said what God willed her to say.” Habiba said: “O Messenger of God, everything he gave me is with me.” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Take it from her.” And she remained with her family.
