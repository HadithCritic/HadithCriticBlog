English
Isnad

Malik informed us, from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, from the Messenger of God ﷺ, who said:

Matn

“When the heat becomes intense, delay the prayer until it cools, for the intensity of the heat is from the breath of Hell.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ أَبِي الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الْأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ:

Matn

«إِذَا اشْتَدَّ الْحَرُّ فَأَبْرِدُوا عَنِ الصَّلَاةِ؛ فَإِنَّ شِدَّةَ الْحَرِّ مِنْ فَيْحِ جَهَنَّمَ»

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 211
English
Isnad

Malik informed us, from Ibn Shihab, from al-A'raj, from 'Abd Allah b. Buhayna, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ led us in two rak'as of prayer for God, then stood up without sitting, and the people stood with him. When he had completed his prayer and we were awaiting his salutation, he said the takbir and performed two prostrations while sitting, before the salutation.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنِ الْأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ بُحَيْنَةَ قَالَ:

Matn

صَلَّى لَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم لِلَّهِ رَكْعَتَيْنِ، ثُمَّ قَامَ فَلَمْ يَجْلِسْ وَقَامَ النَّاسُ مَعَهُ، فَلَمَّا قَضَى صَلَاتَهُ وَنَظَرْنَا تَسْلِيمَهُ كَبَّرَ فَسَجَدَ سَجْدَتَيْنِ وَهُوَ جَالِسٌ قَبْلَ التَّسْلِيمِ

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 211
English
Isnad

Malik informed us, from Hisham b. 'Urwa, from his father, from 'A'isha ﵂, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ prayed in my house while he was ill, so he prayed sitting, and some people prayed behind him standing. He gestured to them to sit. When he finished he said: “The imam is appointed only to be followed. When he bows, bow; when he rises, rise; and when he prays sitting, pray sitting.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ هِشَامِ بْنِ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها قَالَتْ:

Matn

صَلَّى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي بَيْتِي وَهُوَ شَاكٍ فَصَلَّى جَالِسًا وَصَلَّى خَلْفَهُ قَوْمٌ قِيَامًا، فَأَشَارَ إِلَيْهِمْ أَنِ اجْلِسُوا، فَلَمَّا انْصَرَفَ قَالَ: «إِنَّمَا جُعِلَ الْإِمَامُ لِيُؤْتَمَّ بِهِ، فَإِذَا رَكَعَ فَارْكَعُوا، أَوْ إِذَا رَفَعَ فَارْفَعُوا، أَوْ إِذَا صَلَّى جَالِسًا فَصَلُّوا جُلُوسًا»

English
Isnad

Malik informed us, from Hisham b. 'Urwa, from his father, that

Matn

the Messenger of God ﷺ came out during his illness and went to Abu Bakr, who was standing leading the people in prayer. Abu Bakr stepped back, and the Messenger of God ﷺ gestured to him to stay as he was. The Messenger of God ﷺ sat beside Abu Bakr; Abu Bakr prayed following the prayer of the Prophet ﷺ, and the people prayed following the prayer of Abu Bakr. [p. 212] The trustworthy Yahya b. Hassan informed us, from Hammad b. Salama, from Hisham b. 'Urwa, from his father, from 'A'isha ﵂, with the same meaning, not contradicting it but clearer, and he said: Abu Bakr prayed standing beside him. The trustworthy one informed us—and in the other sources—from Yahya b. Sa'id, from Ibn Abi Mulayka, from 'Ubayd b. 'Umayr, who said: The trustworthy one informed me, apparently meaning 'A'isha ﵂, then he described the prayer of the Prophet ﷺ with Abu Bakr at his side, with the same meaning as the report of Hisham b. 'Urwa from his father.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ هِشَامِ بْنِ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ،

Matn

أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم خَرَجَ فِي مَرَضِهِ فَأَتَى أَبَا بَكْرٍ وَهُوَ قَائِمٌ يُصَلِّي بِالنَّاسِ، فَاسْتَأْخَرَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ، فَأَشَارَ إِلَيْهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنْ كَمَا أَنْتَ، فَجَلَسَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلَى جَنْبِ أَبِي بَكْرٍ، فَكَانَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ يُصَلِّي بِصَلَاةِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم، وَكَانَ النَّاسُ يُصَلُّونَ بِصَلَاةِ أَبِي بَكْرٍ ⦗ص: 212⦘ أَخْبَرَنَا الثِّقَةُ يَحْيَى بْنُ حَسَّانَ عَنْ حَمَّادِ بْنِ سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ هِشَامِ بْنِ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها بِمِثْلِ مَعْنَاهُ، لَا يُخَالِفُهُ، وَأَوْضَحَ مِنْهُ، وَقَالَ: صَلَّى أَبُو بَكْرٍ إِلَى جَنْبِهِ قَائِمًا أَخْبَرَنَا الثِّقَةُ، وَفِي سَائِرِ الْأُصُولِ عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ سَعِيدٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي مُلَيْكَةَ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ بْنِ عُمَيْرٍ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي الثِّقَةُ، كَأَنَّهُ يَعْنِي عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها، ثُمَّ ذَكَرَ صَلَاةَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَبُو بَكْرٍ إِلَى جَانِبِهِ بِمِثْلِ مَعْنَى حَدِيثِ هِشَامِ بْنِ عُرْوَةَ عَنْ أَبِيهِ

English
Isnad

Malik informed us, from Ibn Shihab, from Ibn al-Musayyab and Abu Salama, who both informed him from Abu Hurayra ﵁, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“When the imam says amin, say amin; for whoever's amin coincides with the amin of the angels will have his previous sins forgiven.” Ibn Shihab said: And the Messenger of God ﷺ used to say: “Amin.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ الْمُسَيِّبِ، وَأَبِي سَلَمَةَ أَنَّهُمَا أَخْبَرَاهُ عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رضي الله عنه، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ:

Matn

«إِذَا أَمَّنَ الْإِمَامُ فَأَمِّنُوا؛ فَإِنَّهُ مَنْ وَافَقَ تَأْمِينُهُ تَأْمِينَ الْمَلَائِكَةِ غُفِرَ لَهُ مَا تَقَدَّمَ مِنْ ذَنْبِهِ» قَالَ ابْنُ شِهَابٍ: وَكَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: «آمِينَ»

Isnad

Malik informed us, from 'Abd Allah b. al-Fadl, from Nafi' b. Jubayr, from Ibn 'Abbas ﵄, that the Prophet ﷺ said:

Matn

“A woman without a husband (ayyim) has more right over herself than her guardian, and a virgin is asked for her permission concerning herself, and her permission is her silence.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ الْفَضْلِ، عَنْ نَافِعِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رضي الله عنهما، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ:

Matn

«الْأَيِّمُ أَحَقُّ بِنَفْسِهَا مِنْ وَلِيِّهَا، وَالْبِكْرُ تُسْتَأْذَنُ فِي نَفْسِهَا، وَإِذْنُهَا صُمَاتُهَا»

Isnad

Malik informed us, from Yahya b. Sa'id, from 'Amr b. Kathir b. Aflah, from Abu Muhammad, client of Abu Qatada, from Abu Qatada al-Ansari ﵁, who said:

Matn

We went out with the Messenger of God ﷺ in the year of Hunayn. When we met [the enemy], the Muslims had a rout. I saw a man of the polytheists who had overpowered a man of the Muslims. I circled around until I came to him from behind and struck him on the juncture of his neck and shoulder. He turned on me and gripped me in an embrace in which I felt the scent of death; then death overtook him and he let me go. I caught up with 'Umar b. al-Khattab ﵁ and said to him: “What is the matter with the people?” He said: “God's command.” Then the people returned, and the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Whoever has killed a slain man and has proof of it, his spoils are his.” I stood and said: “Who will testify for me?” Then I sat down. He said it a second time; I stood and said: “Who will testify for me?” and sat. He said it a third time, and I stood the third time. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “What is it with you, Abu Qatada?” I related the story to him. A man from the group said: “He has spoken truly, O Messenger of God, and the spoils of that slain man are with me; so satisfy him from them.” Abu Bakr said: “No, by God! He would not go to one of God's lions who fights on behalf of God and give you his spoils.” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “He has spoken truly; give it to him.” Abu Qatada said: He gave it to me, and I sold the armor and bought with it a date orchard among Banu Salima; it was the first property I acquired in Islam. Malik ﵁ said: The mikhraf is a palm grove.

Zaynab bint Abi Salama said: “Al-Zubayr used to enter upon me while I was combing my hair; he would take hold of a lock of my hair and say: ‘Turn to me and talk to me,’ regarding himself as my father, and his children as my brothers. Then, before [the battle of] al-Harra, 'Abd Allah b. al-Zubayr sent to me and asked for my daughter Umm Kulthum in marriage for Hamza b. al-Zubayr—Hamza being the son of the Kalbi woman. I said to his messenger: ‘Is she lawful for him? She is the daughter of his [foster-]sister.’ [p. 231] 'Abd Allah sent back to me: ‘You only want to prevent this because of what you have in mind; he is no brother to you. I and whatever Asma' bore are your brothers, and whatever of al-Zubayr's children were not from Asma' are not brothers to you. Send and ask about this.’ So I sent and asked, while the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ and the Mothers of the Believers were present in numbers, and they said to her: ‘Fosterage through the man does not make anything unlawful.’ So I gave her in marriage to him, and she remained with him until he died.”
