English
Isnad

Sufyan b. Uyaynah informed us: I heard Jami' b. Abi Rashid and Abd al-Malik b. A'yan, who both heard Abu Wa'il report from Abdullah b. Mas'ud ﵁, saying: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say:

Matn

“There is no man who does not pay the zakah on his wealth except that on the Day of Resurrection it will be made for him into a bald-headed snake, from which he flees while it pursues him until it is hung around his neck.” Then the Messenger of God ﷺ recited to us: “They will be collared with what they withheld through miserliness on the Day of Resurrection” [Al Imran: 180].

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ بْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، سَمِعْتُ جَامِعَ بْنَ أَبِي رَاشِدٍ، وَعَبْدَ الْمَلِكِ بْنَ أَعْيَنَ، سَمِعَا أَبَا وَائِلٍ، يُخْبِرُ عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رضي الله عنه يَقُولُ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ:

Matn

«مَا مِنْ رَجُلٍ لَا يُؤَدِّي زَكَاةَ مَالِهِ إِلَّا مُثِّلَ لَهُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ شُجَاعًا أَقْرَعَ يَفِرُّ مِنْهُ وَهُوَ يَتْبَعُهُ حَتَّى يُطَوَّقَهُ فِي عُنُقِهِ». ثُمَّ قَرَأَ عَلَيْنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: {سَيُطَوَّقُونَ مَا بَخِلُوا بِهِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ} [آل عمرَان: 180]

Ibn Uyaynah informed us, from Ibn Ajlan, from Nafi', that Ibn Umar ﵄ used to say:

Matn

“Any wealth whose zakah is paid is not a hoarded treasure, even if it is buried, and any wealth whose zakah is not paid is a hoarded treasure, even if it is not buried.”

Matn

كُلُّ مَالٍ تُؤَدِّي زَكَاتَهُ فَلَيْسَ بِكَنْزٍ وَإِنْ كَانَ مَدْفُونًا، وَكُلُّ مَالٍ لَا تُؤَدَّى زَكَاتَهُ فَهُوَ كَنْزٌ وَإِنْ لَمْ يَكُنْ مَدْفُونًا

English
Isnad

Al-Qasim b. Abdullah informed us, from al-Muthanna b. Anas, or Ibn so-and-so, or Ibn so-and-so b. Anas — al-Shafi'i was in doubt — from Anas, who said:

Matn

“This is the charity [ruling]; then the sheep and other [livestock] were left aside and the people disliked it. In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful: This is the obligation of charity that the Messenger of God ﷺ made obligatory upon the Muslims, which God commanded. Whoever among the believers is asked for it in its proper manner, let him give it, and whoever is asked for more than that, let him not give it. For twenty-four camels or fewer, [the zakah is] sheep: one sheep for every five. When they reach twenty-five up to thirty-five, then a bint makhad [a female in her second year]; if there is no bint makhad, then a male b. labun [in his third year]. When they reach thirty-six up to forty-five, then a bint labun, a female [in her third year]. When they reach forty-six up to sixty, then a hiqqah [a female in her fourth year] fit to be covered by the male. When they reach sixty-one up to seventy-five, then a jadha'ah [a female in her fifth year]. When they reach seventy-six up to ninety, then two bint labun. When they reach ninety-one up to one hundred and twenty, then two hiqqahs fit to be covered by the male. When they exceed one hundred and twenty, then for every forty a bint labun and for every fifty a hiqqah. There is compensation between the ages of camels in the obligation of charity: whoever's camels reach the charity of a jadha'ah and he has no jadha'ah but has a hiqqah, the hiqqah is accepted from him and he adds with it two sheep, if they are easily available to him, or twenty dirhams. If a hiqqah ⦗p. 89⦘ is due from him and he has no hiqqah but has a jadha'ah, the jadha'ah is accepted from him and the collector gives him twenty dirhams or two sheep.” A number of trustworthy [narrators], all of them, informed me, from Hammad b. Salamah, from Thumamah b. Abdullah b. Anas, from Anas b. Malik ﵁, from the Prophet ﷺ, with the same meaning as this, not differing from it, except that I remember in it: “and he is not given two sheep or twenty dirhams”; I do not remember [the phrase] “if they are easily available to him.” He said: And I think that in the hadith of Hammad from Anas he said: Abu Bakr handed me the Book of Charity from the Messenger of God ﷺ, and he mentioned this meaning as I have described.

Isnad

" هَذِهِ الصَّدَقَةُ، ثُمَّ تُرِكَتِ الْغَنَمُ وَغَيْرُهَا وَكَرِهَهَا النَّاسُ. بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ: هَذِهِ فَرِيضَةُ الصَّدَقَةِ الَّتِي فَرَضَهَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه Above that, up to sixty, a hiqqah fit to be covered by the stud. Above that, up to seventy-five, a jadha'ah. Above that, up to ninety, two bint labun. Above that, up to one hundred and twenty, two hiqqahs fit to be covered by the stud. Whatever exceeds that, for every forty a bint labun and for every fifty a hiqqah. Isnad

Anas b. Iyad informed us, from Dawud b. Qays, that he heard Iyad b. Abdullah b. Sa'd say

Matn

that Abu Sa'id al-Khudri said: “In the time of the Prophet ﷺ we used to give a sa' of food, or a sa' of raisins, or a sa' of aqit, or a sa' of dates, or a sa' of barley, and we continued to give it thus until Mu'awiyah came for Hajj or Umrah and addressed the people, and among what he said to the people was: ‘I see that two mudds of Syrian wheat (samra') equal a sa' of dates.’ So the people adopted that.” And I have included all of these reports, even though their chains of transmission are repeated, because they are in different wording, and they contain additions and omissions.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا أَنَسُ بْنُ عِيَاضٍ، عَنْ دَاوُدَ بْنِ قَيْسٍ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ عِيَاضَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ، يَقُولُ: أَنَّ أَبَا سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيَّ، قَالَ:

Matn

كُنَّا نُخْرِجُ فِي زَمَانِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم صَاعًا مِنْ طَعَامٍ، أَوْ صَاعًا مِنْ زَبِيبٍ، أَوْ صَاعًا مِنْ أَقِطٍ، أَوْ صَاعًا مِنْ تَمْرٍ، أَوْ صَاعًا مِنْ شَعِيرٍ، فَلَمْ نَزَلْ نُخْرِجُهُ كَذَلِكَ حَتَّى قَدِمَ مُعَاوِيَةَ حَاجًّا أَوْ مُعْتَمِرًا فَخَطَبَ النَّاسَ، فَكَانَ فِيمَا كَلَّمَ النَّاسَ بِهِ أَنْ قَالَ: «إِنِّي أَرَى مُدَّيْنِ مِنْ سَمْرَاءِ الشَّامِ تَعْدِلُ صَاعًا مِنْ تَمْرٍ» فَأَخَذَ النَّاسُ بِذَلِكَ وَإِنَّمَا أَخْرَجْتُ هَذِهِ الْأَخْبَارَ كُلَّهَا وَإِنْ كَانَتْ مُعَادَةَ الْأَسَانِيدِ لِأَنَّهَا بِلَفْظٍ آخَرَ، وَفِيهَا زِيَادَةٌ وَنُقْصَانٌ

“By the One in whose hand is my soul, there is no servant who gives charity from good earnings — and God accepts only what is good, and nothing but what is good ascends to heaven — except that it is as though he places it in the hand of the Most Merciful, who raises it for him as one of you raises his foal, until a morsel will come on the Day of Resurrection like a great mountain.” Then he recited: “Do they not know that it is God Who accepts repentance from His servants and takes the charities?” [At-Tawbah: 104].

Matn

«وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ، مَا مِنْ عَبْدٍ يَتَصَدَّقُ بِصَدَقَةٍ مِنْ كَسْبٍ طَيِّبٍ، وَلَا يَقْبَلُ اللَّهُ إِلَّا طَيِّبًا، وَلَا يَصْعَدُ إِلَى السَّمَاءِ إِلَّا طَيِّبٌ، إِلَّا كَأَنَّمَا يَضَعُهَا فِي يَدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، فَيُرَبِّيهَا لَهُ كَمَا يُرَبِّي أَحَدُكُمْ فَلُوَّهُ، حَتَّى أَنَّ اللُّقْمَةَ لَتَأْتِي يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ وَإِنَّهَا لِمِثْلُ الْجَبَلِ الْعَظِيمِ». ثُمَّ قَرَأَ: {أَنَّ اللَّهَ هُوَ يَقْبَلُ التَّوْبَةَ عَنْ عِبَادِهِ وَيَأْخُذُ الصَّدَقَاتِ} [التَّوْبَة: 104]

Isnad

Sufyan informed us, from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayrah ﵁, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“The likeness of the one who spends and the miser is that of two men wearing two coats of mail — or two cloaks — from their breasts to their collarbones. When the one who spends wishes to spend, the coat extends over him, or passes down, until it covers his fingertips and effaces his tracks; and when the miser wishes to spend, it contracts and every ring clings to its place until it grips his neck or his collarbone, and he tries to widen it but it does not widen.” Sufyan informed us, from Ibn Jurayj, from al-Hasan b. Muslim, from Tawus, from Abu Hurayrah, from the Prophet ﷺ, the like of it, except that he said: “and he tries to widen it but it does not expand.”

Isnad

Sufyan informed us, from Hisham b. Urwah, from his father, from his mother Asma bint Abi Bakr, who said:

Matn

My mother came to me, wanting [my help], in the time of the Quraysh treaty, so I asked the Messenger of God ﷺ: “Shall I maintain ties with her?” He said: “Yes.”
