English
Isnad

Ibrahim b. Yahya informed us, Safwan b. Sulaym narrated to me, from Nafi' b. Jubayr b. Mut'im and 'Ata' b. Yasar, from the Prophet ﷺ: that he said:

Matn

“The witness (shahid) is the day of Friday, and the witnessed (mashhud) is the day of 'Arafa.” Ibrahim b. Muhammad informed us, Sharik b. 'Abd Allah b. Abi Namir narrated to me, from 'Ata' b. Yasar, from the Prophet ﷺ, the like of it. Ibrahim b. Muhammad informed us, 'Abd al-Rahman b. Harmala narrated to me, from Ibn al-Musayyab, from the Prophet ﷺ, the like of it.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ يَحْيَى، حَدَّثَنِي صَفْوَانُ بْنُ سُلَيْمٍ، عَنْ نَافِعِ بْنِ جُبَيْرِ بْنِ مُطْعِمٍ، وَعَطَاءِ بْنِ يَسَارٍ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنَّهُ قَالَ:

Matn

" شَاهِدٌ: يَوْمُ الْجُمُعَةِ، وَمَشْهُودٌ: يَوْمُ عَرَفَةَ " أَخْبَرَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنِي شَرِيكُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي نَمِرٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ يَسَارٍ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِثْلَهُ أَخْبَرَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ حَرْمَلَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ الْمُسَيِّبِ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِثْلَهُ

English
Isnad

Ibn 'Uyayna informed us, from 'Abd Allah b. Tawus, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“We are the last, and we are the foremost; except (bayda anna-hum) that they were given the Book before us and we were given it after them. This is the day about which they differed, and God guided us to it; so the people follow us in it: the Jews tomorrow, and the Christians the day after tomorrow.” Sufyan informed us, from Abu l-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, from the Prophet ﷺ [p. 61] the like of it, except that he said: “bayda annahum.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا ابْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ طَاوُسٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم:

Matn

«نَحْنُ الْآخِرُونَ، وَنَحْنُ السَّابِقُونَ بَيْدَ أَنَّهُمْ أُوتُوا الْكِتَابَ مِنْ قَبْلِنَا وَأُوتِينَاهُ مِنْ بَعْدِهِمْ، فَهَذَا الْيَوْمُ الَّذِي اخْتَلَفُوا فِيهِ فَهَدَانَا اللَّهُ، فَالنَّاسُ لَنَا تَبَعٌ، الْيَهُودُ غَدًا، وَالنَّصَارَى بَعْدَ غَدٍ» أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ أَبِي الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الْأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ⦗ص: 61⦘ مِثْلَهُ، إِلَّا أَنَّهُ قَالَ: «بَايْدَ أَنَّهُمْ»

English
Isnad

Ibrahim b. Muhammad informed us, Muhammad b. 'Amr b. 'Alqama narrated to me, from Abu Salama, from Abu Hurayra, from the Prophet ﷺ, who said:

Matn

“We are the last, the foremost on the Day of Resurrection; except that they were given the Book before us and we were given it after them. Then this is their day which was made obligatory upon them” — meaning Friday — “and they differed about it, and God guided us to it; so the people follow us in it: the Sabbath [Saturday] and Sunday.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَمْرِو بْنِ عَلْقَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ:

Matn

«نَحْنُ الْآخِرُونَ السَّابِقُونَ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ بَيْدَ أَنَّهُمْ أُوتُوا الْكِتَابَ مِنْ قَبْلِنَا وَأُوتِينَاهُ مِنْ بَعْدِهِمْ، ثُمَّ هَذَا يَوْمُهُمُ الَّذِي فُرِضَ عَلَيْهِمْ، يَعْنِي الْجُمُعَةَ، فَاخْتَلَفُوا فِيهِ، فَهَدَانَا اللَّهُ لَهُ، فَالنَّاسُ لَنَا فِيهِ تَبَعٌ، السَّبْتُ وَالْأَحَدُ»

English
Isnad

The trustworthy informant informed us, from al-Zuhri, from al-Sa'ib b. Yazid: that

Matn

the call to prayer (adhan) for Friday was originally given when the imam sat on the minbar, in the time of the Messenger of God ﷺ, Abu Bakr and 'Umar; then when it was [p. 62] the caliphate of 'Uthman and the people had become numerous, 'Uthman ordered a second adhan, and it was called, and the matter remained so. 'Ata' used to deny that 'Uthman had introduced it and would say: “Mu'awiya introduced it.” And God knows best.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا الثِّقَةُ عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنِ السَّائِبِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ،

Matn

أَنَّ الْأَذَانَ، كَانَ أَوَّلُهُ لِلْجُمُعَةِ حِينَ يَجْلِسُ الْإِمَامُ عَلَى الْمِنْبَرِ عَلَى عَهْدِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَبِي بَكْرٍ وَعُمَرَ، فَلَمَّا كَانَ ⦗ص: 62⦘ خِلَافَةُ عُثْمَانَ وَكَثُرَ النَّاسُ أَمَرَ عُثْمَانُ بِأَذَانٍ ثَانٍ فَأُذِّنَ بِهِ، فَثَبَتَ الْأَمْرُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ وَكَانَ عَطَاءٌ يُنْكِرُ أَنْ يَكُونَ أَحْدَثَهُ عُثْمَانُ وَيَقُولُ: «أَحْدَثَهُ مُعَاوِيَةُ»، وَاللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ

English
Isnad

Sufyan narrated to us, from al-Zuhri, from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, from Abu Hurayra, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“When it is the day of Friday, at every door of the mosque there are angels recording the people according to their ranks, the first and then the next; when the imam comes out, the scrolls are folded and they listen to the sermon. The one who goes early (muhajjir) to the prayer is like one who offers a camel (badana), then the one after him is like one who offers a cow, then the one after him is like one who offers a ram” — until he mentioned the hen and the egg.

“I saw Abu Sa'id al-Khudri come while Marwan was giving the sermon; he stood and prayed two rak'as. The guards (ahras) came to him to make him sit, but he refused to sit until he had prayed two rak'as. When he finished the prayer we went to him and said: ‘O Abu Sa'id, these people were about to do [something] to you.’ He said: ‘I would not leave it for anything, after what I saw from the Messenger of God ﷺ. I saw the Prophet ﷺ — a man came while he was giving the sermon and entered the mosque in a shabby condition. He said: “Have you prayed?” He said: “No.” He said: “Then pray two rak'as.” Then he urged the people to give charity, so they threw down garments, and the Messenger of God ﷺ gave the man two garments from them. When the next Friday came, the man came while the Prophet ﷺ was giving the sermon, and the Prophet ﷺ said to him: “Have you prayed?” He said: “No.” He said: “Then pray two rak'as.” Then he urged the people to give charity, and the man threw down one of his two garments; the Messenger of God ﷺ cried out and said: “Take it.” So he took it. Then the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Look at this man: he came that [previous] Friday in a shabby condition, so I commanded the people to give charity and they threw down garments, and I gave him two of them; and when this Friday came I commanded the people to give charity, and he came and threw down one of his two garments.”’”

Isnad

Ibrahim b. Muhammad informed us, saying: 'Abd Allah b. Muhammad b. 'Aqil informed me, from al-Tufayl b. Ubayy b. Ka'b, from his father, who said:

Matn

“The Prophet ﷺ used to pray facing a palm trunk when the mosque was [only] a shelter of branches ('arish), and he used to give the sermon [facing] that trunk. A man among his Companions said: ‘O Messenger of God, would you like us to make a minbar for you, on which you would stand on Friday so that the people hear your sermon?’ He said: ‘Yes.’ So three steps were made for him, which are those on the minbar. When the minbar had been made and placed in the place where the Messenger of God ﷺ placed it, it occurred to the Prophet ﷺ to stand on that minbar and give the sermon on it, so he passed toward it; and when he passed beyond that trunk against which he used to give the sermon, it lowed (khara) until it split and cracked. The Prophet ﷺ came down when he heard the sound of the trunk, wiped it with his hand, then returned to the minbar. When the mosque was demolished, Ubayy b. Ka'b ﵁ took that trunk, and it remained with him in his house until it decayed, the termite ate it, and it became crumbled dust.”

Matn

English
Isnad

Malik informed us, from Yazid b. 'Abd Allah b. al-Had, from Muhammad b. Ibrahim b. Abi l-Harith, from Abu Salama, from Abu Hurayra ﵁, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“The best day on which the sun has risen is the day of Friday: in it Adam was created, in it he was sent down, in it his repentance was accepted, in it he died, and in it the Hour will be established. There is no animal that is not listening attentively (musikha) on Friday, from dawn until the sun rises, in fear of the Hour, except jinn and humans. And in it is an hour which no Muslim servant encounters, asking God for something, except that He gives it to him.” Abu Hurayra said: 'Abd Allah b. Salam said: “It is the last hour of the day of Friday.” I said to him: “How can it be the last hour when the Prophet ﷺ said: ‘No Muslim servant encounters it while he is praying’ — and that is an hour in which no prayer is performed?” Ibn Salam said: “Did the Prophet ﷺ not say: ‘Whoever sits in a sitting waiting for the prayer is in prayer until he prays’?” He said: I said: “Yes.” He said: “Then that is it.”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

«خَيْرُ يَوْمٍ طَلَعَتْ فِيهِ الشَّمْسُ يَوْمُ الْجُمُعَةِ؛ فِيهِ خُلِقَ آدَمُ، وَفِيهِ أُهْبِطَ، وَفِيهِ تِيبَ عَلَيْهِ، وَفِيهِ مَاتَ، وَفِيهِ تَقُومُ السَّاعَةُ، وَمَا مِنْ دَابَّةٍ إِلَّا وَهِيَ مُصِيخَةٌ يَوْمَ الْجُمُعَةِ مِنْ حِينَ تُصْبِحُ حَتَّى تَطْلُعَ الشَّمْسُ شَفَقًا مِنَ السَّاعَةِ، إِلَّا الْجِنَّ وَالْإِنْسَ، وَفِيهِ سَاعَةٌ لَا يُصَادِفُهَا عَبْدٌ مُسْلِمٌ يَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ شَيْئًا إِلَّا أَعْطَاهُ إِيَّاهُ» قَالَ أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ: قَالَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ سَلَامٍ: هِيَ آخِرُ سَاعَةٍ مِنْ يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ. فَقُلْتُ لَهُ: كَيْفَ تَكُونُ آخِرَ سَاعَةٍ وَقَدْ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: " لَا يُصَادِفُهَا عَبْدٌ مُسْلِمٌ وَهُوَ يُصَلِّي، وَتِلْكَ سَاعَةٌ لَا يُصَلَّى فِيهَا؟ فَقَالَ ابْنُ سَلَامٍ: أَلَمْ يَقُلِ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «مَنْ جَلَسَ مَجْلِسًا يَنْتَظِرُ الصَّلَاةَ فَهُوَ فِي صَلَاةٍ حَتَّى يُصَلِّيَ»؟ قَالَ: فَقُلْتُ: بَلَى. قَالَ: فَهُوَ ذَاكَ
