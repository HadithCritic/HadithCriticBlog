English
Isnad

The trustworthy one informed us, from Hammad, from Yahya b. Sa'id, from Abu Umama b. Sahl b. Hunayf, from 'Uthman ﵁, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“It is not lawful to kill a Muslim person except for one of three [reasons]: disbelief after belief, adultery after marriage (ihsan), or killing a person other than in retaliation for a person.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا الثِّقَةُ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي أُمَامَةَ بْنِ سَهْلِ بْنِ حُنَيْفٍ، عَنْ عُثْمَانَ رضي الله عنه، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ:

Matn

" لَا يَحِلُّ قَتْلُ امْرِئٍ مُسْلِمٍ إِلَّا بِإِحْدَى ثَلَاثٍ: كُفْرٌ بَعْدَ إِيمَانٍ، أَوْ زِنًى بَعْدَ إِحْصَانٍ، أَوْ قَتْلُ نَفْسٍ بِغَيْرِ نَفْسٍ "

English
Isnad

'Abd al-'Aziz informed us, from Muhammad b. 'Amr, from Abu Salama, from Abu Hurayra ﵁, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“I will continue to fight people until they say ‘There is no god but God.’ When they say it, they have protected from me their blood and their property, except by its right, and their reckoning is with God.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رضي الله عنه، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ:

Matn

«لَا أَزَالُ أُقَاتِلُ النَّاسَ حَتَّى يَقُولُوا لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، فَإِذَا قَالُوهَا فَقَدْ عَصَمُوا مِنِّي دِمَاءَهُمْ وَأَمْوَالَهُمْ إِلَّا بِحَقِّهَا، وَحِسَابُهُمْ عَلَى اللَّهِ»

English
Isnad

Yahya b. Hassan informed us, from al-Layth, from Ibn Shihab, from 'Ata' b. Yazid al-Laythi, from 'Ubayd Allah b. 'Adi b. al-Khiyar, from al-Miqdad ﵁, who informed him that he said:

Matn

“O Messenger of God, what if I meet a man of the unbelievers and he fights me and strikes one of my hands with the sword and cuts it off, then takes refuge from me by a tree and says, ‘I have submitted to God’—may I kill him, O Messenger of God, after he has said it?” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Do not kill him.” I said: “O Messenger of God, he cut off my hand and then said that after cutting it off. May I kill him?” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Do not kill him. If you kill him, he will be in your position before you killed him, and you will be in his position before he said the word he said.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ حَسَّانَ، عَنِ اللَّيْثِ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ اللَّيْثِيِّ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَدِيِّ بْنِ الْخِيَارِ، عَنِ الْمِقْدَادِ رضي الله عنه أَنَّهُ أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّهُ، قَالَ:

Matn

يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَرَأَيْتَ إِنْ لَقِيتُ رَجُلًا مِنَ الْكُفَّارِ فَقَاتَلَنِي فَضَرَبَ إِحْدَى يَدَيَّ بِالسَّيْفِ فَقَطَعَهَا ثُمَّ لَاذَ مِنِّي بِشَجَرَةٍ فَقَالَ: أَسْلَمْتُ لِلَّهِ، أَفَأَقْتُلُهُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ بَعْدَ أَنْ قَالَهَا؟ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لَا تَقْتُلْهُ». فَقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، إِنَّهُ قَطَعَ يَدِي ثُمَّ قَالَ ذَلِكَ بَعْدَ أَنْ قَطَعَهَا، أَفَأَقْتُلُهُ؟ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لَا تَقْتُلْهُ، فَإِنْ قَتَلْتَهُ فَإِنَّهُ بِمَنْزِلَتِكَ قَبْلَ أَنْ تَقْتُلَهُ، وَإِنَّكَ بِمَنْزِلَتِهِ قَبْلَ أَنْ يَقُولَ كَلِمَتَهُ الَّتِي قَالَ»

English
Isnad

Ibrahim b. Muhammad informed us, from Ja'far b. Muhammad, from his father, from his grandfather, who said:

Matn

A writing was found in the hilt of the sword of the Prophet ﷺ: “The most hostile of people toward God, Glorified and Exalted, is the one who kills someone other than his killer, the one who strikes someone other than his assailant, and whoever allies himself with someone other than his own patrons (mawali) has disbelieved in what God revealed to Muhammad ﷺ.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ جَعْفَرِ بْنِ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ جَدِّهِ قَالَ:

Matn

وُجِدَ فِي قَائِمِ سَيْفِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم كِتَابٌ: إِنَّ أَعْدَى النَّاسِ عَلَى اللَّهِ سبحانه وتعالى الْقَاتِلُ غَيْرَ قَاتِلِهِ، وَالضَّارِبُ غَيْرَ ضَارِبِهِ، وَمَنْ تَوَلَّى غَيْرَ مَوَالِيهِ فَقَدْ كَفَرَ بِمَا أَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ سُبْحَانَهُ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ

Isnad

Sufyan informed us, from Ibn Abi Layla, from al-Hakam—or from 'Isa b. Abi Layla—from Ibn Abi Layla, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Whoever delights in killing a believer is subject to retaliation by his own hand, unless he satisfies the guardian of the slain. Whoever stands in the way of it, upon him is the curse of God and His wrath; neither obligatory nor voluntary [worship] will be accepted from him.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي لَيْلَى، عَنِ الْحَكَمِ، أَوْ عَنْ عِيسَى بْنِ أَبِي لَيْلَى، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي لَيْلَى قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم:

Matn

«مَنِ اغْتَبَطَ مُؤْمِنًا بِقَتْلٍ فَهُوَ قَوَدُ يَدِهِ، إِلَّا أَنْ يُرْضِيَ وَلِيَّ الْمَقْتُولِ، فَمَنْ حَالَ دُونَهُ فَعَلَيْهِ لَعْنَةُ اللَّهِ وَغَضَبُهُ، لَا يُقْبَلُ مِنْهُ صَرْفٌ وَلَا عَدْلٌ»

Isnad

Ibn 'Uyayna informed us, from 'Abd al-Malik b. Sa'id ibn Abjar, from Iyad b. Laqit, from Abu Rimtha, who said:

Matn

I entered with my father upon the Messenger of God ﷺ, and my father saw what was on the back of the Messenger of God ﷺ and said: “Let me treat what is on your back, for I am a gentle physician.” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Who is this with you?” He said: “My son; I testify to him.” He said: “He will not commit an offense against you, nor will you commit an offense against him.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا ابْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْمَلِكِ بْنِ سَعِيدِ بْنِ أَبْجَرَ، عَنْ إِيَادِ بْنِ لَقِيطٍ، عَنْ أَبِي رِمْثَةَ قَالَ:

Matn

English
Isnad

Ibn 'Uyayna informed us, from 'Ali b. Zayd b. Jud'an, from al-Qasim b. Rabi'a, from [p. 199] Ibn 'Umar ﵄, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Indeed, in a killing that resembles intentional killing—[killing] by the whip or the staffs—the blood-money is a hundred camels of heavy grade, of which forty are pregnant she-camels.” Al-Thaqafi informed us, from Khalid al-Hadhdha', from al-Qasim b. Rabi'a, from 'Uqba b. Aws, from a man of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ, meaning the same.

العربية
Isnad

Isnad

Malik informed us, from Hisham b. 'Urwa, from his father, from 'A'isha ﵂, that she said:

Matn

Barira came to me and said: “I made a contract of manumission (kitaba) with my people for nine uqiyas, one uqiya each year, so help me.” 'A'isha said to her: “If your people are willing that I pay it to them in one sum and your wala' be mine, I will do so.” [p. 205] Barira went to her people while the Messenger of God ﷺ was sitting, and said: “I offered that to them, but they refused unless the wala' were theirs.” The Messenger of God ﷺ heard that and asked her, and 'A'isha informed him. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Take her and stipulate the wala', for wala' belongs only to the one who frees.” 'A'isha did so. Then the Messenger of God ﷺ stood among the people, praised God, and said: “To proceed: what is the matter with men who stipulate conditions that are not in the Book of God Almighty? Any condition that is not in the Book of God Almighty is void, even if it be a hundred conditions. The ruling of God is more rightful and His condition more binding, and wala' belongs only to the one who frees.”

Isnad

Malik informed us, from 'Abd Allah b. Abi Bakr, from 'Abd al-Malik b. Abi Bakr b. al-Harith b. Hisham, from his father, who informed him that

Matn

al-'As b. Hisham died and left three sons: two by one mother, and one by a co-wife ('illa, i.e., a rival wife). One of the two who shared a mother died, leaving property and clients (mawali). His full brother, of his mother and father, inherited his property and the wala' of his clients. Then the one who had inherited the property and the wala' died, leaving his son and his paternal half-brother. His son said: “I have taken possession of what my father possessed of the property and the wala' of the clients.” His uncle said: “Not so; you have taken the property, but as for the wala' of the clients, no. Do you not see that if my brother had died today I would inherit from him?” They took their dispute to 'Uthman ﵁, who ruled in favor of the brother [the uncle] regarding the wala' of the clients.
