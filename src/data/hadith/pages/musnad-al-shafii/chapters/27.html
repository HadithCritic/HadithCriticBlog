Report1166/ 9
Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 256
English
Isnad

Malik informed us, saying: Ibn Shihab narrated to me that Sahl b. Sa'd al-Sa'idi informed him that

Matn

'Uwaymir al-'Ajlani came to 'Asim b. 'Adi al-Ansari and said to him: “What do you think, 'Asim: if a man found another man with his wife, should he kill him, so that you kill him [in retaliation], or what should he do? Ask the Messenger of God ﷺ about this for me, 'Asim.” 'Asim asked the Messenger of God ﷺ about it, and the Messenger of God ﷺ disliked such questions and criticized them, until what 'Asim heard from the Messenger of God ﷺ weighed heavily on him. When 'Asim returned to his family, 'Uwaymir came to him and said: “'Asim, what did the Messenger of God ﷺ say to you?” 'Asim said to 'Uwaymir: “You did not bring me any good; the Messenger of God ﷺ disliked the question I asked him about.” 'Uwaymir said: “By God, I will not stop until I ask him about it.” 'Uwaymir went until he came to the Messenger of God ﷺ in the midst of the people and said: “O Messenger of God, what do you think of a man who finds another man with his wife—should he kill him, so that you kill him, or what should he do?” The Prophet ﷺ said: “God has revealed [a ruling] concerning you and your wife. Go and bring her.” Sahl b. Sa'd said: So they performed li'an while I was with the people at the Messenger of God's ﷺ place. When they had finished their li'an, 'Uwaymir said: “I would have lied against her, O Messenger of God, if I kept her,” and he divorced her three times before the Messenger of God ﷺ commanded him. Ibn Shihab said: That became the practice for those who perform li'an.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ قَالَ: حَدَّثَنِي ابْنُ شِهَابٍ، أَنَّ سَهْلَ بْنَ سَعْدٍ السَّاعِدِيَّ، أَخْبَرَهُ

Matn

أَنَّ عُوَيْمِرًا الْعَجْلَانِيَّ جَاءَ إِلَى عَاصِمِ بْنِ عَدِيٍّ الْأَنْصَارِيِّ فَقَالَ لَهُ: أَرَأَيْتَ يَا عَاصِمُ لَوْ أَنَّ رَجُلًا وَجَدَ مَعَ امْرَأَتِهِ رَجُلًا، أَيَقْتُلُهُ فَتَقْتُلُونَهُ، أَمْ كَيْفَ يَفْعَلُ؟ سَلْ لِي يَا عَاصِمُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ ذَلِكَ، فَسَأَلَ عَاصِمٌ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ ذَلِكَ، فَكَرِهَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم الْمَسَائِلَ وَعَابَهَا حَتَّى كَبُرَ عَلَى عَاصِمٍ مَا سَمِعَ مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَلَمَّا رَجَعَ عَاصِمٌ إِلَى أَهْلِهِ جَاءَهُ عُوَيْمِرٌ فَقَالَ: يَا عَاصِمُ، مَاذَا قَالَ لَكَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم؟ فَقَالَ عَاصِمٌ لِعُوَيْمِرٍ: لَمْ تَأْتِنِي بِخَيْرٍ، قَدْ كَرِهَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم الْمَسْأَلَةَ الَّتِي سَأَلْتُهُ عَنْهَا، فَقَالَ عُوَيْمِرٌ: وَاللَّهِ لَا أَنْتَهِي حَتَّى أَسْأَلَهُ عَنْهَا، فَأَقْبَلَ عُوَيْمِرٌ حَتَّى أَتَى رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَسَطَ النَّاسِ فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَرَأَيْتَ رَجُلًا وَجَدَ مَعَ امْرَأَتِهِ رَجُلًا، أَيَقْتُلُهُ فَتَقْتُلُونَهُ، أَمْ كَيْفَ يَفْعَلُ؟ فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «قَدْ أَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ فِيكَ وَفِي صَاحِبَتِكَ، فَاذْهَبْ فَأْتِ بِهَا». فَقَالَ سَهْلُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ: فَتَلَاعَنَا، وَأَنَا مَعَ النَّاسِ عِنْدَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَلَمَّا فَرَغَا مِنْ تَلَاعُنِهِمَا قَالَ عُوَيْمِرٌ: كَذَبْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنْ أَمْسَكْتُهَا، فَطَلَّقَهَا ثَلَاثًا قَبْلَ أَنْ يَأْمُرَهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ابْنُ شِهَابٍ: فَكَانَتْ تِلْكَ سُنَّةُ الْمُتَلَاعِنَيْنِ

English
Isnad

Ibrahim b. Sa'd informed us, from Ibn Shihab, from Sahl b. Sa'd, who informed him, saying:

Matn

'Uwaymir al-'Ajlani came to 'Asim b. 'Adi and said: “'Asim b. 'Adi, ask the Messenger of God ﷺ for me about a man who finds another man with his wife and kills him: is he killed for it, or what should he do?” 'Asim asked the Prophet ﷺ, and the Prophet ﷺ criticized such questions. 'Uwaymir met him and said: “What did you do?” He said: “I did it; you brought me no good. I asked the Messenger of God ﷺ and he criticized the questions.” 'Uwaymir said: “By God, I will go to the Messenger of God ﷺ and ask him.” He came to him and found that [a revelation] had been sent down to him concerning them both. He summoned them and had them perform li'an. 'Uwaymir said: “If I take her away, I will have lied against her,” and he separated from her before the Messenger of God ﷺ commanded him. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Watch her. If she brings it [the child] dark, with large dark eyes and large buttocks, I think only that he has spoken truly; and if she brings it reddish like a lizard (wahara), I think only that he lied.” She brought it in the disliked description. Ibn Shihab said: So it became the practice for those who perform li'an.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ، أَخْبَرَهُ قَالَ:

Matn

جَاءَ عُوَيْمِرٌ الْعَجْلَانِيُّ إِلَى عَاصِمِ بْنِ عَدِيٍّ فَقَالَ: يَا عَاصِمَ بْنَ عَدِيٍّ، سَلْ لِي رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ رَجُلٍ وَجَدَ مَعَ امْرَأَتِهِ رَجُلًا فَيَقْتُلُهُ، أَيُقْتَلُ بِهِ أَمْ كَيْفَ يَصْنَعُ؟ فَسَأَلَ عَاصِمٌ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَعَابَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم الْمَسَائِلَ، فَلَقِيَهُ عُوَيْمِرٌ فَقَالَ: مَا صَنَعْتَ؟ قَالَ: صَنَعْتُ إِنَّكَ لَمْ تَأْتِنِي بِخَيْرٍ، سَأَلْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَعَابَ الْمَسَائِلَ، فَقَالَ عُوَيْمِرٌ: وَاللَّهِ لَآتِيَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَلَأَسْأَلَنَّهُ، فَأَتَاهُ فَوَجَدَهُ قَدْ أُنْزِلَ عَلَيْهِ فِيهِمَا، فَدَعَاهُمَا فَلَاعَنَ بَيْنَهُمَا، فَقَالَ عُوَيْمِرٌ: لَإِنِ انْطَلَقْتُ بِهَا لَقَدْ كَذَبْتُ عَلَيْهَا، فَفَارَقَهَا قَبْلَ أَنْ يَأْمُرَهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم. قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «انْظُرُوهَا، فَإِنْ جَاءَتْ بِهِ أَسْحَمَ، أَدْعَجَ، عَظِيمَ الْإِلْيَتَيْنِ فَلَا أُرَاهُ إِلَّا قَدْ صَدَقَ، وَإِنْ جَاءَتْ بِهِ أُحَيْمِرَ كَأَنَّهُ وَحَرَةٌ فَلَا أُرَاهُ إِلَّا كَاذِبًا». فَجَاءَتْ بِهِ عَلَى النَّعْتِ الْمَكْرُوهِ قَالَ ابْنُ شِهَابٍ: فَصَارَتْ سُنَّةُ الْمُتَلَاعِنَيْنِ

Isnad

'Abd Allah b. Nafi' informed us, from Ibn Abi Dhi'b, from Ibn Shihab, from Sahl b. Sa'd, that

Matn

'Uwaymir came to 'Asim and said: “What do you think: if a man found another man with his wife and killed him, would you kill him? Ask the Messenger of God ﷺ for me, 'Asim.” He asked the Prophet ﷺ, and the Messenger of God ﷺ disliked such questions and criticized them. 'Asim returned to 'Uwaymir and informed him that the Prophet ﷺ disliked such questions and criticized them. 'Uwaymir said: “By God, I will go to the Messenger of God ﷺ.” He came, and Qur'an had been revealed contrary to 'Asim's [expectation]. He asked the Messenger of God ﷺ, who said: “Qur'an has been revealed concerning you both.” They came forward and performed li'an. Then he said: “I would have lied against her if I kept her,” and he separated from her without the Prophet ﷺ commanding him; so the practice of those who perform li'an proceeded. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Watch her. If she brings it reddish and short, like a lizard, I think only that he lied against her; and if she brings it dark, with [large] eyes and large buttocks, I think only that he spoke truly about her.” She brought it in the disliked description. I heard Ibrahim b. Sa'd narrate from his father, from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab and 'Ubayd Allah b. 'Utba, that the Prophet ﷺ said: “If she brings it blond and lank-haired [p. 258], it is her husband's; and if she brings it very dark-eyed, it belongs to the one he accuses.” He said: She brought it very dark-eyed.

English
Isnad

Sa'id ibn Salim informed us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Ibn Shihab, from Sahl b. Sa'd, of Banu Sa'ida, that

Matn

a man of the Ansar came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: “O Messenger of God, what do you think of a man who finds another man with his wife—should he kill him, so that you kill him, or what should he do?” So God Almighty revealed concerning his case what is mentioned in the Qur'an about those who perform li'an. He said: The Prophet ﷺ said to him: “A ruling has been made concerning you and your wife.” He said: They performed li'an while I was present, then he separated from her in the presence of the Prophet ﷺ. After them it became the practice that those who perform li'an are separated. She was pregnant and he denied [the child], so her son used to be called by his mother's name.

Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا سَعِيدُ بْنُ سَالِمٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ جُرَيْجٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ، أَخِي بَنِي سَاعِدَةَ

Matn

أَنَّ رَجُلًا، مِنَ الْأَنْصَارِ جَاءَ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَرَأَيْتَ رَجُلًا وَجَدَ مَعَ امْرَأَتِهِ رَجُلًا، أَيَقْتُلُهُ فَتَقْتُلُونَهُ، أَمْ كَيْفَ يَصْنَعُ؟ فَأَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ عز وجل فِي شَأْنِهِ مَا ذُكِرَ فِي الْقُرْآنِ مِنْ أَمْرِ الْمُتَلَاعِنَيْنِ، قَالَ: فَقَالَ لَهُ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «قَدْ قُضِيَ فِيكَ وَفِي امْرَأَتِكَ». قَالَ: فَتَلَاعَنَا وَأَنَا شَاهِدٌ ثُمَّ فَارَقَهَا عِنْدَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَكَانَتْ سُنَّةٌ بَعْدَهُمَا أَنْ يُفَرَّقَ بَيْنَ الْمُتَلَاعِنَيْنِ، وَكَانَتْ حَامِلًا فَأَنْكَرَهَا، فَكَانَ ابْنُهَا يُدْعَى إِلَى أُمِّهِ

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 258
English
Isnad

Sufyan informed us, from Abu al-Zinad, from al-Qasim b. Muhammad, who said:

Matn

I was present when Ibn 'Abbas narrated the hadith of those who performed li'an, and Ibn Shaddad said to him: “Is she the one about whom the Prophet ﷺ said, ‘If I were to stone anyone without proof, I would stone her’?” Ibn 'Abbas said: “No; that was a woman who had made [her conduct] public.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ أَبِي الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الْقَاسِمِ بْنِ مُحَمَّدٍ قَالَ:

Matn

شَهِدْتُ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ يُحَدِّثُ بِحَدِيثِ الْمُتَلَاعِنَيْنِ فَقَالَ لَهُ ابْنُ شَدَّادٍ: أَهِيَ الَّتِي قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «لَوْ كُنْتُ رَاجِمًا أَحَدًا بِغَيْرِ بَيِّنَةٍ رَجَمْتُهَا»؟ فَقَالَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ: لَا، تِلْكُ امْرَأَةٌ قَدْ أَعْلَنَتْ

English
Isnad

'Abd al-'Aziz b. Muhammad informed us, from Ibn al-Had, from 'Abd Allah b. Yunus, that he heard al-Maqburi narrate to al-Qurazi. Al-Maqburi said: Abu Hurayra narrated to me that he heard the Prophet ﷺ say,

Matn

when the verse of li'an was revealed, the Prophet ﷺ said: “Any woman who brings into a people someone who is not of them has nothing from God, and God will not admit her to His Paradise; and any man who denies his child while looking at him, God will veil Himself from him and expose him before the heads of creation, among the earlier and later peoples.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ عَنِ ابْنِ الْهَادِ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ يُونُسَ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ الْمَقْبُرِيَّ، يُحَدِّثُ الْقُرَظِيَّ، قَالَ الْمَقْبُرِيُّ: حَدَّثَنِي أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ:

Matn

لَمَّا نَزَلَتْ آيَةُ الْمُلَاعَنَةِ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «أَيُّمَا امْرَأَةٍ أَدْخَلَتْ عَلَى قَوْمٍ مَنْ لَيْسَ مِنْهُمْ فَلَيْسَتْ مِنَ اللَّهِ فِي شَيْءٍ، وَلَمْ يُدْخِلْهَا اللَّهُ جَنَّتَهُ، وَأَيُّمَا رَجُلٍ جَحَدَ وَلَدَهُ وَهُوَ يَنْظُرُ إِلَيْهِ احْتَجَبَ اللَّهُ مِنْهُ وَفَضَحَهُ عَلَى رُءُوسِ الْخَلَائِقِ فِي الْأَوَّلِينَ وَالْآخِرِينَ»

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 258
English
Isnad

And I heard Sufyan b. 'Uyayna say: 'Amr b. Dinar informed us, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, from Ibn 'Umar, that

Matn

the Prophet ﷺ said to the couple who had performed li'an: “Your reckoning is with God; one of you is lying. You have no claim over her.” He said: “O Messenger of God, my property?” He said: “You have no property: if you spoke truly, it is in return for what you made lawful of her private parts; and if you lied against her, then that puts it further from you than from her.”

العربية
Isnad

وَسَمِعْتُ سُفْيَانَ بْنَ عُيَيْنَةَ يَقُولُ: أَخْبَرَنَا عَمْرُو بْنُ دِينَارٍ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ

Matn

أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ لِلْمُتَلَاعِنَيْنِ: «حِسَابُكُمَا عَلَى اللَّهِ، أَحَدُكُمَا كَاذِبٌ، لَا سَبِيلَ لَكَ عَلَيْهَا». قَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، مَالِي، قَالَ: «لَا مَالَ لَكَ، إِنْ كُنْتَ صَدَقْتَ فَهُوَ بِمَا اسْتَحْلَلْتَ مِنْ فَرْجِهَا، وَإِنْ كُنْتَ كَذَبْتَ عَلَيْهَا فَذَلِكَ أَبْعَدُ لَكَ مِنْهَا، أَوْ مِنْهُ»

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 259
English
Isnad

Sufyan informed us, from Ayyub, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, who said: I heard Ibn 'Umar say:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ separated the two siblings of Banu al-'Ajlan [the couple], and he said thus—with his two fingers, the index and the middle—separating them, the middle finger and the one next to it, meaning the index finger. And he said: “God knows that one of you is lying; is either of you repentant?”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا سُفْيَانُ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ ابْنَ عُمَرَ، يَقُولُ:

Matn

فَرَّقَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بَيْنَ أَخَوَيْ بَنِي الْعَجْلَانِ، وَقَالَ: هَكَذَا، بِأُصْبُعَيْهِ الْمُسَبِّحَةِ وَالْوسْطَى، فَفَرَّقَهُمَا الْوسْطَى وَالَّتِي تَلِيهَا، يَعْنِي الْمُسَبِّحَةَ، وَقَالَ: «اللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ أَنَّ أَحَدَكُمَا كَاذِبٌ، فَهَلْ مِنْكُمَا تَائِبٌ»

Musnad al-Shāfiʿī · Vol. 1, p. 259
English
Isnad

Malik informed us, from Nafi', from Ibn 'Umar ﵄, that

Matn

a man performed li'an against his wife in the time of the Prophet ﷺ and disowned her child; so the Messenger of God ﷺ separated them and attached the child to the woman.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ رضي الله عنهما

Matn

أَنَّ رَجُلًا، لَاعَنَ امْرَأَتَهُ فِي زَمَانِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَانْتَفَى مِنْ وَلَدِهَا، فَفَرَّقَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بَيْنَهُمَا، وَأَلْحَقَ الْوَلَدَ بِالْمَرْأَةِ
