Compiled byMuḥammad ibn Idrīs al-Shāfiʿīd. 204 AH

Every report is given in Arabic and English with its chain set apart from its words.

About the edition.

Edition. Dār al-Kutub al-ʿIlmiyya, Beirut, 1400 AH, one volume of 391 pages, corrected against the Būlāq (Amīriyya) printing and the Indian printing (Shamela 9344). The colophon says the text was collated with the Amīriyya copy, which had itself been collated with an old Syrian manuscript bearing audition notes, the last dated 784 AH.

Structure of the work. Not a musnad arranged by Companion. The reports with chains in al-Shāfiʿī’s books, as his student al-Rabīʿ ibn Sulaymān transmitted them in Egypt, were taken out and set under 68 headings, each naming the book they came from. The gathering is usually credited to Abū al-ʿAbbās al-Aṣamm (d. 346 AH), who heard the books from al-Rabīʿ. Some headings say that al-Rabīʿ did not hear that material from al-Shāfiʿī himself. The headings and the reports under them keep the order of the printed edition.

Numbering. The edition prints no report numbers. The 1678 entries are the text records of the Shamela edition, numbered here 1 to 1678 in their printed order; a page reference stands beside each. A record can hold more than one chain for the same report, or al-Shāfiʿī’s remark after it, and these are shown together as the edition prints them.

English translation. The English is the supplied translation, assembled from several batches that name no translator and spell names in more than one way. It has not been checked word by word against the Arabic.

Honorifics. Blessings and honorifics after a name are set as symbols (ﷺ, ﵁, ﵂, ﵄ and the like), where the translation spelled them out or abbreviated them. Each symbol follows the formula the Arabic prints at that place. Formulas about God stay in words.

Arabic text. The Arabic is shown as supplied, with its honorific formulas in words as the edition prints them. Page markers are carried over in both languages.

Isnad and matn. The Arabic is divided where its chain ends and the English at the same link of the chain, by a fixed rule that does not alter the wording. A further chain given for the same report stays with the matn. A passage the rule cannot divide is shown whole and says so.

Transmitters. Each transmission path lists the names exactly as the isnad words them, read from the text between its transmission terms. English names are shown only where they line up one for one with the Arabic. No transmitter is identified beyond the wording of the source.

Front matter. The edition’s title page, its short note on al-Shāfiʿī and its opening prayer stand before the first heading. The Shamela catalog header is not reproduced.

Dating. The date shown is the death year of al-Shāfiʿī, 204 AH. The gathering was made later, from al-Rabīʿ’s transmission (al-Rabīʿ d. 270 AH).
