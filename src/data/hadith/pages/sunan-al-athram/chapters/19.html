English
Isnad

al-Qa'nabi narrated to us, from Malik, from Zayd b. Aslam, that 'Umar b. al-Khattab said:

Matn

When one of you sleeps lying on his side, let him perform ablution.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْقَعْنَبِيُّ عَنْ مَالِكٍ عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ،

Matn

أَنَّ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ، قَالَ إِذَا نَامَ أَحَدُكُمْ مُضْطَجِعًا فَلْيَتَوَضَّأْ.

Isnad

Musa b. Isma'il narrated to us. Hammad narrated to us. Sa'id al-Jurayri informed us, from Khalid b. Ghalaq [p. 271]:

Matn

That Abu Hurayra said: When one of you is due (istahaqqa) sleep, ablution has become obligatory for him.

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا موسى بن إسماعيل حدثنا حماد أخبرنا سعيد الجريري عن خالد بن غلاق ⦗ص: 271⦘

Matn

أَنَّ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ إِذَا اسْتَحَقَّ أَحَدُكُمْ نَوْمًا فَقَدْ وَجَبَ عَلَيْهِ الْوُضُوءُ.

English
Isnad

al-Athram said: I heard Abu 'Abd Allah (Ahmad b. Hanbal) being asked

Matn

about a man who slept [p. 273] sitting hugging his knees with a wrap (muhtabiyan)—does he perform ablution? He said: Yes, he performs ablution. He said: And the one leaning performs ablution. I said to him: And if he slept prostrating? He said: The one prostrating also performs ablution, if it is long. Abu 'Abd Allah said: And I say: the one sleeping sitting, if he prolongs the sleep, performs ablution—except that sitting and sitting cross-legged are lighter than hugging the knees and leaning.

العربية
Isnad

قال الأَثْرَمُ: وسمعت أبا عبد الله يُسْأَل عَنْ رَجُلٍ نَامَ ⦗ص: 273⦘ مُحْتَبِيًا أَيَتَوَضَّأُ

Matn

قَالَ نَعَمْ يَتَوَضَّأُ قَالَ وَالْمُسْتَنِدُ يَتَوَضَّأُ. قُلْتُ لَهُ فَنَامَ سَاجِدًا قَالَ وَالسَّاجِدُ يَتَوَضَّأُ إِذَا أَطَالَ. قَالَ أَبُو عَبْدِ اللَّهِ وَأَنا أَقُولُ النَّائِمُ قَاعِدًا إِذَا أَطَالَ النَّوْمَ تَوَضَّأَ إِلا أَنَّ الْقَاعِدَ والمتربع أهون من المحتبي والمستند.

Isnad

Abu 'Ubayda b. Fayyad narrated to us. Hisham b. Abi 'Abd Allah al-Dastuwa'i narrated to us, from Qatada, from Anas b. Malik, who said:

Matn

The Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ used to wait for the last evening prayer ('isha') until their heads nodded, then they would pray and not perform ablution.

Narration142/ 17
Sunan al-Athram · p. 274
English
Isnad

I heard Abu 'Abd Allah

Matn

and he mentioned the hadith of Anas, and said: There is nothing better than the hadith of Anas, who said: “The Companions of the Prophet ﷺ used to sleep and then [p. 275] pray and not perform ablution.” Abu 'Abd Allah said: So some of them said; and Hisham said: “The Companions of the Prophet ﷺ used to nod their heads”; and Ibn Abi 'Aruba said: “they used to lay down their sides” [.......]. Abu 'Abd Allah said: this [.......] Sa'id—and he mentioned the hadith.

Isnad

قَالَ كَانَ أَصْحَابُ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَنَامُونَ ثم ⦗ص: 275⦘ يصلون ولا يتوضؤون قال أبو عبد الله هكذا قال بعضهم وقال هشام كَانَ أَصْحَابُ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يخفقون برؤوسهم وقال ابن أبي عروبة يضعون جنوبهم [.......] أبو عبد الله قال هذا [.......] سَعِيدٌ، فَذَكَرَ الحَدِيثَ] .
