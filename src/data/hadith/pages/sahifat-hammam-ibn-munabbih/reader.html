Narration 01

EnglishTranslation

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "My likeness and the likeness of the prophets before me is that of a man who built houses and made them excellent, beautiful, and complete, except for the place of one brick in one of the corners. People began to walk around, and the building pleased them, and they said, 'If only a brick had been placed here, and its construction would be complete!'" Muḥammad ﷺ said: "So I am the brick."

03   Opening transmission and introductory report Read full text ↓

Our Shaykh, the Imam, the most eminent, the unique, the Ḥāfiẓ, Tāj al-Dīn, Bahāʾ al-Islām, Badīʿ al-Zamān, Abū ʿAbd Allāh Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn Muḥammad ibn Masʿūd al-Bandahī, may God grant him success and give him insight into his own faults, narrated to us, by his reading to us from the original of his hearing, from which it was copied, in the Nāṣiriyya Ṣalāḥiyya school (may God perpetuate the dominion of its endower), on the twenty-sixth of Dhū al-Qaʿda in the year five hundred and seventy-seven. He said: The trustworthy, righteous Shaykh Abū al-Khayr Muḥammad ibn Aḥmad ibn Muḥammad ibn ʿUmar al-Muqaddar al-Aṣbahānī informed us, by reading to him while I listened. He said: Shaykh Abū ʿAmr ʿAbd al-Wahhāb ibn Abī ʿAbd Allāh ibn Isḥāq ibn Muḥammad ibn Yaḥyā ibn Mandah al-Aṣbahānī informed us. He said: My father, the Imam Abū ʿAbd Allāh Muḥammad ibn Isḥāq, informed us. He said: Abū Bakr Muḥammad ibn al-Ḥusayn ibn al-Ḥasan ibn al-Khalīl al-Qaṭṭān informed us. He said: Abū al-Ḥasan Aḥmad ibn Yūsuf al-Sulamī narrated to us. He said: ʿAbd al-Razzāq ibn Hammām ibn Nāfiʿ al-Ḥimyarī narrated to us, from Maʿmar, from Hammām ibn Munabbih, who said: This is what Abū Hurayra narrated to us from Muḥammad, the Messenger of God He said: "We are the last, yet the foremost on the Day of Resurrection, although they were given the Book before us, and we were given it after them. This is their day, which was made obligatory upon them, but they differed about it, and God guided us to it. So they follow us in it: the Jews tomorrow, and the Christians the day after tomorrow."
