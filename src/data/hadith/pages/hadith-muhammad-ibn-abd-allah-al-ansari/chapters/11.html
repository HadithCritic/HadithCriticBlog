Narration25/ 7
Ḥadīth al-Anṣārī · p. 45
English
Isnad

al-Ansari narrated to us. Ibn 'Awn narrated to us, from al-Sha'bi, who said:

Matn

I heard al-Nu'man b. Bashir say—and I do not hear anyone after him saying ‘I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ’—I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: “The lawful is clear and the unlawful is clear, and between them are doubtful matters”—sometimes he said: “doubtful things”—“and I shall give you an example of that: God has a protected pasture, and God’s protected pasture is what God has prohibited. Whoever grazes around the protected pasture is about to enter it”—and sometimes he said: “whoever mixes with doubt is about to venture into it.”

English
Isnad

al-Ansari narrated to us. Ibn 'Awn said: He narrated it to me; he said:

Matn

I and Muslim al-Batin entered upon Abu Wa'il, and we said to a slave girl of his called Burayda: Tell Abu Wa'il to narrate to us what he heard from 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud. She said: O Abu Wa'il, narrate to the people what you heard Ibn Mas'ud say. He said: I heard Ibn Mas'ud say: O people, you will be gathered on one plain, the caller will make you hear, and the eye will take you all in. Indeed the wretched one is he who was wretched in his mother’s womb. Ibn 'Awn said: And I think he followed it with: and the fortunate one is he who takes admonition from another. Then it was said to her: Say to him: What do you testify about al-Hajjaj? She said: O Abu Wa'il, what do you testify about al-Hajjaj—do you testify that he is in the Fire? He said: Glory be to Him! Shall I pass judgment on God, mighty and majestic?

Isnad

Ḥadīth al-Anṣārī · p. 47
English
Isnad

al-Ansari narrated to us. Ibn 'Awn narrated to us, from Muhammad:

Matn

That when al-Jarud came to 'Umar with a message from 'Ali b. 'Affan—or: 'Ali b. 'Awf—he met 'Umar and informed him. 'Umar said: I had intended to place al-Jarud before one of three (choices): to bring him forward and strike his neck, or to send him to al-Sham, or to keep him with me, humiliated and banished. Ibn 'Awn said: Sometimes he said: muqsan (banished). He said: So he said to him: O Commander of the Faithful, I did not take his part in coming down here. Then he turned to al-Jarud and informed him of that. He said: Al-Jarud said: Woe! All of them are good for me: either he brings me forward and strikes my neck—by God, he would not reproach me for a blessing; or he sends me to al-Sham, the land of the gathering and the resurrection; or he keeps me with him humiliated and banished—by God, I would be in the neighbourhood of the Messenger of God ﷺ and his wives, and I would not dislike that. He said: Then when he entered upon 'Umar he said: O Commander of the Faithful, you have appointed over us one who drinks wine. He said: Who are your witnesses? He said: Abu Hurayra. He said: Your son-in-law (khatan), your son-in-law? Al-Ansari said: Al-Jarud’s sister was married to Abu Hurayra. He said: By God, I will make his back ache with the whip. He said: He (al-Jarud) said to him: It is not right that your son-in-law should drink and you flog mine. He said: And who (else)? He said: 'Alqama. So they testified before him and he ordered him flogged, and said: Since I was appointed I have favoured no one in my governorship except him, and I was not blessed in it; so go and flog him.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْأَنْصَارِيِّ، ثنا ابْنُ عَوْنٍ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدٍ،

Matn

أَنَّ الْجَارُودَ، لَمَّا قَدِمَ عَلَى عُمَرَ بِرِسَالَةِ عَلِيِّ بْنِ عَفَّانَ، أَوْ: عَلِيِّ بْنِ عَوْفٍ، قَالَ: فَلَقِيَ عُمَرَ فَأَخْبَرَهُ، قَالَ: فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: لَقَدْ هَمَمْتُ أَنْ أُصَيِّرَ الْجَارُودَ بَيْنَ إِحْدَى ثَلَاثٍ: بَيْنَ أَنْ أُقَدِّمَهُ فَأَضْرِبَ عُنُقَهُ، وَبَيْنَ أَنْ أُسْنِدَهُ إِلَى الشَّامِ، وَبَيْنَ أَنْ أَحْبِسَهُ عِنْدِي مُهَانًا مُقْصَيًا، قَالَ ابْنُ عَوْنٍ: وَرُبَّمَا قَالَ: مُقْصًى، قَالَ: فَقَالَ لَهُ: يَا أَمِيرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ، مَا نَزَلْتُ لَهُ مُتَحَيِّزًا، ثُمَّ مَالَ إِلَى الْجَارُودِ فَأَخْبَرَهُ بِذَلِكَ، قَالَ: فَقَالَ الْجَارُودُ: وَيْلٌ كُلُّهُنَّ لِي خَيْرَةٌ، إِمَّا أَنْ يُقَدِّمُنِيَ فَيَضْرِبَ عُنُقِي، فَوَاللَّهِ مَا كَانَ لِيُؤَنِّبَنِي عَلَى نِعْمَةٍ، وَإِمَّا أَنْ يُسْنِدَنِي إِلَى الشَّامِ فَأَرْضُ الْمَحْشَرِ وَالْمَنْشَرِ، وَإِمَّا أَنْ يَحْبِسَنِي عِنْدَهُ مُهَانًا مُقْصَيًا، فَوَاللَّهِ مَا فِي جِوَارٍ مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَزْوَاجِهِ مَا أَكْرَهُ، قَالَ: فَلَمَّا دَخَلَ عَلَى عُمَرَ قَالَ: يَا أَمِيرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ، اسْتَعْمَلْتَ عَلَيْنَا مَنْ يَشْرَبُ الْخَمْرَ، فَقَالَ: مَنْ شُهُودُكَ؟ قَالَ: أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ: خَتْنُكَ خَتْنُكَ؟، قَالَ الْأَنْصَارِيُّ: وَكَانَتْ أُخْتُ الْجَارُودِ تَحْتَ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ: أَمَا وَاللَّهِ لَأُوجِعَنَّ مَتْنَهُ بِالسَّوْطِ، قَالَ: فَقَالَ لَهُ: مَا ذَاكَ فِي الْحَقِّ أَنْ يَشْرَبَ خَتْنُكَ وَتَجْلِدَ خَتْنِي، قَالَ: وَمَنْ قَالَ: عَلْقَمَةُ، فَشَهِدُوا عِنْدَهُ، فَأَمَرَ بِجَلْدِهِ، وَقَالَ: مَا حَبَيْتُ فِي إِمَارَتِي أَحَدًا مُنْذُ وُلِّيتُ غَيْرَهُ، فَمَا بُورِكَ لِي فِيهِ، فَاذْهَبُوا فَاجْلِدُوهُ

English
Isnad

al-Ansari narrated to us. Ibn 'Awn narrated to us. Muhammad b. Muhammad b. al-Aswad informed me, from 'Amir b. Sa'd, who said:

Matn

While Sa'd was walking he passed a man who was reviling 'Ali, Talha and al-Zubayr. Sa'd said to him: You are reviling people for whom God’s precedence has been established; you will stop your reviling, or I will invoke God against you. He said: He threatens me as though he were a prophet. Sa'd said: O God, if this man is reviling people for whom Your precedence was established, then make him today an example (nakal). He said: Then a Bactrian she-camel came, and the people made way for it, and it trampled him. He said: And I saw the people following Sa'd and saying: God has answered you, O Abu Ishaq. [p. 49]

Isnad

English
Isnad

al-Ansari narrated to us, who said:

Matn

I asked Ibn 'Awn about the counterfeit (za'if) dirham: May a man buy something with it? He said: With disclosure? I said: No. He said: Muhammad used to dislike it. I said: And if he discloses it? He said: Muhammad did not like it. Abu 'Abd Allah al-Ansari said: He said to me: What would you say if a man sold a good that had a defect in it? I said: He should disclose the defect. He said: I do not dislike that. I said: And likewise the counterfeit dirham if he does not disclose it. He said: And if he discloses the defect? I said: I see no harm in it. He said: And likewise the counterfeit dirham.

al-Ansari narrated to us, from Ibn 'Awn, from Ibrahim, who said:

Matn

They disliked that, when they gathered, a man should bring out the best of his hadith, or of the best that he had.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْأَنْصَارِيِّ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَوْنٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، قَالَ:

Matn

كَانُوا يَكْرَهُونَ إِذَا اجْتَمَعُوا أَنْ يُخْرِجَ الرَّجُلُ أَحْسَنَ حَدِيثِهِ، أَوْ مِنْ أَحْسَنِ مَا عِنْدَهُ

English
Isnad

al-Ansari narrated to us, from Ibn 'Awn, from al-Sha'bi, who said:

Matn

Shurayh said: Whenever two men meet, the nearer of the two to God is the one who begins with the greeting.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا الْأَنْصَارِيُّ عَنِ ابْنِ عَوْنٍ، عَنِ الشَّعْبِيِّ، قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ شُرَيْحٌ: مَا الْتَقَى رَجُلَانِ إِلَّا كَانَ أَوْلَاهُمَا بِاللَّهِ الَّذِي يَبْدَأُ بِالسَّلَامِ
