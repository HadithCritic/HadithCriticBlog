Adam and Moses dispute the decree

EnglishTranslation
Transmission · Isnād

Abu Bakr 'Abd Allah b. Sulayman b. al-Ash'ath b. Ishaq al-Sijistani narrated to us, reading from the original of his book, in the year 311 [AH]; Abu Ja'far Ahmad b. Sa'id b. Bishr al-Hamdani al-Misri narrated to us; 'Abd Allah b. Wahb informed us; Anas b. 'Iyad told us, from al-Harith b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. Abi Dhubab, from Yazid b. Hurmuz, who said: I heard Abu Hurayra ؓ say: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Reported text · Matn

“Adam and Musa ؑ disputed before their Lord ﷻ, and Adam bested Musa. Musa said: 'You are Adam, whom God created with His own hand, into whom He breathed of His spirit, before whom He made His angels prostrate, and whom He settled in His Garden; then you brought people down to the earth because of your sin?' Adam said: 'You are Musa, whom God chose for His message and His speech, to whom He gave the Tablets in which is the clarification of everything, and whom He brought near in confidential converse. By how many years did you find that God ﷻ had written the Torah before He created me?' Musa said: 'By forty years.' Adam said: 'Did you find in it: And Adam disobeyed his Lord and went astray?' He said: 'Yes.' He said: 'Then do you blame me for doing a deed that God had written against me forty years before He created me?' The Messenger of God ﷺ said: So Adam bested Musa.”

Source & transmission

Two parts of a collection attributed to ʿAbd Allāh ibn Wahb.

The uploaded file is titled Kitāb al-Qadar of Ibn Wahb (Arabic: القدر وما ورد في ذلك من الآثار). It presents 51 numbered entries in two populated parts: reports on predestination, creation, the writing of deeds, divine guidance, and the relationship between decree and human action.

Attribution is not authorship proof. The reports are cited through transmission paths, including much later witnesses. Their preservation in a named collection does not demonstrate that Ibn Wahb personally authored the extant arrangement or that each later addition came through him.
