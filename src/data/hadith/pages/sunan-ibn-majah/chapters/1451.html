Chapter3957/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 831
English
Isnad

Hisham b. 'Ammar and Muhammad b. al-Sabbah narrated to us; they said: 'Abd al-'Aziz b. Abi Hazim narrated to us: My father narrated to me, from 'Umarah b. Hazm, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr,

Matn

that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'What will become of you when a time soon comes in which people are sifted through like a sieve, leaving only their dregs, whose covenants and trusts have become confused, and they dispute and become like this?' He interlaced his fingers. They asked: 'Messenger of God, what are we to do then?' He said: 'Hold to what you recognize as right, abandon what you disapprove of, attend to your own circle, and leave the affairs of the general populace.'

Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامُ بْنُ عَمَّارٍ وَمُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الصَّبَّاحِ، قَالَا: حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ بْنُ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، حَدَّثَنِي أَبِي، عَنْ عُمَارَةَ بْنِ حَزْمٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْن عَمْرٍو،

Matn

أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: "كَيْفَ بِكُمْ وَبِزَمَانٍ يُوشِكُ أَنْ يَأْتِيَ، يُغَرْبَلُ النَّاسُ فِيهِ غَرْبَلَةً، [وَ] (9) تَبْقَى حُثَالَةٌ مِنَ النَّاسِ، قَدْ مَرِجَتْ (10) عُهُودُهُم وَأَمَانَاتُهُمْ، فَاخْتَلَفُوا وَكَانُوا هَكَذَا؟ " - وَشَبَّكَ بَيْنَ أَصَابِعِهِ (11) - قَالُوا: كَيْفَ بِنَا يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! إِذَا كَانَ ذَلِكَ؟ قَالَ: "تَأْخُذُونَ بِمَا تَعْرِفُونَ، وَتَدَعُونَ مَا تُنْكِرُونَ، وَتُقْبِلُونَ عَلَى خَاصَّتِكُمْ، وَتَذَرُونَ أَمْرَ عَوَامِّهِمْ (12) ".

Chapter3958/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 831
English
Isnad

Ahmad b. 'Abdah narrated to us: Hammad b. Zayd narrated to us, from Abu 'Imran al-Jawni, from al-Musha''ath b. Tarif, from 'Abd Allah b. al-Samit, from Abu Dharr, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ asked: 'Abu Dharr, how will you be when death strikes people so widely that a house'—meaning a grave—'is valued at the price of a young servant?' I answered: 'Whatever God and His Messenger choose for me,' or, 'God and His Messenger know best.' He said: 'Be patient.' He then asked: 'How will you be when famine strikes people so severely that you go to your mosque but cannot return to your bed, or rise from your bed to reach your mosque?' I said: 'God and His Messenger know best,' or, 'Whatever God and His Messenger choose for me.' He said: 'You must remain chaste and restrained.' He then asked: 'How will you be when slaughter strikes people until the stones of al-Zayt are submerged in blood?' I answered: 'Whatever God and His Messenger choose for me.' He said: 'Stay with your own people.' I said: 'Messenger of God, should I not seize my sword and strike those who do this?' He said: 'Then you would join them. Instead, enter your house.' I asked: 'What if someone enters my house?' He said: 'If you fear the flash of the sword will overwhelm you, cover your face with the edge of your cloak, so that he bears his sin and yours and becomes one of the people of the Fire.'

Chapter3959/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 832
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Bashshar narrated to us, saying: Muhammad b. Ja'far narrated to us: 'Awf narrated to us, from al-Hasan, who said: Usayd b. al-Mutashammis narrated to us: Abu Musa narrated to us:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'Before the Hour there will be harj.' I asked: 'Messenger of God, what is harj?' He replied: 'Killing, killing.' Some Muslims said: 'Messenger of God, in a single year we already kill such-and-such numbers of polytheists.' He replied: 'I do not mean killing polytheists. I mean that you will kill one another, until a man kills his neighbor, paternal cousin, and relative.' Some asked: 'Messenger of God, will we still have our minds that day?' He answered: 'No. The minds of most people of that time will be taken away; they will be succeeded by worthless people without sense.' Abu Musa al-Ash'ari then said: 'By God, I think this will overtake both me and you. By God, if it overtakes us, there is no way out for me or you, according to what our Prophet ﷺ charged us with, except to emerge from it as we entered it.' *Additional routes:* Abu al-Hasan said: Ibrahim b. Nasr narrated it to us: al-Himmani narrated to us: Abu 'Awanah narrated to us: Qatadah narrated to us, from al-Hasan, from Usayd b. al-Mutashammis, and mentioned something similar. He also said: Ibrahim b. Nasr narrated it to us: Musaddad and Muhammad b. al-Minhal narrated to us; they said: Yazid, meaning Ibn Zuray', narrated to us, the wording belonging to Muhammad: Yunus b. 'Ubayd informed us, from al-Hasan, from Usayd b. al-Mutashammis, from Abu Musa, with a similar report.

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Bashshar narrated to us, saying: Safwan b. 'Isa narrated to us, saying: 'Abd Allah b. 'Ubayd, the muezzin of the mosque of Juradan, narrated to us, saying: 'Udaysah bt. Uhban narrated to me, saying:

Matn

'When 'Ali b. Abi Talib came here to Basra, he entered upon my father and said: Abu Muslim, will you not help me against these people? He said: Yes. He called his servant girl: Girl, bring out my sword! She brought it out. He drew it a handspan, and it was made of wood. He said: My dear friend and your cousin ﷺ instructed me that when tribulation arises among Muslims, I should take a wooden sword. If you wish, I will come with you. 'Ali replied: I have no need of you or your sword.'

Chapter3961/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 833
English
Isnad

'Imran b. Musa al-Laythi narrated to us: 'Abd al-Warith b. Sa'id narrated to us: Muhammad b. Juhadah narrated to us, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Tharwan, from Huzayl b. Shurahbil, from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'Before the Hour there will be trials like portions of a dark night. A man will be a believer in the morning and an unbeliever by evening, or a believer in the evening and an unbeliever by morning. The seated will be better than the standing, the standing better than the walking, and the walking better than the running. Break your bows, cut your bowstrings, and strike stones with your swords. If someone enters upon one of you, let him be like the better of Adam's two sons.' Ibn Majah said: 'This Tharwan is Abu Qays al-Awdi.'

Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا عِمْرَانُ بْنُ مُوسَى اللَّيْثِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ جُحَادَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَن بْنِ ثَرْوَانَ، عَنْ هُزَيْلٍ بْنِ شُرَحْبِيلَ، عَنْ أَبِي مُوسَى الْأَشْعَرِيِّ قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "إِنَّ بَيْنَ يَدَيِ السَّاعَةِ فِتَنًا كَقِطَعِ اللَّيْلِ الْمُظْلِمِ، يُصْبحُ الرَّجُلُ فِيهَا مُؤْمِنًا وَيُمْسِي كَافِرًا، وَيُمْسِي مُؤمِنًا وَيُصْبحُ كَافِرًا، الْقَاعِدُ فِيهَا خَيْرٌ مِنَ الْقَائِمِ، وَالْقَائِمُ فِيهَا خَيْرٌ مِنَ الْمَاشِي، وَالْمَاشِي فِيهَا خَيْرٌ مِنَ السَّاعِي، فَكَسِّرُوا قِسِيَّكُمْ، وَقَطِّعُوا أَوْتَارَكُمْ، وَاضْربُوا بِسُيُوفِكُمُ الْحِجَارَةَ، فَإِنْ دُخِلَ عَلَى أَحَدِكُمْ، فَلْيَكُنْ كَخَيْرِ ابْنَيْ (4) آدَمَ". [د: 4259، ت: 2204، تحفة: 9032] قَالَ ابْنُ مَاجَهْ: ثَرْوَانُ هَذَا أَبُو قَيْسٍ الْأَوْدِيُّ هُوَ (5) .

Chapter3962/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 833
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shaybah narrated to us: Yazid b. Harun narrated to us, from Hammad b. Salamah, from Thabit—or from 'Ali b. Zayd b. Jud'an, about which Abu Bakr was uncertain—from Abu Burdah, who said:

Matn

'I entered upon Muhammad b. Maslamah, and he said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: There will be tribulation, division and disagreement. When that happens, take your sword to Mount Uhud and strike it until it breaks. Then remain in your house until an errant hand or the death ordained for you overtakes you. It has indeed happened, and I have done what the Messenger of God ﷺ said.'

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا يَزِيدُ بْنُ هَارُونَ، عَنْ حَمَّادِ بْنِ سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ ثَابِتٍ - أَوْ عَلِيِّ بْنِ زَيْدِ بْنِ جُدْعَانَ، شَكَّ أَبُو بَكْرٍ - عَنْ أَبِي بُرْدَةَ قَالَ:

Matn

دَخَلْتُ عَلَى مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ مَسْلَمَةَ فَقَالَ: إِنَّ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: "إِنَّهَا سَتَكُونُ فِتْنَةٌ وَفُرْقَةٌ وَاخْتِلَافٌ، فَإِذَا كَانَ ذَلِكَ (6) ، فَأْتِ بِسَيْفِكَ أُحُدًا، فَاضْرِبْهُ حَتَّى يَنْقَطِعَ، ثُمَّ اجْلِسْ فِي بَيْتِكَ حَتَّى تَأْتِيَكَ يَدٌ خَاطِئَةٌ، أَوْ مَنِيَّةٌ قَاضِيَةٌ". فَقَدْ وَقَعَتْ، وَفَعَلْتُ مَا قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم.
