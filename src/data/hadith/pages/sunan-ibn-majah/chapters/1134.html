Chapter3162/ 5
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 680
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shaybah and Hisham b. 'Ammar narrated to us, saying: Sufyan b. 'Uyaynah narrated to us, from 'Ubaydullah b. Abi Yazid, from his father, from Siba' (3) b. Thabit, from Umm Kurz, who said:

Matn

I heard the Prophet ﷺ say: "For a boy, two equivalent sheep (4), and for a girl, one sheep."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ وَهِشَامُ بْنُ عَمَّارٍ، قَالَا: حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ بْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللهِ بْنِ أَبِي يَزِيدَ، عَنْ أبِيهِ، عَنْ سِبَاعِ (3) بْنِ ثَابِتٍ، عَنْ أُمِّ كَرْزٍ قَالَتْ:

Matn

سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: "عَنِ الغُلَامِ شَاتَانِ مُكَافَأَتَانِ (4) ، وَعَنِ الْجَارِيَةِ شَاةٌ".

Printed page 680
1. In the printed edition: "Book". 2. Addition from al-Mahmudiyyah. 3. In the Turkish copy it is vowelled with a fathah on the sin; then in its margin it is vowelled with a kasrah on the sin, with a correction mark placed over it. I say: Al-Nawawi and Ibn Hajar stated explicitly that it is with a kasrah on the sin. 4. Equal. I say: Thus in the Turkish copy, while in the Paris copy: "mutakafi'atan". Al-'Iraqi said in Tarh al-Tathrib (5/214): "It is with a kasrah on the fa' and a hamzah after it; this is the correct form according to the people of the language. Among those who stated it explicitly is al-Jawhari in his Sihah, who said: 'The hadith scholars say mukafa'atan, meaning with a fathah on the fa', but the correct form is with a kasrah.' End quote." Then he reported from al-Khattabi that the fathah is preferable, and reported from al-Zamakhshari that both are permissible. 5. In al-Taymuriyyah: "shatan" (two sheep). 6. In the Paris copy: "'aqiqatan"; and in the margin of the Turkish copy he wrote: "A copy: 'aqiqah". 7. Addition from al-Taymuriyyah. 8. In the Paris copy: "al-sabi'" (the seventh). 9. A sheep slaughtered in Rajab.

Chapter3163/ 5
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 680
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shaybah narrated to us: 'Affan narrated to us, saying: Hammad b. Salamah narrated to us, saying: 'Abdullah b. 'Uthman b. Khuthaym informed us, from Yusuf b. Mahak, from Hafsah bint 'Abd al-Rahman, from 'A'ishah, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ commanded us to perform the 'aqiqah: for a boy, two sheep (5), and for a girl, one sheep.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا عَفَّانُ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا حَمَّادُ بْنُ سَلَمَةَ، قَالَ: أخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ اللهِ بْنُ عُثْمَانَ بْنِ خُثَيْمٍ، عَنْ يُوسُفَ بْنِ مَاهَكَ، عَنْ حَفْصَةَ بِنْتِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ قَالَتْ:

Matn

أَمَرَنَا رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنْ نَعُقَّ، عَنِ الْغُلَامِ شَاتَيْنِ (5) وَعَنِ الجَارِيَةِ شَاةً.

Chapter3164/ 5
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 680
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shaybah narrated to us: 'Abdullah b. Numayr narrated to us: Hisham b. Hassan narrated to us, from Hafsah bint Sirin, from Salman b. 'Amir,

Matn

that he heard the Prophet ﷺ say: "With the boy is his 'aqiqah (6), so shed blood on his behalf and remove the harm from him."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللهِ بْنُ نُمَيْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامُ بْنُ حَسَّانَ، عَنْ حَفْصَةَ بِنْتِ سِيرِينَ، عَنْ سَلْمَانَ بْنِ عَامِرٍ:

Matn

أنَّهُ سَمِعَ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: "إِنَّ مَعَ الْغُلامِ عَقِيقَتُهُ (6) ، فَأَهْرِيقُوا عَنْهُ دَمًا، وَأَمِيطُوا عَنْهُ الْأَذَى".

Chapter3165/ 5
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 680
English
Isnad

Hisham b. 'Ammar narrated to us: Shu'ayb b. Ishaq narrated to us: Sa'id [b. Abi 'Arubah] (7) narrated to us, from Qatadah, from al-Hasan, from Samurah, from the Prophet ﷺ, who said:

Matn

"Every boy is held in pledge for his 'aqiqah; it is slaughtered for him on his seventh day (8), his head is shaved, and he is named."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامُ بْنُ عَمَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعَيْبُ بْنُ إِسْحَاقَ، حَدَّثَنَا سَعِيدُ [بْنُ أَبِي عَرُوبَةَ] (7) ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنِ الحَسَنِ، عَنْ سَمُرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ:

Matn

"كُلُّ غُلَامٍ مُرْتَهَنٌ بِعَقِيقَتِهِ، تُذْبَحُ عَنْهُ يَوْمَ سَابِعِهِ (8) ، وَيُحْلَقُ رَأْسُهُ، وَيُسَمَّى".

Chapter3166/ 5
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 680
English
Isnad

Ya'qub [b. Humayd] (7) b. Kasib narrated to us: 'Abdullah b. Wahb narrated to us: 'Amr b. al-Harith narrated to me, from Ayyub b. Musa,

Matn

that he narrated to him that Yazid b. 'Abd al-Muzani narrated to him that the Prophet ﷺ said: "The 'aqiqah is sacrificed for the boy, and his head is not touched with blood."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا يَعْقُوبُ [بْنُ حُمَيْدِ] (7) بْنِ كَاسِبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللهِ بْنُ وَهْبٍ، حَدَّثَنِي عَمْرُو بْنُ الْحَارِثِ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ بْنِ مُوسَى،

Matn

أنَّهُ حَدَّثَهُ أنَّ يزِيدَ بْنَ عَبْدٍ الْمُزَنِيَّ حَدَّثَهُ: أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: "يُعَقُّ عَنِ الْغُلَامِ، وَلَا يُمَسُّ رَأْسُهُ بِدَمٍ".
