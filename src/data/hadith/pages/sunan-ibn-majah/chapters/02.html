Chapter12/ 11
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 51
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shayba narrated to us; [he said:] Zayd b. al-Hubab narrated to us, from Mu'awiya b. Salih, who said: al-Hasan b. Jabir narrated to me, from al-Miqdam b. Ma'dikarib al-Kindi,

Matn

that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: ‘Soon a man will recline on his couch and be told one of my reports. He will say: Between us and you is the Book of God: what we find lawful in it we regard as lawful, and what we find forbidden in it we regard as forbidden. Be aware that what the Messenger of God has forbidden is like what God has forbidden.’

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، [قَالَ:] (4) حَدَّثَنَا زَيْدُ بْنُ الْحُبَابِ، عَنْ مُعَاوِيَةَ بْنِ صَالِحٍ قَالَ: حَدَّثَنِي الْحَسَنُ بْنُ جَابِرٍ، عَنِ الْمِقْدَامِ بْنِ مَعْدِي كَرِبَ الْكِنْدِيِّ؛ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ:

Matn

"يُوشِكُ الرَّجُلُ مُتَّكِئًا عَلَى أَرِيكَتِهِ يُحَدَّثُ بِحَدِيثٍ مِنْ حَدِيثِي، فَيَقُولُ: بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَكُمْ كِتَابُ اللهِ [عز وجل] (4) ، فَمَا (7) وَجَدْنَا فِيهِ مِنْ حَلَالٍ اسْتَحْلَلْنَاهُ، وَمَا وَجَدْنَا فِيهِ مِنْ حَرَامٍ حَرَّمْنَاهُ! أَلَّا وَإِنَّ مَا حَرَّمَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِثْلُ مَا حَرَّمَ اللهُ [عز وجل] (4) ".

Chapter13/ 11
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 51
English
Isnad

Nasr b. 'Ali al-Jahdami narrated to us. He said: Sufyan b. 'Uyayna narrated to me in his house, when I asked him about it, from Salim Abu al-Nadr, then he proceeded in the narration and said,

Matn

“or Zayd b. Aslam,” from 'Ubayd Allah b. Abi Rafi', from his father, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Let me not find one of you reclining on his couch when an instruction or prohibition of mine reaches him, saying, ‘I do not know. We follow only what we find in the Book of God.’”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا نَصْرُ بْنُ عَلِيٍّ الْجَهْضَمِيُّ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي سفْيَانُ بْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ فِي بَيْتِهِ - أَنَا سَأَلْتُهُ عَنْهُ - عَنْ سَالِمٍ أَبِي النَّضْرِ - ثُمَّ مَرَّ فِي الْحَدِيثِ، قَالَ:

Matn

أَوْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ - عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللهِ بْنِ أَبِي رَافِعٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: "لَا أُلْفِيَنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ مُتَّكِئًا عَلَى أَرِيكَتِهِ، يَأْتِيهِ الْأَمْرُ مِمَّا أَمَرْتُ بِهِ أَوْ نَهَيْتُ عَنْهُ، فَيَقُولُ: لَا أَدْرِي، مَا وَجَدْنَا فِي كِتَابِ اللهِ اتَّبَعْنَاهُ".

Chapter15/ 11
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 51
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Rumh b. al-Muhajir al-Misri narrated to us; [he said:] al-Layth b. Sa'd informed us, from Ibn Shihab, from 'Urwa b. al-Zubayr,

Matn

that 'Abd Allah b. al-Zubayr told him that a man of the Ansar disputed with al-Zubayr before the Messenger of God ﷺ about the channels of the stony lava tract through which they watered their date palms. The Ansari said, “Let the water pass through,” but al-Zubayr refused. They brought their dispute before the Messenger of God ﷺ, who said: “Water your land, al-Zubayr, and then release [the water] to your neighbor.” The Ansari became angry and said, “Is it because he is your aunt’s son?” The Messenger of God’s ﷺ face changed color. He then said: “Al-Zubayr, water your land, then hold the water until it reaches the retaining walls.” Al-Zubayr said: I believe the verse was revealed about this: “But no, by your Lord, they will not believe until they make you judge concerning what they dispute among themselves, then find no discomfort within themselves about what you have judged and submit fully” [Qur'an 4:65].

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ رُمْحِ بْنِ الْمُهَاجِرِ الْمِصْرِيُّ [قَالَ:] (8) أَخْبَرَنَا اللَّيْثُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ بْنِ الزُّبَيْرِ،

Matn

أَنَّ عَبْدَ اللهِ بْنَ الزُّبَيْرِ حَدَّثَهُ: أَنَّ رَجُلًا مِنَ الْأَنْصَارِ خَاصَمَ الزُّبَيْرَ عِنْدَ رَسُولِ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي شِرَاجِ الْحَرَّةِ (9) الَّتِي يَسْقُونَ بِهَا النَّخْلَ، فَقَالَ الْأَنْصَارِيُّ: سَرِّحِ الْمَاءَ يَمُرُّ، فَأَبَى عَلَيْهِ، فَاخْتَصَمُوا (1) عِنْدَ رَسُولِ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "اسْقِ يَا زُبَيْرُ! ثُمَّ أَرْسِلِ [الْمَاءَ] (2) إِلَى جَارِكَ". فَغَضِبَ الْأَنْصَارِيُّ، فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! أَنْ كَانَ ابْنَ عَمَّتِكَ؟! فَتَلَوَّنَ وَجْهُ رَسُولِ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، ثُمَّ قَالَ: "يَا زُبَيْرُ! اسْقِ، ثُمَّ احْبِسِ الْمَاءَ حَتَّى يَرْجِعَ إِلَى الْجَدْرِ" (3) ، قَالَ: فَقَالَ الزُّبَيْرُ: وَاللهِ إِنِّي لَأَحْسِبُ هَذِهِ الْآيَةَ نَزَلَتْ فِي ذَلِكَ: {فَلَا وَرَبِّكَ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ حَتَّى يُحَكِّمُوكَ فِيمَا شَجَرَ بَيْنَهُمْ ثُمَّ لَا يَجِدُوا فِي أَنْفُسِهِمْ حَرَجًا مِمَّا قَضَيْتَ وَيُسَلِّمُوا تَسْلِيمًا (65)} [النساء: 65] (4) .

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya al-Naysaburi narrated to us; 'Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us; Ma'mar informed us, from al-Zuhri, from Salim, from Ibn 'Umar,

Matn

that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Do not prevent the female servants of God from praying in the mosque.” One of Ibn 'Umar’s sons said, “We will certainly prevent them.” Ibn 'Umar became extremely angry and said: “[I] am telling you a report from the Messenger of God ﷺ and [you] say, ‘We will prevent them’?”

Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ يَحْيَى النَّيْسَابُورِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ سَالِمٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ،

Matn

أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: "لَا تَمْنَعُوا إِمَاءَ اللهِ أَنْ يُصَلِّينَ فِي الْمَسْجِدِ"، فَقَالَ ابْنٌ لَهُ: إِنَّا لَنَمْنَعُهُنَّ، قَالَ: فَغَضِبَ غَضَبًا شَدِيدًا، فَقَالَ: [إِنِّي] (5) أُحَدِّثُكَ عَنْ رَسُولِ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَ [إِنَّكَ] (5) تَقُولُ: إِنَّا لَنَمْنَعُهُنَّ؟!

Chapter17/ 11
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 52
English
Isnad

Ahmad b. Thabit al-Jahdari and Abu 'Umar Hafs b. 'Amr narrated to us. They said: 'Abd al-Wahhab al-Thaqafi narrated to us; [he said:] Ayyub narrated to us, from Sa'id b. Jubayr, from 'Abd Allah b. Mughaffal. A nephew of his was sitting beside him and was flicking pebbles. He forbade him, saying that the Messenger of God ﷺ had prohibited this, saying:

Matn

“It neither brings down game nor wounds an enemy, but it breaks teeth and puts out eyes.” His nephew resumed flicking pebbles, so he said: “I tell you that the Messenger of God ﷺ forbade it, and then [you go back to] flicking pebbles? I shall never speak to you again.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ بْنُ ثَابِتٍ الْجَحْدَرِيُّ وَأَبُو عُمَرَ (6) حَفْصُ بْنُ عَمْرٍو (7) ، قَالَا: حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَهَّابِ الثَّقَفِيُّ [قَالَ:] (8) حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْن مُغَفَّلٍ،

Matn

أَنَّهُ كَانَ جَالِسًا إِلَى جَنْبِهِ ابْنُ أَخٍ لَهُ، فَخَذَفَ (9) ، فَنَهَاهُ، وَقَالَ: إِنَّ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم نَهَى عَنْهَا، فَقَالَ (10) : "إِنَّهَا لَا تَصِيدُ صَيْدًا، وَلَا تَنْكَأُ (11) عَدُوًّا، وَإِنَّهَا تَكْسِرُ السِّنَّ، وَتَفْقَأُ الْعَيْنَ". قَالَ: فَعَادَ ابْنُ أَخِيهِ يَخْذِفُ (12) ، فَقَالَ: أُحَدِّثُكَ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم نَهَى عَنْهَا، ثُمَّ [عُدْتَ] (13) تَخْذِفُ؟! لَا أُكَلِّمُكَ أَبَدًا.

Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 52
English
Isnad

Hisham b. 'Ammar narrated to us; [he said:] Yahya b. Hamza narrated to us; Burd b. Sinan narrated to me, from Ishaq b. Qabisa, from his father,

Matn

that 'Ubada b. al-Samit, the Ansari leader and Companion of the Messenger of God ﷺ, went on an expedition with Mu'awiya into Byzantine territory. He saw people exchanging pieces of gold for gold dinars and pieces of silver for silver dirhams. He said: “People, you are consuming usury. I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: ‘Do not exchange gold for gold except equal for equal, without any surplus or deferment.’” Mu'awiya told him: “Abu al-Walid, I see no usury in this other than when payment is deferred.” 'Ubada replied: “I am narrating to you from the Messenger of God ﷺ, and you give me your own opinion? If God grants me the opportunity to depart, I shall not live alongside you in any land over which you have authority over me.” On returning from the expedition he went to Medina. 'Umar b. al-Khattab asked him: “What has brought you back, Abu al-Walid?” He told him the story and what he had said about not living alongside Mu'awiya. 'Umar replied: “Return to your land, Abu al-Walid! May God disgrace any land in which you and people like you are absent!” He wrote to Mu'awiya: “You have no authority over him. Require the people to follow what he has said, for that is the correct ruling.”
