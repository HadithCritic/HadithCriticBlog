Chapter4273/ 9
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 899
English
Isnad

[Editorial marker 7.] Abu Bakr b. Abi Shayba narrated to us; 'Abbad b. al-'Awwam narrated to us, from Hajjaj, from 'Atiyya, from Abu Sa'id, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: ‘The two keepers of the Horn have two horns in their hands—or, each has a horn in his hands—and keep looking intently, awaiting the command to blow.’

العربية
Isnad

(7) حَدَّثنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبَّادُ بْنُ الْعَوَّامِ، عَنْ حَجَّاجٍ، عَنْ عَطِيَّةَ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "إِنَّ صَاحِبَيِ الصُّورِ، بِأَيْدِيهِمَا -أَوْ فِي أَيْدِيهِمَا- قَرْنَانِ، يُلَاحِظَان النَّظَرَ مَتَى يُؤْمَرَانِ" (1) .

1. I say: The annotator of the Risalah edition of Sunan Ibn Majah said: "Note: In the addenda to al-Albani's Silsilat al-Ahadith al-Sahihah (3/489) it says: 'Note: Ibn Jarir al-Tabari said that reports have converged from the Messenger of God ﷺ that he said: Israfil has placed the Trumpet to his mouth and lowered his forehead, waiting to be ordered to blow. Al-Hafiz Ibn Kathir quoted this in his Tafsir (2/172), adding: Muslim transmitted it in his Sahih. This is a complete error.' Then he spoke about Ibn Kathir's abridger in terms unbefitting scholars. The shaykh, whom his admirers call the hafiz of the age, should have verified whether Ibn Kathir really said 'Muslim transmitted it' or whether this was inserted by copyists. Had he engaged in proper textual editing, he would have consulted the carefully edited edition and found no such attribution. See volume 3 (p. 276) of the al-Sha'b edition, whose three editors explain that earlier printed editions attributed the report to Muslim, whereas the Azhar manuscript they relied upon did not..." I say: This annotator's words show hostility and injustice toward our teacher. Our teacher, ﵀, informed readers that in the printed Tafsir of Ibn Kathir the report was attributed to Muslim; that is true of all the earlier printed editions. Must he be required to consult manuscripts and seek the most accurate copies for a passing reference when his project was not a critical edition of Ibn Kathir's Tafsir? Therefore he simply wrote: "This is a complete error." The annotator also ignored and suppressed the rest of our teacher's words, which exposed his beloved al-Sabuni. Our teacher wrote: "This is a complete error, which his abridger, Shaykh al-Sabuni (1/590), followed. This shows his ignorance of this discipline, his lack of care for it, and his blind imitation. He did not stop there but added another wrong: he stole this source reference from Ibn Kathir and put it in his own footnote, giving the reader the impression that it was his own learning!" These were our teacher's precise words. The annotator omitted them, substituting: "Then he spoke about his abridger in terms unbefitting scholars." Our question to this annotator, may God guide him, is: Who more deserves criticism: our teacher, who denied that the report occurs in Sahih Muslim, or al-Sabuni, who affirmed its presence there by citing it in his footnote? Who ought to consult the manuscripts and carefully edited copies—the one who made a passing quotation from a book, or the person who set about abridging, editing and annotating it? Is it fair, learned annotator, for you to end your discussion with: "He should not use these errors as grounds to disparage or insult others," while excusing al-Sabuni, who accepted what was in the printed Tafsir of Ibn Kathir and even attributed it to himself, yet refusing to excuse al-Albani and blaming him for not consulting manuscripts, as though the readings in Ibn Kathir's older printed editions had come out of nothing? Moreover, does the fact that a reading is absent from the carefully prepared Azhar manuscript prove it cannot exist in other carefully prepared manuscripts? You yourself edited Sunan Ibn Majah using a careful manuscript, yet found material missing from it but present in less carefully copied manuscripts. That material undoubtedly belongs to the Sunan, as is shown by the attributions of the great hadith scholars to Ibn Majah. That is what my pen has produced in this brief response, although the subject deserves a fuller reply, perhaps elsewhere. May God guide us and the learned editor to what He loves and approves. 2. In the Paris manuscript, the Arabic reading is: “فذكر”. 3. An addition found in the Taymuriyya manuscript.

Chapter4274/ 9
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 900
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shayba narrated to us; 'Ali b. Mushir narrated to us, from Muhammad b. 'Amr, from Abu Salama, from Abu Hurayra, who said:

Matn

A Jewish man in the market of Medina said: ‘By Him who chose Moses above mankind!’ A man of the Ansar raised his hand and slapped him, saying: ‘Do you say that while the Messenger of God ﷺ is among us?’ I mentioned it to the Messenger of God ﷺ, who said: ‘God Most High said: “The Horn will be blown, and all in the heavens and all on earth will fall unconscious, except whom God wills. Then it will be blown again, and at once they will stand looking on” (Qur'an 39:68). I will be the first to raise my head and will find Moses holding one of the legs of the Throne. I do not know whether he raised his head before me or was among those God exempted. Whoever says, “I am better than Jonah son of Matta,” has lied.’

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ مُسْهِرٍ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَجُلٌ مِنَ الْيَهُودِ بِسُوقِ الْمَدِينَةِ: وَالَّذِي اصْطفَى مُوسَى عَلَى الْبَشَرِ، فَرَفَعَ رَجُلٌ مِنَ الْأَنْصَارِ يَدَهُ فَلَطَمَهُ، قَالَ: تَقُولُ هَذَا، وَفِينَا رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَذَكَرَتُ (2) ذَلِكَ لِرسُولِ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَقَالَ: "قَالَ اللهُ عز وجل: {وَنُفِخَ فِي الصُّورِ فَصَعِقَ مَنْ فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَنْ فِي الْأَرْضِ إِلَّا مَنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ ثُمَّ نُفِخَ فِيهِ أُخْرَى فَإِذَا هُمْ قِيَامٌ يَنْظُرُونَ (68)} [الزمر: 68] فَأَكُونُ أَوَّلَ مَنْ رَفَعَ رَأْسَهُ، فَإِذَا أَنَا بِمُوسى آخِذٌ بِقَائِمَةٍ مِنْ قَوَائِمِ الْعَرْشِ، فَلَا أَدْرِي أَرَفَعَ رَأْسَهُ قَبْلِي، أَوْ كَانَ مِمَّنِ اسْتَثْنَى اللهُ [عز وجل] (3) ؟ وَمَنْ قَالَ: أَنَا خَيْرٌ مِنْ يُونُسَ بْنِ مَتَّى، فَقَدْ كَذَبَ".

Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 900
English
Isnad

Hisham b. 'Ammar and Muhammad b. al-Sabbah narrated to us; both said: 'Abd al-'Aziz b. Abi Hazim narrated to us, saying: My father narrated to me, from 'Ubayd Allah b. Miqsam, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Umar, who said:

Matn

I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ on the pulpit say: ‘The Almighty will take His heavens and His earths in His hand’—and he clenched his hand, opening and closing it—‘and then say: “I am the Almighty! I am the King! Where are the tyrants? Where are the arrogant?”’ The Messenger of God ﷺ swayed from side to side until I saw the base of the pulpit moving so much that I thought it might fall with the Messenger of God ﷺ upon it.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامُ بْنُ عَمَّارٍ وَمُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الصَّبَّاحِ، قَالَا: حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ بْنُ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنِي أَبِي، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللهِ بْنِ مِقْسَمٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ قَالَ:

Matn

سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَهُوَ عَلى الْمِنْبَرِ يَقُولُ: "يَأْخُذُ الْجَبَّارُ سَمَاوَاتِهِ وَأَرَضِيهِ بِيَدِهِ - وَقَبَضَ يَدَهُ، فَجَعَلَ يَقبِضُهَا وَيَبْسُطُهَا -، ثُمَّ يَقُولُ: أَنَا الْجَبَّارُ، أَنَا الْمَلِكُ، أَيْنَ الْجَبَّارُونَ؟ أَيْنَ الْمُتَكَبِّرُونَ؟ " قَالَ: وَيَتَمَايَلُ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ يَمِينِهِ وَعَنْ شِمَالِهِ، حَتَّى نَظَرْتُ إِلَى الْمِنْبَرِ يَتَحَرَّكُ مِنْ أَسْفَلِ شَيْءٍ مِنْهُ، حَتَّى إِنِّي لَأقُولُ: أَسَاقِطٌ هُوَ بِرَسُولِ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم"".

Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 901
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shayba narrated to us; Abu Khalid al-Ahmar narrated to us, from Hatim b. Abi Saghira, from Ibn Abi Mulayka, from al-Qasim, who said:

Matn

'A'isha said: I asked: ‘Messenger of God, how will people be gathered on the Day of Resurrection?’ He replied: ‘Barefoot and naked.’ I said: ‘And the women?’ He replied: ‘And the women.’ I said: ‘Messenger of God, will they not be ashamed?’ He said: ‘'A'isha, the situation will be too grave for them to look at one another.’

Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shayba narrated to us, saying: Waki' narrated to us, from 'Ali b. 'Ali b. Rifa'a, from al-Hasan, from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, who said:

Matn

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shayba narrated to us; 'Isa b. Yunus and Abu Khalid al-Ahmar narrated to us, from Ibn 'Awn, from Nafi', from Ibn 'Umar, from the Prophet ﷺ, concerning ‘The day when mankind stands before the Lord of the Worlds’ (Qur'an 83:6). He said:

حَدَّثنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا عِيسَى بْنُ يُونُسَ وَأَبُو خَالِدٍ الْأَحْمَرُ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَوْنٍ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم:

Matn

{يَوْمَ يَقُومُ النَّاسُ لِرَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ (6)} [المطففين: 6] قَالَ: "يَقُومُ أَحَدُهُمْ فِي رَشْحِهِ إِلَى أَنْصَافِ أُذُنَيْهِ".

Chapter4279/ 9
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 901
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shayba narrated to us; 'Ali b. Mushir narrated to us, from Dawud, from al-Sha'bi, from Masruq, from 'A'isha, who said:

Matn

I asked the Messenger of God ﷺ about the words: ‘On the day when the earth is changed into another earth, and the heavens’ (Qur'an 14:48): ‘Where will people be that day?’ He replied: ‘Upon the Bridge.’

Isnad

حَدَّثنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ مُسْهِرٍ، عَنْ دَاوُدَ، عَنِ الشَّعْبِيِّ، عَنْ مَسْرُوقٍ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ قَالَتْ:

Chapter4280/ 9
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 901
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shayba narrated to us, saying: 'Abd al-A'la narrated to us, from Muhammad b. Ishaq; 'Ubayd Allah b. al-Mughira narrated to me, from Sulayman b. 'Amr b. 'Abd al-'Utwari, a man of Banu Layth raised in the care of Abu Sa'id, who said: I heard him—that is, Abu Sa'id—say:

Matn

I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: ‘The Bridge will be set across the middle of Hell, with hooks like the hooked thorns of the sa'dan plant. People will attempt to cross it: some will pass safely and unharmed; some will be scratched but then escape; others will be detained by it and cast upside down into Hell.’

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْأَعْلَى، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ إِسْحَاقَ، حَدَّثَنِي عُبَيْدُ اللهِ بْنُ الْمُغِيرَةِ، عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ عَمْرِو بْنِ عَبْدٍ (3) الْعُتْوَارِيِّ - أَحَدِ بَنِي لَيْثٍ - قَالَ:

Matn

وَكَانَ فِي حَجْرِ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُهُ - يَعْنِي: أَبَا سَعِيدٍ يَقُولُ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: "يُوضَعُ الصِّرَاطُ بَيْنَ ظَهْرَانَيْ جَهَنَّمَ، عَلَى حَسَكٍ كَحَسَكِ (4) السَّعْدَانِ، ثُمَّ يَسْتَجِيزُ النَّاسُ، فَنَاجٍ مُسَلَّمٌ، وَمَخْدُوجٌ بِهِ ثُمَّ نَاجٍ، وَمُحْتَبَسٌ بِهِ وَمَنْكُوسٌ فِيهَا".

Chapter4281/ 9
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 901
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shaybah narrated to us; Abu Mu'awiyah narrated to us, from al-A'mash, from Abu Sufyan, from Jabir, from Umm Mubashshir, from Hafsah, who said:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ said, 'I hope that, God willing, none of those who were present at Badr and al-Hudaybiyah will enter the Fire.' I said, 'Messenger of God, did God not say, {There is none of you but will come to it; that is a decree binding upon your Lord} [Maryam 19:71]?' He said, 'Have you not heard Him say, {Then We shall deliver those who were mindful of God and leave the wrongdoers therein upon their knees} [Maryam 19:72]?'

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مُعَاوِيَةَ، عَنِ الْأَعْمَشِ، عَنْ أَبِي سُفْيَانَ، عَنْ جَابِرٍ، عَنْ أُمِّ مُبَشِّرٍ، عَنْ حَفْصَةَ قَالَتْ:

Matn

قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "إِنِّي لَأَرْجُو أَلَّا يَدْخُلَ النَّارَ أَحَدٌ - إِنْ شَاءَ اللهُ تَعَالَى - مِمَّنْ شَهِدَ بَدْرًا وَالْحُدَيْبِيَةَ"، قَالَتْ: قُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! أَلَيْسَ قَدْ قَالَ اللهُ: {وَإِنْ مِنْكُمْ إِلَّا وَارِدُهَا كَانَ عَلَى رَبِّكَ حَتْمًا مَقْضِيًّا (71)} [مريم: 71]؟ قَالَ: "أَلَمْ (1) تَسْمَعِيهِ يَقُولُ: {ثُمَّ نُنَجِّي الَّذِينَ اتَّقَوْا وَنَذَرُ الظَّالِمِينَ فِيهَا جِثِيًّا (72)} [مريم: 72]؟ ".
