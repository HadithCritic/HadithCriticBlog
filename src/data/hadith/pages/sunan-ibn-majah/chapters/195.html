Chapter765/ 3
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 196
English
Isnad

'Ali b. Muhammad narrated to us: Waki' narrated to us, from Abu Sinan Sa'id b. Sinan, from 'Alqamah b. Marthad, from Sulayman b. Buraydah, from his father, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ had prayed when a man called out, “Who has seen the red camel?” The Prophet ﷺ said: “May you not find it! Mosques were built only for the purposes for which they were built.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا وَكِيعٌ، عَنْ أَبِي سِنَانٍ سَعِيدِ بْنِ سِنَانٍ، عَنْ عَلْقَمَةَ بْنِ مَرْثَدٍ، عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ بُرَيْدَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ قَالَ:

Matn

صَلَّى رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَقَالَ رَجُلٌ: مَنْ دَعَا إِلَى الْجَمَلِ الْأَحْمَرِ؟ فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "لَا وَجَدْتَهُ، إِنَّمَا بُنِيَتِ الْمَسَاجِدُ لِمَا بُنِيَتْ لَهُ".

Chapter766/ 3
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 196
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Rumh narrated to us: 'Abd Allah b. Lahi'ah informed us; and Abu Kurayb narrated to us: Hatim b. Isma'il narrated to us. Both transmitted from Ibn 'Ajlan, from 'Amr b. Shu'ayb, from his father, from his grandfather,

Matn

that the Messenger of God ﷺ forbade announcing a lost item in the mosque.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ رُمْحٍ، أَنْبَأَنَا عَبْدُ اللهِ بْنُ لَهِيعَةَ ح وَحَدَّثَنَا أَبُو كُرَيْبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا حَاتِمُ بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، جَمِيعًا عَنِ ابْنِ عَجْلَانَ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ شُعَيْبٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ جَدِّهِ،

Matn

أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم نَهَى عَنْ إِنْشَادِ الضَّالَّةِ فِي الْمَسْجِدِ.

Chapter767/ 3
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 197
English
Isnad

Ya'qub b. Humayd b. Kasib narrated to us: 'Abd Allah b. Wahb narrated to us: Haywah b. Shurayh informed me, from Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Rahman al-Asadi, Abu al-Aswad, from Abu 'Abd Allah, the freedman of Shaddad b. al-Had, who heard Abu Hurayrah say:

Matn

I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: “Whoever hears a man announcing a lost item in the mosque should say, ‘May God not restore it to you!’ Mosques were not built for this.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا يَعْقُوبُ بْنُ حُمَيْدِ بْنِ كَاسِبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللهِ بْنُ وَهْبٍ، أَخْبَرَنِي حَيْوَةُ بْنُ شُرَيْحٍ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ الْأَسَدِيِّ أَبِي الْأَسْوَدِ، عَنْ أَبِي عَبْدِ اللهِ مَوْلَىَ شَدَّادِ بْنِ الْهَادِ،

Matn

أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ يَقُولُ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: "مَنْ سَمِعَ رَجُلًا يَنْشُدُ ضَالَّةً فِي الْمَسْجِدِ فَلْيَقُلْ: لَا رَدَّ اللهُ عَلَيْكَ؛ فَإِنَّ الْمَسَاجِدَ لَمْ تُبْنَ لِهَذَا".

Some of the brothers who collected revisions to our teacher’s judgments, ﵀, maintained that our teacher had withdrawn his authentication of the opening formula ‘In the name of God’ and the phrase about forgiveness in this hadith. They requested that the verdict of our teacher in Brother Mashhur’s edition of Sunan Ibn Majah be corrected. The opposite is true. Our teacher initially regarded the words ‘In the name of God’ as weak, but later judged them fair in the new edition of al-Kalim al-Tayyib (no. 64). He likewise initially considered the phrase about forgiveness weak, but later authenticated it on the basis of corroborating reports. What led the brother in question into error was his finding the earlier weak verdicts in al-Thamar al-Mustatab and Hidayat al-Ruwah, both of which were printed after Sahih Sunan Ibn Majah. That reasoning is mistaken. Printing the two books later does not establish that their critical judgments were later. Al-Thamar al-Mustatab was published after our teacher’s death, and its judgments were among his earlier judgments; indeed, he revised many of them, as those close to him know. The same is true of Hidayat al-Ruwah, which was published after his death without his review. Its judgments are not necessarily his final judgments; rather, some of them he subsequently revised. The evaluations in Hidayat al-Ruwah were drawn from our teacher’s second set of annotations on al-Mishkah, which did not represent his final verdicts, and he changed and revised them. I apologize for this digression, which was prompted by the need to remind our brothers to be careful when reporting revisions to our teacher’s judgments.
