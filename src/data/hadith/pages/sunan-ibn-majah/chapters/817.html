Chapter2430/ 3
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 519
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Khalaf al-Asqalani narrated to us, Ya'la narrated to us, Sulayman b. Yasir (4) narrated to us, from Qays b. Rumi, who said:

Matn

Sulayman (5) b. Adhnan (6) used to lend Alqama a thousand dirhams until his stipend came. When his stipend came out, he demanded it from him and was harsh with him, so [Alqama] paid him (7). Alqama seemed to be angry, and he stayed away for months, then came to him and said: Lend me a thousand dirhams until my stipend. He said: Yes, and with honor. O Umm Utba, bring (8) that sealed bag that you have. He said: So she brought it, and he said: By God, these are your very dirhams that you paid me; I have not moved a single [dirham] (2) of them. He said: What a remarkable man you are! What led you to do what you did to me?! He said: What I heard from you. He said: What did you hear from me? He said: I heard you narrate from Ibn Mas'ud that the Prophet ﷺ said: "There is no Muslim who lends a Muslim a loan twice, except that it is like [giving] charity once." He said: So Ibn Mas'ud informed me.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ خَلَفٍ الْعَسْقَلَانِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا يَعْلَى، حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ يُسَيْرٍ (4) ، عَنْ قَيْسِ بْنِ رُومِيٍّ قَالَ:

Matn

كَانَ سُلَيْمَانُ (5) بْنُ أُذُنَانٍ (6) يُقْرِضُ عَلْقَمَةَ أَلْفَ دِرْهَمِ إِلَى عَطَائِهِ، فَلَمَّا خَرَجَ عَطَاؤُهُ تَقَاضَاهَا مِنْهُ وَاشْتَدَّ عَلَيْهِ، فَقَضَاهُ (7) ، فَكَأَنَّ عَلْقَمَةَ غَضِبَ، فَمَكَثَ أَشهُرًا ثُمَّ أَتَاهُ فَقَالَ: أَقْرِضْنِي أَلْفَ دِرْهَمٍ إِلَى عَطَائِي. قَالَ: نَعَمْ وَكَرَامَةً، يَا أُمَّ عُتْبَةَ، هَاتِي (8) تِلْكَ الْخَرِيطَةَ الْمَخْتُومَةَ الَّتِي عِنْدَكِ. قَالَ: فَجَاءَتْ بِهَا، فَقَالَ: أَمَا وَاللهِ! إِنَّهَا لَدَرَاهِمُكَ الَّتِي قَضَيْتَنِي، مَا حَرَّكْتُ مِنْهَا دِرْهَمًا [وَاحِدًا] (2) . قَالَ: فَلِلَّهِ أَبُوكَ! مَا حَمَلَكَ عَلَى مَا فَعَلْتَ بِي؟! قَالَ: مَا سَمِعْتُ مِنْكَ. قَالَ: مَا سَمِعْتَ مِنِّي؟ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُكَ تَذْكُرُ عَنِ ابْنِ مَسْعُودٍ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: "مَا مِنْ مُسْلِمٍ يُقْرِضُ مُسْلِمًا قَرْضًا مَرَّتَيْنِ، إِلَّا كَانَ كَصَدَقَتِهَا مَرَّةً". قَالَ: كَذَلِكَ أَنْبَأَنِي ابْنُ مَسْعُودٍ.

Chapter2431/ 3
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 520
English
Isnad

(1) Ubaydullah b. Abd al-Karim narrated to us, Hisham b. Khalid narrated to us, (2) Khalid b. Yazid b. Abi Malik narrated to us, from his father, from Anas b. Malik, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "On the night I was taken on the Night Journey, I saw written on the door of Paradise: Charity is [rewarded] ten times its like, and the loan eighteen times. So I said: O Jibril (3), why is the loan better than charity? He said: Because the beggar asks while he has [something], whereas the one who seeks a loan only seeks a loan out of need."

العربية
Isnad

(1) حَدَّثَنَا عُبَيْدُ اللهِ بْنُ عَبْدِ الْكَرِيمِ، حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامُ بْنُ خَالِدٍ، (2) حَدَّثَنَا خَالِدُ بْنُ يَزِيدَ بْنِ أَبِي مَالِكٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "رَأَيْتُ لَيْلَةَ أُسْرِيَ بِي عَلَى بَابِ الْجَنَّةِ مَكْتُوبًا: الصَّدَقَةُ بِعَشْرِ أَمْثَالِهَا، وَالْقَرْضُ بِثَمَانِيَةَ عَشَرَ، فَقُلْتُ: يَا جِبْرِيلُ (3) ، مَا بَالُ الْقَرْضِ أَفْضَلُ مِنَ الصَّدَقَةِ؟ قَالَ: لأنَّ السَّائِلَ يَسْأَلُ وَعِنْدَهُ، وَالْمُسْتَقْرِضُ لَا يَسْتَقْرِضُ إِلَّا مِنْ حَاجَةٍ".

Printed page 520
1. It is authentic with the wording: "A man entered Paradise and saw written on its door: Charity is [rewarded] ten times its like, and the loan eighteen times." Our shaykh took it out in al-Sahiha (3407). 2. It occurs in Murad and Paris: "and Abu Hatim narrated to us, Hisham b. Khalid narrated to us," except that the copyist in Murad struck a light line through it, indicating that it is in one copy and not another, and that is his habit. This addition does not occur in Tuhfat al-Ashraf, and hence there is disagreement over whether it is among the additions (zawa'id) of Ibn al-Qattan or of Ibn Majah. What I consider more likely is that it is of Ibn al-Qattan's additions; because Abu Hatim, although he is one of Ibn Majah's teachers, [Ibn Majah] did not narrate from him in the Sunan, whereas Ibn al-Qattan narrated from him extensively, as in his additions to the Sunan. I say: Then I saw this addition in the margin of al-Turkiyya, from the words of Abu al-Hasan, so it is established without doubt that it is from the additions of Ibn al-Qattan. 3. In al-Turkiyya: "to Jibril (li-Jibril)." 4. Al-Mizzi and more than one scholar counted it an error. Al-Mizzi said in Tahdhib al-Kamal (31/201): "The well-known is that al-Hunai is Yahya b. Yazid, as will come in its place," and al-Mizzi said in the biography of Utba, when mentioning his teachers: "and Yahya b. Abi Ishaq al-Hunai (Q [Ibn Majah]), if it is preserved; and it is said: Yahya b. Yazid al-Hunai, and that is the correct one." And al-Bayhaqi said in al-Sunan al-Kubra (5/573): "Hisham said: In this hadith [it is] Yahya b. Abi Ishaq al-Hunai, and I only consider it an error, and this is the hadith of Yahya b. Yazid al-Hunai, from Anas." I say: Al-Hafiz Ibn Hajar rebutted al-Mizzi, saying in al-Nukat al-Zirraf: "What prevents the father of al-Hunai from also having the kunya Abu Ishaq? And it cannot be said: perhaps Hisham b. Ammar, the teacher of Ibn Majah, erred in saying al-Hunai; because Sa'id b. Mansur narrated it from Isma'il b. Ayyash, the teacher of Hisham, and said in it: Yahya b. Abi Ishaq al-Hunai, as Hisham said, and God knows best." I say: Thus said Ibn Hajar, whereas what is in al-Sunan al-Kubra of al-Bayhaqi, and what Ibn Abd al-Hadi mentioned in al-Tanqih from the route of Sa'id b. Mansur, is: "Yazid b. Abi Yahya," and Ibn Abd al-Hadi rebutted it in Tanqih al-Tahqiq (4/107): "Thus in it: from Yazid b. Abi Yahya, and it is a mistake, and no one called Yazid b. Abi Yahya is known among the narrators." A note: In al-Taymuriyya "al-Bunani" occurs, and in the margin of Paris: "a copy: al-Bunani informed me." 5. In al-Taymuriyya: "to him." 6. In al-Turkiyya he added between the lines the word: "his brother (akhahu)." 7. In al-Turkiyya: "a mount (dabba)." 8. An addition from Murad.

Chapter2432/ 3
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 520
English
Isnad

Hisham b. Ammar narrated to us, Isma'il b. Ayyash narrated to us, Utba b. Humayd al-Dabbi narrated to me, from Yahya b. Abi Ishaq al-Hunai (4), who said:

Matn

I asked Anas b. Malik: A man among us lends his brother money, and he gives him a gift (5)? He said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "When one of you lends a loan and [the borrower] gives him a gift (5), or carries him on a mount (7), let him not ride it and not accept it, unless that was already customary between the two of them before that."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامُ بْنُ عَمَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ عَيَّاشٍ، حَدَّثَنِي عُتْبَةُ بْنُ حُمَيْدٍ الضَّبِّيُّ، عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ الْهُنَائِيِّ (4) قَالَ:

Matn

سَأَلْتُ أَنَسَ بْنَ مَالِكٍ: الرَّجُلُ مِنَّا يُقْرِضُ أَخَاهُ الْمَالَ فَيُهْدِي لَهُ (5) ؟ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "إِذَا أَقْرَضَ أَحَدُكُمْ (6) قَرْضًا فَأَهْدَى لَهُ (5) ، أَوْ حَمَلَهُ عَلَى الدَّابَّةِ (7) ، فَلَا يَرْكَبْهَا وَلَا يَقْبَلْهُ، إِلَّا أَنْ يَكُونَ جَرَى بَيْنَهُ وَبَيْنَهُ قَبْلَ ذَلِكَ".
