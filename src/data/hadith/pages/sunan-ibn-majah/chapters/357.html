Chapter1269/ 4
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 290
English
Isnad

Abu Kurayb narrated to us, [saying:] Abu Mu'awiyah narrated to us, from al-A'mash, from 'Amr b. Murrah, from Salim b. Abi al-Ja'd, from Shurahbil b. al-Simt,

Matn

that he said to Ka'b: “O Ka'b b. Murrah, narrate to us from the Messenger of God ﷺ, and be careful.” He said: A man came to the Prophet ﷺ and said, “O Messenger of God, ask God [the Almighty] for rain.” So the Messenger of God ﷺ raised his hand and said, “O God, give us rain: wholesome, pleasant, fertilizing, covering, swift, not delayed, beneficial, not harmful.” He said: They did not even gather for the Friday prayer before they were revived. Then they came to him and complained of the rain, saying, “O Messenger of God, houses have collapsed.” He said, “O God, around us and not upon us.” He said: The clouds began to break apart to the right and the left.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو كُرَيْبٍ، [قَالَ:] (2) حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مُعَاوِيَةَ، عَنِ الْأَعْمَشِ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ مُرَّةَ، عَنْ سَالِمِ بْنِ أَبِي الْجَعْدِ، عَنْ شُرَحْبِيلَ بْنِ السِّمْطِ،

Matn

أَنَّهُ قَالَ لِكَعْبِ: يَا كَعْبُ بْنَ مُرَّةَ! حَدِّثْنَا عَنْ رَسُولِ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَاحْذَرْ، قَالَ: جَاءَ رَجُلٌ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! اسْتَسْقِ اللهَ [عز وجل] (1) ، فَرَفَعَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَدَهُ (2) فَقَالَ: "اللَّهُمَّ! اسْقِنَا غَيْثًا مَرِيئًا مَرِيعًا طَبَقًا عَاجِلًا غَيْرَ رَائِثٍ، نَافِعًا غَيْرَ ضَارٍّ"، قَالَ: فَمَا جَمَّعُوا حَتَّى أُحْيُوا (3) ، قَالَ: فَأَتَوْهُ فَشَكَوْا إِلَيْهِ الْمَطَرَ، فَقَالُوا: يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! تَهَدَّمَتِ الْبُيُوتُ، فَقَالَ: "اللَّهُمَّ! حَوَالَيْنَا وَلَا عَلَيْنَا"، قَالَ: فَجَعَلَ السَّحَابُ (4) يَنْقَطِعُ (5) يَمِينًا وَشِمَالًا.

1. An addition from the Taymuriyyah manuscript. 2. The Murad and Paris manuscripts read: «يديه» (his hands). 3. The Taymuriyyah reads: «أجيبوا» (they were answered), with a marginal alternative «أحيوا» (they were given life). Al-Sindi said: 'In the passive voice, from ihya', meaning life, as in some authoritative copies. Other manuscripts read ujibu, with jim, from answering. The former can also be understood in the active voice, from the expression “the people came alive,” meaning they entered a state of fertility and abundance.' 4. The Turkish manuscript reads: «فجعلت السحابة تقطع» (and the cloud began to break apart). 5. The Murad manuscript reads: «يتقطع» (it breaks apart). 6. I say: Our shaykh graded the hadith weak in Da'if Ibn Majah and explained the supposed defect in al-Irwa', attributing it to Habib b. Abi Thabit's tadlis. That is not its actual defect. Its defect is that the report is mursal: Hushaym and Za'idah narrated it from Husayn, from Habib as mursal; Ibn Jurayj also narrated it from Habib as mursal. The text, however, is preserved in several other reports, which is why Ibn al-Mulaqqin graded it hasan. 7. The Turkish manuscript reads: «راعي» (my shepherd/a shepherd); both forms are correct. 8. The Murad and Paris manuscripts read: «طبقًا مريعًا» (an extensive, lush [rain cloud]). 9. The Turkish margin reads: 'Variant: “then it descended.” This is al-Qattan's reading.' 10. See our shaykh's commentary on Mukhtasar Sahih al-Bukhari (1/305). 11. The other manuscripts read: «نزل» (it descended). 12. Meaning: it pours and flows with water. The other manuscript originals read: «جيش» (an army). 13. The genitive case is also grammatically valid. 14. That is, relief or rain.

Chapter1270/ 4
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 291
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Abi al-Qasim Abu al-Ahwas narrated to us, [saying:] al-Hasan b. al-Rabi' narrated to us, [saying:] 'Abdullah b. Idris narrated to us, saying: Husayn narrated to us, from Habib b. Abi Thabit, from Ibn 'Abbas, who said:

Matn

A Bedouin came to the Prophet ﷺ and said, “O Messenger of God, I have come to you from a people whose shepherds can no longer provision themselves, and whose stallions no longer strut (the livestock is exhausted).” So he ascended the minbar, praised God, and said, “O God, give us rain: relieving, wholesome, fertilizing, covering, abundant, swift, not delayed.” Then he descended, and no one came to him from any direction without saying, “We have been revived.”

العربية
Isnad

(6) حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ أَبِي الْقَاسِمِ أَبُو الْأَحْوَصِ، [قَالَ:] (1) حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ الرَّبِيعِ، [قَالَ:] (1) حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللهِ بْنُ إِدْرِيسَ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا حُصَيْنٌ، عَنْ حَبِيبِ بْنِ أَبِي ثَابِتٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسِ قَالَ:

Matn

جَاءَ أَعْرَابِيٌّ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! لَقَدْ جِئْتُكَ مِنْ عِنْدِ قَوْمٍ مَا يَتَزَوَّدُ لَهُمْ رَاعٍ (7) ، وَلَا يَخْطِرُ لَهُمْ فَحْلٌ، فَصَعِدَ الْمِنْبَرَ فَحَمِدَ اللهَ ثُمَّ قَالَ: "اللَّهُمَّ! اسْقِنَا غَيْثًا مُغِيثًا مَرِيئًا مَرِيعًا طَبَقًا (8) غَدَقًا، عَاجِلًا غَيْرَ رَائِثٍ"، ثُمَّ نَزَلَ (9) ، فَمَا يَأْتِيهِ أَحَدٌ مِنْ وَجْهٍ مِنَ الْوُجُوهِ إِلَّا قَالُوا: قَدْ أُحْيِينَا.

Chapter1271/ 4
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 291
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shaybah narrated to us, [saying:] 'Affan narrated to us, [saying:] Mu'tamir narrated to us, from his father, from Barakah, from Bashir b. Nahik, from Abu Hurayrah:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ asked for rain (raising his hands) until I saw — or it was seen — the whiteness of his armpits. Mu'tamir said: I think it was during the prayer for rain.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، [قَالَ:] (1) حَدَّثَنَا عَفَّانُ، [قَالَ:] (1) حَدَّثَنَا مُعْتَمِرٌ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ بَرَكَةَ، عَنْ بَشِيرِ بْنِ نَهِيكٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ،

Matn

أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم اسْتَسْقَى حَتَّى رَأَيْتُ -أَوْ رُئِيَ- بَيَاضُ إِبْطَيْهِ. قَالَ مُعْتَمِرٌ: أُرَاهُ فِي الِاسْتِسْقَاءِ.

Chapter1272/ 4
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 291
English
Isnad

Ahmad b. al-Azhar narrated to us: Abu al-Nadr narrated to us: Abu 'Aqil narrated to us, from 'Umar b. Hamzah, who said: Salim narrated to us, from his father, who said:

Matn

Often I would recall the words of the poet while looking at the face of the Prophet ﷺ on the minbar — he would not come down until every gutter in Madinah was flowing — and I would recall the poet's words: “And the fair-faced one by whose countenance the clouds are asked for rain, the refuge of orphans, the protector of widows.” This is the verse of Abu Talib.

العربية
Isnad

(10) حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ بْنُ الْأَزْهَرِ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو النَّضْرِ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَقِيلٍ، عَنْ عُمَرَ بْنِ حَمْزَةَ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا سَالِمٌ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ قَالَ:

Matn

رُبَّمَا ذَكَرْتُ قَوْلَ الشَّاعِرِ وَأَنَا أَنْظُرُ إِلَى وَجْهِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَلَى الْمِنْبَرِ، فَمَا ينْزِلُ (11) حَتَّى يَجِيشَ (12) كُلُّ مِيزَابٍ بِالْمَدِينَةِ، فَأَذْكُرُ قَوْلَ الشَّاعِرِ: وَأَبْيَضَ (13) يُسْتَسْقَى الْغَمَامُ بِوَجْهِهِ … ثِمَالَ (14) الْيَتَامَى عِصْمَةً لِلأْرَامِلِ وَهُوَ قَوْلُ أَبِي طَالِبٍ.
