English
Isnad

'Ali b. Muhammad al-Tanafisi [said:] (6) Waki' [said:] (5) Sufyan narrated to us, from Suhayl b. Abi Salih, from 'Abdullah b. Dinar, from Abu Salih, from Abu Hurayrah, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Faith has sixty-odd (7) — or seventy-odd (8) — branches; the lowest of them is removing harm from the road, and the highest of them is the saying 'There is no god but God'; and modesty is a branch of faith." (9) [Muslim: 35, Abu Dawud: 4676, al-Tirmidhi: 2614, al-Nasa'i: 5005, Tuhfah: 12816] 57 (Muslim) – (Sahih) Abu Bakr b. Dinar, from Abu Salih, from Abu Hurayrah, from the Prophet ﷺ … similar to it.

Chapter60/ 19
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 62
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya [said:] (1) 'Abd al-Razzaq [said:] (1) Ma'mar informed us, from Zayd b. Aslam, from 'Ata' b. Yasar, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "When God has delivered the believers from the Fire and they are safe, then none of you in disputing with his companion over a right he has in this world is more insistent than the believers will be with their Lord concerning their brothers who were entered into the Fire." He said: "They will say: 'Our Lord! Our brothers used to pray with us, fast with us and perform Hajj with us, and You have entered them into the Fire.' He will say: 'Go and bring out whomever you recognize among them.' So they will come to them and recognize them by their forms (2); the Fire does not consume their forms (3). Some of them the Fire will have seized up to the middle of their shins, and some up to their ankles. They will bring them out and say: 'Our Lord! We have brought out those You commanded us to.'" He said: "Then He will say: 'Bring out whoever has in his heart the weight (4) of a dinar of faith.' Then whoever has in his heart the weight (5) of half a dinar. Then whoever has in his heart the weight of a mustard-seed's atom (6).'" Abu Sa'id said: "Whoever does not believe this, let him recite: {Indeed, God does not wrong [anyone] even by an atom's weight; and if it is a good deed, He multiplies it and gives from Himself a great reward (40)} [al-Nisa': 40]."

English
Isnad

'Ali b. Muhammad [said:] (1) Waki' narrated to us, from Kahmas b. al-Hasan, from 'Abdullah b. Buraydah, from Yahya b. Ya'mar, from Ibn 'Umar, from 'Umar, who said:

Matn

"We were sitting with the Prophet ﷺ when a man came, intensely white of clothing, intensely black of hair, with no sign of travel (10) upon him, and none of us knew him. [He said:] (11) He sat before the Prophet ﷺ, pressed his knees against his knees, and placed his hands (12) on his thighs, then said: 'O Muhammad! What is Islam?' He said: 'The testimony that there is no god but God and that I am the Messenger of God, establishing the prayer, giving the zakah, fasting Ramadan, and performing Hajj to the House.' He said: 'You have spoken truly.' We were astonished at him: he asks him and then confirms him. Then he said: 'O Muhammad! What is faith?' He said: 'That you believe in God, His angels, His Books, His Messengers (1), the Last Day, and the divine decree, its good and its bad.' He said: 'You have spoken truly.' We were astonished (2) at him: he asks him and then confirms him. Then he said: 'O Muhammad! What (3) is ihsan?' He said: 'That you worship God as if you see Him; for even if you do not see Him (4), He sees you.' He said: 'Then when is the Hour?' He said: 'The one asked about it knows no more than the one asking.' He said: 'Then what are its signs?' He said: 'That the slave woman gives birth to her mistress (5)' — Waki' said: meaning that non-Arabs will give birth to Arabs — 'and that you see the barefoot, naked, destitute (6) shepherds of sheep competing (7) in constructing tall buildings.' He ('Umar) said: Then he went away, and the Prophet ﷺ met me after three days and said: 'Do you know who the man was?' I said: 'God and His Messenger know best.' He said: 'That was Jibril. He came to teach you the fundamentals of your religion.'"

Chapter69/ 19
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 64
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Abdullah b. Numayr [said:] (1) 'Affan narrated to us: Shu'bah narrated to us, from al-A'mash; (transition) and Hisham b. 'Ammar [said:] (1) 'Isa b. Yunus [said:] (1) al-A'mash narrated to us, from Abu Wa'il, from 'Abdullah, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Insulting a Muslim is wickedness, and fighting him is disbelief."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْن نُمَيْرٍ، [قَالَ:] (1) حَدَّثَنَا عَفَّانُ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنِ الْأَعْمَشِ ح وَحَدَّثَنَا هِشَامُ بْنُ عَمَّارٍ، [قَالَ:] (1) حَدَّثَنَا عِيسَى بْنُ يُونُسَ، [قَالَ:] (1) حَدَّثَنَا الْأَعْمَشُ، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "سِبَابُ الْمُسْلِمِ فُسُوقٌ، وَقِتَالُهُ كُفْرٌ".

Chapter70/ 19
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 64
English
Isnad

Nasr b. 'Ali al-Jahdami [said:] (1) Abu Ahmad [said:] (5) Abu Ja'far al-Razi narrated to us, from al-Rabi' b. Anas, from Anas b. Malik, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Whoever departs this world in sincerity to God alone, worshipping Him with no partner, establishing the prayer and giving the zakah, dies with God pleased with him." Anas said: "That is the religion of God that the Messengers brought and conveyed from their Lord, before the confusion of narrations (6) and the disagreement of desires. And its confirmation is in the Book of God [Mighty and Majestic] (1), in the last of what was revealed, where God says (7): {But if they repent} [al-Tawbah: 5]" — he said: "they cast off (8) idols and their worship — {and establish prayer and give zakah} [al-Baqarah: 277]." And He said in another verse: {But if they repent, establish prayer, and give zakah, then they are your brothers in religion} [al-Tawbah: 11].

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا نَصْرُ بْنُ عَلِيٍّ الْجَهْضَمِيُّ، [قَالَ:] (1) حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو أَحْمَدَ [قَالَ:] (5) حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو جَعْفَرٍ الرَّازِيُّ، عَنِ الرَّبِيعِ بْنِ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "مَنْ فَارَقَ الدُّنْيَا عَلَى الْإِخْلَاصِ لِلَّهِ وَحْدَهُ، وَعِبَادَتِهِ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، وَإِقَامِ الصَّلَاةِ، وَإِيِتَاءِ الزَّكَاةِ، مَاتَ وَاللهُ عَنْهُ رَاضٍ". قَالَ أَنَسٌ: وَهُوَ دِينُ اللهِ الَّذِي جَاءَتْ بِهِ الرُّسُلُ، وَبَلَّغُوهُ عَنْ رَبِّهِمْ قَبْلَ هَرْجِ الْأَحَادِيثِ (6) وَاخْتِلَافِ الْأَهْوَاءِ، وَتَصْدِيقُ ذَلِكَ فِي كِتَابِ اللهِ [عز وجل] (1) ، فِي آخرِ مَا نَزَلَ، يَقُولُ اللهُ (7) : {فَإِنْ تَابُوا} [التوبة: 5] قَالَ: خَلَعُوا (8) الْأَوْثَانَ وَعِبَادَتَهَا {وَأَقَامُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَآتَوُا الزَّكَاةَ} [البقرة: 277] وَقَالَ فِي آيَةٍ أُخْرَى: {فَإِنْ تَابُوا وَأَقَامُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَآتَوُا الزَّكَاةَ فَإِخْوَانُكُمْ فِي الدِّينِ} [التوبة: 11].

Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 65
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Isma'il al-Razi narrated to us: Yunus b. Muhammad informed us: 'Abdullah b. Muhammad al-Laythi narrated to us: Nizar b. Hayyan narrated to us, from 'Ikrimah, from Ibn 'Abbas and from Jabir b. 'Abdullah, who both said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Two groups of my Ummah have no share in Islam (6): the people of irja' and the people of qadar."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ الرَّازِيُّ، أَخْبَرَنَا يُونُسُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللهِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ اللَّيْثِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا نِزَارُ بْنُ حَيَّانَ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ وَعَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللهِ قَالَا:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "صِنْفَانِ مِنْ أُمَّتِي لَيْسَ لَهُمَا فِي الْإِسْلَامِ (6) نَصِيبٌ: أَهْلُ الْإِرْجَاءِ، وَأَهْلُ الْقَدَرِ".
