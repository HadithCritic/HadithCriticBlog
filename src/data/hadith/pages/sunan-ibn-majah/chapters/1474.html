Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 856
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Abd Allah b. Numayr and 'Ali b. Muhammad narrated to us; they said: Abu Mu'awiyah narrated to us: al-A'mash narrated to us, from Shaqiq, from Hudhayfah, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'The Antichrist is blind in his left eye and has thick, disheveled hair. With him are a garden and a fire, but his fire is a garden and his garden is a fire.'

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "الدَّجَّالُ أَعْوَرُ عَيْنِ الْيُسْرَى، جُفَالُ الشَّعَرِ (7) ، مَعَهُ جَنَّةٌ وَنَارٌ، فَنَارُهُ جَنَّةٌ، وَجَنَّتُهُ نَارٌ".

Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 857
English
Isnad

Nasr b. 'Ali al-Jahdami, Muhammad b. Bashshar and Muhammad b. al-Muthanna narrated to us; they said: Ruh b. 'Ubadah narrated to us: Sa'id b. Abi 'Arubah narrated to us, from Abu al-Tayyah, from al-Mughirah b. Subay', from 'Amr b. Hurayth, from Abu Bakr al-Siddiq, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ told us:

Matn

'The Antichrist will emerge from a land in the east called Khurasan. He will be followed by people whose faces resemble hammered shields.' *Additional route:* Abu al-Hasan said: al-Harith narrated it to us: Ruh b. 'Ubadah narrated to us, and he mentioned something similar.

Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 857
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Abd Allah b. Numayr and 'Ali b. Muhammad narrated to us; they said: Waki' narrated to us: Isma'il b. Abi Khalid narrated to us, from Qays b. Abi Hazim, from al-Mughirah b. Shu'bah, who said:

Matn

'No one questioned the Prophet ﷺ about the Antichrist more than I did'—Ibn Numayr's wording was, 'more insistently than I did.' 'He asked me: Why do you ask about him? I said: People claim he will have food and drink with him. He replied: He is too insignificant before God for that.'

Chapter4074/ 11
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 857
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Abd Allah b. Numayr narrated to us: My father narrated to us: Isma'il b. Abi Khalid narrated to us, from Mujalid, from al-Sha'bi, from Fatimah bt. Qays, who said:

Matn

'One day the Messenger of God ﷺ performed the prayer and ascended the pulpit. Previously he would only ascend it on Fridays, so people were alarmed, some standing and others sitting. He motioned for them to sit, saying: By God, I did not stand here because of a matter that diminishes you, nor from desire or fear. Rather, Tamim al-Dari came to me with news that kept me from my midday rest, such was my joy and delight. I wished to share your Prophet's happiness with you. A cousin of Tamim al-Dari told me that the wind had driven them to an unknown island. They boarded the ship's small boats and went ashore. They encountered a black, hairy creature, thickly covered with hair, and asked: What are you? It answered: I am al-Jassasah. They asked it to tell them about itself. It said: I will neither tell you anything nor ask you anything. But go to the monastery you see over there; inside is a man eager to receive your news and give you news. They entered and found an elderly man tightly bound, visibly grieving and complaining bitterly. He asked: Where are you from? They replied: From Syria. He asked: What have the Arabs done? They said: We are Arabs. What are you asking about? He asked: What has that man who emerged among you done? They said: Well. He opposed a people and God granted him victory over them; now they are united, with one God and one religion. He asked: What has happened to the spring of Zughar? They said: It is well; they irrigate their fields with it and draw water to drink. He asked: What about the date palms between 'Amman and Baysan? They said: They bear fruit each year. He asked: What about Lake Tiberias? They said: Its shores overflow from the abundance of water. He sighed three times and said: If I should break free of these bonds, there is no land I would fail to tread with these two feet, except Taybah, for I have no way into it.' The Prophet ﷺ continued: 'That is the source of my joy. This is Taybah [Medina]. By Him in whose hand is my soul, there is neither narrow nor broad road, level ground nor mountain in it without an angel standing with sword drawn, until the Day of Resurrection.'

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ نُمَيْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي، حَدَّثنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ أَبِي خَالِدٍ، عَنْ مُجَالِدٍ، عَنِ الشَّعْبِيِّ، عَنْ فَاطِمَةَ بِنْتِ قَيْسٍ قَالَتْ:

Matn

صَلَّى رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ذَاتَ يَوْمٍ، وَصَعِدَ الْمِنْبَرَ، وَكَانَ لَا يَصْعَدُ عَلَيْهِ قَبْلَ (3) ذَلِكَ إِلَّا يَوْمَ الْجُمُعَةِ، فَاشْتَدَّ ذَلِكَ عَلَى النَّاسِ، فَمِنْ بَيْنِ قَائِمٍ وَجَالِسٍ، فَأَشَارَ إِلَيْهِمْ بِيَدِهِ أَنِ اقْعُدُوا: "فَاِنِّي، وَاللهِ مَا قُمْتُ مَقَامِي هَذَا لِأَمْرٍ يَنْقُصُكُمْ (4) لِرَغْبَةٍ وَلَا لِرَهْبَةٍ، وَلَكِنَّ تَمِيمًا الدَّارِيَّ أَتَانِي فَأَخْبَرَنِي خَبَرًا مَنَعَنِي الْقَيْلُولَةَ (5) ، مِنَ الْفَرَح وَقُرَّةِ الْعَيْنِ، فَأَحْبَبْتُ أَنْ أَنْشُرَ عَلَيْكُمْ فَرَحَ نَبِيِّكُمْ، أَلَا إِنَّ ابْنَ عَمٍّ لِتَمِيمٍ الدَّارِيِّ أَخْبَرَنِي: أَنَّ الرِّيحَ أَلْجَأتْهُمْ إِلَى جَزِيرَةٍ لَا يَعْرِفُونَهَا، فَقَعَدُوا فِي قَوَارِبِ السَّفِينَةِ، فَخَرَجُوا فِيهَا (6) ، فَإِذَا هُمْ بِشَيْءٍ أَهْدَبَ (7) ، أَسْوَدَ، كَثِيرِ الشَّعْرِ، قَالُوا لَهُ: مَا أَنْتَ؟ قَالَ: أَنَا الْجَسَّاسَةُ. قَالُوا: أَخْبِرِينَا، قَالَتْ: مَا أَنَا بِمُخْبِرَتِكُمْ شَيْئًا وَلَا سَائِلَتِكُمْ، وَلَكِنْ هَذَا الدَّيْرُ قَدْ رَمَقْتُمُوهُ، فَأْتُوهُ، فَإِنَّ فِيهِ رَجُلًا بِالْأَشْوَاقِ إِلَى أَنْ تُخْبِرُوهُ وَيُخْبِرَكُمْ. فَأتَوْهُ فَدَخَلُوا عَلَيْهِ، فَإذَا هُمْ بِشَيْخٍ مُوثَقٍ، شَدِيدِ الْوَثَاقِ، يُظْهِرُ الْحُزْنَ، شَدِيدِ التَّشَكِّي، فَقَالَ لَهُمْ: مِنْ أَيْنَ؟ قَالُوا: مِنَ الشَّامِ. قَالَ: مَا فَعَلَتِ الْعَرَبُ؟ قَالُوا: نَحْنُ قَوْمٌ مِنَ الْعَرَبِ، عَمَّ تَسْأَلُ؟ قَالَ: مَا فَعَلَ [هَذَا] (8) الرَّجُلُ الَّذِي خَرَجَ فِيكُمْ؟ قَالُوا: خَيْرًا، نَاوَأَ قَوْمًا، فَأَظْهَرَهُ اللهُ عَلَيْهِمْ، فَأَمْرُهُمُ الْيَوْمَ جَمِيعٌ، إِلَهُهُمْ وَاحِدٌ، وَدِينُهُمْ وَاحِدٌ، قَالَ: مَا فَعَلَتْ عَيْنُ زُغَرَ؟ قَالُوا: خَيْرًا، يَسْقُونَ مِنْهَا زُرُوعَهُمْ، وَيَسْتَقُونَ مِنْهَا لِسَقْيِهِمْ. قَالَ: فَمَا فَعَلَ نَخْلٌ بَيْنَ عَمَّانَ وَبَيْسَانَ؟ قَالُوا: يُطْعِمُ ثَمَرَهُ كُلَّ عَامٍ. قَالَ: فَمَا فَعَلَتْ بُحَيْرَةُ الطَّبَرِيَّةِ؟ قَالُوا: تَدَفَّقُ جَنَبَاتُهَا مِنْ كَثْرَةِ الْمَاءِ. قَالَ: فَزَفَرَ ثَلَاثَ زَفَرَاتٍ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: لَوْ قَدِ انْفَلَتُّ مِنْ وَثَاقِي هَذَا، لَمْ أَدَعْ أَرْضًا إِلَّا وَطِئْتُهَا بِرِجْلَيَّ هَاتَيْنِ إِلَّا طَيْبَةَ، لَيْسَ لِي عَلَيْهَا سَبِيلٌ". قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "إِلَى هَذَا يَنْتَهِي فَرَحِي، هَذِهِ طَيْبَةُ، وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ، مَا فِيهَا طَرِيقٌ ضَيِّقٌ وَلَا وَاسِعٌ، وَلَا سَهْلٌ وَلَا جَبَلٌ، إِلَّا وَعَلَيْهِ مَلَكٌ شَاهِرٌ سَيْفَهُ، إِلَى يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ".

English
Isnad

Hisham b. 'Ammar narrated to us: Yahya b. Hamzah narrated to us: 'Abd al-Rahman b. Yazid b. Jabir narrated to us, saying: 'Abd al-Rahman b. Jubayr b. Nufayr narrated to me, saying: My father narrated to me that he heard al-Nawwas b. Sam'an al-Kilabi say:

Matn

'The Messenger of God ﷺ spoke of the Antichrist one morning, sometimes lowering his voice and sometimes raising it, until we thought the Antichrist might be among a stand of date palms. When we returned to him later, he saw our distress and asked: What is wrong with you? We said: Messenger of God, you spoke this morning of the Antichrist, lowering and raising your voice until we thought he was among the date palms. He said: There is something besides the Antichrist that I fear more for you. If he emerges while I am among you, I shall argue against him on your behalf. If he appears when I am no longer among you, each person must defend himself, and God is my guardian over every Muslim. He is a young man with tightly curled hair and a protruding eye. I would liken him to 'Abd al-'Uzza b. Qatan. Whoever among you sees him should recite over him the opening verses of Surat al-Kahf. He will emerge from a passage between Syria and Iraq, causing destruction to right and left. Servants of God, remain firm! We asked: Messenger of God, how long will he remain on earth? He replied: Forty days: one day like a year, one day like a month, one day like a week, and the rest like your ordinary days. We asked: Will the prayers of a single day suffice for the day that is like a year? He replied: No; estimate its time accordingly. We asked: How quickly will he move across the earth? He replied: Like rain driven by the wind. He will come to a people and call them; they will answer him and believe in him. He will command the sky to rain and it will rain, and the earth to produce vegetation and it will do so. Their grazing animals will return with the tallest humps, the fullest udders and the most extended flanks. Then he will come to a people and call them, but they will reject his words. He will depart, and by morning they will be destitute, with nothing remaining in their possession. He will pass a ruined place and say: Bring out your treasures! Its treasures will follow him like swarms of bees. Then he will call a young man in the fullness of youth and strike him with a sword, cutting him into two pieces separated by the distance of an arrow shot at a target. He will call him again, and the man will approach beaming and laughing. While this is happening, God will send Jesus son of Mary. He will descend at the white minaret to the east of Damascus, wearing two dyed garments, his hands resting upon the wings of two angels. When he lowers his head, water will drip from it; when he raises it, drops like pearls will fall. No unbeliever who smells his breath can survive; his breath will reach as far as his sight. He will pursue the Antichrist, catch him at the gate of Ludd, and kill him. Jesus, the prophet of God, will then visit a people whom God has protected, stroke their faces, and tell them of their ranks in Paradise. While they are there, God will reveal to him: Jesus, I have brought forth servants of Mine whom no one has the power to fight; take My servants to safety at Mount Tur. God will release Gog and Magog, who, as God said, hasten from every elevation [21:96]. Their first ranks will pass Lake Tiberias and drink its water; when their last ranks pass, they will say: There was once water here. Jesus and his companions will be besieged until the head of an ox is worth more to one of them than a hundred dinars are worth to one of you today. Jesus and his companions will beseech God, and God will send worms into the necks of Gog and Magog; they will all die together as though they were a single person. Jesus and his companions will descend but will find no handspan of ground not filled with their stench, blood and putrefaction. They will beseech God, who will send birds with necks like Bactrian camels. The birds will carry the bodies away and cast them wherever God wills. Then God will send rain that no mud-brick house or woolen tent can shelter from; it will wash the earth clean until it is like a smooth surface. The earth will be told: Bring forth your fruit and return your blessing. On that day a whole group will eat from a single pomegranate until satisfied and shelter beneath its rind. God will bless the milk of livestock so much that one milking she-camel will suffice for a crowd, one cow for a tribe, and one ewe for a clan. While they live like this, God will send a pleasant wind that will reach them beneath their armpits and take the soul of every Muslim. The remaining people will copulate openly like donkeys, and upon them the Hour will come.'

'Ali b. Muhammad narrated to us: 'Abd al-Rahman al-Muharibi narrated to us, from Isma'il b. Rafi' Abu Rafi', from Abu Zur'ah al-Shaybani Yahya b. Abi 'Amr, from Abu Umamah al-Bahili, who said:

Matn

'The Messenger of God ﷺ delivered a sermon to us, most of which concerned the Antichrist and warned us against him. He said: Since God created the children of Adam, there has been no tribulation on earth greater than the tribulation of the Antichrist. God sent no prophet who did not warn his community of the Antichrist. I am the last of the prophets and you are the last of the nations, and he will certainly emerge among you. If he emerges while I am among you, I will argue against him for every Muslim. If he appears after me, each person will defend himself and God will be my guardian over every Muslim. He will emerge from a passage between Syria and Iraq, spreading havoc to right and left. Servants of God, people, remain steadfast. I shall describe him to you in a way no prophet before me has described him. He will begin by saying: I am a prophet. But there is no prophet after me. Then he will claim: I am your Lord. But you will not see your Lord until you die. He is one-eyed, while your Lord is not one-eyed. Between his eyes is written: Unbeliever, which every believer will be able to read, whether literate or not. Part of his trial is that he will possess a garden and a fire; his fire is a garden and his garden is a fire. Whoever is tried with his fire should seek God's help and recite the opening verses of al-Kahf; it will be cool and safe for him, just as the fire was for Abraham. Part of his trial is that he will ask a Bedouin: If I bring your father and mother back to life, will you testify that I am your Lord? The man will answer: Yes. Two devils will appear in the likeness of his parents, saying: Son, follow him, for he is your Lord. Another of his trials is that he will be given power over one person. He will kill him and saw him into two halves, then say: Look at this servant of mine. I shall revive him now, and then he will claim he has a Lord other than me. God will restore the man to life, and the wicked one will ask him: Who is your Lord? The man will answer: My Lord is God, and you are God's enemy, the Antichrist. By God, I have never been more certain about you than I am today.'

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'Gog and Magog dig every day until they almost see a ray of the sun. Their leader says: Go back; we shall dig tomorrow. God restores the barrier to an even stronger state. When their appointed time comes and God wills to send them against people, they dig until they almost see sunlight, and their leader says: Go back; we shall dig tomorrow, God willing. This time they add the condition, God willing. They return and find it as they left it, dig through, and emerge among the people. They drain the water, and people take refuge in their fortresses. They shoot their arrows skyward, and the arrows return with a blood-like substance on them. They say: We have overcome the inhabitants of earth and prevailed over those in heaven. God then sends worms into the backs of their necks, killing them.' The Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'By Him in whose hand is my soul, the creatures of the earth will become fat and well nourished from their flesh.'

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "إِنَّ يَأْجُوجَ وَمَأْجُوجَ يَحْفِرُونَ كُلَّ يَوْم، حَتَّى إِذَا كَادُوا يَرَوْنَ شُعَاعَ الشَّمْسِ، قَالَ الَّذِي عَلَيْهِمُ: ارْجِعُوا فَسَنَحْفِرُهُ غَدًا، فَيُعِيدُهُ اللهُ أَشَدَّ مَا كَان، حَتَّى إِذَا بَلَغَتْ مُدَّتُهُمْ، وَأَرَادَ اللهُ أَنْ يَبْعَثَهُمْ عَلَى النَّاسِ، حَفَرُوا حَتَّى إِذَا كَادُوا يَرَوْنَ شُعَاعَ الشَّمْسِ (6) ، قَالَ: ارْجِعُوا، فَسَتَحْفِرُونَهُ (7) إِنْ شَاءَ اللهُ تَعَالَى، وَاسْتَثْنَوْا، فَيَعُودُونَ إِلَيْهِ، وَهُوَ كَهَيْئَتِهِ حِينَ تَرَكُوهُ، فَيَحْفِرُونَهُ وَيَخْرُجُونَ عَلَى النَّاسِ؛ فَيُنَشِّفُونَ الْمَاءَ، وَيَتَحَصَّنُ النَّاسُ مِنْهُمْ فِي حُصُونِهِمْ، فَيَرْمُونَ سِهَامَهُمْ (8) إِلَى السَّمَاءِ، فَتَرْجِعُ عَلَيْهَا الدَّمُ الَّذِي اجْفَظَّ، فيَقُولُونَ: قَهَرْنَا أَهْلَ الْأرْضِ، وَعَلَوْنَا أَهْلَ السَّمَاءِ، فيَبْعَثُ اللهُ نَغَفًا فِي أَقْفَائِهِمْ فيَقْتُلُهُمْ (9) [بِهَا] (4) ". قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ إِنَّ دَوَابَّ الْأرْضِ لَتَسْمَنُ (1) وَتَشْكَرُ شَكَرًا مِنْ لُحُومِهِمْ".

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Bashshar narrated to us: Yazid b. Harun narrated to us: al-'Awwam b. Hawshab narrated to us, saying: Jabalah b. Suhaym narrated to me, from Mu'thir b. 'Afazah, from 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud, who said:

Matn

'On the night the Messenger of God ﷺ was taken on the night journey he met Abraham, Moses and Jesus, peace be upon them, and they discussed the Hour. They first asked Abraham, who had no knowledge of it, then Moses, who likewise did not know. They turned to Jesus son of Mary, who said: I have been told about what comes before its actual onset; the onset itself is known only to God. He mentioned the emergence of the Antichrist and said: I shall descend and kill him. The people will return to their lands and be met by Gog and Magog, hastening from every elevation. They will drink every body of water they encounter and ruin everything they pass. The people will cry out to God, and I will pray to God to kill them. The earth will stink from their bodies. People will cry out to God, and I will pray; He will send rain that carries them into the sea. Then the mountains will be blown away and the earth spread flat like a hide. I have been told that when this occurs, the Hour will be as close to people as the childbirth of a pregnant woman, whose family does not know when labor will surprise them.' al-'Awwam said: 'Confirmation of this is found in God's Book: Until Gog and Magog are released, and they hasten from every elevation [21:96].'

حَدَّثنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارِ، حَدَّثَنَا يَزِيدُ بْنُ هَارُونَ، حَدَّثَنَا الْعَوَّامُ بْنُ حَوْشَبِ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنِي جَبَلَةُ بْنُ سُحَيْمِ، عَنْ مُؤْثِرِ بْنِ عَفَازَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ قَالَ:

Matn

لَمَّا كَانَ لَيْلَةَ أُسْرِيَ بِرَسُولِ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، لَقِيَ إِبْرَاهَيمَ وَمُوسَى وَعِيسَى عليهم السلام، فَتَذَاكَرُوا السَّاعَةَ، فَبَدَؤُوا بِإِبْرَاهِيمَ، فَسَأَلُوهُ عَنْهَا، فَلَمْ يَكُنْ عِنْدَهُ مِنْهَا عِلْمٌ، ثُمَّ سَأَلُوا مُوسَى فَلَمْ يَكُنْ عِنْدَهُ مِنْهَا عِلْمٌ، فَرُدَّ الْحَدِيثُ إِلَى عِيسَى ابْنِ مَرْيَمَ، فَقَالَ: قَدْ عُهِدَ إِلَيَّ فِيمَا دُونَ وَجْبَتِهَا، فَأَمَّا وَجْبَتُهَا فَلَا يَعْلَمُهَا إِلَّا اللهُ، فَذَكَرَ خُرُوجَ الدَّجَّالِ، قَالَ: فَأَنْزِلُ فَأَقْتُلُهُ، فَيَرْجِعُ النَّاسُ إِلَى بِلَادِهِمْ، فَيَسْتَقْبِلُهُمْ يَأجُوجُ وَمَأْجُوجُ وَهُمْ مِنْ كُلِّ حَدَبٍ يَنْسِلُونَ، فَلَا يَمُرُّونَ بِمَاءٍ إِلَّا شَرِبُوهُ، وَلَا بِشَيءٍ إِلَّا أَفْسَدُوهُ، فَيَجْأَرُونَ إِلَى اللهِ، فَأَدْعُو اللهَ أَن يُمِيتَهُمْ، فَتَنْتُنُ الْأَرْضُ مِنْ رِيحِهِمْ، فَيَجْأَرُونَ إِلَى اللهِ، فَأَدْعُو اللهَ، فَيُرْسِلُ السَّمَاءَ بِالْمَاءِ، فَيَحْمِلُهُم (2) فَيُلْقِيهِمْ (3) فِي الْبَحْرِ، ثُمَّ تُنْسَفُ (4) الْجِبَالُ، وَتُمَدُّ (5) الْأَرْضُ مَدَّ الأَدِيمِ، فَعُهِدَ إِلَيَّ (6) : مَا (7) كَان ذَلِكَ، كَانَتِ السَّاعَةُ مِنَ النَّاسِ كَالْحَامِلِ الَّتِي لَا يَدْرِي أَهْلُهَا مَتَى تَفْجَؤُهُمْ بِوِلَادَتِهَا. قَالَ الْعَوَّامُ: وَوُجِدَ تَصدِيقُ ذَلِكَ فِي كِتَابِ اللهِ تَعَالَى: {حَتَّى إِذَا فُتِحَتْ يَأْجُوجُ وَمَأْجُوجُ وَهُمْ مِنْ كُلِّ حَدَبٍ يَنْسِلُونَ (96)} [الأنبيَاء: 96].
