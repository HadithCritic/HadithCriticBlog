Chapter177/ 26
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 86
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Abdullah b. Numayr narrated to us: my father and Waki' narrated to us; (transition) and 'Ali b. Muhammad narrated to us, he said: my maternal uncle Ya'la, Waki' and Abu Mu'awiyah narrated to us, they said: Isma'il b. Abi Khalid narrated to us, from Qays b. Abi Hazim, from Jarir b. 'Abdullah, who said:

Matn

"We were sitting with the Messenger of God ﷺ, and he looked at the moon on the night of the full moon and said: 'You will see your Lord just as you see this moon, without being crowded or harmed (12) in seeing Him. So if you are able not to be overcome in a prayer before the rising of the sun and before its setting, then do so.' Then he recited: {And glorify the praise of your Lord before the rising of the sun and before the setting} [Qaf: 39]."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ نُمَيْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي وَوَكِيعٌ ح وَحَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا خَالِي يَعْلَى وَوَكِيعٌ وَأَبُو مُعَاوِيَةَ، قَالُوا: حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ أَبِي خَالِدٍ، عَنْ قَيْسِ بْنِ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، عَنْ جَرِيرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللهِ قَالَ:

Matn

كُنَّا جُلُوسًا عِنْدَ رَسُولِ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَنَظَرَ إِلَى الْقَمَرِ لَيْلَةَ الْبَدْرِ، فَقَالَ: "إِنَّكُمْ سَتَرَوْنَ رَبَّكُمْ كَمَا تَرَوْنَ هَذَا الْقَمَرَ، لَا تَضَامُّونَ (12) فِي رُؤْيَتِهِ، فَإِنِ اسْتَطَعْتُمْ أَنْ لَا تُغْلَبُوا عَلَى صَلَاةٍ قَبْلَ طُلُوعِ الشَّمْسِ وَقَبْلَ غُرُوبِهَا فَافْعَلُوا"، ثُمَّ قَرَأَ: {وَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ قَبْلَ طُلُوعِ الشَّمْسِ وَقَبْلَ الْغُرُوبِ} [ق: 39].

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Abdullah b. Numayr narrated to us: Yahya b. 'Isa al-Ramli narrated to us, from al-A'mash, from Abu Salih, from Abu Hurayrah, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Do you suffer any harm in seeing the moon on the night of the full moon?" They said: "No." He said: "Likewise you will suffer no harm in seeing your Lord on the Day of Resurrection."

العربية
Isnad

Matn

Chapter179/ 26
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 87
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. al-'Ala' al-Hamdani narrated to us: 'Abdullah b. Idris narrated to us, from al-A'mash, from Abu Salih al-Samman, from Abu Sa'id, who said:

Matn

"We said: 'O Messenger of God! Will we see our Lord?' He said: 'Are you crowded out (1) in seeing the sun at noon when there are no clouds?' We said: 'No.' He said: 'Do you suffer harm in seeing the moon on the night of the full moon when there are no clouds?' They said: 'No.' He said: 'Indeed, you will suffer no harm in seeing Him, except as you suffer harm in seeing those two.'"

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْعَلَاءِ الْهَمْدَانِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللهِ بْنُ إِدْرِيسَ، عَنِ الْأَعْمَشِ، عَنْ أَبِي صَالِحٍ السَّمَّانِ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ قَالَ:

Matn

قُلْنَا: يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! أَنَرَى رَبَّنَا؟ قَالَ: "تَضَامُّونَ (1) فِي رُؤْيَةِ الشَّمْسِ فِي الظَّهِيرَةِ فِي غَيْرِ سَحَابٍ؟ " قُلْنَا: لَا، قَالَ: "فَتَضَارُّونَ فِي رُؤْيَةِ الْقَمَرِ لَيْلَةَ الْبَدْرِ فِي غَيْرِ سَحَابٍ؟ " قَالُوا: لَا، قَالَ: "إِنَّكُمْ. لَا تَضَارُّونَ فِي رُؤْيَتِهِ إِلَّا كَمَا تَضَارُّونَ فِي رُؤْيَتِهِمَا".

English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shaybah narrated to us: Yazid b. Harun narrated to us: Hammad b. Salamah informed us, from Ya'la b. 'Ata', from Waki' b. Hudus (2), from his paternal uncle Abu Razin, who said:

Matn

"I said: 'O Messenger of God! Will each one of us see God on the Day of Resurrection? And what is the sign of that in His creation?' He said: 'O Abu Razin! Does not each one of you see the moon alone with it?' I said: 'Yes.' He said: 'God is greater, and that is His sign (4) in His creation.'" [Abu Dawud: 4731, Tuhfah: 11175] [Ibn Majah said: All the people say: Waki' b. 'Udus with an 'ayn, except Hammad b. Salamah, for he says it with a ha'] (5).

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا يَزِيدُ بْنُ هَارُونَ، أَخْبَرَنَا حَمَّادُ بْنُ سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ يَعْلَى بْنِ عَطَاءٍ، عَنْ وَكِيعِ بْنِ حُدُسٍ (2) ، عَنْ عَمِّهِ أَبِي رَزِينٍ قَالَ:

Matn

قُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! أَكُلُّنَا يَرَى (3) اللهَ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ؟ وَمَا آيَةُ ذَلِكَ فِي خَلْقِهِ؟ قَالَ: "يَا أَبَا رَزِينٍ! أَلَيْسَ كُلُّكُمْ يَرَى الْقَمَرَ مُخْلِيًا بِهِ؟ " قَالَ: قُلْتُ: بَلَى. قَالَ: "فَاللهُ أَعْظَمُ، وَذَلِكَ آيَتُهُ (4) فِي خَلْقِهِ". [د: 4731، تحفة: 11175] [قَالَ ابْنُ مَاجَهْ: النَّاسُ كُلُّهُمْ يَقُولُونَ: وَكِيعُ بْنُ عُدُسٍ بِالْعَيْنِ غَيْرُ حَمَّادُ بْنُ سَلَمَةَ، إِنَّهُ يَقُولُهُ بِالْحَاءِ] (5) .

English
Isnad

Humayd b. Mas'adah narrated to us: Khalid b. al-Harith narrated to us: Sa'id narrated to us, from Qatadah, from Safwan b. Muhriz al-Mazini, who said:

Matn

"While we were with 'Abdullah b. 'Umar as he was circling the House, a man approached him and said: 'O Ibn 'Umar! What did you hear the Messenger of God ﷺ say about the private conversation (najwa)?' He said: 'I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: "The believer will be brought near to his Lord on the Day of Resurrection until He places His covering (1) over him, then He makes him acknowledge his sins (2): 'Do you recognize it?' He says: 'My Lord! I recognize it.' Until, when He has taken him as far as God wills, He says: 'I concealed them for you (3) in the world, and today I forgive them for you.' Then he is given the record of his good deeds, or (4) his book, in his right hand. As for the disbeliever or the hypocrite, he is called out before the witnesses — Khalid said: there is something of a break in [the wording] 'the witnesses' — {These are the ones who lied about their Lord. Surely the curse of God is upon the wrongdoers} [Hud: 18]."'" [al-Bukhari: 2441, Muslim: 2768, Tuhfah: 7096] Abu 'Abdullah Ibn Majah said:.... (5) Khalid b. al-Harith is trustworthy (6), God willing.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا حُمَيْدُ بْنُ مَسْعَدَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا خَالِدُ بْنُ الْحَارِثِ، حَدَّثَنَا سَعِيدٌ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنْ صَفْوَانَ بْنِ مُحْرِزٍ الْمَازِنِيِّ قَالَ:

Chapter190/ 26
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 89
English
Isnad

Ibrahim b. al-Mundhir al-Hizami and Yahya b. Habib b. 'Arabi narrated to us; both said: Musa b. Ibrahim b. Kathir al-Ansari al-Harami (4) narrated to us, saying: I heard Talhah b. Khirash say: I heard Jabir b. 'Abd Allah say:

Matn

“When 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr b. Haram was killed on the day of Uhud, the Messenger of God ﷺ met me and said: ‘Jabir! Shall I tell you what God [Mighty and Majestic] (5) said to your father?’” Yahya's version says: “He said: ‘Jabir! Why do I see you so downcast?’ He (6) said: ‘Messenger of God! My father was martyred, leaving dependents and debts.’ He said: ‘Shall I give you glad tidings of the manner in which God received your father?’ I said: ‘Yes, Messenger of God!’ He said: ‘God has never spoken to anyone except from behind a veil, but He spoke to your father face to face (7). He said: “My servant! Wish for something from Me, and I shall give it to you.” He said: “My Lord! Bring me back to life so that I may be killed in Your cause a second time.” The Lord, Blessed and Exalted (8), said: “I have already decreed that they shall not return to it.” He said: “My Lord! Then convey this to those I have left behind.”’ So God [Most High] (5) revealed: {Do not consider those who have been killed in the way of God to be dead. Rather, they are alive with their Lord, receiving provision} [Al 'Imran 3:169].”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ الْمُنْذِرِ الْحِزَامِيُّ وَيَحْيَى بْنُ حَبِيبِ بْنِ عَرَبِيٍّ، قَالَا: حَدَّثَنَا مُوسَى بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ بْنِ كَثِيرٍ الْأَنْصَارِيُّ الْحَرَامِيُّ (4) ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ طَلْحَةَ بْنَ خِرَاشٍ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ جَابِرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللهِ يَقُولُ:

Matn

لَمَّا قُتِلَ عَبْدُ اللهِ بْنُ عَمْرِو بْنِ حَرَامٍ يَوْمَ أُحُدٍ، لَقِيَنِي رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم، فَقَالَ: "يَا جَابِرُ! أَلَا أُخْبِرُكَ مَا قَالَ اللهُ [عز وجل] (5) لِأَبِيكَ؟ " وَقَالَ يَحْيَى فِي حَدِيثِهِ: فَقَالَ: "يَا جَابِرُ! مَا لِي أَرَاكَ مُنْكَسِرًا؟ " قَالَ (6) : يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! اسْتُشْهِدَ أَبِي وَتَرَكَ عِيَالًا وَدَيْنًا، قَالَ: "أَفَلَا أُبَشِّرُكَ بِمَا لَقِيَ اللهُ بِهِ أَبَاكَ؟ " قَالَ: بَلَى يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! قَالَ: "مَا كَلَّمَ اللهُ أَحَدًا قَطُّ إِلَّا مِنْ وَرَاءِ حِجَابٍ، وَكلَّمَ أَبَاكَ كِفَاحًا (7) ، فَقَالَ: يَا عَبْدِي! تَمَنَّ عَلَيَّ أُعْطِكَ، قَالَ: يَا رَبِّ! تُحْيِينِي فَأُقْتَلُ فِيكَ ثَانِيَةً، فَقَالَ الرَّبُّ تبارك وتعالى (8) : إِنَّهُ سَبَقَ مِنِّي أَنَّهُمْ إِلَيْهَا لَا يَرْجِعُونَ، قَالَ: يَا رَبِّ! فَأَبْلِغْ مَنْ وَرَائِي، قَالَ: فَأَنْزَلَ اللهُ [تَعَالَى:] (5) {وَلَا تَحْسَبَنَّ الَّذِينَ قُتِلُوا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ أَمْوَاتًا بَلْ أَحْيَاءٌ عِنْدَ رَبِّهِمْ يُرْزَقُونَ (169)} [آل عمران: 169] ".

Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; [he said:] (1) Muhammad b. al-Sabbah narrated to us: al-Walid b. Abi Thawr al-Hamdani narrated to us, from Simak, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Umayrah, from al-Ahnaf b. Qays, from al-'Abbas b. 'Abd al-Muttalib, who said:

Matn

“We were (2) in al-Batha' with a group that included the Messenger of God ﷺ. A cloud passed above him. He looked at it and said: ‘What do you call this?’ They said: ‘Al-sahab (cloud).’ (3) He said: ‘And al-muzn.’ We said (4): ‘And al-muzn.’ He said: ‘And al-'anan.’ [Abu Bakr said: They said: ‘And al-'anan.’] (1) He said: ‘How far do you think it is between you and the sky?’ They said: ‘We do not know.’ He said: ‘[Indeed] (1) between you and it is seventy-one, seventy-two (5), or seventy-three years' journey. The heaven above it is likewise,’ until he counted seven heavens. ‘Then above the seventh [heaven] (6) is a sea whose distance from top to bottom is like that from one heaven to another. Above that are eight (8) mountain goats [aw'al], with the distance between their hooves and knees (9) like that between one heaven and another. Then upon their backs is the Throne, whose distance between top and bottom is likewise that between one heaven and another. Then God [Mighty and Majestic] (1), Blessed and Exalted, is above that.’”

English
Isnad

Ya'qub b. Humayd b. Kasib narrated to us: Sufyan b. 'Uyaynah narrated to us, from 'Amr b. Dinar, from 'Ikrimah, from Abu Hurayrah,

Matn

that the Prophet ﷺ said: “When God decrees a matter in heaven, the angels beat their wings (10–11) at His word, which sounds like a chain striking smooth stone. Then {when terror is removed from their hearts, they ask, ‘What did your Lord say?’ They answer, ‘The truth; and He is the Most High, the Most Great’} [Saba' 34:23]. Those who steal a hearing overhear it, one above another. One hears a word (13) and passes it to the one below him. Sometimes the shooting star reaches him before he can pass it on to the one beneath him; at other times he passes it on until it is cast onto the tongue of the soothsayer or sorcerer. He may add a hundred lies to it, and that one word heard from heaven is believed.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا يَعْقُوبُ بْنُ حُمَيْدِ بْنِ كَاسِبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ بْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ دِينَارٍ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ،

Matn

أَنَّ النَّبيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: "إِذًا قَضَى اللهُ أَمْرًا فِي السَّمَاءِ ضَرَبَتِ الْمَلَائِكَةُ أَجْنِحَتَهَا (10) (11) لِقَوْلِهِ، كَأَنَّهُ سِلْسِلَةٌ عَلَى صَفْوَانٍ فَـ {إِذَا فُزِّعَ عَنْ قُلُوبِهِمْ قَالُوا مَاذَا قَالَ رَبُّكُمْ قَالُوا الْحَقَّ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْكَبِيرُ} [سبأ: 23] فَيَسْمَعُهَا مُسْتَرِقُو (12) السَّمْعِ بَعْضُهُمْ فَوْقَ بَعْضٍ، فَيَسْمَعُ الْكَلِمَةَ (13) ، فَيُلْقِيهَا إِلَى مَنْ تَحْتَهُ، فَرُبَّمَا أَدْرَكَهُ الشِّهَابُ قَبْلَ أَنْ يُلْقِيَهَا إِلَى الَّذِي تَحْتَهُ، فَيُلْقِيهَا عَلَى لِسَانِ الْكَاهِنِ أَوِ السَّاحِرِ، فَرُبَّمَا لَمْ يُدْرَكْ حَتَّى يُلْقِيَهَا، فَيَكْذِبُ مَعَهَا مِئَةَ كَذْبَةٍ، فَتُصَدَّقُ (1) تِلْكَ الْكَلِمَةُ الَّتِي سُمِعَتْ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ".
