Chapter45/ 7
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 58
English
Isnad

Suwayd b. Sa'id and Ahmad b. Thabit al-Jahdari narrated to us, they said: 'Abd al-Wahhab al-Thaqafi narrated to us, from Ja'far b. Muhammad, from his father, from Jabir b. 'Abdullah, who said:

Matn

"When the Messenger of God ﷺ delivered a sermon, his eyes would turn red, his voice would rise and his anger would intensify — as if he were warning of an army — saying: 'The enemy will attack you in the morning, the enemy will attack you in the evening.' And he would say: 'I and the Hour (4) have been sent like these two,' joining (5) his two fingers, the index and the middle; and he would say (6): 'To proceed: the best of matters is the Book of God, the best guidance is the guidance of Muhammad, and the worst of matters are the newly invented ones, and every innovation is misguidance.' And he would say: 'Whoever leaves wealth, it is for his family; and whoever leaves a debt or dependents (7), then it is upon me and to me.'"

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا سُوَيْدُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ وَأَحْمَدُ بْنُ ثَابِتٍ الْجَحْدَرِيُّ، قَالَا: حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَهَّابِ الثَّقَفِيُّ، عَنْ جَعْفَرِ بْنِ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللهِ، قَالَ:

Matn

كَانَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِذَا خَطَبَ احْمَرَّتْ عَيْنَاهُ، وَعَلَا صَوْتُهُ، وَاشْتَدَّ غَضَبُهُ - كَأَنَّهُ مُنْذِرُ جَيْشٍ - يَقُولُ: "صَبَّحَكُمْ مَسَّاكُمْ"، وَيَقُولُ: "بُعِثْتُ أَنَا وَالسَّاعَةَ (4) كهَاتَيْنِ"، وَيَقْرُنُ (5) بَيْنَ إِصْبَعَيْهِ السَّبَّابَةِ وَالْوُسْطَى، وَيَقُولُ (6) : "أَمَّا بَعْدُ: فَإِنَّ خَيْرَ الْأَمُورِ كِتَابُ اللهِ، وَخَيْرَ الْهَدْيِ هَدْيُ مُحَمَّدٍ، وَشَرَّ الْأُمُورِ مُحْدَثَاتُهَا، وَكُلَّ بِدْعَةٍ ضَلَالَةٌ". وَكَانَ يقُولُ: مَنْ تَرَكَ مَالًا فَلأَهْلِهِ، وَمَنْ تَرَكَ دَيْنًا أَوْ ضَيَاعًا (7) فَعَلَيَّ وَإِلَيَّ.

Chapter46/ 7
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 58
English
Isnad

(8) Muhammad b. 'Ubayd b. Maymun al-Madani, Abu 'Ubayd, [said:] (3) My father narrated to us, from Muhammad b. Ja'far b. Abi Kathir, from Musa b. 'Uqbah, from Abu Ishaq, from Abu al-Ahwas, from 'Abdullah b. Mas'ud,

Matn

that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: "There are only two things: speech and guidance. The best speech is the Speech of God, and the best guidance is the guidance of Muhammad ﷺ. Beware of newly invented matters, for the worst of matters are the newly invented ones, every newly invented matter is an innovation, and every innovation is misguidance. Beware, let not the time (9) grow long for you so that your hearts become hard. Beware, what is coming is near, and what is far is only what will not come. Beware, the wretched one is he who is wretched in his mother's womb, and the fortunate one is he who takes admonition from others. Beware, fighting a believer is disbelief, insulting him is wickedness, and it is not lawful for a Muslim to shun his brother for more than three days. Beware of lying, for lying is not fit either in earnest or in jest, and a man should not promise (11) his child something and then not fulfill it for him. For lying leads to immorality, and immorality leads to the Fire; and truthfulness leads to righteousness, and righteousness leads to Paradise. It is said to the truthful one: 'He spoke the truth and was righteous,' and it is said to the liar: 'He lied and was immoral.' Beware, a servant keeps lying until he is recorded with God [Mighty and Majestic] (3) as a great liar (12)."

العربية
Isnad

(8) حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عُبَيْدِ بْنِ مَيْمُونٍ الْمَدَنِيُّ، أَبُو عُبَيْدٍ، [قَالَ:] (3) حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ جَعْفَرِ بْنِ أَبِي كَثِيرٍ، عَنْ مُوسَى بْنِ عُقْبَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ أَبِي الْأَحْوَصِ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ،

Matn

أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ: "إِنَّمَا هُمَا اثْنَتَانِ: الْكَلَامُ وَالْهَدْيُ، فَأَحْسَنُ الْكَلَامِ كَلَامُ اللهِ، وَأَحْسَنُ الْهَدْيِ هَدْيُ مُحَمَّدٍ صلى الله عليه وسلم، أَلَا وَإِيَّاكُمْ وَمُحْدَثَاتِ الْأُمُورِ، فَإِنَّ شَرَّ الْأُمُورِ مُحْدَثَاتُهَا، وَكُلَّ مُحْدَثَةٍ بِدْعَةٌ، وَكُلَّ بِدْعَةٍ ضَلَالَةٌ، أَلَا لَا يَطُولَنَّ عَلَيْكُمُ الْأَمَدُ (9) فَتَقْسُوَ قُلُوبُكُمْ، أَلَا إِنَّ مَا هُوَ آتٍ قَرِيبٌ، وَإِنَّمَا الْبَعِيدُ مَا لَيْسَ بِآتٍ، أَلَا إِنَّمَا (10) الشَّقِيُّ مَنْ شَقِيَ فِي بَطْنِ أُمِّهِ، وَالسَّعِيدُ مَنْ وُعِظَ بِغَيْرِهِ، أَلَا إِنَّ قِتَالَ الْمُؤْمِنِ كُفْرٌ، وَسِبَابَهُ فُسُوقٌ، وَلَا يَحِلُّ لِمُسْلِمٍ أَنْ يَهْجُرَ أَخَاهُ فَوْقَ ثَلَاثٍ، أَلَا وَإِيَّاكُمْ وَالْكَذِبَ، فَإِنَّ الْكَذِبَ لَا يَصْلُحُ بِالْجِدِّ وَلَا بِالْهَزْلِ، وَلَا يَعِدُ (11) الرَّجُلُ صَبِيَّهُ ثُمَّ لَا يَفِي لَهُ، فَإِنَّ الْكَذِبَ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْفُجُورِ؛ وَإِنَّ الْفُجُورَ يَهْدِي إِلَى النَّارِ، وَإِنَّ الصِّدْقَ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْبِرِّ؟ وَإِنَّ الْبِرَّ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ، وَإِنَّهُ يُقَالُ لِلصَّادِقِ: صَدَقَ وَبَرَّ، وَيُقَالُ لِلْكَاذِبِ: كَذَبَ وَفَجَرَ، أَلَا وَإِنَّ الْعَبْدَ يَكْذِبُ حَتَّى يُكْتَبَ عِنْدَ اللهِ [عز وجل] (3) كَذَّابًا (12) ".

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Khalid b. Khidash [said:] (1) Isma'il b. 'Ulayyah narrated to us: Ayyub narrated to us (2); (transition) and Ahmad b. Thabit al-Jahdari and Yahya b. Hakim narrated to us, they said: 'Abd al-Wahhab [said:] (3) Ayyub narrated to us, from 'Abdullah b. Abi Mulaykah, from 'A'ishah, who said:

Matn

"The Messenger of God ﷺ recited this verse: {It is He who has sent down to you the Book; in it are verses that are precise — they are the foundation of the Book — and others that are ambiguous} [Al 'Imran: 7] up to His saying {And none will be reminded except those of understanding} [al-Baqarah: 269]. Then he said: 'O 'A'ishah! When you see those who dispute about it, they are the ones whom God [Mighty and Majestic] (3) meant (4), so beware of them.'"

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ خَالِدِ بْنِ خِدَاشٍ [قَالَ:] (1) حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ ابْنُ عُلَيَّةَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ (2) ح وَحَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ بْنُ ثَابِتٍ الْجَحْدَرِيُّ وَيَحْيَى بْنُ حَكِيمٍ، قَالَا: حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَهَّاب، [قال:] (3) حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ أَبِي مُلَيْكَةَ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ قَالَتْ:

Matn

تَلَا رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم هَذِهِ الْآيَةَ: {هُوَ الَّذِي أَنْزَلَ عَلَيْكَ الْكِتَابَ مِنْهُ آيَاتٌ مُحْكَمَاتٌ هُنَّ أُمُّ الْكِتَابِ وَأُخَرُ مُتَشَابِهَاتٌ} [آل عمران: 7] إِلَى قَوْلِهِ {وَمَا يَذَّكَّرُ إِلَّا أُولُو الْأَلْبَابِ} [البقرة: 269] فَقَالَ: "يَا عَائِشَةُ! إِذَا رَأَيْتُمُ الَّذِينَ يُجَادِلُونَ فِيهِ؛ فَهُمُ الَّذِينَ عَنَى (4) اللهُ [عز وجل] (3) ، فَاحْذَرُوهُمْ".

Isnad

Dawud b. Sulayman al-'Askari [said:] (1) Muhammad b. 'Ali Abu Hashim b. Abi Khidash al-Mawsili narrated to us, he said: Muhammad b. Mihsan narrated to us, from Ibrahim b. Abi 'Ablah, from 'Abdullah b. al-Daylami, from Hudhayfah, who said:

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "God does not accept from an innovator fasting, prayer, charity, Hajj, 'Umrah, jihad, any obligatory or voluntary act; he leaves Islam as a hair is drawn out of dough." (5)

Chapter51/ 7
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 59
English
Isnad

(7) 'Abd al-Rahman b. Ibrahim al-Dimashqi and Harun b. Ishaq narrated to us, they said: Ibn Abi Fudayk narrated to us, from Salamah b. Wardan, from Anas b. Malik, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Whoever gives up lying while it is falsehood, a palace will be built for him on the outskirts of Paradise; whoever gives up disputation while in the right, one will be built for him in the middle of it; and whoever perfects his character, one will be built for him in its highest part."

العربية
Isnad

(7) حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ الدِّمَشْقِيُّ وَهَارُونُ بْنُ إِسْحَاقَ، قَالَا: حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ أَبِي فُدَيْكٍ، عَنْ سَلَمَةَ بْنِ وَرْدَانَ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ، قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "مَنْ تَرَكَ الْكَذِبَ وَهُوَ بَاطِلٌ بُنِيَ لَهُ قَصْرٌ فِي رَبَضِ الْجَنَّةِ، وَمَنْ تَرَكَ الْمِرَاءَ وَهُوَ مُحِقٌّ بُنِيَ لَهُ فِي وَسَطِهَا، وَمَنْ حَسَّنَ خُلُقَهُ بُنِيَ لَهُ فِي أَعْلَاهَا".
