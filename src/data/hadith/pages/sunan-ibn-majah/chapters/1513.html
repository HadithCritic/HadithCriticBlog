Chapter4301/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 906
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shaybah narrated to us; Muhammad b. Bishr narrated to us; Zakariyya narrated to us; 'Atiyyah narrated to us, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri,

Matn

that the Prophet ﷺ said, 'I have a Pool stretching from the Ka'bah to Jerusalem. It is white as milk, and its vessels are as numerous as the stars. I shall have the greatest following among the prophets on the Day of Resurrection.'

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بِشْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا زَكرِيَّا، حَدَّثَنَا عَطِيَّةُ عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ [الْخُدْرِيِّ] (2) ،

Matn

أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَال: "إِنَّ لِي حَوْضًا مَا بَيْنَ الْكَعْبَةِ وَبَيْتِ (3) الْمَقْدِسِ، أَبْيَضَ مِثْلَ اللَّبَنِ، آنِيَتُهُ عَدَدُ النُّجُومِ، وَإِنِّي لَأَكْثَرُ الْأَنْبِيَاءِ تَبَعًا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ".

Chapter4302/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 906
English
Isnad

'Uthman b. Abi Shaybah narrated to us; 'Ali b. Mushir narrated to us, from Abu Malik Sa'd b. Tariq, from Rib'i, from Hudhayfah, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said, 'My Pool stretches farther than the distance from Aylah to Aden. By the One in whose hand is my soul, its vessels outnumber the stars; it is whiter than milk and sweeter than honey. By the One in whose hand is my soul, I shall drive men away from it, just as a man drives unfamiliar camels away from his watering-place.' He was asked, 'Messenger of God, will you recognize us?' He replied, 'Yes. You will come to me with radiant faces and shining limbs from the marks of ablution, something given to none besides you.'

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا عُثْمَانُ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ مُسْهِرٍ، عَنْ أَبِي مَالِكٍ سَعْدِ بْنِ طَارِقٍ، عَنْ رِبْعِيٍّ، عَنْ حُذَيْفَةَ قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "إِنَّ حَوْضِي لَأَبْعَدُ مِنْ أَيْلَةَ مِنْ (4) عَدَنَ، وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بيَدِهِ! لآنِيَتُهُ أَكْثَرُ مِنْ عَدَدِ النُّجُوم، وَلَهُوَ أَشَدُّ بَيَاضًا مِنَ اللَّبَنِ، وَأَحْلَى مِنَ الْعَسَلِ، وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بيَدِهِ! إِنِّي لَأَذُودُ عَنْهُ الرِّجَالَ، كَمَا يَذُودُ اَلرَّجُلُ الْإِبِلَ الْغَرِيبَةَ عَنْ حَوْضِهِ"، قِيلَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! أَتَعْرِفُنَا (5) ؟ قَالَ: "نَعَمْ، تَرِدُونَ عَلَيَّ غُرًّا مُحَجَّلِينَ (6) مِنْ آثَارِ (7) الْوُضُوءِ، لَيْسَتْ لِأَحَدٍ غَيْرِكُمْ".

Chapter4303/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 906
English
Isnad

Mahmud b. Khalid al-Dimashqi narrated to us; Marwan b. Muhammad narrated to us; Muhammad b. Muhajir narrated to us; al-'Abbas b. Salim al-Dimashqi, who was reliable, narrated to me, from Abu Sallam al-Habashi, who said:

Matn

'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz sent for me, so I came to him by the postal relay. When I arrived, he said, 'Abu Sallam, we have caused you hardship with your journey.' I said, 'Yes, by God, Commander of the Faithful.' He said, 'By God, I did not intend to make things difficult for you, but I heard that you transmit a report from Thawban, the freedman of the Messenger of God ﷺ, about the Pool, and I wished to hear it directly from your lips.' I said, 'Thawban, freedman of the Messenger of God ﷺ, narrated to me that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: My Pool reaches from Aden to Aylah. It is whiter than milk and sweeter than honey. Its drinking vessels are as numerous as the stars of heaven. Whoever drinks a draught from it will never thirst thereafter. The first to come to me at it will be the poor Emigrants, whose garments are soiled and hair disheveled, who do not marry women raised in luxury and for whom doors are not opened.' 'Umar wept until his beard was wet. He then said, 'Yet I have married women raised in luxury, and doors have been opened for me. I shall certainly not wash the garment nearest my body until it becomes soiled, nor oil my head until my hair becomes disheveled.'

Chapter4306/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 907
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Bashshar narrated to us; Muhammad b. Ja'far narrated to us; Shu'bah narrated to us, from al-'Ala' b. 'Abd al-Rahman, from his father, from Abu Hurayrah, from the Prophet ﷺ:

Matn

He came to the graveyard and greeted its inhabitants, saying, 'Peace be upon you, abode of believing people; God willing, we will join you.' Then he said, 'I wish we had seen our brothers.' They asked, 'Messenger of God, are we not your brothers?' He said, 'You are my Companions; my brothers are those who will come after me, and I shall precede you to the Pool.' They said, 'Messenger of God, how will you recognize those of your community who have not yet come?' He asked, 'If a man had horses with bright facial markings and white legs among solid black horses, would he not recognize them?' They said, 'Indeed.' He said, 'They will come on the Day of Resurrection with radiant faces and shining limbs from the marks of ablution. I shall precede you to the Pool.' Then he said, 'Some men will certainly be driven away from my Pool as a stray camel is driven away. I will call to them, Come here! But it will be said, They changed things after you and kept turning back on their heels. I will say, Away with them! Away with them!'

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ جَعْفَرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنِ الْعَلَاءِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم،

Matn

أَنَّهُ أَتَى الْمَقْبَرَةَ فَسَلَّمَ عَلَى الْمَقْبَرَةِ، فَقَالَ: "السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ دَارَ قَوْمٍ مُؤْمِنِينَ، وَإِنَّا إِنْ شَاءَ اللهُ بِكُمْ لَاحِقُونَ"، ثُمَّ قَالَ: "وَدِدْتُ (6) أَنَّا قَدْ رَأَيْنَا إِخْوَانَنَا"، قَالُوا: يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! أَوَلَسْنَا إِخْوَانَكَ؟ قَالَ: "أَنْتُمْ أَصْحَابِي، وَإِخْوَانِي الَّذِينَ يَأْتُونَ مِنْ بَعْدِي (7) ، وَأَنَا فَرَطُكُمْ (8) عَلَى الْحَوْضِ"، قَالُوا: يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! كَيْفَ تَعْرِفُ مَنْ لَمْ يَأْتِ مِنْ أُمَّتِكَ، قَالَ: "أَرَأَيْتُمْ (9) لَوْ أَنَّ رَجُلًا لَهُ خَيْلٌ غُرٌّ مُحَجَّلَةٌ (10) ، بَيْنَ ظَهْرَانَي خَيْلٍ دُهْم (11) بُهْمٍ، أَلَمْ يَكُنْ يَعْرِفُهَا؟ " قَالُوا: بَلَى. قَالَ: "فَإِنَّهُمْ يَأْتُونَ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ غُرًّا مُحَجَّلِينَ (12) مِنْ آثَارِ (13) الْوُضُوءِ"، قَالَ: "أَنَا فَرَطُكُمْ (14) عَلَى الْحَوْضِ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: لَيُذَادَنَّ (15) رِجَالٌ عَنْ حَوْضِي، كمَا يُذَادُ الْبَعِيرُ الضَّالُّ، فَأُنَادِيهِمْ: أَلَا هَلُمُّوا، فَيُقَالُ: إِنَّهُمْ [قَدْ] (16) بَدَّلُوا بَعْدَكَ، وَلَمْ يَزَالُوا يَرْجِعُونَ عَلَى أَعْقَابِهِمْ، فَأَقُولُ: أَلَا سُحْقًا سُحْقًا".
