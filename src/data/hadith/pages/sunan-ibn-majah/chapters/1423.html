Chapter3867/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 811
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr b. Abi Shayba narrated to us, [he said:] (1) al-Hasan b. Musa narrated to us: Hammad b. Salama narrated to us, from Suhayl b. Abi Salih, from his father, from Abu 'Ayyash al-Zuraqi, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Whoever says in the morning: There is no god but God alone, with no partner; to Him belongs the dominion and to Him belongs all praise, and He has power over all things, it will be for him equal to freeing a slave from the descendants of Isma'il; ten sins will be erased (1) from him, ten ranks will be raised for him, and he will be in a safeguard from Satan until the evening; and when he says it in the evening, the same until the morning." He said: Then a man saw the Messenger of God ﷺ in what a sleeper sees, and said: O Messenger of God! Abu 'Ayyash narrates from you such-and-such. He said: "Abu 'Ayyash has spoken truly."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، [قَالَ:] (1) حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ مُوسَى، حَدَّثَنَا حَمَّادُ بْنُ سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ سُهَيْلِ بْنِ أَبِي صَالِحٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ أَبِي عَيَّاشٍ الزُّرَقِيِّ قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "مَنْ قَالَ حِينَ يُصْبِحُ: لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهُ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، كَانَ لَهُ عَدْلَ رَقَبَةٍ مِنْ وَلَدِ إِسمَاعِيلَ، وَحُطَّ (1) عَنْهُ عَشْرُ خَطِيئَاتٍ، وَرُفِعَ لَهُ عَشْرُ دَرَجَاتٍ، وَكَانَ فِي حِرْزٍ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ، حَتَّى يُمْسِيَ، وَإِذَا أَمْسَى، فَمِثْلُ ذَلِكَ حَتَّى يُصْبِحَ". قَالَ: فَرَأَى رَجُلٌ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِيمَا يَرَى النَّائِمُ، فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ! إِنَّ أَبَا عَيَّاشٍ يَرْوِي عَنْكَ كَذَا وَكَذَا، فَقَالَ: صدَقَ أَبُو عَيَّاشٍ.

Chapter3868/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 812
English
Isnad

Ya'qub b. Humayd b. Kasib narrated to us: 'Abd al-'Aziz b. Abi Hazim narrated to us, from Suhayl, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "When you reach the morning, say: O God! By You we have reached the morning, and by You we have reached the evening, by You we live and by You we die. And when you reach the evening, say: O God! By You we have reached the evening, and by You we have reached the morning, by You we live and by You we die, and to You is the return" (2).

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا يَعْقُوبُ بْنُ حُمَيْدِ بْنِ كَاسِبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْعَزِيزِ بْنُ أَبِي حَازِمٍ، عَنْ سُهَيْلٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "إِذَا أَصْبَحْتُمْ فَقُولُوا: اللَّهُمَّ! بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا، وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِك نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِذَا أَمْسَيْتُمْ فَقُولُوا: اللَّهُمَّ! بِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإلَيْكَ الْمَصِيرُ" (2) .

Chapter3869/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 812
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Bashshar narrated to us, [he said:] (3) Abu Dawud narrated to us: Ibn Abi al-Zinad narrated to us, from his father (4), from Aban b. 'Uthman, who said: I heard 'Uthman b. 'Affan say:

Matn

I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: "There is no servant who says, every morning of the day and every evening of the night: In the name of God, with whose name nothing on earth or in heaven can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing — three times — and then is harmed by anything." He said: And Aban had been struck by a touch of paralysis, and the man began to look at him. Aban said to him: Why do you look at me? The hadith is just as I narrated it to you, but I did not say it that day, so that God might carry out His decree upon me.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا محمد بْنُ بَشَّارٍ، [قَالَ:] (3) حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو دَاوُدَ، حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ أَبِي الزِّنَادِ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ (4) ، عَنْ أَبَانَ بْنِ عُثْمَانَ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ عُثْمَانَ بْنَ عَفَّانَ يَقُولُ:

Matn

سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ: "مَا مِنْ عَبْدٍ يَقُولُ فِي صَبَاحِ كُلِّ يَوْمٍ وَمَسَاءِ كُلِّ لَيْلَةٍ: بِسْمِ اللهِ، الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ، وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ، ثَلَاثَ مَرَّاتٍ فَيَضُرَّهُ شَيْءٌ". قَالَ: وَكَانَ أَبَانُ قَدْ أَصَابَهُ طَرَفٌ مِنَ الْفَالِجِ، فَجَعَلَ الرَّجُلُ يَنْظُرُ إِلَيْهِ، فَقَالَ لَهُ أَبَانُ: مَا تَنْظُرُ إِلَيَّ، أَمَا إِنَّ الْحَدِيثَ كَمَا قَدْ حَدَّثْتُكَ، وَلَكِنِّي لَمْ أَقُلْهُ يَوْمَئِذٍ، لِيُمْضِيَ اللهُ عَلَيَّ قَدَرَهُ.

Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 812
English
Isnad

(5) Abu Bakr b. Abi Shayba narrated to us: Muhammad b. Bishr narrated to us: Mis'ar narrated to us: Abu 'Aqil narrated to us, from Sabiq, from Abu Sallam, the servant (6) of the Prophet ﷺ, from the Prophet ﷺ, who said:

Matn

"There is no Muslim — or person, or servant — who says, when he reaches the evening and when he reaches the morning: I am pleased with God as Lord, with Islam as religion, and with Muhammad as Prophet, except that it is a right upon God, Mighty and Majestic, to please him on the Day of Resurrection."

العربية
Isnad

(5) حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بِشْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا مِسْعَرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَقِيلٍ، عَنْ سَابِقٍ، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَّامٍ خَادِمِ (6) النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ:

Matn

"مَا مِنْ مُسْلِمٍ، أَوْ إِنْسَانٍ، أَوْ عَبْدٍ، يَقُولُ حِينَ يُمْسِي وَحِينَ يُصْبِحُ: رَضِيتُ بِاللهِ رَبًّا، وَبِالْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا، وَبِمُحَمَّدٍ نَبِيًّا، إِلَّا كَانَ حَقًّا عَلَى اللهِ عز وجل، أَنْ يُرْضِيَهُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ".

Chapter3871/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 812
English
Isnad

Ali b. Muhammad [al-Tanafisi] (3) narrated to us: Waki' narrated to us: 'Ubada b. Muslim narrated to us: Jubayr b. Abi Sulayman b. Jubayr b. Mut'im narrated to us, he said: I heard Ibn 'Umar say:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ never used to leave off these supplications (1), when he reached the evening and when he reached the morning: "O God! I ask You for well-being in this world and the Hereafter. O God! I ask You for pardon and well-being in my religion and my worldly life, my family and my wealth. O God! Conceal my faults, and calm my fears; preserve me from before me and from behind me, from my right and from my left, and from above me; and I seek refuge in You from being struck down from beneath me." Waki' said: That is, being swallowed by the earth.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ [الطَّنَافِسِيُّ] (3) ، حَدَّثَنَا وَكِيعٌ، حَدَّثَنَا عُبَادَةُ بْنُ مُسْلِمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا جُبَيْرُ بْنُ أَبِي سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ جُبَيْرِ بْنِ مُطْعِمٍ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ ابْنَ عُمَرَ يَقُولُ:

Matn

لَمْ يَكُنْ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَدَعُ هَؤُلَاءِ الدَّعَوَاتِ (1) ، حِينَ يُمْسِي وَحِينَ يُصْبِحُ: "اللَّهُمَّ! إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَافِيَةَ، فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ، اللَّهُمَّ! إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ، فِي دِينِي وَدُنْيَايَ، وَأَهْلِي وَمَالِي، اللَّهُمَّ! اسْتُرْ عَوْرَاتِي، وَآمِنْ رَوْعَاتِي، وَاحْفَظْنِي مِنْ بَيْنِ يَدَيَّ، وَمِنْ خَلْفِي، وَعَنْ يَمِينِي، وَعَنْ شِمَالِي، وَمِنْ فَوْقِي، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ أُغْتَالَ مِنْ تَحْتِي". قَالَ وَكِيعٌ: يَعْنِي: الْخَسْفَ.

Chapter3872/ 6
Sunan Ibn Mājah · p. 813
English
Isnad

Ali b. Muhammad narrated to us: Ibrahim b. 'Uyayna (2) narrated to us: al-Walid b. Tha'laba narrated to us, from 'Abdullah b. Buraydah, from his father, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "O God! You are my Lord; there is no god but You. You created me and I am Your servant, [and I am] (3) upon Your covenant and Your promise as far as I am able. I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I have done. I acknowledge Your favor upon me, and I acknowledge my sin; so forgive me, for none forgives sins except You." He said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Whoever says it during his day or his night and dies on that day or that night will enter Paradise, if God the Exalted wills."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ (2) ، حَدَّثَنَا الْوَلِيدُ بْنُ ثَعْلَبَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ بُرَيْدَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "اللَّهُمَّ! أَنْتَ رَبِّي، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، [وَأَنَا] (3) عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ بِنِعْمَتِكَ، وَأَبُوءُ بِذَنْبِي، فَاغْفِرْ لِي، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ". قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: "مَنْ قَالَهَا فِي يَوْمِهِ وَلَيْلَتِهِ، فَمَاتَ فِي ذَلِكَ الْيَوْمِ أَوْ تِلْكَ اللَّيْلَةِ، دَخَلَ الْجَنَّةَ إِنْ شَاءَ اللهُ تَعَالَى".
