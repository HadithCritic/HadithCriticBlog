The work

About this compilation.

The supplied edition of Sunan Ibn Mājah is arranged in 38 kitābs and 1,516 bābs, containing 4,341 numbered reports. Its topical order moves from the opening chapters on the Sunnah through worship, law, household and social life, and concluding chapters on tribulations and asceticism.

The reader preserves the edition’s book and chapter divisions so that the Arabic and English can be read alongside the source numbering.
