English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb: And 'Abd al-'Aziz b. Abi Salama said:

Matn

As for anyone who kills by stealth (ghila) without suspicion, enmity or feud, or who wages war against the Muslims by brigandage without any religious interpretation or doubt, but only out of shamelessness, depravity, warring against the Muslims and defection: the family of the slain have no hold over that, nor any condition of pardon or otherwise; only the Imam is its authority.

Isnad

Matn

مَا كَانَ مَنْ قَتَلَ غِيلَةً عَنْ غَيْرِ ظِنَّةٍ وَلا عَدَاوَةٍ وَلا نَائِرَةٍ، أَوْ مُحَارَبَةً لِلْمُسْلِمِينَ بِلُصُوصِيَّةٍ عَنْ غَيْرِ تَأْوِيلٍ فِي دِينٍ وَلا شُبْهَةٍ إِلا خُلُوعًا وَفِسْقًا وَمُحَارَبَةً لِلْمُسْلِمِينَ وَمُرُوقًا، فَإِنَّهُ لَيْسَ لأَهْلِ الدَّمِ فِي ذَلِكَ قَبْضٌ وَلا شَرْطٌ مِنْ عَفْوٍ وَلا غَيْرِهِ، إِنَّمَا وَلِيَ ذَلِكَ الإِمَامُ.

Entry11/ 52
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 1
English
Isnad

He said: Malik said:

Matn

Killing by stealth is when a man kills another without a blood feud or enmity, or when a man is killed for his property. That is not pardoned; it is not like intentional killing out of enmity and feud. The killer by stealth is counted among those who wage war. So whatever killing by stealth is without suspicion, enmity or feud, but is warring against the Muslims by brigandage, the Imam alone is its authority.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: قَالَ مَالِكٌ:

Matn

قَتْلُ الْغِيلَةِ أَنْ يَقْتُلَ رَجُلٌ رَجُلا عَلَى غَيْرِ ذِحْلٍ وَلا عَدَاوَةٍ، وَأَنْ يَقْتُلَ رَجُلٌ عَلَى مَالِهِ، فَإِنَّ ذَلِكَ لَيْسَ يُعْفَى عَنْهُ، لَيْسَ بِمَنْزِلَةِ قَتْلِ الْعَمْدِ عَلَى وَجْهِ الْعَدَاوَةِ وَالنَّائِرَةِ، وَإِنَّمَا قَاتِلُ الْغِيلَةِ يُعَدُّ مِنَ الْمُحَارِبَةِ، فَمَا كَانَ مِنْ قَتْلِ غِيلَةٍ عَنْ غَيْرِ ظِنَّةٍ وَلا عَدَاوَةٍ وَلا نَائِرَةٍ إِلا مُحَارَبَةً لِلْمُسْلِمِينَ بِلُصُوصِيَّةٍ، فَإِنَّمَا وَلِيَ ذَلِكَ الإِمَامُ.

English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: Yunus b. Yazid informed me, from Ibn Shihab, who said: It has reached us from our scholars that they used to say:

Matn

Whoever wages war against the religion and kills a victim, or kills a man by stealth for his property, the ruler kills him for it; the guardian of the slain has no say over his life or death, even if he is his father or brother.

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 1
English
Isnad

And Ibn Shihab said, concerning one who wages war against the community of the Muslims, openly, by stealth or by corruption:

Matn

Ibn Shihab said: The Imam is the authority over his punishment; he kills him for a killing if he killed, whether openly or by stealth, in corruption, according to what God has prescribed for the people of corruption. Among that: killing a Muslim for the money he has with him; cutting the road by banditry (khariba) or brigandage in the open; stealth; raiding the Muslims and the protected peoples (ahl al-dhimma); notorious corruption in the land; and the rebuff (al-rad') in which one shows hostility to the ruler and displays disobedience to him until the corruption becomes great. All of this is among what God mentioned in the verse.

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ ابْنُ شِهَابٍ فِيمَنْ حَارَبَ جَمَاعَةَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ فِي عَلانِيَةٍ أَوْ غِيلَةٍ أَوْ فَسَادٍ،

Matn

قَالَ ابْنُ شِهَابٍ: وَالإِمَامُ وَلِيُّ عُقُوبَتِهِ يَقْتُلُهُ بِقَتْلٍ إِنْ قُتِلَ فِي عَلانِيَةٍ أَوْ غِيلَةٍ بِالْفَسَادِ بِمَا فَرَضَ اللَّهُ عَلَى أَهْلِ الْفَسَادِ، مِنْ ذَلِكَ قَتْلُ الْمُسْلِمِ عَلَى الْمَالِ يَكُونُ مَعَهُ، أَوْ قَطْعُ السَّبِيلِ بِالْخَرَابَةِ أَوِ اللُّصُوصِيَّةُ فِي الْعَلانِيَةِ، أَوِ الْغِيلَةُ أَوِ الْغَارَةُ عَلَى الْمُسْلِمِينَ وَأَهْلِ الذِّمَّةِ، وَالْفَسَادُ الْمَشْهُورُ فِي الأَرْضِ، وَالرَّدْعُ الَّذِي يُعَادِي فِيهِ وَلِيَّ الأَمْرِ، وَيُظْهِرُ فِيهِ مَعْصِيَتَهُ حَتَّى يَعْظُمَ فِيهِ الْفَسَادُ، كُلُّ هَذَا مِمَّا ذَكَرَ اللَّهُ فِي الآيَةِ.

Entry14/ 52
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 1
English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: Ibn Sam'an informed me that Ibn Shihab informed him that he said:

Matn

The sunna has gone that the armed robber and bandit (al-muharib al-kharib), if he kills in aggression, rebellion, corruption in the land and treachery against the religion, and did not take any property, the imams who are rulers over him kill him; it is not proper for the Imam to spare him. If he killed the father or brother of a man and he pardoned him, that is not for him [to decide]; the Imam has contravened the sunna if he lets him live. If he took property and did not kill, his hand and foot are cut off from opposite sides. If he is seized on suspicion of banditry and no evidence stands against him except a right [claim] arising from his hostility and the appearance of depravity in his affair, he is made an example of, banished to a town other than his own, and imprisoned. It is not fitting for the governor to leave in his town someone whom the Muslims fear for their blood and property.

العربية
Isnad

ابْنُ وَهْبٍ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي ابْنُ سَمْعَانَ، أَنَّ ابْنَ شِهَابٍ أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّهُ قَالَ:

Matn

مَضَتِ السُّنَّةُ فِي الْمُحَارِبِ الْخَارِبِ إِذَا قَتَلَ عُدْوَانًا وَبَغْيًا وَفَسَادًا فِي الأَرْضِ وَغِيلَةً فِي الدِّينِ وَلَمْ يُصِبْ مِنَ الأَمْوَالِ شَيْئًا أَنَّ الأَئِمَّةَ وُلَاةَ قِبَلِهِ يَقْتُلُونَهُ، لا يَصْلُحُ لِلإِمَامِ اسْتِبْقَاءَهُ، وَإِنْ قَتَلَ أَبَا رَجُلٍ أَوْ أَخَاهُ وَعَفَا عَنْهُ فَلَيْسَ ذَلِكَ إِلَيْهِ، خَالَفَ الإِمَامُ السُّنَّةَ إِنْ أَحْيَاهُ. وَإِذَا أَصَابَ الأَمْوَالَ وَلَمْ يَقْتُلْ قُطِعَتْ يَدُهُ وَرِجْلُهُ مِنْ خِلافٍ، وَإِذَا أُخِذَ فِي تُهْمَةٍ فِي الْخَرَابَةِ وَلَمْ تَقُمْ عَلَيْهِ بَيِّنَةٌ إِلا بِحَقٍّ قَامَ عَدَائُهُ، وَظُهُورُ الْفِسْقِ فِي أَمْرِهِ نُكِّلَ وَنُفِيَ إِلَى بَلَدٍ سِوَاهُ، وَحُبِسَ فِي السِّجْنِ وَلَمْ يَنْبَغِ لِلْوَالِي أَنْ يُرْسِلَ بِبَلَدِهِ مَنْ يَتَخَوَّفَهُ الْمُسْلِمُونَ عَلَى دِمَائِهِمْ وَأَمْوَالِهِمْ

Isnad

And Yunus b. Yazid informed me, from Ibn Shihab, that he said:

Matn

We hold that whoever kills is to be killed, and whoever seizes property is to be cut, and ijtihad is to be exerted regarding one who terrifies people. If the Imam cuts in both cases, or sees otherwise, his opinion is followed concerning them. And whoever shelters one who introduced an innovation in religion is to be made an example of.

English
Isnad

'Abd Allah b. 'Umar b. Hafs and others informed us, from Humayd al-Tawil, from Anas b. Malik,

Matn

that some people of 'Uraynah came to the Messenger of God ﷺ and found Medina unhealthy. The Messenger of God sent them to a herd of camels of his, and they drank of their milk and urine. When they recovered, they apostatized from Islam, killed the shepherd of the Messenger of God ﷺ, a believer, and drove off the camels. The Messenger of God ﷺ sent after them in their tracks and they were seized. He had their hands and feet cut off, their eyes put out, and they were crucified.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عُمَرَ بْنِ حَفْصٍ وَغَيْرُهُ، عَنْ حُمَيْدٍ الطَّوِيلِ، عَنْ أَنَسِ ابْنِ مَالِكٍ،

Matn

أَنَّ نَاسًا مِنْ عُرَيْنَةَ قَدِمُوا عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﵇، فَاجْتَوَوُا الْمَدِينَةَ، فَبَعَثَهُمْ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ إِلَى ذَودٍ لَهُ، فَشَرِبُوا مِنْ أَلْبَانِهَا وَأَبْوَالِهَا، فَلَمَّا صَحُّوا ارْتَدُّوا عَنِ الإِسْلامِ وَقَتَلُوا رَاعِيَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﵇ مُؤْمِنًا وَاسْتَاقُوا الإِبِلَ، فَبَعَثَ إِلَيْهِمْ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﵇ فِي آثَارِهِمْ فَأُخِذُوا، فَقَطَعَ أَيْدِيَهُمْ وَأَرْجُلَهُمْ وَسَمَلَ أَعْيُنَهُمْ وَصَلَبَهُمْ

English
Isnad

Mu'awiya b. Salih and Yahya b. Ayyub informed me, from Yahya b. Sa'id, from Sa'id b. al-Musayyab, that he said:

Matn

Some people of the Arabs came to the Messenger of God and became Muslims; then they fell ill in Medina and found it unwholesome. The Messenger of God ﷺ sent them to a herd of milch camels of his so that they might drink of their milk, and they stayed there. Then they went for the shepherd, a boy of the Messenger of God ﷺ, killed him, and drove off the herd. He claimed that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “May God make thirsty tonight whoever made the family of Muhammad thirsty.” The Messenger of God ﷺ sent in search of them, and they were seized; he had their hands and feet cut off and their eyes put out; and some of them add to others [in the account], except that Mu'awiya said in the hadith: they drove them to the land of the polytheists.

English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: Yunus informed me, from Ibn Shihab, that he said, about a man who killed, or fornicated, or stole and fled to the land of unbelief and was given a guarantee of safety before being seized: is he held to any of those hudud penalties, or does the guarantee of safety and covenant rescue him?

Matn

Ibn Shihab said: It has reached us from our scholars that they used to say: Whoever wages war against the religion and kills a victim, or a man kills for his property by stealth, the ruler kills him for it; the guardian of the slain has nothing to do with his life, even if he is his father or brother.

Yunus said: And Rabi'a b. Abi 'Abd al-Rahman said:

Matn

As for the muharib among the protected peoples (ahl al-dhimma): if he commits highway robbery, or assists against a vulnerable point of the Muslims or a religion among them, or in some matter in which his covenant of protection is broken, God has prescribed for him what He prescribed in proportion to his sins, according to what they were certain of regarding the action of whoever did it, when suspicion falls on him. His rule is what God prescribed for him of the blood-wits; so the one on whom suspicion falls is attributed with evil and banished to the land of the enemy, and no haste is made to kill him, because his deed was not certain and nothing has appeared beyond suspicion. God said: {Except for those who repent before you gain power over them} [al-Ma'ida 5:34]. So whoever reveals his sin and repents to God is safe regarding his life and is upon his covenant; and whoever the Muslims discovered before that, there is for him what God prescribed of exemplary punishment. Just as the Messenger of God ﷺ expelled the Banu al-Huqayq of the Banu al-Nadir to Khaybar, suspecting them; and as 'Umar b. al-Khattab expelled the people of Fadak and the people of Hunayn to Jericho (Ariha), and their co-religionists from the people of Syria. Their expulsion, when they were banished, was only because of the lands that had reached the Arabs, owing to their nearness to the Muslims, because what is in their hands does not set them right. Today no one is banished to an enemy that the Muslims' interests and dwellings do not reach, beyond the passes and the seas; rather the exemplary punishment that God commanded is applied to the protected peoples in such a matter, and imprisonment, in labor from which they pay the jizya, for what they were suspected of.

Matn

God said in His Book: {The recompense of those who wage war against God and His Messenger and strive upon the earth [to cause] corruption is none but that they be killed or crucified} [al-Ma'ida 5:33], the whole verse. Ibn Abi Salama said: Whoever takes up this position in the abode of the Muslims, even if he was of the people of their call, and wages war against them, cuts their road and strives with corruption in the land against them: these rulings apply to them by the Imam's ijtihad after consultation with those qualified for it. If this muharib killed anyone, he is killed for it; and if he did not kill but caused great corruption, his blood is made lawful by that, and the Imam uses his judgment about it: if he killed him, or crucified him, or cut him, that is his right; because God only said {and strive upon the earth [to cause] corruption}, and did not mention killing specifically, except what includes killing and other things within the sum of corruption. If someone says: it is not proper to kill unless he killed, [the reply is that] God has made killing a soul lawful for corruption when He said: {whoever kills a soul other than for a soul or for corruption in the land} [al-Ma'ida 5:32]. He says: other than for a soul or other than for corruption in the land. He says: whoever kills him when he has not killed a soul and has not corrupted {it is as if he had slain mankind entirely} [al-Ma'ida 5:32]. God did not specify killing in the mention of muharaba by anything except what includes it and other forms of corruption; but corruption varies: some is great and serious, and some is otherwise; so the Imam exercises his judgment about it regarding killing, cutting or crucifixion.

So if he is overpowered for this and has killed, the Imam has no right to pardon him; the killer by stealth is counted among the muharibs.
