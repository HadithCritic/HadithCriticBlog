Matn

Entry151/ 62
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 27
English
Isnad

And Ibn Shihab said, regarding a man who kills a slave intentionally:

Matn

He said: He is punished with a painful, exemplary punishment [in his hair and his purchase(?)], and publicly exposed, and made to pay the highest price of the slave on the day of his killing from his own property outright, even if his price was a thousand dinars, then that is delivered to the slave's master. Ibn Shihab said: And we hold that he is held in prison until he pays the penalty and the humiliation, unless he repents with an acceptable repentance and is released for his repentance and makes the expiation that God commanded for killing.

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ ابْنُ شِهَابٍ فِي الرَّجُلِ يَقْتُلُ الْمَمْلُوكَ عَمْدًا ، قَالَ:

Matn

يُعَاقَبُ عُقُوبَةً مُوجِعَةً مُنْكِلَةً فِي شعره وَشِرَائِهِ ، وَيسمَعُ بِهِ وَيُغَرَّمُ أَغْلَى ثَمَنِ الْعَبْدِ يَوْمَ قَتْلِهِ مِنْ مَالِهِ خَالِصًا ، وَإِنْ كَانَ ثَمَنُهُ أَلْفَ دِينَارٍ ، ثُمَّ يَدْفَعُ ذَلِكَ إِلَى سَيِّدِ الْعَبْدِ. قَالَ ابْنُ شِهَابٍ: وَنَرَى أَنْ يُضَمَّنَ السِّجْنَ حَتَّى يَدِيَ الْجَزَاءَ وَالصِّغَارَ إِلا أَنْ يَتُوبَ تَوْبَةً تُرْضَى مِنْهُ فَيُطْلَقُ لِتَوْبَتِهِ وَيُكَفِّرُ بِالْكَفَّارَةِ الَّتِي أَمَرَ اللَّهُ بِهَا فِي الْقَتْلِ.

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 27
English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: Ibn Sam'an informed me, from Ibn Shihab, that he used to say:

Matn

If a man kills a slave by mistake, his value on the day he was struck is due upon him, whatever it amounts to if the price rose; and if the price fell, the blood-money fell; and he makes expiation with the expiation God commanded for killing, by freeing a believing slave; whoever cannot find one, fasting two consecutive months.

العربية
Isnad

English
Isnad

Malik said regarding the slave:

Matn

He is merely property among properties. If a slave is struck intentionally or by mistake and his master brings one witness, he swears with his witness and then has the price of his slave if he was killed; it is merely property on which his master swears. There is no qasama among slaves, because no one of his people swears with the master of the slain; it is merely property that he takes; nor does anyone of the clan of the killer's master pay with him; his master swears one oath and is entitled to his price. And if he was killed intentionally, there is likewise no qasama or oath in it, and his master is not entitled to that. This is the best that I have heard on it.

And 'Abd al-'Aziz b. Abi Salama said to me:

Matn

The wounds of the slave: his value is assessed sound or assessed wounded, then one looks at the difference between that, and the wounder pays it; we know nothing more just than that. That is because the hand of a slave or a man, if cut off, brings a calamity greater than half his price, and then it has no price after; while his ear brings a calamity less than half his price if he is a boy who weaves brocade and embroidered cloth, or works at something else that raises his price. When the calamity is assessed at whatever it amounts to, the master is not wronged and no one is wronged to him: if the calamity is little, little; if much, much; except that the slave's mawdiha, munaqqila, ma'muma and ja'ifa must have something [fixed] in them, for if they were taken by valuation they would have no value, because they do not return a calamity to his [value], and there is no defect or loss in them except what is of no account, though they have a place in the head and brain and sometimes reach the bone of the soul; so we hold that it be set in his price on the same reckoning as the free man's blood-money.

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 27
English
Isnad

He said: And I heard Malik say about the slave's offense:

Matn

Whatever he inflicts of a wound with which he wounded a person, or something he snatched from a person, or a camel he stole, or hanging fruit he took, or a theft he committed for which there is no cutting: all that is on the slave's person; if his master wishes to give the value of what he took, spoiled or wounded he may, and if he wishes he surrenders him; his master has the choice in that. As for what was handed to him for his work or what he took on credit, that is in his own liability. Malik said: That is the practice with us.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: وَسَمِعْتُ مَالِكًا يَقُولُ فِي جِنَايَةِ الْعَبْدِ:

Matn

إِنَّ مَا أَصَابَ مِنْ جُرْحٍ جَرَحَ بِهِ إِنْسَانًا، أَوْ شَيْءٍ اخْتَلَسَهُ مِنْ إِنْسَانٍ، أَوْ بَعِيرٍ احْتَرَسَهُ، أَوْ ثَمَرٍ مُعَلَّقٍ أَخَذَهُ، أَوْ سَرِقَةٍ سَرَقَهُ لا قَطْعَ فِيهَا، إِنَّ ذَلِكَ فِي رَقَبَةِ الْعَبْدِ إِنْ شَاءَ سَيِّدُهُ أَنْ يُعْطِيَ قِيمَةَ مَا أَخَذَ أَوْ أَفْسَدَ أَوْ جَرَحَ، وَإِنْ شَاءَ أَسْلَمَهُ فَسَيِّدُهُ فِي ذَلِكَ بِالْخِيَارِ، فَأَمَّا مَا دَفَعَ إِلَيْهِ بِعَمَلِهِ أَوْ أَدَانَ بِهِ، فَإِنَّ ذَلِكَ يَكُونُ فِي ذِمَّتِهِ. قَالَ مَالِكٌ: وَذَلِكَ الأَمْرُ عِنْدَنَا.
