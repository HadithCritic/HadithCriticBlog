Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 19
English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: Yunus b. Yazid and Ibn Sam'an informed me, from Ibn Shihab, that he said:

Matn

The first fitna broke out and caught a number of the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ who had been present at Badr with the Messenger of God ﷺ. It has reached us that they held that the matter of the fitna should be set aside, so that no retaliation is applied in it against a man who killed on the basis of an interpretation of the Qur'an, nor any hadd for [taking] a woman captive, and we do not see a hadd upon him; nor do we see li'an between her and her husband; nor do we see that anyone may accuse her without being flogged the hadd; and we hold that she is returned to her first husband after she observes the waiting period and her waiting period from the other husband ends; and we hold that she inherits from her first husband.

العربية
Isnad

ابْنُ وَهْبٍ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي يُونُسُ بْنُ يَزِيدَ، وَابْنُ سَمْعَانَ ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ أَنَّهُ قَالَ:

Matn

هَاجَتِ الْفِتْنَةُ الأُولَى فَأَدْرَكَتْ رِجَالا ذَوِي عَدَدٍ مِنْ أَصْحَابِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ مِمَّنْ شَهِدَ بَدْرًا مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﵇، فَبَلَغَنَا أَنَّهُمْ كَانُوا يَرَوْنَ أَنْ يُهْدَمَ أَمْرُ الْفِتْنَةِ فَلا يُقَامُ فِيهَا عَلَى رَجُلٍ قَاتِلٍ فِي تَأْوِيلِ الْقُرْآنِ قِصَاصٌ فِيمَنْ قَتَلَ، وَلا حَدٌّ فِي سَبْيِ امْرَأَةٍ سُبِيَتْ، وَلا نَرَى عَلَيْهِ حَدًّا، وَلا نَرَى بَيْنَهَا وَبَيْنَ زَوْجِهَا مُلاعَنَةً، وَلا نَرَى أَنْ يَقْذِفَهَا أَحَدٌ إِلا جُلِدَ الْحَدَّ، وَنَرَى أَنْ تُرَدَّ إِلَى زَوْجِهَا الأَوَّلِ بَعْدَ أَنْ تَعْتَدَّ فَتَنْقَضِي عِدَّتُهَا مِنْ زَوْجِهَا الآخَرِ، وَنَرَى أَنْ تَرِثَ زَوْجَهَا الأَوَّلَ

English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: Ibn Abi Dhi'b informed me, from Ibn Shihab, from men who had been present at Badr,

Matn

that they used to say: If a man interpreted [the Qur'an] with the Haruriyya and fought and killed, and then died; or a woman interpreted the Qur'an and went out and joined the Khawarij, leaving her husband, and fought with them and married among them, and then came repentant, there would be no hadd upon her, and the hadd would be upon whoever accused her; there would be no li'an between her and her husband; she would be kept from her husband until her womb is cleared, then return to her first husband.

العربية
Isnad

ابْنُ وَهْبٍ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي ابْنُ أَبِي ذِئْبٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ رِجَالٍ شَهِدُوا بَدْرًا

Matn

أَنَّهُمْ كَانُوا يَقُولُونَ: لَوْ أَنَّ رَجُلا تَأَوَّلَ مَعَ الْحَرُورِيَّةِ فَقَاتَلَ وَقُتِلَ، ثُمَّ مَاتَ، أَوِ امْرَأَةً تَأَوَّلَتِ الْقُرْآنَ فَخَرَجَتْ حَتَّى لَحِقَتْ بِالْخَوَارِجِ وَتَرَكَتْ زَوْجَهَا وَقَاتَلَتْ مَعَهُمْ وَتَزَوَّجَتْ فِيهِمْ، ثُمَّ جَاءَتْ تَائِبَةً لَمْ يَكُنْ عَلَيْهَا حَدٌّ، وَكَانَ عَلَى مَنْ قَذَفَهَا الْحَدُّ، وَلَمْ تَكُنْ بَيْنَهَا وَبَيْنَ زَوْجِهَا مُلاعَنَةٌ، وَحُبِسَتْ عَنْ زَوْجِهَا حَتَّى تُسْتَبْرَأَ، ثُمَّ تَرْجِعَ إِلَى زَوْجِهَا الأَوَّلِ

English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: Ibn Sam'an informed me that those he met of the early generations

Matn

used to say: There are two kinds of apostasy: an apostasy of unbelief by which killing, enslavement and the cutting off of inheritances become lawful; and an apostasy of violating the laws of Islam, for which its people are fought, but their enslavement and the taking of their property are not lawful. And this is the practice of Abu Bakr al-Siddiq regarding those who apostatized in his time.

العربية
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: al-Harith b. Nabhan informed me, from Ayyub al-Sakhtiyani, narrating from Ibn Sirin,

Matn

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 23
English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: al-Layth b. Sa'd informed me; he said: Rabi'a wrote to me, saying about some of the Copts of Egypt who become Muslim and then become Christian:

Matn

Were it not for what was left between them and the people of that, until its delusion struck them and they felt secure from its effort, you would have seen killing upon them, abased and contemptible, and not letting them live. They had an excuse in the people's leaving them alone and the scarcity of rebuke to them in that when they withdrew from Islam. So the opinion is that notice be given to them and that they know what is upon them in withdrawing from Islam; hence I am wary lest only people of sincere intention enter Islam, so that the proof is against whoever leaves Islam. And Rabi'a said: Nothing is sought from a Muslim other than his response to Islam. The one who takes cover in Islam is shielded from [scrutiny of] his hidden state, with clearer [reason] in the stupefaction of Islam for whoever covered him from what he concealed. So the hypocrites were covered in the time of the Messenger of God by what they proclaimed of Islam, though he knew them by their marks and knew them in the tone of speech and their reports came to him. No cause is sought against whoever displays Islam and is not truthful, and in the matter of Islam they have delusion [by] leaving their homelands and settling in a foreign land. If the Christian's marriage is permissible, then it is more permissible for him; and Islam clothes him, and not all Muslims are put to the test, to be examined and questioned about jurisprudence; how many Muslims are not suspected regarding Islam, though they may be neither reciters nor jurists.

Isnad

English
Isnad

Malik said:

Matn

Regarding one who was an unbeliever and becomes Muslim until his Islam is good, and then disbelieves: if he does not repent he is killed. He said: The slave and the free man are equal in that; if the slave does not repent he is killed.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ مَالِكٌ:

Matn

فِي الَّذِي يَكُونُ كَافِرًا فَيُسْلِمُ حَتَّى يَحْسُنَ إِسْلامُهُ ، ثُمَّ يَكْفُرُ ، إِنَّهُ إِنْ لَمْ يَتُبْ قُتِلَ ، قَالَ: وَالْعَبْدُ وَالْحُرُّ فِي ذَلِكَ سَوَاءٌ ، إِنْ لَمْ يَتُبِ الْعَبْدُ قُتِلَ.
