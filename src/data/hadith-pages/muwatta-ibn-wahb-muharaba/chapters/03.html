Entry62/ 15
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 13
English
Isnad

He said: And Muhammad b. 'Amr informed me, from Ibn Jurayj, who said:

Matn

I said to 'Ata' b. Abi Rabah: “What makes it lawful for me to fight the Haruriyya?” He said: “When they cut the road and terrify the secure.” I said to 'Ata': “The Haruriyya fought, and then were seized?” He said: “Whoever of them killed is killed, and the goods are taken from whoever took them, and he is not cut; and whoever [is liable to] banishment is imprisoned; and they are asked to repent and are not killed.” Ibn Jurayj said: 'Abd al-Karim said it too.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: وَأَخْبَرَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَمْرٍو، عَنِ ابْنِ جُرَيْجٍ قَالَ:

Matn

قُلْتُ لِعَطَاءِ بْنِ أَبِي رَبَاحٍ: مَا يُحِلُّ لِي قِتَالَ الْحَرُورِيَّةِ قَالَ: إِذَا قَطَعُوا السَّبِيلَ وَأَخَافُوا الآمِنَ، قَالَ: فَقُلْتُ لِعَطَاءٍ: فَقَاتَلَتِ الْحَرُورِيَّةُ، ثُمَّ أُخِذُوا، قَالَ: يُقْتَلُ مِنْهُمْ مَنْ قَتَلَ، وَيُؤْخَذُ الْمَتَاعُ مِمَّنْ أَخَذَهُ مِنْهُمْ، وَلا يُقَطَّعُ، وَيُسْجَنُ مِنْ نَفْيٍ، وَيُسْتَتَابُونَ وَلا يُقْتَلُونَ. قَالَ ابْنُ جُرَيْجٍ: وَقَالَهُ عَبْدُ الْكَرِيمِ

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 13
English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: And it has reached me concerning 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz

Matn

that he wrote regarding a Kharijite who rose in Khurasan and brandished his sword and was seized: “If he killed, he is killed; if he wounded, he is wounded; otherwise he is committed to prison. Place his family close to him until he repents of his evil opinion.” And al-Dahhak b. Muzahim said the like of it.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ ابْنُ وَهْبٍ: وَبَلَغَنِي عَنْ عُمَرَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ

Matn

أَنَّهُ كَتَبَ فِي خَارِجِيٍّ خَرَجَ بِخُرَسَانَ فَأَشَارَ بِسَيْفِهِ فَأُخِذَ، إِنْ كَانَ قَتَلَ قُتِلَ، وَإِنْ كَانَ جَرَحَ جُرِحَ، وَإِلا اسْتُودِعَ السِّجْنَ، فَاجْعَلُوا أَهْلَهُ قَرِيبًا مِنْهُ حَتَّى يَتُوبَ مِنْ رَأْيِهِ السُّوءِ. وَقَالَ الضَّحَّاكُ بْنُ مُزَاحِمٍ مِثْلَهُ.

Entry66/ 15
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 13
English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: Muhammad b. 'Amr informed me, from Ibn Jurayj, from 'Abd al-Karim,

Matn

that the Haruriyya rose, disputed with 'Ali, left him and testified against him of polytheism, and he did not attack them. Then they went out to Harura'. 'Ali b. Abi Talib was told that they were equipping themselves at al-Kufa; he said: “Leave them.” Then they went out and camped at al-Nahrawan and stayed there a month. It was said to him: “Fight them now.” He said: “No, not until they spill blood, cut the road, and terrify the secure.” So he did not attack them until they killed; then he sent forces against them, and they were killed.

العربية
Isnad

ابْنُ وَهْبٍ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَمْرٍو، عَنِ ابْنِ جُرَيْجٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْكَرِيمِ

Matn

Entry67/ 15
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 14
English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: Yunus informed me, from Ibn Shihab, who said: Abu Salama b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf informed me, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, who said:

Matn

While we were with the Messenger of God ﷺ, who was dividing a share, Dhu al-Khuwaysira, a man of the Banu Tamim, came to him and said: “O Messenger of God, be just!” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Woe to you! Who will be just if I am not just? You would be ruined and lost if I were not just.” 'Umar said: “O Messenger of God, permit me to strike his neck.” He said: “Leave him, for he has companions such that one of you would belittle his prayer beside their prayer and his fasting beside their fasting. They recite the Qur'an but it does not pass their collarbones. They pass through Islam as the arrow passes through the game. One looks at its blade and finds nothing on it; then at its binding (risaf) and finds nothing; then at its shaft (nadiyy), which is the wooden stick (qidh), and finds nothing; then at its feathers (qudhadh) and finds nothing: it has outstripped the dung and blood. Their sign is a black man, one of whose arms is like a woman's breast, or like a quivering piece of flesh. They go out at a time of division among the best group of people.” Abu Sa'id al-Khudri said: I bear witness that I heard this from the Messenger of God, and I bear witness that 'Ali b. Abi Talib fought them, and I was with him. He ordered that man be sought; he was found and brought, until I looked at him, matching the description of the Messenger of God ﷺ that he had described.

العربية
Isnad

ابْنُ وَهْبٍ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي يُونُسُ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي أَبُو سَلَمَةَ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ عَوْفٍ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيِّ، قَالَ:

Matn

بَيْنَا نَحْنُ عِنْدَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﵇، وَهُوَ يَقْسِمُ قَسْمًا، أَتَاهُ ذُو الْخُوَيْصِرَةِ، وَهُوَ رَجُلٌ مِنْ بَنِي تَمِيمٍ، فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَعْدِلْ، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﵇: وَيْلَكَ، وَمَنْ يَعْدِلُ إِذَا لَمْ أَعْدِلْ، قَدْ خِبْتَ وَخَسِرْتَ إِنْ لَمْ أَعْدِلْ، قَالَ عُمَرُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، ائْذَنْ لِي فِيهِ أَضْرِبَ عُنُقَهُ، قَالَ: دَعْهُ، فَإِنَّ لَهُ أَصْحَابًا يُحَقِّرُ أَحَدُكُمْ صَلاتَهُ مَعَ صَلاتِهِمْ وَصِيَامَهُ مَعَ صِيَامِهِمْ، يَقْرَءُونَ الْقُرْآنَ لا يُجَاوِزُ تَرَاقِيَهُمْ، يَمْرُقُونَ مِنَ الإِسْلامِ كَمَا يَمْرُقُ السَّهْمُ مِنَ الرَّمِيَّةِ، يُنْظَرُ إِلَى نَصْلِهِ فَلا يُوجَدُ فِيهِ شَيْءٌ، ثُمَّ يُنْظَرُ إِلَى رِصَافِهِ فَلا يُوجَدُ فِيهِ شَيْءٌ، ثُمَّ يُنْظَرُ إِلَى نَضِيِّهِ فَلا يُوجَدُ فِيهِ شَيْءٌ، وَهُوَ الْقِدْحُ، ثُمَّ يُنْظَرُ إِلَى قُذَذِهِ فَلا يُوجَدُ فِيهِ شَيْءٌ، سَبَقَ الْفَرْثَ وَالدَّمَ، آيَتُهُمْ رَجُلٌ أَسْوَدُ، إِحْدَى عَضُدَيْهِ مِثْلَ ثَدْيِ الْمَرْأَةِ، أَوْ مِثْلَ الْبَضْعَةِ تَدَرْدَرُ، يَخْرُجُونَ عَلَى خَيْرِ فِرْقَةٍ مِنَ النَّاسِ. قَالَ أَبُو سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيُّ: فَأَشْهَدُ أَنِّي سَمِعْتُ هَذَا مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ عَلِيَّ بْنَ أَبِي طَالِبٍ قَاتَلَهُمْ وَأَنَا مَعَهُ، فَأَمَرَ بِذَلِكَ الرَّجُلِ، فَالْتُمِسَ فَوُجِدَ فَأُتِيَ بِهِ حَتَّى نَظَرْتُ إِلَيْهِ عَلَى نَعْتِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﵇ الَّذِي نَعَتَ

Entry68/ 15
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 15
English
Isnad

And 'Amr b. al-Harith informed me, from Bukayr b. al-Ashajj, from Busr b. Sa'id, from 'Ubayd Allah b. Abi Rafi', mawla of the Messenger of God ﷺ, who said:

Matn

When the Haruriyya rose, while he was with 'Ali b. Abi Talib, and they said: “Judgment belongs only to God,” 'Ali said: “A word of truth by which falsehood is intended. The Messenger of God ﷺ described some people, and I know their description in these: they speak the truth with their tongues, and it does not pass this” (and he pointed to his throat). “The most hateful of God's creation to Him among them is a black man, one of whose hands is like a ewe's teat or the nipple of a breast.” When 'Ali b. Abi Talib had killed them, he said: “Look.” They looked and found nothing. He said: “Go back; by God, I have not lied and I have not been lied to,” twice or three times. Then they found him in a ruin and brought him until they placed him before him. 'Ubayd Allah said: I was present at that, at their affair and at 'Ali's statement about them. Bukayr said: And a man narrated to me from Ibn Hunayn that he said: I saw that black man.

العربية
Isnad

Matn

Ibn 'Ayyash said: “The people are recently afflicted with a severe calamity, and if you speak to them they will say yes, then flee from you and not fight with you.” When the two saw that, they departed that night, and I was with them and some people, and they joined Mecca. Then God turned those Haruriyya away from Medina and they did not come to it.

He wrote to them calling them to the community and denouncing their rising contrary to the truth and the sunna, [saying]: “and you are few and abased.” They wrote back an answer in which they were forceful, saying: “As for what you mentioned of our fewness and abasement, God said to the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ: {And remember when you were few and oppressed in the land, fearing that people might abduct you, but He sheltered you and supported you with His help} [al-Anfal 8:26]; and we hope for that.” So he sent to them 'Awn b. 'Abd Allah b. 'Utba b. Mas'ud al-Hudhali. When he came to them he said to them: “You fought all your time for [the desire] that someone act as 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz has acted; and when your opinion that you were seeking came, and people said: this, by God, is their opinion, you were the first to flee from it.” They said: “By God, you have spoken truly: we sought nothing but what he did; but he did not disown those before him and did not curse them.” I said: “Will you answer me truthfully about what I ask?” They said: “Yes; you will not ask us anything but we will tell you the truth.” I said: “When did you last curse Haman?” They said: “We never cursed him.” I said to them: “Does it suffice you to leave the minister of Pharaoh who carried out his command, yet it does not suffice 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz to act with the truth and withhold cursing from the people of his qibla, if they erred in something and acted without the truth?” He returned to 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz and told him, and he said: “I would not have wished to send anyone to them but you.” He said to him: “How did you think of Haman?” He said: “I feared that if I mentioned Pharaoh they would say: we have cursed him, for he is accursed, vile.” He said: And 'Umar wrote to Yahya b. Yahya al-Ghassani, who was in charge of al-Mawsil: “Leave them as long as they do not shed blood, cut the road, or terrify a covenanter; if they do any of that they are the enemy, so kill them wherever you find them.” So they held back their hands until 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz died. Then they rose in the caliphate of Yazid b. 'Abd al-Malik and were killed.

Isnad

وَأَخْبَرَنِي اللَّيْثُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ قَالَ:

Matn

خَرَجَ أَرْبَعُونَ حَرُورِيًّا فِي زَمَانِ عُمَرَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ، فَكَتَبَ إِلَيْهِمْ يَدْعُوهُمْ إِلَى الْجَمَاعَةِ وَيُنْكِرُ عَلَيْهِمْ خُرُوجَهُمْ خِلافًا لِلْحَقِّ وَالسُّنَّةِ، وَأَنْتُمْ قَلِيلٌ أَذِلَّةٌ، فَكَتَبُوا إِلَيْهِ جَوَابًا أَبْلَغُوا فِيهِ فَقَالُوا: أَمَّا مَا ذَكَرْتَ مِنْ قِلَّتِنَا وَذِلَّتِنَا فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ قَالَ لأَصْحَابِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﵇: {وَاذْكُرُوا إِذْ أَنْتُمْ قَلِيلٌ مُسْتَضْعَفُونَ فِي الأَرْضِ تَخَافُونَ أَنْ يَتَخَطَّفَكُمُ النَّاسُ فَآوَاكُمْ وَأَيَّدَكُمْ بِنَصْرِهِ} [الأنفال: 26] ، فَنَحْنُ نَرْجُو ذَلِكَ. فَبَعَثَ إِلَيْهِمْ عَوْنَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُتْبَةَ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ الْهُذَلِيُّ، فَلَمَّا قَدِمَ عَلَيْهِمْ قَالَ لَهُمْ: قَاتَلْتُمْ دَهْرَكُمْ كُلَّهُ عَلَى أَنْ يَعْمَلَ بِالَّذِي عَمِلَ بِهِ عُمَرُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ، فَلَمَّا جَاءَ رَأْيُكُمُ الَّذِي كُنْتُمْ تَطْلُبُونَ، وَقَالَ النَّاسُ: هَذَا، وَاللَّهِ ، رَأْيُهُمْ، كُنْتُمْ أَوَّلُ مَنْ نَفَرَ عَنْهُ، قَالُوا: وَاللَّهِ ، لَقَدْ صَدَقْتَ مَا كُنَّا نَطْلُبُ إِلا الَّذِي عَمِلَ بِهِ، وَلَكِنَّهُ لَمْ يَتَبَرَّأْ مِنَ الَّذِينَ كَانُوا قَبْلَهُ، وَلَمْ يَلْعَنْهُمْ ، فَقُلْتُ: هَلْ أَنْتُمْ صَادِقِيَّ عَمَّا أَسْأَلُكُمْ عَنْهُ، فَقَالُوا: نَعَمْ، لَمْ تَسْأَلْنَا عَنْ شَيْءٍ إِلا صَدَقْنَاكَ، فَقُلْتُ: مَتَى عَهْدُكُمْ بِلَعْنِ هَامَانَ؟ فَقَالُوا: مَا لَعَنَّاهُ قَطُّ فَقُلْتُ لَهُمْ: أَفَيَسَعُكُمْ أَنْ تَتْرُكُوا وَزِيرَ فِرْعَوْنَ الْمُنَفِّذَ لأَمْرِهِ، وَلا يَسَعُ عُمَرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ أَنْ يَعْمَلَ بِالْحَقِّ وَيَكُفَّ اللَّعْنَ عَنْ أَهْلِ قِبْلَتِهِ إِنْ كَانُوا أَخْطَئُوا فِي شَيْءٍ وَعَمِلُوا بِغَيْرِ الْحَقِّ. فَرَجَعَ إِلَى عُمَرَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ فَأَخْبَرَهُ، فَقَالَ: مَا أُحِبُّ أَنِّي بَعَثْتُ إِلَيْهِمْ غَيْرَكَ، فَقَالَ لَهُ: كَيْفَ فَطِنْتَ لِهَامَانَ، فَقَالَ: تَخَوَّفْتُ إِنْ ذَكَرْتُ فِرْعَوْنَ أَنْ يَقُولُوا قَدْ لَعَنَّاهُ فَإِنَّهُ مُلْعَنٌ خَبِيثٌ. قَالَ: وَكَتَبَ عُمَرُ إِلَى يَحْيَى بْنِ يَحْيَى الْغَسَّانِيِّ وَكَانَ عَلَى الْمَوْصِلِ أَنْ أَقْرِرْهُمْ مَا لَمْ يَسْفِكُوا دَمًا أَوْ يَقْطَعُوا سَبِيلا أَوْ يُخِيفُوا مُعَاهِدًا، فَإِنْ فَعَلُوا شَيْئًا مِنْ ذَلِكَ فَهُمُ الْعَدُوُّ، فَاقْتُلُوهُمْ حَيْثُ ثَقِفْتُمُوهُمْ، فَأَمْسَكُوا بِأَيْدِيهِمْ حَتَّى تُوُفِّيَ عُمَرُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ. ثُمَّ خَرَجُوا فِي خِلافَةِ يَزِيدَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الْمَلِكِ، فَقُتِلُوا.

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Armed Robbery · p. 16
English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: Ibn Lahi'a informed me, from Yazid b. Abi Habib,

Matn

that a man of the Haruriyya said to 'Abd Allah b. 'Umar: “You are an unbeliever.” 'Abd Allah b. 'Umar said to him: “You lie.” Then he met 'Abd Allah b. 'Abbas and said the same to him. Ibn 'Abbas said: “In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Say: O disbelievers, I do not worship what you worship”, and he recited it until he finished it. The Haruri said: “God said: {Rather, they are a contentious people} [al-Zukhruf 43:58].”
