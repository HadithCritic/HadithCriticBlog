Entry20063/ 41
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 111
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported: Ma'mar reported from al-Zuhri, from Ibn al-Musayyib, that 'Umar b. al-Khattab said: “

Matn

Prophet of God, do we act regarding something already settled or something that lies ahead of us?” He answered: “Regarding something already settled.” 'Umar said: “Then why act?” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “None attains it except through action.” 'Umar said: “Then we shall strive.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنِ ابْنِ الْمُسَيِّبِ، أَنَّ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ، قَالَ:

Matn

يَا نَبِيَّ اللَّهِ، أَرَأَيْتَ مَا نَعْمَلُ أَلِأَمْرٍ قَدْ فُرِغَ مِنْهُ، أَمْ لِأَمْرٍ نَسْتَقْبِلُهُ استِقْبَالًا؟ قَالَ: «§بَلْ لِأَمْرٍ قَدْ فُرِغَ مِنْهُ» ، فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: فَفِيمَ الْعَمَلُ؟ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «كُلٌّ لَا يُنَالُ إِلَّا بِالْعَمَلِ» ، فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: إِنَّا نَجْتَهِدُ

Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Ibn Tawus, from his father:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ was asked: “Why do we act, Messenger of God? Is it about something we are beginning anew, or something already settled?” He answered: “Something already settled.” They asked: “Then why act?” He answered: “Each person is made ready [for his course].” They said: “Now we shall strive.”

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 112
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from Humayd b. 'Abd al-Rahman, from his mother Umm Kulthum bint 'Uqba—one of the earliest female Emigrants—

Matn

that 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf fell unconscious, and they thought his life was ending. She went out to the mosque to seek help through what she had been commanded: patience and prayer. When he recovered, he asked: “Was I unconscious?” They said: “Yes.” He replied: “You are right. Two angels came to me during this unconsciousness and said: ‘Come, let us bring you to judgment before the Mighty, the Trustworthy.’ Another angel said: ‘Send him back. He is among those for whom happiness was written while they were in their mothers’ wombs, and God will allow his children to benefit from him for as long as He wills.’” He lived a month afterward and then died.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ حُمَيْدِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنِ أُمِّهِ أُمِّ كُلْثُومٍ ابْنَةِ عُقْبَةَ - وَكَانَتْ مِنَ الْمُهَاجِرَاتِ الْأُوَلِ -

Matn

أَنَّ عَبْدَ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنَ عَوْفٍ غُشِيَ عَلَيْهِ غَشْيَةً، ظَنُّوا أَنَّ نَفْسَهُ فِيهَا، فَخَرَجَتْ إِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ تَسْتَعِينُ بِمَا أُمِرَتْ أَنْ تَسْتَعِينَ بِهِ مِنَ الصَّبْرِ وَالصَّلَاةِ، فَلَمَّا أَفَاقَ، قَالَ: «أَغُشِيَ عَلَيَّ؟» ، قَالُوا: نَعَمْ، قَالَ: «صَدَقْتُمْ، إِنَّهُ §أَتَانِي مَلَكَانِ فِي غَشْيَتِي هَذِهِ، فَقَالَا: أَلَا تَنْطَلِقُ فَنُحَاكِمَكَ إِلَى الْعَزِيزِ الْأَمِينِ؟ فَقَالَ مَلَكٌ آخَرُ: أَرْجِعَاهُ فَإِنَّ هَذَا مِمَّنْ كُتِبَتْ لَهُ السَّعَادَةُ، وَهُمْ فِي بُطُونِ أُمَّهَاتِهِمْ، وَسَيُمَتِّعُ اللَّهُ بِهِ بَنِيهِ مَا شَاءَ اللَّهُ» ، قَالَ: فَعَاشَ شَهْرًا ثُمَّ مَاتَ

Matn

Adam and Moses disputed. Moses said to Adam: ‘Are you the Adam who brought your descendants into the Fire?’ Adam replied: ‘Moses, God chose you for His message and speech and revealed the Torah to you. Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Hammam b. Munabbih, from Abu Hurayra, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “

Matn

Adam and Moses argued. Moses said: ‘You are the one who led people astray and brought them out of Paradise to earth.’ Adam replied: ‘Are you the one to whom God granted knowledge of everything and whom He chose above people with His message?’ Moses said: ‘Yes.’ Adam said: ‘Then do you blame me for something written before I did it—or, he said, before I was created?’ Thus Adam prevailed over Moses.”

Isnad

Matn

تَحَاجَّ آدَمُ وَمُوسَى، فَقَالَ مُوسَى: أَنْتَ الَّذِي أَغْوَيْتَ النَّاسَ وَأَخْرَجْتَهُمْ مِنَ الْجَنَّةِ إِلَى الْأَرْضِ، فَقَالَ لَهُ آدَمُ: أَنْتَ الَّذِي أَعْطَاكَ اللَّهُ عِلْمَ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ، وَاصْطَفَاكَ عَلَى النَّاسِ بِرِسَالَتِهِ؟ قَالَ: نَعَمْ، قَالَ: أَفَتَلُومُنِي عَلَى أَمْرٍ كَانَ قَدْ كُتِبَ قَبْلَ أَنْ أَفْعَلَهُ - أَوْ قَالَ: مِنْ قَبْلِ أَنْ أُخْلَقَ - قَالَ: فَحَجَّ آدَمُ مُوسَى» .

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, and from Ibn Tawus, from his father, both of whom said: “

Matn

Jesus son of Mary met Iblis and said: ‘Do you not know that nothing will happen to you except what is decreed for you?’ Iblis replied: ‘Then climb to the summit of this mountain and throw yourself down, and see whether you live or die.’ According to Ibn Tawus, from his father, Jesus answered: ‘Do you not know that God has said: “Let not My servant test Me, for I do whatever I will”?’ According to al-Zuhri, he said: ‘A servant does not test his Lord; it is God who tests His servant.’ Thus he defeated Iblis in the argument.”

Isnad

Matn

لَقِيَ عِيسَى ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ إِبْلِيسَ فَقَالَ: أَمَا عَلِمْتَ أَنَّهُ لَا يُصِيبُكَ إِلَّا مَا قُدِّرَ لَكَ؟ فَقَالَ إِبْلِيسُ: فَأَوْفِ بِذِرْوَةِ هَذَا الْجَبَلِ، فتَرَدَّ مِنْهُ، فَانْظُرْ أَتَعِيشُ أَمْ لَا؟ - قَالَ ابْنُ طَاوُسٍ: عَنْ أَبِيهِ - فَقَالَ: أَمَا عَلِمْتَ أَنَّ اللَّهَ قَالَ: لَا يُجَرِّبُنِي عَبْدِي، فَإِنِّي أَفْعَلُ مَا شِئْتُ، قَالَ: - وَقَالَ الزُّهْرِيُّ: قَالَ: - إِنَّ الْعَبْدَ لَا يَبْتَلِي رَبَّهُ، وَلَكُنَّ اللَّهَ يَبْتَلِي عَبْدَهُ، قَالَ: فَخَصَمَهُ»

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported: Ma'mar reported from al-Zuhri, who said: “

Matn

It reached me that three tablets, each bearing an inscription, were found at the Station of Abraham. On the first was written: ‘I am God, Lord of Bakka. I fashioned it on the day I fashioned the sun, surrounded it with seven angels, and blessed its people with meat and milk.’ On the second: ‘I am God, Lord of Bakka. I created the ties of kinship, deriving a name for them from My name. Whoever maintains them, I maintain connection with him; whoever severs them, I sever him.’ On the third: ‘I am God, Lord of Bakka. I created good and evil. Blessed is one through whose hands good comes; woe to one through whose hands evil comes.’”

'Abd al-Razzaq reported: Ma'mar reported from Mansur, from Sa'd b. 'Ubayda, from Abu 'Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, from 'Ali b. Abi Talib, who said:

Matn

We went out for a funeral. As we were in al-Baqi', the Messenger of God ﷺ came to us holding a small staff. He sat down and scratched the earth with it for a time, then said: “There is no living soul whose place in Paradise or the Fire has not been written, and who has not been written as wretched or blessed.” A man asked: “Shall we not rely on what has been written for us, Messenger of God, and give up deeds?” He said: “No; rather, act. Everyone is made ready [for his course]. Those destined for wretchedness are made ready for the deeds of the wretched, and those destined for blessedness for the deeds of the blessed.” He then recited: “As for him who gives and is mindful [of God], and affirms the best, We shall ease him toward ease; but whoever is miserly, deems himself self-sufficient, and denies the best, We shall ease him toward hardship” (92:5–10). [The source's printed verse marker cites 92:6 although the quotation extends beyond that verse.]

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported: Ma'mar reported from Abu Ishaq, from Abu al-Ahwas, from Ibn Mas'ud, who said: “

Matn

There are only two things: guidance and speech. The best speech is God's speech, and the best guidance is the guidance of Muhammad ﷺ. Beware of forbidden matters and innovations, for the worst matters are those newly introduced, and every innovation is error. Do not let time stretch on for you so that your hearts grow hard. Everything that is coming is near, and the truly distant thing is that which will never come. The wretched one is the one made wretched in his mother's womb, and the blessed one takes warning from what happens to others. The worst accounts are false accounts. Lying is not permissible either in earnest or in jest, nor for a man to promise his child something and fail to deliver it. Lying leads to wickedness, and wickedness leads to the Fire. Truthfulness leads to righteousness, and righteousness leads to Paradise. The truthful person is said to have spoken truth and acted rightly, and the liar to have lied and acted wickedly. I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: ‘A servant continues to lie until he is written down as a liar, and continues to speak truth until he is written down as a most truthful person.’” He then said: “Beware of 'ida. Do you know what 'ida is? Tale-bearing and passing stories between people.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ أَبِي الْأَحْوَصِ، عَنِ ابْنِ مَسْعُودٍ، قَالَ:

Matn

إِنَّمَا هُمَا اثْنَتَانِ: الْهَدْي وَالْكَلَامُ، فَأَحْسَنُ الْكَلَامِ كَلَامُ اللَّهِ، وَأَحْسَنُ الْهَدْيِ هَدْي مُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، أَلَا إِيَّاكُمْ وَالْمُحَرَّمَاتِ وَالْبِدَعَ، فَإِنَّ شَرَّ الْأُمُورِ مُحْدَثَاتُهَا، وَكُلَّ مُحْدَثَةٍ ضَلَالَةٌ، أَلَا لَا يَطُولُ عَلَيْكُمُ الْأَمَدُ فَتَقْسُوَ قُلُوبُكُمْ، أَلَا كُلُّ مَا هُوَ آتٍ قَرِيبٌ، أَلَا إِنَّ الْبَعِيدَ مَا لَيْسَ بِآتٍ، أَلَا إِنَّ الشَّقِيَّ مَنْ شَقِيَ فِي بَطْنِ أُمِّهِ، وَإِنَّ السَّعِيدَ مَنْ وُعِظَ بِغَيْرِهِ، أَلَا وَإِنَّ شَرَّ الرَّوَايَا رَوَايَا الْكَذِبِ، أَلَا وَإِنَّ الْكَذِبَ لَا يَصْلُحُ فِي جِدٍّ وَلَا هَزْلٍ، وَلَا أَنْ يَعِدَ الرَّجُلُ صَبِيَّهُ ثُمَّ لَا يُنْجِزُ لَهُ، أَلَا وَإِنَّ الْكَذِبَ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْفُجُورِ، وَإِنَّ الْفُجُورَ يَهْدِي إِلَى النَّارِ، وَإِنَّ الصِّدْقَ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْبِرِّ، وَإِنَّ الْبِرَّ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ، وَإِنَّهُ يُقَالُ لِلصَّادِقِ: صَدَقَ وَبَرَّ، وَيُقَالُ لِلْكَاذِبِ: كَذَبَ وَفَجَرَ، وَإِنِّي سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ يَقُولُ: «إِنَّ §الْعَبْدَ لَيَكْذِبُ حَتَّى يُكْتَبَ كَذَّابًا، وَيَصْدُقُ حَتَّى يُكْتَبَ صِدِّيقًا» ثُمَّ قَالَ: « إِيَّاكُمْ وَالْعِضَةَ، أَتَدْرُونَ مَا الْعِضَةُ؟ النَّمِيمَةُ، وَنَقْلُ الْأَحَادِيثِ»

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib, from 'Abdullah b. Hafs, from Ya'la b. Murra, who said:

Matn

A number of us, companions of 'Ali, gathered, and I said: “We should guard the Commander of the Believers, since he is at war and we cannot be sure someone will not assassinate him.” We guarded the door of his chamber until he came out for dawn prayer. He asked: “What is the matter with you?” We replied: “We have been guarding you, Commander of the Believers, because you are at war and we feared you might be assassinated.” He asked: “Are you guarding me against the inhabitants of heaven or the inhabitants of earth?” We said: “Against the inhabitants of earth. How could we guard you from the inhabitants of heaven?” He answered: “Nothing occurs on earth until it has been decreed in heaven. Every person has two angels entrusted to repel harm from him and protect him until his appointed decree arrives. When it comes, they leave him to what has been decreed for him.”
