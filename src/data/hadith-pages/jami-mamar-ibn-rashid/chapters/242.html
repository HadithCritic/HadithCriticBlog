Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 374
English
Isnad

The transmitters recited to 'Abd al-Razzaq from Ma'mar, from Qatada, from al-Hasan, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “

Matn

The Hour will not come until mountains move from their places and you witness the tremendous event that you never used to see.”

العربية
Isnad

قَرَأْنَا عَلَى عَبْدِ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنِ الْحَسَنِ، قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «§

Matn

لَا تَقُومُ السَّاعَةُ حَتَّى تَزُولَ الْجِبَالُ مِنْ أَمَاكِنِهَا، وَحَتَّى تَرَوُا الْأَمْرَ الْعَظِيمَ الَّذِي لَمْ تَكُونُوا تَرَوْنَهُ»

Entry20790/ 32
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 376
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq transmitted from Ma'mar, from Qatada, from Shahr b. Hawshab: “

Matn

When allegiance to Yazid b. Mu'awiya reached us, I decided to depart for Syria to avoid the trouble of that pledge. I went and heard of a gathering where Nawf spoke. I arrived and saw a man with damaged eyes in a striped cloak: 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr b. al-'As. When Nawf saw him he stopped speaking. 'Abd Allah asked him to continue, but Nawf said that as a Companion of the Messenger ﷺ he had a greater right to speak. 'Abd Allah said: ‘These rulers have prevented us from transmitting reports.’ Nawf insisted that he relate something he heard directly. 'Abd Allah said that he heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: ‘There will be another emigration after the first, when the best people migrate to the place Abraham migrated. Only the worst inhabitants of the earth will remain, cast out by their lands and abhorred by God. Fire will gather them along with apes and pigs; it will stay with them overnight where they stay, rest with them where they rest, and devour whoever falls behind.’”

العربية

Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar, from Matar and others, from al-Hasan, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "

Matn

Your hands will be filled from the non-Arabs. Then they will become lions who do not flee, then they will strike your necks and eat your spoils."
