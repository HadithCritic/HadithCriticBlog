Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 319
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Ayyub, from Nafi', from Ibn 'Umar, who said that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “

Matn

All of you are shepherds, and each is responsible for those under his care. The ruler of the people is a shepherd responsible for his subjects. A man is a shepherd over his household and responsible for them. A woman is a shepherd over her husband's property. A servant is a shepherd over his master's property and responsible for it. Indeed, all of you are shepherds and responsible.”

كُلُّكُمْ رَاعٍ وَمَسْئُولٌ عَنْ رَعِيَّتِهِ، فَالْإِمَامُ الَّذِي عَلَى النَّاسِ رَاعٍ وَمَسْئُولٌ عَنْ رَعِيَّتِهِ، وَالرَّجُلُ رَاعٍ عَلَى أَهْلِ بَيْتِهِ وَمَسْئُولٌ عَنْهُمْ، وَالْمَرْأَةُ رَاعِيَةٌ عَلَى مَالِ زَوْجِهَا، وَالْعَبْدُ رَاعٍ عَلَى مَالِ سَيِّدِهِ وَمَسْئُولٌ عَنْهُ، أَلَا فَكُلُّكُمْ رَاعٍ وَمَسْئُولٌ»

Entry20651/ 24
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 319
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq transmitted from Ma'mar, from more than one person, from al-Hasan: 'Ubayd God b. Ziyad visited Ma'qil b. Yasar during his illness. Ma'qil said to him: “I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: ‘

Matn

Whoever is entrusted by God with a people and does not look after them with sincere counsel, but dies while deceiving them, God will admit him to the Fire.’” 'Ubayd God asked why he had not told him earlier. Ma'qil replied: “No; if I knew I would recover from this illness, I would not have told you.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ غَيْرِ وَاحِدٍ، عَنِ الْحَسَنِ، قَالَ: دَخَلَ عُبَيْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ زِيَادٍ، عَلَى مَعْقِلِ بْنِ يَسَارٍ وَهُوَ مَرِيضٌ، فَقَالَ لَهُ مَعْقِلٌ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ يَقُولُ: «§

Matn

مَنِ اسْتَرْعَاهُ اللَّهُ رَعِيَّةً فَلَمْ يُحِطْ مِنْ وَرَائِهَا بِالنَّصِيحَةِ، وَمَاتَ وَهُوَ لَهَا غَاشٌّ، أَدْخَلَهُ اللَّهُ النَّارَ» قَالَ: فَقَالَ لَهُ عُبَيْدُ اللَّهِ: فَهَلَّا قَبْلَ الْيَوْمِ، قَالَ: لَا، وَلَوْ كُنْتُ أَعْلَمُ أَنِّي أَقُومُ مِنْ مَرَضِي هَذَا مَا حَدَّثْتُكَ بِهِ

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq transmitted from Ma'mar, from Zayd b. Rufay', from Haram b. Mu'awiya, who said that the Prophet ﷺ said: “

Matn

Whoever assumes some authority and opens his door to those in need, poverty, and destitution, God will open the gates of heaven to his own need, poverty, and destitution. Whoever closes his door against those in need, poverty, and destitution, God will close the gates of heaven against his need, poverty, and destitution.”

Entry20656/ 24
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 321
English
Isnad

Ma'mar transmitted from Matar al-Warraq, from 'Amr b. Sa'id, from an unnamed member of the Tayyi' tribe, from Rafi' al-Khayr al-Ta'i, who said: “

Matn

I accompanied Abu Bakr on an expedition. When we returned and the people were about to disperse, I said: ‘Abu Bakr, a man could accompany you for so long and then part from you without having gained some good from you. Give me advice, but do not make it so long that I forget.’ He said: ‘May God have mercy on you, may God have mercy on you; may God bless you, may God bless you. Perform the prescribed prayers at their times, pay the alms on your property willingly, fast Ramadan, and make pilgrimage to the House. Know that emigration in Islam is good and that fighting in God's cause while in emigration is good. And never become a governor.’ I said: ‘I know that what you said about prayer, fasting, alms, pilgrimage, emigration, and struggle is good; but why say I should not be a governor? By God, it seems to me that the best of you today are your governors.’ He said: ‘You told me not to make the advice long, but this is where I must speak at length. This authority, which today seems easy, will soon spread and become corrupted, until people unfit for it obtain it. Whoever becomes a governor will face among the longest reckonings and the harshest punishments, whereas the one who does not will face among the easiest reckonings and lightest punishments. Governors are the people most likely to wrong the believers, thereby violating God's protection. The believers are God's neighbors and those who visit Him. By God, if one of you lost his neighbor's sheep—or his neighbor's camel—he would spend the night with his muscles swollen in distress, saying: “My neighbor's sheep! My neighbor's camel!” God has a greater right to be angered on behalf of His neighbors.’”

العربية
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq transmitted from Ma'mar, from Ayyub, from Ibn Sirin: 'Umar b. al-Khattab

Matn

appointed Abu Hurayra governor of Bahrain. When he returned with ten thousand, 'Umar said: “Have you appropriated these funds for yourself, enemy of God and His Book?” Abu Hurayra answered: “I am not an enemy of God or His Book, but an enemy of whoever is hostile to them.” 'Umar asked where the money had come from. He replied: “Horses of mine that bred, revenue from slaves of mine, and stipends that accumulated.” They investigated and found his account accurate. Later 'Umar invited him to accept another appointment, but he refused. 'Umar said: “Do you dislike public office when someone better than you—Joseph—asked for it?” He replied: “Joseph was a prophet, the son of a prophet, son of a prophet. I am Abu Hurayra, son of Umayma. I fear three things and two things.” 'Umar asked why he did not simply say five. He answered: “No. I fear speaking without knowledge, judging without justice, having my back beaten, my property seized, and my honor insulted.”

مَثَلُ الْإِمَامِ كَمَثَلِ عَيْنٍ عَظِيمَةٍ صَافِيَةٍ طَيِّبَةِ الْمَاءِ، يَجْرِي مِنْهَا إِلَى نَهْرٍ عَظِيمٍ، فَيَخُوضُ النَّاسُ النَّهْرَ، فَيُكَدِّرُونَهُ وَيَعُودُ عَلَيْهِ صَفْوُ الْعَيْنِ، قَالَ: فَإِذَا كَانَ الْكَدَرُ مِنْ قِبَلِ الْعَيْنِ فَسَدَ النَّهْرُ، قَالَ: وَمَثَلُ الْإِمَامِ وَالنَّاسِ كَمَثَلِ فُسْطَاطٍ، لَا يَسْتَقِلُّ إِلَّا بِعَمُودٍ، وَلَا يَقُومُ الْعَمُودُ إِلَّا بِأَطْنَابٍ - أَوْ قَالَ: أَوْتَادٍ، فَكُلَّمَا نُزِعَ وَتَدٌ ازْدَادَ الْعَمُودُ وَهْنًا، وَلَا يَصْلُحُ النَّاسُ إِلَّا بِالْإِمَامِ، وَلَا يَصْلُحُ الْإِمَامُ إِلَّا بِالنَّاسِ»
