Entry20962/ 13
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 444
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq told us: Ma'mar told us, from al-Zuhri, from Kathir b. Aflah, the freedman of Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, from his father, who said: Ibn Salam used to go in to the chiefs of Quraysh before the people of Egypt came, and say to them: "

Matn

Do not kill this man," meaning 'Uthman. They would say: "By God, we do not want to kill him." Aflah said: He went out leaning on my hands and said: "By God, you will kill him." He said: And Ibn Salam said to them when ['Uthman] was besieged: "Leave this man alone for forty nights; by God, if you leave him alone, he will die within that time." They refused. Then he went out to them some days later and said: "Leave him for fifteen; by God, if you leave him alone he will die within that time."

Isnad

Matn

لَا تَقْتُلُوا هَذَا الرَّجُلَ» - يَعْنِي عُثْمَانَ - فَيَقُولُونَ وَاللَّهِ مَا نُرِيدُ قَتْلَهُ، قَالَ أَفْلَحُ: فَخَرَجَ وَهُوَ مُتَّكِئٌ عَلَى يَدَيَّ فَيَقُولُ: «وَاللَّهِ لَتَقْتُلُنَّهُ» ، قَالَ: وَقَالَ لَهُمُ ابْنُ سَلَامٍ حِينَ حُصِرَ: «اتْرُكُوا هَذَا الرَّجُلَ أَرْبَعِينَ لَيْلَةً، فَوَاللَّهِ لَئِنْ تَرَكْتُمُوهُ لَيَمُوتَنَّ إِلَيْهَا» ، فَأَبَوْا، ثُمَّ خَرَجَ إِلَيْهِمْ بَعْدَ ذَلِكَ بِأَيَّامٍ، فَقَالَ: «اتْرُكُوهُ خَمْسَ عَشْرَةَ، فَوَاللَّهِ لَئِنْ تَرَكْتُمُوهُ لَيَمُوتَنَّ إِلَيْهَا»

Entry20963/ 13
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 445
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar, from Ayyub, from Humayd b. Hilal, who said: Ibn Salam said to them: "

Matn

The angels have not ceased to surround this city of yours since the Messenger of God ﷺ came to it until today. By God, if you kill him they will leave and never return. By God, no man among you will kill him but he will meet God leprous, with no hand. The sword of God has remained sheathed against you, and by God, if you kill him God will draw it and will not sheathe it against you (either he said 'ever' or he said 'until the Day of Resurrection'). No prophet was ever killed but seventy thousand were killed on his account, and no caliph but thirty-five thousand were killed on his account before they became united." And he mentioned "that seventy thousand were killed for the blood of Yahya b. Zakariyya."

Entry20964/ 13
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 445
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar, from one who heard Ibn Sirin say: 'Uthman

Matn

sent Salit b. Salit and 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Attab b. Usayd and said: "Go to Ibn Salam and disguise yourselves from him as if you were strangers, and say to him: 'What has happened among the people is as you see; what do you command us?'" They came to Ibn Salam and said something like what he had told them to say. He said to one of them: "You are so-and-so, son of so-and-so," and to the other: "You are so-and-so, son of so-and-so. The Commander of the Faithful sent you. Convey greetings of peace to him and tell him that he will be killed, so let him hold back, for that is stronger for his case on the Day of Resurrection before God." They came back and told him. 'Uthman said: "I bind you by oath that none of you fight alongside me." Marwan said: "And I bind myself by oath that I will fight." So he fought and was struck on his neck, and he remained with his chin on his chest until he died.

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 446
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq told us: Ma'mar told us, from Qatada, who said: Ibn Salam said: "

Matn

If the killing of 'Uthman was right guidance, you will milk milk; and if the killing of 'Uthman was error, you will milk blood." And Hudhayfa said: "Hearts have flown from their places; the mother of every brave hero of the Arabs is bereaved of him today. By God, after this nothing will come to you but what is smaller and truncated, and the later is worse." [The last sentence is somewhat garbled.]

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 446
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar, from Aban, who said: Sallam told me… from 'Abdullah b. Rabah, who said:

Matn

Abu Qatada and I went in to 'Uthman while he was besieged, and asked his permission to perform the Hajj, and he gave us permission. We said: "Commander of the Faithful, the matter of these people is as you see; what do you command us?" He said: "Keep to the community." We said: "We fear that the community may be with those who oppose you." He said: "Keep to the community wherever it is." Then we left him, and I met al-Hasan b. 'Ali going in to him, so we returned with him to hear what he would say. He said: "Here I am, Commander of the Faithful; command me as you wish." He said: "Sit, my nephew, until God brings about His command, for I have no need of the world (or he said: of fighting)."

Entry20967/ 13
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 447
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from 'Urwa, who said:

Matn

I went in to 'A'isha together with 'Ubaydullah b. 'Adi b. al-Khiyar, and I mentioned 'Uthman, and she said: "Would that I had been something forgotten and discarded! By God, I did not violate anything of 'Uthman's but the like of it was violated against me, so that had I wished him killed, I would have been killed." Then she said: "'Ubaydullah b. 'Adi, let no one deceive you after the group whom you know. By God, the deeds of the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ were not belittled until the reciters appeared who attacked 'Uthman. They recited a recitation like none other, prayed a prayer like none other, fasted a fast like none other, and spoke words the like of which we cannot speak. But when I considered their conduct, they were nowhere near the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ. So when you hear a man's fine speech, say: 'Act, for God will see your deeds, and so will His Messenger and the believers' [9:105], and let no one make light of you."

Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq told us: Ma'mar told us, from 'Ali b. Zayd, from al-Hasan, from Qays b. 'Abbad, who said:

Matn

We were with 'Ali, and whenever he witnessed a scene, or climbed a hillock, or descended into a valley, he would say: "God and His Messenger spoke the truth." I said to a man of Banu Yashkur: "Come with us to the Commander of the Faithful so that we may ask him about his saying: 'God and His Messenger spoke the truth.'" We went to him and said: "Commander of the Faithful, we have seen that when you witness a scene, or descend into a valley, or climb a hillock, you say: 'God and His Messenger spoke the truth.' Did the Messenger of God entrust anything to you concerning that?" He turned away from us, and we pressed him. When he saw that, he said: "By God, the Messenger of God did not entrust me with any covenant except something he entrusted to the people. But the people fell upon 'Uthman and killed him, and others than I were worse in state and deeds in it than I. Then I saw that I was the most entitled of them to this matter, so I seized it. And God knows best whether we were right or wrong."

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 450
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar, from Ibn Tawus, from his father, who said: "

Matn

When the trial of 'Uthman occurred, a man said to his family: 'Bind me in iron, for I am mad.' When 'Uthman was killed he said: 'Release me. Praise be to God who cured me of madness and spared me from the killing of 'Uthman.'"

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ طَاوُسٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، قَالَ: «§

Matn

لَمَّا وَقَعَتْ فِتْنَةُ عُثْمَانَ قَالَ رَجُلٌ لِأَهْلِهِ: أَوْثِقُونِي بِالْحَدِيدِ، فَإِنِّي مَجْنُونٌ، فَلَمَّا قُتِلَ عُثْمَانُ قَالَ: خَلُّوا عَنِّي فَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي شَفَانِي مِنَ الْجُنُونِ وَعَافَانِي مِنْ قَتْلِ عُثْمَانَ»

Entry20974/ 13
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 450
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar, from al-A'mash, who said:

Matn

'Uthman said to Hudhayfa when he met him: "By God, what reaches me about you behind my back does not leave me [in peace]." Then Hudhayfa turned away, and when he had passed on, ['Uthman] said: "Bring him back." 'Uthman said to him again the like of his first words. Hudhayfa said to him: "By God, you will come out as the bull comes out, and you will rage as the camel rages."

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الْأَعْمَشِ، قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ عُثْمَانُ لِحُذَيْفَةَ وَلَقِيَهُ: «وَاللَّهِ مَا يَدَعُنِي مَا يَبْلُغُنِي عَنْكَ بِظَهْرِ الْغَيْبِ» ، ثُمَّ وَلَّى حُذَيْفَةُ، فَلَمَّا أَجَازَ قَالَ: رُدُّوهُ، قَالَ لَهُ عُثْمَانُ أَيْضًا مِثْلَ قَوْلِهِ الْأَوَّلِ، فَقَالَ لَهُ حُذَيْفَةُ: «§وَاللَّهِ لَتَخْرُجَنَّ كَمَا يَخْرُجُ الثَّوْرُ، وَلَتَسْخَطَنَّ كَمَا يَسْخَطُ الْجَمَلُ»
