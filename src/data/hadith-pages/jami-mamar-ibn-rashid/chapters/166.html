Entry20300/ 6
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 192
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from Abu Qilaba, from more than one person: Sa'd al-Dahhak was passed by companions of the Prophet ﷺ and asked them: “Advise me.” They gave him advice. Mu'adh b. Jabal was the last to pass, so he said: “Advise me, may God have mercy on you.” Mu'adh replied: “

Matn

They have advised you thoroughly and have not withheld counsel. I will sum up your affair in a few words: Know that you cannot dispense with your share of worldly life, so arrange it carefully for yourself; it will then accompany you wherever you go.” [The final phrase follows the Arabic source closely, though its meaning is somewhat obscure.]

Isnad

Matn

Entry20301/ 6
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 192
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from al-Hasan, who said:

Matn

God says: “My servant draws near to Me through nothing like what I have made obligatory upon him. My servant continues drawing near to Me with voluntary deeds until I love him. Then I become his eyes with which he sees, his ears with which he hears, his hands with which he grasps, and his feet with which he walks. When he calls upon Me, I answer; when he asks Me, I give; and when he seeks My forgiveness, I forgive him.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الْحَسَنِ، قَالَ: «

Matn

يَقُولُ اللَّهُ: §مَا تَقَرَّبَ إِلَيَّ عَبْدِي بِمِثْلِ مَا افْتَرَضْتُ عَلَيْهِ، وَمَا يَزَالُ عَبْدِي يَتَقَرَّبُ إِلَيَّ بِالنَّوَافِلِ حَتَّى أُحِبَّهُ، فَأَكُونَ عَيْنَيْهِ اللَّتَيْنِ يُبْصِرُ بِهِمَا، وَأُذُنَيْهِ اللَّتَيْنِ يَسْمَعُ بِهِمَا، وَيَدَيْهِ اللَّتَيْنِ يَبْطِشُ بِهِمَا، وَرِجْلَيْهِ اللَّتَيْنِ يَمْشِي بِهِمَا، فَإِذَا دَعَانِي أَجَبْتُهُ، وَإِذَا سَأَلَنِي أَعْطَيْتُهُ، وَإِنِ اسْتَغْفَرَنِي غَفَرْتُ لَهُ»

Entry20302/ 6
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 193
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, who said:

Matn

A man passed by a group, and one of them said: “I hate that man for God's sake.” The others said: “By God, you should not leave that unsubstantiated. Go, so-and-so, and tell him.” The messenger told the man what had been said. The man went to the Prophet ﷺ and said: “So-and-so claims to hate me for God's sake.” The Messenger of God ﷺ sent for the other man and asked: “Why do you hate him?” He replied: “He is my neighbor; I know him better than anyone. By God, I have never seen him perform any prayer except these obligatory prayers that the righteous and the wicked both perform.” The other man said: “Ask him, Messenger of God: Has he ever seen me delay them past their appointed times or perform their ablutions, bowing, or prostrations badly?” He replied: “No.” The accuser continued: “I have never seen him fast a day except during this month, which both the righteous and the wicked fast.” The man said: “Ask him: Has he seen me break any day of it, or treat its rights lightly?” He said: “No.” The accuser continued: “I have never seen him give charity except the obligatory alms that both the righteous and the wicked pay.” The man said: “Ask him: Have I concealed them, delayed them—or,” he said, “withheld them?” He replied: “No.” The Messenger of God ﷺ then said: “Leave him alone; perhaps he is better than you.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ:

Matn

مَرَّ رَجُلٌ بِقَوْمٍ، فَقَالَ رَجُلٌ مِنْهُمْ: إِنِّي لَأَبْغَضُ هَذَا لِلَّهِ، فَقَالَ الْقَوْمُ: وَاللَّهِ لَا بِرَّ لَهَا، اذْهَبْ يَا فُلَانُ فَبَلِّغْهُ، قَالَ: فَقَالَ لَهُ الَّذِي قَالَ، فَذَهَبَ الرَّجُلُ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فَقَالَ: إِنَّ فُلَانًا يَزْعُمُ أَنَّهُ يَبْغَضُنِي فِي اللَّهِ، فَأَرْسَلَ إِلَيْهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فَقَالَ: «§عَلَامَ تَبْغَضُ هَذَا؟» ، قَالَ: هُوَ لِي جَارٌ وَأَنَا أَعْلَمُ شَيْءٍ بِهِ، وَأَخْبَرُ شَيْءٍ بِهِ، وَاللَّهِ مَا رَأَيْتُهُ صَلَّى صَلَاةً قَطُّ إِلَّا هَذِهِ الصَّلَاةَ الْمَكْتُوبَةَ الَّتِي يُصَلِّيهَا الْبَرُّ وَالْفَاجِرُ، قَالَ: سَلْهُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، هَلْ رَآنِي أَخَّرْتُهَا عَنْ وَقْتِهَا، أَوْ أَسَأْتُ فِي وُضُوئِهَا، أَوْ رُكُوعِهَا أَوْ سُجودِهَا؟ قَالَ: لَا، قَالَ: وَلَا رَأَيْتُهُ صَامَ يَوْمًا قَطُّ إِلَّا هَذَا الشَّهْرَ الَّذِي يَصُومُهُ الْبَرُّ وَالْفَاجِرُ، قَالَ: سَلْهُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، هَلْ رَآنِي أَفْطَرْتُ مِنْهُ يَوْمًا، أَوِ استَخْفَفْتُ بِحَقِّهِ؟ قَالَ: لَا، قَالَ: وَلَا رَأَيْتُهُ تَصَدَّقَ بِشَيْءٍ قَطُّ إِلَّا هَذِهِ الزَّكَاةَ الَّتِي يُؤَدِّيهَا الْبَرُّ وَالْفَاجِرُ، قَالَ: سَلْهُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، هَلْ كَتَمْتُهَا، أَوْ أَخَّرْتُهَا - أَوْ قَالَ: مَنَعْتُهَا -؟ قَالَ: لَا، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «دَعْهُ فَلَعَلَّهُ أَنْ يَكُونَ خَيْرًا مِنْكَ»

Entry20303/ 6
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 194
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us: Ma'mar reported from 'Asim b. Abi al-Najud, from Abu Wa'il, from Mu'adh b. Jabal, who said:

Matn

I was traveling with the Messenger of God ﷺ and came close to him as we rode one morning. I asked: “Messenger of God, tell me a deed that will admit me to Paradise and keep me far from the Fire.” He said: “You have asked about something great, yet it is easy for one to whom God makes it easy: worship God without associating anything with Him, establish prayer, give alms, fast Ramadan, and make pilgrimage to the House.” Then he said: “Shall I point you to the gates of goodness? Fasting is a shield, charity extinguishes sin, and a person's prayer in the depth of the night.” He recited the passage from “Their sides forsake their beds” [32:16] through “as reward for what they used to do” [32:17]. Then he asked: “Shall I tell you the head of the matter, its pillar, and the peak of its summit?” I said: “Yes, Messenger of God.” He replied: “The head of the matter is Islam, its pillar is prayer, and the peak of its summit is striving [in God's cause].” Then he asked: “Shall I tell you what controls all of that?” I replied: “Yes, Prophet of God.” He took hold of his tongue and said: “Restrain this.” I asked: “Messenger of God, are we held accountable for what we say?” He answered: “May your mother be bereaved of you, Mu'adh! What else throws people face-down—or he said, on their noses—into the Fire except what their tongues reap?”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنْ عَاصِمِ بْنِ أَبِي النَّجُودِ، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، عَنْ مُعَاذِ بْنِ جَبَلٍ، قَالَ:

Matn

كُنْتُ مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فِي سَفَرٍ، فَأَصْبَحْتُ قَرِيبًا مِنْهُ وَنَحْنُ نَسِيرُ، فَقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَلَا تُخْبِرُنِي بِعَمَلٍ يُدْخِلُنِي الْجَنَّةَ، وَيُبَاعِدُنِي مِنَ النَّارِ، قَالَ: «لَقَدْ سَأَلْتَ عَنْ عَظِيمٍ، وَإِنَّهُ لَيَسِيرٌ عَلَى مَنْ يَسَّرَهُ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ، §تَعْبُدُ اللَّهَ لَا تُشْرِكُ بِهِ شَيْئًا، وَتُقِيمُ الصَّلَاةَ، وَتُؤْتِي الزَّكَاةَ، وَتَصُومُ شَهْرَ رَمَضَانَ، وَتَحُجُّ الْبَيْتَ» ثُمَّ قَالَ: «أَدُلُّكَ عَلَى أَبْوَابِ الْخَيْرِ: الصَّوْمُ جُنَّةٌ، وَالصَّدَقَةُ تُطْفِئُ الْخَطِيئَةَ، وَصَلَاةُ الرَّجُلِ مِنْ جَوْفِ اللَّيْلِ، ثُمَّ قَرَأَ: {تَتَجَافَى جُنُوبُهُمْ عَنِ الْمَضَاجِعِ} [السجدة: ١٦] - حَتَّى - {جَزَاءً بِمَا كَانُوا يَعْمَلُونَ} [السجدة: ١٧] ثُمَّ قَالَ: أَلَا أُخْبِرُكَ بِرَأْسِ الْأَمْرِ وَعَمُودِهِ وَذِرْوَةِ سَنَامِهِ؟» ، فَقُلْتُ: بَلَى يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، قَالَ: رَأْسُ الْأَمْرِ الْإِسْلَامُ، وَعَمُودُهُ الصَّلَاةُ، وَذِرْوَةُ سَنَامِهِ الْجِهَادُ «ثُمَّ قَالَ:» أَلَا أُخْبِرُكَ بِمِلَاكِ ذَلِكَ كُلِّهِ؟ «، قَالَ: قُلْتُ: بَلَى يَا نَبِيَّ اللَّهِ، فَأَخَذَ بِلِسَانِهِ قَالَ:» اكْفُفْ عَلَيْكَ هَذَا «، فَقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَوَإِنَّا لَمَأْخُوذُونَ بِمَا نَتَكَلَّمُ؟ قَالَ:» ثَكِلَتْكَ أُمُّكَ يَا مُعَاذُ، وَهَلْ يُكِبُّ النَّاسَ فِي النَّارِ عَلَى وُجُوهِهِمْ - أَوْ قَالَ: عَلَى مَنَاخِرِهِمْ - إِلَّا حَصَائِدُ أَلْسِنَتِهِمْ "

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us: Ma'mar reported from al-Zuhri, from 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz, from his father, that the Messenger of God ﷺ was asked: “

Matn

Which deeds are best?” He replied: “The tolerant, upright monotheistic way.”

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 195
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us: Ma'mar reported from Layth, who raised the report [to the Prophet ﷺ], saying: God said: “

Matn

Son of Adam, devote yourself to worshiping Me and I shall fill your heart with sufficiency and close off your poverty. If you do not, I shall fill your heart with preoccupation and not close off your poverty. Son of Adam, for as long as you call upon Me and hope in Me, I shall forgive you whatever has happened. It is incumbent upon Me not to mislead My servant while he asks Me for guidance. I am the Judge.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنْ لَيْثٍ يَرْفَعُ الْحَدِيثَ قَالَ: «إِنَّ اللَّهَ قَالَ: §

Matn

يَا ابْنَ آدَمَ تَفَرَّغْ لِعِبَادَتِي أَمْلَأْ قَلْبَكَ غِنًى، وَأَسْدُدْ عَلَيْكَ فَقْرَكَ، فَإِنْ لَمْ تَفْعَلْ مَلَأْتُ قَلْبَكَ شُغْلًا وَلَمْ أَسْدُدْ عَلَيْكَ فَقْرَكَ، يَا ابْنَ آدَمَ إِنَّكَ مَا دَعَوْتَنِي وَرَجَوْتَنِي، فَإِنِّي أَغْفِرُ لَكَ عَلَى مَا كَانَ، وَحَقٌّ عَلَيَّ أَلَّا أُضِلَّ عَبْدِي وَهُوَ يَسْأَلُنِي الْهُدَى، وَأَنَا الْحَكَمُ»
