Entry20306/ 11
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 195
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, who said: Fatima al-Khuza'iyya—who had met most of the companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ—told me that the Messenger of God ﷺ visited an Ansari woman who was ill. He asked: “How do you feel?” She replied: “Well, Messenger of God, except that Umm Mildam”—meaning fever—“has severely afflicted me.” He said: “

Matn

Be patient, for it removes the impurities of a human being just as a furnace removes the impurities of iron.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَتْنِي فَاطِمَةُ الْخُزَاعِيَّةُ - وَكَانَتْ قَدْ أَدْرَكَتْ عَامَّةَ أَصْحَابِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ عَادَ امْرَأَةً مِنَ الْأَنْصَارِ وَهِيَ وَجِعَةٌ، فَقَالَ لَهَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «كَيْفَ تَجِدِينَكِ؟» ، فَقَالَتْ: بِخَيْرٍ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، وَقَدْ بَرَّحَتْ بِي أُمُّ مِلْدَمٍ - تُرِيدُ الْحُمَّى - فَقَالَ لَهَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «§

Matn

اصْبِرِي فَإِنَّهَا تُذْهِبُ مِنْ خَبَثِ الْإِنْسَانِ كَمَا يُذْهِبُ الْكِيرُ مِنْ خَبَثِ الْحَدِيدِ»

English
Isnad

Ma'mar informed us, from al-Zuhri, from Ibn al-Musayyib, from Abu Hurayra, who said the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “

Matn

The believer resembles growing crops, constantly bent by the wind, and the believer continues to be afflicted with trials. The hypocrite resembles a cedar tree, standing erect until it is harvested [cut down].”

Isnad

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 196
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us: Ma'mar reported from 'Asim b. Abi al-Najud, from Khaythama, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr, who said the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “

Matn

When a servant follows a good practice of worship and then becomes ill, the angel assigned to him is told: ‘Write for him the same deeds as when he was free to act, until I release him [from illness] or take him to Myself.’”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

Entry20309/ 11
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 197
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from Zayd b. Aslam, who said:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ visited a sick man and said: “Be patient, for it”—meaning fever—“is a purification.” The man replied: “No; it is a raging fever upon an old man that sends him to the graves.” The Prophet ﷺ said: “Yes, then it is so.” The man died.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ، قَالَ:

Matn

دَخَلَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ عَلَى رَجُلٍ يَعُودُهُ، فَقَالَ: «§اصْبِرْ فَإِنَّهَا طَهُورٌ» - يَعْنِي الْحُمَّى -، قَالَ: كَلَّا، بَلْ حُمَّى تَفُورُ، عَلَى شَيْخٍ كَبِيرٍ، تُزِيرُهُ الْقُبُورَ، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «نَعَمْ، فَهُوَ كَذَلِكَ» فَمَاتَ الرَّجُلُ

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us: Ma'mar reported from Abu Ishaq, from al-'Ayzar b. Hurayth, from 'Umar b. Sa'd, from his father, who said the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “

Matn

How remarkable is the believer! When good befalls him he praises and thanks God; when a calamity befalls him he praises God and bears it patiently. The believer is rewarded in every part of his life, even for the morsel he raises to his wife's mouth.”

Isnad

Matn

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 197
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from 'Urwa, from 'A'isha, who said the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “

Matn

No illness or pain afflicts a believer without being an expiation for his sins, even a thorn that pricks him or a mishap that befalls him.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، قَالَتْ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «§

Matn

مَا مِنْ مَرَضٍ أَوْ وَجَعٍ يُصِيبُ الْمُؤْمِنَ إِلَّا كَانَ كَفَّارَةً لِذُنُوبِهِ، حَتَّى الشَّوْكَةِ يُشَاكُهَا أَوِ النَّكْبَةِ يُنْكَبُهَا»

Entry20313/ 11
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 197
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us: Ma'mar reported from Ayyub, from Ibn Sirin, from al-Rabab al-Qushayri, who said:

Matn

We entered to visit Abu al-Darda' during an illness. A Bedouin entered and asked: “What is wrong with your governor?”—for Abu al-Darda' was governor at the time. We said: “He is ill.” The Bedouin said: “By God, I have never been ill”—or “I have never had a headache.” Abu al-Darda' said: “Take him away from me; let him die with his sins! I would not exchange every bout of illness I have suffered for red camels. A believer's illness expiates his sins.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنِ ابْنِ سِيرِينَ، عَنِ الرَّبَابِ الْقُشَيْرِيِّ، قَالَ:

Matn

دَخَلْنَا عَلَى أَبِي الدَّرْدَاءِ نَعُودُهُ، فَدَخَلَ عَلَيْهِ أَعْرَابِيٌّ فَقَالَ: مَا لِأَمِيرِكُمْ - وَأَبُو الدَّرْدَاءِ يَوْمَئِذٍ أَمِيرٌ - قَالَ: قُلْنَا: هُوَ شَاكٍ، قَالَ: وَاللَّهِ مَا اشْتَكَيْتُ قَطُّ - أَوْ قَالَ: وَاللَّهِ مَا صُدِعْتُ قَطُّ - قَالَ: فَقَالَ أَبُو الدَّرْدَاءِ: «أَخْرِجُوهُ عَنِّي لِيَمُتْ بِخَطَايَاهُ، §مَا أُحِبُّ أَنَّ لِي بِكُلِّ وَصَبٍ وَصِبْتُهُ، حُمْرَ النَّعَمِ إِنَّ وَصَبَ الْمُؤْمِنِ يُكَفِّرُ خَطَايَاهُ»

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us: Ma'mar informed us, from Zayd b. Aslam, that

Matn

a healthy-looking Bedouin entered the mosque while the Prophet ﷺ was there. The Messenger of God ﷺ asked: “Have you ever been ill?” He answered: “No.” He asked, pointing to his temples: “Have these two ever throbbed with pain?” He replied: “No.” When he turned to leave, the Prophet ﷺ said: “Whoever wishes to look at a man among the people of the Fire should look at this one.”

العربية
Isnad

أخبرنا عبد الرزاق قال أخبرنا معمر عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ بَيْنَا هُوَ فِي الْمَسْجِدِ إِذْ

Matn

دَخَلَ عَلَيْهِ أَعْرَابِيٌّ مُصَحَّحٌ - أَوْ قَالَ: ظَاهِرُ الصِّحَّةِ - قَالَ: فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «هَلْ شَكَيْتَ قَطُّ؟» ، قَالَ: لَا، قَالَ: «هَلْ ضَرَبَ عَلَيْكَ هَذَانِ قَطُّ؟» - وَأَشَارَ إِلَى صُدْغَيْهِ - قَالَ: لَا، فَلَمَّا وَلَّى قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «§مَنْ سَرَّهُ أَنْ يَنْظُرَ إِلَى رَجُلٍ مِنْ أَهْلِ النَّارِ فَلْيَنْظُرْ إِلَى هَذَا»

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 198
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from al-Hasan, who said: “

Matn

Fever is from the furnace of Hell, so extinguish it with cold water.” Ma'mar said: It also reached me that on the day of Khaybar the Prophet ﷺ instructed his companions to pour water upon the fever at the end of the night; they had suffered from it, but it no longer harmed them.

Ma'mar informed us: It reached me that Ibn Mas'ud fell ill and appeared distressed by it. He was asked about this and replied: “

Matn

The matter has come upon me; it is more apt to awaken me and bring me closer [to awareness] than heedlessness.” [The final expression is difficult in the source.]
