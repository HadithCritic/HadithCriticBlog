Entry19957/ 22
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 76
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from Ayyub, from Ibn Sirin, from Dafira, from Umm Salama,

Matn

who disliked garments decorated with crosses, meaning garments on which crosses were depicted. Ma'mar said: Someone who saw al-Hasan wearing a cross-patterned cloak told me of it.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنِ ابْنِ سِيرِينَ، عَنْ دَفِرَةَ، عَنْ أُمِّ سَلَمَةَ: «

Matn

أَنَّهَا §كَرِهَتِ الثِّيَابَ الْمُصَلَّبَةَ» ، يَعْنِي الَّتِي تُصَوَّرُ فِيهَا الصُّلُبُ، قَالَ مَعْمَرٌ: وَأَخْبَرَنِي مَنْ رَأَى عَلَى الْحَسَنِ كِسَاءً مُصَلَّبًا "

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from 'Abd al-Karim al-Jazari, who said: “

Matn

I saw Anas b. Malik wearing a khazz coat and khazz cloak when I was circumambulating the House with Sa'id ibn Jubayr.” Sa'id said: “Had the early generations seen him in that, they would have treated him harshly.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْكَرِيمِ الْجَزَرِيِّ، قَالَ: «§

Matn

رَأَيْتُ عَلَى أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ جُبَّةَ خَزٍّ، وَكِسَاءَ خَزٍّ، وَأَنَا أَطُوفُ مَعَ سَعِيدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ بِالْبَيْتِ» فَقَالَ سَعِيدٌ: «لَوْ أَدْرَكَهُ السَّلَفُ لَأَوْجَعُوهُ»

Matn

I saw five of the Prophet's ﷺ Companions wearing khazz: Sa'd b. Abi Waqqas, Ibn 'Umar, Jabir b. 'Abd Allah, Abu Sa'id, Abu Hurayra, and Anas.” [The source says “five” but lists six names.]

Entry19965/ 22
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 77
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from Ibn Tawus, from his father, who said:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ saw 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr b. al-'As wearing two safflower-dyed garments. He said: “Did your mother dress you in these?” He said: “Yes, Messenger of God. Should I take them off?” The Prophet ﷺ said: “Rather, burn them.” Ma'mar added: Yahya b. Abi Kathir informed me that the Prophet ﷺ stared at him when he saw the two garments and said: “Red is one of Satan's adornments, and Satan loves red.”

Entry19975/ 22
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 79
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from a man, from al-Hasan,

Matn

that the Prophet ﷺ said: “Red is part of Satan's adornment, and Satan loves red.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ رَجُلٍ، عَنِ الْحَسَنِ،

Matn

أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ قَالَ: «§الْحُمْرَةُ مِنْ زِينَةِ الشَّيْطَانِ، وَإِنَّ الشَّيْطَانَ يُحِبُّ الْحُمْرَةَ»
