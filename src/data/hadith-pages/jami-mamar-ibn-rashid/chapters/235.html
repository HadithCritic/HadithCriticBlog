Entry20707/ 8
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 339
English
Isnad

Ma'mar transmitted from Ayyub, from Ghaylan b. Jarir, from Ziyad b. Rabah, from Abu Hurayra, who said he heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: “

Matn

Whoever separates from the community, withdraws from obedience, and dies has died a death of the age of ignorance. Whoever rises against my community with his sword, striking its righteous and wicked alike, sparing no believer out of regard for his faith and honoring no covenant with those under treaty, does not belong to my community. Whoever is killed beneath a banner whose purpose is obscure, becoming angry for tribal partisanship, fighting for it, or calling to it, has suffered a death of the age of ignorance.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنْ غَيْلَانَ بْنِ جَرِيرٍ، عَنْ زِيَادِ بْنِ رَبَاحٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ يَقُولُ: «§

Matn

مَنْ فَارَقَ الْجَمَاعَةَ، وَخَرَجَ مِنَ الطَّاعَةِ فَمَاتَ فَمِيتَتُهُ جَاهِلِيَّةٌ، وَمَنْ خَرَجَ عَلَى أُمَّتِي بِسَيْفِهِ فَيَضْرِبُ بَرَّهَا وَفَاجِرَهَا لَا يَتَحَاشَى مُؤْمِنًا لِإِيمَانِهِ، وَلَا يَفِي لِذِي عَهْدٍ بِعَهْدِهِ، فَلَيْسَ مِنْ أُمَّتِي، وَمَنْ قُتِلَ تَحْتَ رَايَةٍ عِمِّيَّةٍ يَغْضَبُ لِلْعَصَبِيَّةِ، أَوْ يُقَاتِلُ لِلْعَصَبِيَّةِ، أَوْ يَدْعُو إِلَى الْعَصَبِيَّةِ، فَقِتْلَتُهُ جَاهِلِيَّةٌ»

Entry20709/ 8
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 339
English
Isnad

Ma'mar transmitted from Yahya b. Abi Kathir, who said: “It reached us that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: ‘

Matn

John son of Zechariah was commanded to convey five instructions to the Children of Israel, teach them, act upon them, and command them to act. He delayed, so Jesus was told to command John to convey them, or else deliver them himself. When Jesus told John this, John said: “Do not do so. I fear that if you deliver them instead I will be punished or God will cause the earth to swallow me.” John gathered the Children of Israel at Jerusalem until the sanctuary was full and people sat on its upper levels. He said: “God has commanded me to teach you five instructions and to command you to practice them. First, associate nothing with God. Someone who associates others with God is like a slave whose master buys him, houses him, and tells him to give his earnings to that master, but the slave works and pays someone else. Which of you would want such a slave? God created and sustains you; do not associate anything with Him. I command you to pray, and when you pray do not turn aside, for God directs His face toward His praying servant so long as the servant does not turn away—if I recall the wording correctly. I command you to give charity: it is like a prisoner seized by an enemy who is about to be executed, and who says: ‘Will you let me ransom myself for such-and-such?’ They agree and he ransoms himself. Thus charity extinguishes sin. I command you to fast: the fasting person is like a man among a group carrying a pouch of musk when no one else has any; all want to smell it. The fasting person’s scent is more pleasant to God than musk. And I command you to remember God: remembrance is like a man fleeing pursuers who finds refuge in a fortified stronghold and escapes. Nothing shelters a person from Satan except remembrance of God.”’”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ أَبِي كَثِيرٍ، قَالَ: بَلَغَنَا أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ قَالَ: «§

Matn

أُمِرَ يَحْيَى بْنُ زَكَرِيَّا بِخَمْسِ كَلِمَاتٍ أَنْ يُبَلِّغَهُنَّ وَيُعَلِّمَهُنَّ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ، وَيَعْمَلَ بِهِنَّ، وَيَأْمُرَ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ أَنْ يَعْمَلُوا بِهِنَّ، فَكَأَنَّهُ أَبْطَأَ فَقِيلَ لِعِيسَى: مُرْ يَحْيَى أَنْ يَأْمُرَ بِهَذِهِ الْكَلِمَاتِ وَإِلَّا فَأْمُرْ بِهِنَّ أَنْتَ، فَقَالَ عِيسَى لِيَحْيَى ذَلِكَ، فَقَالَ يَحْيَى: لَا تَفْعَلْ، فَإِنِّي أَخَافُ إِنْ أَمَرْتُ بِهِنَّ أَنْ أُعَذَّبَ أَوْ يَخْسِفَ اللَّهُ بِيَ الْأَرْضَ، قَالَ: فَجَمَعَ يَحْيَى بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ فِي بَيْتِ الْمَقْدِسِ حَتَّى امْتَلَأَ الْمَسْجِدُ، ثُمَّ جَلَسُوا عَلَى شُرَفِهِ فَقَالَ: إِنَّ اللَّهَ أَمَرَنِي بِخَمْسِ كَلِمَاتٍ أَنْ أُعَلِّمَكُمُوهُنَّ وَآمُرَكُمْ أَنْ تَعْمَلُوا بِهِنَّ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: أُولَاهُنَّ: أَلَّا تُشْرِكُوا بِاللَّهِ شَيْئًا، فَإنَّ مَثَلَ مَنْ يُشْرِكُ بِاللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ رَجُلٍ اشْتَرَى عَبْدًا، فَجَعَلَهُ فِي دَارِهِ، وَقَالَ: هَذِهِ دَارِي وَهَذَا عَمَلِي، فَأَدِّ إِلَيَّ عَمَلَكَ، فَجَعَلَ يَعْمَلُ وَيُؤَدِّي عَمَلَهُ إِلَى غَيْرِ سَيِّدِهِ، فَأَيُّكُمْ يُحِبُّ أَنْ يَكُونَ لَهُ عَبْدٌ كَذَلِكَ، وَإِنَّ اللَّهَ هُوَ الَّذِي خَلَقَكُمْ وَرَزَقَكُمْ فَلَا تُشْرِكُوا بِهِ شَيْئًا، وَآمُرُكُمْ بِالصَّلَاةِ، فَإِذَا صَلَّيْتُمْ فَلَا تَلْتَفِتُوا فِي صَلَاتِكُمْ، فَإنَّ اللَّهَ يَنْصِبُ - حَسِبْتُهُ قَالَ - وَجْهَهُ لِعَبْدِهِ فِي صَلَاتِهِ مَا لَمْ يَلْتَفِتْ، قَالَ: وَآمُرُكُمْ بِالصَّدَقَةِ، فَإنَّ مَثَلَ الصَّدَقَةِ كَمَثَلِ رَجُلٍ أَخَذَهُ الْعَدُوُّ فَقَدَّمُوهُ لِيَضْرِبُوا عُنُقَهُ، فَقَالَ: مَا تَصْنَعُونَ بِضَرْبِ عُنُقِي، أَلَا أَفْتَدِي نَفْسِي مِنْكُمْ بِكَذَا وَكَذَا؟ قَالُوا: بَلَى، فَافْتَدَى نَفْسَهُ مِنْهُمْ، فَكَذَلِكَ الصَّدَقَةُ تُطْفِئُ الْخَطِيئَةَ، قَالَ: وَآمُرُكُمْ بِالصِّيَامِ، فَإنَّ مَثَلَ الصَّائِمِ كَمَثَلِ رَجُلٍ فِي قَوْمٍ مَعَهُ صُرَّةُ مِسْكٍ لَيْسَ مَعَ أَحَدٍ مِنَ الْقَوْمِ مِسْكٌ غَيْرُهُ، فَكُلُّهُمْ يُحِبُّ أَنْ يَجِدَ رِيحَهُ، فَكَذَلِكَ الصَّائِمُ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ أَطْيَبُ مِنْ رِيحِ الْمِسْكِ، وَآمُرُكُمْ بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ، فَإنَّ مَثَلَ ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ رَجُلٍ انْطَلَقَ فَارًّا مِنَ الْعَدُوِّ وَهُمْ يَطْلُبُونَهُ حَتَّى لَجَأَ إِلَى حِصْنٍ حَصِينٍ، فأفْلَتَ مِنْهُمْ، وَكَذَلِكَ الشَّيْطَانُ لَا يُحْرِزُ مِنْهُ إِلَّا ذِكْرُ اللَّهِ»

Unnumbered continuation, printed page 340

Yahya said: Al-Harith al-Ash'ari told me that the Prophet ﷺ said: “I command you five things: hearing, obedience, adherence to the community, emigration, and fighting in God’s cause. Whoever leaves the community by even a handspan has cast the bond of Islam from his neck until he returns; and whoever calls with a call of the age of ignorance is among those crouching in Hell.” A man asked: “Even if he prays and fasts?” He said: “Even if he prays and fasts. Call yourselves by the names God has given you: His servants, Muslims, believers.”

العربية

قَالَ يَحْيَى: فَأَخْبَرَنِي الْحَارِثُ الْأَشْعَرِيُّ أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ قَالَ: «وَأَنَا §آمُرُكُمْ بِخَمْسٍ: بِالسَّمْعِ، وَالطَّاعَةِ، وَالْجَمَاعَةِ، وَالْهِجْرَةِ، وَالْجِهَادِ - [٣٤١] - فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ، فَمَنَ خَرَجَ مِنَ الْجَمَاعَةِ قِيدَ شِبْرٍ فَقَدْ خَلَعَ رِبْقَةَ الْإِسْلَامِ مِنْ رَأْسِهِ حَتَّى يُرَاجِعَ، وَمَنْ دَعَا دَعْوَةَ جَاهِلِيَّةٍ فَإِنَّهُ مِنْ جُثَا جَهَنَّمَ» ، فَقَالَ رَجُلٌ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، وَإِنْ صَلَّى وَصَامَ؟ قَالَ: «نَعَمْ، وَإِنْ صَلَّى وَصَامَ، وَلَكِنْ تَسَمُّوا بِاسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي سَمَّاكُمْ عِبَادَ اللَّهِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ»

Entry20710/ 8
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 341
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq transmitted from Ma'mar, from 'Abd al-Malik b. 'Umayr, from 'Abd Allah b. al-Zubayr: 'Umar b. al-Khattab stood preaching at al-Jabiya and said: “The Messenger of God ﷺ once stood among us as I stand among you and said: ‘

Matn

Honor my Companions: they are the best of you, then those who follow them, then those after them. Thereafter lies will appear, until a man volunteers an oath without being asked and offers testimony without being asked. Whoever wishes the heartland of Paradise must remain with the community. Satan is with a solitary individual and farther from two. Let no man be alone with a woman, for Satan is their third. Whoever is pleased by his good deed and distressed by his evil deed is a believer.’”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الْمَلِكِ بْنِ عُمَيْرٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ الزُّبَيْرِ أَنَّ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ قَامَ بِالْجَابِيَةِ خَطِيبًا، فَقَالَ: إِنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ قَامَ فِينَا مَقَامِي فِيكُمْ فَقَالَ: «§

Matn

أَكْرِمُوا أَصْحَابِي؛ فَإِنَّهُمْ خِيَارُكُمْ، ثُمَّ الَّذِينَ يَلُونَهُمْ، ثُمَّ الَّذِينَ يَلُونَهُمْ، ثُمَّ يَظْهَرُ الْكَذِبُ حَتَّى يَحْلِفَ الْإِنْسَانُ عَلَى الْيَمِينِ لَا يُسْأَلُهَا، وَيَشْهَدَ عَلَى الشَّهَادَةِ لَا يُسْأَلُهَا، فَمَنْ سَرَّهُ بُحْبُوحَةُ الْجَنَّةِ فَعَلَيْهِ بِالْجَمَاعَةِ، فَإنَّ الشَّيْطَانَ مَعَ الْفَذِّ، وَهُوَ مِنَ الِاثْنَيْنِ أَبْعَدُ، وَلَا يَخْلُوَنَّ رَجُلٌ بِامْرَأَةٍ، فَإنَّ الشَّيْطَانَ ثَالِثُهُمْ، وَمَنْ سَرَّتْهُ حَسَنَتُهُ وَسَاءَتْهُ سَيِّئَتُهُ فَهُوَ مُؤْمِنٌ»

Entry20711/ 8
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 341
English
Isnad

Ma'mar transmitted from Qatada, from Nasr b. 'Asim al-Laythi, from Khalid b. Khalid al-Yashkuri:

Matn

Around the conquest of Tustar he traveled to Kufa and entered a mosque, where a circle surrounded a well-built, pleasant-looking man, recognizably from the Hijaz. The people identified him as Hudhayfa b. al-Yaman. Khalid sat down, and Hudhayfa related: “People used to ask the Messenger of God ﷺ about good, but I asked him about evil.” The group objected, so he explained: “Islam came with a reality different from the age of ignorance, and I had been given an understanding of the Quran. I asked: ‘Messenger of God, will there be evil after this good, as there was before it?’ He said, ‘Yes.’ I asked, ‘What protection is there?’ He said, ‘The sword.’ I asked whether anything would remain after the sword. He said: ‘Yes, rule amid irritations and a truce clouded by rancor.’ I asked what would follow. He said: ‘Then callers to error will arise. If at that time God has a caliph on earth, hold fast to him even if he beats your back and seizes your property. Otherwise, die while biting onto the stump of a tree.’ I asked what came next. He said: ‘Then the Dajjal will appear, with a river and a fire. Whoever enters his fire will receive his reward and have his burden removed; whoever enters his river will incur his burden and lose his reward.’ I asked what would follow. He said: ‘A foal will be born that will not be ridden before the Hour comes.’” Qatada explained that the description of Hudhayfa means he was of moderate, well-proportioned build. Ma'mar reported Qatada's interpretation that “the sword” refers to the apostasy conflicts in Abu Bakr's time; the “truce” means reconciliation, while its “smoke” means hidden animosities.

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq transmitted from Ma'mar, from Abu Ishaq, from Zayd b. Yuthay', from Hudhayfa b. al-Yaman, who said: “

Matn

People, what will you do when you are required to give what is due and do so, yet are denied your own rights?” They replied that whoever lived to see that would be patient. He said: “Then, by the Lord of the Ka'ba, you will enter it”—meaning Paradise.

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq transmitted from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from Salim, from his father:

Matn

Whenever 'Umar b. al-Khattab forbade the people something, he would gather his family and say: “I have forbidden this or that. People watch you as birds watch meat. If you fall into it, they will too; if you hold back, so will they. By God, if any of you is brought before me for doing what I forbade the people, I will double his punishment because of his relationship to me. Let anyone who wishes proceed, and anyone who wishes withdraw.”
