Entry20352/ 15
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 211
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq said: Ma'mar reported from Ayyub, from Ibn Sirin, from Abu Hurayra, from the Prophet ﷺ, who said: “

Matn

Near the end of time, a believer's dream will hardly be false. Those who are most truthful in speech will have the truest dreams. Dreams are of three kinds: a good dream, which is glad tidings from God; a dream concerning things a person has been thinking about; and a dream that brings sorrow from Satan. If any of you sees a dream he dislikes, let him not tell it to anyone, but rise and pray.” Abu Hurayra said: “I like the shackle [seen in a dream], but dislike the iron collar. The shackle signifies firmness in religion.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنِ ابْنِ سِيرِينَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ قَالَ: «§

Matn

فِي آخِرِ الزَّمَانِ لَا تَكَادُ رُؤْيَا الْمُؤْمِنِ تَكْذِبُ، وَأَصْدَقُهُمْ رُؤْيَا أَصْدَقُهُمْ حَدِيثًا، وَالرُّؤْيَا ثَلَاثٌ: الرُّؤْيَا الْحَسَنَةُ بُشْرَى مِنَ اللَّهِ، وَالرُّؤْيَا يُحَدِّثُ بِهَا الرَّجُلُ نَفْسَهُ، وَالرُّؤْيَا تَحْزِينٌ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ، فَإِذَا رَأَى أَحَدُكُمْ رُؤْيَا يَكْرَهُهَا فَلَا يُحَدِّثْ بِهَا أَحَدًا وَلْيَقُمْ فَلْيُصَلِّ» ، قَالَ أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ: «يُعْجِبُنِي الْقَيْدُ وَأَكْرَهُ الْغُلَّ، الْقَيْدُ ثَبَاتٌ فِي الدِّينِ»

Unnumbered continuation, printed page 211

The Prophet ﷺ also said: “A believer’s dream is one of forty-six parts of prophecy.”

العربية

وَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «§رُؤْيَا الْمُؤْمِنِ جُزْءٌ مِنْ سِتَّةٍ وَأَرْبَعِينَ جُزْءًا مِنَ النُّبُوَّةِ»

Entry20353/ 15
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 212
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from Abu Salama, who said:

Matn

I used to suffer greatly from dreams, though not to the point of being wrapped up [with illness], until Abu Qatada told me that he heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: “A vision comes from God, and a distressing dream comes from Satan. When one of you dreams something he dislikes, let him spit lightly to his left three times and seek refuge from Satan; then it will not harm him.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ أَبِي سَلَمَةَ، قَالَ:

Matn

كُنْتُ أَلْقَى مِنَ الرُّؤْيَا شِدَّةً غَيْرَ أَنِّي لَا أُزَمَّلُ حَتَّى حَدَّثَنِي أَبُو قَتَادَةَ أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ يَقُولُ: «§الرُّؤْيَا مِنَ اللَّهِ، وَالْحُلْمُ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ، فَإِذَا حَلَمَ أَحَدُكُمْ شَيْئًا يَكْرَهُهُ فَلْيَبْصُقْ عَنْ شِمَالِهِ ثَلَاثَ نَفَثَاتٍ، وَلْيَسْتَعِذْ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَضُرُّهُ»

Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from Ayyub, from Abu Qilaba, who said the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “

Matn

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from Qatada, who said: 'Umar wrote to Abu Musa: “

Matn

As for what follows, I have been commanding you according to what the Quran commands and forbidding you according to what Muhammad ﷺ forbade, instructing you to follow understanding and customary practice and to study the Arabic language. When one of you sees a dream and tells it to his brother, let him say: ‘Good for us and evil for our enemies.’”

Isnad

Ma'mar informed us, from Abu Ishaq, from 'Amr b. 'Asim, from Ibn Mas'ud, who said: “

Matn

The dream of a believer is one of seventy parts of prophecy. This fire of yours is one of seventy parts of the fire of Hell; and the scorching wind from which God created the jinn is one of seventy parts of the heat of Hell.”

Matn

رُؤْيَا الْمُؤْمِنِ جُزْءٌ مِنْ سَبْعِينَ جُزْءًا مِنَ النُّبُوَّةِ، وَإِنَّ نَارَكُمْ هَذِهِ لَجُزْءٌ مِنْ سَبْعِينَ جُزْءًا مِنْ نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ، وَإِنَّ السَّمُومَ الْحَارَّ الَّتِي خَلَقَ اللَّهُ مِنْهَا الْجَانَّ لَجُزْءٌ مِنْ سَبْعِينَ جُزْءًا مِنْ حَرِّ جَهَنَّمَ»

Entry20360/ 15
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 214
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from 'Ubayd God b. 'Abd Allah, from Abu Hurayra:

Matn

A man came to the Messenger of God ﷺ and said: “Last night I dreamed of a canopy from which butter and honey were dripping. I saw people catching it in their hands, some taking much and others little. I saw a rope extending from heaven to earth. I saw you take hold of it and rise; then another man took hold and rose, and a third man took hold and rose, then a fourth took hold but it broke with him, and then it was joined for him and he rose.” Abu Bakr said: “Messenger of God, may my father and mother be your ransom! Please let me interpret it.” He said: “Interpret it.” Abu Bakr said: “The canopy represents Islam. The dripping butter and honey are the Quran, its softness and sweetness; those who gathered much or little are those who take much or little of the Quran. The rope extending from heaven to earth is the truth upon which you stand: you take hold of it and God raises you. After you, another man takes hold and rises, then another man rises, then another whose rope breaks but is joined for him and he rises.” Abu Bakr asked: “Messenger of God, tell me: was I right or wrong?” He replied: “You were right about some things and wrong about others.” Abu Bakr said: “I adjure you, Messenger of God, tell me where I was wrong.” He replied: “Do not swear an oath.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ،

Matn

أَنَّ رَجُلًا أَتَى رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فَقَالَ: إِنِّي أَرَى اللَّيْلَةَ الظُّلَّةَ يُنْطَفُ مِنْهَا السَّمْنُ وَالْعَسَلُ، فَأَرَى النَّاسَ يَتَكَفَّفُونَ مِنْهَا بِأَيْدِيهِمْ، فَالْمُسْتَكْثِرُ وَالْمُسْتَقِلُّ، وَأَرَى سَبَبًا وَاصِلًا مِنَ السَّمَاءِ إِلَى الْأَرْضِ، فَأَرَاكَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ أَخَذْتَ بِهِ فَعَلَوْتَ، ثُمَّ أَخَذَ بِهِ رَجُلٌ آخَرُ فَعَلَا، ثُمَّ أَخَذَ بِهِ رَجُلٌ آخَرُ فَعَلَا، ثُمَّ أَخَذَ بِهِ رَجُلٌ آخَرُ فَانْقَطَعَ بِهِ، ثُمَّ وُصِلَ لَهُ فَعَلَا بِهِ، فَقَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، بِأَبِي أَنْتَ وَأُمِّي، وَاللَّهِ لَتَدَعَنِّي فَلَأَعْبُرَنَّهَا، فَقَالَ: «§اعْبُرْهَا» فَقَالَ: أَمَّا الظُّلَّةُ فَظُلَّةُ الْإِسْلَامُ، وَأَمَّا مَا يُنْطَفُ مِنَ السَّمْنِ وَالْعَسَلِ فَهُوَ الْقُرْآنُ لِينُهُ وَحَلَاوَتُهُ، وَأَمَّا الْمُسْتَكْثِرُ وَالْمُسْتَقِلُّ فَهُوَ الْمُسْتَكْثِرُ مِنَ الْقُرْآنِ، وَالْمُسْتَقِلُّ مِنْهُ، وَأَمَّا السَّبَبُ الْوَاصِلُ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ إِلَى الْأَرْضِ فَهُوَ الْحَقُّ الَّذِي أَنْتَ عَلَيْهِ، تَأْخُذُ بِهِ فَيُعْلِيكَ اللَّهُ، ثُمَّ يَأْخُذُ بِهِ رَجُلٌ آخَرُ بَعْدَكَ فَيَعْلُو بِهِ، ثُمَّ يَأْخُذُ بِهِ رَجُلٌ آخَرُ بَعْدَهُ فَيَعْلُو بِهِ، ثُمَّ يَأْخُذُ بِهِ رَجُلٌ آخَرُ فَيَنْقَطِعُ بِهِ، ثُمَّ يُوصَلُ لَهُ فَيَعْلُو بِهِ، أَيْ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، لَتُحَدِّثْنِي أَصَبْتُ أَمْ أَخْطَأْتُ؟ قَالَ: «أَصَبْتَ بَعْضًا، وَأَخْطَأْتَ بَعْضًا» قَالَ: أَقْسَمْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، لَتُخْبِرَنِّي بِالَّذِي أَخْطَأْتُ قَالَ: «لَا تُقْسِمْ»

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from Qatada, who said:

Matn

A man came to 'Umar b. al-Khattab and said: “I dreamed that the earth grew green, then became barren, then green, then barren.” 'Umar said: “You are a man who will believe, then disbelieve, then believe, then disbelieve, and die an unbeliever.” The man said: “I saw nothing [of the sort]!” 'Umar replied, quoting Quran 12:41: “The matter about which you asked has been decided. What was decreed for Joseph's fellow prisoner has been decreed for you.”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

جَاءَ رَجُلٌ إِلَى عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ فَقَالَ: إِنِّي رَأَيْتُ كَأَنَّ الْأَرْضَ أعْشَبَتْ، ثُمَّ أجْدَبَتْ، ثُمَّ أعْشَبَتْ، ثُمَّ أجْدَبَتْ، فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: «§أَنْتَ رَجُلٌ تُؤْمِنُ ثُمَّ تَكْفُرُ، ثُمَّ تُؤْمِنُ ثُمَّ تَكْفُرُ، ثُمَّ تَمُوتُ كَافِرًا» ، فَقَالَ الرَّجُلُ: لَمْ أَرَ شَيْئًا، فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: {قُضِيَ الْأَمْرُ الَّذِي فِيهِ تَسْتَفْتِيَانِ} [يوسف: ٤١] ، «قَدْ قُضِيَ لَكَ مَا قُضِيَ لِصَاحِبِ يُوسُفَ»
