Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from someone who heard al-Hasan say that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “

Matn

My companions among the people are like salt in food.” Al-Hasan would then say: “Alas! The salt of the people has gone.”

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from Abu Harun al-'Abdi, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, who said: “

Matn

Soon an expedition will go out, and people will ask, ‘Are there any companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ among them?’ One, two, or three will be found, and help will be sought through them. Then an army will go out, and people will ask, ‘Are there any companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ among them?’ None will be found, so they will ask, ‘Is anyone among them who accompanied the companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ?’ One or two will be found; even if such a man were across the sea, they would cross over to him to learn understanding from him.”

Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هَارُونَ الْعَبْدِيِّ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيِّ، قَالَ: «§

Matn

أَوْشَكَ أَنْ يَخْرُجَ الْبَعْثُ، فَيُقَالُ: هَلْ فِيهِمْ مِنْ أَصْحَابِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ أَحَدٌ؟ فَيُوجَدُ الرَّجُلُ وَالرَّجُلَانِ وَالثَّلَاثُ فَيُسْتَنْصَرُ بِهِمْ، ثُمَّ يَخْرُجُ الْجَيْشُ، فَيُقَالُ: هَلْ فِيهِمْ مِنْ أَصْحَابِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ أَحَدٌ؟ فَلَا يُوجَدُ، فَيُقَالُ: هَلْ فِيهِمْ مَنْ صَحِبَ صَحَابَةَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ؟ فَيُوجَدُ الرَّجُلُ وَالرَّجُلَانِ حَتَّى لَوْ كَانَ أَحَدُهُمْ مِنْ وَرَاءِ الْبَحْرِ لَرَكِبُوا إِلَيْهِ يَتَفَقَّهُونَ مِنْهُ»

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 222
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from one of the descendants of 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf, who said:

Matn

I was traveling with 'Umar along the road to Mecca. We stopped for the midday rest and fell asleep. I dreamed that 'Umar passed me and nudged Umm Kulthum daughter of 'Uqba with his foot, then went on. I hurriedly dressed, followed, and caught up to him. I said: “Commander of the Believers, I did not catch up with you until I was exhausted, and I do not think people will catch up to you without becoming exhausted.” 'Umar said: “I do not think I was walking quickly.” 'Abd al-Rahman said: “By the One in whose hand is my soul, I think he carried it out—or that he will carry it out.” [The Arabic includes difficult wording about the dream and its fulfillment; this is a close rendering without imposing an external interpretation.]

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ بَعْضِ، بَنِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ عَوْفٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ عَوْفٍ، قَالَ:

Matn

كُنْتُ مَعَ عُمَرَ فِي سَفَرٍ بِطَرِيقِ مَكَّةَ، فَنَزَلْنَا فِي الْقَائِلَةِ فَنِمْنَا، فَرَأَيْتُ كَأَنَّ عُمَرَ مَرَّ بِي، فرَكَضَ أُمَّ كُلْثُومٍ ابْنَةَ عُقْبَةَ بِرِجْلِهِ، ثُمَّ مَضَى فَشَدَدْتُ عَلَيَّ ثِيَابِي، ثُمَّ اتَّبَعْتُهُ فَأَدْرَكْتُهُ، فَقُلْتُ: «يَا أَمِيرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ §مَا أَدْرَكْتُكَ حَتَّى حُسِرْتُ، وَمَا أَرَى النَّاسَ يُدْرِكُوكَ حَتَّى يُحْسَرُوا» ، فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: مَا أَحْسِبُنِي أَسْرَعْتُ، قَالَ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ: «وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ إِنِّي لَأَرَاهُ عَمِلَهُ - أَوْ إِنَّهُ لَيَعْمَلُهُ»

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq informed us, from Ma'mar, from Ibn Tawus, from 'Ikrima b. Khalid,

Matn

that Hafsa, Ibn Muti', and 'Abd Allah b. 'Umar said to 'Umar b. al-Khattab: “If you ate some finer food, it would give you more strength to uphold what is right.” He asked: “Is this the opinion of all of you?” They said: “Yes.” He replied: “I know each of you wishes me well, but I left my two companions on a straight path. If I abandon their path, I shall not catch up with them at the final abode.” Then a year of hardship overtook the people; during that year he ate neither clarified butter nor rich food until the people recovered.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ طَاوُسٍ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ بْنِ خَالِدٍ،

Matn

أَنَّ حَفْصَةَ، وَابْنَ مُطِيعٍ، وَعَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ عُمَرَ كَلَّمُوا عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ، فَقَالُوا: لَوْ أَكَلْتَ طَعَامًا طَيِّبًا كَانَ أَقْوَى لَكَ عَلَى الْحَقِّ؟ قَالَ: «أكُلُّكُمْ عَلَى هَذَا الرَّأْيِ؟» ، قَالُوا: نَعَمْ، قَالَ: «§قَدْ عَلِمْتُ أَنَّهُ لَيْسَ مِنْكُمْ إِلَّا نَاصِحٌ، وَلَكِنِّي تَرَكْتُ صَاحِبَيَّ عَلَى الْجَادَّةِ، فَإِنْ تَرَكْتُ جَادَّتَهُمْ لَمْ أَدْرِكْهُمَا فِي الْمَنْزِلِ» قَالَ: وَأَصَابَ النَّاسَ سَنَةٌ، فَمَا أَكَلَ عَامَئِذٍ سَمْنًا وَلَا سَمِينًا حَتَّى أُحْيِيَ النَّاسُ

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, who said: “

Matn

Hudhayfa b. al-Yaman belonged to Banu 'Abs and was counted among the Helpers. He and his father al-Yaman fought fiercely alongside the Messenger of God ﷺ at Uhud. The Muslims surrounded al-Yaman and struck at him with their swords. Later, while the Prophet ﷺ was traveling to Tabuk, he dismounted so revelation could come to him. His camel, which he had made kneel, got up and wandered off dragging its rein. Hudhayfa met it, took its rein, led it back, made it kneel and sat beside it. When the Prophet ﷺ returned for his camel, he asked, ‘Who is this?’ He replied, ‘Hudhayfa b. al-Yaman.’ The Prophet ﷺ said, ‘I shall confide a secret to you; never tell anyone. I have been forbidden to perform the funeral prayer for certain named men,’ referring to a number of hypocrites. After the Messenger of God ﷺ died and 'Umar became caliph, whenever a Companion whom 'Umar suspected of belonging to that group died, he would take Hudhayfa by the hand and lead him toward the funeral. If Hudhayfa accompanied him, 'Umar prayed over the man; if Hudhayfa pulled away, 'Umar did not, but instructed someone else to lead the prayer.”

Hazm entered upon 'Amr b. al-'As and said: ‘'Ammar has been killed, and I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say, “The rebellious faction will kill him.”’ Alarmed, 'Amr arose, repeating words of distress, and went in to Mu'awiya. Mu'awiya asked, ‘What is the matter?’ He said, ‘'Ammar has been killed.’ Mu'awiya asked, ‘So what if 'Ammar was killed?’ 'Amr said, ‘I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say, “The rebellious faction will kill him.”’ Mu'awiya replied, ‘Your argument has collapsed. Did we kill him? Rather, 'Ali and his companions killed him by bringing him here and throwing him beneath our spears—or,’ the transmitter said, ‘between our swords.’”
