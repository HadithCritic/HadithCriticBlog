Entry20036/ 14
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 99
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from Ibrahim b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf, who said:

Matn

When the treasures of Khusraw were brought to 'Umar, 'Abdullah b. al-Arqam al-Zuhri asked him: “Why not place them in the treasury until you distribute them?” He replied: “No roof will shelter them until I have distributed them.” He ordered them placed in the mosque courtyard, and guards watched them overnight. In the morning the coverings were removed, and he saw gold and silver gleaming so brightly as almost to dazzle the eyes. 'Umar wept. 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf said: “Why do you weep, Commander of the Believers? By God, this is a day for gratitude, joy, and happiness.” 'Umar replied: “No. Whenever a people were given this, enmity and hatred were cast among them.” Then he asked: “Shall we measure their shares by the sa', or heap it into their hands?” 'Ali said: “Heap it out to them.” He called Hasan b. 'Ali first and he took his handfuls; then he called Husayn and afterward gave to the people. He established the registers of stipends and assigned each of the Emigrants five thousand dirhams per year, each of the Ansar four thousand, and each wife of the Prophet ﷺ twelve thousand, except Safiyya and Juwayriya, for each of whom he assigned six thousand dirhams.

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ عَوْفٍ، قَالَ:

Matn

لَمَّا أُتِيَ عُمَرُ بِكُنُوزِ كِسْرَى، قَالَ لَهُ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ الْأَرْقَمِ الزُّهْرِيُّ: أَلَا تَجْعَلُهَا فِي بَيْتِ الْمَالِ حَتَّى تَقْسِمَهَا؟ قَالَ: «لَا يُظِلُّهَا سَقْفٌ حَتَّى أُمْضِيَهَا» ، فَأَمَرَ بِهَا، فَوُضِعَتْ فِي صَرْحِ الْمَسْجِدِ، فَبَاتُوا يَحْرُسُونَهَا، فَلَمَّا أَصْبَحَ أَمَرَ بِهَا فَكُشِفَ عَنْهَا، فَرَأَى فِيهَا مِنَ الْحَمْرَاءِ وَالْبَيْضَاءِ مَا يَكَادُ يَتَلَأْلَأُ مِنْهُ الْبَصَرُ، قَالَ: فَبَكَى عُمَرُ، فَقَالَ لَهُ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ عَوْفٍ: مَا يُبْكِيكَ يَا أَمِيرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ؟ فَوَاللَّهِ إِنْ كَانَ هَذَا لَيَوْمَ شُكْرٍ، وَيَوْمَ سُرُورٍ، وَيَوْمَ فَرَحٍ، فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: «كَلَّا، إِنَّ §هَذَا لَمْ يُعْطَهُ قَوْمٌ إِلَّا أُلْقِيَ بَيْنَهُمُ الْعَدَاوَةُ وَالْبَغْضَاءُ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: أَنَكِيلُ لَهُمْ بِالصَّاعِ أَمْ نَحْثُوَ؟» ، فَقَالَ عَلِيٌّ: بَلِ احْثُوا لَهُمْ، ثُمَّ دَعَا حَسَنَ بْنَ عَلِيٍّ أَوَّلَ النَّاسِ فَحَثَا لَهُ، ثُمَّ دَعَا حُسَيْنًا ثُمَّ أَعْطَى النَّاسَ، وَدَوَّنَ الدَّوَاوِينَ، وَفَرَضَ لِلْمُهَاجِرِينَ لِكُلِّ رَجُلٍ مِنْهُمْ خَمْسَةَ آلَافِ دِرْهَمٍ فِي كُلِّ سَنَةٍ، وَلِلْأَنْصَارِ لِكُلِّ رَجُلٍ مِنْهُمْ أَرْبَعَةَ آلَافِ دِرْهَمٍ، وَفَرَضَ لِأَزْوَاجِ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ لِكُلِّ امْرَأَةٍ مِنْهُنَّ اثْنَيْ عَشَرَ أَلْفَ دِرْهَمٍ، إِلَّا صَفِيَّةَ وَجُوَيْرِيَةَ، فَرَضَ لِكُلِّ وَاحِدَةٍ مِنْهُمَا سِتَّةَ آلَافِ دِرْهَمٍ

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 101
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, who said:

Matn

A young she-camel from the charity livestock broke a limb, so 'Umar had it prepared in serving dishes and invited people to eat. Al-'Abbas asked: “Why do you not do this for us more often?” 'Umar replied: “By God, we have found no lawful way to handle this wealth except to take it where due, place it where due, and withhold it from no one who has a right to it.”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

انْكَسَرَتْ قَلُوصٌ مِنْ إِبِلِ الصَّدَقَةِ، فجَفنَهَا عُمَرُ، وَدَعَا النَّاسَ عَلَيْهَا، فَقَالَ لَهُ الْعَبَّاسُ: لَوْ كُنْتَ تَصْنَعُ بِنَا هَكَذَا، فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: «§إِنَّا وَاللَّهِ مَا وَجَدْنَا لِهَذَا الْمَالِ سَبِيلًا، إِلَّا أَنْ يُؤْخَذَ مِنْ حَقٍّ وَيُوضَعَ فِي حَقٍّ، وَلَا يُمْنَعَ مِنْ حَقٍّ»

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from Malik b. Aws b. al-Hadathan, who heard 'Umar b. al-Khattab say: “

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 101
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Ayyub, from 'Ikrima b. Khalid, from Malik b. Aws b. al-Hadathan, who said:

Matn

'Umar recited, “Alms are only for the poor...” (9:60), to the end of the verse, “Knowing, Wise.” He said: “These belong to those [named in the verse].” Then he recited, “Know that whatever you acquire as spoils, a fifth belongs to God...” (8:41), until the mention of the traveler, and said: “These belong to those [named there].” Then he recited the passage beginning “Whatever God has restored to His messenger from the people of the towns...” (59:7), continuing to “those who came after them” (59:10). He said: “This encompasses all Muslims. If I live, even a herdsman in the heights of Himyar will receive his share of it, though he has not sweated a drop to acquire it.” [The Arabic source associates one quoted phrase with 2:177; its context here is the spoils verse, 8:41.]

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ بْنِ خَالِدٍ، عَنْ مَالِكِ بْنِ أَوْسِ بْنِ الْحَدَثَانِ، قَالَ:

Matn

قَرَأَ عُمَرُ: «{§إِنَّمَا الصَّدَقَاتُ لِلْفُقُرَاءِ} [التوبة: ٦٠] حَتَّى بَلَغَ {عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌ} [التوبة: ٦٠] ثُمَّ قَالَ: هَذِهِ لِهَؤُلَاءِ ثُمَّ قَرَأَ: {وَاعْلَمُوا أَنَّمَا غَنِمْتُمْ مِنْ شَيْءٍ فَأَنَّ لِلَّهِ خُمُسَهُ} [الأنفال: ٤١] حَتَّى بَلَغَ {وَابْنِ السَّبِيلِ} [البقرة: ١٧٧] ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: هَذِهِ لِهَؤُلَاءِ، ثُمَّ قَرَأَ: {مَا أَفَاءَ} [الحشر: ٧] اللَّهُ عَلَى رَسُولِهِ مِنْ أَهْلِ الْقُرَى حَتَّى بَلَغَ {وَالَّذِينَ جَاءُوا مِنْ بَعْدِهِمْ} ثُمَّ قَالَ: هَذِهِ اسْتَوْعَبَتِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ عَامَّةً، فَلَئِنْ عِشْتُ لَيَأْتِيَنَّ الرَّاعِي وَهُوَ بِسَرْوِ حِمْيَرَ نَصِيبُهُ مِنْهَا، لَمْ يَعْرَقْ فِيهَا جَبِينُهُ»

Entry20041/ 14
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 102
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from 'Urwa b. al-Zubayr and Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib, and from Hisham, from his father:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ gave Hakim b. Hizam less than he had given others. Hakim said: “Messenger of God, I never thought you would put me below anyone.” The Prophet ﷺ increased his gift. He asked for more, and he increased it again until Hakim was satisfied. He asked: “Messenger of God, which of your gifts was best?” He replied: “The first.” Then the Prophet ﷺ said: “Hakim b. Hizam, this wealth is green and sweet. Whoever takes it with an open, contented soul and uses it well will be blessed in it. Whoever takes it with craving and uses it badly will not be blessed in it and will be like one who eats yet is never satisfied. The upper hand is better than the lower hand.” He asked: “Even if it is taken from you, Messenger of God?” He answered: “Even from me.” Hakim said: “By the One who sent you with the truth, after you I shall take nothing from anyone.” He accepted neither a stipend nor a place on the public payroll until he died. Later, 'Umar summoned him to receive his entitlement, but he refused. 'Umar said: “God, I call You to witness regarding Hakim b. Hizam that I invite him to take his right from this wealth, but he refuses; I absolve myself of responsibility before God.” Hakim said: “By God, I will never take anything from you or anyone else.” He died as one of the wealthiest men of Quraysh.

Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, who said: Ja'far b. Burqan told me, from Maymun b. Mihran, who said:

Matn

Muhammad b. Marwan invited me to have my name entered on the stipend register, but I refused. He said: “Do you not dislike having no share among the Muslims?” I replied: “I have a share among the Muslims even without being on the register.” He asked: “Do you know anyone among the early generations who was not on the register?” I said: “Yes.” He asked: “Who?” I replied: “Hakim b. Hizam.”

Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from al-Sa'ib b. Yazid:

Matn

'Umar b. al-Khattab met 'Abdullah b. al-Sa'di and said: “Have I not been told that you serve in an office for the Muslims and are then given payment for your service, yet refuse to accept it?” He replied: “I am well provided for; I have servants and horses and do not need it. I prefer my service to be a charitable contribution to the Muslims.” 'Umar said: “Do not do that. The Messenger of God ﷺ would give me gifts, and I would say: ‘Prophet of God, give it to someone else.’ One day he said: ‘Take it, 'Umar. Either make it your property or give it away in charity. Whatever of this wealth God brings you while you neither covet nor ask for it, take it. As for what does not come that way, do not let your heart pursue it.’”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنِ السَّائِبِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ، قَالَ:

Matn

Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Ayyub, from Ibn Sirin, from al-Ahnaf b. Qays, who said:

Matn

We were sitting by 'Umar's door when a young woman came out. We said: “She is a concubine of the Commander of the Believers.” She replied: “By God, I am not his concubine, nor am I lawful to him. I belong to God's wealth [the public treasury].” She went inside, then 'Umar came out and asked: “What do you think it is lawful for me to take from God's wealth—or from this wealth?” We said: “The Commander of the Believers knows better than we do.” I think he questioned us again and we answered in the same way. He said: “If you wish, I shall tell you what I deem lawful to take from it: a riding animal for my pilgrimage and 'umra; a set of clothes for winter and another for summer; enough food to feed my household; and my share along with the other Muslims, for I am merely one of the Muslims.” Ma'mar said: “The animal on which he performed pilgrimage and 'umra was only a single camel.”

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنِ ابْنِ سِيرِينَ، عَنِ الْأَحْنَفِ بْنِ قَيْسٍ، قَالَ:

Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 105
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from Ayyub, from Ibn Sirin, who said:

Matn

'Umar b. al-Khattab met a relative who approached him hoping to receive money. 'Umar rebuked and scolded him, and the man departed. Later 'Umar met him and said: “Did you come to me so that I might give you God's money? What would I say to God if I met Him as a treacherous ruler? Why did you not ask me from my own money?” He then gave him a considerable amount of his personal wealth. The narrator said: “I think he said ten thousand dirhams.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ أَيُّوبَ، عَنِ ابْنِ سِيرِينَ، قَالَ:

Matn

لَقِيَ عُمَرُ بْنُ الْخَطَّابِ ذَا قَرَابَةٍ لَهُ، فَعَرَضَ لِعُمَرَ أَنْ يُعْطِيَهُ مِنَ الْمَالِ، فَانْتَهَرَهُ عُمَرُ وَزَبَرَهُ، فَانْطَلَقَ الرَّجُلُ، ثُمَّ لَقِيَهُ عُمَرُ بَعْدُ، فَقَالَ لَهُ: «§أَجِئْتَنِي لِأُعْطِيَكَ مَالَ اللَّهِ؟ مَاذَا أَقُولُ لِلَّهِ إِذَا لَقِيتُهُ مَلِكًا خَائِنًا؟ أَفَلَا كُنْتَ سَأَلْتَنِي مِنْ مَالِي» ، فَأَعْطَاهُ مِنْ مَالِهِ مَالًا كَثِيرًا، قَالَ: حَسِبْتُ أَنَّهُ قَالَ: عَشَرَةَ آلَافِ دِرْهَمٍ

English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, who said:

Matn

When Abu Bakr became caliph, he said: “My people know that my trade was adequate to support my family. But I have become occupied with the Muslims' affairs, so I shall devote my work to their affairs, and Abu Bakr's household shall eat from this wealth.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ:

Matn

لَمَّا اسْتُخْلِفَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ، قَالَ: «§قَدْ عَلِمَ قَوْمِي أَنَّ حِرْفَتِي لَمْ تَكُنَ لِتَعْجِزَ عَنْ مَئُونَةِ أَهْلِي، وَقَدْ شُغِلْتُ فِي أُمُورِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ، فَسَأَتَحَرَّفُ لِلْمُسْلِمِينَ فِي أُمُورٍ، وَسَيَأْكُلُ آلُ أَبِي بَكْرٍ مِنْ هَذَا الْمَالِ»

Entry20049/ 14
Jāmiʿ Maʿmar ibn Rāshid · p. 105
English
Isnad

'Abd al-Razzaq reported: Ma'mar informed us from al-Zuhri, from 'Umar b. Muhammad, from Muhammad b. Jubayr b. Mut'im, from his father, who said:

Matn

As the Messenger of God ﷺ returned from the campaign of Hunayn, Bedouins followed him asking for gifts. They pressed him against a thorn tree, which snatched his cloak as he sat on his mount. He said: “Give me back my cloak! Do you fear I am miserly? By God, if I had livestock equal in number to these thorn trees I would distribute it among you, and you would find me neither miserly, cowardly, nor a liar.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ عُمَرَ بْنِ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرِ بْنِ مُطْعِمٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، قَالَ:

Matn

لَمَّا قَفَلَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ مِنْ غَزْوَةِ حُنَيْنٍ، تَبِعَهُ الْأَعْرَابُ يَسْأَلُونَهُ، فَأَلْجَئُوهُ إِلَى سَمُرَةٍ، فَخَطَفَتْ رِدَاءَهُ وَهُوَ عَلَى رَاحِلَتِهِ، فَقَالَ: «رُدُّوا عَلَيَّ رِدَائِي، §أَتَخْشَوْنَ عَلَيَّ الْبُخْلَ، فَوَاللَّهِ لَوْ كَانَ لِي عَدَدُ هَذِهِ الْعِضَاهِ نَعَمًا لَقَسَمْتُهُ بَيْنَكُمْ، ثُمَّ لَا تَجِدُونِي بَخِيلًا، وَلَا جَبَانًا، وَلَا كَذَّابًا»
