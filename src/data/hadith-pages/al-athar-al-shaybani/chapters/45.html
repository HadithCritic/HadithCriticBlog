Entry145/ 13
Kitāb al-Āthār of al-Shaybānī · Vol. 1, p. 374
English
Isnad

Muhammad said Abu Hanifah reported to us from Hammad,

Matn

who said: I asked Ibrahim about the prayer before Maghrib, and he forbade me from it, saying, "The Prophet, ﷺ, Abu Bakr, and Umar, ؓ, did not pray it." Muhammad said: We adopt this position. When the sun has set, there is no prayer for a funeral or otherwise before the Maghrib prayer. This is the opinion of Abu Hanifah, ؓ.

العربية
Isnad

مُحَمَّدٌ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنَا أَبُو حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ،

Matn

قَالَ سَأَلْتُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ عَنِ الصَّلَاةِ قَبْلَ الْمَغْرِبِ فَنَهَانِي عَنْهَا، وَقَالَ: «إِنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَبَا بَكْرٍ وَعُمَرَ رضي الله عنهما لَمْ يُصَلُّوهَا» قَالَ مُحَمَّدٌ: وَبِهِ نَأْخُذُ، إِذَا غَابَتِ الشَّمْسُ فَلَا صَلَاةَ عَلَى جَنَازَةٍ وَلَا غَيْرِهَا قَبْلَ صَلَاةِ الْمَغْرِبِ، وَهُوَ قَوْلُ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ

Entry146/ 13
Kitāb al-Āthār of al-Shaybānī · Vol. 1, p. 377
English
Isnad

Muhammad said Abu Hanifah reported to us from Hammad, from Ibrahim, who said:

Matn

If the blood is the size of a dirham, or urine or other [impurities], then repeat your prayer. But if it is less than the size of a dirham, then continue with your prayer. Muhammad said: His prayer is valid until that [impurity] is more than the size of the large dirham, which is the mithqal. If it is that much, his prayer is not valid. This is the opinion of Abu Hanifah, ؓ.

العربية
Isnad

مُحَمَّدٌ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنَا أَبُو حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ،

Matn

قَالَ «إِذَا كَانَ الدَّمُ قَدْرَ الدِّرْهَمِ وَالْبَوْلُ وَغَيْرُهُ، فَأَعِدْ صَلَاتَكَ، وَإِنْ كَانَ أَقَلَّ مِنْ قَدْرِ الدِّرْهَمِ، فَامْضِ عَلَى صَلَاتِكَ» وَقَالَ مُحَمَّدٌ: يُجْزِئُهُ صَلَاتُهُ حَتَّى يَكُونَ ذَلِكَ أَكْثَرَ مِنْ قَدْرِ الدِّرْهَمِ الْكَبِيرِ الْمِثْقَالَ فَإِذَا كَانَ كَذَلِكَ لَمْ تُجْزِئْهُ صَلَاتُهُ، وَهُوَ قَوْلُ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ

English
Isnad

Muhammad said Abu Hanifah informed us, saying: Ali b. al-Aqmar narrated

Matn

that the Prophet, ﷺ, passed by a man who was letting his garment hang loose during prayer, so he tucked it up for him. Muhammad said: We adopt this position. Letting the garment hang loose during prayer, whether on the shirt or otherwise, is disliked because it resembles the practice of the People of the Book. This is the opinion of Abu Hanifah, ؓ.

English
Isnad

Muhammad said Abu Hanifah informed us, saying: Abdul-Malik b. Umayr narrated to us from Qaza'ah, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, ؓ, from the Prophet, ﷺ, that he said:

Matn

"There is no prayer after the morning prayer until the sun rises, and no prayer after the afternoon prayer until the sun sets. These two days are not to be fasted: Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha. Journeys should not be undertaken except to three mosques: the Sacred Mosque, my mosque, and the Farthest Mosque. A woman should not travel except with a mahram from among her relatives." Muhammad said: We adhere to all of this, and it is not proper for a woman to travel except with her husband or with a mahram from among her relatives. This is the opinion of Abu Hanifah, ؓ.

العربية
Isnad

مُحَمَّدٌ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنَا أَبُو حَنِيفَةَ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْمَلِكِ بْنُ عُمَيْرٍ، عَنْ قَزَعَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيِّ رضي الله عنه عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم، أَنَّهُ قَالَ : "

Matn

لَا صَلَاةَ بَعْدَ الْغَدَاةِ حَتَّى تَطْلُعَ الشَّمْسُ، وَلَا صَلَاةَ بَعْدَ الْعَصْرِ حَتَّى تَغْرُبَ ، وَلَا يُصَامُ هَذَانِ الْيَوْمَانِ: الْفِطْرُ وَالْأَضْحَى، وَلَا تُشَدُّ الرِّحَالُ إِلَّا إِلَى ثَلَاثَةِ مَسَاجِدَ: الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ، وَمَسْجِدِي وَالْمَسْجِدِ الْأَقْصَى، وَلَا تُسَافِرُ الْمَرْأَةُ إِلَّا مَعَ ذِي مَحْرَمٍ مِنْهَا " قَالَ مُحَمَّدٌ: وَبِهَذَا كُلِّهِ نَأْخُذُ، وَلَا يَنْبَغِي لِلْمَرْأَةِ أَنْ تُسَافِرَ إِلَّا مَعَ زَوْجِهَا أَوْ مَعَ ذِي مَحْرَمٍ مِنْهَا. وَهُوَ قَوْلُ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ

English
Isnad

Muhammad said Abu Hanifah reported to us from Hammad, from Ibrahim that he "

Matn

disapproved of cracking one's fingers during prayer, or letting one's cloak fall from the shoulders, or placing one's hand on the hip, or burying large pebbles, or squatting on the heels, or fidgeting with one's beard." Muhammad said: We adopt this position because it is fidgeting during prayer that distracts from it. This is also the opinion of Abu Hanifah, ؓ.

العربية
Isnad

مُحَمَّدٌ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنَا أَبُو حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ أَنَّهُ «

Matn

كَرِهَ أَنْ يُفَرْقِعَ أَصَابِعَهُ فِي الصَّلَاةِ أَوْ يُلْقِيَ رِدَاءَهُ عَنْ مَنْكِبَيْهِ، أَوْ يَضَعَ يَدَهُ عَلَى خَاصِرَتِهِ، أَوْ يَدْفِنَ كِبَارَ الْحَصَى أَوْ يُقْعِيَ عَلَى عَقِبَيْهِ أَوْ يَعْبَثَ بِلِحْيَتِهِ» قَالَ مُحَمَّدٌ: وَبِهِ نَأْخُذُ، لِأَنَّهُ عَبَثٌ فِي الصَّلَاةِ يُشْغِلُ عَنْهَا. وَهُوَ قَوْلُ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ

English
Isnad

Muhammad said Abu Hanifah reported to us from Hammad, from Ibrahim,

Matn

that Umar b. al-Khattab, ؓ, led his companions in the Maghrib prayer but did not recite anything in any of its rak'ahs until he finished. His companions said to him, "What prevented you from reciting, O Commander of the Faithful?" He replied, "Did I not do so? I was preparing a camel caravan for Syria that evening and kept moving it from one stage of the journey to the next until it reached Syria." He repeated this, and his companions also repeated their question. Muhammad said: We adopt this position, and it is the view of Abu Hanifah, ؓ.

Matn

أَنَّ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ رضي الله عنه صَلَّى بِأَصْحَابِهِ الْمَغْرِبَ، فَلَمْ يَقْرَأْ فِي شَيْءٍ مِنْهَا حَتَّى انْصَرَفَ ، فَقَالَ لَهُ أَصْحَابُهُ: مَا مَنَعَكَ أَنْ تَقْرَأَ يَا أَمِيرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ؟ قَالَ: «أَوَ مَا فَعَلْتُ إِنِّي جَهَّزْتُ عِيرًا الْعَشِيَّةَ إِلَى الشَّامِ، فَلَمْ أَزَلْ أَرْحَلُهَا مَنْقَلَةً مَنْقَلَةً حَتَّى وَرَدَتِ الشَّامَ . فَأَعَادَ وَأَعَادَ أَصْحَابُهُ» قَالَ مُحَمَّدٌ: وَبِهِ نَأْخُذُ، وَهُوَ قَوْلُ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ

Kitāb al-Āthār of al-Shaybānī · Vol. 1, p. 404
English
Isnad

Muhammad said Abu Hanifah reported to us from Hammad, from Ibrahim, who said:

Matn

If you join a people’s prayer while you do not intend their prayer, it will not suffice you. Even if the imam intends the prayer and those behind him intend something else, it will suffice the imam but will not suffice them. Muhammad said: We adopt this, and it is the opinion of Abu Hanifah, ؓ.

Kitāb al-Āthār of al-Shaybānī · Vol. 1, p. 405
English
Isnad

Muhammad said Abu Hanifah reported to us from Hammad, from Ibrahim, who said: "

Matn

I would not consider a man's prayer when the sun reddens worth two pennies." Muhammad said: Prayer at that time is disliked, except if one has missed the afternoon prayer on that day, then let him pray it at that time. As for other obligatory prayers and voluntary prayers, it is not appropriate for him to perform them. This is the opinion of Abu Hanifah, ؓ.

English
Isnad

Muhammad said Abu Hanifah reported to us from Hammad, from Ibrahim, who said:

Matn

If the blood on your body or on your garment is the size of a dirham, repeat your prayer. If it is less than that, proceed with your prayer. Muhammad said: Blood on the garment and on the body are the same—if it is more than the size of a large dirham (mithqal), repeat the prayer. This is the opinion of Abu Hanifah, ؓ.

Kitāb al-Āthār of al-Shaybānī · Vol. 1, p. 409
English
Isnad

Muhammad said Abu Hanifah informed us, saying: Asim b. Abi al-Najud narrated to us, from Abu Razin, from Abdullah b. Mas'ud, ؓ,

Matn

that he "took a louse during prayer and buried it, then said: 'Have We not made the earth a receptacle for the living and the dead?'" Muhammad said: We adopt this view—we see no harm in killing a louse and burying it during prayer, and this is the opinion of Abu Hanifah, ؓ.

English
Isnad

Muhammad said Abu Hanifah reported to us from Hammad, who said:

Matn

I asked Ibrahim about "a man who slaughters a sheep while in a state of ablution, and blood touches his hand." He said: "He should wash what the blood touched, but he does not need to repeat the ablution." Muhammad said: We adopt this position, and it is the opinion of Abu Hanifah, ؓ.
