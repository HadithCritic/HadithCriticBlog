About the edition.

Edition. ed. Abū al-Wafāʾ al-Afghānī (Lajnat Iḥyāʾ al-Maʿārif al-Nuʿmāniyya, Hyderabad)

Coverage. The supplied export contains 269 reports in 79 chapters, which are the first 79 of the 306 chapter headings in the printed contents index. The remaining chapters are not part of the supplied source and are not shown here.

English translation. The English is the working translation supplied with the source export. The export names no translator. 268 of 269 reports are supplied bilingual pairs; for the remaining report the export had no English and the English shown was translated from the Arabic. All reports had their isnad and matn boundaries reviewed by hand, but the edition is not certified and was not collated against the printed text.

Numbering. Reports carry their printed numbers 1 to 268. One report in chapter 10 is unnumbered in the source and is labeled Unnumbered.

Transmitters. Transmitter names are shown as written in each report.
