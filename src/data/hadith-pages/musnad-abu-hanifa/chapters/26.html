Isnad

From Hammad, from Ibrahim, from al-Aswad, from 'A'isha ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

"If gentleness were a creature that could be seen, no creature of God Almighty seen would be more beautiful than it; and if harshness (khuruq) were a creature that could be seen, no creature of God Almighty seen would be uglier than it."

العربية
Isnad

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «لَوْ أَنَّ الرِّفْقَ خَلْقٌ يُرَى، لَمَا رُئِيَ مِنْ خَلْقِ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى خَلْقٌ أَحْسَنُ مِنْهُ، وَلَوْ أَنَّ الْخَرَقَ خَلْقٌ يُرَى، لَمَا رُئِيَ مِنْ خَلْقِ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى خَلْقٌ أَقْبَحُ مِنْهُ»

Entry8/ 32
Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 26
English
Isnad

From Muhammad, from Anas, who said:

Matn

"The Messenger of God ﷺ never stretched out his knee in front of one sitting with him; rather he ﷺ would sit level with them. No one ever took his hand and he left it, until that person was the one to let go. No one ever sat with the Messenger of God ﷺ and rose before he himself rose. And I never found anything more fragrant than the scent of the Messenger of God ﷺ." In another narration, he said: "No man ever stood with the Messenger of God ﷺ for a need and departed before him, until [the Prophet] was the one to depart." In another narration: The Messenger of God ﷺ "when he shook hands with anyone, would not let go of his hand unless the other was the one who let go."

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ مُحَمَّدٍ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، قَالَ:

Matn

«مَا أَخْرَجَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ رُكْبَتَهُ بَيْنَ يَدَيْ جَلِيسٍ لَهُ قَطُّ، بَلْ يَقْعُدُ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ مُسَاوِيًا لَهُمْ، وَلَا تَنَاوَلَ أَحَدٌ يَدَهُ، فَتَرَكَهَا قَطُّ حَتَّى يَكُونَ يَدَعُهَا، وَمَا جَلَسَ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ أَحَدٌ قَطُّ، فَقَامَ حَتَّى يَقُومَ قَبْلَهُ، وَمَا وَجَدْتُ شَيْئًا قَطُّ أَطْيَبَ مِنْ رِيحِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ»، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ، قَالَ: «وَمَا قَامَ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ رَجُلٌ فِي حَاجَةٍ فَانْصَرَفَ قَبْلَهُ، حَتَّى يَكُونَ هُوَ الْمُنْصَرِفَ». وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ: كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «إِذَا صَافَحَ أَحَدًا لَا يَتْرُكُ يَدَهُ، إِلَّا أَنْ يَكُونَ هُوَ الَّذِي يَتْرُكُ»

Isnad

From 'Alqama, from Ibn Burayda, from his father, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

"Whoever does not accept the excuse of a Muslim who apologizes to him, his burden is like the burden of the sahib maks." They said: "O Messenger, what is a sahib maks?" He said: "A tax collector ('ashshar)."

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ عَلْقَمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ بُرَيْدَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، قَالَ:

Matn

Isnad

From 'Asim, from Abu Burda:

Matn

"The Prophet ﷺ visited some of the Ansar in their homes. They slaughtered a sheep for him and made food from it. He took some of the meat and chewed it, chewing it for a while without being able to swallow it. He said: 'What is the matter with this meat?' They said: 'It is a sheep belonging to so-and-so; we slaughtered it [intending] that when he comes we will satisfy him with its price.' The Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'Feed it to the captives.'" In another narration, from 'Asim b. Kulayb, from his father: A man from the companions of Muhammad ﷺ "made food and invited him. The Prophet ﷺ stood to go to him, and we stood with him. When the food was set down, the Prophet ﷺ took a morsel of that meat and chewed it in his mouth for a long time, unable to eat it, so he cast it out of his mouth and held back from the food. When we saw that from the Prophet ﷺ, we held back as well. The Prophet ﷺ called the host and said: 'Tell me about this meat, where is it from?' He said: 'O Messenger of God, it is a sheep that belonged to a friend of ours. We did not have [a sheep] to buy, so we hurried, slaughtered it, and prepared it for you, [intending] that when he comes we would give him its price.' The Prophet ﷺ ordered the food removed and ordered that it be fed to the captives." 'Abd al-Wahid said: I said to Abu Hanifa: "From where did you take this [ruling]: that a man who works with another man's money without his permission gives the profit in charity?" He said: "I took it from the hadith of 'Asim."
