Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 4
English
Isnad

From Hammad, from Ibrahim, from 'Abd Allah, from Abu Dharr ؓ:

Matn

That he prayed a prayer and made it brief, and made much bowing and prostration. When he finished, a man said to him: "You are a Companion of the Messenger of God ﷺ, and you pray this prayer?" Abu Dharr said: "Did I not complete the bowing and prostration?" He said: "Yes." He said: "Indeed I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: 'Whoever prostrates one prostration to God, he is raised by it one degree in Paradise,' so I loved that degrees be given to me, or that degrees be recorded for me." In a narration, from Ibrahim, from one who narrated to him: That he passed by Abu Dharr ؓ, at al-Rabadha while he was praying a light prayer in which he made much bowing and prostration. When Abu Dharr gave the salam, the man said to him: "You pray this prayer, when you have accompanied the Messenger of God ﷺ?!" Abu Dharr said: "I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: 'Whoever prostrates one prostration to God Almighty raises him by it one degree in Paradise.'" "For that reason I make many prostrations in it."

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنْ أَبِي ذَرٍّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا

Matn

أَنَّهُ صَلَّى صَلَاةً فَخَفَّفَهَا، وَأَكْثَرَ الرُّكُوعَ وَالسُّجُودَ، فَلَمَّا انْصَرَفَ، قَالَ لَهُ رَجُلٌ: أَنْتَ صَاحِبُ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ وَتُصَلِّي هَذِهِ الصَّلَاةَ؟ فَقَالَ أَبُو ذَرٍّ: أَلَمْ أُتِمَّ الرُّكُوعَ وَالسُّجُودَ؟ قَالَ: بَلَى، قَالَ: فَإِنِّي سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، يَقُولُ: «مَنْ سَجَدَ لِلَّهِ سَجْدَةً رُفِعَ بِهَا دَرَجَةً فِي الْجَنَّةِ»، فَأَحْبَبْتُ أَنْ تُؤْتَى دَرَجَاتٌ، أَوْ تُكْتَبُ لِي دَرَجَاتٌ، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ مَنْ حَدَّثَهُ: أَنَّهُ مَرَّ بِأَبِي ذَرٍّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ بِالْرَبْذَةِ، وَهُوَ يُصَلِّي صَلَاةً خَفِيفَةً، يُكْثِرُ فِيهَا الرُّكُوعَ وَالسُّجُودَ، فَلَمَّا سَلَّمَ أَبُو ذَرٍّ، قَالَ لَهُ الرَّجُلُ: تُصَلِّي هَذِهِ الصَّلَاةَ، وَقَدْ صَحِبْتَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ؟! فَقَالَ أَبُو ذَرٍّ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، يَقُولُ: «مَنْ سَجَدَ لِلَّهِ سَجْدَةً رَفَعَهُ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى بِهَا دَرَجَةً فِي الْجَنَّةِ». فَلِذَلِكَ أُكْثِرُ فِيهَا السُّجُودَ

Entry4/ 116
Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 4
English
Isnad

Abu Qurra said: Ibn Jurayj mentioned, from 'Abd al-Rahman, that a man said:

Matn

"O Messenger of God, may a man pray in a single garment?" The Prophet ﷺ said: "Does each of you have two garments?!" Abu Qurra said: I heard Abu Hanifa mention, from al-Zuhri, from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, from Abu Hurayra, that he asked the Prophet about praying in a single garment, and the Prophet ﷺ said: "Not every one of you finds two garments."

العربية

أَبُو قُرَّةَ , قَالَ: ذَكَرَ ابْنُ جُرَيْجٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ أَنَّ رَجُلًا، قَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، يُصَلِّي الرَّجُلُ فِي الثَّوْبِ الْوَاحِدِ؟ فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «أَوَلِكُلِّكُمْ ثَوْبَانِ؟!» قَالَ أَبُو قُرَّةَ: فَسَمِعْتُ أَبَا حَنِيفَةَ يَذْكُرُ , عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ الْمُسَيِّبِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّهُ سَأَلَ النَّبِيَّ عَنِ الصّلَاةِ فِي الثَّوْبِ الْوَاحِدِ، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «لَيْسَ كُلُّكُمْ يَجِدُ ثَوْبَيْنِ»

Isnad

From Shayban, from Yahya, from Burayda ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

"Hasten with the 'Asr prayer." In a narration: from Burayda al-Aslami ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Hasten with the 'Asr prayer on a cloudy day, for whoever misses the 'Asr prayer until the sun sets, his deed has been voided."

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «بَكِّرُوا بِصَلَاةِ الْعَصْرِ»، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ: عَنْ بُرَيْدَةَ الْأَسْلَمِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «بَكِّرُوا بِصَلَاةِ الْعَصْرِ فِي يَوْمِ غَيْمٍ، فَإِنَّ مَنْ فَاتَتْهُ صَلَاةُ الْعَصْرِ حَتَّى تَغْرُبَ الشَّمْسُ، فَقَدْ حَبِطَ عَمَلُهُ»

Entry10/ 116
Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 4
English
Isnad

From 'Abd al-Malik, from Qaza'a, from Abu Sa'id ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

"There is no prayer after the morning prayer until the sun rises, and no prayer after 'Asr until it sets; and these two days are not to be fasted: al-Adha and al-Fitr; and saddles are not to be tied [for journeys] except to three mosques: the Sacred Mosque, the Aqsa Mosque, and this mosque of mine; and a woman does not travel two days except with a mahram."

العربية
Isnad

عنْ عَبْدِ الْمَلِكِ، عَنْ قَزْعَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: "لَا صَلَاةَ بَعْدَ الْغَدَاةِ حَتَّى تَطْلُعَ الشَّمْسُ، وَلَا صَلَاةَ بَعْدَ الْعَصْرِ حَتَّى تَغِيبَ، وَلَا يُصَامُ هَذَانِ الْيَوْمَانِ: الْأَضْحَى وَالْفِطْرُ، وَلَا تُشَدُّ الرِّحَالُ إِلَّا إِلَى ثَلَاثَةِ مَسَاجِدَ: إِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ، وَالْمَسْجِدِ الْأَقْصَى، وَإِلَى مَسْجِدِي هَذَا، وَلَا تُسَافِرُ الْمَرْأَةُ يَوْمَيْنِ إِلَّا مَعَ ذِي مَحْرَمٍ"

Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 4
English
Isnad

From 'Alqama, from Ibn Burayda:

Matn

That a man of the Ansar passed by the Messenger of God ﷺ, and saw him sorrowful. The man, when he ate, people would gather to him. He went off sorrowful at what he had seen of the Messenger of God ﷺ, and left his food and those who used to gather to him, and entered his mosque to pray. While he was thus, he dozed off, and a visitor came to him in sleep and said: "Do you know the sorrow of the Messenger of God ﷺ?" He said: "No." He said: "It is on account of this announcing [of prayer]. Go to him and tell him to command Bilal to call the adhan for God." So he taught him the adhan: "God is greatest, God is greatest" twice; "I bear witness that there is no god but God" twice; "I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of God" twice; "Come to prayer" twice; "Come to success" twice; "God is greatest, God is greatest; there is no god but God." Then he taught him the iqama likewise, and said in another [form]: "The prayer has been established, the prayer has been established; God is greatest, God is greatest; there is no god but God," like the adhan of the people and their iqama. The Ansari went and sat at the door of the Prophet ﷺ. Abu Bakr ؓ passed by and said: "Ask permission for me," for he had seen the like of that, and he informed the Prophet ﷺ, of it. Then the Ansari asked permission, entered, and informed him of what he had seen. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Abu Bakr has already told us the like of that." So he commanded Bilal to call the adhan thus. In a narration: "That a man of the Ansar passed by the Messenger of God ﷺ, and saw him sorrowful. The man was one with food, at which supper was served. He turned back because of what he saw of the sorrow of the Messenger of God ﷺ, and left his food, and entered his mosque to pray. While he was thus, he dozed off, and a visitor came to him in sleep and said to him: 'Do you know what has saddened the Messenger of God ﷺ?' He said: 'No.' He said: 'It is the call [to prayer]; go to him that he command Bilal.' The man said: 'So I was taught the adhan: "God is greatest, God is greatest" twice; "I bear witness that there is no god but God" twice; "I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of God" twice; "Come to prayer" twice; "Come to success" twice; "God is greatest, God is greatest; there is no god but God." Then he taught me the iqama likewise, then said at its end: "The prayer has been established" twice, like the adhan of the people and their iqama.' The Ansari woke up, came to the Messenger of God ﷺ, and sat at the door. Abu Bakr ؓ came, and the Ansari said: 'Ask permission for me.' Abu Bakr entered and informed the Messenger of God ﷺ, of the like of that. Then the Ansari entered and informed the Prophet ﷺ, of what he had seen. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'Abu Bakr has already told us.' He said: 'Command Bilal [to do] the like of that.'"

Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 4
English
Isnad

From Hammad, from Ibrahim, that he said regarding Wa'il b. Hujr:

Matn

"And a Bedouin who had never prayed a prayer with the Prophet ﷺ, before it, he is more knowledgeable than 'Abd Allah and his Companions; he memorized and they did not memorize," meaning: the raising of the hands. In a narration: That he mentioned the hadith of Wa'il b. Hujr and said: "A Bedouin; I do not know that he prayed a prayer with the Prophet ﷺ, before it; he is more knowledgeable than 'Abd Allah." In a narration: The hadith of Wa'il b. Hujr was mentioned in his presence: That he saw the Prophet ﷺ, "raise his hands at bowing and at prostration." He said: "He is a Bedouin who does not know the laws of Islam; he did not pray with the Prophet ﷺ except one prayer." "And one whom I cannot count has narrated to me from 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud ؓ: that he raised his hands only at the beginning of the prayer, and he related it from the Prophet ﷺ." Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 4
English
Isnad

From Hammad, from Abu Wa'il Shaqiq b. Salama, from 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud ؓ, who said:

Matn

"When we prayed behind the Prophet ﷺ, we would say: 'Peace be upon God.'" In a narration, an addition from 'Ibada [sic]: "Peace be upon Gabriel and Michael." The Prophet ﷺ turned to us and said: "God is Peace. So when one of you recites the tashahhud, let him say: Greetings are for God, and the prayers and the good things. Peace be upon you, O Prophet, and the mercy of God and His blessings. Peace be upon us and upon the righteous servants of God. I bear witness that there is no god but God, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger." In a narration: That they used to say: "Peace be upon God, peace be upon Gabriel, peace be upon the Messenger of God," and the Messenger of God said: "Do not say: 'Peace be upon Peace,' but say: Greetings are for God, and the prayers and the good things" … to the end of the tashahhud. In a narration: That the Messenger of God ﷺ, "taught them the greetings to the end of the tashahhud." In a narration: "He taught us." In a narration, he said: When we prayed with the Prophet ﷺ, we would say, when we sat at the end of the prayer: "Peace be upon God, peace be upon the Messenger of God, and upon angels whom we would name among the angels." The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Do not say thus, but say: Greetings are for God, and the prayers and the good things."

العربية

عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ أَبِي وَاثِلَةَ شَقِيقِ بْنِ سَلَمَةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، قَالَ: "كُنَّا إِذَا صَلَّيْنَا خَلْفَ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، نَقُولُ: السَّلَامُ عَلَى اللَّهِ". وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ زِيَادَةٌ مِنْ عِبَادَةٍ: السَّلَامُ عَلَى جِبرِيلَ وَمِيكَائِيلَ، فَأَقْبَلَ عَلَيْنَا النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، فَقَالَ: "إِنَّ اللَّهَ هُوَ السَّلَامُ، فَإِذَا تَشَهَّدَ أَحَدُكُمْ، فَلْيَقُلْ: التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَى عِبَادِ اللَّهِ الصَّالِحِينَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنَّ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ"، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ: أَنَّهُمْ كَانُوا يَقُولُونَ: السَّلَامُ عَلَى اللَّهِ، السَّلَامُ عَلَى جِبْرِيلَ، السَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ: "لَا تَقُولُوا: السَّلَامُ عَلَى السَّلَامِ، وَلَكِنْ قُولُوا: التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ" … إِلَى آخِرِ التَّشَهُّدِ. وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ: أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ «عَلَّمَهُمُ التَّحِيَّاتِ إِلَى آخِرِ التَّشَهُّدِ». وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ: عَلَّمَنَا. وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ، قَالَ: كُنَّا إِذَا صَلَّيْنَا مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، نَقُولُ إِذَا جَلَسْنَا فِي آخِرِ الصَّلَاةِ: السَّلَامُ عَلَى اللَّهِ، السَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، وَعَلَى مَلَائِكَةٍ نُسَمِّيهِمْ مِنَ الْمَلَائِكَةِ، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: "لَا تَقُولُوا كَذَا، وَقُولُوا: التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ"

Isnad

From Hammad, from Ibrahim, from al-Aswad, from 'A'isha ؓ:

Matn

That when the Prophet ﷺ fell ill with the illness in which he was taken, he felt some relief from the pain. When prayer time came, he said to 'A'isha ؓ: "Order Abu Bakr to lead the people in prayer." So she sent to Abu Bakr ؓ [saying] that the Messenger of God ﷺ commands you to lead the people in prayer. He sent to her: "O my daughter, I am an old man, tender-hearted, and whenever I do not see the Messenger of God ﷺ, in his place, I become tender for that. So you and Hafsa ؓ assemble before the Messenger of God ﷺ, that he send for 'Umar ؓ, and let him lead them." She did so, and the Messenger of God ﷺ said: "You are the companions of Joseph; order Abu Bakr to lead the people in prayer." When the call to prayer was made, the Prophet ﷺ heard the mu'adhdhin saying "Come to prayer," and the Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Lift me up." 'A'isha said: "I have ordered Abu Bakr to lead the people in prayer, and you are excused." He said: "Lift me up, for the delight of my eye has been placed in prayer." 'A'isha said: He was lifted between two [men], his feet dragging on the ground. When Abu Bakr heard the sound of the Messenger of God ﷺ he stepped back, and the Messenger of God ﷺ gestured to him. The Prophet ﷺ sat to the left of Abu Bakr ؓ, and the Prophet ﷺ was beside him saying the takbir, and Abu Bakr said the takbir with the Prophet's takbir, and the people said the takbir with Abu Bakr's takbir until he finished. Then he did not lead the people in prayer other than that prayer until he was taken. And Abu Bakr was the imam, while the Prophet ﷺ was in pain, until he was taken.

Isnad

From 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib, from his father, from Ibn 'Amr, who said:

Matn

The sun was eclipsed on the day Ibrahim, the son of the Messenger of God ﷺ died, and the people said: "The sun was eclipsed for the death of Ibrahim." The Prophet ﷺ stood a long standing until they thought he would not bow. Then he bowed, and his bowing was equal to his standing; then he raised his head, and his standing was equal to his bowing; then he prostrated equal to his standing, and his sitting between the two prostrations was equal to his prostration; then he prostrated equal to his sitting. Then he prayed the second rak'a and did likewise, until, when it was the prostration of it, he wept, and his weeping intensified, and we heard him saying: "Did You not promise me that You would not punish them while I am among them?" Then he sat and recited the tashahhud, then turned and faced them, and said: "The sun and the moon are two signs among the signs of God, with which God Almighty frightens His servants; they are not eclipsed for the death of anyone nor for his life. So when it is thus, hold to prayer. I saw myself brought near to Paradise so that, had I wished to take a branch from the branches of its trees, I would have done so; and I saw myself brought near to the Fire so that I began to guard myself; and I saw in it the thief of the Messenger of God ﷺ." In a narration: "the thief of the house of the Messenger of God ﷺ being punished in the Fire; and I saw in it 'Abd b. Da'da', the thief of the pilgrims with his crook; and I saw in it a tawny Himyarite woman being punished over a cat of hers which she had tied, neither feeding it nor leaving it to eat from the vermin and insects of the earth." Da'da', the thief of the pilgrim with his crook; when [it] was concealed he would go off, and if someone saw him, he would say: it only caught on my crook." From Hammad, from Ibrahim, from 'Alqama, from 'Abd Allah ؓ, who said:

Matn

"The Messenger of God ﷺ used to teach us istikhara in a matter as he taught us a sura of the Qur'an." In a narration, he said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "When one of you intends a matter, let him perform ablution and pray two rak'as other than the obligatory prayer, then let him say: O God, I seek Your choice by Your knowledge, and I seek ability by Your power, and I ask You of Your bounty; for You know and I do not know, You are able and I am not able, and You are the Knower of the unseen. O God, if this matter is good for me in my livelihood and good for me in the outcome of my affair, then make it easy for me and bless me in it." And he added in a narration: "And if it is other than that, decree for me the good wherever it may be, then make me content with it."

Isnad

From 'Alqama, from Ibn al-Aqmar, from Humran, who said:

Matn

I never met Ibn 'Umar but that the closest of people in seating was Humran. One day he said: "O Humran, I do not see you attending us except that you want good for yourself?" He said: "Yes, O Abu 'Abd al-Rahman." He said: "As for two, I forbid you from them; as for one, I command you with it, for I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ command it." He said: "What are these three qualities, O Abu 'Abd al-Rahman?" Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 4
English
Isnad

From 'Alqama, from Ibn Burayda, from his father, who said:

Matn

We went out with the Prophet ﷺ, in a funeral procession, and he came to the grave of his mother, weeping with the most intense weeping, until his soul almost left from between his sides. He said: We said: "O Messenger of God, what makes you weep?" He said: "I asked my Lord's permission to visit the grave of Muhammad's mother, and He permitted me; and I asked His permission for intercession, and He refused me." In a narration, he said: "The Prophet ﷺ asked permission to visit the grave of his mother and was permitted. He set out and the Muslims set out with him until they reached a place near the grave. The Muslims stayed, and the Prophet ﷺ went on and stayed a long while; then his weeping intensified until we thought he would not calm down. He came back weeping, and 'Umar said to him: 'What made you weep, O Prophet of God, my father and mother be your ransom?' He said: 'I asked my Lord's permission to visit the grave of my mother, and He permitted me; and I asked His permission for intercession, and He refused. So I wept in mercy for her.' And the Muslims wept in mercy for the Prophet ﷺ."

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ عَلْقَمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ بُرَيْدَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، قَالَ:

Matn

خَرَجْنَا مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فِي جَنَازَةٍ، فَأَتَى قَبْرَ أُمِّهِ، وَهُوَ يَبْكِي أَشَدَّ الْبُكَاءِ، حَتَّى كَادَتْ نَفْسُهُ تَخْرُجُ مِنْ بَيْنِ جَنْبَيْهِ، قَالَ: قُلْنَا: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، مَا يُبْكِيكَ؟ قَالَ: «اسْتَأْذَنْتُ رَبِّي فِي زِيَارَةِ قَبْرِ أُمِّ مُحَمَّدٍ، فَأَذِنَ لِي، وَاسْتَأْذَنْتُهُ فِي الشَّفَاعَةِ فَأَبَى عَلَيَّ»، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ، قَالَ: "اسْتَأْذَنَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فِي زِيَارَةِ قَبْرِ أُمِّهِ، فَأُذِنَ لَهُ، فَانْطَلَقَ وَانْطَلَقَ مَعَهُ الْمُسْلِمُونَ حَتَّى انْتَهَوْا إِلَى قَرِيبٍ مِنَ الْقَبْرِ، فَمَكَثَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ، وَمَضَى النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فَمَكَثَ طَوِيلًا، ثُمَّ اشْتَدَّ بُكَاؤُهُ حَتَّى ظَنَنَّا أَنَّهُ لَا يَسْكُنُ، فَأَقْبَلَ، وَهُوَ يَبْكِي، فَقَالَ لَهُ عُمَرُ: مَا أَبْكَاكَ يَا نَبِيَّ اللَّهِ بِأَبِي أَنْتَ وَأُمِّي؟ قَالَ: اسْتَأْذَنْتُ رَبِّي فِي زِيَارَةِ قَبْرِ أُمِّي، فَأَذِنَ لِي، وَاسْتَأْذَنْتُهُ فِي الشَّفَاعَةِ، فَأَبَى، فَبَكَيْتُ رَحْمَةً لَهَا، وَبَكَى الْمُسْلِمُونَ رَحْمَةً لِلنَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ"

Isnad

From 'Alqama, from Ibn Burayda, from his father, who said:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ, when he went out to the graveyards, would say: "Peace be upon the people of the abodes among the Muslims, and we, God willing, will join you; we ask God for well-being for us and for you."
