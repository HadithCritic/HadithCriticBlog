Isnad

From al-Haytham and Rabi'a, from Anas ؓ:

Matn

"The Messenger of God ﷺ was taken [in death] at the age of sixty-three, Abu Bakr was taken at the age of sixty-three, and 'Umar was taken at the age of sixty-three."

Matn

Isnad

From Yahya b. Sa'id, from Anas ؓ, who said:

Matn

"The Messenger of God ﷺ was sent [as a prophet] at the head of forty years. He stayed in Mecca ten years and in Medina ten years. The Messenger of God ﷺ died with not twenty white hairs in his beard and head."

Isnad

عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، قَالَ:

Isnad

From Ibrahim, from Anas b. Malik ؓ, who said:

Matn

"I never touched with my hand any khazz [silk-wool fabric] or silk softer than the palm of the Messenger of God ﷺ." In another narration, that is, from the Imam [Abu Hanifa], from Ibrahim b. Muhammad b. al-Muntashir, from Anas, as in the commentary, he said: "The Messenger of God ﷺ was never seen stretching out his knees in front of one sitting with him."

Matn

«مَا مَسَسْتُ بِيَدِي خَزًّا وَلَا حَرِيرًا أَلْيَنَ مِنْ كَفِّ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ»، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ: أَيْ عَنِ الْإِمَامِ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ بْنِ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ الْمُنْتَشِرِ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ كَمَا فِي الشَّرْحِ، قَالَ: «مَا رُؤِيَ رَسُولُ اللَّهُ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ مَادًّا رُكْبَتَيْهِ بَيْنَ جَلِيسٍ لَهُ قَطُّ»

Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 21
English
Isnad

From Hammad, from Ibrahim, from al-Aswad, from 'A'isha ؓ:

Matn

"When the Prophet ﷺ fell ill with the illness in which he was taken, he asked to be released [from his rotation] so as to be in my house, and they [his wives] released him. She said: When I heard that, I got up hurriedly and swept my house, and I had no servant, and I spread for him a bed whose cushion was stuffed with idhkhir [a fragrant grass]. Then the Messenger of God ﷺ came, supported between two men, until he was laid on my bed."

العربية
Isnad

Matn

"أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ لَمَّا مَرِضَ الْمَرَضَ الَّذِي قُبِضَ فِيهِ، اسْتَحَلَّ أَنْ يَكُونَ فِي بَيْتِي، فَأَحْلَلْنَ لَهُ، قَالَتْ: فَلَمَّا سَمِعْتُ ذَلِكَ قُمْتُ مُسْرِعَةً فَكَنَسْتُ بَيْتِي، وَلَيْسَ لِي خَادِمٌ، وَفَرَشْتُ لَهُ فِرَاشًا حَشْوُ مِرْفَقَتِهِ الْإِذْخِرُ، فَأَتَى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، يُهَادِي بَيْنَ رَجُلَيْنِ، حَتَّى وُضِعَ عَلَى فِرَاشِي"

Entry11/ 34
Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 21
English
Isnad

From Yazid, from Anas ؓ:

Matn

"Abu Bakr saw some lightness [improvement] in the Messenger of God ﷺ, so he asked his permission [to go] to his wife, the daughter of Kharija, who was in the orchards of the Ansar. That was the respite before death, and he did not realize it. So he permitted him. Then the Messenger of God ﷺ died that night. [Abu Bakr] woke in the morning and began to see the people agitated, so Abu Bakr ؓ ordered a boy to listen and then report to him. He said: 'I hear them saying: Muhammad has died.' So Abu Bakr ؓ hurried, saying: 'Oh, my back is broken!' Abu Bakr ؓ had not yet reached the mosque when they thought he would not make it. The hypocrites spread rumors and said: 'If Muhammad were a prophet, he would not have died.' 'Umar ؓ said: 'I will not hear a man say that Muhammad ﷺ has died without striking him with the sword.' So they refrained because of that. When Abu Bakr ؓ came, with the Prophet ﷺ lying covered, he uncovered the cloth from his face and began kissing him, and said: 'God would not make you taste death twice. You are too honored with God for that.' Then he said: 'O people, whoever worshipped Muhammad, Muhammad has died; and whoever worshipped the Lord of Muhammad, the Lord of Muhammad does not die.' Then he recited: {Muhammad is not but a messenger. [Other] messengers have passed on before him. So if he was to die or be killed, would you turn back on your heels? And he who turns back on his heels will never harm God at all; and God will reward the grateful.} [Surat Al 'Imran: 144]. He said: 'Umar ؓ said: 'It is as though we had never read it before.' And the people said the same as Abu Bakr's ؓ statement and recitation. He died on the night of Monday, remained for two nights and two days, and was buried on Tuesday. Usama b. Zayd and Aws b. Khawli poured water over him, while 'Ali and al-Fadl ؓ washed him ﷺ."

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ يَزِيدَ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ،

Matn

Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 21
English
Isnad

Abu Hamza al-Ansari said: I heard 'Abd Allah b. Dawud say:

Matn

I said to Abu Hanifa: "Which of the elders did you meet?" He said: "al-Qasim, Salim, Tawus, 'Ikrima, 'Abd Allah b. Dinar, al-Hasan al-Basri, 'Amr b. Dinar, Abu al-Zubayr, 'Ata', Qatada, Ibrahim, al-Sha'bi, Nafi'', and their like."

العربية
Isnad

No isnad: the text is given as a direct statement.

Matn

أَبُو حَمْزَةَ الْأَنْصَارِيُّ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ عَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ دَاوُدَ، يَقُولُ: قُلْتُ لِأَبِي حَنِيفَةَ: "مَنْ أَدْرَكْتَ مِنَ الْكُبَرَاءِ؟ قَالَ: الْقَاسِمَ، وَسَالِمًا، وَطَاوُسًا، وَعِكْرِمَةَ، وَعَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ دِينَارٍ، وَالْحَسَنَ الْبَصْرِيَّ، وَعَمْرَو بْنَ دِينَارٍ، وَأَبَا الزُّبَيْرِ، وَعَطَاءً، وَقَتَادَةَ، وَإِبْرَاهِيمَ، وَالشَّعْبِيَّ، وَنَافِعًا، وَأَمْثَالَهُمْ"
