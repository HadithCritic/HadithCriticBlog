From Abu al-Zubayr, from Jabir, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

Isnad

From 'Ali b. al-Husayn al-Raddad, from Tammam, from Ja'far b. Abi Talib ؓ:

Matn

Some of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ came to the Prophet ﷺ, and he said: "What is the matter that I see you with yellowed teeth? Use the toothstick. Were it not that I would burden my community, I would command them to use the toothstick at every prayer." In another narration: "What is the matter that I see you coming to me with yellowed teeth? Use the toothstick. Were it not that I would burden my community, I would command them to use the toothstick at every prayer, or at every ablution."

Entry8/ 38
Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 3
English
Isnad

From Khalid, from 'Abd Khayr, from 'Ali ؓ:

Matn

"That he called for water, and washed his palms three times, rinsed his mouth three times, sniffed water into his nose three times, washed his face three times, and his forearms three times, and wiped his head three times, and washed his feet three times, then said: This is the ablution of the Messenger of God ﷺ." In a narration: from Khalid, from 'Abd Khayr, from 'Ali ؓ: "That he called for water, and washed his palms three times, sniffed water into his nose three times, washed his face three times, and his forearms three times, and wiped his head once, and washed his feet three times." In another narration: "That he called for water, and a vessel with water in it and a basin were brought." 'Abd Khayr said: "And we were looking at him. He took the vessel in his right hand and poured it over his left hand, then washed his hands three times. Then he put his right hand into the vessel, filled his hand, rinsed his mouth and sniffed water into his nose; he did this three times. Then he washed his face three times, then washed his hands up to the elbows three times, then took water in his hand and wiped his head with it one time, then washed his feet three times each, then scooped with his palm, then said: Whoever would be pleased to look at the purification of the Messenger of God ﷺ this is his purification." In a narration: "That he called for water, and washed his palms three times, rinsed his mouth three times, sniffed water into his nose three times, washed his face three times, and washed his forearms three times, then took water in his palm and poured it over his bald head, then said: Whoever would be pleased to look at the purification of the Messenger of God ﷺ let him look at this." In a narration, from 'Ali ؓ: "That he performed ablution three times each." 'Abd Allah b. Muhammad said, from Ya'qub: Whoever narrated from Abu Hanifa ؓ, in this hadith, from Khalid: that the Prophet ﷺ, "wiped his head three times, [meaning] that he placed his hand on his fontanelle, then extended his hand to the back of his head, then to the front of his head, doing that three times; but that was only once, because his hand did not separate [from the head], nor did he take water three times. So it is like one who put water in his palm, then extended it to his wrist." Do you not see that it is clear in the hadiths that they narrated from him, namely al-Jarud b. Zayd, Kharija b. Mus'ab, and Asad b. 'Umar, that the wiping was once, and he made clear that its meaning is what we mentioned. He said: And it has been narrated from a group of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ many, in this wording: that the Prophet ﷺ wiped his head three times, among them: 'Uthman, 'Ali, 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud, and others ؓ. Al-Bayhaqi said: It has been narrated through unusual routes from 'Uthman: the repetition of the wiping, except that, being contrary to the [reliable] memorizers, it is not a proof among the people of knowledge. Was its meaning anything other than what we mentioned? So whoever makes Abu Hanifa mistaken in narrating the wiping three times has been mistaken, and he himself is more deserving of error and more fitting of it. Shu'ba made a gross error in this hadith according to everyone, namely his narrating this hadith from Malik b. 'Arfat, from 'Abd Khayr, from 'Ali ؓ; he distorted the two names in its chain, saying Malik in place of Khalid and 'Arfat in place of 'Alqama. And had this error been from Abu Hanifa ؒ they would have ascribed to him ignorance and lack of knowledge, and would have expelled him from the religion; and this is from lack of scrupulousness and following of desire.

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ خَالِدٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ خَيْرٍ، عَنْ عَلِيٍّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى عَنْهُ:

Matn

"أَنَّهُ دَعَا بِمَاءٍ، فَغَسَلَ كَفَّيْهِ ثَلَاثًا، وَتَمَضْمَضَ ثَلَاثًا، وَاسْتَنْشَقَ ثَلَاثًا، وَغَسَلَ وَجْهَهُ ثَلَاثًا، وَذِرَاعَيْهِ ثَلَاثًا، وَمَسَحَ رَأْسَهُ ثَلَاثًا، وَغَسَلَ قَدَمَيْهِ ثَلَاثًا، ثُمَّ قَالَ: هَذَا وُضُوءُ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ" وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ: عَنْ خَالِدٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ خَيْرٍ، عَنْ عَلِيٍّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ: «أَنَّهُ دَعَا بِمَاءٍ، فَغَسَلَ كَفَّيْهِ ثَلَاثًا، وَاسْتَنْشَقَ ثَلَاثًا، وَغَسَلَ وَجْهَهُ ثَلَاثًا، وَذِارَعَيْهِ ثَلَاثًا، وَمَسَحَ بِرَأْسِهِ مَرَّةً، وَغَسَلَ قَدَمَيْهِ ثَلَاثًا». وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ أُخْرَى: "أَنَّهُ دَعَا بِمَاءٍ، فَأَتَى بِإِنَاءٍ فِيهِ مَاءٌ وَطِسْتٍ، قَالَ عَبْدُ خَيْرٍ: وَنَحْنُ نَنْظُرُ إِلَيْهِ، فَأَخَذَ بِيَدِهِ الْيُمْنَى الْإِنَاءَ، فَأَكْفَأَ عَلَى يَدِهِ الْيُسْرَى، ثُمَّ غَسَلَ يَدَيْهِ ثَلَاثَ مَرَّاتٍ، ثُمَّ أَدْخَلَ يَدَهُ الْيُمْنَى فِي الْإِنَاءِ، فَمَلَأَ يَدَهُ فَمَضْمَضَ وَاسْتَنْشَقَ، فَعَلَ هَذَا ثَلَاثَ مَرَّاتٍ، ثُمَّ غَسَلَ وَجْهَهُ ثَلَاثَ مَرَّاتٍ، ثُمَّ غَسَلَ يَدَيْهِ إِلَى الْمَرَافِقِ ثَلَاثِ مَرَّاتٍ، ثُمَّ أَخَذَ الْمَاءَ بِيَدِهِ ثُمَّ مَسَحَ بِهَا رَأْسَهُ مَرَّةً وَاحِدَةً، ثُمَّ غَسَلَ قَدَمَيْهِ ثَلَاثًا ثَلَاثًا، ثُمَّ غَرَفَ بِكَفِّهِ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: مَنْ سَرَّهُ أَنْ يَنْظُرَ إِلَى طَهُورِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، فَهَذَا طَهُورِهِ". وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ: "أَنَّهُ دَعَا بِمَاءٍ، فَغَسَلَ كَفَّيْهِ ثَلَاثًا، وَمَضْمَضَ ثَلَاثًا، وَاسْتَنْشَقَ ثَلَاثًا، وَغَسَلَ وَجْهَهُ ثَلَاثًا، وَغَسَلَ ذِرَاعَيْهِ ثَلَاثًا، ثُمَّ أَخَذَ مَاءً فِي كَفِّهِ، فَصَبَّهُ عَلَى صَلْعَتِهِ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: مَنْ سَرَّهُ أَنْ يَنْظُرَ إِلَى طَهُورِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، فَلْيَنْظُرْ إِلَى هَذَا. وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ، عَنْ عَلِيٍّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ: «أَنَّهُ تَوَضَّأَ ثَلَاثًا ثَلَاثًا». قَالَ
