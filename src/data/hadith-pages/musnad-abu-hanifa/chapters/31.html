Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 31
English
Isnad

From Abu Farwa, from 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib, from Abu al-Duha, from Ibn 'Abbas ؓ, on the saying of God Almighty:

Matn

"{Alif, Lam, Mim}": "He said: 'I am God, I know and I see.'"

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ أَبِي فَرْوَةَ، عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ السَّائِبِ، عَنْ أَبِي الضُّحَى، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا فِي قَوْلِ اللَّهِ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ: "(الم)، قَالَ:

Matn

أَنَا اللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ وَأَرَى"

Entry2/ 15
Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 31
English
Isnad

From Salama b. Nubayt, who said:

Matn

I was with al-Dahhak b. Muzahim when a man asked him about this verse: "{Indeed, we see you to be among the doers of good}" [Surat Yusuf: 78]. He said: "When he saw a man in straitened circumstances, he would ease things for him; when he saw a sick person, he would attend to him; and when he saw a needy person, he would ask him [about his need] in order to fulfill it."

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ سَلَمَةَ بْنِ نُبَيْطٍ، قَالَ:

Matn

كُنْتُ عِنْدَ الضَّحَّاكِ بْنِ مُزَاحِمٍ، فَسَأَلَهُ رَجُلٌ عَنْ هَذِهِ الْآيَةِ: "{إِنَّا نَرَاكَ مِنَ الْمُحْسِنِينَ} [يوسف: 78]، قَالَ: كَانَ إِذَا رَأَى رَجُلًا مُضَيَّقًا عَلَيْهِ وَسَّعَ عَلَيْهِ، وَإِذَا رَأَى مَرِيضًا قَامَ عَلَيْهِ، وَإِذَا رَأَى مُحْتَاجًا سَأَلَهُ لِقَضَاءِ حَاجَتِهِ"

Entry3/ 15
Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 31
English
Isnad

From 'Atiyya, from Abu Sa'id, from the Prophet ﷺ, who said:

Matn

"Beware the discernment (firasa) of the believer, for he sees by the light of God Almighty." Then he recited: "{Indeed in that are signs for those who discern}" [Surat al-Hijr: 75], meaning: those with insight (al-mutafarrisin).

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ عَطِيَّةَ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، قَالَ:

Matn

"اتَّقُوا فِرَاسَةَ الْمُؤْمِنِ، فَإِنَّهُ يَنْظُرُ بِنُورِ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى، ثُمَّ قَرَأَ: {إِنَّ فِي ذَلِكَ لآيَاتٍ لِلْمُتَوَسِّمِينَ} [الحجر: 75]: الْمُتَفَرِّسِينَ"

Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 31
English
Isnad

From 'Abd Allah, from Ibn 'Abbas, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said, regarding the saying of God Almighty: "{By your Lord, We will surely question them all about what they used to do}" [Surat al-Hijr: 92–93]: "About 'there is no god but God.'"

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ،

Matn

فِي قَوْلِهِ تَعَالَى: "{فَوَرَبِّكَ لَنَسْأَلَنَّهُمْ أَجْمَعِينَ عَمَّا كَانُوا يَعْمَلُونَ} [الحجر: 92-93]، قَالَ: عَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ"

Entry5/ 15
Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 31
English
Isnad

From Dharr, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, from Ibn 'Abbas ؓ:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said to Gabriel ؑ: "What is the matter that you do not visit us more than you do?" So, after some nights, there was revealed: "{And we [angels] descend not except by the command of your Lord. To Him belongs that which is before us and that which is behind us}" [Surat Maryam: 64], the verse.

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ ذَرٍّ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ جُبَيْرٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ لِجِبْرِيلَ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَامُ: "مَالَكَ لَا تَزُورُنَا أَكْثَرَ مِمَّا تَزُورُ؟ فَأُنْزِلَتْ بَعْدَ لَيَالٍ: {وَمَا نَتَنَزَّلُ إِلا بِأَمْرِ رَبِّكَ لَهُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِينَا وَمَا خَلْفَنَا} [مريم: 64] الْآيَةُ"

Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 31
English
Isnad

From Simak, from Abu Salih, from Umm Hani' bint Abi Talib ؓ, who said:

Matn

I said: "O Messenger of God, what was the abomination that they used to commit in their assembly?" He said: "They used to flick date-pits or pebbles and mock the passersby."

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ سِمَاكٍ، عَنْ أَبِي صَالِحٍ، عَنْ أُمِّ هَانِئٍ بِنْتِ أَبِي طَالِبٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا، قَالَتْ:

Matn

قُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، مَا كَانَ الْمُنْكَرُ الَّذِي كَانُوا يَأْتُونَ فِي نَادِيهِمْ؟ قَالَ: «كَانَ يَخْذِفُونَ بِالنَّوَاةِ أَوِ الْحَصَاةِ، وَيَسْخَرُونَ مِنْ أَهْلِ الطَّرِيقِ»

Isnad

From 'Atiyya, from Ibn 'Umar ؓ:

Matn

that he recited to the Prophet ﷺ: "{God is the One who created you from weakness, then made after weakness strength, then made after strength weakness and white hair}" [Surat al-Rum: 54], and he corrected him and said: "Say: min da'f [from weakness]."

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ عَطِيَّةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ،

Matn

أَنَّهُ قَرَأَ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: "{اللَّهُ الَّذِي خَلَقَكُمْ مِنْ ضَعْفٍ ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنْ بَعْدِ ضَعْفٍ قُوَّةً ثُمَّ جَعَلَ مِنْ بَعْدِ قُوَّةٍ ضَعْفًا وَشَيْبَةً} [الروم: 54]، فَرَدَّ عَلَيْهِ، وَقَالَ: قُلْ: مِنْ ضَعْفٍ"

Entry10/ 15
Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 31
English
Isnad

From Makki b. Ibrahim, from Ibn Lahi'a, from Abu Qubayl, who said: I heard Abu 'Abd al-Rahman al-Muzani say:

Matn

I heard Thawban, the freed slave of the Messenger of God ﷺ, say: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: "I would not like to have the world and what is in it in exchange for this verse: {Say, O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of God. Indeed, God forgives all sins}" [Surat al-Zumar: 53]. A man said: "And [what of] shirk?" The Messenger of God ﷺ was silent, then said: "And whoever commits shirk?" The Messenger of God ﷺ was silent, then said: "Indeed, and whoever commits shirk."

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ مَكِّيِّ بْنِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنِ ابْنِ لَهِيعَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي قُبَيْلٍ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ أَبَا عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ الْمُزَنِيِّ، يَقُولُ: سَمِعْتُ ثَوْبَانَ مَوْلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، يَقُولُ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ:

Matn

"مَا أُحِبُّ أَنَّ لِيَ الدُّنْيَا بِمَا فِيهَا بِهَذِهِ الْآيَةِ: {قُلْ يَا عِبَادِيَ الَّذِينَ أَسْرَفُوا عَلَى أَنْفُسِهِمْ لا تَقْنَطُوا مِنْ رَحْمَةِ اللَّهِ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا} [الزمر: 53]. فَقَالَ رَجُلٌ: وَمِنَ الشِّرْكِ؟ فَسَكَتَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: وَمَنْ أَشْرَكَ؟ فَسَكَتَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، ثُمَّ قَالَ: أَلَا وَمَنْ أَشْرَكَ"

Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 31
English
Isnad

From Muhammad b. al-Sa'ib al-Kalbi, from Abu Salih, from Ibn 'Abbas ؓ:

Matn

"When Wahshi killed Hamza, he remained [as he was] for a time, then Islam fell into his heart. He sent word to the Messenger of God ﷺ that Islam had fallen into his heart, [saying]: 'I have heard you say from God Almighty: {And those who do not invoke with God another deity and do not kill the soul which God has forbidden [to be killed] except by right, and do not commit unlawful sexual intercourse; and whoever should do that will meet a penalty. Multiplied for him is the punishment on the Day of Resurrection, and he will abide therein humiliated} [Surat al-Furqan: 68–69]. And I have done all of them, so is there a concession for me?' He said: Gabriel descended and said: 'O Muhammad, say to him: {Except for those who repent, believe and do righteous work; for those, God will replace their evil deeds with good. And ever is God Forgiving and Merciful} [Surat al-Furqan: 70].' The Messenger of God ﷺ sent him this verse. When it was read to him, Wahshi said: 'In this verse are conditions, and I fear I will not fulfill them, and I cannot be certain whether I will do righteous work or not. Do you have something broader than this, O Muhammad?' So Gabriel descended with this verse: {Say, O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of God. Indeed, God forgives all sins. Indeed, it is He who is the Forgiving, the Merciful} [Surat al-Zumar: 53]. He said: The Messenger of God ﷺ wrote it and sent it to Wahshi. When it was read to him, he said: 'As for this verse, yes.' Then he accepted Islam and sent word to the Messenger of God ﷺ: 'I have accepted Islam, so permit me to meet you.' The Messenger of God ﷺ sent word to him: 'Keep your face hidden from me, for I cannot fill my eyes with the killer of Hamza, my uncle.' He said: Wahshi fell silent, until Musaylima wrote to the Messenger of God ﷺ: 'From Musaylima, messenger of God, to Muhammad, messenger of God. To proceed: I have been made a partner in the earth, so I have half the earth and Quraysh have half, except that Quraysh are a people who transgress.' He said: Two men came with his letter to the Messenger of God ﷺ. When the letter was read to the Messenger of God ﷺ, he said to the two messengers: 'Were it not that you are messengers, I would have killed you.' Then he called for 'Ali b. Abi Talib ؓ and said: 'Write: In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. From Muhammad, Messenger of God, to Musaylima the liar. Peace be upon whoever follows guidance. To proceed: {Indeed, the earth belongs to God; He causes to inherit it whom He wills of His servants, and the [best] outcome is for the righteous} [Surat al-A'raf: 128]. And blessings upon our master Muhammad ﷺ.' He said: When what Musaylima had written to the Messenger of God ﷺ reached [Wahshi], he took out the dhira', polished it, and resolved to kill Musaylima, and he did not cease in that resolve until he killed him on the Day of Yamama."
