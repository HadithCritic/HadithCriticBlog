"A dabb [spiny-tailed lizard] was gifted to her. She asked the Prophet ﷺ, and he forbade her from eating it. Then a beggar came, and she ordered that it be given to him. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'Do you feed [others] what you do not eat?!'"

Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 23
English
Isnad

From Hammad, from Ibrahim, from Hammam, from 'Adi b. Hatim, who said:

Matn

I asked the Messenger of God ﷺ, saying: "O Messenger of God, we send out trained dogs; may we eat of what they catch for us?" He said: "If you mentioned the name of God Almighty, so long as no other dog joined them." I said: "Even if it killed [the game]?" He said: "Even if it killed." I said: "O Messenger of God, one of us throws the mi'rad [a headless, pointed arrow/stick]?" He said: "If you throw it having said God's name, and it pierces [the game], eat; but if it strikes with its flat side, do not eat."

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ هَمَّامٍ، عَنْ عَدِيِّ بْنِ حَاتِمٍ، قَالَ:

Matn

سَأَلْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، فَقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، إِنَّا نَبْعَثُ الْكِلَابَ الْمُعَلَّمَةَ، أَفَنَأْكُلُ مِمَّا أَمْسَكْنَ عَلَيْنَا؟ فَقَالَ: "إذَا ذَكَرْتَ اسْمَ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى مَا لَمْ يَشْرِكْهَا كَلْبٌ غَيْرُهَا، قُلْتُ: وَإِنْ قَتَلَ؟ قَالَ: وَإِنْ قَتَلَ، قُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَحَدُنَا يَرْمِي بِالْمِعْرَاضِ؟ قَالَ: إذَا رَمَيْتَ فَسَمَّيْتَ، فَحَزَقَ فَكُلْ، فَإِنْ أَصَابَ بِعَرْضِهِ فَلَا تَأْكُلْ"

Musnad Abī Ḥanīfa · Book 23
English
Isnad

From Sa'id, from 'Abaya b. Rifa'a, from Rafi' b. Khadij:

Matn

"A camel from the charity camels bolted, and they pursued it. When they were unable to catch it, a man shot it with an arrow and struck a fatal spot. They asked the Prophet ﷺ, and he ordered that it be eaten, and said: 'These [animals] have wild traits like the wild traits of wild beasts; so if you fear [harm] from any of them, do with it as you did with this camel, then eat it.'" In another narration: A camel from the charity camels bolted, and a man shot it with an arrow and killed it. The Prophet ﷺ was asked about eating it, and he said: "Eat it, for these have wild traits like the wild traits of wild beasts."

العربية
Isnad

عَنْ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ عَبَايَةَ بْنِ رِفَاعَةَ، عَنْ رَافِعِ بْنِ خَدِيجٍ،

Matn

"أَنَّ بَعِيرًا مِنْ إِبِلِ الصَّدَقَةِ نَدَّ فَطَلَبُوهُ، فَلَمَّا أَعْيَاهُمْ أَنْ يَأْخُذُوهُ رَمَاهُ رَجُلٌ بِسَهْمٍ، فَأَصَابَ مَقْتَلَهُ، فَسَأَلُوا النَّبِيَّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فَأَمَرَ بِأَكْلِهِ، وَقَالَ: «إِنَّ لَهَا أَوَابِدَ كَأَوَابِدِ الْوُحُوشِ؛ فَإِذَا خَشِيتُمْ مِنْهَا فَاصْنَعُوا مِثْلَمَا صَنَعْتُمْ بِهَذَا الْبَعِيرِ، ثُمَّ كُلُوهُ»، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ: إِنَّ بَعِيرًا مِنْ إِبِلِ الصَّدَقَةِ نَدَّ، فَرَمَاهُ رَجُلٌ بِسَهْمٍ فَقَتَلَهُ، فَسُئِلَ النَّبِيُّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ عَنْ أَكْلِهِ، فَقَالَ: «كُلُوهُ، فَإِنَّ لَهَا أَوَابِدَ كَأَوَابِدِ الْوَحْشِ»

Isnad

From 'Alqama, from Sulayman b. Burayda, from his father ؓ, from the Prophet ﷺ, who said:

Matn

"I [had] forbidden you from visiting graves, but Muhammad ﷺ has been permitted to visit the grave of his mother, so visit them, and do not utter anything vile. And [I forbade you] from keeping the meat of sacrificial animals beyond three days. I forbade you so that the well-off among you might be generous to your poor; now God has granted you abundance, so eat and take provisions. And [I forbade you] from drinking in the hantam and the muzaffat [pitch-coated vessel]." In another narration: "And from the naqir [hollowed palm-trunk vessel] and the dubba'. So drink in any vessel you wish, for the vessel neither makes anything lawful nor unlawful, but do not drink any intoxicant." In another narration, he said: "We forbade you three things: visiting graves, so visit them; and we forbade you from keeping the meat of sacrificial animals beyond three days, so keep it and take provisions, and we only forbade you so that the wealthy among you might be generous to your poor; and we forbade you from drinking in the dubba' and the muzaffat, so drink in whatever is available to you, for the vessel neither makes anything lawful nor unlawful, but do not drink any intoxicant." In another narration, similar, and in it: "And [we forbade] nabidh [date/raisin infusion] in the dubba', the hantam, and the muzaffat; so drink in any vessel, but do not drink any intoxicant."

From Muhammad b. Qays al-Hamdani, from Abu 'Amir al-Thaqafi:

Matn

"He used to gift the Prophet ﷺ every year a skin of wine." In another narration: A man of Thaqif, called Abu 'Amir, "used to gift the Prophet ﷺ every year a skin of wine. In the year in which wine was forbidden, he gifted a skin as he used to. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'O Abu 'Amir, God Almighty has forbidden wine, so we have no need of your wine.'
