Entry860/ 14
Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 190
English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Ibrahim, from Ali b. Abi Talib, ؓ,

Matn

that he said regarding the slave who has a contract of manumission: "He is freed in proportion to what he has paid, and remains enslaved in proportion to what he has not paid."

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عَلِيِّ بْنِ أَبِي طَالِبٍ رضي الله عنه

Matn

أَنَّهُ قَالَ فِي الْمُكَاتَبِ: «يُعْتَقُ مِنْهُ بِقَدْرِ مَا أَدَّى وَيُرَقُّ مِنْهُ بِقَدْرِ مَا لَمْ يُؤَدِّ»

English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father from Abu Hanifah from Hammad from Ibrahim from Zayd b. Thabit, ؓ,

Matn

who said "He is a slave as long as a dirham remains upon him." And Zayd said: "If he dies, his master takes all his wealth."

Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ ثَابِتٍ رضي الله عنه،

Matn

أَنَّهُ قَالَ «هُوَ عَبْدٌ مَا بَقَى عَلَيْهِ دِرْهَمٌ» ، وَقَالَ زَيْدٌ: «إِنْ مَاتَ أَخَذَ مَوْلَاهُ مَالَهُ كُلَّهُ»

Entry863/ 14
Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 190
English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father from Abu Hanifah from Hammad from Ibrahim from Ali, Abdullah b. Mas'ud, and Shurayh, ؓ,

Matn

that they said regarding the slave who has a contract of manumission and dies leaving sufficient wealth: "He pays the remainder of his contract, and what remains is inheritance for his heirs."

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عَلِيٍّ، وَعَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ، وَشُرَيْحٍ رضي الله عنهم،

Matn

أَنَّهُمْ قَالُوا فِي الْمُكَاتَبِ يَمُوتُ وَيَتْرُكُ وَفَاءً: «يُؤَدِّي بَقِيَّةَ مُكَاتَبَتِهِ وَمَا بَقَى فَهُوَ مِيرَاثٌ لِوَرَثَتِهِ»

Yusuf narrated from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Ibrahim,

Matn

who said The opinion of Ali, Ibn Mas'ud, and Shurayh, ؓ, regarding the slave who has a contract of manumission and then dies is more preferable to me than the opinion of Zayd. However, the opinion of Zayd during his lifetime is more preferable to me than their opinion.

Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 191
English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father from Abu Hanifah from al-Haytham from a man from Aisha, ؓ,

Matn

that she had a slave who had a contract of manumission with her, and some of his payment was still due. He used to enter upon her, but when he heard the statement of Zayd, he said "He wants to enslave me again." So he paid her what was due, and she then veiled herself from him.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنِ الْهَيْثَمِ، عَنْ رَجُلٍ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها، "

Matn

أَنَّهُ كَانَ لَهَا مُكَاتَبٌ عَلَيْهِ شَيْءٌ مِنْ مُكَاتَبَتِهِ يَدْخُلُ عَلَيْهَا، فَبَلَغَهُ قَوْلُ زَيْدٍ، فَقَالَ يُرِيدُ أَنْ يَسْتَرِقَّنِي، فَأَدَّى إِلَيْهَا فَاحْتَجَبَتْ عَنْهُ "

Entry868/ 14
Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 191
English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Ibrahim,

Matn

that he said When a man writes a single contract of manumission by payment for his slaves, making their installments the same, and says, “If you pay, you are free, and if you are unable, you remain slaves,” then if one of them dies, it does not reduce anything from their obligation.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ،

Matn

أَنَّهُ قَالَ " إِذَا كَاتَبَ الرَّجُلُ عَبِيدَهَ مُكَاتَبَةً وَاحِدَةً، فَجَعَلَ نُجُومَهُمْ وَاحِدَةً، وَقَالَ: إِنْ أَدَّيْتُمَ فَأَنْتُمْ أَحْرَارٌ، وَإِنْ عَجَزْتُمْ فَأَنْتُمْ رَقِيقٌ، فَمَاتَ وَاحِدٌ لَمْ يَرْفَعْ عَنْهُمْ بِهِ شَيْئًا "

English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father from Abu Hanifah from Hammad from Ibrahim

Matn

that a man guaranteed to another the amount owed by his mukatab. That guarantee is invalid. How could it be valid? He has only guaranteed his own money on behalf of his own slave.

Isnad

Entry871/ 14
Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 192
English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father from Abu Hanifah from Hammad from Ibrahim

Matn

that he said The child of a mudabbirah and the child of an umm walad share her status.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ،

Matn

أَنَّهُ قَالَ «وَلَدُ الْمُدَبَّرَةِ وَوَلَدُ أُمِّ الْوَلَدِ بِمَنْزِلَتِهَا»

EntryUnnumbered/ 14
Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 192
English
Isnad

Abu Yusuf said A narrator related to me from Amir

Matn

that he said: "It is neither to be sold nor gifted. If a slave woman is contracted for emancipation and her master has intercourse with her, her child holds the same status as her and is emancipated from one-third [of the estate]."

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ أَبُو يُوسُفَ: حَدَّثَنِي مُحَدِّثٌ عَنْ عَامِرٍ،

Matn

أَنَّهُ قَالَ: «لَا يُبَاعُ وَلَا يُوهَبُ، وَإِنْ كَاتَبَ جَارِيَةً فَوَطِئَهَا مَوْلَاهَا فَوَلَدُهَا بِمَنْزِلَتِهَا يُعْتَقُ مِنَ الثُّلُثِ»

Entry872/ 14
Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 192
English
Isnad

Yusuf narrated from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Ibrahim,

Matn

that Umar b. al-Khattab, ؓ, would announce from the pulpit of the Messenger of God, ﷺ, that "the sale of mothers of children is forbidden. When a slave woman gives birth to her master’s child, she is no longer subject to slavery thereafter."

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ رضي الله عنه أَنَّهُ كَانَ يُنَادِي عَلَى مِنْبَرِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم

Matn

أَنَّ «بَيْعَ أُمَّهَاتِ الْأَوْلَادِ حَرَامٌ، إِذَا وَلَدَتِ الْأَمَةُ لِسَيِّدِهَا فَلَيْسَ عَلَيْهَا رِقٌّ بَعْدَهُ»
