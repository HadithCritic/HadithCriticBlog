Entry961/ 30
Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 218
English
Isnad

Yusuf b. Abi Yusuf reported from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Ibrahim,

Matn

who said regarding killing that it occurs in three forms: Intentional killing is when you deliberately strike someone with a weapon, and in this case, retaliation (qisas) is required. Unintentional killing is when you intend to strike something but strike someone else with a weapon, and in this case, the blood money (diyah) is upon the clan (aqilah). Semi-intentional killing is when you deliberately strike someone without a weapon, and in this case, the blood money is increased and is upon the clan if it results in death. In cases of wounds, semi-intentional applies to anything where you deliberately strike someone with or without a weapon, but retaliation is not possible, so the blood money is increased.

English
Isnad

Yusuf narrated from his father from Abu Hanifah from Hammad from Ibrahim,

Matn

who said regarding blood money: For a tooth, half of a tenth. Likewise for a wound exposing the bone. For a displaced bone, a tenth and a half of a tenth. For a penetrating wound, a third of the blood money. For a wound reaching the skull, a third of the blood money. If the intellect is lost, the full blood money is due. For the nose, the full blood money. For the nasal septum, the full blood money. For the penis, the full blood money. For the glans, the full blood money. For the testicles, the full blood money. For the tongue, the full blood money. For both eyes, the full blood money, and for one eye, half. Likewise for both hands and both feet, half the blood money for each. For both ears, the full blood money, and for one ear, half. For both eyebrows, the full blood money.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ،

Matn

أَنَّهُ قَالَ فِي «السِّنِّ نِصْفُ الْعُشْرِ، وَكَذَلِكَ الْمُوضِحَةِ، وَفِي الْمُنَقِّلَةِ الْعُشْرُ وَنِصْفُ الْعُشْرِ، وَفِي الْجَائِفَةِ ثُلُثُ الدِّيَةِ، وَفِي الْآمَّةِ ثُلُثُ الدِّيَةِ، فَإِذَا ذَهَبَ الْعَقْلُ فَفِيهَا الدِّيَةُ كَامِلَةٌ، وَفِي الْأَنْفِ الدِّيَةُ، وَفِي الْمَارِنِ الدِّيَةُ، وَفِي الذَّكَرِ الدِّيَةُ، وَفِي الْحَشَفَةِ الدِّيَةُ، وَفِي الْأُنْثَيَيْنِ الدِّيَةُ، وَفِي اللِّسَانِ الدِّيَةُ، وَفِي الْعَيْنَيْنِ الدِّيَةُ، وَفِي الْوَاحِدَةِ النِّصْفُ، وَكَذَلِكَ الْيَدَيْنِ وَالرِّجْلَيْنِ فِي كُلِّ وَاحِدَةٍ مِنْهُمَا نِصْفُ الدِّيَةِ، وَفِي الْأُذُنَيْنِ الدِّيَةُ، وَفِي إِحْدَاهُمَا النِّصْفُ، وَفِي الْحَاجِبَيْنِ الدِّيَةُ»
