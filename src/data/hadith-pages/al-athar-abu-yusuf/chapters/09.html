Isnad

Yusuf b. Abi Yusuf reported from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Ibrahim, who said:

Matn

Yusuf reported from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, who said:

Matn

“I asked Ibrahim, ‘At what point should a traveler shorten the prayer?’ He replied, ‘When you leave Kufah for al-Mada’in, shorten the prayer as soon as you leave the houses.’ Hammad said, ‘I then asked Sa‘id ibn Jubayr, and he set a similar point.’”
