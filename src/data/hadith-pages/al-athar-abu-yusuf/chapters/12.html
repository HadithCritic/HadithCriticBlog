Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 85
English
Isnad

Yusuf narrated from his father from Abu Hanifah from Hammad from Ibrahim, who said There is no zakat

Matn

on fewer than five camels. When they reach five, there is one sheep due on them up to nine. When they are ten, there are two sheep due up to fourteen. When they are fifteen, there are three sheep due up to nineteen. When they are twenty, there are four sheep due up to twenty-four. When they reach twenty-five, there is a bint makhad (a one-year-old she-camel) due up to thirty-five. If they increase by one, there is a bint labun (a two-year-old she-camel) due up to forty-five. If they increase by one, there is a hiqqah (a three-year-old she-camel) due up to sixty. If they increase by one, there is a jatha'ah (a four-year-old she-camel) due up to seventy-five. If they increase by one, there are two bint labun due up to ninety. If they increase by one, there are two hiqqah due up to one hundred and twenty. Then the obligation restarts—if the camels are many, there is one hiqqah for every fifty.

Matn

Isnad

Yusuf narrated from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Ibrahim, who said There is no alms

Matn

due on fewer than forty sheep. When there are forty grazing sheep, one sheep is due on them up to one hundred and twenty. If there is one more, then two sheep are due up to two hundred. If there is one more, then three sheep are due up to three hundred. When the sheep become many, then one sheep is due for every hundred sheep.

English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father, from ‘Ata b. ‘Ajlan, from al-Hasan, that ‘Umar, ؓ, sent Sufyan

Matn

ibn Malik as a zakat collector to Basra. He stayed there for a time, then asked Umar for leave to go on a military expedition. Umar said, “Are you not already engaged in strenuous work?” He replied, “How so, when people say, ‘He is treating us unjustly’?” Umar asked, “In what way?” He replied, “They say, ‘You count the young lamb against us but do not take it from us.’” Umar said, “Then count it against them—even if the shepherd brings it carried on his shoulder. Do you not leave them the ewe nursing her lamb, the animal kept for food, the pregnant ewe, and the breeding ram?”

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ عَجْلَانَ، عَنِ الْحَسَنِ، أَنَّ عُمَرَ رضي الله عنه بَعَثَ سُفْيَانَ

Matn

بْنَ مَالِكٍ سَاعِيًا إِلَى الْبَصْرَةِ فَمَكَثَ حِينًا، ثُمَّ اسْتَأْذَنَهُ فِي الْجِهَادِ، فَقَالَ «أَوَلَسْتَ فِي الْجَهْدِ» قَالَ: وَمِنْ أَيْنَ وَالنَّاسُ يَقُولُونَ: هُوَ يَظْلِمُنَا، قَالَ: «فَبِمَاذَا؟ قَالُوا» : قَالَ: يَقُولُونَ: تَعُدُّ عَلَيْنَا السَّخْلَةَ، وَلَا تَأْخُذُهَا مِنَّا، قَالَ: «فَاعْدُدْهَا عَلَيْهِمْ، وَإِنْ جَاءَ بِهَا الرَّاعِي يَحْمِلُهَا عَلَى كَتِفِهِ، أَوَلَسْتَ تَدَعُ لَهُمُ الرُّبَّى وَالْأَكِيلَةَ وَالْمَاخِضَ وَفَحْلَ الْغَنَمِ»
