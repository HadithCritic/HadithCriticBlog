Entry632/ 11
Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 139
English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Ibrahim, from Ali, ؓ, who said regarding the “choose” option:

Matn

“If she chooses her husband, it is one revocable divorce; if she chooses herself, it is one irrevocable divorce.”

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عَلِيٍّ رضي الله عنه

Matn

أَنَّهُ قَالَ فِي اخْتَارِي: «إِنِ اخْتَارَتْ زَوْجَهَا فَوَاحِدَةٌ يَمْلِكُ الرَّجْعَةَ، وَإِنِ اخْتَارَتْ نَفْسَهَا فَوَاحِدَةٌ بَائِنَةٌ»

Entry633/ 11
Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 139
English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Ibrahim, from Umar b. al-Khattab and Ibn Mas'ud, ؓ, who said regarding the wife’s option to choose:

Matn

“If she chooses her husband, she remains his wife; if she chooses herself, it is one divorce, and he retains authority over her.” Zayd b. Thabit, ؓ, said of her: “If she chooses her husband, she remains his wife; if she chooses herself, it is three divorces.”

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ، وَابْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رضي الله عنهما

Matn

أَنَّهُمَا قَالَا فِي اخْتَارِي: «إِنِ اخْتَارَتْ زَوْجَهَا فَهِيَ امْرَأَتُهُ، وَإِنِ اخْتَارَتْ نَفْسَهَا فَوَاحِدَةٌ وَهُوَ أَمْلَكُ بِهَا» وَإِنَّ زَيْدَ بْنَ ثَابِتٍ رضي الله عنه قَالَ فِيهَا: «إِنِ اخْتَارَتْ زَوْجَهَا فَهِيَ امْرَأَتُهُ، وَإِنِ اخْتَارَتْ نَفْسَهَا فَهِيَ ثَلَاثٌ»

Entry638/ 11
Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 141
English
Isnad

Yusuf narrated from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Ibrahim, from Aisha, ؓ,

Matn

that the Prophet, ﷺ, said to her: “Buy Barirah and emancipate her, for the right of wala' (the bond of manumission-based clientage) belongs to the one who emancipates.” So she bought and emancipated her, and Barirah was given the choice. Her husband was a client of the family of Abu Ahmad.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رضي الله عنها

Matn

أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ لَهَا: «اشْتَرِي بَرِيرَةَ فَأَعْتِقِيهَا؛ فَإِنَّ الْوَلَاءَ لِمَنْ أَعْتَقَ» ، فَاشْتَرَتْهَا فَأَعْتَقَتْهَا، فَخُيِّرَتْ وَكَانَ زَوْجُهَا مَوْلَى لِآلِ أَبِي أَحْمَدَ

English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father from Abu Hanifah from Hammad from Ibrahim

Matn

that he said regarding the impotent man: “He is given a respite of one year. If he is able to have intercourse with her during that time, then the matter is resolved. Otherwise, his wife is given the choice: if she wishes, she may stay with her husband, and if she wishes, she may choose herself. If she chooses herself, it is one irrevocable divorce.”

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: ثنا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ،

Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father from Abu Hanifah from Hammad from Ibrahim

Matn

that he said regarding the slave woman: When she is manumitted, she is given the choice. If she chooses herself and her husband has not consummated the marriage with her, then she is not entitled to a dowry. But if she chooses her husband and he has consummated the marriage with her, then the dowry belongs to her master.

العربية
Isnad

Entry642/ 11
Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 141
English
Isnad

Yusuf said, and Abu Yusuf said: Isma'il b. Muslim reported to us from al-Hasan al-Basri, from Umar b. al-Khattab, ؓ,

Matn

that he said regarding the impotent man: “He is given a respite of one year. If he has intercourse with her during that time, the marriage continues; otherwise, they are separated and she is entitled to the full dowry. This is one irrevocable divorce.” Abu Hanifah also related something similar from Umar.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ يُوسُفُ وَقَالَ أَبُو يُوسُفَ حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ مُسْلِمٍ، عَنِ الْحَسَنِ الْبَصْرِيِّ، عَنْ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ رضي الله عنه

Matn

أَنَّهُ قَالَ فِي الْعِنِّينِ: «يُؤَجَّلُ سَنَةً، فَإِنْ وَصَلَ إِلَيْهَا، وَإِلَّا فُرِّقَ بَيْنَهُمَا وَلَهَا الْمَهْرُ كَامِلًا، وَهِيَ تَطْلِيقَةٌ بَائِنَةٌ» وَذَكَرَ أَبُو حَنِيفَةَ نَحْوًا مِنْهُ عَنْهُ
