Kitāb al-Āthār of Abū Yūsuf · Vol. 1, p. 36
English
Isnad

Yusuf narrated from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Abu Wa’il, from Ibn Mas’ud, ؓ, who said

Matn

When one of you is praying and does not know whether he has prayed three or four rak’ahs, let him seek what is most likely correct. If he is more certain that it was three, let him pray a fourth. If he is more certain that it was four, let him finish, prostrate twice for forgetfulness, recite the tashahhud, and give the salutation.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ أَبِي وَائِلٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رضي الله عنه أَنَّهُ قَالَ

Matn

«إِذَا كَانَ أَحَدُكُمْ يُصَلِّي، فَلَمْ يَدْرِ أَثَلَاثًا صَلَّى أَمْ أَرْبَعًا، فَلْيَتَحَرَّ الصَّوَابَ، فَإِنْ كَانَ أَكْثَرَ رَأْيِهِ أَنَّهُ ثَلَاثٌ، فَلْيُصَلِّ إِلَيْهَا رَابِعَةً، وَإِنْ كَانَ أَكْثَرَ رَأْيِهِ أَنَّهُ أَرْبَعٌ، فَلْيَنْصَرِفْ وَيَسْجُدْ سَجْدَتَيِ السَّهْوِ وَيَتَشَهَّدْ وَيُسَلِّمْ»

English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father from Abu Hanifah from Hammad from Ibrahim

Matn

that he would perform the two prostrations of forgetfulness in every voluntary or obligatory prayer. He said , "They rectify what has been corrupted in the prayer." And he would say, "Performing them when they are not obligatory upon me is more beloved to me than leaving them when they are obligatory upon me."

English
Isnad

Yusuf reported from his father, from Abu Hanifah, from Hammad, from Ibrahim, who said

Matn

who said: “If you forget something while praying behind the imam and the imam remembers correctly, there is no prostration of forgetfulness for you. If the imam forgets while you remember, then the prostration of forgetfulness is due from you. If the imam does not perform the prostration, do not perform it. The same applies if everyone praying with the imam forgets, or if the imam forgets.”

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا يُوسُفُ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ أَبِي حَنِيفَةَ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، أَنَّهُ قَالَ

Matn
