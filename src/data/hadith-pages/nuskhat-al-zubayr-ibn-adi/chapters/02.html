Narration1/ 79
Copy of al-Zubayr ibn ʿAdī · p. 2
English
Isnad

The shaykh, imam, scholar, the unique one (al-awhad), the great chief (al-sadr al-kabir), Taj al-Din Abu l-Yumn Zayd b. al-Hasan b. Zayd al-Kindi — may God preserve him — informed us, saying: the shaykh Abu Muhammad 'Abd Allah b. 'Ali b. Ahmad al-Muqri' informed us; Abu 'Ali al-Hasan b. Muhammad b. al-Qasim b. 'Abd Allah b. Zina informed us; Abu l-Fath Hilal b. Muhammad b. Ja'far al-Haffar informed us, saying: I recited to Abu l-Fadl 'Isa b. Musa b. al-Mutawakkil 'ala Allah, who said: Abu 'Abd Allah al-Husayn b. Muhammad b. Muhammad b. 'Ufayr b. Muhammad b. Sahl b. Abi Hathma al-Ansari informed us; Abu Muhammad al-Hajjaj b. Yusuf b. Qutayba al-Asbahani narrated to us; Bishr b. al-Husayn narrated to us; al-Zubayr b. 'Adi narrated to us, from Anas b. Malik al-Ansari, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Whoever leaves the Friday prayer three times without necessity is recorded as a hypocrite in the Book of God, which neither erases nor is altered.”

العربية
Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا الشَّيْخُ الْإِمَامُ الْعَالِمُ الأَوْحَدُ الصَّدْرُ الْكَبِيرُ تَاجُ الدِّينِ أَبُو الْيَمَنِ زَيْدُ بْنُ الْحَسَنِ بْنِ زَيْدٍ الْكِنْدِيُّ ، أَبْقَاهُ اللَّهُ، قَالَ:

Matn

أَخْبَرَنَا الشَّيْخُ أَبُو مُحَمَّدٍ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عَلِيِّ بْنِ أَحْمَدَ الْمُقْرِئُ ، أنبا أَبُو عَلِيٍّ الْحَسَنُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ الْقَاسِمِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ زِينَةَ ، أَنَبا أَبُو الْفَتْحِ هِلالُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ جَعْفَرٍ الْحَفَّارُ ، قَالَ: قَرَأْتُ عَلَى أَبِي الْفَضْلِ عِيسَى بْنِ مُوسَى بْنِ الْمُتَوَكِّلِ عَلَى اللَّهِ ، قَالَ: أنبا أَبُو عَبْدِ اللَّهِ الْحُسَيْنُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ عُفَيْرِ بْنِ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ سَهْلِ بْنِ أَبِي حَثْمَةَ الْأَنْصَارِيُّ ، ثنا أَبُو مُحَمَّدٍ الْحَجَّاجُ بْنُ يُوسُفَ بْنِ قُتَيْبَةَ الأَصْبَهَانِيُّ ، ثنا بِشْرُ بْنُ الْحُسَيْنِ ، ثنا الزُّبَيْرُ بْنُ عَدِيٍّ ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ الْأَنْصَارِيِّ ، قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: ‌ ‌«مَنْ تَرَكَ الْجُمُعَةَ ثَلاثًا مِنْ غَيْرِ ضَرُورَةٍ كُتِبَ مُنَافِقًا فِي كِتَابِ اللَّهِ لا يَمْحُو أَوْ لا يُبَدَّلُ»

Narration3/ 79
Copy of al-Zubayr ibn ʿAdī · p. 4
English
Isnad

And by his chain of transmission, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Jibril came to me with a mirror in which there was a black spot. I said: What is this, O Jibril? He said: This is Friday; you and your people have been favored with it, and its name with your Lord is the Day of Increase (yawm al-mazid). He said: I said: And what is this spot? He said: The Hour will be established on it. I said: And what is the Day of Increase? He said: Your Lord, Blessed and Exalted, has made in Paradise a spacious valley in which are hills of musk; and when it is the day of Friday, the Lord, Blessed and Exalted, descends, and surrounds His assembly with pulpits of light, and the prophets sit upon them; and those pulpits are surrounded by pulpits of gold, crowned with rubies and chrysolite, and the truthful (siddiqun) and the martyrs sit upon them; and the people of the Gardens of Eden descend. They remain so, and God says: I am your Lord; I have been true to My promise to you; so ask Me and I shall give you. They say: We ask You for Your good pleasure (ridwan). He says: I am pleased with you, and with Me there is more. And there is no day more beloved to them than the day of Friday.”

العربية
Isnad

وَبِإِسْنَادِهِ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ، قَالَ:

Matn

"‌ ‌ أَتَانِي جِبْرِيلُ بِمِرْآةٍ فِيهَا نُكْتَةٌ سَوْدَاءُ ، فَقُلْتُ: مَا هَذِهِ يَا جِبْرِيلُ؟ قَالَ: هَذِهِ الْجُمُعَةُ فُضِّلْتُ بِهَا أَنْتَ وَقَوْمُكَ ، وَاسْمُهَا عِنْدَ رَبِّكَ يَوْمُ الْمَزِيدِ ، قَالَ: فَقُلْتُ: فَمَا هَذِهِ النُّكْتَةُ؟ قَالَ: تَقُومُ فِيهَا السَّاعَةُ ، قُلْتُ: فَمَا يَوْمُ الْمَزِيدِ؟ قَالَ: إِنَّ رَبَّكَ تبارك وتعالى اتَّخَذَ فِي الْجَنَّةِ وَادِيًا أَفْيَحَ فِيهِ كُثُبٌ مِنْ مِسْكٍ ، فَإِذَا كَانَ يَوْمُ الْجُمُعَةِ نَزَلَ الرَّبُّ تبارك وتعالى ، وَحَفَّ مَجْلِسَهُ بِمَنَابِرَ مِنْ نُورٍ ، فَجَلَسَ عَلَيْهَا النَّبِيُّونَ ، وَحُفَّتْ تِلْكَ الْمَنَابِرُ بِمَنَابِرَ مِنْ ذَهَبٍ مُكَّلَلَةٍ بِالْيَاقُوتِ وَالزَّبَرْجَدِ ، فَجَلَسَ عَلَيْهَا الصِّدِّيقُونَ وَالشُّهَدَاءُ ، وَنَزَلَ أَهْلُ جَنَّاتِ عَدْنٍ ، فَكَانُوا عَلَى ذَلِكَ ، فَيَقُولُ اللَّهُ: أَنَا رَبُّكُمْ قَدْ صَدَقْتُكُمْ وَعْدِى ، فَاسْأَلُونِي أُعْطِكُمْ. فَيَقُولُونَ: نَسْأَلُكَ رِضْوَانَكَ. فَيَقُولُ: قَدْ رَضِيتُ عَنْكُمْ وَلَدَيَّ مَزِيدٌ. وَلا يَوْمَ أَحَبَّ إِلَيْهِمْ مِنْ يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ "

Narration5/ 79
Copy of al-Zubayr ibn ʿAdī · p. 6
English
Isnad

And by it he said:

Matn

A roasted bird was given as a gift to the Messenger of God ﷺ. When it was placed before him he said: “O God, bring me the one most beloved of Your creation to You to eat with me of the bird.” He said: I said to myself: O God, make him a man of the Ansar. He said: Then 'Ali ﵇ came and knocked at the door softly. I said: Who is this? He said: 'Ali. I said: The Messenger of God ﷺ is occupied with something; so he left. He said: I returned to the Messenger of God ﷺ, and he was saying for the second time: “O God, bring me the one most beloved of Your creation to You to eat with me of this bird.” I said: O God, make him a man of the Ansar. He said: Then 'Ali came and knocked at the door. I said: Did I not tell you that the Messenger of God ﷺ is occupied? Leave. I returned to the Messenger of God ﷺ, and he was saying for the third time: “O God, bring me the one most beloved of Your creation to You to eat with me of this bird.” He said: Then 'Ali came and struck the door violently, and the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Open, open, open!” He said: When the Messenger of God ﷺ looked at him, he said: “O God, befriend [him]; O God, befriend [him]; O God, befriend [him].” So he sat with the Messenger of God ﷺ, eating the bird with him.

العربية
Isnad

وَبِهِ قَالَ:

Matn

أُهْدِيَ لِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم طَيْرٌ مَشْوِيٌّ ، فَلَمَّا وُضِعَ بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ قَالَ: ‌ ‌«اللَّهُمَّ ائْتِنِي بِأَحَبِّ خَلْقِكَ إِلَيْكَ يَأْكُلُ مَعِيَ مِنَ الطَّيْرِ» . قَالَ: فَقُلْتُ فِي نَفْسِي: اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْهُ رَجُلا مِنَ الأَنْصَارِ ، قَالَ: فَجَاءَ عَلِيٌّ عليه السلام ، فَقَرَعَ الْبَابَ قَرْعًا خَفِيًّا ، فَقُلْتُ: مَنْ هَذَا؟ قَالَ: عَلِيٌّ. فَقُلْتُ: إِنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَلَى حَاجَةٍ ، فَانْصَرَفَ ، قَالَ: فَرَجَعْتُ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ، وَهُوَ يَقُولُ الثَّانِيَةَ: «اللَّهُمَّ ائْتِنِي بِأَحَبِّ خَلْقِكَ إِلَيْكَ يَأْكُلُ مَعِيَ مِنْ هَذَا الطَّيْرِ» . فَقُلْتُ: اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْهُ رَجُلا مِنَ الأَنْصَارِ ، قَالَ: فَجَاءَ عَلِيٌّ ، فَقَرَعَ الْبَابَ ، فَقُلْتُ: أَلَمْ أُخْبِرْكَ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَلَى حَاجَةٍ ، انْصَرَفَ ، فَرَجَعْتُ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ، وَهُوَ يَقُولُ الثَّالِثَةُ: «اللَّهُمَّ ائْتِنِي بِأَحَبِّ خَلْقِكَ إِلَيْكَ يَأْكُلُ مَعِيَ مِنْ هَذَا الطَّيْرِ» . قَالَ: فَجَاءَ عَلِيٌّ فَضَرَبَ الْبَابَ ضَرْبًا شَدِيدًا ، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «افْتَحْ افْتَحْ افْتَحْ» . قَالَ: فَلَمَّا نَظَرَ إِلَيْهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ، قَالَ: «اللَّهُمَّ وَالِ ، اللَّهُمُّ وَالِ ، اللَّهُمَّ وَالِ» . فَجَلَسَ مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَأْكُلُ مَعَهُ الطَّيْرَ

And by it Bishr informed us, from al-Zubayr, who said:

Matn

It reached me that 'Abd Allah b. 'Abbas entered upon 'Umar at his death and said: “Rejoice, O Commander of the Believers; rejoice in Paradise. 'Umar said: Enough! Ibn 'Abbas said: Indeed: you accepted Islam when they disbelieved, and you waged jihad alongside the Messenger of God ﷺ when they abandoned him, and the Messenger of God ﷺ died while he was pleased with you.” Bishr informed us, saying: al-Zubayr recited this verse: {And this Qur'an was revealed to me that I may warn you by it and whomever it reaches} [al-An'am: 19]. Whomever the Qur'an reaches, it is as though he had seen the Messenger of God ﷺ and the prophets before him; then he recited: {And this Qur'an was revealed to me that I may warn you by it and whomever it reaches} [al-An'am: 19]. Al-Zubayr narrated to me, saying: A man came to Ibrahim al-Nakha'i and said: What is your opinion: does the imam on the day of Friday give the sermon sitting or standing? He said to him: How do you read in the Book of God: {And when they see trade or amusement, they scatter to it and leave you} [al-Jumu'a: 11] — standing or sitting? He said: I read it: {standing} [al-Jumu'a: 11]. He said: Then that is it. From al-Zubayr, who said: A man came to Ibrahim al-Nakha'i and said: When my mother wants to perform ablution, she takes the water in her hand, then pours it out, then wipes her face with her palms. He said: Ibrahim al-Nakha'i turned away from him; then he repeated it to him a second time, and he turned away from him; then he repeated it a third time, and Ibrahim turned away from him; then Ibrahim said: Is ablution anything but this? We never knew them to slap their faces with water.

Al-Zubayr narrated to me, from Masruq, who said:

Matn

A Bedouin came to Ibn 'Abbas and said: “I herd my family’s camels, and I am away from them for two nights or three; and a passer-by passes me and asks for water; shall I give him to drink? He said: No. He said: The Bedouin went off; so Ibn 'Abbas said: Bring him back. He said to him: What did you say? He said: I said: I herd my family’s camels and I am away from them for two nights or three, and a passer-by passes me and asks for water; shall I give him to drink? He said: Yes, as much as will carry him on to someone other than you; then ask permission of your masters.”
