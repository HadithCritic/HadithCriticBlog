About the edition.

Edition. ed. Muṣṭafā Ḥasan Abū al-Khayr (Dār Ibn al-Jawzī)

English translation. The English is the working translation supplied with the source export. The export names no translator. The source counts 691 of its 730 reports as supplied bilingual pairs. For 39 reports the export had no English, and the English shown was translated from the Arabic in the working edition. 36 reports carry a note about a gap or a mismatch in the source. Every report here has both Arabic and English.

Corrections. Where the export ran two reports together or lost a heading or page number, the entry was separated or corrected as the printed edition shows it, and a note on the report says so.

Books and chapters. The verified contents index inserts Kitāb al-Ṣamt before the speech chapter; it is a parent heading for sections 10–16, not a hadith-bearing topical section.

Transmitters. Transmitter names are shown as written in each report.
