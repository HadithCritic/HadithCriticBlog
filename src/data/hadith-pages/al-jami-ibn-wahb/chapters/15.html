English
Isnad

Ibn Sam'an reported to me that 'Abd Allah b. Abi Najih informed him from the companions of 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud, from Ibn Mas'ud, who said 'The Messenger of God, ﷺ,

Matn

forbade amulets and charms.' It was said to 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud: 'We know what amulets are, but what are charms?' He replied: 'What some women use to endear themselves to their husbands, like soothsaying and the like.'

English
Isnad

Yahya b. Ayyub reported to me from Ibn Zahr, from Bakr b. Sawadah, from a man of Suda', who said

Matn

We came to the Prophet, ﷺ, twelve men and pledged allegiance, but he left one man without taking his pledge. We said: 'Pledge allegiance to him, O Prophet of God.' He said: 'I will not take his pledge until he removes what he is wearing, for whoever among us wears such a thing is a polytheist as long as he wears it.' We looked and saw that on his upper arm was a strap containing something from the bark of a tree or a piece of a tree.

Isnad

Matn

قَالَ: أَتَيْنَا النَّبِيَّ ، صلى الله عليه وسلم ، اثْنَا عَشَرَ رَجُلًا فَبَايَعْنَا وَتَرَكَ مِنَّا رَجُلًا لَمْ يُبَايِعْهُ، فَقُلْنَا: بَايِعْهُ يَا نَبِيَّ اللَّهِ، قَالَ: «لَنْ أُبَايِعَهُ حَتَّى يَنْزِعَ الَّذِي عَلَيْهِ؛ إِنَّهُ مَنْ كَانَ مِنَّا عَلَيْهِ مِثْلَ الَّذِي عَلَيْهِ كَانَ مُشْرِكًا، مَا كَانَتْ عَلَيْهِ» قَالَ: فَنَظَرْنَا فَإِذَا فِي عَضُدِهِ سَيْرٌ فِيهِ شَيْءٌ مِنْ لِحَا شَجَرَةٍ، أَوْ شَيْءٌ مِنْ شَجَرَةٍ

Isnad

'Amr b. al-Harith reported to me that Bukayr b. 'Abd Allah informed him that his mother told him that

Matn

she sent her brother Makhramah to 'A'ishah, the wife of the Prophet, ﷺ. He was being treated for an ulcer that affects children. When 'A'ishah saw him and finished with him, she noticed two iron anklets on his legs. 'A'ishah said 'Do you think these two anklets will ward off anything that God has decreed for him? Isnad

Hamzah b. 'Abd al-Wahid wrote to me, saying: 'Alqamah b. Abi 'Alqamah reported to me from his mother, from 'A'ishah,

Matn

that she used to be brought children when they were born so that she could invoke God’s blessing upon them. Isnad

Ibn Lahi'ah reported to me from Yazid b. Abi Habib, from Hurmuz, the freedman of 'Amr b. al-'As,

Matn

that … 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr saw the male goats mounting … “What is here? I did not know the wild animals here were so large.” They said, “Yes, but they have been [the Arabic verb is unclear; literally, ‘tied/contracted’], so they mix with the livestock without provoking them.” 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr said, “Small branches …”
