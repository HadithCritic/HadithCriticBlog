English
Isnad

Malik b. Anas reported to me from Muhammad b. 'Amr b. 'Alqamah, from his father, from Bilal b. al-Harith al-Muzani, that the Messenger of God, ﷺ, said:

Matn

“A person may utter a word that pleases God, never imagining how far its effect will reach; God records His pleasure for him because of it until the Day he meets Him. And a person may utter a word that angers God, never imagining how far its effect will reach; God records His anger against him because of it until the Day he meets Him.”

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي مَالِكُ بْنُ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ عَمْرِو بْنِ عَلْقَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ بِلَالِ بْنِ الْحَارِثِ الْمُزَنِيِّ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ «

Matn

إِنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ لَيَتَكَلَّمُ بِالْكَلِمَةِ مِنْ رِضْوَانِ اللَّهِ مَا يَظُنُّ أَنْ تَبْلُغَ مَا بَلَغَتْ يَكْتُبُ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِهَا رِضْوَانَهُ إِلَى يَوْمِ يَلْقَاهُ، وَإِنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ لَيَتَكَلَّمُ بِالْكَلِمَةِ مِنْ سَخَطِ اللَّهِ مَا كَانَ يَظُنُّ أَنْ تَبْلُغَ مَا بَلَغَتْ، يَكْتُبُ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِهَا سَخَطَهُ إِلَى يَوْمِ يَلْقَاهُ»

Isnad

Malik b. Anas reported to me from ‘Abd Allah b. Dinar, from Abi Salih, from Abi Hurayrah, who used to say: '

Matn

A man may speak a word without giving it much thought, and by it he plunges into the Fire of Hell. And a man may speak a word without giving it much thought, and by it God raises him in Paradise.'

English
Isnad

Malik b. Anas narrated to me from ‘Abd Allah b. al-Walid that al-Muttalib b. ‘Abd Allah b. Hantab al-Makhzumi informed him

Matn

that a man asked the Messenger of God, ﷺ: What is backbiting? The Messenger of God, ﷺ, said to him: 'Mentioning something about a person that he would dislike to hear.' The man said: O Messenger of God, even if it is true? The Messenger of God, ﷺ, said: 'If you say something false, that is slander.'

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ وَحَدَّثَنِي مَالِكُ بْنُ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ الْوَلِيدِ، أَنَّ الْمُطَّلِبَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ حَنْطَبٍ الْمَخْزُومِيَّ، أَخْبَرَهُ

Matn

أَنَّ رَجُلًا سَأَلَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: مَا الْغِيبَةُ؟ فَقَالَ لَهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «أَنْ تَذْكُرَ مِنَ الْمَرْءِ مَا يَكْرَهُ أَنْ يَسْمَعَ» ، فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، وَإِنْ كَانَ حَقًّا؟، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ عليه السلام: «إِذَا قُلْتَ بَاطِلًا فَذَلِكَ الْبُهْتَانُ»

The guidance your brother gains through your admonition is better than the worldly benefit he receives through your charity; and if your admonition saves your brother, that is better than your charity saving him from poverty. So admonish those whom you admonish in fulfillment of a duty you owe, and likewise listen when you are admonished. Be like the experienced, knowledgeable physician: if he applies medicine where it should not go, he harms and is blamed; if he withholds it where it should be applied, he is ignorant and sinful. If he is to treat an insane person, he does not do so while the person is unrestrained; he first secures and restrains him, fearing that he may fail to achieve the good he seeks while avoiding the harm he fears. Matn
