English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said al-Harith b. Nabhan informed me from Aban b. Abi Ayyash that Salman entered upon Umar b. al-Khattab, who placed a cushion for him. He sat on it and said: 'Allahu Akbar.' Umar said: 'One of your wonders, O Abu Abdullah.' He said: 'I heard the Messenger of God say:

Matn

When one of you, a Muslim, places something for his brother to honor him and he sits on it... they are both forgiven.'

English
Isnad

And he informed me... from Abdullah b. Abdur-Rahman b. Abi Husayn, from Shahr b. Hawshab, from Abu Idris al-Khawlani, who said

Matn

I said to Mu'adh b. Jabal: 'By God, I love you for the sake of God.' He said: 'Why?' I said: 'For the majesty of God...' until he placed his knees against mine. Then he said: 'Rejoice, for I heard the Messenger of God, ﷺ, say: Those who love each other for the majesty of God are in the shade of God on the Day when there is no shade but His.' Then he mentioned Ubadah b. al-Samit, and I said: 'Will you not listen? I am telling you good news, shall I not tell you?' I said: 'Tell me.' He said: '... My love is due for those who love each other in Me, My love is due for those who give to each other in Me, My love is due for those who... My love is due for those who visit each other in Me.'

English
Isnad

And he narrated to me from Ibrahim b. Nashit... from Abdur-Rahman b. Ghanm, from Abu Malik and Abu Amir,

Matn

that while they were with the Messenger of God, ﷺ, and this verse was revealed: {O you who have believed, do not ask about things which, if they are shown to you, will distress you} [Al-Ma'idah: 101], the Messenger of God, ﷺ, said 'Peace is the attribute of a people who are neither prophets nor martyrs, whom the prophets and martyrs envy for their closeness and position with God on the Day of Resurrection.' They fell silent and did not ask about anything. A Bedouin knelt and said: 'O Messenger of God, tell us about them so we may know them.' I saw the Bedouin say: 'They are servants of God from various lands and tribes, with no kinship ties to connect them, nor worldly possessions to exchange. They love each other with the spirit of God. God will place for them pulpits of red ruby before the Most Merciful. People will be terrified, but they will not be terrified. People will fear, but they will not fear.'

العربية
Isnad

وَحَدَّثَنِي إِبْرَاهِيمُ بْنُ نَشِيطٍ،. . .، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ غَنْمٍ، عَنْ أَبِي مَالِكٍ، وَأَبِي عَامِرٍ

Matn

أَنَّهُمْ بَيْنَمَا هُمْ عِنْدَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ عليه السلام وَقَدْ نَزَلَتْ هَذِهِ الْآيَةُ: {يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تَسْأَلُوا عَنْ أَشْيَاءَ إِنْ تُبْدَ لَكُمْ تَسُؤْكُمْ} [المائدة: 101] " قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ عليه السلام: «السِّلْمُ صِفَةُ قَوْمٍ لَيْسُوا بِأَنْبِيَاءَ وَلَا شُهَدَاءَ يَغْبِطُهُمُ النَّبِيُّونَ وَالشُّهَدَاءُ بِقُرْبِهِمْ وَمَقْعَدِهِمْ مِنَ اللَّهِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ» ، فَسَكَتُوا، فَلَمْ يَسْأَلُوا عَنْ شَيْءٍ وَجَثَا أَعْرَابِيٌّ عَلَى رُكْبَتِيهِ. . . يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، حَتَّى نَعْرِفَهُمْ حَدِّثْنَا عَنْهُمْ، فَرَأَيْتُ الْأَعْرَابِيَّ قَالَ: هُمْ عِبَادُ اللَّهِ مِنْ بُلْدَانٍ شَتَّى، وَقَبَائِلَ شَتَّى، لَمْ يَكُنْ بَيْنَهُمْ أَرْحَامٌ يَتَوَاصَلُونَ بِهَا، وَلَا دُنْيَا يَتَبَاذَلُونَهَا، تَحَابَّوْا بِرُوحِ اللَّهِ، يُجْعَلُ الله لَهُمْ مَنَابِرُ مِنْ لُؤْلُؤٍ قُدَّامَ الرَّحْمَنِ تَعَالَى، يَفْزَعُ النَّاسُ وَلَا يَفْزَعُونَ، وَيَخَافُ النَّاسُ وَلَا يَخَافُونَ "

A Muslim is the brother of another Muslim; he does not wrong him, nor forsake him, nor betray him. Piety is here—pointing to his chest. It is enough evil for a man to despise his Muslim brother.'

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ وَأَخْبَرَنِي هِشَامُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ عليه السلام قَالَ: «

الْمُسْلِمُ أَخُو الْمُسْلِمِ، لَا يَظْلِمُهُ وَلَا يَخْذُلُهُ، وَلَا يُسْلِمُهُ، التَّقْوَى هَاهُنَا، حَسْبُ امْرِيءٍ مِنَ الشَّرِّ أَنْ يَحْقِرَ أَخَاهُ الْمُسْلِمَ»
