Matn

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. ‘Amr reported to me from Ibn Jurayj, from Ibn Shihab, who said about binding teeth with gold: '

Matn

There is no harm in it. ‘Abd al-Malik b. Marwan bound his teeth with gold.'

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ وَأَخْبَرَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَمْرٍو، عَنِ ابْنِ جُرَيْجٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، أَنَّهُ قَالَ فِي رَبْطِ الْأَسْنَانِ بِالذَّهَبِ، قَالَ: «

Matn

Matn
