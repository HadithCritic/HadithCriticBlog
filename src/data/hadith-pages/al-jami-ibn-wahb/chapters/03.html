English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said Ashhal b. Hatim informed me, from Sulayman b. Mughirah, from Thabit al-Bunani, from Anas b. Malik, who said:

Matn

Abu Talhah had a young son called Abu Umayr. The Prophet, ﷺ, said: 'O Abu Umayr, what happened to the nughayr (a small bird)?'

العربية
Isnad

Matn

كَانَ لِأَبِي طَلْحَةَ ابْنٌ صَغِيرٌ يُقَالُ أَبُو عُمَيْرٍ، فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ عليه السلام: «يَا أَبَا عُمَيْرٍ، أَيْنَ النُّغَيْرُ؟»

Isnad

And he informed me from Muawiyah b. Salih, from al-Hasan b. Jabir, that the Messenger of God, ﷺ, said '

Matn

Among the names, you should have Yazid, for there is no one except that he increases in good and evil; al-Harith, for there is no one except that he cultivates for his Hereafter or his worldly life; and Hammam, for there is no one except that he is concerned with his Hereafter or his worldly life. If these names escape you, then name them with names that denote servitude.'

Al-Jāmiʿ fī al-Ḥadīth of Ibn Wahb · Vol. 1, p. 112
English

Hisham b. Sa'd reported to me from Zayd b. Aslam, from his father, who said: “An umm walad (enslaved woman who had borne him a child) belonging to 'Umar b. al-Khattab’s son came to 'Umar and asked him for clothing. He said, ‘Who is your master that he does not clothe you?’ She replied, ‘Abu 'Isa.’ He said, ‘Abu 'Isa?’ Aslam said: I was told, ‘Go and summon him, but do not tell him why.’ 'Umar used to tell us: ‘When I send you to summon someone, do not tell him why; Satan may teach him a lie and he will come to me with it.’ I went to summon him. He asked why, and I said I did not know. He came with me, continuing to ask, until we reached the door. I had found many chickens there; he gave me one in exchange for telling him, so I told him. I entered, and he followed behind me. 'Umar asked him, ‘Did he tell you why I summoned you?’ He said yes. 'Umar called me over, held both my hands in his left hand, and began striking me with his whip. I kept shrinking away. Aslam said: 'Umar kept saying, ‘He is resolute!’ Then he let me go and said, ‘By God, you are making me do things to you that I dislike.’ He then went to his son and began striking him with the whip until he weakened. He said: ‘Do you know the names of the Arabs, you wretch? 'Amir, 'Uwaymir, Malik, Sirm, Muwaylik, and Sararah—that is, Murrah.’ He repeated this two or three times. Then he said: ‘Give up 'Isa, for by God, we know no father of 'Isa.’”

English
Isnad

And he informed me from Haywah b. Shurayh, from Ibn al-Had, from Abdullah b. Dinar, from Abdullah b. Umar,

Matn

that a Bedouin met him on the road to Mecca. Abdullah b. Umar greeted him, let him ride the donkey he was riding, and gave him the turban he was wearing on his head. Ibn Dinar said We said to him: 'May God rectify you, they are Bedouins, and they are content with little.' Abdullah said: 'This man's father was a close friend of Umar b. al-Khattab, and I heard the Messenger of God, ﷺ, say: 'Goodness is the child's maintaining the ties of his father's friends.''
