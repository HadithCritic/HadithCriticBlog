English
Isnad

No isnad: the text is given as a direct statement.

Matn

The text of the juz' of Yazid b. Abi Habib: In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The two shaykhs informed us—the trustworthy Shaykh Abu Muhammad Hibat Allah b. Ahmad b. Muhammad al-Akfani, and Shaykh Abu l-Qasim Yahya b. Bitriq b. Bishr al-Tarasusi, by recitation to him on Friday, the twenty-fourth of Jumada al-Akhirah in the year 520, and Abu Muhammad 'Abd al-Karim b. Hamzah b. al-Khidr al-Sulami—they said: Abu l-Hasan Muhammad b. al-Mikyal b. 'Uthman b. 'Abd Allah al-Azdi al-Misri reported to us, by recitation to him in Damascus, in the Congregational Mosque, in the month of Rabi' al-Awwal of the year 457, saying: The noble Abu l-Qasim al-Maymun b. Hamzah b. al-Husayn al-'Alawi al-Husayni, the trustworthy, informed us from his book, by recitation to him, with the recitation of Khalaf b. Muhammad b. 'Ali al-Wasiti, the hafiz, who said: Abu Bakr Ahmad b. 'Abd al-Warith b. Jarir al-'Assal informed us in Muharram of the year 319, saying: Abu Musa 'Isa b. Hammad Zughbah reported to us in the year 242, saying: al-Layth b. Sa'd informed us, from Yazid b. Abi Habib, from Muhammad b. Ishaq, from Ja'far b. 'Abd Allah b. al-Hakam: that in the past the imam on Fridays would mention nothing but the Qur'an and remembrance (dhikr); then, when the people had reconciled, a governor was appointed over Medina, and he began to curse certain men: “Perhaps, if you live, you will be content with the companionship of those who are worse than they.”

Isnad

Matn

وَالشَّيْحُ أَبُو الْقَاسِمِ يَحْيَى بْنُ بَطْرِيقِ بْنِ بِشْرٍ الطَّرَسُوسِيُّ ، قِرَاءَةً عَلَيْهِ فِي يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ الرَّابِعِ وَالْعِشْرِينَ مِنْ جُمَادَى الآخِرَةِ سَنَةَ عِشْرِينَ وَخَمْسِ مِائَةٍ، وَأَبُو مُحَمَّدٍ عَبْدُ الْكَرِيمِ بْنُ حَمْزَةَ بْنِ الْخَضِرِ السُّلَمِيُّ ، قَالُوا: أَنْبَأَ أَبُو الْحَسَنِ مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْمِكْيَالِ بْنِ عُثْمَانَ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ الأَزْدِيُّ الْمِصْرِيُّ ، قِرَاءَةً عَلَيْهِ بِدِمَشْقَ ، فِي الْمَسْجِدِ الْجَامِعِ فِي شَهْرِ رَبِيعٍ الأَوَّلِ مِنْ سَنَةِ سَبْعٍ وَخَمْسِينَ وَأَرْبَعِ مِائَةٍ، قَالَ: أَنَا الشَّرِيفُ أَبُو الْقَاسِمِ الْمَيْمُونُ بْنُ حَمْزَةَ بْنِ الْحُسَيْنِ الْعَلَوِيُّ الْحُسَيْنِيُّ الثِّقَةُ مِنْ كِتَابِهِ قِرَاءَةً عَلَيْهِ. بِقِرَاءَةِ خَلَفِ بْنِ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ عَلِيٍّ الْوَاسِطِيِّ الْحَافِظِ ، قَالَ: أَنَا أَبُو بَكْرٍ أَحْمَدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الْوَارِثِ بْنِ جَرِيرٍ الْعَسَّالُ فِي الْمُحَرَّمِ سَنَةَ تِسْعَ عَشْرَةَ وَثَلاثِ مِائَةٍ، قَالَ: أَنْبَأَ أَبُو مُوسَى عِيسَى بْنُ حَمَّادٍ زُغْبَةُ سَنَةَ اثْنَتَيْنِ وَأَرْبَعِينَ وَمِائَتَيْنِ، قَالَ: أَنَا اللَّيْثُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ، عَنْ يَزِيدَ بْنِ أَبِي حَبِيبٍ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ جَعْفَرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ الْحَكَمِ أَنَّهُ كَانَ فِيمَا خَلا لا يَذْكُرُ الإِمَامُ يَوْمَ الْجُمُعَةِ إِلا الْقُرْآنَ وَالذِّكْرَ، فَلَمَّا اصْطَلَحَ النَّاسُ أِمِّرَ أَمِيرٌ عَلَى الْمَدِينَةِ فَجَعَلَ يَلْعَنُ رِجَالا لَعَلَّكَ إِنْ بَقِيتَ لَتَرْضَيَنَّ بِصَحَابَةِ مَنْ هُوَ شَرٌّ مِنْهُمْ

English
Isnad

al-Maymun b. Hamzah informed us, saying: Ahmad b. 'Abd al-Warith narrated to us, saying: 'Isa b. Hammad narrated to us, saying: al-Layth informed us, from Yazid b. Abi Habib, from Muhammad b. Ishaq, from Makhul:

Matn

that 'Ali ﵇ used to set fifty each for the front incisors (thanaya), forty each for the lateral incisors (raba'iyyat), thirty each for the canines, and twenty-six each for the molars.

Matn

أَنَّ عَلِيًّا ، عليه السلام ، كَانَ يَجْعَلُ فِي الثَّنَايَا خَمْسِينَ خَمْسِينَ، وَفِي الرَّبَاعِيَّاتِ أَرْبَعِينَ أَرْبَعِينَ، وَفِي الأَنْيَابِ ثَلاثِينَ ثَلاثِينَ، وَفِي الأَضْرَاسِ سِتَّةً وَعِشْرِينَ سِتَّةً وَعِشْرِينَ

English
Isnad

al-Maymun informed us, saying: Ahmad reported to us, saying: 'Isa narrated to us, saying: al-Layth reported to us, from Aban b. Salih:

Matn

that part of the story of Barirah was that the Messenger of God ﷺ entered upon 'A'ishah ﵀ and found meat with her. He said: “What is this meat, 'A'ishah?” She said: “Meat that Barirah gave us as a gift; it had been given to her as charity. The only thing that kept us from serving it is that you do not eat charity.” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “It is charity for Barirah, and it is a gift for us.”

Matn

English
Isnad

al-Maymun informed us, saying: Ahmad narrated to us, saying: 'Isa narrated to us, saying: al-Layth informed us, from Yazid b. Abi Habib, from Sa'id ibn Abi Hilal:

Matn

that the first time a loan ('ariyah) was made subject to guarantee was when the Messenger of God ﷺ summoned Safwan b. Umayyah in the year of the Conquest, as the Messenger of God ﷺ intended to set out for Hunayn. He said: “It has been mentioned to us that you have weapons; give them to us so that we may make use of them in this war of ours.” He said: “By force?” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “No; rather, a loan, guaranteed by us until we bring it back to you.”

Isnad

Matn

أَنَّ أَوَّلَ مَا ضُمِنَتِ الْعَارِيَةُ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ، صلى الله عليه وسلم ، دَعَا صَفْوَانَ بْنَ أُمَيَّةَ عَامَ الْفَتْحِ، وَرَسُولُ اللَّهِ ، صلى الله عليه وسلم ، يُرِيدُ أَنْ يَخْرُجَ إِلَى حُنَيْنٍ. فَقَالَ: «إِنَّهُ قَدْ ذُكِرَتْ لَنَا عِنْدَكَ سِلاحٌ فَأَعْطِنَاهَا نَسْتَعِينَ بِهَا فِي حَرْبِنَا هَذِهِ» ، فَقَالَ: أَقَسْرًا؟ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ، صلى الله عليه وسلم: «بَلْ عَارِيَةٌ هِيَ عَلَيْنَا ضَامِنَةٌ حَتَّى نَأْتِيَكَ بِهَا»

al-Maymun informed us, saying: Ahmad narrated to us, saying: 'Isa narrated to us, saying: al-Layth informed us, from Yazid b. Abi Habib, from Muhammad b. Talhah b. Yazid b. Rukanah,

Matn

of Banu l-Muttalib: that 'Amr b. Dinar, the client (mawla) of Badhan, told him that it had reached him that Safwan b. Umayyah came to the Messenger of God ﷺ in Medina. He said: “Abu Umayyah, what has brought you?” He said: “Messenger of God, the people claim that whoever does not emigrate has no share.” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “I adjure you to go back until you wake up in the valley of Mecca.” So Safwan went out and slept in the mosque, and a man stole his cloak from beneath his head while he was asleep. He woke up, pursued him, and caught him, and brought him to the Messenger of God ﷺ, who ordered that his hand be cut off. He said: “Messenger of God, rather let him go—it is his.” He said: “Why not before you brought him to me, Abu Umayyah?” Then the Messenger of God ﷺ gave the order, and his hand was cut off.

al-Maymun informed us, saying: Ahmad narrated to us, saying: 'Isa narrated to us, saying: al-Layth informed us, from Yazid b. Abi Habib, from Khalid b. Kathir: that Abu Hafs al-'Umari told him that al-Hasan al-Basri told him

Matn

that al-Mughirah b. Shu'bah said to him: The Messenger of God ﷺ went to relieve himself, so I took the water-skin and went with him. When the Messenger of God ﷺ had finished his need, he performed ablution, wearing a jubbah with tight sleeves. The Messenger of God ﷺ tried to bring his forearms out, but could not because of its tightness, so he brought his hand out and placed it over his shoulder; then he washed his face and his hands, and wiped over his leather socks (khuffayn) and the turban. That was the day he prayed behind 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf: the Prophet ﷺ had been delayed in coming out, and he arrived while his Companions were praying behind 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf. 'Abd al-Rahman stepped back, but the Messenger of God ﷺ pushed him forward and prayed behind him. Al-Layth said: It has reached us that the Messenger of God ﷺ went out to the open ground to relieve himself and stayed there a long time; the prayer was called, and 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf stepped forward and led them in one rak'ah and two prostrations. Then the Messenger of God ﷺ came and joined the prayer without their noticing, and bowed with them for one rak'ah. 'Abd al-Rahman gave the closing salutation (salam), and the Messenger of God ﷺ stood and completed the second rak'ah of the dawn prayer; the people were alarmed. When the Messenger of God ﷺ gave the closing salutation, he said: “You have done well.”
