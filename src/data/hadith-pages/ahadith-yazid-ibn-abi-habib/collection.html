About the edition.

Kind of work. Fascicle (juzʾ)

Structure. A juzʾ of numbered narrations. Page 1 carries the transmission of the copy and the first narration. A narration that continues on a later printed page is shown again under the same number, marked as a continuation. The supplied file holds 69 page records.

Catalog header. The first page record repeats the Shamela catalog header (title, author, editor, publisher). It is not reproduced as an entry.

English translation. English translations added in the text_english field of each page; all original Arabic fields are unchanged.

Arabic text. The Arabic is shown exactly as supplied. Where an entry opens with its printed number, the number appears in the label instead of the text. Square brackets and page markers are carried over from the source.

Isnad and matn. Where the Arabic gives a chain before the words, the chain and the words are shown apart and the English is divided at the same point. Entries with no chain say so. The division follows a fixed rule and does not alter the text.

Transmitters. The source supplies no transmitter data, so this edition does not link transmitters to the Rijāl Register.

Dating. Century is based on the compiler/associated authority's death year, not a verified date of composition. The date shown is the death year of Yazid ibn Abi Habib, 129 AH.
