Hadith1/ 15
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 33
English
Isnad

Abū Rajāʾ Qutayba b. Saʿīd narrated to us, from Mālik b. Anas, from Rabīʿa b. Abī ʿAbd al-Raḥmān, from Anas b. Mālik, that he heard him say:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ was neither excessively tall nor short, neither chalk-white (amhaq) nor dark-brown (ādam), neither tightly curled nor lank in hair. God the Exalted sent him at the head of forty years. He stayed ten years in Mecca and ten years in Medina, and God took him at the head of sixty years, with not even twenty white hairs in his head and beard.

العربية

English
Isnad

Muḥammad b. Ismāʿīl narrated to us; he said: Abū Nuʿaym narrated to us; he said: al-Masʿūdī narrated to us, from ʿUthmān b. Muslim b. Hurmuz, from Nāfiʿ b. Jubayr b. Muṭʿim, from ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib, who said:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ was neither tall nor short. He was thick in the hands and feet (shathn al-kaffayn wa-l-qadamayn), large-headed, large-jointed (ḍakhm al-karādīs), and had a long line of hair running down his chest (ṭawīl al-masruba). When he walked he leaned forward, as if descending a slope. I have not seen anyone like him, before or after, ﷺ.

Hadith7/ 15
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 36
English
Isnad

Aḥmad b. ʿAbda al-Ḍabbī al-Baṣrī, ʿAlī b. Ḥujr, and Abū Jaʿfar Muḥammad b. al-Ḥusayn, who is Ibn Abī Ḥalīma, narrated to us, the meaning being one; they said: ʿĪsā b. Yūnus narrated to us, from ʿUmar b. ʿAbd Allāh, mawlā of Ghufra; he said: Ibrāhīm b. Muḥammad, of the descendants of ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib, narrated to me; he said:

Matn

When ʿAlī described the Messenger of God ﷺ he would say: “He was not excessively tall and gangling (mumaghghaṭ), nor short and squat (mutaraddid); among people he was of medium height. His hair was neither tightly curled nor lank, but wavy and curling (jaʿd rajil). He was not heavy-bodied (muṭahham) nor round-faced to excess (mukalthum), though his face had some roundness. He was white tinged with red (abyaḍ mushrab), with very black eyes (adʿaj al-ʿaynayn), long eyelashes (ahdab al-ashfār), large joints (jalīl al-mushāsh) and a prominent upper back (kātad). He was free of body hair (ajrad) apart from the line of hair on his chest (masruba), and thick in the hands and feet. When he walked he lifted his feet as if descending a slope, and when he turned he turned with his whole body. Between his shoulders was the seal of prophethood, and he is the seal of the prophets. He was the most generous of people in heart (ajwad al-nās ṣadran), the most truthful in speech, the gentlest in disposition, and the noblest in companionship. Whoever saw him suddenly stood in awe of him, and whoever mixed with him, knowing him, loved him. His describer says: I have not seen anyone like him, before or after, ﷺ.”

العربية

حدثنا أحمد بن عبدة الضبي البصري ، وعلي بن حجر ، وأبو جعفر محمد بن الحسين وهو ابن أبي حليمة ، والمعنى واحد - قالوا : حدثنا عيسى بن يونس عن عمر بن عبد الله مولى غفرة ، قال : حدثني إبراهيم بن محمد - من ولد علي بن أبي طالب - قال : كان علي إذا وصف رسول الله - صلى الله عليه وسلم - قال : « لم يكن بالطويل الممغط ولا بالقصير المتردد ، وكان ربعة من القوم . لم يكن بالجعد القطط ولا بالسبط ، كان جعدا رجلا . ولم يكن بالمطهم ولا بالمكلثم ، وكان في وجهه تدوير . أبيض مشرب ، أدعج العينين ، أهدب الأشفار ، جليل المشاش والكتد . أجرد ذو مسربة ، شثن الكفين والقدمين . إذا مشى تقلع كأنما ينحط في صبب ، وإذا التفت التفت معا . بين كتفيه خاتم النبوة ، وهو خاتم النبيين . أجود الناس صدرا ، وأصدق الناس لهجة ، وألينهم عريكة ، وأكرمهم عشرة . من رآه بديهة هابه ، ومن خالطه معرفة أحبه . يقول ناعته : لم أر قبله ولا بعده مثله ، صلى الله عليه وسلم

Hadith8/ 15
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 38
English
Isnad

Sufyān b. Wakīʿ narrated to us; he said: Jumayʿ b. ʿUmar b. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-ʿIjlī narrated to us, dictating to us from his book; he said: A man of Banū Tamīm, of the descendants of Abū Hāla, the husband of Khadīja, with the kunya Abū ʿAbd Allāh, informed me, from a son of Abū Hāla, from al-Ḥasan b. ʿAlī, who said: I asked my maternal uncle Hind b. Abī Hāla, who was a masterly describer, about the appearance of the Prophet ﷺ, and I wished that he would describe some of it for me so that I might hold on to it. He said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ was majestic and held in great esteem (fakhman mufakhkhaman). His face shone like the moon on the night of the full moon. He was taller than a man of medium height and shorter than a very tall man (mushadhdhab). He had a large head and wavy hair (rajil al-shaʿr). If his forelock (ʿaqīqa) parted, he left it parted; otherwise his hair did not go beyond the lobes of his ears when he let it grow full. His complexion was radiant (azhar al-lawn) and his forehead broad. His eyebrows were finely arched and full, without meeting, and between them was a vein that anger caused to swell. His nose was aquiline (aqnā al-ʿirnīn) and a light rested on it, so that whoever did not look closely thought him high-bridged (ashamm). His beard was thick, his cheeks smooth, his mouth wide (ḍaliʿ al-fam), his teeth spaced (mufallaj al-asnān), and the line of hair on his chest fine. His neck was like the neck of an ivory statue (dumya) in the purity of silver. His build was well proportioned, solid and compact (bādin mutamāsik), with belly and chest level. His chest was broad, he was wide between the shoulders and large-jointed (ḍakhm al-karādīs), and his bare skin was luminous (anwar al-mutajarrad). A line of hair joined the upper chest to the navel, running like a thread, and his breasts and belly were bare of hair apart from that. He had hair on his forearms, shoulders and upper chest. His forearms were long, his palm wide, his hands and feet thick (shathn), and his extremities long (sāʾil al-aṭrāf), or he said: lifted (shāʾil al-aṭrāf). The hollows of his insteps were deep (khumṣān al-akhmaṣayn) and his feet smooth (masīḥ al-qadamayn), so that water slid off them. When he moved, he moved with force, lifting his feet (qalʿan), stepping forward with a lean (takaffiyan) and walking with ease (hawnan). His stride was long and quick (dharīʿ al-mishya), as if he were descending a slope when he walked, and when he turned he turned with his whole body. He lowered his gaze; his looking toward the ground was longer than his looking toward the sky, and most of his looking was in glances (mulāḥaẓa). He made his Companions walk ahead of him, and he was the first to greet whomever he met with the salām.

العربية
