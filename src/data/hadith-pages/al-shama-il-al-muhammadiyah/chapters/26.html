Isnad

Muḥammad b. Sahl b. ʿAskar and ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān narrated to us; they said: Yaḥyā b. Ḥassān narrated to us; he said: Sulaymān b. Bilāl narrated to us, from Hishām b. ʿUrwa, from his father, from ʿĀʾisha, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“What an excellent condiment (idām) vinegar is.” ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān said in his report: “What an excellent condiment, idām or udum, vinegar is.”
