English
Isnad

Suwayd b. Naṣr narrated to us; he said: ʿAbd Allāh b. al-Mubārak narrated to us, from Ḥammād b. Salama, from Thābit, from Muṭarrif — that is, Ibn ʿAbd Allāh b. al-Shikhkhīr — from his father, who said:

Matn

I came to the Messenger of God ﷺ while he was praying, and in his chest was a bubbling sound (azīz) like the bubbling of a cooking pot, from weeping.

English
Isnad

Maḥmūd b. Ghaylān narrated to us; he said: Muʿāwiya b. Hishām narrated to us; he said: Sufyān narrated to us, from al-Aʿmash, from Ibrāhīm, from ʿUbayda, from ʿAbd Allāh b. Masʿūd, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said to me: “Recite to me!” I said: “Messenger of God, shall I recite to you when it was revealed to you?” He said: “I love to hear it from someone other than myself!” So I recited Sūrat al-Nisāʾ until I reached: {And We brought you as a witness over these}. He said: Then I saw the eyes of the Messenger of God ﷺ overflowing with tears.

Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 182
English
Isnad

Qutayba narrated to us; he said: Jarīr narrated to us, from ʿAṭāʾ b. al-Sāʾib, from his father, from ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAmr, who said:

Matn

The sun was eclipsed one day in the time of the Messenger of God ﷺ, and the Messenger of God ﷺ stood to pray, so long that he hardly bowed; then he bowed. He hardly raised his head; then he raised his head. He hardly prostrated; then he prostrated. He hardly raised his head; then he raised his head. He hardly prostrated; then he prostrated. He hardly raised his head, and he began to blow and weep, saying: “My Lord, did You not promise me that You would not punish them while I am among them? My Lord, did You not promise me that You would not punish them while they seek forgiveness? And we seek Your forgiveness.” When he had prayed two rakʿas, the sun cleared. He stood and praised God, the Exalted, and extolled Him, then said: “The sun and the moon are two of the signs of God; they are not eclipsed for the death of anyone nor for his life. So when they are eclipsed, hasten to the remembrance of God.”

العربية

حدثنا قتيبة قال : حدثنا جرير عن عطاء بن السائب ، عن أبيه ، عن عبد الله بن عمرو قال : انكسفت الشمس يوما على عهد رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فقام رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم يصلي ، حتى لم يكد يركع ثم ركع . فلم يكد يرفع رأسه ، ثم رفع رأسه . فلم يكد أن يسجد ، ثم سجد . فلم يكد أن يرفع رأسه ، ثم رفع رأسه . فلم يكد أن يسجد ، ثم سجد . فلم يكد أن يرفع رأسه ، فجعل ينفخ ويبكي ، ويقول : « رب ، ألم تعدني أن لا تعذبهم وأنا فيهم ؟ رب ، ألم تعدني أن لا تعذبهم وهم يستغفرون ؟ ونحن نستغفرك » . فلما صلى ركعتين انجلت الشمس . فقام فحمد الله تعالى وأثنى عليه ، ثم قال : « إن الشمس والقمر آيتان من آيات الله ، لا ينكسفان لموت أحد ولا لحياته ، فإذا انكسفا فافزعوا إلى ذكر الله .

English
Isnad

Maḥmūd b. Ghaylān narrated to us; he said: Abū Aḥmad narrated to us; he said: Sufyān narrated to us, from ʿAṭāʾ b. al-Sāʾib, from ʿIkrima, from Ibn ʿAbbās, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ was given a daughter of his who was dying; he embraced her and placed her in front of him, and she died while she was in front of him. Umm Ayman cried out, and he ﷺ said: “Do you weep in the presence of the Messenger of God?” She said: “Do I not see you weeping?” He said: “I am not weeping; it is only mercy. The believer is in every good in every state: his soul is drawn out from between his sides while he praises God, Mighty and Majestic.”

Hadith327/ 6
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 184
English
Isnad

Isḥāq b. Manṣūr narrated to us; he said: Abū ʿĀmir informed us; he said: Fulayḥ — that is, Ibn Sulaymān — narrated to us, from Hilāl b. ʿAlī, from Anas b. Mālik, who said:

Matn

We attended the burial of a daughter of the Messenger of God ﷺ while the Messenger of God was sitting at the grave, and I saw his eyes shedding tears. He said: “Is there among you a man who did not have marital relations last night?” Abū Ṭalḥa said: “I.” He said: “Go down,” and he went down into her grave.

العربية
