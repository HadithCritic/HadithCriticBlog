Hadith54/ 17
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 59
English
Isnad

Muḥammad b. Ḥumayd al-Rāzī narrated to us; he said: al-Faḍl b. Mūsā, Abū Tumayla and Zayd b. al-Ḥubāb narrated to us, from ʿAbd al-Muʾmin b. Khālid, from ʿAbd Allāh b. Burayda, from Umm Salama, who said:

Matn

The garment most beloved to the Messenger of God ﷺ was the shirt (qamīṣ).

Isnad

Abū ʿAmmār al-Ḥusayn b. Ḥurayth narrated to us; he said: Abū Nuʿaym narrated to us; he said: Zuhayr narrated to us, from ʿUrwa b. ʿAbd Allāh b. Qushayr, from Muʿāwiya b. Qurra, from his father, who said:

Matn

I came to the Messenger of God ﷺ with a group from Muzayna to pledge allegiance to him, and his shirt was unfastened — or he said: the button of his shirt was unfastened. He said: I put my hand into the opening of his shirt and touched the seal.

Hadith60/ 17
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 61
English
Isnad

Suwayd b. Naṣr narrated to us; he said: ʿAbd Allāh b. al-Mubārak narrated to us, from Saʿīd b. Iyās al-Jurayrī, from Abū Naḍra, from Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī, who said:

Matn

When the Messenger of God ﷺ put on a new garment, he would call it by its name, whether a turban, a shirt or a cloak, and then say: “O God, to You belongs praise, as You have clothed me with it. I ask You for its good and the good of that for which it was made, and I seek refuge in You from its evil and the evil of that for which it was made.”

العربية
