English
Isnad

Aḥmad b. Manīʿ narrated to us; he said: ʿAbbād b. al-ʿAwwām narrated to us; he said: al-Ḥajjāj, that is Ibn Arṭāt, informed us, from Simāk b. Ḥarb, from Jābir b. Samura; he said:

Matn

The shins of the Messenger of God ﷺ were slender (ḥamūsha), and he laughed only in a smile. Whenever I looked at him I would say: his eyes are lined with kohl, though they were not.

العربية

Permalink ↗
Hadith229/ 9
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 135
English
Isnad

Abū ʿAmmār al-Ḥusayn b. Ḥurayth narrated to us; he said: Wakīʿ narrated to us; he said: al-Aʿmash narrated to us, from al-Maʿrūr b. Suwayd, from Abū Dharr; he said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“I know the first man to enter Paradise and the last man to leave the Fire. A man will be brought on the Day of Resurrection, and it will be said: ‘Present to him his minor sins,’ while his major sins are hidden from him. It will be said to him: ‘On such a day you did such and such,’ and he admits it and denies nothing, fearful for his major sins. Then it will be said: ‘Give him a good deed in place of every evil deed he did.’ He will say: ‘I have sins that I do not see here.’” Abū Dharr said: I saw the Messenger of God ﷺ laugh until his molars showed.

العربية

حدثنا أبو عمار الحسين بن حريث قال : حدثنا وكيع قال : حدثنا الأعمش عن المعرور بن سويد ، عن أبي ذر قال : قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : إني لأعلم أول رجل يدخل الجنة وآخر رجل يخرج من النار ! يؤتى بالرجل يوم القيامة ، فيقال : اعرضوا عليه صغار ذنوبه ويخبأ عنه كبارها ، فيقال له : عملت يوم كذا وكذا كذا ، وهو مقر لا ينكر ، وهو مشفق من كبارها . فيقال : أعطوه مكان كل سيئة عملها حسنة ! فيقول : إن لي ذنوبا ما أراها هاهنا » قال أبو ذر : فلقد رأيت رسول الله - صلى الله عليه وسلم - ضحك حتى بدت نواجذه

English
Isnad

Hannād b. al-Sarī narrated to us; he said: Abū Muʿāwiya narrated to us, from al-Aʿmash, from Ibrāhīm, from ʿUbayda al-Salmānī, from ʿAbd Allāh b. Masʿūd; he said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“I know the last of the people of the Fire to come out of it: a man who comes out crawling, and it is said to him: ‘Go and enter Paradise.’” He said: “He goes to enter Paradise and finds that the people have taken the dwellings. He returns and says: ‘My Lord, the people have taken the dwellings.’ It is said to him: ‘Do you remember the time you were in?’ He says: ‘Yes.’” He said: “It is said to him: ‘Make a wish.’” He said: “So he wishes. It is said to him: ‘You have what you wished, and ten times the like of the world.’” He said: “He says: ‘Do You mock me, when You are the King?’” He said: I saw the Messenger of God ﷺ laugh until his molars showed.

English
Isnad

Qutayba b. Saʿīd narrated to us; he said: Abū al-Aḥwaṣ narrated to us, from Abū Isḥāq, from ʿAlī b. Rabīʿa; he said:

Matn

I witnessed ʿAlī being brought a riding animal to mount. When he placed his foot in the stirrup he said: “In the name of God.” When he settled on its back he said: “Praise be to God.” Then he said: {Glory to Him who has subjected this to us, though we could not have subdued it; and to our Lord we shall return}. Then he said: “Praise be to God” three times, and “God is greatest” three times, “Glory to You, I have wronged myself, so forgive me, for none forgives sins except You.” Then he laughed. I said to him: “At what did you laugh, Commander of the Believers?” He said: “I saw the Messenger of God ﷺ do as I did, then laugh. I said: ‘At what did you laugh, Messenger of God?’ He said: ‘Your Lord marvels at His servant when he says: My Lord, forgive me my sins; none forgives sins but You.’”

العربية

English
Isnad

Muḥammad b. Bashshār narrated to us; he said: Muḥammad b. ʿAbd Allāh al-Anṣārī narrated to us; he said: ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAwn narrated to us, from Muḥammad b. Muḥammad b. al-Aswad, from ʿĀmir b. Saʿd; he said: Saʿd said:

Matn

I saw the Prophet ﷺ laugh on the day of the Trench until his molars showed. He said: I said: “How was that?” He said: “A man had a shield with him, and Saʿd was an archer. The man would do like this with the shield, covering his forehead, and Saʿd drew an arrow against him. When the man raised his head, he shot him and did not miss this spot of him,” meaning his forehead, “and the man fell over and kicked up his leg. And the Prophet ﷺ laughed until his molars showed.” He said: I said: “At what did he laugh?” He said: “At what he had done to the man.”

العربية

حدثنا محمد بن بشار قال : حدثنا محمد بن عبد الله الأنصاري قال : حدثنا عبد الله بن عون عن محمد بن محمد بن الأسود ، عن عامر بن سعد قال : قال سعد : لقد رأيت النبي - صلى الله عليه وسلم - ضحك يوم الخندق حتى بدت نواجذه . قال : قلت : كيف كان ؟ قال : كان رجل معه ترس ، وكان سعد راميا ، وكان يقول كذا وكذا بالترس يغطي جبهته ، فنزع له سعد بسهم . فلما رفع رأسه رماه فلم يخطئ هذه منه يعني جبهته ، وانقلب الرجل ، وشال برجله . « فضحك النبي - صلى الله عليه وسلم - حتى بدت نواجذه » . قال : قلت : من أي شيء ضحك ؟ قال : من فعله بالرجل
