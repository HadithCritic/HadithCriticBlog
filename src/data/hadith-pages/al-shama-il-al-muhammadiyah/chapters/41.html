English
Isnad

Maḥmūd b. Ghaylān narrated to us; he said: Abū Dāwūd al-Ṭayālisī narrated to us; he said: Shuʿba narrated to us, from Yazīd al-Rishk; he said: I heard Muʿādha say: I said to ʿĀʾisha:

Matn

“Did the Prophet ﷺ pray the forenoon prayer (ḍuḥā)?” She said: “Yes, four rakʿas, and he would add as much as God, Mighty and Majestic, willed.”

English
Isnad

Muḥammad b. al-Muthannā narrated to us; he said: Muḥammad b. Jaʿfar narrated to us; he said: Shuʿba narrated to us, from ʿAmr b. Murra, from ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Abī Laylā, who said:

Matn

No one informed me that he had seen the Prophet ﷺ pray the forenoon prayer except Umm Hāniʾ, for she narrated that the Messenger of God ﷺ entered her house on the day of the conquest of Mecca and bathed, then prayed eight rakʿas; I never saw him ﷺ perform a lighter prayer than it, except that he completed the bowing and the prostration.

Isnad

Aḥmad b. Manīʿ narrated to us, from Hushaym; he said: ʿUbayda narrated to us, from Ibrāhīm, from Sahm b. Minjāb, from Qarthaʿ al-Ḍabbī — or from Qazaʿa, from Qarthaʿ — from Abū Ayyūb al-Anṣārī, that the Prophet ﷺ

Matn

used to persist in praying four rakʿas when the sun passed its zenith. I said: “Messenger of God, you persist in these four rakʿas when the sun passes its zenith!” He said: “The gates of heaven are opened when the sun passes its zenith, and they are not closed until the noon prayer is prayed, and I love that something good ascend for me at that hour.” I said: “Is there recitation in all of them?” He said: “Yes.” I said: “Is there a separating salutation between them?” He said: “No.”
