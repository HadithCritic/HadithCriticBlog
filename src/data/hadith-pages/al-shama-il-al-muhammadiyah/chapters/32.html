Hadith209/ 10
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 126
English
Isnad

Abū Kurayb Muḥammad b. al-ʿAlāʾ and Muḥammad b. Ṭarīf al-Kūfī narrated to us; they said: Ibn Fuḍayl narrated to us, from al-Aʿmash, from ʿAbd al-Malik b. Maysara, from al-Nazzāl b. Sabra; he said:

Matn

A jug of water was brought to ʿAlī while he was in the open court (raḥba). He took a handful from it and washed his hands, rinsed his mouth, sniffed water into his nose, and wiped his face, his forearms and his head, then drank while standing. Then he said: “This is the ablution of one who has not broken his wuḍūʾ. This is how I saw the Messenger of God ﷺ do.”
