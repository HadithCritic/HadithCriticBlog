Isnad

Qutayba b. Saʿīd and Bishr b. Muʿādh narrated to us; they said: Abū ʿAwāna narrated to us, from Ziyād b. ʿIlāqa, from al-Mughīra b. Shuʿba; he said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ prayed until his feet swelled. It was said to him: “Do you burden yourself with this when God has forgiven you what came before of your sin and what is to come?” He said: “Shall I not be a thankful servant?”

Isnad

Muḥammad b. Bashshār narrated to us; he said: Muḥammad b. Jaʿfar narrated to us; he said: Shuʿba narrated to us, from Abū Isḥāq, from al-Aswad b. Yazīd; he said:

Matn

I asked ʿĀʾisha about the night prayer of the Messenger of God ﷺ, and she said: “He would sleep at the beginning of the night, then stand to pray. At the last part of the night he would pray the witr, then go to his bed. If he had need, he would be with his wife. When he heard the call to prayer he would jump up. If he was in a state of major impurity (junub), he poured water over himself; otherwise he performed ablution and went out to the prayer.”

Isnad

Qutayba b. Saʿīd narrated to us, from Mālik b. Anas (ḥ). And Isḥāq b. Mūsā al-Anṣārī narrated to us; he said: Maʿn narrated to us, from Mālik, from Makhrama b. Sulaymān, from Kurayb, from Ibn ʿAbbās, that he informed him

Matn

that he spent the night with Maymūna, who was his maternal aunt. He said: “I lay down across the width of the cushion, and the Messenger of God ﷺ lay down along its length. The Messenger of God ﷺ slept until, when the night was half over, or a little before it or a little after it, the Messenger of God ﷺ woke and began to wipe the sleep from his face. Then he recited the last ten verses of Sūrat Āl ʿImrān, then stood up to a hanging water-skin (shann) and performed ablution from it, performing the ablution well, then stood to pray.” ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAbbās said: “I stood at his side, and the Messenger of God ﷺ placed his right hand on my head, then took hold of my right ear and twisted it. He prayed two rakʿas, then two, then two, then two, then two, then two” — Maʿn said: six times — “then prayed witr, then lay down until the muʾadhdhin came to him. He stood and prayed two light rakʿas, then went out and prayed the dawn prayer.”
