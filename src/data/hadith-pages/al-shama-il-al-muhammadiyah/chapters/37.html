that the Prophet ﷺ entered Mecca for the ʿumra of the fulfillment (ʿumrat al-qaḍāʾ), with Ibn Rawāḥa reciting verse before him and saying: “Al-khalīl khalīluhu…” [the verse is incomplete in the source text]. ʿUmar said to him: “Ibn Rawāḥa, in the presence of the Messenger of God ﷺ and in the sanctuary of God, you recite poetry?” The Prophet ﷺ said: “Leave him, ʿUmar, for it works on them faster than the shower of arrows.”

Isnad

Ismāʿīl b. Mūsā al-Fazārī and ʿAlī b. Ḥujr narrated to us, the meaning being one; they said: ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Abī al-Zinād narrated to us, from Hishām b. ʿUrwa, from his father, from ʿĀʾisha; she said:
