Hadith37/ 8
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 52
English
Isnad

Muḥammad b. Bashshār narrated to us; he said: Abū Dāwūd informed us; he said: Hammām informed us, from Qatāda; he said:

Matn

I said to Anas b. Mālik: “Did the Messenger of God ﷺ dye his hair?” He said: “He had not reached that point. There was only some grey in his temples. Abū Bakr, however, dyed his hair with henna and katam.”

English
Isnad

Abū Kurayb Muḥammad b. al-ʿAlāʾ narrated to us; he said: Muʿāwiya b. Hishām narrated to us, from Shaybān, from Abū Isḥāq, from ʿIkrima, from Ibn ʿAbbās, who said:

Abū Bakr said: “Messenger of God, you have turned grey!” He said: “Hūd, al-Wāqiʿa, al-Mursalāt, {About what are they asking one another?} and {When the sun is wrapped up} have turned me grey.”

Hadith44/ 8
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 55
English
Isnad

Aḥmad b. Manīʿ narrated to us; he said: Surayj b. al-Nuʿmān narrated to us; he said: Ḥammād b. Salama narrated to us, from Simāk b. Ḥarb; he said:

Matn

Jābir b. Samura was asked: “Was there grey hair in the head of the Messenger of God ﷺ?” He said: “There was no grey in the head of the Messenger of God ﷺ except a few hairs at the parting of his head, and when he oiled it, the oil concealed them.”
