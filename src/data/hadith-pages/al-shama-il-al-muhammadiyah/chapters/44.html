English
Isnad

Qutayba b. Saʿīd narrated to us; he said: al-Layth narrated to us, from Ibn Abī Mulayka, from Yaʿlā b. Mamlak, that he

Matn

asked Umm Salama about the recitation of the Messenger of God ﷺ, and she described “a recitation made distinct, letter by letter.”

Hadith316/ 8
Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 179
English
Isnad

ʿAlī b. Ḥujr narrated to us; he said: Yaḥyā b. Saʿīd al-Umawī narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Ibn Abī Mulayka, from Umm Salama, who said:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ used to divide his recitation into verses: he would say, {Praise be to God, Lord of the worlds}, then stop; then say, {the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate}, then stop; and he used to recite {Master (Malik) of the Day of Judgment}.

Isnad

Qutayba narrated to us; he said: al-Layth narrated to us, from Muʿāwiya b. Ṣāliḥ, from ʿAbd Allāh b. Abī Qays, who said:

Matn

I asked ʿĀʾisha about the recitation of the Prophet ﷺ: “Did he recite silently or aloud?” She said: “He did all of that; sometimes he recited silently and sometimes aloud.” I said: “Praise be to God, who made room in the matter.”

Shamāʾil al-Tirmidhī · p. 181
English
Isnad

Qutayba b. Saʿīd narrated to us; he said: Nūḥ b. Qays al-Ḥuddānī narrated to us, from Ḥusām b. Miṣakk, from Qatāda, who said:

Matn

God did not send a prophet except one with a handsome face and a beautiful voice. And your Prophet ﷺ had a handsome face and a beautiful voice, and he did not use quavering (tarjīʿ) in his recitation.

English
Isnad

ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān narrated to us; he said: Yaḥyā b. Ḥassān narrated to us; he said: ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Abī al-Zinād narrated to us, from ʿAmr b. Abī ʿAmr, from ʿIkrima, from Ibn ʿAbbās, who said:
