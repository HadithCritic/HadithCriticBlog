Narration1/ 8
Wakīʿ’s Copy from al-Aʿmash · p. 97
English
Isnad

Abu l-Hasan narrated to us, saying: Ahmad narrated to us, saying: al-Hasan b. Ahmad b. Abi Shu'ayb al-Harrani narrated to us, saying: Suwayd b. Sa'id al-Hadathani narrated to us, saying: Mu'tamir b. Sulayman narrated to us, from Talha b. 'Amr, from 'Ata', from Abu Hurayra ﵁, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Visit at intervals (ghibban), and you will increase in affection.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْحَسَنِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ أَحْمَدَ بْنِ أَبِي شُعَيْبٍ الْحَرَّانِيُّ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا سُوَيْدُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ الْحَدَثَانِيُّ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا مُعْتَمِرُ بْنُ سُلَيْمَانَ، عَنْ طَلْحَةَ بْنِ عَمْرٍو، عَنْ عَطَاءٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، رضي الله عنه قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: ‌

Matn

‌«زُرْ غِبًّا، تَزْدَدْ حُبًّا»

Wakīʿ’s Copy from al-Aʿmash · p. 98
English
Isnad

Abu l-Hasan narrated to us, saying: Ahmad narrated to us, saying: al-Hasan b. Ahmad narrated to us, saying: Suwayd narrated to us, saying: Abu Bakr b. 'Ayyash narrated to us, from Sa'id ibn 'Abd al-Karim, from Abu 'Ammar, from Anas ﵁, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Seeking knowledge is an obligation upon every Muslim; and the seeker of knowledge — or: the possessor of knowledge — is asked forgiveness for by everything, even the fish in the sea.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْحَسَنِ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ أَحْمَدَ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا سُوَيْدٌ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ عَيَّاشٍ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الْكَرِيمِ، عَنْ أَبِي عَمَّارِ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، رضي الله عنه، قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: ‌

Matn

‌«طَلَبُ الْعِلْمِ فَرِيضَةٌ عَلَى كُلِّ مُسْلِمٍ، وَطَالِبُ الْعِلْمِ أَوْ صَاحِبُ الْعِلْمِ يَسْتَغْفِرُ لَهُ كُلُّ شَيْءٍ حَتَّى الْحُوتُ فِي الْبَحْرِ»

Narration3/ 8
Wakīʿ’s Copy from al-Aʿmash · p. 99
English
Isnad

Abu l-Hasan narrated to us, saying: Ahmad narrated to us: al-Hasan b. Ahmad narrated to us: Suwayd b. Sa'id narrated to us: Baqiyya b. al-Walid narrated to me, from Abu Bakr b. Abi Maryam, from 'Atiyya b. Qays, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Servants have not spoken any speech more beloved to God than His speech” — meaning the Qur'an — “nor have they returned to God any speech more beloved to Him than His speech.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْحَسَنِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ، حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ أَحْمَدَ، حَدَّثَنَا سُوَيْدُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنِي بَقِيَّةُ بْنُ الْوَلِيدِ، عَنْ أَبِي بَكْرِ بْنِ أَبِي مَرْيَمَ، عَنْ عَطِيَّةَ بْنِ قَيْسٍ، قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: ‌

Matn

‌«مَا تَكَلَّمَ الْعِبَادُ بِكَلَامٍ أَحَبَّ إِلَى اللَّهِ مِنْ كَلَامِهِ: يَعْنِي الْقُرْآنَ، وَلَا رَدُّوا إِلَى اللَّهِ كَلَامًا أَحَبَّ إِلَيْهِ مِنْ كَلَامِهِ»

Narration4/ 8
Wakīʿ’s Copy from al-Aʿmash · p. 99
English
Isnad

Abu l-Hasan narrated to us, saying: Ahmad narrated to us, saying: al-Hasan b. Ahmad narrated to us: Muhammad b. Jarir narrated to me: al-Fadl b. Sukhayt — who used to be called Sindi, because he was the son of a man [associated] with Sind — narrated to us, saying: 'Abd Allah b. Muhammad al-Ghanawi narrated to me, from 'Amr b. Jumay', from Maymun b. Mihran, from Ibn 'Abbas ﵁, who said:

Matn

“The Khawarij said to 'Ali b. Abi Talib ﵇: Have you set up two arbitrators? He said: ‘I have not made a created being arbiter; I have made only the Qur'an arbiter.’”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْحَسَنِ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ أَحْمَدَ، حَدَّثَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ جَرِيرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا الْفَضْلُ بْنُ سُخَيْتٍ، وَكَانَ، يُقَالُ لَهُ: سِنْدِيٌّ؛ لِابْنِ رَجُلٍ إِلَى السِّنْدِ، قَالَ:

Matn

حَدَّثَنِي عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ الْغَنَوِيُّ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ جُمَيْعٍ عَنْ مَيْمُونِ بْنِ مِهْرَانَ عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رضي الله عنه، قَالَ: «قَالَتِ الْخَوَارِجُ لِعَلِيِّ بْنِ أَبِي طَالِبٍ عليه السلام: حَكَّمْتَ الْحَكَمَيْنِ؟ قَالَ:»‌ ‌ مَا حَكَّمْتُ مَخْلُوقًا، إِنَّمَا حَكَّمْتُ الْقُرْآنَ "

Transmission overview

No path data

No separate transmission-path annotation is supplied for this report.

Translator note

The explanation of the nickname Sindi (لِابْنِ رَجُلٍ إِلَى السِّنْدِ) is syntactically unclear in the Arabic; translated closely, with the bracketed words supplied for sense.

Narration5/ 8
Wakīʿ’s Copy from al-Aʿmash · p. 100
English
Isnad

Abu l-Hasan narrated to us, saying: Ahmad narrated to us, saying: al-Hasan b. Abi Sa'id narrated to us: Suwayd b. Sa'id narrated to me: al-Mu'tamir b. Sulayman narrated to us, from his father, from Abu 'Imran, from Nawf,

Matn

that Musa b. 'Imran ﵇, when he heard the Speech, said: Who are you that speak to me? He said: I am your Lord, the Most High.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْحَسَنِ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنِي سُوَيْدُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا الْمُعْتَمِرُ بْنُ سُلَيْمَانَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ أَبِي عِمْرَانُ، عَنْ نَوْفٍ،‌ ‌ أَنَّ مُوسَى بْنَ عِمْرَانَ، عليه السلام، لَمَّا سَمِعَ الْكَلَامَ، قَالَ:

Matn

مَنْ أَنْتَ الَّذِي تُكَلِّمُنِي؟ قَالَ: أَنَا رَبُّكَ الْأَعْلَى

Transmission overview

No path data

No separate transmission-path annotation is supplied for this report.

Translator note

The name “al-Hasan b. Abi Sa'id” is printed as such here, where the parallel chains have “al-Hasan b. Ahmad”; translated as printed.

Narration6/ 8
Wakīʿ’s Copy from al-Aʿmash · p. 100
English
Isnad

Abu l-Hasan narrated to us, saying: Ahmad narrated to us: al-Hasan b. Ahmad narrated to us: Muhammad b. Bakkar narrated to us: Abu Ma'shar narrated to us, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Mu'awiya, who said:

Matn

God, Mighty and Majestic, spoke to Musa ﵇ only to the extent that he could bear of His speech; and had He spoken with His [full] speech, nothing would have been able to bear it.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْحَسَنِ، قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ، حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ أَحْمَدَ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَكَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مَعْشَرٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ مُعَاوِيَةَ، قَالَ:‌ ‌

Matn

إِنَّمَا كَلَّمَ اللَّهُ عز وجل مُوسَى عليه السلام بِقَدْرِ مَا يُطِيقُ مِنْ كَلَامِهِ، وَلَوْ تَكَلَّمَ بِكَلَامِهِ لَمْ يُطِقْهُ شَيْءٌ

Narration7/ 8
Wakīʿ’s Copy from al-Aʿmash · p. 101
English
Isnad

Abu l-Hasan narrated to us, saying: Ahmad narrated to us, saying: Abu Shu'ayb recited:

Matn

[Meter: al-kamil] They struck Ibn Hanbal with whips in their wrongdoing — a curse [upon them] — and he was made steadfast by the most luminous exposition (bayan). The divinely aided one (al-Muwaffaq) said, when he was stretched out among them, like the stretching of tanned hide upon the level plain (al-sa'id al-qarqar): I shall die, and I shall not bear the boast of it, by the burning (sala) of its calamities, the station of the fabricator.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الْحَسَنِ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ قَالَ:

Matn

أَنْشَدَ أَبُو شُعَيْبٍ: [الْبَحْر الْكَامِل] ‌ ‌ضَرَبُوا ابْنَ حَنْبَلَ بِالسِّيَاطِ بِظُلْمِهِمْ … لَعْنًا، فَثُبِّتَ بِالْبَيَانِ الْأَنْوَرِ قَالَ الْمُوَفَّقُ حِينَ مَدَّدَ بَيْنَهُمْ … مَدَّ الْأَدِيمِ عَلَى الصَّعِيدِ الْقَرْقَرِ إِنِّي أَمُوتُ، وَلَا أَبُوءُ بِفَخْرِهِ … بِصَلَا بَوَائِقِهَا مَحَلَّ الْمُفْتَرِي

Transmission overview

No path data

No separate transmission-path annotation is supplied for this report.

Translator note

The verses are obscure and possibly corrupt; the third line in particular (بصلا بوائقها محل المفتري) is translated closely and its sense is uncertain.
