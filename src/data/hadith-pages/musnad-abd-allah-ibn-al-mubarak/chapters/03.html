Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Chapter 3
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Ma'mar, from Yahya b. Abi Kathir, from Zayd b. Sallam, from his grandfather, who said: I heard Abu Umama say:

Matn

A man asked the Prophet ﷺ: "What is sin?" He said: "Whatever troubles your heart, leave it." He asked: "And what is faith?" He said: "When your evil deed grieves you and your good deed pleases you, then you are a believer."

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from al-Layth b. Sa'd; Abu Hani' al-Khawlani narrated to me, from 'Amr b. Malik; Fadala b. 'Ubayd narrated to me, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said during the Farewell Pilgrimage: "Shall I not tell you who the believer is? He is the one whom the people trust with their wealth and their lives. And the Muslim is the one from whose tongue and hand the people are safe. And the mujahid is the one who strives against his own self in obedience to God. And the muhajir is the one who abandons wrongdoings and sins."

Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Chapter 3
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Shu'ba, from Qatada, from Anas b. Malik, who heard him relate from the Prophet ﷺ, who said:

Matn

"There are three things; whoever has them will find the sweetness of faith: that he loves a person and loves him only for God's sake; that God and His Messenger are dearer to him than anything else; and that being thrown into the Fire is more beloved to him than returning to disbelief after God has rescued him from it."

Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Chapter 3
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Ma'mar informed us; al-Zuhri informed us, from al-Sa'ib b. Yazid, from 'Abd Allah b. al-Sa'di, who said:

Matn

'Umar b. al-Khattab said (to me): "Have I not been told that you take charge of some of the people's affairs, and when you are given your wage you do not accept it?" I said: "Yes." He said: "What do you intend by that?" I said: "I am wealthy; I have horses and I have slaves, and I want my work to be a charity on behalf of the Muslims." 'Umar said: "Do not do that, for I used to do what you do. When the Messenger of God ﷺ gave me a gift, I would say: 'Give it to someone poorer than I.' Until once he gave me some money and I said: 'O Messenger of God, give it to someone who is more in need of it than I.' So he said: 'Take it. Either keep it as property or give it in charity. And whatever of this wealth comes to you when you are not craving it and not asking for it, take it; otherwise, do not let your soul pursue it.'"

Entry33/ 9
Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 18
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Hisham b. Sa'd narrated to us, from Qays b. Bishr al-Taghlibi, who said:

Matn

My father used to sit with Abu al-Darda' in Damascus. In Damascus there was a man from the Ansar, a Companion of the Prophet ﷺ, called Ibn al-Hanzaliyya. He was a solitary man who seldom sat with people; he was either in prayer, or, when he finished, he would be glorifying God, saying "God is Greatest" and "There is no god but God," until he came to his family. One day he passed by us while we were with Abu al-Darda', and he greeted us. Abu al-Darda' said to him: "Could you share a word with us that benefits us and does not harm you?" He said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said to us: "You are about to arrive among your brethren, so make your clothing presentable and make your saddles and mounts presentable, so that you are like a beauty mark among the people. God does not love obscenity or the affectation of obscenity."

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Sulayman b. al-Mughira, who said: I heard Sa'id al-Jurayri relate from Abu Nadra, from Usayr b. Jabir, who said:

Matn

We used to sit in Kufa with one of our hadith narrators. When the people dispersed, some men would remain, among them a man whose speech was unlike anyone else's I heard. I came to love him, and he took a place in my heart. While I was in that state, I lost him. I said to my companions: "That man, so-and-so, who used to sit with us; does anyone of you know him?" A man said: "Yes, that is Uways al-Qarani." I said: "Will you guide me to his dwelling?" He said: "Yes." So I set out with him until I came upon him suddenly like a spear. He came out. I said: "My brother, what kept you from coming to us?" He said: "I had no suitable clothing to come to you in; I was wearing a cloak." I said to him: "Put on this cloak." He said: "Do not do that, for if I wear this cloak they will harm me." I kept at him until he wore it. Then he went out among them, and they said: "Who deceived him about this cloak of his?" So he came, set it down, and got dressed. I went to them and said: "What do you want from this man? You have harmed him: a man is clothed once and naked another time?" I took them to task sharply with my tongue. A man among his companions, the one who mocked him, rebelled. Then the people of Kufa sent a delegation to 'Umar, and that man was among the delegation. 'Umar said: "Is there anyone here from the Qaran tribe?" So that man came. 'Umar said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said to us: "A man from the people of Yemen will come to you, called Uways al-Qarani. He will leave no one behind in Yemen except his mother. He had a white mark, and he prayed to God and God removed it from him, except for a spot the size of a dinar or a dirham. Whoever of you meets him, tell him to ask forgiveness for you." The man then said: “He came to us here, and I asked him, ‘Who are you?’ He replied, ‘I am Uways.’ I asked, ‘Whom did you leave behind in Yemen?’ He replied, ‘My mother.’ I asked, ‘You had a white mark, and you prayed to God and He removed it, except for a spot the size of a dinar or a dirham?’ He said, ‘Yes.’ I said, ‘Ask forgiveness for me.’ He said, ‘O Commander of the Faithful, should someone like me ask forgiveness for someone like you?’ I said, ‘You are my brother; do not leave me.’ But he slipped away from me, and I was informed that he had come to you in Kufa.” The account continues: the man began to belittle him because of what 'Umar had said about him. I said to him: "You say what is not so about us, and we do not know this man?" 'Umar said: “Indeed, he is a man like so-and-so,” and began to diminish his standing, meaning to weaken his position. Then that man said: "That one with us, whom we mock, is called Uways." Umar replied: “He is the very one. Go after him, though I do not think you will catch up with him.” So the man set out and went in to him before even going to his own family. Uways said: "This was not your habit; what has occurred to you?" He said: "I adjure you by God! 'Umar met me and said such-and-such, so ask forgiveness for me." He said: "I will not ask forgiveness for you until you take it upon yourself not to mock me and not to mention to anyone what you heard from 'Umar." He said: "You have that." So he asked forgiveness for him. Usayr said: It was not long before his story spread in Kufa. I came to him and said: "My brother, I see that you are the wonder, and we did not realize." He said: "There was nothing in this that warranted conveying it to the people; every servant is rewarded only for his deed." So when the story spread, he fled and went away.

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "God has one hundred mercies. He sent down one of them among the jinn, humankind, the beasts and the crawling creatures; by it they show kindness to one another and have mercy on one another, and by it the wild animals are tender toward their young. Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Chapter 3
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from 'Ikrima, from Damdam b. Jaws, who said:

Matn

I entered the mosque of Medina, and an old man called out to me: “O Yamani, O Yamani, come here.” I did not know him. He said: “Never say to anyone, ‘By God, God will never forgive you,’ or, ‘God will never admit you to Paradise.’” I asked, “Who are you, may God have mercy on you?” He identified himself as Abu Hurayra. I (Damdam) said, “One of us says this to a member of his family when angry, or to his wife or servant.” Abu Hurayra said, “I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say that there were two men among the Children of Israel who loved one another. One was diligent in worship, while the other was, as it were, a sinner. The devout man kept saying, ‘Stop! Stop what you are doing!’ The other would say, ‘Leave me and my Lord,’ until one day the devout man found him committing a sin he considered grave. He said, ‘Stop!’ The other said, ‘Leave me and my Lord! Were you sent as a watcher over me?’ The devout man said, ‘By God, God will never forgive you,’ or, ‘You will never enter Paradise.’ God then sent an angel to them, who took both their souls, and they came together before Him. He said to the sinner, ‘Enter Paradise by My mercy,’ and to the other, ‘Can you bar My mercy from My servant?’ He replied, ‘No, my Lord.’ God said, ‘Take him away to the Fire.’” Abu Hurayra added, “By Him in whose hand is my soul, he spoke a word that ruined his worldly life and his Hereafter.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ ، عَنْ ضَمْضَمِ بْنِ جَوْسٍ ، قَالَ:

Matn

دَخَلْتُ مَسْجِدَ الْمَدِينَةِ ، فَنَادَانِي شَيْخٌ ، فَقَالَ: يَا يَمَانِيُّ ، يَا يَمَانِيُّ ، تَعَالَ. وَمَا أَعْرِفُهُ فَقَالَ: لَا تَقُولَنَّ لِرَجُلٍ: وَاللَّهِ لَا يَغْفِرُ اللَّهُ لَكَ أَبَدًا ، وَلَا يُدْخِلُكَ الْجَنَّةَ أَبَدًا. قُلْتُ: وَمَنْ أَنْتَ يَرْحَمُكَ اللَّهُ؟ فَقَالَ أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ: فَقُلْتُ: إِنَّ هَذِهِ الْكَلِمَةَ يَقُولُهَا أَحَدُنَا لِبَعْضِ أَهْلِهِ إِذَا غَضِبَ ، أَوْ لِزَوْجَتِهِ ، أَوْ لِخَادِمِهِ. قَالَ: فَإِنِّي سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ، يَقُولُ: «إِنَّ رَجُلَيْنِ كَانَا فِي بَنِي إسْرَائِيلَ مُتَحَابَّيْنِ ، أَحَدُهُمَا مُجتهِدٌ فِي الْعِبَادَةِ ، وَالْآخَرُ كَأَنَّهُ يَقُولُ: مُذْنِبٌ . فَجَعَلَ يَقُولُ: أَقْصِرْ ، أَقْصِرْ عَمَّا أَنْتَ عَلَيْهِ. قَالَ: فَيَقُولُ: خَلِّنِي وَرَبِّي حَتَّى وَجَدَهُ يَوْمًا عَلَى ذَنْبٍ اسْتَعْظَمَهُ ، قَالَ: أَقْصِرْ. قَالَ: خَلِّنِي وَرَبِّي ، أَبُعِثْتَ عَلَيَّ رَقِيبًا؟ قَالَ: وَاللَّهِ لَا يَغْفِرُ اللَّهُ لَكَ أَبَدًا ، أَوْ لَا تَدْخُلُ الْجَنَّةَ أَبَدًا. قَالَ: فَبَعَثَ اللَّهُ إِلَيْهِمَا مَلَكًا ، فَقَبَضَ رُوحَيْهِمَا فَاجْتَمَعَا عِنْدَهُ ، فَقَالَ للمُذْنِبِ: ادْخُلِ الْجَنَّةَ بِرَحْمَتِي ، وَقَالَ لِلَآخَرِ: أَتَسْتَطِيعُ أَنْ تَحْظُرَ عَلَى عَبْدِي رَحْمَتِي؟ قَالَ: لَا يَا رَبِّ. قَالَ: اذْهَبُوا بِهِ إِلَى النَّارِ» . قَالَ أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ: وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ ، لَتَكَلَّمَ بِكَلِمَةٍ أَوْبَقَتْ دُنْيَاهُ وَآخِرَتَهُ
