Entry226/ 10
Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 140
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Isma'il b. Abi Khalid informed us, from al-Sha'bi, who said:

Matn

Qaraza wanted to go to Iraq with some people from the Banu 'Abd al-Ashhal, and 'Umar b. al-Khattab went out with them. He called for water and made ablution, then said: "Do you know why I came out with you?" They said: "(Out of) affection for us, and (our) right." He said: "(It is) your right, but I came for a (single) statement: 'Narrate little from the Prophet ﷺ, and I am your partner in that.'" He (Qaraza) said: "And I did not narrate from the Prophet ﷺ after 'Umar's words."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، أَنا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ أَبِي خَالِدٍ ، عَنِ الشَّعْبِيِّ ، قَالَ:

Matn

أَرَادَ قَرَظَةُ أَنْ يَأْتِيَ الْعِرَاقَ فِي أُنَاسٍ مِنْ بَنِي عَبْدِ الْأَشْهَلِ ، فَخَرَجَ مَعَهُمْ عُمَرُ بْنُ الْخَطَّابِ ، فَدَعَا بِمَاءٍ فَتَوَضَّأَ ، فَقَالَ: هَلْ تَدْرُونَ لِمَ خَرَجْتُ مَعَكُمْ؟ قَالُوا: وُدًّا لَنَا وَحَقًّا. قَالَ: لَكُمْ حَقًّا وَلَكِنِّي جِئْتُ فِي كَلِمَةٍ: «أَقِلُّوا الْحَدِيثَ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَنَا شَرِيكُكُمْ فِيهِ» ، قَالَ: فَمَا كُنْتُ أُحَدِّثُ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ بَعْدَ قَوْلِ عُمَرَ

Entry228/ 10
Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 141
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from al-Mas'udi, from Muslim al-Batin, from 'Amr b. Maymun, who said:

Matn

I used to go to 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud for a year, and I never heard him say in it: "The Messenger of God ﷺ said," except that he once narrated a hadith, and in the course of his words he said, "The Messenger of God ﷺ said," whereupon a severe distress came over him until I saw the sweat running down from his forehead. Then he said: "God willing, either more than that, or about that, or something close to that."

Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، عَنِ الْمَسْعُودِيِّ ، عَنْ مُسْلِمٍ الْبَطِينِ ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ مَيْمُونٍ ، قَالَ:

Matn

Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Chapter 12
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Ma'mar, from Ibn Tawus, from his father, from Ibn 'Abbas, who said:

Matn

"We used to memorize hadith, and the hadith was memorized from the Messenger of God ﷺ (carefully), until you rode in it both the difficult (mount) and the docile one (i.e., you took to narrating by any means)."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ ، عَنِ ابْنِ طَاوُسٍ ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ ، قَالَ:

Matn

«كُنَّا نَحْفَظُ الْحَدِيثَ ، وَالْحَدِيثُ يُحْفَظُ عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم حَتَّى رَكِبْتُمْ فِيهِ الصَّعْبَةَ وَالذَّلُولَةَ»

Entry230/ 10
Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 142
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Hammad b. Zayd, from Yahya b. Sa'id, al-Sa'ib b. Yazid narrated to us, saying:

Matn

I went out with Sa'd b. Malik from Medina to Mecca, "and I did not hear him narrate a single hadith from the Prophet ﷺ until we returned." And Hammad said: "(They used to treat) narrating hadith from the Prophet ﷺ with great reverence, whereas we, there being between us and the Prophet ﷺ such-and-such (a gap), are lax (in it)."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، عَنْ حَمَّادِ بْنِ زَيْدٍ ، عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ سَعِيدٍ ، نَا السَّائِبُ بْنُ يَزِيدَ ، قَالَ:

Matn

خَرَجْتُ مَعَ سَعْدِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ مِنَ الْمَدِينَةِ إِلَى مَكَّةَ ، «فَمَا سَمِعْتُهُ يُحَدِّثُ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِحَدِيثٍ حَتَّى رَجَعْنَا» ، وَقَالَ حَمَّادٌ: يُعَظَّمُ الْحَدِيثُ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَنَحْنُ بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم كَذَا وَكَذَا وَنَحنُ نُضَيِّعُ

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Sulayman b. al-Mughira, from Thabit, from Anas; Mahmud b. al-Rabi' narrated to us, from 'Itban b. Malik, his hadith concerning Ibn al-Dukhayshim. Anas said:

Matn

"I came to Medina and met 'Itban, and he narrated it to me." Anas said: "This hadith pleased me, so I said to my son: 'Write it down,' and he wrote it down."

العربية
Isnad

No isnad: the text is given as a direct statement.

Matn

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ الْمُغِيرَةِ ، عَنْ ثَابِتٍ ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ، نَا مَحْمُودُ بْنُ الرَّبِيعِ ، عَنْ عِتْبَانَ بْنِ مَالِكٍ، حَدِيثُهُ فِي ابْنِ الدُّخَيْشَمِ ، قَالَ أَنَسٌ: «فَقَدِمْتُ الْمَدِينَةَ ، فَلَقِيتُ عِتْبَانَ فحَدَّثَنِي ، قَالَ أَنَسٌ: فَأَعْجَبَنِي هَذَا الْحَدِيثُ. فَقُلْتُ لِابْنِي: اكْتُبْهُ فَكَتَبَهُ»

Husayn, that a man came to him and asked him about something, and he narrated (a hadith) to him. The man said: "Narrate to us from the Book of God and do not narrate from anything else." He said:

Matn

"You are a foolish man. Do you find in the Book of God that the noon prayer is four (units) in which (the recitation) is not aloud?" and he enumerated the prayers and the zakat and the like. Then he said: "Do you find this explained in detail in the Book of God? God has made these (matters) firm, and the Sunnah explains them."
