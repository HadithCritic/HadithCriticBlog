Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Chapter 7
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Hibban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Hammam, from Qatada, from Anas b. Malik, who informed him that a Jew crushed the head of a girl between two stones. She was asked:

Matn

"Who did this to you?" It was said: "So-and-so, or so-and-so," until the name of the Jew was mentioned, and she nodded her head, meaning: yes. So the Jew was summoned and confessed, "and the Prophet ﷺ commanded that his head be crushed between two stones," or he said: stones.

Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 83
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Hibban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Shu'ba, from Mansur, from Ibrahim, from 'Ubayd b. Nadla, from al-Mughira b. Shu'ba:

Matn

Two women were wives of a man from the Banu Hudhayl, and one of them struck the other with a tent pole, and she miscarried. They brought their dispute to the Prophet ﷺ, and they said: "How are we to pay blood-money for one who neither cried out, nor made a sound at birth, nor drank, nor ate?" The Prophet ﷺ said: "Is it rhymed speech like the rhymed speech of the bedouins?" And he ruled in it a ghurra (a slave, male or female, as compensation), and placed it upon the 'aqila (the paternal kin-group) of the woman.

العربية
Matn

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حِبَّانُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ عَنْ شُعْبَةَ ، عَنْ مَنْصُورٍ ، عَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ ، عَنْ عُبَيْدِ بْنِ نَضْلَةَ ، عَنِ الْمُغِيرَةِ بْنِ شُعْبَةَ أَنَّ امْرَأَتَيْنِ كَانَتَا تَحْتَ رَجُلٍ مِنْ بَنِي هُذَيْلٍ ، فَرَمَتْ إِحْدَاهُمَا الْأُخْرَى بِعَمُودِ فُسْطَاطٍ فَأُسْقِطَتْ ، فَاخْتَصَمُوا إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فَقَالُوا: كَيْفَ نَدِي مَنْ لَا صَاحَ وَلَا اسْتَهَلَّ وَلَا شَرِبَ وَلَا أَكَلَ؟ فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «أَسَجْعٌ كَسَجْعِ الْأَعْرَابِ ، فَقَضَى فِيهِ بِغُرَّةٍ ، وَجَعَلَهُ عَلَى عَاقِلَةِ الْمَرْأَةِ»

Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Hibban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Mujalid, from al-Sha'bi, from Jabir b. 'Abd Allah, who said:

Matn

The Jews brought a Jewish man and a Jewish woman to the Messenger of God ﷺ and said: "Carry out the prescribed punishment against them." He said: "Why did you not carry it out upon them yourselves?" They said: "If we had power we would have done so, but since our dominion has gone, we do not." He said: "Call for me the two most knowledgeable men among you." So they brought the two sons of Suraya. The Prophet ﷺ said to them: "Are you two the most knowledgeable of those behind you?" They said: "They claim that." He said: "I adjure you by God, who sent down the Torah upon Moses: what do you find in the Torah as the prescribed punishment?" They said: "We find in the Torah that when a man is alone with a woman in a house, if no prescribed punishment applies (ma hudda) they are released, though there is a punishment in it; and if he is found to have lain beside her, he is released, though there is a punishment in it; and if he is found upon her belly, he is released, though there is a punishment in it; but if he has entered her fully, as the stick enters the kohl-vial, then in it is stoning." So the Messenger of God ﷺ ordered that the two of them be stoned, and they were stoned. He (Jabir) said: And before that Ma'iz b. Malik al-Aslami was stoned; he testified against himself four times, and the Messenger of God ﷺ ordered that he be stoned, and he was stoned. Al-Sha'bi said: Jabir showed me the place where he was stoned.

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Hibban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Hammad b. Salama, from 'Aqil b. Talha al-Sulami, from Muslim b. Haydam, from al-Ash'ath b. Qays, who said:

Matn

We came to the Messenger of God ﷺ with a group from Kinda, who thought that I was the best of them. I said: "O Messenger of God, we claim that you are from us." He said: "We are the sons of al-Nadr b. Kinana; we do not slander our mother nor disown our father." Al-Ash'ath said: "By God, I shall not hear of any man who denies that Quraysh are descended from al-Nadr but I will flog him the prescribed punishment."
