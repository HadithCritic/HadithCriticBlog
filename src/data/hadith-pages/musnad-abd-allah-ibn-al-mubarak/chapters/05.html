English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Yahya b. 'Ubayd God, who said:

Matn

I heard my father say: I heard Abu Hurayra say: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "The prayers are expiations for wrongdoings; the prayers are expiations for wrongdoings. Recite, if you wish: *'Indeed, good deeds do away with evil deeds. That is a reminder for those who remember'* [Hud 11:114]."

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Isma'il al-Makki informed us, relating from al-Hasan, from Sa'sa'a b. Mu'awiya, who said:

Matn

I met Abu Hurayra, and he said: "Where are you from?" I said: "From the people of Iraq." He said: "Shall I not relate to you a hadith that will benefit those after you?" I said: "Please do." He said: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: "The first thing for which people will be held to account on the Day of Resurrection is the prayer. God will say to the angels: 'Look at the prayer of My servant. If it is complete, it will be recorded complete; and if it is deficient,' God, the Mighty and Majestic, will say, in His forbearance and the abundance of His affection for His servant: 'Look whether he has any voluntary prayers; if he has voluntary prayers, (the deficiency) will be completed with them.'" The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Then deeds will be taken up on that basis."

Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 24
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from al-Awza'i; al-Awza'i said: al-Muttalib b. Hantab al-Makhzumi narrated to me; 'Abd al-Rahman b. Abi 'Amra al-Ansari narrated to me, saying:

Matn

My father narrated to me, saying: We were with the Messenger of God ﷺ on a military expedition, and the people were afflicted with hunger. The people asked permission of the Messenger of God ﷺ to slaughter some of their mounts, saying: "Will it get us through?" When 'Umar b. al-Khattab saw that the Messenger of God ﷺ would not permit them to slaughter some of their mounts, he said: "O Messenger of God, how will we fare if we meet the opposing force tomorrow hungry and on foot? If you see fit, O Messenger of God, call the people to bring the remainder of their provisions and gather them together, then pray to God for blessing in them, for God will bring us through by your prayer," or he said, "God will bless us through your prayer." So the Messenger of God ﷺ called for the remainder of their provisions, and the people began to come with a handful of food, or more than that; the greatest amount anyone brought was a sa' of dates. The Messenger of God ﷺ gathered it, then stood and prayed as God willed him to pray, then called the army with their containers and told them to take (from it) by handfuls. There was no container in the army that they did not fill, and the like of it remained. The Messenger of God ﷺ laughed until his molars showed, and said: "I bear witness that there is no god but God, and I bear witness that I am the Messenger of God. No servant who believes in these two (testimonies) meets God the Fire will be kept from him on the Day of Resurrection."

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, who narrated to him that Mahmud b. al-Rabi' said that he remembered the Messenger of God ﷺ spitting a mouthful of water from a bucket that was in their house. He said: I heard 'Itban b. Malik al-Ansari say:

Matn

I used to lead my people, the Banu Salim, in prayer. I came to the Messenger of God ﷺ and said: "My eyesight has weakened, and the floods come between me and the mosque of my people. I would love for you to come and pray in my house at a place that I could take as a place of prayer." The Prophet ﷺ said: "I will do so, God willing." The Messenger of God ﷺ came to me in the morning, with Abu Bakr, after the day had grown hot. The Prophet ﷺ asked permission (to enter) and I permitted him. He did not sit until he said: "Where in your house would you like me to pray?" I pointed to the place I wanted. The Messenger of God ﷺ stood, and we lined up behind him. Then he gave the salutation and we gave the salutation when he did. We detained him for a khazira (a dish of meat and flour) that had been prepared for him. The people of the neighborhood heard of it, and they were calling (one another) from their houses (in the area), and they came until the house was full. A man said: "Where is Malik b. al-Dukhshun?" A man among us said: "That is a hypocrite; he does not love God and His Messenger." The Prophet ﷺ said: "Do not say that. He says 'There is no god but God,' seeking by it the Face of God, the Mighty and Majestic?" He said: "As for us, we see his face and his conversation inclining toward the hypocrites." The Prophet ﷺ said again: "Do not say that. He says 'There is no god but God,' seeking by it the Face of God?" They said: "Yes, O Messenger of God." The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "No servant will come on the Day of Resurrection saying 'There is no god but God,' seeking by it the Face of God, the Mighty and Majestic, except that God will forbid the Fire for him." Mahmud said: I related this to some people, among them Abu Ayyub, a Companion of the Messenger of God ﷺ, on his campaign on which he died, with Yazid b. Mu'awiya. He rejected that from me and said: "I do not think the Messenger of God ﷺ ever said that." That weighed heavily on me, and I made a vow to God that if He kept me safe until I returned from my campaign, I would ask 'Itban b. Malik about it, if I found him alive. We entered the state of pilgrimage (ihram) from Iliya' (Jerusalem) for hajj or 'umra until I reached Medina. I came to the Banu Salim, and there was 'Itban, an old man whose sight had gone, and he was the imam of his people. When he finished his prayer with the salutation, I came and greeted him and told him who I was. He narrated it to me exactly as he had narrated it to me the first time.

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنْبَأَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ الْمُغِيرَةِ ، عَنْ ثَابِتٍ ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ، قَالَ:

Matn

لَمَّا انْتَهَتْ عِدَّةُ زَيْنَبَ ، قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم لِزَيْدٍ: «اذْكُرْهَا عَلَيَّ» . قَالَ: فَانْطَلَقْتُ ، فَقُلْتُ: يَا زَيْنَبُ ، أَبْشِرِي ، أَرْسَلَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يُذكِّرُكَ ، فَقَالَتْ: «مَا أَنَا بِصَانِعَةٍ شَيْئًا حَتَّى أُوَامِرَ رَبِّي ، فَقَامَتْ إِلَى مَسْجِدِهَا وَنَزَلَ الْقُرْآنُ ، وَجَاءَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ حَتَّى دَخَلَ عَلَيْهَا بِغَيْرِ إِذْنٍ»

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Sulayman b. al-Mughira, from Thabit, from Anas, who said:
حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنْبَأَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ الْمُغِيرَةِ ، عَنْ ثَابِتٍ ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ ، قَالَ:
