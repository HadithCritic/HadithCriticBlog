English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Sulayman b. Mihran al-A'mash, from 'Abd Allah b. Murra, from Masruq, from 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ stood among us, as I stand among you, and said: "By Him besides whom there is no god, the blood of a man who bears witness that there is no god but God and that I am the Messenger of God is not lawful, except in one of three cases: a life for a life; the previously married person who commits adultery; and the one who abandons his religion and separates from the community," or he said: "the one who abandons Islam."

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Isma'il b. Abi Khalid, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'A'idh, that 'Uqba b. 'Amir al-Juhani came to the Farthest Mosque (al-Masjid al-Aqsa) and prayed in it. Some people caught up with him, walking with him, and he said:

Matn

"What brings you?" They said: "Because of your companionship with the Messenger of God ﷺ, we have come to greet you and to hear from you." He said: "Dismount and pray." Then he said: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: "Whoever dies not having associated anything with God, and not having been smeared with anything of forbidden bloodshed, will enter by whichever of the gates of Paradise he wishes."

Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Sa'id al-Jurayri informed us that Abu Nadra narrated to them, saying:

Matn

One who witnessed the sermon of the Prophet ﷺ at Mina narrated to me, saying: The Messenger of God ﷺ stood in the middle of the days of Tashriq and said: "O people, your Lord is one, and your father is one. Behold, there is no superiority of an Arab over a non-Arab, nor of a non-Arab over an Arab, nor of a black person over a red, nor of a red over a black, except by God-consciousness (taqwa). Have I conveyed (the message)?" They said: "The Messenger of God ﷺ has conveyed it." He said: "Then let the one present convey it to the absent." Then he said: "Which month is this?" They said: "A sacred month." He said: "Which day is this?" They said: "A sacred day." He said: "Which land is this?" They said: "A sacred land." He said: "Indeed your blood and your wealth," (the narrator) said: and I think he said: "and your honor," "are sacred to you, like the sacredness of this day of yours, in this month of yours, in this land of yours. Have I conveyed it?" They said: "The Messenger of God ﷺ has conveyed it." He said: "Then let the one present convey it to the absent."

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Muhammad b. Suqa informed us, from 'Abd Allah b. Dinar, from Ibn 'Umar, that 'Umar b. al-Khattab delivered a sermon at al-Jabiya and said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ stood among us as I stand among you and said: "Treat my Companions well; then those who follow them, then those who follow them. Then falsehood will spread, until a man will hasten to give testimony before he is asked for it. So whoever of you wants the center of Paradise, let him keep to the community, for Satan is with the lone individual, and he is farther from two. And let none of you be alone with a woman, for Satan is the third of them. And whoever is pleased by his good deed and grieved by his evil deed is a believer."

العربية
Isnad

Was he not your brother of old? The people have held back from him because of your holding back." He said: "Ibn al-Zubayr put his hand in his qiff (as printed; the word is unclear), and do you know what a qiff is?" And by God, I will be like the heavy camel (al-hamal al-radah)." He said: "Do you know what the heavy camel is?" He said: "No." He said: "It is the camel that goes off alone and kneels and does not leave its kneeling place until it is slaughtered there. I am like that camel: I will keep to my house until the one who will slaughter me there comes to me, or until the people unite upon one man. If they unite upon one near at hand for the good of their community, (well and good); if they fall into discord, I will not join them in their division, and I will not side with either of two men, after what I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: 'There is no man whom God has made a shepherd over a flock but God will ask him about it on the Day of Resurrection, whether he upheld God's command among them or neglected it, until a man will be asked about his own family, whether he upheld the command of God among them or neglected it.'"

Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Mubarak b. Fadala informed us, from al-Hasan, from Usayd b. al-Mutashammis b. Mu'awiya, who said:

Matn

We campaigned with Abu Musa at Isbahan, and it was not long before he conquered it; then it was not long before he returned, and we returned with him. We stopped at a halting place, and 'Uqayl was a neighbor there. He said: "Who is the man who will unload his lina (as printed)?" So I stood up and unloaded it. He said: "Shall I not narrate to you a hadith that Muhammad ﷺ used to narrate to us?" We said: "Yes, may God have mercy on you." He said: The Messenger of God ﷺ narrated to us: "Before the Hour there will be al-harj." We said: "What is al-harj?" He said: "Killing." We said: "More than we kill each year of the disbelievers? We kill in (a single battle) so-and-so many." He said: "By God, it is not your killing of disbelievers; rather, it will be killing amongst you, in a time of peace, O company of the people of Islam, until a man kills his neighbor, and his cousin, and kills his brother, and kills his father." He said: We were stunned into silence, so that no one moved. We looked at one another and said: "How could a man among us kill his neighbor, his cousin or his father, given the affection that God has put in our hearts today?" And we knew that our companion would not exceed the truth with us (as printed; the wording is unclear). We said: "Do you think our minds will be with us on that day?" He said: "No, by God; minds will be taken away from the people of that time, and... (the text here is unclear in the source)... of the people; we think that most of them are upon something while they are not upon anything. By God, I fear that it will reach me and you; and by God, if it reaches me I know of no way out for myself and for you, according to what the Prophet ﷺ entrusted to us, except that we leave it as we entered it." Al-Hasan said: "That is, safe (unscathed)."

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Ma'mar informed us, from Salim, from Ishaq b. Rashid, from 'Amr b. Wabisa al-Asadi, from his father, who said:

Matn

I was in Kufa in my house when I heard at the door of the house: "Peace be upon you." I said: "And upon you be peace." He came in persistently, and when he entered, it was 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud. I said: "O Abu 'Abd al-Rahman, what time of day is this for a visit?" and that was in the depth of the noon heat. He said: "The day grew long for me, so I thought of someone to talk with." He said: He began to narrate to me from the Messenger of God ﷺ, and I narrated to him; then he began to relate to me, saying: I heard him say: "There will be a tribulation in which the sleeper is better than the one lying down, the one lying down is better than the one sitting, the one sitting is better than the one standing, the one standing is better than the one walking, the one walking is better than the one riding, and the one riding is better than the one running. Those slain in it are all in the Fire." I said: "O Messenger of God, and when will that be?" He said: "That will be the harj (killing)." I said: "And when are the days of harj?" He said: "When a man is not safe from his own companion." I said: "Then what do you command me to do if I live to see it?" He said: "Restrain yourself and your hands, and enter your house." I said: "O Messenger of God, what if someone enters upon me in my house?" He said: "Then enter your room." I said: "And what if someone enters upon me in my room?" He said: "Then enter your place of prayer, then do like this," then he gripped his right hand around his wrist, "and say: 'My Lord is God,' until you are killed in that (state)."

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Shu'ba informed us, from Zubayd, from Sa'd b. 'Ubayda, from Abu 'Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, from 'Ali (ﷺ), who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ sent out an army and appointed a man as commander over them. He kindled a fire and said: "Enter it." Some people wanted to enter it, while others said: "We fled from it (by accepting Islam)." That was mentioned to the Messenger of God ﷺ, and he said to those who had wanted to enter it: "Had you entered it, you would have remained in it until the Day of Resurrection, forever." And he spoke kindly to the others and said: "You did well; there is no obedience to anyone in disobedience to God; obedience is only in what is right."

Isnad

Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; 'Awf narrated to us, from al-Hasan, who said:

Matn

Ma'qil b. Yasar fell ill with the illness in which he died. When it became severe, Ibn Ziyad visited him in his house. When he sat with him, Ma'qil said: "I am going to narrate to you a hadith I heard from the Messenger of God ﷺ. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'Whoever is made shepherd over a flock and does not surround them with sincere counsel, God has forbidden him the scent of Paradise, though its scent can be found from a journey of a hundred years.'" Ibn Ziyad said: "Why did you not narrate this to me before today?" He said: "Today, were it not for the state I am in, I would not have narrated it to you."

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Jahm b. Aws informed us, saying:

Matn

I heard 'Abd Allah b. Abi Maryam, when 'Abd Allah b. Rustam passed by him on his mount and said to Ibn Abi Maryam: "I long for your assemblies and your conversation." When he had gone, Ibn Abi Maryam said: I heard Abu Hurayra say: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Do not envy a wicked person for a blessing, for you do not know what he will meet after his death; he has with God a killer who does not die." That reached Wahb b. Munabbih, so Wahb sent to him Abu Dawud al-A'war, and he said: "O so-and-so, what is the killer who does not die?" Ibn Abi Maryam said: "The Fire."

Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Chapter 13
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Sufyan b. 'Uyayna informed us, from 'Amr b. Dinar, from Abu Qabus, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "The merciful will be shown mercy by the Most Merciful. Show mercy to those on earth, and those in heaven will show mercy to you."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، أَنا سُفْيَانُ بْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ دِينَارٍ ، عَنْ أَبِي قَابُوسَ ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو ، قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «الرَّاحِمُونَ يَرْحَمُهُمُ الرَّحْمَنُ ، ارْحَمُوا مَنْ فِي الْأَرْضِ يَرْحَمْكُمْ أَهْلُ السَّمَاءِ»

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Sufyan al-Thawri informed us, from Ja'far b. Burqan, from 'Abd Allah b. Dinar, from 'A'isha, from the Prophet ﷺ:

Matn

"Whoever is gentle with my community, God will be gentle with him; and whoever burdens my community, God will burden him."

العربية
Isnad

No isnad: the text is given as a direct statement.

Matn

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، أَنا سُفْيَانُ الثَّوْرِيُّ ، عَنْ جَعْفَرِ بْنِ بُرْقَانَ ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ دِينَارٍ ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «مَنْ رَفَقَ بِأُمَّتِي رَفَقَ اللَّهُ بِهِ ، وَمَنْ شَقَّ عَلَى أُمَّتِي شَقَّ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ»

Entry272/ 39
Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Chapter 13
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; al-Awza'i informed us; al-Zuhri informed us; Abu Salama b. 'Abd al-Rahman informed us, from Abu Hurayra, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "There is no governor or commander but he has two groups of confidants: a group that commands him to do right and forbids him wrong, and a group that spares no effort to corrupt him. Whoever is protected from the evil of the latter is (truly) protected; and he (the ruler) goes with whichever of the two prevails."

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، أَنا الْأَوْزَاعِيُّ ، أَنا الزُّهْرِيُّ ، أَنا أَبُو سَلَمَةَ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ، قَالَ:

Matn

قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم: «مَا مِنْ وَالٍ وَلَا أَمِيرٍ إِلَّا لَهُ بِطَانَتَانِ ، بِطَانَةٌ تَأْمُرُهُ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ وَتَنْهَى عَنِ الْمُنْكَرِ ، وَبِطَانَةٌ لَا تَأْلُوهُ خَبَالًا ، فَمَنْ وُقِيَ شَرَّهَا فَقَدْ وُقِيَ ، وَهُوَ لِلَّذِي يَغْلِبُ مِنْهُمَا»
