English
Isnad

My grandfather informed us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Ma'mar b. Rashid, from al-Zuhri, from Anas b. Malik (ؓ), who said:

Matn

We were sitting with the Messenger of God ﷺ, and he said: "A man from the people of Paradise will now appear before you." Then a man from the Ansar appeared, his beard dripping with the water of his ablution, holding his sandals hanging in his left hand. The next day the Prophet ﷺ said the same thing, and that same man appeared just as the first time. On the third day the Prophet ﷺ again said the same words, and that man appeared in the same state as before. When the Prophet ﷺ rose to leave, 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr followed him and said: "I quarreled with my father and swore that I would not go in to him for three days. If you see fit to take me in with you until that time has passed, (please) do so." He replied: "Yes." Anas said: 'Abd Allah used to relate that he spent those three nights with him and did not see him get up at all during the night; except that whenever he woke and turned over on his bed, he would mention God, the Mighty and Majestic, and say "God is Greatest," until the dawn prayer. 'Abd Allah said: "But I never heard him say anything except good. When the three nights had passed and I had almost come to look down on his deeds, I said: 'O servant of God, there was no anger or estrangement between me and my father; rather I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say about you three times: "A man from the people of Paradise will now appear before you," and you appeared all three times. So I wanted to stay with you and see what you do so that I might follow your example, but I did not see you do any great deed. What, then, brought you to what the Messenger of God ﷺ said?' He said: 'It is nothing but what you saw.' When I turned to leave, he called me back and said: 'It is nothing but what you saw, except that I find in myself no ill will toward any of the Muslims, and I do not envy anyone for any good that God has given him.' 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr said to him: 'This is what has brought you to that, and it is something we cannot bear.'"

Isnad

أَخْبَرَنَا جَدِّي، نَا حَبَّانُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرِ بْنِ رَاشِدٍ ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ رضي الله عنه ، قَالَ:

Matn

كُنَّا جُلُوسًا مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فَقَالَ: «يَطْلُعُ الْآنَ عَلَيْكُمْ رَجُلٌ مِنْ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ» ، فَطَلَعَ رَجُلٌ مِنَ الْأَنْصَارِ تَنْطِفُ لِحْيَتُهُ مِنْ مَاءِ وُضُوئِهِ ، قَدْ عَلَّقَ نَعَلَيْهِ بِيَدِهِ الشِّمَالِ ، فَلَمَّا كَانَ الْغَدُ ، قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ ، فَطَلَعَ ذَلِكَ الرَّجُلُ مِثْلَ الْمَرَّةِ الْأُولَى ، فَلَمَّا كَانَ الْيَوْمُ الثَّالِثُ ، قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِثْلَ مَقَالَتِهِ أَيْضًا ، فَطَلَعَ ذَلِكَ الرَّجُلُ عَلَى مِثْلِ حَالِهِ الْأَوَّلِ ، فَلَمَّا قَامَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم تَبِعَهُ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عَمْرٍو ? فَقَالَ: إِنِّي لَاحَيْتُ أَبِي ، فَأَقْسَمْتُ أَنِّي لَا أَدْخُلُ عَلَيْهِ ثَلَاثًا ، فَإِنْ رَأَيْتَ أَنْ تُؤْوِيَنِي إِلَيْكَ حَتَّى تَمْضِيَ فَعَلْتَ. قَالَ: «نَعَمْ» . قَالَ أَنَسٌ: فَكَانَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ يُحَدِّثُ أَنْ بَاتَ مَعَهُ تِلْكَ الثَّلَاثَ اللَّيَالِيَ فَلَمْ يَرَهُ يَقُومُ مِنَ اللَّيْلِ شَيْئًا ، غَيْرَ أَنَّهُ إِذَا تَعَارَّ تَقَلَّبَ عَلَى فِرَاشِهِ ذَكَرَ اللَّهَ عز وجل ، وَكَبَّرَ حَتَّى صَلَاةِ الْفَجْرِ ، قَالَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ: غَيْرَ أَنِّي لَمْ أَسْمَعْهُ يَقُولُ إِلَّا خَيْرًا ، فَلَمَّا مَضَتِ الثَّلَاثُ اللَّيَالِي وَكِدْتُ أَنْ أَحْتَقِرَ عَمَلَهُ ، قُلْتُ: يَا عَبْدَ اللَّهِ ، لَمْ يَكُنْ بَيْنِي وَبَيْنَ أَبِي غَضَبٌ وَلَا هِجْرَةٌ ، وَلَكِنِّي سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ لَكَ ثَلَاثَ مَرَّاتٍ: «يَطْلُعُ عَلَيْكُمُ الْآنَ رَجُلٌ مِنْ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ» فَطَلَعْتَ أَنْتَ الثَّلَاثَ الْمَرَّاتِ ، فَأَرَدْتُ أَنْ آوِيَ إِلَيْكَ فَأَنْظُرَ مَا عَمِلْتَ فَأَقْتَدِيَ بَكَ ، فَلَمْ أَرَ عَمِلْتَ كَبِيرَ عَمَلٍ ، فَمَا الَّذِي بَلَغَ بِكَ مَا قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم؟ قَالَ: مَا هُوَ إِلَّا مَا رَأَيْتَ. فَلَمَّا وَلَّيْتُ دَعَانِي ، فَقَالَ: مَا هُوَ إِلَّا مَا رَأَيْتَ ، غَيْرَ أَنِّي لَا أَجِدُ فِي نَفْسِي لِأَحَدٍ مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ غِشًّا ، وَلَا أَحْسُدُهُ عَلَى شَيْءٍ أَعْطَاهُ اللَّهُ إِيَّاهُ. فَقَالَ لَهُ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عَمْرٍو هَذَا الَّذِي بَلَغَتْ بِكَ ، وَهِيَ الَّتِي لَا نُطِيقُ

Isnad

My grandfather informed us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from al-Muthanna b. al-Sabbah, from 'Amr b. Shu'ayb, from his father, from his grandfather:

Matn

that they mentioned a man in the presence of the Messenger of God ﷺ, and said: "He does not eat until he is fed, and does not set out until a mount is prepared for him." The Prophet ﷺ said: "You have backbitten him." They said: "We only told what is in him." He said: "It is enough (as sin) for you that you mention your brother with what is in him."

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah b. al-Mubarak informed us, (from) Hammad b. Salama, from Thabit, from Abu Rafi', from Abu Hurayra:

Matn

A man visited his brother in another town, so God stationed an angel on his path. When he came upon him, (the angel) said: "Where are you headed?" He said: "I want to visit a brother of mine in this town." (The angel) said: "Do you have some favor owed to you from him that you are tending?" He said: "No, except that I love him for God's sake." (The angel) said: "I am the messenger of God to you: God has loved you as you loved him."

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Ibn Lahi'a; al-Harith b. Yazid narrated to us, from Jundab b. 'Abd Allah, that he heard Sufyan b. 'Awf al-Qari say: I heard 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr say:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said one day, while we were with him: "Glad tidings to the strangers! Glad tidings to the strangers!" It was asked: "Who are the strangers, O Messenger of God?" He said: "Righteous people, few among many evil people; those who disobey them outnumber those who obey them." And we were with the Messenger of God ﷺ another day when the sun had risen, and he said: "There will come, on the Day of Resurrection, people from my community whose light is like the light of the sun." We said: "Who are they, O Messenger of God?" He said: "The poor among the Emigrants (Muhajirun), through whom hardships are warded off. One of them dies with his need still in his chest. They will be gathered from the corners of the earth."

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Shu'ba, from Yazid al-Rishk, from Mu'adha al-'Adawiyya, who said: I heard Hisham b. 'Amir say: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say:

Matn

"It is not permissible for a Muslim to forsake (shun) another Muslim for more than three nights. For they both deviate from the truth as long as they remain in their estrangement. Whichever of them is first to return (to reconciliation), his taking the initiative in returning will be an expiation for him. If he greets (the other) and he does not accept, and (the other) does not return his greeting, the angels return it to him, and Satan answers the other. And if both die in their estrangement, they will not enter Paradise together." I think he said: "ever."
