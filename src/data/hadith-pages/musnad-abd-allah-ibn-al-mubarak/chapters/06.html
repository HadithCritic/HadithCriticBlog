English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Sufyan, from Ja'far b. Muhammad, from his father, from Jabir, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "I and the Hour have been sent like these two," and when he mentioned the Hour his cheeks would redden, his voice would rise and his anger would intensify, as if he were a warner of an army saying: "It has come upon you in the morning and in the evening."

Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Chapter 6
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Mu'tamir informed us, from 'Ali b. Zayd, from Abu Nadra, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ led us in the 'Asr prayer while it was still day, then addressed us until the sun set. He left nothing that would occur until the Day of Resurrection without telling us of it; whoever memorized it memorized it, and whoever forgot it forgot it. And when the sun was going down in the west, he said: "What has passed of your world, compared to what remains of it, is like what has passed of this day of yours compared to what remains of it."

Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Khalid Abu al-'Ala' narrated to us, from 'Atiyya, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "How can I be at ease when the one with the Horn has put the Horn to his mouth and is listening for the permission, (waiting) for when he will be commanded to blow, and will blow?" That seemed heavy on the Companions of Muhammad ﷺ, so at that point he said to them: "Say: 'God is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs; in God we have put our trust.'"

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Sa'id ibn Abi Ayyub informed us; Ibn Abi Sulayman narrated to me, from Sa'id al-Maqburi, from Abu Hurayra, who said:

Matn

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Abu Hayyan al-Taymi, from Abu Zur'a b. 'Amr b. Jarir, from Abu Hurayra, who said:

Meat was brought to the Messenger of God ﷺ, and the foreleg was raised to him, and he liked it. He bit off a piece of it, then said: "I am the master of the people on the Day of Resurrection. Do you know why? God will gather the first and the last people on one plain, so that the caller can make them all hear and the sight can penetrate them all, and the sun will draw near. The people will be afflicted with distress and anguish that they cannot bear or endure. Some people will say to others: 'Do you not see what has reached you? Will you not look for someone who will intercede for you with your Lord?' Some people will say to others: 'Go to Adam.' So they will go to Adam and say: 'You are the father of mankind. God created you with His hand, breathed into you of His spirit, and commanded the angels, who prostrated to you. Intercede for us with your Lord. Do you not see what we are in? Do you not see what has reached us?' Adam will say to them: 'My Lord is angry today with an anger such as He has never been angry with before and will never be angry with after. He forbade me the tree and I disobeyed Him. Myself, myself! Go to someone else. Go to Noah.' Do you not see what we are in? Do you not see what has reached us?' He will say: 'My Lord is angry today with an anger such as He has never been angry with before and will never be angry with after. I had one supplication that I made against my people. Myself, myself! Go to someone else. Go to Abraham.' So they will go to Abraham and say: 'O Abraham, you are the Prophet of God and His intimate friend among the people of the earth. Intercede for us with your Lord. Do you not see what we are in?' He will say to them: 'My Lord is angry today with an anger such as He has never been angry with before and will never be angry with after. I told three lies,'" and Abu Hayyan mentioned them in the hadith. "'Myself, myself! Go to someone else. Go to Moses.' So they will go to Moses and say: 'O Moses, you are the Messenger of God, whom He preferred over the people with His messages and by speaking to him. Intercede for us with your Lord. Do you not see what we are in?' He will say: 'My Lord is angry today with an anger such as He has never been angry with before and will never be angry with after. I killed a person whom I had not been commanded to kill. Myself, myself! Go to someone else. Go to Jesus.' So they will go and say: 'O Jesus, you are the Messenger of God, and His Word which He cast to Mary, and a spirit from Him, and you spoke to people in the cradle. Intercede for us with your Lord. My grandfather narrated to us; Hibban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Rishdin b. Sa'd; Abu Hani' al-Khawlani informed me; 'Amr b. Malik al-Janbi informed me that Fadala b. 'Ubayd and 'Ubada b. al-Samit narrated to him that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

"On the Day of Resurrection, when God has finished judging creation, two men will remain who are commanded to be taken to the Fire. One of them will turn around, and the Compeller (al-Jabbar) will say: 'Bring him back.' So he will be brought back, and it will be said to him: 'Why did you turn around?' He will say: 'I was hoping that You would admit me to Paradise.' He (the Prophet) said: Then he will be commanded to go to Paradise. He (God) will say: 'This is a gift from my Lord; even if I fed all the people of Paradise, it would not diminish what I have at all.'" Whenever the Messenger of God ﷺ mentioned it, joy could be seen on his face.

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Hibban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Rishdin; Ibn Nu'm informed me, from Abu 'Uthman, from Abu Hurayra, from the Messenger of God ﷺ, who said:

Matn

"Two men among those who entered the Fire cried out loudly. The Lord, the Mighty and Majestic, said: 'Bring them out.' When they were brought out, He said to them: 'Why did you cry out so loudly?' They said: 'We did that so that You would have mercy on us.' He said: 'My mercy to you is that you go and throw yourselves back where you were in the Fire.' So they went, and one of them threw himself in, and God made it cool and safe for him. The other stood and did not throw himself in. The Lord said to him: 'What prevented you from throwing yourself in as your companion did?' He said: 'My Lord, I hope that You will not return me to it after You have brought me out.' The Lord said to him: 'You have your hope.' So both of them entered Paradise by His mercy."
