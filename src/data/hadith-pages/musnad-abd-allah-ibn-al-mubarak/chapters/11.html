Entry198/ 28
Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 123
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Abu Hayyan al-Taymi informed us, from al-Sha'bi, from al-Nu'man b. Bashir, who said:

Matn

My mother asked my father for some gift for me from his wealth, and he put it off for a year, then it occurred to him and he gave it to me. She said: "I will not be satisfied until you have the Messenger of God ﷺ bear witness to what you have given my son." So he took me by the hand, and I was a boy at that time, and brought me to the Prophet ﷺ. He said: "O Prophet of God, the mother of this boy, the daughter of Rawaha, has been pressing me for a year about some gift for this son of mine. It has occurred to me, and I have given it to him; and she would like you to bear witness, O Messenger of God." He said: "O Bashir, do you have any child besides this one?" He said: "Yes." And I think he said: "No! Do not make me a witness to this."

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Fitr, from Muslim b. Subayh, who said: I heard al-Nu'man b. Bashir while he was delivering a sermon:

Matn

"My father took me to the Prophet ﷺ to have him witness a gift he had given me." He said: "Do you have other sons besides him?" He said: "Yes." He said: "Treat them all equally."

Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Usama b. Zayd, from 'Amr b. Shu'ayb, from his father, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr, that a man gave a gift and then took it back. The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

"This is like the dog that eats until, when it is full, it vomits what is in its belly, then goes back to it and eats it." 'Amr b. Shu'ayb said: I was present when 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz said that during his caliphate to a man.

No isnad: the text is given as a direct statement.

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from al-Husayn b. Dhakwan al-Mu'allim, from 'Amr b. Shu'ayb, from Tawus, from Ibn 'Umar and Ibn 'Abbas, who both attributed it to the Messenger of God ﷺ, who said:

Matn

"It is not permissible for a man to give a gift and then take it back, except a father with regard to what he gives his child. The example of one who gives a gift and then takes it back is like that of a dog that eats until, when it is full, it vomits, and then returns to it."

Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 126
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from al-Muthanna, from 'Ata', from Jabir b. 'Abd Allah, from the Prophet ﷺ, who said:

Matn

"Do not give anything as a ruqba (a conditional lifelong gift, "watch-for-death"): whoever gives something as a ruqba, it is his (the recipient's). And do not give 'umra (a lifelong grant): whoever gives something as an 'umra, it belongs to his heirs."

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from 'Urwa, from 'A'isha, who said:

Matn

'Utba b. Abi Waqqas said to his brother Sa'd (and 'Utba was a disbeliever and Sa'd a Muslim): "I charge you that the son of Zam'a's slave woman is my son (and Zam'a was of the Banu 'Amir b. Lu'ayy), so take custody of the son of Zam'a's slave woman when you meet him." 'A'isha said: When it was the day of the Conquest, Sa'd met the son of Zam'a's slave woman and said: "My nephew," and he embraced him. 'Abd b. Zam'a said: "Rather, he is my brother, born on my father's bed (i.e., in his household) of his slave woman." They brought their dispute to the Prophet ﷺ, and Sa'd said: "O Messenger of God, this is my brother's son; look at his resemblance to my brother 'Utba." 'Abd b. Zam'a said: "Rather, he is my brother, O Messenger of God, born on my father's bed of his slave woman." The Messenger of God ﷺ looked, and saw that no one resembled 'Utba more clearly than he did. Yet he said: "He is yours, O 'Abd b. Zam'a. The child belongs to the bed (the marriage or household); and veil yourself from him, O Sawda." And he never saw her until she died.
