Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 97
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Ibrahim b. 'Abd Allah al-Khallal narrated to us; 'Abd Allah b. al-Mubarak informed us, Ma'mar informed us, from al-Zuhri, from 'Ali b. al-Husayn, from 'Amr b. 'Uthman, from Usama b. Zayd, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "A Muslim does not inherit from a disbeliever, nor does a disbeliever inherit from a Muslim."

Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Chapter 8
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Ibrahim narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Sufyan, from Abu Ishaq, from al-Harith, from 'Ali, who said:

Matn

"The Messenger of God ﷺ ruled that debt is (paid) before the bequest, though you recite: *'after any bequest which is bequeathed or any debt'* [al-Nisa' 4:12]. And (he ruled) that full siblings (the sons of the same mother) inherit, to the exclusion of half-siblings (banu al-'allat)," meaning brothers by father and mother, to the exclusion of brothers by the father only.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ عَنْ سُفْيَانَ ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ ، عَنِ الْحَارِثِ ، عَنْ عَلِيٍّ ، قَالَ:

Matn

«قَضَى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالدَّيْنِ قَبْلَ الْوَصِيَّةِ ، وَأَنْتُمْ تَقْرَءُونَ: {مِنْ بَعْدِ وَصِيَّةٍ يُوصَى بِهَا أَوْ دَيْنٍ} [النِّسَاء: 12] ، وَإِنَّ أَعْيَانَ بَنِي الْأُمِّ يَتَوَارَثُونَ دُونَ بَنِي الْعَلَّاتِ ، يَعْنِي الْإِخْوَةَ لِلْأَبِ وَالْأُمِّ دُونَ الْإِخْوَةِ لِلْأَبِ»

My grandfather narrated to us; Ibrahim narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Sufyan informed us, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. al-Asbahani, from Mujahid b. Wardan, from 'Urwa b. al-Zubayr, from 'A'isha,

Matn

that a freedman of the Prophet ﷺ fell from a date-palm and died and left some property. That was mentioned to the Prophet ﷺ, and he said: "Does he have a child or a close relative?" They said: "No." He said: "Then look for someone from the people of his village and give it to him."

Entry167/ 8
Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 100
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to me; Ibrahim informed us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Ya'qub b. Muhammad b. Tahla' narrated to us, Khalid b. Abi Hayyan informed me, saying:

Matn

A woman of the Banu Dinar had freed me, and she married among the Banu Salima and bore children among them, and then she died. I entered upon Jabir b. 'Abd Allah, and some of the people said: "O Abu 'Abd Allah, this man was asked about your patronage (wala')." I said: "God forbid! I am the freedman of so-and-so, of the Banu Dinar." Jabir said: "Indeed, O son of my sister. I bear witness that I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: 'Whoever attaches himself to someone other than his own patrons has cast off the noose (ribqa) of faith from his neck,'" and he would gesture with his hand like this three times.

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنِي جَدِّي، أَنا إِبْرَاهِيمُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ نَا يَعْقُوبُ بْنُ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ طَحْلَاءَ ، أَخْبَرَنِي خَالِدُ بْنُ أَبِي حَيَّانَ ، قَالَ:

Matn

كَانَتِ امْرَأَةٌ مِنْ بَنِي دِينَارٍ أَعْتَقَتْنِي فَتَزَوَّجَتْ فِي بَنِي سَلِمَةَ فَوَلَدَتْ فِيهِمْ ، ثُمَّ مَاتَتْ ، فَدَخَلْتُ عَلَى جَابِرِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ، فَقَالَ بَعْضُ الْقَوْمِ: يَا أَبَا عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ، هَذَا سُئِلَ مِنْ وَلَائِكَ ، فَقُلْتُ: مَعَاذَ اللَّهِ ، أَنَا مَوْلَى فُلَانَةَ مِنْ بَنِي الدِّينَارِ ، فَقَالَ جَابِرٌ: أَجَلْ يَا ابْنَ أُخْتِي ، فَإِنِّي أَشْهَدُ لَسَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ، يَقُولُ: «مَنْ تَوَلَّى غَيْرَ مَوَالِيهِ خَلَعَ رِبْقَةَ الْإِيمَانِ مِنْ عُنُقِهِ» ، وَيَقُولُ بِيَدِهِ هَكَذَا ثَلَاثَ مَرَّاتٍ

Entry168/ 8
Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 101
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Ibrahim narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Muhammad b. Abi Hafsa and Ma'mar informed us, from al-Zuhri, from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, that a woman came to 'Umar b. al-Khattab seeking her inheritance from her husband. 'Umar said: "The blood-money belongs to the 'aqila (the paternal kin responsible for paying it), and I know nothing for you." And Ma'mar said: "I do not see the blood-money as belonging except to the paternal male relatives (al-'asaba), because they pay it." So I adjured the people, and he said:

Matn

"Does anyone have knowledge of this?" Al-Dahhak b. Sufyan al-Kilabi said: "The Messenger of God ﷺ wrote to me that I should give the wife of Ashyam al-Dibabi an inheritance from the blood-money of her husband." So 'Umar gave her the inheritance.

العربية
Matn

حَدَّثَنَا جَدِّي، نَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ، أَنا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ أَنا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ أَبِي حَفْصَةَ ، وَمَعْمَرٌ ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ الْمُسَيِّبِ ، أَنَّ امْرَأَةً جَاءَتْ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ تَطْلُبُ مِيرَاثَهَا مِنْ زَوْجِهَا ، فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: إِنَّمَا الدِّيَةُ لِلْعَاقِلَةِ وَلَا أَعْلَمُ لَكِ شَيْئًا. وَقَالَ مَعْمَرٌ: مَا أَرَى الدِّيَةَ إِلَّا لِلْعَصَبَةِ لِأَنَّهُمْ يَعْقِلُونَ ، فَنَشَدْتُ النَّاسَ ، فَقَالَ: هَلْ أَحَدٌ عِنْدَهُ مِنْ هَذَا عِلْمٌ؟ فَقَالَ الضَّحَّاكُ بْنُ سُفْيَانَ الْكِلَابِيُّ: «إِنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم كَتَبَ إِلَيَّ أَنْ أُوَرِّثَ امْرَأَةَ أَشْيَمَ الضَّبَابِيِّ مِنْ دِيَةِ زَوْجِهَا ، فَوَرَّثَهَا عُمَرُ»
