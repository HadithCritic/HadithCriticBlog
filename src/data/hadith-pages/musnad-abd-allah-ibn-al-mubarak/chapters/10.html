Musnad Ibn al-Mubārak · Vol. 1, p. 111
English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; Hibban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Yunus b. Abi Ishaq; Abu al-Waddak narrated to me; Abu Sa'id al-Khudri narrated to me, saying:

Matn

We took captive women on the day of Khaybar. We would practice 'azl (withdrawal) with them, while seeking what could be had in exchange for them (ransom) from their families. Some of us said to others: "Are you doing this while the Messenger of God ﷺ is among you? Go to him and ask him." So we went to him and mentioned that, and he said: "Not from all fluid does a child result; when God decrees a matter, it comes to be." He said: Then (the Prophet) passed by cooking pots that were boiling, and said to us: "What is this meat?" We said: "Donkey meat." He said: "Domestic or wild?" We said: "No, it is domestic." He said to us: "Then overturn them." So we overturned them, though we were hungry and craving them. He said: And that day we were tying up the waterskins.

Isnad

Matn

English
Isnad

My grandfather narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us; Khalid al-Hadhdha' narrated to us, from Abu Qilaba, from Abu Tha'laba al-Khushani, who said:

Matn

"The Messenger of God ﷺ forbade the meat of domestic donkeys, and every predatory beast with a fang." My grandfather narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, from Abu Idris al-Khawlani, from Abu Tha'laba al-Khushani: "The Messenger of God ﷺ forbade every fanged beast among the predators." My grandfather narrated to us; Habban narrated to us; 'Abd Allah informed us, from Yunus and Malik, similar to it.

English
Isnad

The Shaykh Abu al-Qasim al-Husayn b. al-Hasan b. Muhammad al-Asadi informed us, by my reading to him in his house; Abu al-Faraj Sahl b. Bishr b. Ahmad al-Isfarayini informed us, by reading to him; Abu al-Faraj 'Abd al-Wahhab b. al-Husayn b. 'Umar b. Burhan al-Ghazzal al-Baghdadi informed us at the frontier fortress of Tyre; Abu Ya'qub Ishaq b. Sa'd b. al-Husayn b. Sufyan al-Fasawi informed us in Safar of the year 372 (AH); my grandfather Abu al-'Abbas al-Husayn b. Sufyan informed us; Habban b. Musa narrated to us; 'Abd Allah b. al-Mubarak (narrated to us), from Sufyan b. 'Uyayna, from Suhayl b. Abi Salih, from 'Abd Allah b. Yazid al-Sa'di, who said:

Matn

I asked Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab about the hyena. He said: "Eating it is not appropriate; does anyone eat it?" I said: "Some of my people carry them off and eat them." He said: "Eating it is not appropriate." An old man who was with him said: "If you wish, I will relate to you what I heard from Abu al-Darda'. I heard him say: 'The Messenger of God ﷺ forbade all plundering, all snatching, all mujaththama (an animal tied up and shot at for sport), and every fanged beast among the predators.'" Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab said: "You have spoken the truth."

She presented the lizard to the Messenger of God ﷺ, and food was rarely presented to him until he was told about it and its name was mentioned to him. The Messenger of God ﷺ reached with his hand toward the lizard, and one of the women present said: "Tell the Messenger of God ﷺ what you have presented to him." They said: "It is the lizard, O Messenger of God." So the Messenger of God ﷺ lifted his hand from the lizard. Khalid b. al-Walid said: "Is the lizard forbidden, O Messenger of God?" He said: "No, but it was not (found) in the land of my people, and I find that I dislike it." Khalid said: "So I drew it toward me and ate it while the Messenger of God ﷺ looked at me, and he did not forbid me."
