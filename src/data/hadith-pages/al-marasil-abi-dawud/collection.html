About the edition.

Edition. Muassasat al-Risala, Beirut, 2nd ed. 1418/1998, ed. Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut (named on Alifta's card; not independently confirmed)

English translation. The English is a retranslation that has not yet been reviewed. For 145 reports, a note beneath the report records a part of the Arabic that the English leaves out or another point still open. The supplied English is not divided into isnad and matn, so it appears as one passage beside the divided Arabic.

Transmitters. Names link to the Rijāl Register using the identifiers supplied with the source. Names the source does not identify stay unlinked.
