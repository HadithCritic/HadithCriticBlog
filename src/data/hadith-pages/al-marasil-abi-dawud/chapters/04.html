Entry19/ 7
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 80
English
Isnad

‘Abbad b. Musa narrated to us: Hushaym narrated to us, from al-Mughira, from al-Sha‘bi, who said:

Matn

The Prophet (ﷺ) was concerned about how to gather the people for the prayer. [He said:] So ‘Abdullah b. Zayd went away troubled by the Prophet’s concern, and someone came to him in a dream and said to him: “Tell the Prophet (ﷺ) to order a man, when the time for prayer arrives, to give the call to prayer, and let him say: Allahu akbar, Allahu akbar” — he related the adhan, each phrase twice. “Then when he has finished, let him pause until the sleeper wakes and whoever wishes to perform ablution does so. Then, when the people have gathered for their prayer, let him repeat it and say the like of what he said, until he reaches: ‘Come to prayer, come to success (hayya ‘ala al-falah),’ then let him say: ‘The prayer has been established, the prayer has been established, Allahu akbar, Allahu akbar, there is no god but God.’”

Entry20/ 7
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 81
English
Isnad

Ahmad b. Ibrahim narrated to us: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj: 'Ata' informed me

Matn

that he heard 'Ubayd b. 'Umayr say: The Prophet ﷺ and his Companions took counsel about what they should make, when they wished to assemble for prayer, by which they would be gathered for it. They deliberated about the bell (naqus). Then, while 'Umar b. al-Khattab was about to buy two pieces of wood for a bell, 'Umar saw in a dream [the instruction]: “Do not make a bell; rather, call the adhan for the prayer.” So 'Umar went to the Prophet ﷺ to tell him what he had seen, but revelation had already come about it. 'Umar was suddenly startled to hear Bilal calling the adhan. The Prophet ﷺ said: “Revelation has already preceded you in that,” when ['Umar told him of it].

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا أحمد بن إبراهيم ، حدثنا حجاج ، عن ابن جريج ، أخبرني عطاء ، أنه سمع . عبيد بن عمير ، يقول :

Matn

ائتمر النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم هو وأصحابه كيف يجعلون شيئا إذا أرادوا جمع الصلاة اجتمعوا لها به ، فائتمروا بالناقوس ، فبينما عمر بن الخطاب يريد أن يبتاع خشبتين لناقوس إذ رأى عمر في المنام أن لا تجعلوا الناقوس ; بل أذنوا بالصلاة ، فذهب عمر إلى النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم ليخبره بالذي رأى ، وقد جاء الوحي بذلك ، فما راع عمر إلا بلال يؤذن ، فقال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم : قد سبقك بذلك الوحي حين أخبره [عمر بذلك]

Entry21/ 7
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 82
English
Isnad

Musa b. Isma'il narrated to us: Hammad narrated to us: 'Ata' al-Khurasani narrated to us:

Matn

that the Messenger of God ﷺ visited 'Abd Allah b. Zayd (in his illness) and supplicated for him for good, then left him and entered the mosque and said to his Companions: “Advise us on something by which the people of the mosque may be called...” — and he related the report. Then the Prophet ﷺ said: “O Bilal, go up [to the open space],” and he said to 'Abd Allah b. Zayd: “Stand beside him and teach him the adhan.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا موسى بن إسماعيل ، حدثنا حماد . حدثنا عطاء الخراساني ،

Matn

أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم عاد عبد الله بن زيد فدعا له بخير ثم خرج من عنده فدخل المسجد فقال لأصحابه : أشيروا علينا بشيء يؤذن به أصحاب المسجد ... وساق الحديث ، فقال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم : يا بلال اصعد [الرحبة] ، وقال لعبد الله بن زيد : قم إلى جنبه فعلمه الأذان

Entry22/ 7
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 82
English
Isnad

Harun b. Sa‘id al-Ayli narrated to us: Ibn Wahb narrated to us; (transfer of isnad) and Makhlad b. Khalid narrated to us: ‘Uthman b. ‘Umar narrated to us, from Yunus, from Ibn Shihab: Hafs b. ‘Umar b. Sa‘d, the muezzin, informed me, that Bilal came to the Prophet (ﷺ) for the dawn prayer, and was told:

Matn

“The Messenger of God (ﷺ) is asleep.” So Bilal said — Makhlad said in his narration: at the top of his voice — “Prayer is better than sleep! Prayer is better than sleep!” So it was established (kept in place) as part of the call to the Fajr prayer.

العربية
Isnad

Matn

أن بلالا ، أتى النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم في صلاة الصبح ، فقيل له : إن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم نائم . فقال بلال : الصلاة - قال مخلد في حديثه : بأعلى صوته - الصلاة خير من النوم ، فأقرت في أذان صلاة الفجر

English
Isnad

Ahmad b. Yunus narrated to us: al-Hasan b. Salih narrated to us, from Abu al-Mu'tamir

Matn

— an old man who lived in al-Hirah, whose name was Yazid b. Tahman — from Ibn Sirin: that Bilal put his two fingers in his ears during some of his adhans, or his iqamah, in a voice that was neither high nor low.

أن بلالا جعل أصبعيه في أذنيه في بعض أذانه ، أو في إقامته بصوت ليس بالرفيع ولا بالوضيع

English
Isnad

Ahmad b. Abi al-Hawari narrated to us: al-Walid narrated to us, from Abu 'Amr and others, from Ibn Harmalah, from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, that the Prophet ﷺ said:

Matn

“No one leaves the mosque after the call to prayer except a hypocrite — except someone whom a need has driven out and who intends to return.”

Matn

لا يخرج من المسجد أحد بعد النداء إلا منافق ، إلا أحد أخرجته حاجة وهو يريد الرجوع
