English
Isnad

Qutayba b. Sa‘id narrated to us: Hafs informed us, from Ibn Jurayj, from ‘Amr b. Dinar:

Matn

that ‘Umar b. al-Khattab saw in Medina the karaj [sic: a term of uncertain meaning] and said: “As for me — had I not seen the Messenger of God (ﷺ) allow you to remain, I would not have allowed you to remain.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا قتيبة بن سعيد ، أخبرنا حفص ، عن ابن جريج . عن عمرو بن دينار ،

Matn

أن عمر بن الخطاب ، رأى بالمدينة الكرج فقال : أما [أنا] لولا أني رأيت رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أقرك ما أقررتك

English
Isnad

Wahb b. Baqiyyah narrated to us: Khalid narrated to us, from Yunus, from al-Hasan, from the Prophet ﷺ:

Matn

that he passed by some people who were gathered around a man who was making them laugh, and said: “What is this?” They said: “A man who imitates a donkey to make his companions laugh.” He said: “Glory be to God! What assures this man — though God has made his form beautiful — that He will not transform him into the form of a donkey?”

العربية

Matn

أنه مر بقوم وهم يطيفون برجل وهو يضحكهم ، فقال : ما هذا ؟ قالوا : رجل يتشبه بالحمار يضحك أصحابه ، فقال : سبحان الله وما يؤمن هذا - وقد أحسن الله صورته - أن يحوله في صورة حمار

Isnad

Sulayman b. Dawud narrated to us: Ibn Wahb informed us: Yunus narrated to me, from Ibn Shihab, that Muhammad b. 'Urwah informed him, from 'Urwah, who said:

Matn

A woman died whom the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ used to laugh at. Bilal said of her: “Poor woman — she has found rest.” The Messenger of God ﷺ said [to him]: “Only one who has been forgiven finds rest.”

Isnad

Musa b. Isma'il narrated to us: Aban narrated to us: Qatadah narrated to us,

Matn

that it had reached him that the Messenger of God ﷺ, when he saw the new moon, would say: “A moon of good and right guidance; a moon of good and right guidance; I believe in Him who created you” — three times — then he would say: “Praise be to God who has taken away such-and-such month and brought such-and-such month.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا موسى بن إسماعيل ، حدثنا أبان . حدثنا قتادة ، أنه بلغه أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم

Matn

كان إذا رأى الهلال قال : هلال خير ورشد ، هلال خير ورشد ، آمنت بالذي خلقك ، ثلاث مرات ، ثم يقول : الحمد لله الذي ذهب بشهر كذا وجاء بشهر كذا

Isnad

Muhammad b. al-'Ala' narrated to us: that Zayd b. al-Hubab informed them, from Abu Hilal, from Qatadah,

Matn

that the Messenger of God ﷺ, when he saw the new moon, would turn his face away from it.
