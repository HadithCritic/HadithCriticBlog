Entry83/ 12
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 116
English

al-Hasan b. ‘Ali narrated to us: Yazid b. Harun narrated to us: al-Hajjaj b. Hassan informed us, from Muqatil b. Hayyan, who traced it back (to the Prophet), saying: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: “If a man comes and finds no one [to stand with], let him pull a man toward himself from the row so that he stands with him — how great is the reward of the one who is pulled!”

Matn

Entry89/ 12
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 118
English

Sulayman b. Dawud narrated to us: Ibn Wahb narrated to us: Mu‘awiya b. Salih informed me, from ‘Abd al-Qahir, from Khalid b. Abi ‘Imran, who said: While the Messenger of God (ﷺ) was invoking (God) against Mudar, Jibril came to him and gestured to him to be silent, so he fell silent. He said: “O Muhammad, God did not send you to be one who reviles or one who curses. He sent you only as a mercy, and He did not send you as a punishment: {You have no say in the matter whether He turns to them in mercy or punishes them, for they are wrongdoers}.” He said: Then he taught him this qunut: “O God, we seek Your help and Your forgiveness; we believe in You and submit humbly to You; we cast off and forsake whoever disbelieves in You. O God, You alone we worship, to You we pray and prostrate, and toward You we strive and hasten. We hope for Your mercy and fear Your true punishment; indeed Your punishment will overtake the disbelievers.”

Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 122
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us: Abu al-Yaman narrated to us: Shu'ayb informed us, from al-Zuhri, who said: I read a document held by the family of Abu Bakr b. Muhammad b. 'Amr b. Hazm, which mentioned that the Messenger of God ﷺ wrote it for 'Amr b. Hazm when he appointed him over Najran. And he related the report, in which: “The lesser pilgrimage is the 'umrah, and none may touch the Qur'an except one who is pure.”
