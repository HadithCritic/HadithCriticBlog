Entry106/ 13
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 128
English

Musa b. Isma'il narrated to us; he said: Hammad said: I said to Qays b. Sa'd: “Take for me the letter of Muhammad b. 'Amr b. Hazm.” He gave me a letter and informed me that he had taken it from Abu Bakr b. Muhammad b. 'Amr b. Hazm: that the Prophet ﷺ wrote it for his grandfather. I read it, and in it was the mention of what is to be paid of the obligatory zakat on camels. He related the report until he reached one hundred and twenty: “Then when it is more than that, count a hiqqah (a three-year-old female camel) for every fifty; and whatever is left over is returned to the first obligation for camels. And whatever is fewer than twenty-five, there are sheep in it: for every five camels, one sheep — it shall not be a male, nor an aged one, nor one with a defect among the sheep.”

Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 129
English

'Abd Allah b. Maslamah narrated to us, from Malik, from Humayd b. Qays, from Tawus al-Yamani: that Mu'adh b. Jabal took a tabi' (a yearling calf) from thirty cows, and a musinnah (a two-year-old cow) from forty cows. Then he was brought something less than that, and he refused to take anything from it, and said: “I have not heard anything from the [Prophet] ﷺ about this, until I meet him and ask him.” But the [Prophet] ﷺ died before Mu'adh b. Jabal returned.

Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 131
English

Muhammad b. ‘Ubayd narrated to us: Ibn Thawr narrated to us, from Ma‘mar, from Ayyub, who said: For a time I used to hear them saying: “Take from us what the Prophet (ﷺ) took,” and I used to wonder why that was not accepted from them, until al-Zuhri told me that the Prophet (ﷺ) had written down these obligations, but he died before sending them to his agents; so Abu Bakr acted on them according to what had been written. I only know that he mentioned cattle as well.

Entry112/ 13
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 131
English

Muhammad b. Mansur al-Tusi narrated to us: Ya'qub narrated to us: my father narrated to us, [from Ibn Ishaq]; he said: Muhammad b. Muslim al-Zuhri mentioned: that among the matters of zakat that the Messenger of God ﷺ settled was that, after his first letter with Mu'adh b. Jabal, he set a ruling for the awqas (the non-taxable brackets) of cattle. The awqas are: five cattle and more, up to ten; so for ten he set two sheep. Then he made the zakat on cattle similar to the zakat on camels.
