English
Isnad

Yahya b. Ma‘in narrated to us: Hatim b. Isma‘il narrated to us: Ibn Hurmuz al-Fadaki narrated to us, from Sa‘id and Muhammad,

Matn

the two sons of ‘Ubayd, from Abu Hatim al-Muzani, who said: The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “If there comes to you one with whose religion and character you are pleased, then marry (your daughter) to him” — three times.

Matn

إذا جاءكم من ترضون دينه وخلقه فأنكحوه ثلاث مرات .

Entry226/ 9
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 193
English
Isnad

'Abd Allah b. al-Jarrah narrated to us: Jarir narrated to us, from al-Shaybani, from al-Hakam b. 'Utaybah:

Matn

that the Prophet ﷺ sent Bilal to a household of the Ansar to propose marriage to them. They said: “An Abyssinian slave?” Bilal said: “Were it not that the Prophet ﷺ ordered me to come to you, I would not have come to you.” They said: “The Prophet ﷺ ordered you?” He said: “Yes.” They said: “Then you have obtained her.” So he came to the Prophet ﷺ and told him. Then a piece of gold was brought in to the Prophet ﷺ, and he gave it to him, saying: “Take this to your wife,” and he said to his Companions: “Gather [contributions] for your brother for his wedding feast.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا عبد الله بن الجراح ، حدثنا جرير ، عن الشيباني . عن الحكم بن عتيبة ،

Matn

أن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم أرسل بلالا إلى أهل بيت من الأنصار يخطب إليهم ، فقالوا : عبد حبشي ، فقال بلال : لولا أن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم أمرني أن آتيكم ما أتيتكم ، فقالوا : النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم أمرك ، قال : نعم ، قالوا : قد ملكت ، فجاء [إلى] النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم فأخبره ، فأدخلت على النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم قطعة من ذهب فأعطاه إياها فقال : سق هذا إلى امرأتك ، وقال لأصحابه : اجمعوا لأخيكم في وليمته

Isnad

‘Uthman narrated to us: Jarir narrated to us, from Mughira, from ‘Amir, who said:

Matn

Bilal went with his brother to ask for a woman’s hand on his behalf from a clan of the Arabs. They said: “Two Abyssinian slaves!” [Bilal said: “Yes,”] we were astray and God guided us, and we were slaves and God — [Mighty and Majestic] — freed us.

Isnad

Harun b. Zayd narrated to us: my father narrated to us, from Hisham b. Sa'd, from Zayd b. Aslam:

Matn

that Banu Bukayr came to [the Messenger of God] ﷺ and said: “Marry our sister to so-and-so.” He said: “Where are you with regard to Bilal?” They repeated their request, and he repeated the statement three times, so they married her to him. He said: Banu Bukayr were among the Emigrants, of Banu Layth.

Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 195
English
Isnad

'Amr b. 'Uthman and Kathir b. 'Ubayd narrated to us; both said: Baqiyyah narrated to us: al-Zubaydi narrated to me: al-Zuhri narrated to me, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ ordered Banu Bayadah to marry a woman of theirs to Abu Hind, and they said: “O Messenger of God, shall we marry our daughters to our freedmen?” So God — [Mighty and Majestic] — revealed: {Indeed, We have created you from male and female and made you peoples and tribes} — the verse. Al-Zuhri said: It was revealed specifically concerning Abu Hind.

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا عمرو بن عثمان ، وكثير بن عبيد ، قالا : حدثنا بقية ، حدثني الزبيدي . حدثني الزهري ، قال :

Matn

أمر رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم بني بياضة أن يزوجوا أبا هند امرأة منهم ، فقالوا : يا رسول الله نزوج بناتنا موالينا ؟ فأنزل الله - [عز وجل] - : { إنا خلقناكم من ذكر وأنثى وجعلناكم شعوبا وقبائل } الآية . قال الزهري : نزلت في أبي هند خاصة

Isnad

Ibn 'Ubayd narrated to us,

Matn

in the report of Hammad, from Ayyub, from 'Ikrimah: that a man married off his daughter while she disliked it, so she came to the Prophet ﷺ and mentioned that to him. He said: “Do you dislike him?” She said: “Yes.” So he placed her affair in her own hands.
