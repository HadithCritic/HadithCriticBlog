Entry12/ 7
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 77
English
Isnad

Ibn al-Muthanna narrated to us: Ibn Abi ‘Adi narrated to us, from Sa‘id, from Qatada, from al-Hasan, who said:

Matn

When the Messenger of God (ﷺ) brought them to his people — meaning the prayers — he left them (without acting on them) until the sun had declined from the middle of the sky. Then the call went out among them: “The prayer is being gathered (al-salat jami‘a)!” So they assembled for it, and they were alarmed. The Prophet of God (ﷺ) led them in four rak‘ahs without reciting aloud, with Jibril in front of the Messenger of God (ﷺ), and the Messenger of God (ﷺ) [in front of] the people; the people were following their Prophet (ﷺ), and the Prophet of God (ﷺ) was following Jibril (ﷺ). Then he left them (without acting on them) until, when the sun had sunk low while still white and clear, the call went out among them: “The prayer is being gathered!” So they assembled for it, and the Prophet of God (ﷺ) led them in four rak‘ahs, shorter than the Dhuhr prayer. Then Ibn al-Muthanna related (the rest) as he had related it for the Dhuhr. He said: Then he held off from them until, when the sun had set, the call went out among them: “The prayer is being gathered!” So they assembled for it, and [the Prophet] (ﷺ) led them in three rak‘ahs, reciting aloud in two rak‘ahs, and in the third rak‘ah not reciting aloud; the Messenger of God (ﷺ) was in front of the people and Jibril was in front of [the Prophet] (ﷺ). Then he related (the rest) as he had related it for the ‘Asr. (He said:) Then, when the twilight came and the ‘Isha’ was delayed, the call went out among them: “The prayer is being gathered!” So they assembled for it, and the Messenger of God (ﷺ) led them in four rak‘ahs, reciting aloud in two and not reciting aloud in two — and he related (the rest) as he had related it for the Maghrib. He said: So they spent the night not knowing whether anything would be added to that or not. Then, when dawn broke, the call went out among them: “The prayer is being gathered!” So they assembled for it, and the Prophet of God (ﷺ) led them in two rak‘ahs, reciting aloud in them and making the recitation long in them, with Jibril (ﷺ) in front of the Messenger of God (ﷺ), and the Messenger of God (ﷺ) in front of the people, the people following their Prophet (ﷺ), and their Prophet following Jibril.

Entry15/ 7
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 78
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Salamah al-Muradi narrated to us: Ibn Wahb narrated to us, from Ibn Lahi'ah, that Bukayr b. al-Ashajj narrated to him:

Matn

that in Medina there were nine mosques in addition to the mosque of the Prophet ﷺ, whose people could hear Bilal's call to prayer in the time of the Messenger of God ﷺ, and they would pray in their own mosques. The nearest of them were: the mosque of Banu 'Amr b. Mabdhul of Banu al-Najjar; the mosque of Banu Sa'idah; the mosque of Banu 'Ubayd; the mosque of Banu Salimah; the mosque of Banu Rabih of Banu 'Abd al-Ashhal; the mosque of Banu Zurayq; the mosque of Banu Ghifar; the mosque of Aslam; and the mosque of Juhaynah — and he was unsure about the ninth.

Entry17/ 7
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 80
English
Isnad

'Ubayd God b. Mu'adh narrated to us: my father narrated to us: Ash'ath narrated to us, from al-Hasan:

Matn

that the delegation of Thaqif came to the Messenger of God ﷺ, and a tent was pitched for them at the rear of the mosque so that they could watch the prayer of the Muslims, their bowing and their prostration. It was said: “O Messenger of God, will you lodge them in the mosque when they are polytheists?” He said: “The earth is not made impure; only the son of Adam is made impure.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا عبيد الله بن معاذ ، حدثنا أبي ، حدثنا أشعث . عن الحسن

Matn

أن وفد ثقيف أتوا رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فضربت لهم قبة في مؤخر المسجد لينظروا إلى صلاة المسلمين ، وإلى ركوعهم وسجودهم ، فقيل : يا رسول الله أتنزلهم المسجد وهم مشركون ؟ فقال : إن الأرض لا تنجس ، إنما ينجس ابن آدم

Entry18/ 7
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 80
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Salamah al-Muradi narrated to us: Ibn Wahb narrated to us, from Yunus, from Ibn Shihab: Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab informed me:

Matn

that Abu Sufyan used to enter the mosque in Medina while he was a disbeliever, but that was not permissible for him in the Sacred Mosque, because of what God — [Mighty and Majestic] — said: {The polytheists are but impure, so let them not approach al-Masjid al-Haram} — the verse.
