Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 299
English
Isnad

Ziyad b. Ayyub narrated to us: Hushaym narrated to us: Mansur informed us, from al-Hasan, who said:

Matn

A red velvet cloak (qatifa) that he had obtained on the day of Khaybar was placed in the niche-grave (lahd) of [the Prophet], because the ground of Medina is saline.

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا زياد بن أيوب ، حدثنا هشيم ، أخبرنا منصور . عن الحسن ، قال :

Matn

جعل في لحد [النبي] صلى الله عليه وسلم قطيفة حمراء أصابها يوم خيبر ; لأن المدينة أرض سبخة

Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 304
English
Isnad

‘Abdullah b. Maslama b. Qa‘nab and ‘Abdullah b. ‘Umar b. Muhammad b. Aban b. Salih narrated to us, that ‘Abd al-‘Aziz b. Muhammad narrated to them, from ‘Abdullah [ibn Muhammad

Matn

— meaning Ibn ‘Umar —], from his father: that the Messenger of God (ﷺ) [sprinkled water on the grave of] his son Ibrahim. Ibn ‘Umar added in his hadith: and it was the first grave to be sprinkled upon; and that when he [Ibrahim] was buried and he had finished at its head, he said: “Peace be upon you.” And I only know that he said: he threw earth upon it with his hand.

العربية
Isnad

Matn

أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم [رش على قبر] ابنه إبراهيم . زاد ابن عمر في حديثه : وإنه أول قبر رش عليه ، وإنه قال حين دفن ، ففرغ منه عند رأسه : سلام عليكم . ولا أعلمه إلا قال : حثا عليه بيده

English
Isnad

'Amr b. 'Uthman narrated to us: Baqiyyah narrated to us,

Matn

[switch of chain] [and Muhammad b. 'Awf narrated to us]: Abu al-Mughirah narrated to us, from Safwan, from Abu al-Yaman al-Huzani, who said: When Abu Talib, the uncle of the [Prophet] ﷺ, died, the [Prophet] ﷺ went out and met his funeral procession — Ibn 'Awf said: and he walked alongside it, saying: “May ties of kinship repay you with kindness, and may you be rewarded with good.” And he did not stand at his grave. (19605) Ibn 'Awf added: and he did not seek forgiveness [for him].

العربية
Isnad

Matn

لما توفي أبو طالب عم [النبي] صلى الله عليه وسلم خرج [النبي] صلى الله عليه وسلم فعارض جنازته ، قال ابن عوف : فجعل يمشي مجانبا لها ويقول : برتك رحم ، وجزيت خيرا . ولم يقم على قبره ( 19605 ) . زاد ابن عوف : ولم يستغفر
