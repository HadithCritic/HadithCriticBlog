Isnad

Ahmad b. Hanbal narrated to us: Hushaym narrated to us: Yunus informed us, from al-Hasan, who said:

Matn

When {And due to God from the people is a pilgrimage to the House — for whoever is able to find thereto a way} was revealed, [he said:] it was asked: “O Messenger of God, what is the way?” He said: “Provisions and a mount.”

Matn

Ka'b al-Qurazi, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “I wish to renew [this matter] in the hearts of the believers: any boy whose family performs Hajj with him and who then dies, it suffices for him; but if he reaches maturity, Hajj is obligatory on him. Isnad

Yusuf b. Musa narrated to us: Abu Usama narrated to us: Sa‘id ibn Abi ‘Aruba narrated to us: Matar al-Warraq narrated to us, that Mu‘awiya b. Qurra narrated to them, from a man of the Ansar:

Matn

A man in a state of ihram let his riding-beast trample an ostrich nest (udhiyy). So the man went to ‘Ali and asked him about it, and ‘Ali (ﷺ) said to him: “For every egg: the covering of a she-camel (dirab naqa), or the fetus of a she-camel.” So the man went to the Prophet (ﷺ) and told him what ‘Ali had said. The Prophet of God (ﷺ) said: “‘Ali has said what you heard, but come to the concession: upon you, for every egg, is the fast of one day.”

العربية
