Entry241/ 9
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 203
English

Hisham b. Khalid narrated to us: al-Hasan b. Yahya al-Khushani narrated to us, from Zayd b. Waqid, from Makhul, from 'Ubadah b. al-Samit, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Establish the legal punishments (hudud) [at home and while traveling], upon the near and the far, and do not fear, in the matter of God, the blame of any blamer.”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

أقيموا الحدود في [الحضر والسفر] على القريب والبعيد ، ولا تبالوا في الله لومة لائم

Entry244/ 9
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 204
English

Ahmad b. 'Abdah narrated to us: Sufyan narrated to us, from Yazid b. Khusayfah, from Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. Thawban: that the Prophet ﷺ was brought a thief who had stolen a shamlah (a wrap). He said: “I do not think you stole.” He said: “Yes, I did.” He said: “Take him away and cut off his hand, then cauterize it, then bring him to me.” So they took him away and cut it off, then cauterized it, then brought him to him. He said: “Repent to God.” He said: “I have repented to God.” He said: “O God, accept his repentance.”

Entry247/ 9
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 206
English

Muhammad b. Sulayman al-Anbari narrated to us: [Hammad b. Mas‘ada narrated to us,] from Ibn Jurayj, from ‘Abd Rabbih b. Abi Umayya, from al-Harith b. ‘Abdullah b. Abi Rabi‘a: that a thief was brought to the Prophet (ﷺ), and it was said: “He is (the support) of orphans of the Ansar, who have no property but him.” So he let him go. Then a second time, and he let him go; then a third time, and he let him go; then a fourth, and he let him go; then a fifth, and he cut off his hand; then a sixth, and he cut off his foot; then a seventh, and he cut off his hand; then an eighth, and he cut off his foot. Then he said: “Four for four.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا محمد بن سليمان الأنباري ، [حدثنا حماد بن مسعدة] ، عن ابن جريج ، عن عبد ربه بن أبي أمية . عن الحارث بن عبد الله بن أبي ربيعة ،

Matn

أن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم أتي بسارق فقيل : هو ليتامى من الأنصار ما لهم مال غيره ، فتركه ثم الثانية ، فتركه ، ثم الثالثة ، فتركه ، ثم الرابعة فتركه ، ثم الخامسة فقطع يده ، ثم السادسة فقطع رجله ، ثم السابعة فقطع يده ، ثم الثامنة فقطع رجله ، ثم قال : أربع بأربع

English

Hannad b. al-Sari narrated to us: Abu al-Ahwas narrated to us, from Simak, from al-Hasan al-Basri, who said: A woman came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: “I have committed adultery” — and he mentioned the hadith — he said: When she gave birth, he gave the order concerning her, so she purified herself and put on her shrouds; then he gave the order, and she was stoned.
