English

Muhammad b. al-Muthanna narrated to us: Ibn Abi 'Adi narrated to us: Husayn al-Mu'allim narrated to us, from Qatadah, from al-Hasan, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Fast, and let your hair grow full, for it is a mujfirah [that which diminishes sexual desire].”

Entry201/ 12
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 179
English

Wahb b. Baqiyya narrated to us, from Khalid, from Husayn, from Abu Malik, concerning His saying [Exalted is He]: {O you who believe, do not forbid the good things that God has made lawful for you}: He said: It was revealed about ‘Uthman b. Maz‘un and his companions. They had forbidden themselves many pleasures and women, and some of them even meant to cut off his member; so God — [Mighty and Majestic] — revealed this verse: {and do not transgress; indeed God does not love the transgressors}.

Entry202/ 12
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 180
English

Ahmad b. Hanbal narrated to us: Mu'adh b. Mu'adh narrated to us: Ibn Jurayj narrated to us; and Ahmad b. Salih narrated to us: 'Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Maymun Abu Mughallis, from Abu Najih — Ahmad said [ibn Hanbal: he is the father of] 'Abd Allah b. Abi Najih — who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Whoever is well-off enough to marry and does not marry is not one of us.”
