Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 93
English
Isnad

Ziyad b. Ayyub narrated to us: Abu Mu'awiyah narrated to us: Sa'd b. Sa'id narrated to us, from Mu'adh b. 'Abd Allah b. Khubayb, from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ prayed the Fajr prayer and recited in the first rak'ah {When the earth is shaken} (Surat al-Zalzalah), then stood for the second and repeated it.

Matn

صلى رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم الفجر ، فقرأ في الركعة الأولى بـ { إذا زلزلت } ، ثم قام في الثانية ، فأعادها

Entry41/ 10
Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 93
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Salama al-Muradi narrated to us: Ibn Wahb narrated to us, from Yunus [al-Ayli], from Ibn Shihab, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God (ﷺ) established it as sunna to recite aloud in the Fajr prayer in both rak‘ahs; to recite in the first two rak‘ahs of the Dhuhr prayer the Mother of the Qur’an (al-Fatiha) and one sura in each rak‘ah, silently to himself; and to recite in the last two rak‘ahs of the Dhuhr prayer the Mother of the Qur’an in each rak‘ah, silently to himself; and to do in the ‘Asr the same as he does in the Dhuhr. The imam recites aloud in the first two rak‘ahs of the Maghrib, reciting in each of them the Mother of the Qur’an and one sura; and he recites in the last rak‘ah of the Maghrib prayer the Mother of the Qur’an in each rak‘ah and one sura; and in the last two rak‘ahs he recites to himself the Mother of the Qur’an. Those behind the imam keep silent and listen to what the imam recites aloud of the Qur’an; no one recites along with him. And the tashahhud in the prayer is [made] when the imam sits, with the people behind him, after the first two rak‘ahs.

العربية
Isnad

Matn

سن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أن يجهر بالقراءة في صلاة الفجر في الركعتين كلتيهما ، ويقرأ في الركعتين الأوليين في صلاة الظهر بأم القرآن وسورة سورة في كل ركعة سرا في نفسه ، ويقرأ في الركعتين الأخريين من صلاة الظهر بأم القرآن في كل ركعة سرا في نفسه ، ويفعل في العصر مثل ما يفعل في الظهر ، ويجهر الإمام بالقراءة في الأوليين من المغرب ، ويقرأ في كل واحدة منهما بأم القرآن وسورة سورة ، ويقرأ في الركعة الآخرة من صلاة المغرب بأم القرآن في كل ركعة وسورة سورة ، ويقرأ في الركعتين الآخرتين في نفسه بأم القرآن ، وينصت من وراء الإمام ، ويستمع لما جهر به الإمام من القرآن لا يقرأ معه أحد ، والتشهد في الصلاة حين يجلس الإمام والناس خلفه في الركعتين الأوليين

English
Isnad

Yazid b. Khalid narrated to us: 'Affan narrated to us: Hammam narrated to us, from Shaqiq Abu Layth: 'Asim b. Kulayb narrated to me, from his father,

Matn

that the Prophet ﷺ, when he prostrated, his knees would reach the ground before his palms did. He said: And when he rose at the end of the two rak'ahs, he would rise upon his knees and support himself on his thighs.

Isnad

Abu Tawbah narrated to us: Abu Ishaq [that is,] al-Fazari narrated to us, from 'Asim, from 'Ikrimah, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ saw a man praying whose nose did not touch the ground, and said: “[Do not do that],” or he said: “The prayer is not valid [if] the nose does not touch,” or he said: “[if] the nose does not receive from it what touches or reaches the forehead.”

Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd · p. 97
English
Isnad

Musa b. Isma'il narrated to us: Hammad [that is, Ibn Salamah] narrated to us, from Burd Abu al-'Ala', from Sulayman b. Musa, from a man of Banu 'Adi b. Ka'b:

Matn

that they entered upon the Prophet ﷺ while he was praying sitting, and said: “What is the matter with you, O Messenger of God?” He said: “A scorpion stung me.” Then he said: “If one of you finds a scorpion while he is praying, let him kill it with his left sandal.”

أنهم دخلوا على النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم وهو يصلي جالسا ، فقالوا : ما شأنك يا رسول الله ؟ فقال : لسعتني عقرب ، ثم قال : إذا وجد أحدكم عقربا وهو يصلي ، فليقتلها بنعله اليسرى
