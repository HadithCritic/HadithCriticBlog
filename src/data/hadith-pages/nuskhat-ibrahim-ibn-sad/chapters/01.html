English

The juz' containing the notebook (nuskhah) of Ibrahim b. Sa'd b. Ibrahim b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf al-Zuhri, may God Most High have mercy on him ﵀, in the transmission of Abu Salih, the scribe of al-Layth—that is, 'Abd Allah b. Salih al-Misri—from him. The transmission of Abu l-Hasan Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Salam b. Abi l-Suwar al-Sarraj, from him. The transmission of Abu Muhammad al-Hasan b. Rashiq al-'Askari, from him. The transmission of Abu l-Hasan b. Rabi'ah b. 'Ali al-Bazzaz, from him. The transmission of Abu Sadiq Murshid b. Yahya b. al-Qasim al-Madini, from him. The transmission of Abu l-Qasim Hibat Allah b. 'Ali b. Sa'ud al-Busiri, from him. The transmission of Abu 'Isa 'Abd Allah b. 'Abd al-Wahid b. Muhammad b. 'Allaq, from him. The transmission of Abu l-Fath Muhammad b. Muhammad al-Maydumi, and 'Abd al-'Aziz b. Muhammad b. Abi Bakr al-Hamawi.

Hurmuz al-A'raj, that he heard Abu Hurayrah say: “You say: ‘Abu Hurayrah narrates too much, and he is the great promiser (al-wa''ad),’ and you say: ‘What is the matter with the Emigrants (muhajirun), that they do not narrate the like of his hadiths? What is the matter with the Helpers (ansar), that they do not narrate the like of his hadiths?’ I will tell you about that. My brothers among the Emigrants were occupied with working their lands and their property, while I was a poor man who kept to the Messenger of God ﷺ for the sake of filling my belly; so I was present when they were absent, and I retained when they forgot. And after the Messenger of God ﷺ said one day: ‘Will not one of you spread out his garment until I have finished this saying of mine, and then gather his garment to his chest? He will never forget anything of my saying.’” Abu Hurayrah said: “So I spread out a striped cloak (ghurrah) I had, which was the only garment I owned, until the Messenger of God ﷺ had finished his saying; then I gathered it to my chest. By the One who sent Muhammad with the truth, I have not forgotten a single word of that saying of his to this day.” Abu Hurayrah said: “By God, were it not for two verses that God revealed in His Book, I would never have narrated anything to you—were it not for the words of God Most High: {Indeed, those who conceal what We sent down of clear proofs and guidance after We made it clear to the people in the Book} [al-Baqarah 2:159]”—to the end of the two verses. 1412 - Abu Salih narrated to us: Ibrahim narrated to me, from Ibn Shihab, from Salim b. 'Abd Allah, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Umar: that the Messenger of God ﷺ was asked what clothing the muhrim (pilgrim in consecration) must leave off. He said: “He does not wear the shirt, nor the turban, nor trousers, nor hooded cloaks (burnus), nor any garment touched by saffron or wars (a yellow dye). And whoever cannot find sandals, let him wear leather socks, and cut them until they are below the ankles.” 1413 - Abu Salih narrated to us: Ibrahim narrated to me, from Ibn Shihab, from 'Amir b. Sa'd and 'Abd Allah b. 'Amir b. Rabi'ah, who both said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “This plague is a punishment (rijz) with which God tormented some of the nations before you, and remnants of it remained in the land; it comes at times and goes at times. So when you hear of it in a land and you are not in it, do not enter it; and when it breaks out in a land in which you are present, do not let flight from it take you out.”
