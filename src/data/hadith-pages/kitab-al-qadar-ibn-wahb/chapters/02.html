Opening of the copy: transmission chain · p. 56

Part Two. In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The shaykh Abu al-Husayn Muhammad b. Hasnun al-Narsi informed us, by reading to him; he said: Abu Bakr Muhammad b. Isma'il al-Warraq informed us, by reading to him, in Muharram of the year three hundred and seventy-five; he said: Abu Bakr 'Abd Allah b. Sulayman b. al-Ash'ath al-Sijistani [narrated], by reading to us, in the year three hundred and eleven.

العربية

الجزء الثاني بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم أخبرنا الشَّيخُ أبو الحُسين؛ محمدُ بن حسنون النَّرسي -قراءةً عليه- قال: أبنا أبو بكر؛ محمَّدُ بن إسماعيلَ الورَّاق -قراءةً عليه- في المُحرَّم؛ سنةَ خمسٍ وسبعينَ وثلاثِ مِئَة، قال: أبو بكر؛ عبدُ الله بن سُليمان بن الأشعَث السِّجِسْتاني -قِراءةً علينا- سنةَ إحدى عشرة وثلاثِ مِئَة.

English
Isnad

He said: Abu Ja'far Ahmad b. Sa'id al-Hamdani informed us in Egypt; he said: 'Abd Allah b. Wahb informed us; he said: Ibn Lahi'a and al-Layth b. Sa'd informed me, from Qays b. al-Hajjaj, from Hanash b. 'Abd Allah, from Ibn 'Abbas, that he said:

Matn

I rode behind the Messenger of God ﷺ one day, and he reached back with his hand behind his back and said: “Boy, shall I not teach you some words? Guard God and He will guard you; guard God and you will find Him before you. If you seek help, seek help from God, and if you ask, ask God. The pens have dried and the scrolls have been lifted. By Him in whose hand is the soul of Muhammad, if the whole community were to strive to benefit you, they could not benefit you except with something God has written for you; and if the whole community were to strive to harm you, they could not harm you except with something that has been written against you.”

العربية
Isnad

قال: أبنا أبو جعفر؛ أحمدُ بن سعيد الهَمْدانيُّ بمِصرَ، قال: أبنا عبدُ الله بن وَهْبٍ، قال: أخبرني ابنُ لَهِيْعَة، والليثُ بن سعد، عن قيس بن الحجَّاج، عن حَنَشِ بنِ عبد الله، عن ابن عبَّاس، أنَّه قال:

Matn

رَدِفْتُ رسولَ الله ﷺ يوماً، فأخلفَ بيدِه خلفَ ظَهرِه فقال: «يا غُلام، ألا أُعَلِّمُكَ كَلِماتٍ؟ احفَظِ اللهَ يحفَظْكَ، احفَظِ اللهَ تجِدْهُ أمامَك، إذا استعَنْتَ فاستَعِنْ بالله، وإذا سألتَ فاسألِ الله، جفَّتِ الأقلامُ ورُفعَتِ الصُّحُفُ. والذي نفسُ محمَّدٍ بيدِهِ، لو جَهَدَتِ الأُمَّةُ على أن ينفَعوكَ؛ لم ينفَعوكَ إلَاّ بشَيءٍ كتبَهُ اللهُ لكَ، ولو جَهَدَتِ الأُمَّةُ على أن يضرُّوكَ؛ لم يضرُّوكَ إلَاّ بشَيءٍ قد كُتِبَ عليكَ».

Isnad

'Abd Allah b. Sulayman narrated to us; Ahmad b. Sa'id al-Hamdani narrated to us; Ibn Wahb informed us; he said: 'Umar b. Muhammad informed me, that Sulayman b. Mihran narrated to him, who said: 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud ﵁ said:

Matn

The first thing [God] ﷻ created of His creation was the Pen. He said to it: “Write.” So it wrote everything that will be in the world until the Day of Resurrection. Then the first Book will be brought together with the deeds of the servants, and not one alif, nor waw, nor mim of them will differ from it.

Abu Bakr 'Abd Allah b. Sulayman narrated to us; he said: al-Hamdani narrated to us; Ibn Wahb informed us; he said: Yunus informed me, from Ibn Shihab, that 'Abd al-Rahman b. Hunayda narrated to them that 'Abd Allah b. 'Umar said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Isnad

'Abd Allah b. Sulayman narrated to us; he said: al-Hamdani narrated to us; he said: Ibn Wahb informed us; he said: 'Amr b. al-Harith informed us, from Abu al-Zubayr al-Makki, that 'Amir b. Wathila, meaning Abu al-Tufayl, informed him that he heard 'Abd Allah b. Mas'ud say:

Matn

The wretched is he who was wretched in his mother's womb, and the happy is he who takes admonition from others. Then a man from among the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ, called Hudhayfa b. Asid al-Ghifari, came to him and told him that, from the saying of Ibn Mas'ud. I said: “How can a man be wretched without a deed?” The man said to him: “Do you wonder at that? I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: 'When forty-two nights have passed over the drop (nutfa), God ﷻ sends an angel to it, who shapes it and creates its hearing, its sight, its skin, its flesh, and its bone. Then he says: “My Lord, male or female?” And God decrees in that what He wills, and the angel writes. Then he says: “My Lord, its term?” And your Lord says what He wills, and the angel writes. Then he says: “My Lord, its provision?” And your Lord decrees what He wills, and the angel writes. Then the angel goes out with the sheet in his hand, and he does not add to what he was commanded, nor subtract from it.'”

Entry39 (ز)/ 24
Kitāb al-Qadar of Ibn Wahb · p. 70
English
Isnad

And Abu Muhammad 'Abd al-Rahman b. Muhammad al-Qurashi, known as Abu Sakhra, informed us; he said: 'Ali b. al-Madini narrated to us; he said: Yahya b. Zakariyya b. Abi Za'ida narrated to us; he said: al-A'mash narrated to us, from Zayd b. Wahb, from Ibn Mas'ud, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ narrated to us, and he is the truthful, the one who is believed:

Matn

“The creation of each of you is gathered in his mother's belly over forty nights; then it is a clot ('alaqa) for a like period; then it is a lump (mudgha) for a like period. Then the angel is sent to him with four words, and he writes: his provision, his deed, his term, and whether wretched or happy. Then one of you does the deeds of the people of the Garden until there is nothing between him and it but a cubit, and the Book overtakes him, so he does the deeds of the people of the Fire, and is sealed, and becomes one of them. And one of you does the deeds of the people of the Fire until there is nothing between him and it but a cubit, and the Book overtakes him, so he does the deeds of the people of the Garden, and is sealed, and becomes one of them.”

العربية
Isnad

وأخبرنا أبو محمَّدٍ؛ عبدُ الرَّحمن بن محمَّدٍ القُرشيُّ، يُعرفُ بأبي صَخرةَ، قال: ثَنَا عليُّ بنُ المديني، قال: ثَنَا يحيى بنُ زكريَّا بن أبي زائِدة، قال: ثَنَا الأعمشُ، عن زيد بن وَهْبٍ، عن ابنِ مسعودٍ، قال: ثَنَا رسولُ الله ﷺ وهو الصَّادق المَصدوقُ:

Matn

«إنَّ خَلْقَ أحَدِكُمْ يُجمَعُ في بطنِ أُمِّهِ في أربعينَ ليلةً، ثم يكونُ عَلَقَةً مثلَ ذلك، ثم يكونُ مُضغَةً مثلَ ذلك، ثم يُبْعَثُ إليه المَلَكُ بأربع كلماتٍ، فيكتبُ: رزقَهُ، وعملَهُ، وأجلَهُ، وشَقيٌّ أم سعيدٌ. فإنَّ أحدَكُم ليَعْمَلُ بعَمَلِ أهلِ الجَنَّةِ؛ حتى ما يكونُ بَيْنَهُ وبَيْنَها إلَاّ ذِراعٌ، فيَسْبِقُ عليه الكتابُ؛ فيَعْمَلُ بعَمَلِ أهلِ النَّارِ، فيُخْتَمُ له؛ فيكونُ مِنْ أهلِها. وإنَّ أحدَكُم ليَعْمَلُ بعَمَلِ أهلِ النَّارِ؛ حتى ما يكونُ بَيْنَهُ وبَيْنَها إلَاّ ذِراعٌ، فيَسْبِقُ عليه الكتابُ؛ فيَعْمَلُ بعَمَلِ أهلِ الجنَّةِ؛ فيُخْتَمُ له؛ فيكونُ مِنْ أهلِها».
