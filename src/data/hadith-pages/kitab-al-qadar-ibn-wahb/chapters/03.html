Editor's introduction (praise and description of the work)

English · p. 5

In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Praise belongs to God; we praise Him, seek His help and ask His forgiveness, and we seek refuge in God from the evils of our selves and the wickedness of our deeds. Whomever God guides, none can lead astray, and whomever He leads astray, none can guide. I bear witness that there is no god but God alone, who has no partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and messenger, ﷺ, with abundant peace. To proceed: it gives us pleasure to place before readers and students of knowledge the “Book of al-Qadar” by the imam, the hafiz, the ascetic 'Abd Allah b. Wahb al-Qurashi al-Misri, who died in the year (197 AH), may God have mercy on him with a wide mercy. In this book, the compiler, may God have mercy on him, followed the method of the early generations (al-salaf) in their compilations in that period: he related the prophetic hadiths and the reports of the early generations that affirm divine decree and predestination, without commentary or chapter headings. True, his arrangement of the hadiths and reports may suggest that they follow a general thematic sequence, but he never made chapter divisions. Then al-Warraq, Abu Bakr Muhammad b. Isma'il, who is the transmitter of the book, added to the original book hadiths from his own shaykhs, which in sum are extractions (istikhraj) and corroborations (mutaba'at) of the hadiths of the book.

العربية

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم إنَّ الحمدَ للهِ نَحْمَدُهُ، ونستعينُهُ ونستغفِرُهُ، ونعوذُ باللهِ من شُرورِ أنفُسنا، وسيِّئاتِ أعمالنا، من يهدِهِ الله فلا مُضِلَّ له، ومَنْ يُضللْ فلا هاديَ له، وأشهدُ أنْ لا إله إلَاّ الله وحده لا شريك له، وأشهد أنَّ محمداً عبدُهُ ورسولُهُ. ﷺ تسليماً كثيراً. أما بعد: فإنَّه مما يسرُّنا أنْ نضَعَ بين يدي القُرَّاءِ وطلبةِ العلم «كتاب القدر» للإمام الحافظِ الزاهدِ عبدِ الله بن وَهْبٍ القُرشيِّ المِصريِّ، المُتوفَّى سنة (197 هـ) ﵀ رحمةً واسعةً. وكتابه هذا؛ سارَ فيه المصنِّفُ ﵀ على طريقة السَّلفِ في تصانيفهم ذاك الزَّمان، فَسَاقَ الأحاديثَ النبويَّةَ، والآثارَ السلفيةَ المُثبتةَ للقضاءِ والقدرِ الإلهي من غيرِ تعليقٍ أو تبويبٍ. نعم؛ قد يُشْعِرُ سياقُهُ للأحاديث والآثار أنها مرتَّبةٌ وَفْقَ تسلسُلٍ موضوعيٍّ عام، إلَاّ أنَّهُ لم يُبوِّبْ قَطُّ. ثم إنَّ الورَّاقَ أبا بكرٍ؛ محمدَ بنَ إسماعيل -وهو راوي الكتاب- قد أضافَ على أصلِ الكتاب أحاديثَ عن شيوخه، هي في جُمْلَتِها استخراجٌ ومتابعةٌ لأحاديثِ الكتاب.

Editor's introduction (reasons for re-editing; note on the manuscripts)

English · p. 6-8

It should be pointed out that this book was published, edited by Dr. 'Abd al-'Aziz b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. Muhammad al-'Uthaym, may God have mercy on him, in the year (1406) in Mecca. What moved me to re-edit and publish it, despite the earlier work of Shaykh al-'Uthaym, may God have mercy on him, were several matters, among them: 1. The Shaykh, may God have mercy on him, intervened directly in the text of the book, by alteration, substitution, addition and omission, sometimes pointing to this procedure and at other times neglecting to do so. It is not hidden that this approach is dangerous and its consequences are not praiseworthy. I will give some examples and models of his procedure. On p. (152) of his edition: al-Warraq commented on the hadith of Jarir b. Hazim, from al-A'mash, from Zayd b. Wahb, from Ibn Mas'ud, saying: “This is a gharib chain from Jarir b. Hazim, from al-A'mash.” Shaykh al-'Uthaym, may God have mercy on him, changed it to: “This is a chain that is [not gharib] from Jarir b. Hazim, from al-A'mash.” His ground was that al-Warraq's statement, in his view, contradicts the corroborations that he cited for the hadith! It is not hidden that al-Warraq intended relative gharaba, not absolute. In any case, his wording should not be altered. On p. (157): al-Warraq said: “The remaining routes of this hadith from al-A'mash come in the hadith of al-A'mash from Zayd b. Wahb.” The Shaykh, may God have mercy on him, replaced it with his words: “and the remaining routes of the hadith of Zayd b. Wahb come in the hadith of [Salama b. Kuhayl],” because his wording is more correct than al-Warraq's, as he said. On p. (137): the hadith of Ibn 'Umar: “When God ﷻ wills to create the soul (nasama), the angel of the wombs says, with it (ma'aha) …” Thus “ma'aha” occurs in the three manuscripts of Ibn Wahb's “Kitab al-Qadar,” and in the manuscript sources of Ibn al-Qayyim's “Shifa' al-'Alil,” where he transmitted this hadith with its chain and text, and in al-Mizzi's “Tahdhib al-Kamal,” where he narrated the hadith with its chain by way of Ibn Wahb's “Kitab al-Qadar.” Al-Mizzi noted that it occurred thus in the transmission, “ma'aha.” The Shaykh deleted it and put “mu'ridan” in its place, without making the slightest indication of this action. As for the additions, they are many, appended from other narrations of the hadith to complete the benefit; and there is no doubt that Ibn Wahb is not concerned with them. Among the evidence that they did not occur in the original book is that those who quoted from the “Kitab al-Qadar” in earlier times, such as Ibn al-Qayyim and others, quoted and narrated its hadiths without those additions. As for his takhrij, it indicates the breadth of the Shaykh's, may God have mercy on him, knowledge of the books of hadith and his familiarity with their routes and men, and the strength of his debating power. But each has his own method and approach. 2. I was granted success in obtaining three manuscript copies of the book, praise be to God, which I symbolized as: the base manuscript (al-Asl), (Z) and (H); their description will come later. Shaykh al-'Uthaym, may God have mercy on him, had mentioned in the introduction of his edition that he relied on two manuscripts, namely the base manuscript and (Z), but on comparison it became clear that he relied on (Z) only and neglected the base manuscript, which caused some defects in his work; I have remedied this by comparing the three copies, by the grace and favor of God. 3. His copies have been out of stock in libraries for years, and obtaining them is difficult, because it has not been republished or reprinted. Finally, I must express my abundant thanks to our brother Ayman Dhu al-Ghina for the effort he expended in reviewing the book and for his concern and care for it; may God place that in the balance of his good deeds; He is All-Hearing, Responsive. And our final prayer is that praise be to God, Lord of the worlds. Written by 'Umar b. Sulayman al-Hafyan, may God forgive him.

العربية

ومما ينبغي التنبيهُ عليه: أنَّ هذا الكتابَ قد طُبِعَ بتحقيق الدكتور: عبد العزيز عبد الرحمن محمد العُثيم ﵀ في عام (1406) في مكَّة المكرمة. والذي حَملَني على إعادةِ تحقيقه ونشره، مع تقدُّمِ ذلك من الشيخ العُثيم ﵀ أمورٌ منها: 1 - أنَّ الشيخ ﵀ فد تدخَّلَ في مَتْنِ الكتاب تدخُّلاً مباشراً؛ بالتَّعديلِ والتَّبديلِ والزِّيادةِ والنُّقصانِ؛ مشيراً أحياناً إلى هذا الصَّنيع، ومغفِلاً ذلك أُخرى. ولا يخفى أنَّ هذا النَّهج خطيرٌ غيرُ محمودِ العواقبِ. وأنا أضرِبُ بعضَ الأمثلةِ والنماذج من صَنيعه: في ص (152) من مطبوعته: عَلَّقَ الورَّاقُ على حديث جرير بنِ حازم، عن الأعمش، عن زيد بن وَهْبٍ، عن ابن مسعود بقوله: «هذا إسنادٌ غريبٌ عن جَرير بن حازم، عن الأعمش». فعدَّلها الشيخُ العُثيم ﵀ إلى: «هذا إسنادٌ [ليس غريباً] عن جرير بن حازم، عن الأعمش». ومستنده: أنَّ كلامَ الوّرَّاقِ -في نظره- مناقضٌ لما أوردَه من متابعات للحديث! ولا يخفى أنَّ الورَّاقَ إنما أراد الغرابةَ النسبيَّةَ لا المطلقة. ومهما يكن من أمرٍ؛ فلا ينبغي تعديلُ عبارته. في ص (157): قال الوَرَّاقُ: «وباقي طُرقِ هذا الحديثِ عن الأعمش، تأتي في حديث الأعمش عن زيد بن وَهْبٍ». فأبدلَها الشيخُ ﵀ بقوله: «وباقي طُرقِ حديثِ زيدِ بن وَهْب يأتي في حديث [سلمةَ بن كُهَيل]» لأن عبارته أصوبُ من عبارة الورَّاق، كما قال. في ص (137): حديث ابن عمر: «إذا أراد الله عز وجل أن يخلقَ النَّسمة قال مَلَكُ الأرحامِ معها … ». كذا ورد «معها» في المخطوطات الثلاث لـ «كتاب القدر» لابن وَهْب. وفي الأصول الخطيَّة لـ «شفاء العليل» لابن القيم -حيث نَقَلَ هذا الحديثَ بإسناده ومتنه- وفي «تهذيب الكمال» للمِزِّيِّ حيث روى الحديثَ بإسناده من طريق «كتاب القدر» لابن وَهْبٍ. ونَبَّهَ المِزِّيُّ على أنها وقعَتْ هكذا في الرِّواية «معها». فحذفها الشيخُ، ووضع مكانها «معرضاً» ولم يُشِرْ أدنى إشارة إلى هذا التصرُّف. أما الزيادات فهي كثيرة، أُلْحِقَتْ من روايات الحديث الأُخرى تتميماً للفائدة. ولا ريب أنَّ ابنَ وَهْبٍ غيرُ مَعْنِيٍّ بها. وممَّا يدلُّ على أنها لم ترد في أصل الكتاب: أن من نقل عن «كتاب القدر» قديماً كابن القيِّم وغيره نقلوا أحاديثَه وروَوها دون تلك الزيادات. أما تخريجُه؛ فهو دالٌّ على سَعةِ اطِّلاع الشيخ ﵀ على كُتُبِ الحديث، ومعرفتِهِ بطُرُقِهِ ورجاله، وقوَّةِ عارضتِهِ. لكن؛ يبقى لكلٍّ منهجُهُ وطريقتُهُ. 2 - أنني قد وُفِّقْتُ للحصولِ على ثلاث نُسَخٍ خطِّيَّةٍ للكتاب، ولله الحمد، رمزت لها بـ: الأصل و (ظ) و (هـ) وسيأتي وصفُها فيما بعد. وكان الشيخ العُثَيم ﵀ قد ذكرَ في مقدِّمة طبعتِه أنَّه اعتمدَ مخطوطتين وهما: الأصل و (ظ)، ولكن لدى المقارنة تبيَّن أنه اعتمد على (ظ) فقط وأهملَ الأصل، ممَّا أوقعَ بعضَ الخَلَل في عمَلِه، وقد تداركتُه بمقارنة النُّسَخ الثلاث بتوفيق الله ومنَّه. 3 - أنَّ نُسَخَهُ قد نَفِدَت من المكتبات منذ سنوات، وعَزَّ الحصولُ عليها؛ لعدم إعادة نَشْرِهَ وطَبْعِهِ. هذا؛ ولا يفوتني في الختام أن أتقدَّم بجزيل الشُّكر لأخينا أيمن ذو الغنى على ما بَذَله من جُهْدٍ في مراجعة الكتاب، واهتمامٍ وعناية به، فجعل الله ذلك في ميزان حسناتِه؛ إنه سميعٌ مجيبٌ. وآخرُ دعوانا أن الحمد لله ربِّ العالمين. كتبه عمر بن سليمان الحفيان غفر الله له * * *

Biography of 'Abd Allah b. Wahb: scholars' statements of criticism and commendation

English · p. 10-13

Statements of the scholars about him, in criticism and commendation: 'Abbas al-Duri and Abu Bakr b. Abi Khaythama, from Yahya b. Ma'in: trustworthy. Ahmad b. Hanbal said: 'Abd Allah b. Wahb was learned, righteous, a jurist, of abundant knowledge. Abu Zur'a said: I examined some thirty thousand hadiths from the hadith of Ibn Wahb, in Egypt and elsewhere, and I do not know that I saw a single hadith of his that has no basis. He is trustworthy. Al-Nasa'i said: Trustworthy; I do not know that he narrated any objectionable hadith from the trustworthy. Al-Khalili said: Trustworthy, agreed upon. A criticism was taken against him, that he was lax in taking [hadith]. Al-Nasa'i said: He was lax in taking [hadith]; but there is no harm in him. Al-Saji said: Truthful, trustworthy; he was among the devout, and he was lax in hearing, because the doctrine of the people of his country was that the ijaza (license) was permissible with them, and he would say regarding it: so-and-so narrated to me. Ibn 'Adi cited him in al-Kamil for this reason. Al-Dhahabi said: Ibn 'Adi was being difficult in citing him in al-Kamil. From Yahya b. Ma'in: that he heard Ibn Wahb say to Sufyan b. 'Uyayna: “O Abu Muhammad, the hadiths, that is, those that so-and-so presented to you yesterday, grant me license for them.” He said: “Yes.” Al-Dhahabi commented: This is the doctrine of the group; and even if 'Abd Allah is open to some reproach in it, Ibn 'Uyayna is his partner in it. I say: this laxity mentioned does not affect his hadith at all, because he did not include them in his compilations and narrations. Imam Ahmad said: It reached me that he did not include in his compilation anything of that. And he also said: 'Abd Allah b. Wahb is sound in hadith; he distinguishes hearing from presentation, and one hadith from another. How sound and firm is his hadith! It was said to him: Did he not take poorly? He said: He may take poorly, but when you examine his hadith, what he narrated from his shaykhs, you find it sound. He was also criticized for this: that in his narration from Ibn Jurayj there are singular reports and errors. Yahya b. Ma'in said: He is not so [strong] in Ibn Jurayj; he considered him young. Ahmad said: In Ibn Wahb's hadith from Ibn Jurayj there is something. Abu 'Awana said: He [that is, Ahmad b. Hanbal] spoke truly, because he brings from him things that others do not bring. Whatever is said, objectionable reports and errors are rare and few in the breadth of what he narrated; indeed the two authors of the Sahih [al-Bukhari and Muslim] extracted his narration from Ibn Jurayj. Ibn 'Adi said: 'Abd Allah b. Wahb is among the eminent people and among their trustworthy ones, and the hadith of the Hijaz and Egypt and the lands thereabouts revolves around the narration of Ibn Wahb, and he collected for them their musnad and their maqtu' reports …. and I do not know of any objectionable hadith of his when a trustworthy one narrates from him. Al-Dhahabi said: He abounded in his compilations in maqtu' (Successor-level) and mu'dal (twice-disconnected) reports, and he took abundantly from Ibn Sam'an and the like of him; and some of the imams have taken exception to Ibn Wahb in his taking of hadith, saying that he was lax in taking; but whether he was lax and considered it permissible, or was strict, one who narrates a hundred thousand hadiths, in whom the objectionable is rare in the breadth of what he narrated, is the ultimate in precision. Yunus b. 'Abd al-A'la said: The judgeship was offered to Ibn Wahb, so he feigned madness and kept to his house. It was also said: when he was sought to take up the judgeship of Egypt, he hid with Harmala b. Yahya for a year and some months. Khalid b. عنه: أبو الحُسين، محمدُ بن أحمد بن حسنون. قال الخطيب: كان صدوقاً، ثقةً، من أهل القرآن؛ حسن الاعتقاد. تُوفِّيَ سنة ستٍّ وخمسينَ وأربع مِئَة. «تاريخ بغداد» (1/ 356). 5 - عنه: أبو غَالب، أحمدُ بن الحَسن بن البنَّاء، البغداديُّ، الحنبليُّ. قال الذهبيُّ: الشيخُ، الصالح، الثقة. تُوفِّيَ سنة سبعٍ وعشرينَ وخمس مِئَة. «السِّير» (19/ 603). 6 - عنه: أبو حَفص، عُمر بن محمَّد بن مُعَمَّر بن طَبَرْزَذ، الدَّارَقَرِّيُّ. قال ابنُ نُقطة: هو مكثرٌ، صحيحُ السَّماعِ، ثقةٌ في الحديث. قال ابنُ النَّجار: كان متهاوناً بأمور الدِّين، وكُنَّا نسمعُ منه يوماً أجمع؛ فنُصَلِّي، ولا يُصلِّي معنا، ولا يقومُ لصلاةٍ، وكان يطلبُ الأجْرَ على روايةِ الحديث. قال الذَّهبيُّ: فمعَ ما أبدَينا من ضعفه، قد تكاثرَ عليه الطَّلبةُ، وانتشر حديثُهُ في الآفاق، وفَرِحَ الحُّفَّاظُ بعَواليه، ثم في الزَّمن الثاني تزاحموا على أصحابه، وحمَلوا عنهم الكثيرَ، وأحسنوا به الظَّنَّ، والله الموعد، ووثَّقه ابنُ نقطة. تُوفِّيَ سنة سبع وست مِئَة. ملاحظة: الطَّبَرْزَذ: هو السُّكَّر. والدَّارَقَزِّيُّ: نسبة إلى دار القَزِّ، محلَّة كبيرة كانت ببغداد. «مختصر تاريخ ابن الدُّبيثي» (5/ 286)، «ذيل تاريخ بغداد» لابن النَّجار ص (118)، «السِّير» للذهبي (21/ 507)، «اللسان» لابن حجر (5/ 234). 7 - عنه: شهابُ الدين، أبو الفضل، عبدُ الرحيم بن يُوسف بن يَحيى، المَوْصِلِيُّ، ابنُ خطيب المِزَّة، الدِّمشقيُّ، المُسْنِدُ. كان ثقةً، فاضلاً، دَيِّناً. «الوافي بالوَفَيات» (18/ 399)، «شذرات الذهب» (5/ 401). 8 - عنه: شمسُ الدين، أبو عبد الله، محمَّد بن أحمد بن إبراهيم، بن حَيدرة، المعروف بابن القمَّاح، الإمام، الثقة، الفقيه، كان آيةً في حفظ القرآن وفي الذَّكاء. تُوفِّي سنة إحدى وأربعين وسبع مئة. «الوافي بالوَفَيات» (2/ 150)، «الدُّرر الكامنة» (3/ 303)، «شذرات الذهب» (6/ 131). * * *

Description of the manuscripts: the Escorial copy (the base manuscript)

English · p. 20

Description of the manuscripts relied upon in the edition. 1. The Escorial copy (the base manuscript): It is an old, precious copy, part of a collection that includes a number of books of hadith and rijal, preserved in the monastery of the Escorial in Spain, with a paper photocopy of it in the library of the Meccan Sanctuary. Number of its leaves: (6) folios, equivalent to (11) pages. The number of lines in each page ranges between 27 and 28 lines. Its script is poor and mostly undotted. It has been collated and corrected, and bears a number of audition certificates. Its copyist: Ahmad b. 'Uthman b. Muhammad b. Ibrahim b. 'Abd Allah, Abu al-Fath, of Kirmani origin, from Cairo, Hanafi, hadith scholar, known as al-Kulutati. He read the great books repeatedly, to the point that he read al-Bukhari more than sixty times, and his shaykhs in it were about as many; he wrote with his own hand a number of the works of the shaykhs, then of the works of his peers such as al-Wali al-'Iraqi, then Ibn Hajar, and his handwriting is poor. He was religious, good, of abundant worship, with the radiance of hadith on his face. He died in the year eight hundred and thirty-five in Cairo. al-Daw' al-Lami' (1/378).

Description of the manuscripts: the Zahiriyya copy (Z)

English · p. 21

2. The Zahiriyya copy: Number of its leaves: (6) folios, equivalent to (11) pages. Its script: Persian, very well executed and dotted. Its copyist collated and corrected it, supplied omissions and corrected errors in its margin. Description of the manuscripts: the Indian copy (H)

English · p. 21-22

3. The Indian copy: Preserved in the Nadwat al-'Ulama' in Lucknow, India, under number (56), with a photograph of it in the Islamic University of Medina under number (27) hadith. Its script: ruq'a, approaching naskh. Its copyist: Sayyid Kalim Ahmad, dated 10 December 1930 CE. It was collated with the original from which it was copied and corrected by 'Izz al-Din al-Falwari, teacher of Arabic language at the Nadwat al-'Ulama' in Lucknow. The corrector said of the original from which it was copied: it was full of confused passages and verbal omissions. So now, by the grace of God, it has come out in the best form that a person can achieve in precision and in its pleasing, admirable arrangement. I say: despite that, it remains full of misreadings (tashifat). It seems to me that it was copied from one of the branches of the Escorial copy. And God knows best. * * *

Glossary (editor's appendix of rare words)

Glossary
حَجَّه
Hajjahu: he overcame him by the argument (hujja), that is, by the proof. Lisan al-'Arab (h-j-j).
غَلَبَهُ بالحُجَّة؛ أي البُرهان. «لسان العرب» (حجج).
النَّسَمَةُ
The human being, the self and the spirit. Every creature that has a spirit within it is a nasama. Lisan al-'Arab (n-s-m).
الإنسانُ والنَّفسُ والرُّوحُ. وكلُّ دابَّةٍ في جَوفها رُوحٌ فهي نسَمَةٌ. «لسان العرب» (نسم).
الوَبيصُ
Brilliance. Wabasa al-shay'u yabisu wasban wa-wabisan wa-bisatan: the thing gleamed and shone. Gharib al-Hadith by Abu 'Ubayd 4/333, and al-Lisan (w-b-s).
البَريقُ، وَبَصَ الشَّيءُ يَبِصُ وَبْصاً ووَبِيصاً وبِصَةً: بَرَقَ ولَمَعَ. «غريب الحديث» لأبي عبيد 4/ 333، و «اللسان» (وبص).
أُجْمِلَ
Ibn Manzur said: that is, they were counted and gathered, so nothing is added to them or subtracted. Lisan al-'Arab (j-m-l).
قال ابن منظور: أي: أُحصُوا وجُمِعوا فلا يُزادُ فيهم ولا يُنقَص. «اللسان» (جمل).
العَزْلُ
Coitus interruptus ('azl) is when a man withdraws his semen from his slave woman when he has intercourse with her, so that she does not conceive. Lisan al-'Arab ('-z-l). Al-Fayyumi said: 'azala al-mujami': when he nears ejaculation, withdraws, and ejaculates outside the vagina. Al-Misbah al-Munir ('-z-l).
عَزْلُ الرَّجل الماءَ عن جاريته إذا جامَعَها لئلَاّ تحمِل. «اللسان» (عزل). قال الفَيُّومي: عَزَلَ المُجامِعُ: إذا قاربَ الإنزالَ فنَزَعَ وأمْنَى خارجَ الفَرْج. «المصباح المنير» (عزل).
العَنَتُ
Corruption, sin, immorality and fornication. Lisan al-'Arab ('-n-t).
الفَساد والإثم والفُجور والزِّنا. «اللسان» (عنت).
المِزْوَد
The provision-bag: a container in which provisions are placed. Lisan al-'Arab (z-w-d).
وِعاءٌ يُجعَلُ فيه الزَّاد. «اللسان» (زود)
النَّغَفُ
Al-Asma'i said: it is the worm that occurs in the noses of camels and sheep. He said: it is also the white worm that occurs in date-stones when they are soaked. The singular is naghafa. He said: any worm other than that is not a naghaf. Gharib al-Hadith 4/203.
قال الأصمعيُّ: هو الدُّود الذي يكونُ في أنوف الإبل والغَنَم. قال: وهو أيضاً الدُّود الأبيض الذي يكونُ في النَّوى إذا أُنقِع. الواحدة: نَغَفَة. قال: وما سِوى ذلك من الدُّود ليس بنَغَف. «غريب الحديث» 4/ 203.
نأتَنِفُه
We take its beginning and start it. Lisan al-'Arab (a-n-f).
نأتَنِفُه: نأخذُ أوَّلَه ونَبتَدِئُه. «اللسان» (أنف).
أدحَضَ حُجَّتَهُ
He refuted his argument: he invalidated it. Lisan al-'Arab (d-h-d).
أبطَلَها. «اللسان» (دحض).
رَدِفْتُ
One says: radiftu al-rajul: when you ride behind him. Lisan al-'Arab (r-d-f).
يقال: رَدِفْتُ الرَّجلَ: إذا ركِبتَ خلفَهُ. «اللسان» (ردف).
جَهَدَتِ
Al-Fayyumi said: jahada fi al-amr jahdan (of the bab of nafa'a): when he sought until he reached the utmost in seeking. Al-Misbah al-Munir (j-h-d). That is: he strove in the matter and was extreme in seeking it. See al-Lisan (j-h-d).
قال الفيُّومي: جَهَدَ في الأمرِ جَهداً من باب (نَفَع): إذا طلبَ حتَّى بلغَ غايتَهُ في الطَّلَب. «المصباح المنير» (جهد). أي: جَدَّ في الأمر، وبالغَ في طلَبه. انظر «اللسان» (جهد).
النَّكبَةُ
A calamity among the calamities of fate. Lisan al-'Arab (n-k-b).
النَّكبَةُ: المُصيبَةُ من مَصائِب الدَّهْر. «اللسان» (نكب).
اخْتَلَجَها
He pulled it and snatched it away. Lisan al-'Arab (kh-l-j).
جَذَبها وانْتَزَعَها. «اللسان» (خلج).
السِّقْطُ
The child that falls from its mother's belly before it is complete; male and female are alike in it. (Al-siqt: with three possible vowels on the sin and a sukun on the qaf; the kasra on the sin is more frequent.) Lisan al-'Arab (s-q-t).
الولدُ الذي يسقُطُ من بطنِ أمِّه قبلَ تمامِه، الذَّكرُ والأُنثى فيه سَواء. (السِّقْط: بتثليث السين وسكون القاف، والكسر في سينها أكثر). «اللسان» (سقط).
