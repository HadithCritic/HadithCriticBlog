Opening of the copy: transmission chain · p. 31

In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. May God bless our master Muhammad, his family and his Companions, and grant them abundant peace. The distinguished shaykh, the musnid, Shihab al-Din Abu al-Fadl 'Abd al-Rahim b. Yusuf b. Yahya al-Dimashqi informed us by permission, saying: Abu Hafs 'Umar b. Muhammad b. Mu'ammar b. Tabarzad al-Daraqazzi informed us, by reading to him while we listened; [he said:] Abu Ghalib Ahmad b. al-Hasan b. Ahmad b. al-Banna, the jurist, informed us; Abu [al-]Husayn Muhammad b. Ahmad b. Muhammad b. Hasnun al-Qurashi informed us, by reading to him in the year four hundred and fifty-five; he said: It was read to Abu Bakr Muhammad b. Isma'il b. al-'Abbas b. Muhammad al-Warraq, while I was listening, in Jumada al-Ula of the year three hundred and seventy-two. Part One.

العربية

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم صلَّى الله على سيِّدنا محمَّدٍ وآلِهِ وصحبِهِ وسَلَّم تسليماً كثيراً. أخبرنا الشَّيخُ الأجلُّ المُسْنِدُ، شهابُ الدِّين، أبو الفَضل، عبدُ الرحيم بنُ يوسف بنِ يحيى الدِّمشقيُّ إذْناً، قال: أنا أبو حفصٍ؛ عُمرُ بنُ محمَّد بنِ مُعَمَّر بن طَبَرْزَذ؛ الدَّارَقَزِّيُّ، قِراءةً عليه ونحن نسمع، أنا أبو غالبٍ، أحمدُ بنُ الحَسَنِ بنَ أحمد بنِ البنَّاء، الفقيه، أبنا [أبو] الحُسين؛ محمَّدُ بنُ أحمد بنِ محمَّد بنِ حسنون، القُرشيُّ، قراءةً عليه في سنة خمسٍ وخمسينَ وأربعِ مئة، قال: قُرِئَ على أبي بكرٍ؛ محمَّد بنِ إسماعيل بنِ العباس بنِ محمَّد، الورَّاقِ، وأنا أسمع في جُمادى الأُولى من سنة اثنينِ وسبعينَ وثلاث مِئَة. الجزء الأول

Entry1/ 27
Kitāb al-Qadar of Ibn Wahb · p. 31-32
English
Isnad

Abu Bakr 'Abd Allah b. Sulayman b. al-Ash'ath b. Ishaq al-Sijistani narrated to us, reading from the original of his book, in the year 311 [AH]; Abu Ja'far Ahmad b. Sa'id b. Bishr al-Hamdani al-Misri narrated to us; 'Abd Allah b. Wahb informed us; Anas b. 'Iyad told us, from al-Harith b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. Abi Dhubab, from Yazid b. Hurmuz, who said: I heard Abu Hurayra ﵁ say: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Adam and Musa ﵇ disputed before their Lord ﷻ, and Adam bested Musa. Musa said: 'You are Adam, whom God created with His own hand, into whom He breathed of His spirit, before whom He made His angels prostrate, and whom He settled in His Garden; then you brought people down to the earth because of your sin?' Adam said: 'You are Musa, whom God chose for His message and His speech, to whom He gave the Tablets in which is the clarification of everything, and whom He brought near in confidential converse. By how many years did you find that God ﷻ had written the Torah before He created me?' Musa said: 'By forty years.' Adam said: 'Did you find in it: {And Adam disobeyed his Lord and went astray}?' He said: 'Yes.' He said: 'Then do you blame me for doing a deed that God had written against me forty years before He created me?' The Messenger of God ﷺ said: So Adam bested Musa.”

العربية
Isnad

حَدَّثَنا أبو بكر؛ عبدُ الله بن سُليمان بن الأشْعَث بن إسحاق السِّجِسْتاني؛ قراءةً من أصل كتابه، سنة إحدى عشرة وثلاث مِئَة، حَدَّثَنا أبو جعفر، أحمدُ بن سعيد بن بِشْر الهَمْدانيُّ المِصريُّ، أبنا عبدُ الله بن وَهْب، أنا أنسُ بن عياض، عن الحارث بن عبدِ الرحمن بن أبي ذُباب، عن يزيدَ بن هُرمز، قال: سمعتُ أبا هُريرةَ ﵁ يقول: قالَ رسول الله ﷺ:

Matn

«احْتَجَّ آدمُ ومُوسَى ﵇ عند ربِّهِما عز وجل، فحَجَّ آدمُ مُوسَى، فقال مُوسَى: أنت آدمُ الذي خَلقَكَ اللهُ بيدهِ، ونفَخَ فيكَ من رُوحِهِ، وأسْجَدَ لك ملائِكَتَهُ، وأسْكَنَكَ في جَنَّتِهِ، ثم أهْبَطْتَ النَّاسَ بخَطيئَتِكَ إلى الأرضِ؟ قال آدمُ: أنت مُوسَى الذي اصْطَفاكَ اللهُ برسالَتِهِ وبكَلامِهِ، وأعطاكَ الألواحَ، فيها تِبْيانُ كُلِّ شيءٍ، وقَرَّبكَ نَجيًّا، فَبِكَمْ وجَدتَ اللهَ عز وجل كتبَ التَّوراةَ مِنْ قَبلِ أنْ يَخْلُقَني؟ قال مُوسى: بأربعينَ عاماً. قال آدمُ: فهل وجدتَ فيها {وعَصَى آدَمُ رَبَّهُ فَغَوى}؟ قالَ: نعم. قال: أفَتَلُومُني على أنْ عَمِلتُ عَمَلاً كَتَبَهُ اللهُ عَلَيَّ قبلَ أنْ يَخْلُقَني بأربعينَ سنةً؟ قالَ رسولُ الله ﷺ: فحَجَّ آدمُ مُوسَى».

Matn

“God ﷻ created Adam and wiped his back with His right hand, and brought forth from it a progeny. He said: 'I created these for the Garden, and they will do the deeds of the people of the Garden.' Then He wiped his back and brought forth from it a progeny, and said: 'I created these for the Fire, and they will do the deeds of the people of the Fire.'” A man said: “O Messenger of God, then what is the use of action?” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “When God ﷻ creates a servant for the Garden, He employs him in the deeds of the people of the Garden until he dies upon one of the deeds of the people of the Garden, and so enters the Garden by it. And when He creates a servant for the Fire, He employs him in the deeds of the people of the Fire until he dies upon one of the deeds of the people of the Fire, and so enters the Fire by it.”

English
Isnad

'Abd Allah b. Sulayman narrated to us; he said: al-Hamdani narrated to us; he said: Ibn Wahb informed us; he said: Ibn Lahi'a, al-Layth b. Sa'd, and 'Amr b. al-Harith informed us, from Abu Qabil al-Ma'afiri, from Shufayy al-Asbahi, from a man among the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ came out to us with two books in his hand, and said: “Do you know what this is?” We said: “No, unless you tell us, O Messenger of God.” He said: “This is a book from the Lord of the Worlds; in it are the names of the people of the Garden and the names of their fathers and their tribes. It has been totalled up to the last of them, so nothing will ever be added to them or subtracted from them. And this is the book of the people of the Fire, with their names and the names of their fathers and their tribes, totalled up to the last of them, so nothing will ever be added to them or subtracted from them.” The Companions of the Prophet ﷺ said: “Then what is the use of action, if the matter has been concluded?” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Aim for what is right and stay close to it, for the man of the Garden is sealed with the deeds of the people of the Garden, whatever deed he may have done, and the man of the Fire is sealed with the Fire, whatever deed he may have done. Your Lord ﷻ has finished with the Book.” Then the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Your Lord has finished with creation: {a party in the Garden and a party in the Blaze}” [al-Shura 42:7]. And the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Deeds are by their endings.”
