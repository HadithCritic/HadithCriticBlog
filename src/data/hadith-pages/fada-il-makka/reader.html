01

A letter of counsel

On remaining within the Sanctuary

ENGLISH

Al-Ḥasan b. Abī l-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī ؒ, wrote to a man among the ascetics called ʿAbd al-Raḥīm or ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Anas al-Ramādī, who used to live in Mecca, may God Most High ennoble it. He was a man of excellence, religion and remembrance of God, who had no occupation in this world except the worship of God Most High. He wished to leave Mecca for Yemen, and word of that reached al-Ḥasan, who was his brother in God Most High. So he wrote me a letter [sic: i.e., to him] urging him to stay in Mecca, may God increase it in honor. It began:

In the name of God, Most Gracious, Most Merciful. May God protect you, my brother, with that by which He protects the people of faith; may He shield you from what is disliked, grant you success in good deeds, and complete His favors upon you in all matters; and may He gather us and you in the Abode of Peace, in the neighborhood of the Merciful, for that is in His hand; and there is no power and no strength save in God, the Exalted, the Mighty.

To proceed: my brother, I have written to you, and I and those around me, my loved ones, relatives and brothers, are in the best state you could wish; and our Lord is the One to be praised. May God bless our master Muḥammad ﷺ, his family and his companions, and grant them peace.

Know, my brother, may God Most High keep you, that it has reached me that you have made up your mind to leave God's Sanctuary and His security and to move from it to Yemen. By God, I disliked that and it grieved me, and I felt a deep unease at it, since Satan wishes to unsettle you from the Sanctuary of God Most High and to make you slip. How astonishing is your reason, that you should intend this in yourself after God has made you one of its people! Had you praised God Most High for what He has bestowed on you and the trial He has tried you with in His Sanctuary and security, and made you one of its people, you would be obliged to thank Him always as long as you live, and you would be occupied with the worship of God, Mighty and Majestic, many times over what you were before, since He has made you one of the people of His Sanctuary and security, and a neighbor of His House. Beware, and again beware, of restlessness and vexation; hold fast to patience, silence and forbearance, for by them you overcome Satan the accursed. Beware, my brother, and again beware, of leaving it and being driven out of it, for you are in the best land, the one most beloved to God Most High, the most excellent, the greatest in worth and the noblest in His sight. We ask God Most High to grant us and you success in good works, for He is the Compassionate, the Bestower; and there is no power and no strength save in God, the Exalted, the Mighty.

كتب الْحسن بن أبي الْحسن الْبَصْرِيّ رمحة الله عَلَيْهِ الى رجل من الزهاد يُقَال لَهُ عبد الرَّحِيم أَو عبد الرَّحْمَن بن أنس الرَّمَادِي كَانَ يسكن مَكَّة شرفها الله تَعَالَى وَكَانَ لَهُ فضل وَدين وَذكر وَلم يكن لَهُ فِي الدُّنْيَا عمل الا عبَادَة الله تَعَالَى وانه أَرَادَ الْخُرُوج من مَكَّة الى الْيمن فَبلغ ذَلِك الْحسن وَكَانَ بواخيه فِي الله تَعَالَى فَكتب الهي كتابا يرغبه فِي الْمقَام بِمَكَّة زَادهَا الله شرفا أَوله بِسم الله الرَّحْمَن الرَّحِيم حفظك الله يَا أخي بِمَا حفظ بِهِ أهل الايمان ووقاك الْمَكْرُوه ووفقك لِلْخَيْرَاتِ وَأتم عَلَيْك النعم فِي كل الْأُمُور وجمعنا وَإِيَّاك فِي دَار السَّلَام فِي جوَار الرَّحْمَن فان ذَلِك بِيَدِهِ وَلَا حول وَلَا قُوَّة الا بِاللَّه الْعلي الْعَظِيم أما بعد يَا أخي فَانِي قد كتبت اليك وَأَنا وَمن قبلي من أهل الْعِنَايَة والأقارب والاخوان على أفضل مَا تحب وربنا الْمَحْمُود وَصلى الله على سيدنَا مُحَمَّد وعَلى آله وَصَحبه وَسلم اعْلَم يَا أخي أبقاك الله تَعَالَى انه بَلغنِي انك قد أَجمعت رَأْيك على الْخُرُوج من حرم الله تَعَالَى وأمنه والتحول مِنْهُ الى الْيمن وَأَنِّي وَالله كرهت ذَلِك وغمني وَاسْتَوْحَشْت من ذَلِك وَحْشَة شَدِيدَة إِذْ أَرَادَ الشَّيْطَان أَن يزعجك من حرم الله تَعَالَى ويستزلك فيا عجبا من عقلك اذ نَوَيْت ذَلِك فِي نَفسك بعد أَن جعلك الله من أَهله وَلَو أَنَّك حمدت الله تَعَالَى على مَا أولاك وأبلاك فِي حرمه وأمنه وصيرك من أَهله لَكَانَ الْوَاجِب عَلَيْك شكره أبدا مَا دمت حَيا ولكنت مَشْغُولًا بِعبَادة الله عز وَجل أَضْعَاف مَا كنت عَلَيْهِ إِذْ جعلك من أهل حرمه وأمنه وجيران بَيته وَإِيَّاك ثمَّ إياك والقلق والضجر وَعَلَيْك بِالصبرِ والصمت والحلم فانك تغلب بِهن الشَّيْطَان الرَّجِيم وَإِيَّاك ثمَّ إياك يَا أخي وَالْخُرُوج مِنْهَا والانزعاج عَنْهَا فانك فِي خير أَرض وَأحب أَرض الله تَعَالَى اليه وأفضلها وَأَعْظَمهَا قدرا وَأَشْرَفهَا عِنْده فنسأل الله تَعَالَى أَن يوفقنا وَإِيَّاك لِلْخَيْرَاتِ فانه الحنان المنان وَلَا حول وَلَا قُوَّة الا بِاللَّه الْعلي الْعَظِيم

03

The virtues of Mecca

The city, its inhabitants, and its sacred history

ENGLISH

Then, my brother, I shall go on to give you what has come from the Prophet ﷺ in reports on the virtues of Mecca, the virtues of its people, and of whoever lives beside it.

Know, my brother, that the Messenger of God ﷺ, when he left Mecca, stopped at al-Ḥazwara, faced the Kaʿba, and said: "By God, I know that you are the most beloved of God's lands to me, and that you are the most beloved of God's earth to God, Mighty and Majestic, and that you are the best spot on the face of the earth and the most beloved of it to God Most High; and had your people not driven me out of you, I would not have left." And he ﷺ said in another hadith: "The best land on the face of the earth, and the most beloved of it to God Most High, is Mecca." The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "The earth was spread out from Mecca; God Most High stretched it from beneath it, so it was called Umm al-Qurā [Mother of Towns]. And he ﷺ said: "A prayer in this mosque of mine is better than a thousand prayers in any other, except the Sacred Mosque, for a prayer in it is equivalent to a hundred thousand prayers elsewhere; and a prayer in the Farthest Mosque is five hundred prayers."

Places of devotion

The Kaʿba, prayer, supplication, and Zamzam

ENGLISH

"And there is no spot on the face of the earth upon which one hundred and twenty mercies descend each day from God Most High, sixty for those circumambulating, forty for those praying, and twenty for those looking at the Kaʿba, except Mecca. And looking at the Kaʿba is worship." The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Whoever looks at the House of God out of faith, seeking reward and in affirmation, his earlier and later sins are forgiven, and he will be gathered on the Day of Resurrection among the secure. And God Most High will gather its people on the Day of Resurrection secure. And there is no land on the face of the earth to which all the gates of Paradise are open "except Mecca. The gates of Paradise are eight, all of them open to it at Mecca until the Day of Resurrection: one gate for the Kaʿba, one beneath the spout, one at the Yemeni Corner, one at the Black Corner, one behind the Station, one at Zamzam, one on al-Ṣafā, and one on al-Marwa. No one enters the Kaʿba except by the mercy of God, and no one leaves it except with the forgiveness of God, Mighty and Majestic.

He Most High said: and whoever enters it is secure [3:97], that is, from the Fire.

"And there is no land on the face of the earth in which supplication is answered in fifteen places except Mecca. First is the interior of the Kaʿba: supplication in it is answered. Supplication at the Black Stone is answered; supplication at the Yemeni Corner is answered; supplication in al-Ḥijr is answered; supplication behind the Station is answered; supplication at "al-Multazam is answered; supplication at the door of the well of Zamzam is answered; supplication on al-Ṣafā and al-Marwa is answered; supplication between al-Ṣafā and al-Marwa is answered; supplication between the Corner and the Station is answered; supplication at Minā is answered; supplication [text as printed] is answered; supplication at ʿArafāt is answered; supplication at the Sacred Monument is answered. These, my brother, are fifteen places, so seize the opportunity to supplicate in them; for they are the places in which supplication is not rejected, and they are the great holy sites in which forgiveness is hoped for. So strive, my brother, in supplication at these great holy sites; and if you leave the Sanctuary of God Most High and His security, the blessing of these holy sites will depart from you."

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "The best of places, "the purest, the most wholesome and the nearest to God Most High, is what lies between the Corner and the Station." And the Messenger of God ﷺ said: "What lies between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Corner is a garden from the gardens of Paradise." And he ﷺ said: "No one supplicates at the Black Corner except that he is answered, and likewise at the Yemeni Corner."

And know, my brother, that no one leaves it except that he regrets.

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Dwelling in Mecca is happiness and leaving it is misery." \ ٢ \ So stay put in your place, and beware of restlessness and vexation, for that is from the work of Satan; do not depart. If you earn in it a gain worth two fils from what is lawful, that is better and more excellent than earning two thousand dirhams elsewhere.

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Whoever dies as a pilgrim or one performing the lesser pilgrimage "will not be presented [for judgment] nor brought to account, and it will be said to him: Enter Paradise in peace with the secure." And the Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Whoever fasts the month of Ramadan in Mecca, God Most High writes for him a hundred thousand months [of fasting] elsewhere; and a prayer in the Sacred Mosque is a hundred thousand prayers, and if he prays it in congregation it is a million prayers. Whoever is ill in Mecca for a single day, God Most High forbids his body and flesh to the Fire." He ﷺ said: "Whoever is ill in Mecca for a day, God writes for him the righteous deeds that he used to do elsewhere, the worship of sixty years. And whoever endures the heat of Mecca for an hour of the day, God Most High removes him from the Fire the distance of five hundred years' travel and brings him near to Paradise "the distance of two hundred years' travel. Mecca and Medina expel their impurity as the bellows expels the impurity of iron. Mecca was founded upon hardships and [steep] ascents, and whoever endures its severity, I shall be an intercessor for him, or a witness, on the Day of Resurrection. Whoever dies in Mecca or Medina, God will raise him on the Day of Resurrection secure from His punishment, with no reckoning, no fear and no torment upon him; he will enter Paradise in peace, and I shall be an intercessor for him on the Day of Resurrection. The people of Mecca are the people of God Most High and the neighbors of His House. And there is no land on the face of the earth in which is the drink of the pious and the place of prayer of the best, except Mecca."

Ibn ʿAbbās ﵄, was asked: "What is the place of prayer of the best?" He said: "Beneath the spout." It was said to him: "What is the drink of the pious?" He said: "The water of Zamzam." "The best valley on the face of the earth is the valley of Abraham ﷺ, and the best well on the face of the earth is the well of Zamzam. And there is no land in which there is something such that if a person touches it he comes out of his sins as on the day his mother bore him, except Mecca; for whoever touches the Black Stone comes out of his sins as on the day his mother bore him. And there is no land on the face of the earth in which a person prays where God commanded His Prophet ﷺ, except Mecca, for God Most High said: And take the Station of Abraham as a place of prayer [2:125]. And whoever prays behind the Station is secure."

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: [...] "his earlier and later sins [are forgiven]; and whoever prays two rakʿas beneath the spout comes out of his sins as on the day his mother bore him; and whoever prays two rakʿas around the Kaʿba comes out of his sins as on the day his mother bore him. The most beloved of places to God Most High is what lies between the Station and al-Multazam. Looking at the Kaʿba is worship and a safeguard from hypocrisy. And there is no spot on the face of the earth in which circumambulation, pilgrimage and the lesser pilgrimage are found except Mecca. Looking into the well of Zamzam is worship. The one who circumambulates the House is like one circumambulating the Throne of the Merciful. The Black Stone is the hand of God Most High on His earth, with which He shakes hands with whom He wills of His servants. The Black Stone and the Station will come on the Day of Resurrection, each of them like Mount Abū Qubays; together they have two eyes, two tongues and two lips, bearing witness for everyone who met them in fulfilment [of his duty]."

وَمن صَبر على حر مَكَّة سَاعَة من نَهَار أبعده اللَّهِ تَعَالَى من النَّار مسيرَة خَمْسمِائَة عَام، وقربه من الْجنَّة مسيرَة مِائَتي عَام، وَإِن مَكَّة وَالْمَدينَة لينفيان خبثهما كَمَا يَنْفِي الْكِير خبث الْحَدِيد، أَلا وَأَن مَكَّة أنشئت على المكروهات والدرجات، وَمن صَبر على شدتها كنت لَهُ شَفِيعًا أَو شَهِيدا يَوْم الْقِيَامَة، وَمن مَاتَ بِمَكَّة أَو بِالْمَدِينَةِ بَعثه اللَّهِ يَوْم الْقِيَامَة آمنا من عَذَابه، لَا حِسَاب عَلَيْهِ، وَلَا خوف وَلَا عَذَاب، وَيدخل الْجنَّة بِسَلام، وَكنت لَهُ شَفِيعًا يَوْم الْقِيَامَة. أَلا إِن أهل مَكَّة هم أهل اللَّهِ تَعَالَى وجيران بَيته، وَمَا على وَجه الأَرْض بَلْدَة فِيهَا شراب الْأَبْرَار، ومصلى الأخيار إِلَّا بِمَكَّة. قيل لِابْنِ عَبَّاس رَضِي اللَّهِ عَنْهُمَا مَا مصلى الأخيار؟ قَالَ: تَحت الْمِيزَاب. فَقيل لَهُ: مَا شرب الْأَبْرَار؟ قَالَ: مَاء زَمْزَم. وَخير وَاد على وَجه الأَرْض وَادي إِبْرَاهِيم - ﷺ وَخير بِئْر على وَجه الأَرْض بِئْر زَمْزَم. وَمَا بَلْدَة يُوجد فِيهَا شَيْء إِذا مَسّه الْإِنْسَان خرج من ذنُوبه كَيَوْم وَلدته أمه إِلَّا بِمَكَّة، فَإِن من مس الْحجر الْأسود خرج من ذنُوبه كَيَوْم وَلدته أمه. وَمَا على وَجه الأَرْض بَلْدَة يُصَلِّي فِيهَا أحد حَيْثُ أَمر اللَّهِ نبيه إِلَّا بِمَكَّة فَإِنَّهُ قَالَ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى وَاتَّخذُوا من مقَام إِبْرَاهِيم مصلى.

وَمن صلى خلف الْمقَام كَانَ آمنا، قَالَ رَسُول اله - ﷺ تقدم من ذَنبه وَمَا تَأَخّر، وَمن صلى تَحت الْمِيزَاب رَكْعَتَيْنِ خرج من ذنُوبه كَيَوْم وَلدته أمه، وَمن صلى حول الْكَعْبَة رَكْعَتَيْنِ خرج من ذنُوبه كَيَوْم وَلدته أمه. وَأحب الْبِقَاع إِلَى اللَّهِ تَعَالَى مَا بَين الْمقَام والملتزم. وَالنَّظَر إِلَى الْكَعْبَة عبَادَة وأمان من النِّفَاق. وَمَا على وَجه الأَرْض بقْعَة يُوجد فِيهَا الطّواف وَالْحج وَالْعمْرَة إِلَّا بِمَكَّة، وَالنَّظَر فِي بِئْر زَمْزَم عبَادَة، والطائف حول الْبَيْت كالطائف حول عرش الرَّحْمَن، وَالْحجر الْأسود يَد اللَّهِ تَعَالَى فِي أرضه يُصَافح فِيهَا من يَشَاء من عباده، وَالْحجر الْأسود وَالْمقَام يأتيان يَوْم الْقِيَامَة كل وَاحِد مِنْهُمَا مثل جبل أبي قبيس، لَهما عينان ولسانان وشفتان يَشْهَدَانِ لكل من وافاهما بِالْوَفَاءِ.

Circumambulation & pilgrimage

Rewards and accounts of worship at the House

ENGLISH

It is narrated from the Prophet ﷺ that he said: "The most honored of the angels in God's sight are those who circumambulate His House. Whoever looks at the House with a single glance, and he has sins like the foam of the sea, God forgives them all for him."

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "God, Mighty and Majestic, has a tablet of red ruby into which God looks three hundred and sixty times every day: one hundred and eighty glances of mercy and one hundred and eighty glances of punishment. And the first of those on whom God Most High looks with mercy are the people of His Sanctuary; whoever He sees standing in prayer, He forgives him. The angels say, and He knows that better: 'Our Lord, none remain but those asleep.' So He, Blessed and Most High, says: 'And those asleep around My House, include them.'"

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Whoever circumambulates the House seven times, God raises him for each step by seventy thousand degrees, "gives him seventy thousand good deeds, and gives him seventy thousand intercessions for whomever he wishes of the Muslims of his household, whether He hastens it for him in this world, or stores it for him in the next."

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Whoever circumambulates the House of God seven times on a summer day of intense heat, bareheaded, touching the Stone at every circuit without harming anyone, and speaking little except the remembrance of God Most High, will have for every step he lifts or sets down seventy thousand good deeds; seventy thousand misdeeds will be erased from him, and seventy thousand degrees will be raised for him. And the superiority of the pilgrim who walks over the pilgrim who rides is like the superiority of the moon "on the night of the full moon over the rest of the stars."

And he ﷺ said: "If the angels were to shake hands with anyone, they would shake hands with the fighter in the way of God, the one dutiful to his parents, and the one who circumambulates the House of God." And he ﷺ said: "The Kaʿba is surrounded by seventy thousand angels who ask forgiveness for the one who circumambulates and pray for him." And he ﷺ said: "The one circumambulating wades in the mercy of God, and God boasts of those circumambulating the House before the angels." And he ﷺ said: "Take your fill of this circumambulation before it is cut off from between you and it; as though I see a man from Abyssinia, bald-headed [aṣlaʿ], with bowed feet [afīdaʿ], with broad flat feet [aṣfaḥ afīḥaj], sitting upon it, demolishing it "stone by stone."

And he ﷺ said: "The pilgrims and those performing the lesser pilgrimage are the delegation of God Most High: if they ask Him, He gives them; if they call upon Him, He answers them; and if they spend, He replaces it for them, for every dirham seven hundred thousand dirhams" (in one version: "a million and seven hundred thousand dirhams"). "By Him in whose hand is my soul, no one who says the tahlīl [lā ilāha illā Llāh] or takbīr [Allāhu akbar] does so, except that everything says the tahlīl with his tahlīl and the takbīr with his takbīr, even to the edge of the dust." [A man said:] "Messenger of God ﷺ, and to this multiplication?" He said: "By Him in whose hand is my soul, as for their spending, God will replace for them the seven hundred thousand before they leave this world; and as for the million, it is stored for them in the next world. By Him in whose hand is my soul, a single dirham is heavier than "this mountain of yours," and he pointed to Abū Qubays.

And he ﷺ said: "Whoever is able to die in one of the two Sanctuaries, let him die there, for I will be the first to intercede for him, and on the Day of Resurrection he will be secure from the punishment of God Most High, with no reckoning and no punishment upon him." And he ﷺ said: "The lesser pilgrimage to the lesser pilgrimage is an expiation for what lies between them; and the accepted pilgrimage has no reward but Paradise." And the Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Whoever performs the pilgrimage and does not commit sexual relations or wrongdoing comes out of his sins as on the day his mother bore him. And no man bequeaths [that a pilgrimage be made] except that three pilgrimages are recorded for him: a pilgrimage for the one who wrote [the will], a pilgrimage for the one who bequeathed it, and a pilgrimage for the one who assumed iḥrām for it "on his behalf. Whoever performs the pilgrimage on behalf of his parents, two pilgrimages are recorded for him: one for him and one for his parents. And whoever performs a pilgrimage on behalf of a dead person without that person having bequeathed it, one pilgrimage is recorded for him and seventy pilgrimages are recorded for the one on whose behalf he performed it.

"And on the evening of ʿArafa, God Glorified and Most High descends to the lowest heaven, looks at His servants and boasts of them before the angels. He, Majestic is His majesty, says: 'My angels, do you not see My servants, who have come from every deep mountain pass, disheveled and dust-covered, hoping for My mercy? I make you witnesses, My angels, that I have given their wrongdoers to their doers of good, and made some of them intercessors for others, and forgiven them all. Depart, My servants, all of you forgiven for what has passed of your sins, small and great, old and new.' An accepted pilgrimage is better than this world and all that is in it. And it is said to the one from whom "it was not accepted: 'Go forth from your sins'; and the one whom God accepts has won a great victory."

Further accounts

Visitation, the Black Stone, and the Sanctuary

ENGLISH

It has been narrated from the Prophet ﷺ that he said: "Whoever visits me after my death is as though he visited me in my lifetime; and whoever did not meet me and did not pledge allegiance to me, then comes to Medina after my death, greets me and visits me at my grave, has pledged allegiance to me. And whoever comes to the Black Corner and kisses it is as though he has pledged allegiance to God Most High and to His Messenger ﷺ." The Prophet ﷺ said: "The Corner is the right hand of God on earth, with which He shakes hands with His servants as one of you shakes hands with his brother; and whoever did not live to pledge allegiance to the Messenger of God ﷺ and touches the Stone has pledged allegiance to God Most High and to His Messenger ﷺ." And he ﷺ said: "Nothing remains of Paradise except this Black Stone; and were it not for what the impurities of the idolaters "and their filth touched of it, no one afflicted, seeking healing by it, would touch it except that he would be cured. Whoever dies in the Sanctuary is as though he died in the fourth heaven; whoever dies in Bayt al-Maqdis [Jerusalem] is as though he died in the lowest heaven; and whoever performs the pilgrimage to the House of God Most High on foot, God writes for him for every step he lifts and sets down seventy thousand good deeds of the good deeds of the Sanctuary." Ibn ʿAbbās ﵄, said: "A good deed of the Sanctuary is a hundred thousand good deeds." And he ﷺ said: "For the pilgrim who rides, for every step his camel takes, one good deed of the good deeds of the Sanctuary." It was said, "O "Messenger of God ﷺ, and what are the good deeds of the Sanctuary?" He said: "Every good deed is a hundred thousand good deeds."

It is narrated from the Prophet ﷺ that he said: "God Most High will raise from the cemetery of Mecca seventy thousand martyrs who will enter Paradise without reckoning, their faces like the moon on the night of Badr, and each of them will intercede for seventy thousand men." It was said: "Who are they, Messenger of God ﷺ?" He said: "The strangers [al-ghurabāʾ]." And whoever dies in the Sanctuary of God Most High or the Sanctuary of the Messenger of God ﷺ, or dies between Mecca and Medina as a pilgrim or one performing the lesser pilgrimage, God will raise him on the Day of Resurrection among the secure. Indeed, drinking one's fill of Zamzam water is a clearance from hypocrisy. And whoever prays two rakʿas in al-Ḥijr, on the side of the Syrian Corner, it is as though he "had revived seventy thousand nights, and he will have the like of the worship of every believing man and woman, and it is as though he had performed forty accepted, righteous pilgrimages. And whoever prays four rakʿas facing the door of the Kaʿba, it is as though he had worshipped God Most High with the worship of all His creation, many times over; God Most High will make him secure on the Day of Resurrection from the Greatest Terror; and God, Mighty and Majestic, will command Gabriel, Michael and all the angels ؑ, to ask forgiveness for him until the Day of Resurrection.

وَقد رُوِيَ عَن النَّبِي - ﷺ: أَنه قَالَ: من زارني بعد وفاتي فَكَأَنَّمَا زارني فِي حَياتِي، وَمن لم يدركني وَلم يبايعني ثمَّ جَاءَ إِلَى الْمَدِينَة بعد وفاتي، وَسلم عَليّ وزارني عِنْد قَبْرِي فقد بايعني.

وَمن أَتَى الرُّكْن الْأسود فَقبله فَكَأَنَّمَا بَايع اللَّهِ تَعَالَى وَرَسُوله - ﷺ قَالَ - ﷺ: أَن الرُّكْن يَمِين اللَّهِ فِي الأَرْض، يُصَافح بهَا عبَادَة كَمَا يُصَافح أحدكُم أَخَاهُ، وَمن لم يدْرك بيعَة رَسُول اللَّهِ - ﷺ - واستلم الْحجر فقد بَايع اللَّهِ تَعَالَى، وَرَسُوله - ﷺ.

وَقَالَ - ﷺ: أَنه لم يبْق شَيْء من الْجنَّة غير هَذَا الْحجر الْأسود، وَلَوْلَا مَا مَسّه من أنجاس الْمُشْركين وأرجاسهم مَا مَسّه ذُو عاهة يستشفي بِهِ إِلَّا برأَ.

وَمن مَاتَ بِالْحرم فَكَأَنَّمَا مَاتَ فِي السَّمَاء الرَّابِعَة، وَمن مَاتَ فِي بَيت الْمُقَدّس فَكَأَنَّمَا مَاتَ فِي سَمَاء الدُّنْيَا، وَمن حج بَيت اللَّهِ تَعَالَى مَاشِيا كتب اللَّهِ لَهُ بِكُل قدم يرفعهُ ويضعه سبعين ألف حَسَنَة من حَسَنَات الْحرم. قَالَ ابْن عَبَّاس رَضِي اللَّهِ عَنْهُمَا: حَسَنَة الْحرم بِمِائَة ألف حَسَنَة.

وَقَالَ - ﷺ: أَن للْحَاج الرَّاكِب لكل خطْوَة يخطوها بعيره حَسَنَة من حَسَنَات الْحرم. قيل يَا رَسُول اللَّهِ وَمَا حَسَنَات الْحرم؟ قَالَ: كل حَسَنَة بِمِائَة ألف حَسَنَة. وَرُوِيَ عَن النَّبِي - ﷺ - أَنه قَالَ: يحْشر اللَّهِ تَعَالَى من مَقْبرَة مَكَّة سبعين ألف شَهِيد يدْخلُونَ الْجنَّة بِغَيْر حِسَاب، وُجُوههم كَالْقَمَرِ لَيْلَة بدر، ويشفع كل وَاحِد مِنْهُم فِي سبعين ألف رجل. فَقيل: من هم يَا رَسُول اللَّهِ؟ قَالَ: الغرباء.

وَمن مَاتَ فِي حرم اللَّهِ تَعَالَى أَو حرم رَسُول اللَّهِ - ﷺ أَو مَاتَ بَين مَكَّة وَالْمَدينَة حَاجا أَو مُعْتَمِرًا بَعثه اللَّهِ يَوْم الْقِيَامَة من الْآمنينَ. أَلا وَإِن التضلع من مَاء زَمْزَم بَرَاءَة من النِّفَاق.

وَمن صلى فِي الْحجر رَكْعَتَيْنِ نَاحيَة الرُّكْن الشَّامي فَكَأَنَّهُ أحيى سبعين ألف لَيْلَة، وَكَانَ كعبادة كل مُؤمن ومؤمنة، وكأنما حج أَرْبَعِينَ حجَّة مبرورة متقبلة، وَمن صلى مُقَابل بَاب الْكَعْبَة أَربع رَكْعَات فَكَأَنَّمَا عبد اللَّهِ تَعَالَى كعبادة جَمِيع خلقه أضعافا مضاعفة، وآمنه اللَّهِ تَعَالَى يَوْم الْقِيَامَة من الْفَزع الْأَكْبَر، وَأمر اللَّهِ عز وَجل جِبْرِيل وَمِيكَائِيل وَجَمِيع الْمَلَائِكَة ؑ أَن يَسْتَغْفِرُوا لَهُ إِلَى يَوْم الْقِيَامَة.
