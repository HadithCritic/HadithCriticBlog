English
Isnad

Qabisa narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us, from 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib, from al-Sha'bi, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“I was commanded [to use] the toothstick until I feared for my mouth.”

العربية

Matn

لقد أمرت بالسواك حتى خشيت على فمي.

Ya'la and Abu Nu'aym and Qabisa narrated to us, who all said: Sufyan narrated to us, from 'Ata', who said:

Isnad

Abu Nu'aym narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us, from 'Ata', from Qays b. Sakan, who said:

Matn

When the Prophet ﷺ prostrated he would say: “My face has prostrated to the One who created it and opened its hearing and sight.” And David, when he prostrated, would say: “My face has prostrated, rubbed in the dust, to its Creator, in the dust, and it is right for Him.”

Isnad

Qabisa narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us, from 'Ata', from his father, from 'Abd Allah [b. Mas'ud], who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“I commend a man to his father, I commend a man to his father, I commend a man to his father.” And from 'Abd Allah: “I commend a man to his mother, I commend a man to his mother, I commend a man to his mother; and I commend a man to his freed slave who is nearest to him, even if it harms him by harming.”

Isnad

Abu Nu'aym and Qabisa narrated to us, from Sufyan, from 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib, from Harb b. 'Ubayd Allah, from a maternal uncle of his from Bakr b. Wa'il, who said:

Matn

He came to the Prophet ﷺ and asked him about the zakat on cattle, camels and sheep, and he told him. When he turned away, he said: “O Messenger of God, shall I take a tenth from them?” He said: “Tithes are only upon the Jews and Christians, and there is no tithe on a Muslim.”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

أتى النبي ﷺ فسأله عن زكاة البقر والإبل والغنم فأخبره فلما ولى قال يا رسول الله أعشرهم قال إنما العشور على اليهود والنصارى وليس على المسلم عشور.

Entry165/ 14
Ḥadīth Sufyān al-Thawrī · p. 105
English
Isnad

Abu Nu'aym and 'Ubayd Allah and Qabisa narrated to us, from Sufyan, from 'Ata', from his father, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Two qualities which no Muslim man keeps count of but he enters Paradise; they are easy and few act upon them.” They said: “What are they, O Messenger of God?” He said: “You glorify God ten times after every obligatory prayer, praise Him ten times and magnify Him ten times: that is a hundred and fifty on the tongue and a thousand five hundred in the scale. And when he goes to his bed he glorifies, praises and magnifies a hundred: that is a hundred on the tongue and a thousand in the scale. Which of you commits two thousand five hundred wrongs in a day and night?” They said: “O Messenger of God, how is it that we do not keep count of them?” He said: “Satan comes to one of you and talks to him until he makes him sleep and forget, and says: Remember such and such a need, until he departs and does not remember them.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا أبو نُعَيْم وعبيد الله وقَبِيصَة عن سفيان عن عَطَاء ، عن أبيه عن عبد الله بن عمرو قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ

Matn

خصلتان لا يحصيهما رجل مسلم، إلا دخل الجنة وهما يسير ومن يعمل بهما قليل قالوا: وما هما يا رسول الله قال: يسبح الله في دبر كل صلاة مكتوبة عشرا، ويحمده عشرا، ويكبره عشرا، فتلك خمسون ومئة على اللسان، وألف وخمسمئة في الميزان، وإذا أوى إلى فراشه سبحه وحمده وكبره، مئة فتلك مئة باللسان على اللسان وألف في الميزان فأيكم يعمل في اليوم، والليلة ألفين وخمسمئة سيئة قالوا: يا رسول الله قالوا: يا رسول الله، وكيف لا يحصيهما قال: يأتي أحدكم الشيطان فيحدثه حتى ينومه ويأتيه فيقول فيقول: اذكر حاجة كذا وكذ احتى ينصرف، ولا يذكرها.

Isnad

Qabisa narrated to us, from Sufyan, from 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib, from 'Arfaja, from 'Utba b. Farqad, from a man of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ, who said:

Matn

Matn

إذا جاء رمضان صفدت فيه الشياطين وغلقت فيه أبواب النار وفتحت فيه أبواب الجنة ونادى مناد يا باغي الخير هلم يا باغي الشر أقصر.

Entry169/ 14
Ḥadīth Sufyān al-Thawrī · p. 107
English
Isnad

Qabisa narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us, from 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Ubayd b. 'Umayr, from his father, from Ibn 'Umar, who said:

Matn

It was said to him: “Shall we touch them, the Black Stone and the Yemeni corner?” He said: “I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: 'Wiping them removes sins.'”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا قَبِيصَة ، حدثنا سفيان عن عَطَاء بن السَّائب عن عبد الله بن عبيد بن عمير قال أراه ، عن أبيه عن ابن عمر ﵄ قال

Matn

قيل له نستلمها الحجر والركن اليماني فقال سمعت رسول الله ﷺ يقول مسحهما يحطان الخطايا.

Entry171/ 14
Ḥadīth Sufyān al-Thawrī · p. 107-108
English
Isnad

Qabisa narrated to us, from Sufyan, from 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib, from Abu 'Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami, who said:

Matn

I attended the Friday prayer with Hudhayfa at al-Mada'in and heard him say: “God the Exalted says: {The Hour has come near, and the moon has split} [al-Qamar 54:1]. Beware: the moon split in the time of the Messenger of God. Beware: the Hour has come near. Beware: today is the training and tomorrow the race.” He said: I said to my father: “Tomorrow the horses race?” He said: “You are heedless!” until I heard him say: “The winner is the one who is first to Paradise, and the finish line is the Fire.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا قَبِيصَة ، عن سفيان عن عَطَاء بن السَّائب عن أبي عبد الرحمن السلمي قال

Matn

جمعت مع حذيفة ﵁ بالمدائن فسمعته يقول إنَّ الله عز وجل تعالى يقول {اقتربت الساعة وانشق القمر} ألا إن القمر قد انشق على عهد رسول الله ﷺ ألا وإن الساعة قد اقتربت ألا وإن المضمار اليوم والسبق غدا. قال فقلت لأُبي غدا تجري الخيل قال إنك لغافل حتى سمعته يقول إن السابق من سبق إلى الجنة والغاية النار.

English
Isnad

Qabisa and Abu Nu'aym narrated to us, from Sufyan, from 'Ata' b. al-Sa'ib, from Umm Kulthum, daughter of 'Ali, who said:

Matn

[I brought her something of the charity, and she said: “Beware of our young men, for Maymun, or Mihran, the freedman of the Prophet ﷺ, informed me that the Prophet ﷺ said: 'We are a household forbidden charity, and our freedmen are of us; so do not eat charity.'”]

Isnad
