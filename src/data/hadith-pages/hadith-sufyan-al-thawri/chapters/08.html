Isnad

From Zayd b. Talha al-Taymi, who said: I heard Ibn 'Abbas, may God be pleased with them both,

Matn

[Ibn 'Abbas] recited over a funeral the Opening of the Book and a sura, reciting aloud, and he said: “I only recited aloud to let you know that it is sunna.” [Variant: the imam makes it light / makes it inaudible.]

From al-Aswad b. Qays, from Nubayh, from Jabir b. 'Abd Allah, who said:

Matn

On the day of Uhud, the slain were carried to be buried in al-Baqi', and the herald of the Messenger of God ﷺ called: “The Messenger of God ﷺ commands you to bury the slain where they fell,” after I had carried my father and my maternal uncle to bury them; so they were returned.

Entry220/ 110
Ḥadīth Sufyān al-Thawrī · p. 134
English
Isnad

From 'Alqama b. Marthad, from Sulayman b. Buraida, from his father, who said:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ came to a grave and sat by it, and the people sat around him. He kept moving his finger and his head as if addressing someone. Then he rose weeping, and 'Umar met him and said: “What makes you weep?” He said: “I asked my Lord, Mighty and Majestic, for permission [to visit] the grave of Muhammad's mother, and He permitted me; and I asked Him to allow me to seek forgiveness [for her], and He refused me; so her mercy overtook me and I wept.” He said: I never saw a time when more people were weeping than that time.

العربية
Isnad

عن علقمة بن مرثد عن سليمان بن بريدة ، عن أبيه قال

Matn

أتى النبي ﷺ قبرا فجلس عليه، وجلس الناس حوله، فجعل يحرك أصبعه ورأسه كالمخاطب. فقام فجعل يبكي فاستقبله عمر ﵁، فقال: ما يبكيك؟ قال إني استأذنت ربي عز وجل ربي في قبر أم محمد فأذن لي وسألته الاستغفار فأبى علي، فأدركتني رحمتها فبكيت. قال: فما رأيت ساعة أكثر باكيا من تلك الساعة.
