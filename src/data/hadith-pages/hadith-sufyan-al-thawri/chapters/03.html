English
Isnad

Qabisa narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us, from 'Abd Allah b. Muhammad b. 'Aqil, from Jabir b. 'Abd Allah, who said:

Matn

We were with the Messenger of God ﷺ in a palm grove (suwar) in Medina, and he said: “A man of the people of Paradise will appear before you.” Abu Bakr came and we congratulated him. Then he said: “A man of the people of Paradise will appear before you.” 'Umar came and we congratulated him. Then he said: “A man of the people of Paradise will appear before you.” He said: I saw the Messenger of God ﷺ tilting his head from beneath the palm fronds, saying: “O God, if You will, make it 'Ali.” Then 'Ali came and we congratulated him.

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا قَبِيصَة ، حدثنا سفيان عن عبد الله بن محمد بن عَقِيل عن جابر بن عبد الله ﵁ قال

Matn

كنا مع رسول الله ﷺ في صير بالمدينة فقال يطلع عليكم رسول الله ﷺ مرجل رجل من أهل الجنة قال فجاء أبو بكر ﵁ فهنيناه، ثُم قال: يطلع عليكم رجل من أهل الجنة قال فجاء عمر ﵁ فهنيناه، ثُم قال: يطلع عليكم رجل من أهل الجنة قال فرأيت رسول الله ﷺ يصغي رأسه من تحت السعف ويقول اللهم إن شئت جعلته عليا فجاء علي ﵁ فهنيناه.

Isnad

Qabisa and Abu Nu'aym narrated to us, who both said: Sufyan narrated to us, from 'Abd Allah [b. Mas'ud], from al-Tufayl b. Ubayy b. Ka'b, from Ubayy b. Ka'b, who said:

Matn

I said: “O Messenger of God, I invoke blessings on you often; how much of my prayer should I devote to you?” He said: “As you wish.” I said: “A quarter?” He said: “As you wish, and if you add, it is better for you.” I said: “A third?” He said: “As you wish, and if you add, it is better for you.” I said: “Half?” He said: “As you wish, and if you add, it is better for you.” I said: “Two thirds?” He said: “As you wish, and if you add, it is better for you.” I said: “Shall I make all my prayer for you?” He said: “Then your worry will be taken care of and your sin forgiven.”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

قلت يا رسول الله ﷺ إني أكثر الصلاة عليك، فكم أجعل لك من صلاتي؟ قال: ما شئت، [قلت: الربع؟ قال: ما شئت، وإن زدت فهو خير لك، قلت النصف؟ قال: ما شئت، فإن زدت فهو خير لك، قلت الثلثين؟ قال: ما شئت] فإن زدت فهو خير لك، قلت: أجعل صلاتي كلها؟ قال: إذن تكفى همك ويغفر ذنبك.

English
Isnad

Qabisa narrated to us, from Sufyan, from Mansur, from Mujahid, from Abu 'Ayyash al-Zuraqi, who said:

Matn

“The Messenger of God ﷺ prayed the noon prayer at 'Usfan, with the polytheists between him and the qibla, and with them, or over them, was Khalid b. al-Walid. The polytheists said: 'They were in a prayer; had we attacked them it would have been the spoils.' Then the polytheists said: 'A prayer is coming that is dearer to them than their fathers and sons.' Jibril came down with the verses between the noon and afternoon prayers, and the Messenger of God ﷺ prayed with the people lined up in two rows behind him. He said takbir and they said takbir with him; he bowed and they bowed; he rose and they rose together; then he prostrated, and the row nearest him prostrated with him, while the rear row stood guarding them with their weapons. Then he rose and they rose; the rear row prostrated, then the front row stepped back and the rear row came forward. He said takbir and they said takbir with him, then he bowed and they all bowed with him, he rose and they all rose, then he prostrated and the row nearest him prostrated, while the rear row stood guarding them with their weapons; then he rose and they rose; then the rear row prostrated; then he gave the salam to them. And he prayed it once more in the land of Banu Sulaym.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا قَبِيصَة ، عن سفيان ، عن مَنْصُور عن مجاهد عن أبي عياش الزرقي قال

Matn

صلى رسول الله ﷺ الظهر بعسفان والمشركون بينه وبين القبلة ، معهم، أو عليهم ، خالد بن الوليد ، فقال المشركون: كانوا في صلاة لو أصبنا منهم كانت هي الغنيمة، فقال المشركون: إنها ستجيء صلاة هي أحب إليهم من أموالهم وآبائهم وأبنائهم قال: فنزل جبريل ﵇ بالآيات فيما بين الظهر والعصر ، فصلى رسول الله ﷺ، وصف الناس خلفه صفين ، [وكبر وكبروا] معه ، ثم ركع وركعوا جميعا ثم رفع ورفعوا جميعا ، ثم سجد وسجد معه الصف الذي يلونه ، وقام الصف المؤخر يحرسونهم بسلاحهم ، ثم رفع ورفعوا ، ثم سجد الصف المؤخر ورفعوا فتأخر الصف المقدم وتقدم الصف المؤخر ، فكبر وكبروا معه ، ثم ركع وركعوا معه جميعا ، ثم رفع ورفعوا جميعا ، ثم سجد وسجدوا الصف الذي يلونه وقام الصف المؤخر يحرسونهم بسلاحهم يحرسونهم، ثم رفع ورفعوا ، ثم سجد الصف المؤخر ثم سلم عليهم وصلاها من آخرىن في أرض بني سليم.

Qabisa narrated to us; Sufyan narrated to us, from Mansur, from Yunus b. Khabbab, from Talq b. Habib, from a man who [was afflicted with al-bashir and] traveled to Syria and Medina, who said:

Matn

I prayed beside an old man, and he said to me: “What brought you?” I said: “My father is afflicted with [a swelling,] al-bashir, and I came out to seek [a remedy] for him.” He said: “I know of nothing for this except some words that I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say; say them, perhaps God will heal him or benefit him by them: 'Our Lord is God who is in the heaven, hallowed be Your name; Your command is in the heaven and the earth; as Your mercy is in the heaven, place Your mercy in the earth; forgive us our sins and faults, Lord of the good; send down a mercy from Your mercy upon this pain, that he may recover.'”
