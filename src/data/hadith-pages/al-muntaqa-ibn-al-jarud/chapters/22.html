Entry46/ 22
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 25
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Bishr b. 'Umar informed us; he said: Malik informed us, from Safwan b. Sulaym, from Sa'id b. Salama, that al-Mughira b. Abi Burda informed him

Matn

that he heard Abu Hurayra, ؓ, say: A man asked the Messenger of God ﷺ and said: “O Messenger of God, we sail the sea and carry a little water with us; if we perform ablution with it, we go thirsty. May we perform ablution with sea water?” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Its water is purifying, and its dead [creatures] are lawful.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا محمد بن يحيى ، قال : ثنا بشر بن عمر ، قال : ثنا مالك ، عن صفوان بن سليم ، عن سعيد بن سلمة ، أن المغيرة بن أبي بردة أخبره أنه سمع أبا هريرة رضي الله عنه يقول :

Matn

سأل رجل رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فقال : يا رسول الله ، إنا نركب البحر فنحمل معنا القليل من الماء ، فإن توضأنا به عطشنا ، أفنتوضأ بماء البحر ؟ فقال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : هو الطهور ماؤه ، الحلال ميتته

Entry47/ 22
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 25
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Uthman al-Warraq, Hajjaj b. Hamza al-Razi and Abu Yahya Muhammad b. Sa'id al-'Attar narrated to us; they said: Abu Usama informed us, from al-Walid b. Kathir, from Muhammad b. 'Abbad b. Ja'far, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Abd Allah b. 'Umar, from his father, ؓ, who said:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ was asked about water and the beasts of prey and animals that come to it. He said: “When the water reaches two qullas (large jars), it does not carry impurity.”

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 26
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Affan b. Muslim informed us; he said: Hammad b. Salama informed us; he said: 'Asim b. al-Mundhir informed me; he said:

Matn

We were in an orchard of ours—or of 'Ubayd God b. 'Abd Allah b. 'Umar—and the time for prayer came. 'Ubayd God went to the orchard’s basin (miqra), in which there was the hide of a camel, and began to perform ablution from it. We said: “Do you perform ablution from this, with this hide in it?” (The source repeats this question.) He said: “My father narrated to me that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: ‘When the water is two qullas, it does not become impure.’”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا محمد بن يحيى ، قال : ثنا عفان بن مسلم ، قال : ثنا حماد بن سلمة ، قال : أخبرني عاصم بن المنذر ، قال : كنا في بستان لنا أو لعبيد الله بن عبد الله بن عمر ، فحضرت الصلاة ، فقام عبيد الله إلى مقرى البستان ، وفيه جلد بعير ، فأخذ يتوضأ منه ، فقلنا : أتتوضأ من هذا وفيه جلد بعير ؟ فأخذ يتوضأ منه ، فقلنا : أنتوضأ من هذا وفيه هذا الجلد ؟ فقال : حدثني أبي ، أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم قال :

Matn

إذا كان الماء قلتين فإنه لا ينجس

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Uthman al-Warraq and Musa b. 'Abd al-Rahman al-Masruqi narrated to us; they both said: Abu Usama informed us, from al-Walid b. Kathir, from Muhammad b. Ka'b, from 'Ubayd God b. 'Abd Allah—al-Masruqi said:

Matn

Ibn Rafi' b. Khadij—from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, ؓ, who said: It was said: “O Messenger of God, may we perform ablution from the well of Buda'a?”—and it is a well into which filth, menstrual rags and dogs’ flesh are thrown. He said: “Water is pure; nothing makes it impure.”

Isnad

Matn

قيل : يا رسول الله ، أنتوضأ من بئر بضاعة ؟ قال : وهي بئر يطرح فيها النتن والحيض ولحوم الكلاب ، فقال : الماء طهور لا ينجسه شيء

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 27
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya, Ahmad b. Yusuf and Ibn 'Awn narrated to us; they said: 'Ubayd God b. Musa informed us; he said: Sufyan informed us, from Simak, from 'Ikrima, from Ibn 'Abbas, ؓ, who said:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ came to one of his wives, where some of her bathwater—or of her ablution water—was left over, and wished to perform ablution with it. She said: “O Messenger of God, I bathed from sexual impurity with it.” He said: “Water does not become impure.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا محمد بن يحيى ، وأحمد بن يوسف وابن عون ، قالوا : ثنا عبيد الله بن موسى ، قال : أنا سفيان ، عن سماك ، عن عكرمة ، عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما ، قال :

Matn

انتهى النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم إلى بعض أزواجه ، وقد فضل من غسلها أو من وضوئها ، فأراد أن يتوضأ به فقالت : يا رسول الله ، إني اغتسلت منه من جنابة ، فقال : إن الماء لا ينجس

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: I read to ‘Abdullah b. Nafi‘, and Mutarrif b. ‘Abdullah narrated to me, from Malik, from Ishaq b. ‘Abdullah b. Abi Talha, from Humayda bint ‘Ubayd b. Rifa‘a, from Kabsha bint Ka‘b b. Malik

Matn

— and she was married to a son of Abu Qatada —: that Abu Qatada came in to her and she poured out ablution water for him. A cat came to drink from it, and he tilted the vessel for it until it drank. Kabsha said: He saw me looking at him and said: “Does it surprise you, my niece?” She said: I said: “Yes.” He said: “The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: ‘It is not impure (najas); it is one of those who go about among you (al-tawwafin ‘alaykum), or the female ones (al-tawwafat).’”
