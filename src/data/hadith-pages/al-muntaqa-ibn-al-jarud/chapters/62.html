Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Hasan b. al-Rabi' narrated to us; he said: Abu l-Ahwas narrated to us, from Simak, from 'Ikrima, from Ibn 'Abbas, ؓ, who said:

Matn

A man came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: “O Messenger of God, is the Hajj every year?” He said: “No, but once. Then whoever wishes to do more voluntarily, let him do so afterward. Had I said ‘every year,’ it would be every year.”

Isnad

And Ibn Tawus, from his father; both said:

Matn

The Messenger of God (ﷺ) appointed Dhu al-Hulayfa as the miqat for the people of Medina, al-Juhfa for the people of Syria, Qarn for the people of Najd — and Ibn Tawus said: Qarn al-Manazil — and Yalamlam for the people of Yemen — ‘Amr said: and Ibn Tawus said: Alamlam. He said: “These are for their own people, and for whoever comes upon them who is not of their people; and whoever is on the near side of them (dunahunna)” — ‘Amr said: “then from his own home.” And Ibn Tawus said: “then from wherever he sets out, and likewise thereafter, until even the people of Mecca enter ihram from it.”

Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: ‘Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us, saying: Ma‘mar informed us, from al-Zuhri, from Salim, from Ibn ‘Umar (ؓ):

Matn

that a man called out and said: “O Messenger of God, which garments should the muhrim avoid?” He said: “He should not wear trousers, nor a shirt, nor the burnus (hooded cloak), nor a turban, nor any garment touched by saffron or wars (a yellow dye). Let one of you enter ihram in an izar, a rida’, and a pair of sandals; and if he does not find sandals, let him wear leather socks (khuffayn) and cut them so that they reach the ankles.”

Matn

We came to Jabir b. 'Abd Allah while he was among Banu Salima and asked him about the Hajj of the Prophet ﷺ. He told us that the Messenger of God ﷺ stayed in Medina for nine years without performing Hajj. Then he announced to the people that the Messenger of God ﷺ would perform Hajj that year. A great number of people came to Medina, every one of them seeking to follow the Messenger of God ﷺ and to do what he did. The Prophet ﷺ set out with five days remaining of Dhu l-Qa'da, and we set out with him. When he reached Dhu l-Hulayfa, Asma' bt. 'Umays gave birth to Muhammad b. Abi Bakr, and she sent to the Messenger of God ﷺ asking: “What should I do?” He said: “Bathe, then bind yourself with a cloth, then enter ihram.” The Messenger of God ﷺ proceeded until, when his she-camel had risen with him on al-Bayda', he pronounced the declaration of divine oneness: “Here I am, O God, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. Truly, the praise and the favor are Yours, and the dominion; You have no partner.” The people pronounced the talbiya, and the people were adding: “Lord of the ascents” and the like. The Prophet ﷺ heard them and said nothing to them. I looked as far as my eye could see in front of the Messenger of God ﷺ, and there were riders and walkers, and behind him the same, and to his right the same, and to his left the same. Jabir said: The Messenger of God ﷺ was among us, the Qur'an being revealed to him, and he knew its interpretation; whatever he did, we did. We set out intending only the Hajj. When we came to the Ka'ba, the Prophet of God ﷺ touched the Black Stone, then jogged three circuits and walked four. When he finished, he went to the Station of Ibrahim, ﷺ, and prayed two rak'as behind it, then recited: “And take the Station of Ibrahim as a place of prayer.” My father said: In them he recited the sura of divine oneness and “Say: O disbelievers.” Then he touched the Stone and went out to al-Safa, and recited: “Indeed, al-Safa and al-Marwa are among the symbols of God.” Then he said: “We begin with what God began with.” He climbed al-Safa until he could see the House, and said the takbir, then said: “There is no god but God alone, without partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. There is no god but God; He fulfilled His promise, gave victory to His servant, and defeated”—or: “overcame”—“the confederates alone.” Then he made supplication, then returned to these words. Then he descended, and when his feet reached the bottom of the valley, he jogged, and when he climbed up, he walked, until he came to al-Marwa and climbed it until he saw the House, and said on it what he had said on al-Safa. When it was the seventh [circuit], at al-Marwa, he said: “O people, if I had known beforehand of my affair what I came to know afterward, I would not have driven sacrificial animals, and I would have made it an 'umra. So whoever has no sacrificial animal with him, let him leave ihram and make it an 'umra.” He said: So all the people left ihram. Suraqa b. Ju'shum, who was at the bottom of al-Marwa, said: “O Messenger of God, is this for our year only, or for ever?” He said: The Messenger of God ﷺ interlaced his fingers and said: “For ever”—three times—then said: “The 'umra has entered into the Hajj until the Day of Resurrection.” He said: 'Ali, ؓ, came from Yemen and brought a sacrificial animal, and the Messenger of God ﷺ had driven sacrificial animals with him from Medina. Fatima, ؓ, had left ihram and put on dyed garments and applied kohl, and 'Ali, ؓ, objected to that in her. She said: “My father ordered me to.” He said: 'Ali, ؓ, said in Kufa—my father said: Jabir, ؓ, did not mention this phrase—“So I went, to incite [the Prophet against her], to ask the Messenger of God ﷺ for a ruling about what Fatima had done. I said: ‘Fatima has put on dyed garments and applied kohl, and she said: My father ordered me to.’ He said: ‘She spoke the truth, she spoke the truth; I ordered her to.’” Jabir said: And he said to 'Ali, ؓ: “With what intention did you enter ihram?” He said: I said: “O God, I enter ihram with the same intention with which the Messenger of God ﷺ entered it.” He said: “I have sacrificial animals with me,” he said, “so do not leave ihram.” He said: The total of the sacrificial animals that 'Ali, ؓ, brought from Yemen and that the Prophet ﷺ brought was one hundred. The Messenger of God ﷺ slaughtered sixty-three with his own hand and gave 'Ali, ؓ, [the knife], and he slaughtered the rest, and [the Prophet] made him a partner in his sacrifice. Then he ordered that a piece be taken from each camel and put in a pot, and the two of them ate from its meat and drank from its broth. Then the Prophet of God ﷺ said: “I have slaughtered here, but all of Mina is a place of slaughter.” He stood at 'Arafa and said: “I have stood here, but all of 'Arafa is a place of standing.” He stood at al-Muzdalifa and said: “I have stood here, but all of al-Muzdalifa is a place of standing.”
