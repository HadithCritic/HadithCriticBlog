Entry1190/ 4
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 412
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us; he said: Ma'mar informed us, from al-Zuhri, from Abu Salama, from Jabir, ؓ, who said: The Prophet ﷺ would not pray over a man who owed a debt. A dead man was brought and he asked: Does he owe a debt? They said: Yes, two dinars. He said: Pray over your companion. Abu Qatada said: They are upon me, O Messenger of God. He said: So he prayed over him. He said: When God granted conquests to His Messenger, he said: I am closer to every believer than his own self; whoever leaves a debt, it is upon me, and whoever leaves wealth, it is for his heirs.

Entry1191/ 4
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 413
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Abu l-Mughira narrated to us; he said: Safwan narrated to us; he said: 'Abd al-Rahman b. Jubayr b. Nufayr narrated to us, from his father, from 'Awf b. Malik, who said: When something came to the Messenger of God ﷺ, he would divide it the same day, giving the married man two portions and the unmarried man one portion. He said: I was called—and I used to be called before 'Ammar b. Yasir—so I was called and he gave me two portions, as I had a family. Then 'Ammar was called after me, and he gave him one portion.

Entry1192/ 4
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 413
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: Ya‘qub b. Ibrahim b. Sa‘d narrated to us, saying: My father narrated to us, from Salih, from Ibn Shihab, from ‘Abdullah b. ‘Abdullah b. al-Harith b. Nawfal b. al-Harith b. ‘Abd al-Muttalib, that ‘Abd al-Muttalib b. Rabi‘a b. al-Harith b. ‘Abd al-Muttalib informed him that Rabi‘a b. al-Harith and al-‘Abbas b. ‘Abd al-Muttalib gathered together and said: “By God, if we were to send these two boys — me and al-Fadl b. al-‘Abbas — to the Messenger of God (ﷺ), and he were to appoint them over these sadaqas…” — and he mentioned part of the hadith. He said: So we spoke to him and said: “O Messenger of God, we have come so that you may appoint us over these sadaqas.” He said: “Indeed, sadaqa is not fitting for Muhammad nor for the family of Muhammad; it is only the impurities of the people. Call Mahmiya b. al-Jaz’ — who was in charge of the tithes — and Abu Sufyan b. al-Harith.” They came to him, and he said to Mahmiya: “Marry your daughter to this boy,” meaning al-Fadl, and he married her to him. And he said to Abu Sufyan: “Marry your daughter to this boy,” and he married her to him. Then he said to Mahmiya: “Pay the dowry on behalf of the two of them from the fifth.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا محمد بن يحيى ، قال : ثنا يعقوب بن إبراهيم بن سعد ، قال : ثنا أبي ، عن صالح ، عن ابن شهاب ، عن عبد الله بن عبد الله بن الحارث بن نوفل بن الحارث بن عبد المطلب ، أنه أخبره عبد المطلب بن ربيعة بن الحارث بن عبد المطلب ، أخبره أنه

Matn

اجتمع ربيعة بن الحارث والعباس بن عبد المطلب فقالا : والله لو بعثنا هذين الغلامين - لي وللفضل بن العباس - إلى رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم فأمرهما على هذه الصدقات ، فذكر بعض الحديث ، قال : فكلمناه فقلنا : يا رسول الله جئناك لتؤمرنا على هذه الصدقات فقال : ألا إن الصدقة لا تنبغي لمحمد ولا لآل محمد ، إنما هي أوساخ الناس ، ادع لي محمية بن الجزء - وكان على العشور - وأبا سفيان بن الحارث ، فأتياه فقال لمحمية : أنكح هذا الغلام ابنتك للفضل ، فأنكحه ، وقال لأبي سفيان : أنكح هذا الغلام ابنتك ، فأنكحه . ثم قال لمحمية : أصدق عنهما من الخمس

Entry1193/ 4
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 414
English

Muhammad b. ‘Abdullah b. al-Hakam informed us that ‘Abdullah b. Nafi‘ narrated to them, saying: Hisham b. Sa‘d narrated to us, from Zayd b. Aslam, from his father, that when Mu‘awiya came to Medina on pilgrimage, ‘Abdullah b. ‘Umar (ؓ) came to him, and Mu‘awiya said to him: “What is your need, Abu ‘Abd al-Rahman?” He said to him: “My need is the stipend of the freed slaves (al-muharrarin), for I saw the Messenger of God (ﷺ), whenever something came to him, he did not begin with the first of them [as in the source text; the sense is likely that he began with them first].”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

أن معاوية لما قدم المدينة حاجا جاءه عبد الله بن عمر رضي الله عنهما ، فقال له معاوية : حاجتك يا أبا عبد الرحمن ؟ فقال له : حاجتي عطاء المحررين ، فإني رأيت رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم حينما جاءه شيء لم يبدأ بأول منهم
