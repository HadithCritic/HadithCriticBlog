Entry919/ 17
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 320
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Ya'la narrated to us; he said: Abu Hayyan narrated to us, from al-Sha'bi, from Ibn 'Umar, ؓ, who said:

Matn

'Umar, ؓ, addressed us from the pulpit of the Messenger of God ﷺ. He praised God and extolled Him, admonished and reminded, and said: To proceed: The prohibition of wine was revealed on the day it was revealed, and it is made from five things: grapes, dates, wheat, barley, and honey. And wine is whatever clouds the mind.

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا محمد بن يحيى ، قال : ثنا يعلى ، قال : ثنا أبو حيان ، عن الشعبي ، عن ابن عمر رضي الله عنهما ، قال :

Matn

خطبنا عمر رضي الله عنه على منبر رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فحمد الله وأثنى عليه ، ووعظ وذكر ، وقال : أما بعد ، فإن الخمر نزل تحريمها يوم نزل ، وهي من خمسة : من العنب ، والتمر ، والحنطة ، والشعير ، والعسل ، والخمر : ما خامر العقل

Isnad

‘Abdullah b. Hashim narrated to us, saying: Yahya b. Sa‘id narrated to us, from Qurra, from Sayyar Abu al-Hakam, from Abu Burda, from Abu Musa (ؓ), who said:

I said: “O Messenger of God, we have drinks — or: a drink — such as this bit‘ made from honey, and mizr made from sorghum and barley; what do you command us?” He said: “I forbid you every intoxicant.”

Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: Muhammad b. ‘Ubayd narrated to us, saying: Muhammad — that is, Ibn ‘Amr — narrated to us, from Abu Salama, from Abu Hurayra (ؓ), who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God (ﷺ) forbade preparing nabidh in the muqayyar (tar-coated vessel), the muzaffat (pitch-coated vessel), the gourd (dubba’), the hantama (glazed jar), and the naqir (hollowed-out palm trunk). He said: “And every intoxicant is forbidden.”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

نهى رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أن ينبذ في المقير ، والمزفت ، والدباء والحنتمة ، والنقير ، قال : وكل مسكر حرام

Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: Abu ‘Asim narrated to us, from Sufyan, from ‘Alqama b. Marthad, from Sulayman b. Buraydah, from his father:

Matn

that the Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “I had forbidden you from visiting graves, but now visit them, for Muhammad was given permission to visit his mother’s grave, and it reminds one of the Hereafter. I had forbidden you from keeping the meat of sacrificial animals beyond three days; I intended by that that those with abundance should provide for those without; so eat and store. And I had forbidden you (the use of certain) vessels; but a vessel neither makes anything lawful nor forbidden; and every intoxicant is forbidden.”

Matn
