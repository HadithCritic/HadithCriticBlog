Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 352
English
Isnad

Abu Sa'id al-Ashajj narrated to us; he said: Abu Usama narrated to us, from Idris al-Awdi, from Talha b. Musarrif, from Sa'id b. Jubayr, from Ibn 'Abbas, ؓ:

Matn

[{And for everyone We have made heirs (mawali)}—he said: heirs; and regarding His saying {And those with whom your oaths have made a pact, give them their share}, he said: When the Emigrants came to Medina, the Emigrant would inherit from the Ansari, rather than his blood relatives, by virtue of the brotherhood that the Messenger of God ﷺ had established between them. When the verse] {And for everyone We have made heirs} was revealed, it abrogated that. Then he recited: {And those with whom your oaths have made a pact, give them their share}—of support, sincere counsel, and assistance—and he may make a bequest for him, but inheritance has gone.

Matn

{ ولكل جعلنا موالي } قال: ورثة ، وفي قوله { والذين عقدت أيمانكم فآتوهم نصيبهم } ، قال: كان المهاجري حين قدموا المدينة يرث الأنصاري دون ذوي رحمه بالأخوة التي آخى رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم بينهم ، فلما نزلت الآية ] { ولكل جعلنا موالي } نسخت ثم قرأ : { والذين عقدت أيمانكم فآتوهم نصيبهم } من النصر ، والنصيحة ، والرفادة ، ويوصي له ، وقد ذهب الميراث

English
Isnad

Ibn al-Muqri’ narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, from Ibn al-Munkadir, who said:

Matn

I heard Jabir b. ‘Abdullah (ؓ) say: I fell ill, and the Messenger of God (ﷺ) came to visit me, he and Abu Bakr, both on foot, while I had lost consciousness. The Messenger of God (ﷺ) performed ablution and then poured his ablution water over me, and I came to. I said: “O Messenger of God, how shall I dispose of my property? What shall I do with my property?” But the Messenger of God (ﷺ) did not answer me until the verse of inheritance was revealed.

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 354
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Bishr b. 'Umar narrated to us; he said: Malik b. Anas narrated to us, from Ibn Shihab, from 'Uthman b. Ishaq b. Kharasha, from Qabisa b. Dhu'ayb, who said:

Matn

A grandmother came to Abu Bakr asking for her inheritance. He said: You have nothing in the Book of God, and I know of nothing for you in the sunna of the Messenger of God ﷺ. Go back until I ask the people. He asked the people, and al-Mughira b. Shu'ba said: I was present when the Messenger of God ﷺ gave her a sixth. Abu Bakr said: Is there anyone with you (to confirm)? Muhammad b. Maslama al-Ansari stood and said the same as al-Mughira said, so Abu Bakr implemented it for her. Then the other grandmother came to 'Umar b. al-Khattab, ؓ, asking for her inheritance. He said: You have nothing in the Book of God, and the ruling that has reached us as made by the Messenger of God ﷺ was only for someone other than you, and I will not add anything to the obligatory shares. But it is that sixth: if the two of you share it, it is between you, and whichever of you is alone, it is hers.

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا محمد بن يحيى ، قال : ثنا بشر بن عمر ، قال : ثنا مالك بن أنس ، عن ابن شهاب ، عن عثمان بن إسحاق بن خرشة ، عن قبيصة بن ذؤيب ، قال :

Matn

جاءت الجدة إلى أبي بكر تسأله ميراثها ، فقال : ما لك في كتاب الله شيء ، وما علمت لك في سنة رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم شيئا ، فارجعي حتى أسأل الناس ، فسأل الناس ، فقال المغيرة بن شعبة : حضرت رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أعطاها السدس ، فقال أبو بكر : هل معك غيرك ؟ فقام محمد بن مسلمة الأنصاري ، فقال مثل ما قال المغيرة ، فأنفذه لها أبو بكر ، ثم جاءت الجدة الأخرى إلى عمر بن الخطاب رضي الله عنه ، تسأله ميراثها ، فقال : ما لك في كتاب الله شيء ، وما القضاء الذي بلغنا أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم قضى به إلا لغيرك ، وما أنا زائد في الفرائض شيئا ، ولكن هو ذلك السدس ، فإن اجتمعتما فيه فهو بينكما ، وأيكما خلت به فهو لها

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: Abu Nu‘aym narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, from ‘Abd al-Rahman b. al-Harith b. ‘Ayyash b. Abi Rabi‘a, from Hakim b. ‘Abbad b. Hunayf, from Abu Umama b. Sahl b. Hunayf, who said:

Matn

‘Umar b. al-Khattab wrote to Abu ‘Ubayda b. al-Jarrah: “Teach your boys to swim, and your fighters to shoot.” He said: They used to go back and forth between the targets. He said: Then a stray arrow came and killed a boy who was in the care of a maternal uncle of his, for whom no (paternal) kin was known. He said: So Abu ‘Ubayda wrote to ‘Umar: “To whom should I pay his blood-money?” ‘Umar (ؓ) wrote that the Messenger of God (ﷺ) used to say: “God and His Messenger are the guardian of whoever has no guardian, and the maternal uncle is the heir of whoever has no heir.”

Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: al-Haytham b. Jamil and Sulayman b. Harb narrated to us, from Hammad b. Zayd, from Budayl b. Maysara, from 'Ali b. Abi Talha, from Rashid b. Sa'd, from Abu 'Amir al-Hawzani, from al-Miqdam al-Kindi, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

And the maternal uncle is the protector of one who has no protector: he inherits his wealth and frees his captive.

Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Ubayd God b. Musa narrated to us; he said: al-Hasan b. Salih informed us, from 'Umar b. Sa'id, from 'Amr b. Shu'ayb; he said: My father informed me,

Matn

from my grandfather: that the Messenger of God ﷺ said on the day of the Conquest of Mecca: The people of two religions do not inherit from one another. A woman inherits from the blood-money and wealth of her husband, and he inherits from her blood-money and wealth, so long as neither of them has killed the other. If one kills the other, he inherits nothing from his blood-money or his wealth; and if one kills the other by mistake, he inherits from his wealth but not from his blood-money.
