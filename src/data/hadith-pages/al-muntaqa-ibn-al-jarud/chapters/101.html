Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 367
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Abd al-Razzaq narrated to me between Maghrib and 'Isha' by lamplight, on the night of the farewell; he said: Ma'mar informed us, from al-Thawri, from Yahya b. Sa'id, from Abu Bakr b. Muhammad, from Abu Salama, from Abu Hurayra, ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

When a judge exercises ijtihad and is correct, he has two rewards; and when he exercises ijtihad and errs, he has one reward.

Matn

إذا اجتهد الحاكم فأصاب فله أجران اثنان ، وإذا اجتهد فأخطأ فله أجر واحد

Entry1074/ 34
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 368
English
Isnad

Mahmud b. Adam narrated to us; he said: Waki' narrated to us, from Usama b. Zayd; he said: 'Abd Allah b. Rafi', the freedman of Umm Salama, narrated to us, from Umm Salama, ؓ, who said:

Matn

Two men of the Ansar came to the Messenger of God ﷺ disputing over inheritances between them that had become obscure, with no proof between them. The Prophet ﷺ said: You bring your disputes to me, and I am only a human being; perhaps some of you are more eloquent in his argument than others. I judge between you only according to what I hear from you. So whoever I judge something for from his brother, let him not take it, for I am only cutting off for him a piece of the Fire, which he will bring on the Day of Resurrection as a burden around his neck. He said: The two men wept, and each of them said: My right is for my brother. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: Since you have done this, go and divide it, aim for the truth, then draw lots, and then let each of you release the other (from any claim).

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا محمود بن آدم ، قال : ثنا وكيع ، عن أسامة بن زيد ، قال : ثنا عبد الله بن رافع مولى أم سلمة ، عن أم سلمة رضي الله عنها ، قالت :

Matn

جاء رجلان من الأنصار إلى رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم يختصمان في مواريث بينهما قد درست ، ليس بينهما بينة ، فقال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم : إنكم تختصمون إلي ، وإنما أنا بشر ولعل بعضكم أن يكون ألحن بحجته من بعض ، وإنما أقضي بينكم على نحو ما أسمع منكم ، فمن قضيت له من أخيه شيئا فلا يأخذه ، فإنما أقطع له قطعة من النار ، يأتي به إسطاما في عنقه يوم القيامة . قال : فبكى الرجلان ، وقال كل واحد منهما : حقي لأخي ، فقال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : أما إذ فعلتما هذا ، فاذهبا فاقتسما ، وتوخيا الحق ثم استهما ، ثم يتحلل كل واحد منكما صاحبه

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Musa b. Isma'il narrated to us; he said: Abu 'Awana narrated to us; he said:

Matn

'Abd al-Malik—meaning Ibn 'Umayr—narrated to us, from 'Alqama b. Wa'il, from his father Wa'il b. Hujr, who said: I was with the Messenger of God ﷺ when two men came to him disputing over land. One of them said: This man seized my land, O Messenger of God, in the Jahiliyya—he was Imru' al-Qays b. 'Abis al-Kindi, and his opponent was Rabi'a b. 'Aydan. He said to him: Your proof. He said: I have none. He said: His oath. He said: Then he will take it away. He said: You have only that. He said: When he stood to swear, the Messenger of God ﷺ said: Whoever wrongfully seizes land will meet God on the Day of Resurrection while He is angry with him.

العربية
Isnad
