Entry167/ 9
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 61
English
Isnad

Ahmad b. Yusuf narrated to us, saying: ‘Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us, (and) both said: Sufyan narrated to us, from ‘Abd al-Rahman b. al-Harith, who said: Hakim b. Hakim narrated to me, from Nafi‘ b. Jubayr, from Ibn ‘Abbas (ؓ), who said: The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said:

Matn

“Jibril (ﷺ) led me in prayer at the House. He prayed Dhuhr with me when the sun had declined, when (the shadow) was the length of a shoelace (shirak); then he prayed ‘Asr with me when the shadow of everything was like itself; then he prayed Maghrib with me when the fasting person breaks his fast; then he prayed ‘Isha’ with me when the twilight (shafaq) had disappeared; then he prayed Fajr with me when food and drink become forbidden to the fasting person. Then, on the next day, he prayed Dhuhr with me when the shadow of everything was like itself; then he prayed ‘Asr with me when the shadow of everything was twice itself; then he prayed Maghrib with me when the fasting person breaks his fast, at the same time (as before); then he prayed ‘Isha’ with me until the first third of the night; then he prayed Fajr with me, making it bright (asfara). Then he turned to me and said: ‘O Muhammad, this is the time of the prophets before you, and the time is between these two times.’”

Entry169/ 9
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 62
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Bazi‘ al-Naysaburi narrated to us, saying: Ishaq — meaning Ibn Yusuf al-Azraq — informed us, saying: Sufyan al-Thawri narrated to us, from ‘Alqama b. Marthad, from Sulayman b. Buraydah, from his father, who said:

Matn

A man came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and asked him about the time of prayer. He said: “Pray with us these two (days).” So he commanded Bilal when the sun had declined, and he gave the adhan; then he commanded him and he gave the iqama for Dhuhr; then he commanded him and he gave the iqama for ‘Asr while the sun was high, white and clear; then he commanded him and he gave the iqama for Maghrib when the sun had set; then he commanded him and he gave the iqama for ‘Isha’ when the twilight had disappeared; then he commanded him and he gave the iqama for Fajr when dawn broke. On the second day he commanded him to delay Dhuhr until it cooled, and he cooled it thoroughly; then he commanded him and he gave the iqama — meaning for ‘Asr — while the sun was higher than it had been; then he commanded him and he gave the iqama for Maghrib before the twilight had disappeared; then he commanded him and he gave the iqama for ‘Isha’ when a third of the night had gone; then he commanded him and he gave the iqama for Fajr, making it bright. Then he said: “Where is the one who asked about the time of prayer?” The man stood up before him, and the Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “The time of your prayer is between what you have seen.”

أتى النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم رجل فسأله عن وقت الصلاة ، فقال : صل معنا هذين ، فأمر بلالا حين زالت الشمس فأذن ، ثم أمره فأقام الظهر ، ثم أمره فأقام العصر والشمس مرتفعة بيضاء نقية ، ثم أمره فأقام المغرب حين غابت الشمس ، ثم أمره فأقام العشاء حين غاب الشفق ، ثم أمره فأقام الفجر حين طلع الفجر ، فلما كان يوم الثاني أمره أن يبرد بالظهر فأنعم أن يبرد بها ، ثم أمره فأقام - يعني : العصر - والشمس مرتفعة فوق ذلك الذي كان ، ثم أمره فأقام المغرب قبل أن يغيب الشفق ، ثم أمره فأقام العشاء حين ذهب ثلث الليل ، ثم أمره فأقام الفجر فأسفر بها ، ثم قال : أين السائل عن وقت الصلاة ؟ فقام إليه الرجل ، فقال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : وقت صلاتكم ما بين ما رأيتم
