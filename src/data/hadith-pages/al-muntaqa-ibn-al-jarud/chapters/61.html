Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 148
English

Muhammad b. Yahya and al-Hasan b. Muhammad al-Za'farani narrated to us; they both said: 'Asim b. 'Ali narrated to us; he said: Shu'ba narrated to us; he said: Abu Jamra informed me; he said: Ibn 'Abbas, ؓ, used to seat me on his couch. He said: When the delegation of 'Abd al-Qays came to the Messenger of God ﷺ, he said: “Who are the people?” or: “Who is the delegation?” They said: “From Rabi'a.” He said: “Welcome to the delegation”—or: “to the people”—“neither disgraced nor regretful.” They said: “O Messenger of God, we cannot come to you except in the sacred month, and between us and you is this tribe of the unbelievers of Mudar. So tell us something decisive that we may pass on to those we left behind, and by which we may enter Paradise.” He said: And they asked him about drinks. He said: So he commanded them to do four things and forbade them four things. He commanded them to believe in God alone. He said: “Do you know what belief in God alone is?” They said: “God and His Messenger know best.” He said: “The testimony that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is the Messenger of God, establishing the prayer, paying the zakat, fasting Ramadan, and giving a fifth of the spoils.” And he forbade them the hantam, the dubba', and the naqir—and perhaps he said—the muqayyar and the muzaffat [vessels used for fermenting drinks]. And he said: “Memorize them and tell those you left behind.”

English

Muhammad b. Yahya and Ahmad b. Yusuf narrated to us; they both said: 'Abd al-Razzaq [narrated to us]; he said: Ibn Jurayj informed us, from 'Amr b. Dinar, that he heard Muhammad b. Hunayn say: Ibn 'Abbas, ؓ, used to object to anticipating the fast of Ramadan when the new moon of Ramadan had not been seen. He would say: The Prophet ﷺ said: “If you do not see the new moon, complete thirty nights.”

English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Asad b. Musa narrated to us; he said: Mu'awiya—meaning Ibn Salih—narrated to us, from 'Abd Allah b. Abi Qays, who said: I was sent to 'A'isha, ؓ, to ask her about fasting Ramadan when the new moon is hidden, and about prayer after the 'Asr. I entered upon 'A'isha and said: “So-and-so sends you greetings. He sent me to you to ask about prayer after the 'Asr, about continuous fasting, and about fasting in the month of Ramadan.” He mentioned part of the hadith. She said: He used to take more care in counting Sha'ban than he did with any other month. Then he would fast on sighting [the new moon of] Ramadan, and if it was hidden from him by clouds, he counted thirty [days of Sha'ban] and then fasted—she means the Messenger of God ﷺ.

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 150
English

Ibrahim b. Marzuq informed us, saying: Rawh narrated to us, saying: Sa‘id ibn Abi ‘Aruba narrated to us, from Qatada, from ‘Azra, from Sa‘id ibn Jubayr, from Ibn ‘Abbas (ؓ), who said: A concession was granted to the very old man and the very old woman, though they were able to fast, to break the fast if they wished, or to feed a poor person each day, with no making up required of them. Then that was abrogated by this verse: {So whoever of you witnesses the month, let him fast it}. And it remained in force for the very old man and the very old woman when they are unable to fast, and for the pregnant and the nursing woman: when they fear (for themselves or their child), they break the fast and feed a poor person each day.
