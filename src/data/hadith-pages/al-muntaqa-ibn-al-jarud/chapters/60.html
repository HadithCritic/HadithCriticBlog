English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: 'Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us, saying: Ibn Jurayj informed us, saying: Abu al-Zubayr informed me

Matn

that he heard Jabir b. 'Abd Allah, ؓ, say: I heard the Messenger of God, ﷺ, say: "There is no owner of camels who does not fulfil their due, except that on the Day of Resurrection they will come in greater numbers than they ever were, and he will be seated for them in a broad level plain, and they will trample him with their legs and their hooves. And no owner of cattle who does not fulfil their due, except that on the Day of Resurrection they will come in greater numbers than they ever were, and he will be seated for them in a broad level plain; they will gore him with their horns and trample him with their legs. And no owner of sheep who does not fulfil their due, except that on the Day of Resurrection they will come in greater numbers than they ever were, and he will be seated for them in a broad level plain; they will gore him with their horns and trample him with their hooves; none among them will be hornless, nor with broken horns. And no owner of a hoarded treasure who does not fulfil its due, except that his treasure will come on the Day of Resurrection as a bald-headed serpent (shuja' aqra') following him with its mouth open; when it reaches him he flees from it, and it calls out to him: 'Take your treasure that you hid, for I have no need of it.' And when he sees that there is no escaping it, he puts his hand into its mouth, and it crunches it as a stallion crunches."

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Muhammad b. 'Abd Allah b. al-Muthanna b. 'Abd Allah b. Anas b. Malik al-Ansari narrated to us; he said: my father narrated to me, from Thumama b. 'Abd Allah b. Anas,

Matn

that he heard Anas b. Malik, ؓ, say: Abu Bakr al-Siddiq sent me to Bahrain and wrote for me this document: “In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. This is the obligation of zakat that the Messenger of God ﷺ imposed upon the Muslims, which God commanded His Messenger ﷺ [to impose]. Whoever among the believers is asked for it according to its proper requirements, let him give it, and whoever is asked for more than that, let him not give it. For twenty-four camels or fewer, [it is paid in] sheep: one sheep for every five. When they reach twenty-five up to thirty-five, then [the due] is a bint makhad, a female [camel in her second year]; if there is no female bint makhad, then a male b. labun. When they reach thirty-six up to forty-five, then [the due] is a bint labun. When they reach forty-six up to sixty, then [the due] is a hiqqa fit to be covered by the male. When they reach sixty-one up to seventy-five, then [the due] is a jadha'a. When they reach seventy-six up to ninety, then [the due] is two bint labun. When they reach ninety-one up to one hundred and twenty, then [the due] is two hiqqas fit to be covered by the male. When they exceed one hundred and twenty, then for every forty a bint labun and for every fifty a hiqqa. When the ages of the camels differ in the obligations of zakat: whoever’s zakat reaches the jadha'a and he has no jadha'a but has a hiqqa, the hiqqa is accepted from him and he adds with it two sheep, if they are easily available, or twenty dirhams. Whoever’s zakat reaches the hiqqa and he has no hiqqa but has a jadha'a, the jadha'a is accepted from him and the collector gives him twenty dirhams or two sheep. Whoever’s zakat reaches the hiqqa and he has only a bint labun, the bint labun is accepted from him and he gives with it two sheep or twenty dirhams. Whoever’s zakat reaches a bint labun and he has none but has a hiqqa, the hiqqa is accepted from him and the collector gives him twenty dirhams or two sheep. Whoever’s zakat reaches a bint labun and he has none but has a bint makhad, the bint makhad is accepted from him and he gives with it twenty dirhams or two sheep. Whoever’s zakat reaches a bint makhad and he has none but has a bint labun, the bint labun is accepted from him and the collector gives him twenty dirhams or two sheep. Whoever does not have a bint makhad as required but has a male b. labun, the b. labun is accepted from him and nothing accompanies it. Whoever has only four camels owes nothing on them unless their owner wishes, and when they reach five camels, [the due] is one sheep. On the zakat of sheep and goats that graze freely: when they number forty, [the due] is one sheep, up to one hundred and twenty. When they exceed one hundred and twenty up to two hundred, [the due] is two sheep. When they exceed two hundred up to three hundred, [the due] is three sheep. When they exceed three hundred, then for every hundred, one sheep. No aged animal, no animal with a defect, and no male goat is to be taken in zakat unless the collector wishes. Animals that are separate are not to be combined, nor those that are combined separated, out of fear of zakat. What belongs to two partners, they settle between themselves equally. English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Ibn Abi Maryam narrated to us; he said: Muhammad—meaning Ibn Ja'far—informed us; he said: al-'Ala' informed me, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“When a person dies, his deeds cease, except for three: ongoing charity, knowledge from which people benefit, or a righteous child who prays for him.”

Matn

إذا مات الإنسان انقطع عنه عمله إلا من ثلاثة : صدقة جارية ، أو علم ينتفع به ، أو ولد صالح يدعو له

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 147
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Abu l-Walid narrated to us; he said: Hammad b. Salama narrated to us, from Humayd, from al-Hasan, from 'Uthman b. Abi l-'As, ؓ:

Matn

that a delegation of Thaqif came to the Messenger of God ﷺ, and he lodged them in the mosque so that their hearts would be softened. They stipulated to the Prophet ﷺ that they would not be mustered [for war], would not be tithed, and would not be made to bow [in prayer], and that no one from outside their own people would be appointed over them. The Prophet ﷺ said: “You will not be mustered, you will not be tithed, and no one other than your own will be appointed over you, but there is no good in a religion that has no bowing.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا محمد بن يحيى ، قال : ثنا أبو الوليد قال : ثنا حماد بن سلمة ، عن حميد ، عن الحسن ، عن عثمان بن أبي العاص رضي الله عنه :

Matn

أن وفد ثقيف قدموا على رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فأنزلهم المسجد ليكون أرق لقلوبهم ، فاشترطوا على النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم أن لا يحشروا ، ولا يعشروا ، ولا يجبوا ، ولا يستعمل عليهم من غيرهم ، فقال النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم : لا تحشرون ، ولا تعشرون ، ولا يستعمل عليكم غيركم ، ولا خير في دين ليس فيه ركوع
