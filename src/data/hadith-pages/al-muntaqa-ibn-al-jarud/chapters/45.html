Entry274/ 6
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 101
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Mutarrif narrated to us, and I read it to Ibn Nafi', from Malik, from Zayd b. Aslam, from 'Ata' b. Yasar, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Abbas, ؓ, who said:

Matn

The sun was eclipsed in the time of the Messenger of God ﷺ. The Messenger of God prayed, and the people with him. He stood for a long time, about the length of Surat al-Baqara, then bowed for a long time, then rose and stood for a long time, shorter than the first standing, then bowed for a long time, shorter than the first bowing, then prostrated. Ibn Yahya said: Perhaps they both said, “then he rose,” or perhaps they did not say it. Then he stood for a long time, shorter than the first standing, then bowed for a long time, shorter than the first bowing, then rose and stood for a long time, shorter than the first standing, then bowed for a long time, shorter than the first bowing, then prostrated. Then he finished, and the sun had cleared. He said: “The sun and the moon are two signs among the signs of God. They are not eclipsed for the death of anyone or for his life. So when you see that, remember God.” They said: “O Messenger of God, we saw you reach for something in this place of yours, then we saw you draw back.” He said: “I saw Paradise—or: I was shown Paradise—and I reached for a bunch of grapes from it. Had I taken it, you would have eaten from it for as long as the world remains. And I saw the Fire, and I have never seen a sight like today’s. I saw that most of its inhabitants are women.” They said: “Why, O Messenger of God?” He said: “Because of their ingratitude.” It was said: “Do they disbelieve in God?” He said: “They are ungrateful to their spouse and ungrateful for kindness. If you treat one of them well for all time, then she sees something [she dislikes] from you, she says: ‘I have never seen any good from you.’”

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 102
English
Isnad

Bahr b. Nasr narrated to us, from Ibn Wahb, from Yunus b. Yazid, from Ibn Shihab, who said: ‘Urwa b. al-Zubayr informed me, from ‘A’isha, the wife of the Prophet (ﷺ), who said:

Matn

The sun was eclipsed during the lifetime of the Messenger of God (ﷺ), so the Messenger of God (ﷺ) went out to the mosque and stood and said the takbir, and the people formed rows behind him. The Messenger of God (ﷺ) recited at length, then said the takbir and bowed for a long bowing, then raised his head and said: “God hears whoever praises Him; our Lord, and to You belongs the praise.” Then he stood and recited at length, but less than the first recitation; then he said the takbir and bowed for a long bowing, which was shorter than the first bowing; then he said: “God hears whoever praises Him; our Lord, and to You belongs the praise.” Then he did the same in the other rak‘a, completing four bowings and four prostrations, and the sun cleared before he finished. Then he stood and addressed the people, praising God as He deserves, and then said: “The sun and the moon are two signs among the signs of God; they are not eclipsed for the death of anyone nor for his life. So when you see them, hasten to prayer.”

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 103
English
Isnad

Harun b. Ishaq narrated to us; 'Abda narrated to us, from Hisham b. 'Urwa, from his father, from 'A'isha, ؓ, who said:

Matn

The sun was eclipsed in the time of the Messenger of God ﷺ. The Messenger of God ﷺ stood and prayed, and made the standing very long. Then he bowed and made the bowing very long. Then he rose and made the standing long, shorter than the first standing. Then he bowed, shorter than the first bowing. Then he prostrated. Then he stood and made the standing long, shorter than the first standing. Then he bowed, shorter than the first bowing. Then he raised his head and stood, shorter than the first standing. Then he bowed, shorter than the first bowing. Then he prostrated and finished his prayer, and the sun had cleared. He stood and addressed the people, praised God and extolled Him, then said: “The sun and the moon are two signs among the signs of God. They are not eclipsed for the death of anyone or for his life. So when you see that, pray, give charity, and remember God.” Then he said: “O community of Muhammad, by God, no one has more protective jealousy than God that His male servant commit fornication or His female servant commit fornication. O community of Muhammad, if you knew what I know, you would weep much and laugh little.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا هارون بن إسحاق ، ثنا عبدة ، عن هشام بن عروة ، عن أبيه ، عن عائشة رضي الله عنها ، قالت :

Matn

خسفت الشمس على عهد رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فقام رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فصلى فأطال القيام جدا ، ثم ركع فأطال الركوع جدا ، ثم رفع فأطال القيام ، وهو دون القيام الأول ، ثم ركع ، وهو دون الركوع الأول ، ثم سجد ، ثم قام فأطال القيام ، وهو دون القيام الأول ، ثم ركع ، وهو دون الركوع الأول ، ثم رفع رأسه فقام ، وهو دون القيام الأول ، ثم ركع ، وهو دون الركوع الأول ، ثم سجد ففرغ من صلاته ، وقد جلي عن الشمس ، فقام فخطب الناس فحمد الله ، وأثنى عليه ، ثم قال : إن الشمس والقمر آيتان من آيات الله لا ينخسفان لموت أحد ، ولا لحياته ، فإذا رأيتم ذلك فصلوا ، وتصدقوا ، واذكروا الله ، ثم قال : يا أمة محمد ، والله ما من أحد أغير من الله أن يزني عبده أو تزني أمته ، يا أمة محمد لو تعلمون ما أعلم لبكيتم كثيرا ، ولضحكتم قليلا .
