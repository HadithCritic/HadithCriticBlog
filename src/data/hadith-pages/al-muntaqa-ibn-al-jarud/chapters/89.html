Entry963/ 7
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 332
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: ‘Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us, saying: al-Thawri informed us, from his father, from ‘Abaya b. Rifa‘a, from Rafi‘ b. Khadij (ؓ), who said: We were with the Messenger of God (ﷺ) at Dhu al-Hulayfa in Tihama, and the people took some sheep and camels as spoils; they hurried with them and set the pots boiling. The Prophet (ﷺ) came upon them and commanded that the pots be overturned, and he equated ten sheep with one camel. He said: A camel of theirs bolted, and a man shot it with an arrow and stopped it, and the Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “These animals have wild traits like the traits of wild beasts; so whichever of them overpowers you, do this with it.” He said: Then Rafi‘ b. Khadij came to him and said: “O Messenger of God, we fear — or: we hope — that we will meet the enemy tomorrow, and we have no knives with us; may we slaughter with reeds?” The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “Whatever makes the blood flow and the name of God has been mentioned over it, eat it — but not (if done with) tooth or nail. And I will tell you: as for the tooth, it is bone; and as for the nail, it is the knife of the Abyssinians.” Then he said: “A water-carrying camel fell into a well in Medina and was slaughtered at its flank (shakila)” — meaning its side — “and Ibn ‘Umar (ؓ) took a tenth (‘ushayr) of it for two dirhams.”

Matn

Entry964/ 7
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 333
English

Ahmad b. Sa‘id al-Darimi narrated to us, saying: Habban — that is, Ibn Hilal — narrated to us, saying: Jarir — that is, Ibn Hazim — narrated to us, saying: Ayyub used to narrate to me from Zayd b. Aslam, so I met Zayd and asked him, and he said: ‘Ata’ b. Yasar narrated to me, from Abu Sa‘id al-Khudri (ؓ), who said: A man of the Ansar had a she-camel grazing in the direction of Uhud; something befell her, so he slaughtered her with a peg. I said to Zayd: “Of iron or of wood?” He said: “No, of wood.” He said: Then he came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and asked him about it, and he commanded him to eat it.

Ibrahim b. 'Abd Allah narrated to us; he said: Yazid b. Harun informed us; he said: Yahya informed us, from Nafi', from Ibn 'Umar, ؓ: that a woman used to pasture sheep belonging to Ka'b b. Malik at Sal', and she feared that one of the sheep would die, so she took a stone and slaughtered it with it. That was mentioned to the Messenger of God ﷺ, and he commanded them to eat it.

English

Abu Sa‘id al-Ashajj narrated to us, from Hafs b. Ghiyath, who said: Khalid al-Hadhdha’ narrated to us, from Abu Qilaba, from Abu al-Ash‘ath al-San‘ani, from Shaddad b. Aws (ؓ), who said: The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “Indeed, God has prescribed excellence in everything; so when you kill, kill well, and when you slaughter, slaughter well; let one of you sharpen his blade and spare his animal suffering.”

Ishaq b. Mansur narrated to us; he said: 'Abd al-Rahman—meaning Ibn Mahdi—informed us; he said: Hammad b. Salama narrated to us, from Abu l-'Ushara', from his father: I said: O Messenger of God, is slaughter (dhakah) not only in the throat and the upper chest?
