Entry1018/ 7
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 350
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Muhammad b. 'Ubayd narrated to us; he said: 'Ubayd God narrated to us, from Nafi', from Ibn 'Umar, ؓ, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: It is not right for a Muslim man who has something to bequeath to spend two nights without his will being written and kept with him.

Matn

ما حق امرئ مسلم يبيت ليلتين وله شيء يوصي فيه إلا ووصيته مكتوبة عنده

Entry1019/ 7
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 350
English

Ibn al-Muqri' narrated to us; he said: Sufyan narrated to us, from al-Zuhri; he said: 'Amir b. Sa'd narrated to us, from his father, ؓ, who said: I fell ill in Mecca with an illness from which I came near to death, and the Messenger of God ﷺ came to visit me. I said: O Messenger of God, I have much wealth, and none will inherit from me except my daughter. May I bequeath two-thirds of my wealth? He said: No. I said: Half? He said: No. I said: A third? He said: A third—and a third is much, or large. For you to leave your heirs wealthy is better than to leave them dependent.

Entry1020/ 7
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 351
English

'Ali b. Khashram narrated to us; he said: Isma'il—meaning Ibn 'Ulayya—informed us, from Ayyub, from Abu Qilaba, from Abu l-Muhallab, from 'Imran b. Husayn, ؓ: that a man freed six slaves of his at the time of his death, and he had no wealth other than them. The Messenger of God ﷺ called them, divided them into three parts, then cast lots among them, freeing two and keeping four in slavery. He said: And the Messenger of God ﷺ spoke harshly to him.

Entry1021/ 7
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 351
English

Abu Ayyub Sulayman b. 'Abd al-Hamid al-Bahrani narrated to us; he said: Yazid b. 'Abd Rabbih narrated to us; he said: al-Walid b. Muslim narrated to us; he said: Ibn Jabir narrated to us; and Sulaym b. 'Amir and others narrated to me, from Abu Umama—and others who attended the sermon of the Messenger of God ﷺ on that day—and among what he said was: Behold, God has given every rightful person his right. Behold, there is no bequest for an heir.

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا أبو أيوب سليمان بن عبد الحميد البهراني ، قال : ثنا يزيد بن عبد ربه ، قال : ثنا الوليد بن مسلم ، قال : ثنا ابن جابر ، وحدثني سليم بن عامر وغيره ، عن أبي أمامة - وغيره ممن شهد خطبة رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم يومئذ - فكان فيما تكلم به :

Matn

ألا إن الله قد أعطى كل ذي حق حقه ، ألا لا وصية لوارث

Entry1022/ 7
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 351
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: Abu ‘Amir al-‘Aqadi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, from Abu Ishaq, from al-Harith, from ‘Ali (ؓ), who said: The Messenger of God (ﷺ) ruled that a debt takes precedence over a bequest — and you recite it: {after any bequest that he may have made, or a debt} — and the Messenger of God (ﷺ) ruled that inheritance goes to the sons of the same mother and father, to the exclusion of the sons of different mothers (banu al-‘allat).

العربية
Isnad

Matn

قضى رسول الله بالدين قبل الوصية ، وأنتم تقرؤونها { من بعد وصية يوصي بها أو دين } وقضى رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم بالميراث لبني الأم والأب ، دون بني العلات

English

Abu Sa'id al-Ashajj and Harun b. Ishaq narrated to us; they both said: 'Abda narrated to us, from Hisham b. 'Urwa, from his father, from 'A'isha, ؓ, regarding His saying: {and whoever is poor, let him eat in a fair manner}; she said: It was revealed concerning the guardian of an orphan, who manages his affairs and looks after him: if he is in need, he may eat from it.

Entry1024/ 7
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 352
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Abd Allah b. 'Abd al-Wahhab al-Hajabi narrated to us; he said: Khalid b. al-Harith narrated to us; he said: Husayn al-Mu'allim narrated to us, from 'Amr b. Shu'ayb, from his father, from his grandfather: that a man came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: I am poor and have nothing, and I have an orphan. He said: Eat from the wealth of your orphan, without extravagance or squandering—or wastefulness; al-Hajabi was unsure—and without accumulating (wealth for yourself).
