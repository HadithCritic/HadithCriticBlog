'Abd Allah b. Hashim narrated to us; he said: Yahya b. Sa'id narrated to us, from Malik; he said: al-Zuhri narrated to me, from Sa'id b. al-Musayyab, from Abu Hurayra, ؓ, from the Prophet ﷺ, who said: “The congregational prayer exceeds the prayer of a man alone by twenty-five degrees.”

Ibn al-Muqri' narrated to us; he said: Sufyan narrated to us, from Abu l-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “I have considered ordering men to begin the prayer, then ordering my young men to go to the people who do not attend it and burn their houses down on them with bundles of firewood. If one of them knew that he would find a fat bone or two fine sheep’s trotters, he would attend the 'Isha' prayer.”

English

Yusuf b. Musa al-Qattan narrated to us; he said: Jarir narrated to us, from al-A'mash, from Isma'il b. Raja', from Aws b. Dam'aj, from Abu Mas'ud al-Ansari, ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “The people are led in prayer by the one who recites the Book of God best. If they are equal in recitation, then by the one who knows the Sunna best. If they are equal in the Sunna, then by the one who emigrated earliest. If they are equal in emigration, then by the oldest in age. A man must not lead another in the latter’s domain of authority, nor sit in his house on his seat of honor, except with his permission.”

English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Abu l-Nu'man narrated to us; he said: Hammad b. Zayd narrated to us, from Ayyub; he said: 'Amr b. Salama Abu Yazid al-Jarmi narrated to us; he said: We lived at a watering place on the path of travelers, and we used to ask them: “What is this matter?” He mentioned part of the hadith. He said: My father set out with the Islam of the people of our encampment. He said: He stayed with the Prophet ﷺ as long as God willed that he stay. He said: Then he returned, and when he drew near, we went out to meet him. When we saw him, he said: “By God, I have come to you from the Messenger of God ﷺ in truth!” Then he said: “He commands you to do such and such and forbids you from such and such, and to pray such and such a prayer at such and such a time. When the prayer is due, let one of you call the adhan, then let the one among you who knows the most Qur'an lead you.” The people of our encampment looked, and found no one who knew more Qur'an than I, because of what I had memorized from the riders. He said: So they put me forward in front of them, and I used to lead them in prayer when I was six years old.

Entry341/ 10
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 123
English

Ibn al-Muqri' narrated to us; he said: Sufyan narrated to us, from Abu Hazim, who heard Sahl b. Sa'd al-Sa'idi say: A dispute arose between two clans of the Ansar over something that had been between them in the Jahiliyya, until Satan stirred things up between them—and once he said: until they came to blows. The Prophet ﷺ was told, so he went to them and was detained there. Bilal called the adhan, and when the Prophet ﷺ was late and did not come, Bilal called the iqama, and Abu Bakr, ؓ, stepped forward. While Abu Bakr was leading the people, the Messenger of God ﷺ came, making his way through the rows until he reached the first row. Abu Bakr did not turn around in his prayer, so the people clapped their hands like this. When he heard the clapping, he turned, and it was the Messenger of God ﷺ. The Prophet ﷺ gestured to him to stay where he was—and once he said: he raised his head to the sky—and Abu Bakr stepped backward, and the Prophet ﷺ stepped forward. When he finished his prayer, he said: “What kept you, Abu Bakr, from staying in place?” He said: “It was not fitting for the son of Abu Quhafa to stand in front of the Prophet of God ﷺ.”

صلى رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم على المنبر يوما والناس وراءه ، فجعل يصلي فيركع ، ثم يرفع يرجع القهقرى ، ويسجد على الأرض ، ثم يرجع فيرتقي عليه ، كلما سجد نزل ، فلما فرغ ، قال : أيها الناس ، إني إنما صليت لكم هكذا كما تروني فتأتمون بي
