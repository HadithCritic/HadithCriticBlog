Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 282
English
Isnad

Ishaq b. Mansur narrated to us, saying: Hammad [ibn] Mas‘ada narrated to us, from Sa‘d b. Ishaq, from his paternal aunt Zaynab bint Ka‘b, from al-Furay‘a bint Malik: that her husband went out in search of some slaves (a‘laj) of his, and caught up with them at al-Qadum, and they leapt upon him and killed him. She came to the Messenger of God (ﷺ) and told him,

Matn

and mentioned that she was in a house far from her family and that she wished to move to them, and he permitted her. She said: So I went out, and when I was in the apartments — or she said: when I had passed beyond the apartments — he called me, or she said: he sent for me, and he called me and said to me: “Observe your waiting period in your husband’s house, the one in which the news of his death reached you, until the prescribed term reaches its end.” She said: When it was the time of ‘Uthman (ؓ), he sent for me and asked me, and I told him.

Matn

أن زوجها خرج في طلب أعلاج له فأدركهم بالقدوم ، فوثبوا عليه فقتلوه ، وأنها جاءت رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم فذكرت له ، وذكرت أنها في منزل شاسع عن أهلها ، وأنها تريد التحول إليهم فأذن لها . قالت : فخرجت حتى إذا كنت في الحجرات أو قالت : جاوزت الحجرات دعاني ، أو قالت : أرسل إلي ، فدعاني فقال لي : اعتدي في بيت زوجك الذي جاءك فيه نعيه ، حتى يبلغ الكتاب أجله . قالت : فلما كان زمن عثمان رضي الله عنه بعث إلي ، فسألني فحدثته

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 282
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Mutarrif narrated to us; he said: Malik b. Anas narrated to us (h). And Ahmad b. Nasr narrated to us; he said: Muhammad b. Harb and 'Abd al-'Aziz b. 'Abd Allah al-Uwaysi informed us, from Malik, from 'Abd Allah b. Yazid,

Matn

the freedman of al-Aswad b. Sufyan, from Abu Salama b. 'Abd al-Rahman, from Fatima bint Qays: that Abu 'Amr b. Hafs divorced her irrevocably while he was away. His agent sent her barley, and she was displeased with it. He said: By God, you have no claim upon us at all. So she came to the Messenger of God ﷺ and mentioned that to him. He said: You have no maintenance from him. And he commanded her to observe her waiting period in the house of Umm Sharik. Then he said: That is a woman whom my Companions visit frequently, so observe your waiting period at the house of Ibn Umm Maktum, for he is a blind man, and you may put down your garments. When you have become free (to marry), let me know. She said: When I became free, I mentioned to him that Mu'awiya b. Abi Sufyan and Abu Jahm had proposed to me. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: As for Abu Jahm, he does not put his staff down from his shoulder, and as for Mu'awiya, he is a pauper with no wealth. Marry Usama b. Zayd. She said: I disliked it. Then he said: Marry Usama b. Zayd. So I married him, and God placed good in him, and I was glad to have him.

Entry823/ 13
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 283
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Yazid b. Harun narrated to us; he said: Yahya—meaning Ibn Sa'id—informed us that Sulayman b. Yasar informed him that Abu Salama informed him:

Matn

that he and Ibn 'Abbas were together at Abu Hurayra's, ؓ, and they mentioned a man who dies leaving a wife, and she gives birth a few nights after him. Ibn 'Abbas, ؓ, said: Her release (from the waiting period) is the later of the two terms. Abu Salama said: When she has given birth, she has become free. They went back and forth about that between them. Abu Hurayra said: I am with my nephew—meaning Abu Salama. So they sent Kurayb, the freedman of Ibn 'Abbas, to Umm Salama, and he asked her. Umm Salama mentioned that Subay'a bint al-Harith al-Aslamiyya's husband died and left her, and she gave birth after him by some nights, and that a man of Banu 'Abd al-Dar, known as Abu l-Sanabil b. Ba'kak, proposed to her and told her that she had become free. She wished to marry someone else, so Abu l-Sanabil said to her: You have not become free. Subay'a mentioned that to the Messenger of God ﷺ, and he commanded her to marry.
