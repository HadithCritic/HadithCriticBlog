English
Isnad

Yusuf b. Musa narrated to us; he said: Sufyan narrated to us, from 'Abd Allah b. Dinar, from Ibn 'Umar, ؓ, from the Prophet ﷺ, who said:

Matn

“The two parties to a sale have the option as long as they have not separated, or unless their sale was made with an option.”

Matn

البيعان بالخيار ما لم يتفرقا ، أو يكون بيعهما عن خيار

Entry674/ 25
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 231
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. ‘Abdullah b. ‘Abd al-Hakam informed us that Ibn Wahb informed them, saying: al-Layth b. Sa‘d informed me that Nafi‘ narrated to him, from Ibn ‘Umar (ؓ):

Matn

that the Prophet (ﷺ) said: “When two men trade with each other, each of them has the option so long as they have not separated and are still together, or one gives the other the choice, and they trade on that basis; then the sale has become binding. And if they separate after having traded, and neither of them has abandoned the sale, then the sale has become binding.”

العربية
Isnad

أخبرنا محمد بن عبد الله بن عبد الحكم أن ابن وهب أخبرهم قال : أخبرني الليث بن سعد أن نافعا حدثه عن ابن عمر رضي الله عنهما أن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم قال :

Matn

إذا تبايع الرجلان فكل واحد منهما بالخيار ما لم يتفرقا وكانا جميعا ، أو يخير أحدهما الآخر ، فتبايعا على ذلك فقد وجب البيع ، وإن تفرقا بعد أن تبايعا ولم يترك واحد منهما البيع فقد وجب البيع

Entry675/ 25
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 231
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: ‘Affan b. Muslim narrated to us, saying: Hammad b. Zayd narrated to us, saying: Jamil b. Murra narrated to us, from Abu al-Wadi‘, who said:

Matn

We went on a military expedition of ours and stopped at a halting place. A companion of ours sold a horse to a man in exchange for a slave, and they stayed the rest of that day and night until morning. He said: When it was time to set out, the man got up to saddle his horse and regretted (the sale). He said: But the other man held him to the sale. So they came to Abu Barza (ؓ) and related their case to him, and he said: “Would you be satisfied if I judged between you with the judgment of the Messenger of God (ﷺ)? The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: ‘The two parties to a sale have the option so long as they have not separated.’”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا محمد بن يحيى ، قال : ثنا عفان بن مسلم ، قال : ثنا حماد بن زيد ، قال : ثنا جميل بن مرة ، عن أبي الوضي ، قال :

Matn

غزونا غزاة لنا فنزلنا منزلا ، فباع صاحب لنا فرسا من رجل بعبد ، فلبثا بقية يومهما وليلتهما حتى أصبحا . قال : فلما حضر الرحل قام الرجل إلى فرسه ليسرجه وندم ، قال : فأخذه الرجل بالبيعة ، فأتيا أبا برزة رضي الله عنه فقصا عليه قصتهما ، فقال : أترضيان أن أقضي بينكما بقضاء رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ؟ قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : البيعان بالخيار ما لم يتفرقا

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 233
English
Isnad

Abu Zur‘a al-Razi narrated to us, saying: Muhammad b. Sa‘id narrated to us, saying: ‘Amr b. Qays narrated to us, from ‘Umar b. Qays al-Masir, from al-Qasim b. ‘Abd al-Rahman, from his father, who said:

Matn

‘Abdullah b. Mas‘ud sold al-Ash‘ath b. Qays some captives from the captives of the state for twenty thousand, and he (al-Ash‘ath) brought him ten thousand. He (Ibn Mas‘ud) said: “I only sold them to you for twenty thousand.” He said: “I only took them for ten thousand.” He said: “Then I am content to abide by your opinion in this.” Ibn Mas‘ud said: “If you wish, I will narrate to you from the Messenger of God (ﷺ), and I will do so.” He said: “Yes.” He said: The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “When two parties to a sale make a sale between whom there are no witnesses, the word is that of the seller, or else they return the sale to one another.” Al-Ash‘ath said: “Then I return it to you.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا أبو زرعة الرازي ، قال : ثنا محمد بن سعيد قال : ثنا عمرو بن قيس ، عن عمر بن قيس الماصر ، عن القاسم بن عبد الرحمن ، عن أبيه ، قال :

Matn

باع عبد الله بن مسعود الأشعث بن قيس سبيا من سبي الإمارة بعشرين ألفا ، فجاءه بعشرة آلاف ، فقال : إنما بعتك بعشرين ألفا ، قال : إنما أخذتها بعشرة آلاف ، قال : فإني أرضى في ذلك برأيك ، فقال ابن مسعود : إن شئت حدثتك عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم فعلت ، قال : أجل ، قال : قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : إذا تبايع المتبايعان بيعا ليس بينهما شهود فالقول ما قال البائع ، أو يترادان البيع . قال الأشعث : فإني قد رددت عليك

Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Umar b. Hafs b. Ghiyath narrated to us; he said: my father narrated to us, from Abu 'Umays; he said: 'Abd al-Rahman b. Qays b. Muhammad b. al-Ash'ath informed me, from his father, from his grandfather, who said:

Matn

al-Ash'ath bought slaves from among the slaves of the fifth from 'Abd Allah for twenty thousand. 'Abd Allah sent to him for their price, and he said: “I took them only for ten thousand.” 'Abd Allah said: “Then choose a man to judge between you and me.” al-Ash'ath said: “You are the judge between me and yourself.” 'Abd Allah said: “I heard the Prophet ﷺ say: ‘When the two parties to a sale disagree and there is no proof between them, then what the owner of the goods says stands, or they abandon the transaction.’”

Matn

Isnad

Hamza b. Malik b. Hamza al-Aslami narrated to us; he said:

Matn

Sufyan—meaning Ibn Hamza—narrated to me, from Kathir, from al-Walid b. Rabah, from Abu Hurayra, ؓ, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Settlement is permissible among the Muslims.”

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Abd Allah b. 'Abd al-Hakam informed us that Ibn Wahb informed them; he said: Ibn Jurayj informed us that Abu l-Zubayr al-Makki informed him, from Jabir b. 'Abd Allah, ؓ, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“If you sell dates to your brother and a calamity strikes them, it is not lawful for you to take anything from him. On what grounds would you take your brother’s money without right?”

Matn
