Entry793/ 12
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 270
English
Isnad

al-Hasan b. Muhammad al-Za‘farani narrated to us, saying: Hajjaj b. Muhammad narrated to us, saying: Ibn Jurayj said: Abu al-Zubayr informed me

Matn

that he heard ‘Abd al-Rahman b. Ayman, the freedman of ‘Urwa, asking Ibn ‘Umar — while Abu al-Zubayr was listening — he said: “What is your view concerning a man who divorced his wife while she was menstruating?” He said: ‘Abdullah divorced his wife while she was menstruating in the time of the Prophet (ﷺ), so ‘Umar asked the Prophet (ﷺ) and said: “‘Abdullah b. ‘Umar divorced his wife while she was menstruating.” The Prophet (ﷺ) said: “Let him take her back.” And he returned her to me, and said: “When she is pure, let him then divorce her or keep her.” Ibn ‘Umar (ؓ) said: And the Prophet (ﷺ) recited: {O Prophet, when you divorce women, divorce them for their waiting period} — at the onset of their waiting period.

Entry794/ 12
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 270
English
Isnad

794 (m) - And al-Za‘farani narrated to us — and the wording is his — saying: Muhammad b. ‘Ubayd narrated to us, saying: ‘Ubaydullah narrated to us, from Nafi‘, from Ibn ‘Umar (ؓ), who said:

Matn

I divorced my wife in the time of the Messenger of God (ﷺ) while she was menstruating, and ‘Umar mentioned that to the Messenger of God (ﷺ). The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “Order him to take her back until she is pure, then she menstruates another time; then when she is pure, let him divorce her if he wishes, before having intercourse with her, or keep her; for that is the waiting period for which God commanded that women be divorced.”

English
Isnad

Yusuf b. Musa al-Qattan and al-Hasan b. Muhammad al-Za'farani narrated to us; they both said: Waki' b. al-Jarrah narrated to us (h). And Muhammad b. Isma'il al-Ahmasi narrated to us; he said: Waki' narrated to us, from Sufyan, from Muhammad b. 'Abd al-Rahman,

Matn

the freedman of the family of Talha, from Salim, from Ibn 'Umar, ؓ: that he divorced his wife during menstruation—and al-Za'farani said: while she was menstruating. 'Umar mentioned that to the Prophet ﷺ, who said: “Order him to take her back, then divorce her when she is pure or pregnant.” Yusuf said: 'Umar asked the Prophet ﷺ.

Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Awf b. Sufyan al-Ta'i narrated to us; he said: Duhaym narrated to us; he said: al-Walid narrated to us; he said: al-Awza'i narrated to us; he said:

Matn

I asked al-Zuhri: “Which of the wives of the Messenger of God ﷺ sought refuge from him?” He said: 'Urwa b. al-Zubayr informed me, from 'A'isha, ؓ, that when the daughter of al-Jawn entered upon the Messenger of God ﷺ and he drew near to her, she said: “I seek refuge in God from you.” The Messenger of God ﷺ said: “You have sought refuge with One Great. Go back to your family.” al-Zuhri said: “Go back to your family” is a single divorce.

English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Uthman b. 'Umar narrated to us; he said: Yunus narrated to us, from al-Zuhri, from Abu Salama, that 'A'isha, ؓ, said:

Matn

When the Messenger of God ﷺ was commanded to give his wives the choice, he began with me and said: “I will tell you something, and you need not hurry until you consult your parents.” Then he said: “God has said: ‘O Prophet, say to your wives: If you desire the life of this world’”—until he reached—“‘then God has prepared for those of you who do good a great reward.’” I said: “About what of this should I consult my parents? I desire God, His Messenger, and the Home of the Hereafter.” She said: Then the wives of the Prophet ﷺ did the same as I did.

Matn

لما أمر رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم بتخيير أزواجه ، بدأ بي فقال : إني مخبرك خبرا ولا عليك ألا تعجلي حتى تستأمري أبويك ، ثم قال : إن الله قال : { يا أيها النبي قل لأزواجك إن كنتن تردن الحياة الدنيا } - حتى بلغ - { فإن الله أعد للمحسنات منكن أجرا عظيما } ، فقلت : في أي هذا أستأمر أبوي ؟ فإني أريد الله ورسوله والدار الآخرة ، قالت : ثم فعل أزواج النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم مثل ما فعلت .

Ishaq b. Ibrahim b. 'Abd al-Rahman narrated to us; he said:

Matn

Isma'il—meaning Ibn 'Ulayya—narrated to us, from Ayyub, from 'Ikrima: that Ibn 'Abbas, ؓ, said about the husband of Barira: “That is Mughith, the slave of the sons of so-and-so. By God, it is as though I can see him now following her through the alleys of Medina, weeping.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا إسحاق بن إبراهيم بن عبد الرحمن ، قال : ثنا إسماعيل - يعني ابن علية - عن أيوب ، عن عكرمة ،

Matn

أن ابن عباس رضي الله عنهما ، قال في زوج بريرة : ذاك مغيث عبد بني فلان والله لكأني أراه الآن يتبعها في سكك المدينة يبكي

Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Abu l-Nu'man narrated to us; he said: Hammad b. Salama narrated to us, from 'Amir al-Ahwal, from 'Amr b. Shu'ayb, from his father, from his grandfather, that the Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“There is no divorce in what one does not own, and no manumission in what one does not own.”

Matn

لا طلاق فيما لا يملك ، ولا عتق فيما لا يملك
