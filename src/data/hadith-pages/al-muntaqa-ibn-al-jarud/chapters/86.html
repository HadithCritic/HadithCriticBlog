and Humayd b. 'Abd al-Rahman, from Abu Bakra, ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ addressed us on the Day of Sacrifice and said: What day is this? We said: God and His Messenger know best. He said: He was silent until we thought he would name it something other than its name. Then he said: Is it not the sacred day (of sacrifice)? We said: Yes. He said: Then God has made your blood and your wealth sacred to you, like the sacredness of this day of yours, in this month of yours, in this land of yours, until the day you meet your Lord. Behold, have I conveyed? They said: Yes. He said: O God, be witness. Let the one present convey to the one absent, for perhaps one to whom it is conveyed will retain it better than the one who hears it. Behold, do not return after me as disbelievers, striking one another's necks.

English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Sulayman b. Harb narrated to us; he said: Hammad b. Zayd narrated to us, from Yahya b. Sa'id, from Abu Umama b. Sahl, who said: I was with 'Uthman, ؓ, while he was besieged in the house. There was an entrance in the house, such that whoever entered it could hear the speech of those on the paved ground outside. 'Uthman went into that entrance and came out with his color changed, and said: They were just threatening me with death. We said: God will suffice you against them, O Commander of the Faithful. He said: And why would they kill me? I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: The blood of a Muslim man is not lawful except for one of three: a man who disbelieves after his Islam, or commits adultery after being muhsan, or kills a person. By God, I never committed adultery, in the Jahiliyya or in Islam; I never wished to have a religion other than mine since God guided me to it; and I have not killed anyone. So for what would they kill me?

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Abu Salama narrated to us; he said: Aban narrated to us; he said: Qatada narrated to us, from Anas b. Malik, ؓ: that a Jewish man crushed the head of a girl with a stone, then took the silver ornaments she had on. They found her with a trace of life in her, and they went around with her asking: Was it this one? Was it this one? until she pointed to the Jewish man. They seized him and he confessed, so the Prophet ﷺ ordered that his head be crushed with stones.

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 320
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Musa b. Harun al-Burdi narrated to us; he said: Hisham b. Yusuf informed us, from al-Qasim b. Fayyad al-Abnawi, from Khallad b. 'Abd al-Rahman, from Ibn al-Musayyab, from Ibn 'Abbas, ؓ: that a man of Banu Bakr b. Layth came to the Prophet ﷺ and admitted four times that he had committed adultery with a woman. He flogged him one hundred lashes, and he was unmarried. Then he asked him for proof regarding the woman, and the woman said: By God, he lies, O Messenger of God. So he flogged him the hadd for slander, eighty (lashes).
