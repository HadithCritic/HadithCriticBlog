Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 213
English

Abu Hashim Ziyad b. Ayyub narrated to us; he said: Isma'il b. Ibrahim narrated to us; he said: Ibn 'Awn narrated to us, from al-Sha'bi; he said: I heard al-Nu'man b. Bashir, ؓ, say: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say—and no, by God, I will not hear after him anyone say “I heard from the Messenger of God ﷺ, who said”: “The lawful is clear and the unlawful is clear, and between them are matters that are doubtful.” He said: and perhaps he said: “doubtful ones.” “And I will give you an example of that: God has set aside a protected preserve, and the preserve of God is what He has forbidden. Whoever grazes his flock around the preserve is almost certain to graze in it, and whoever mixes with doubt is almost certain to dare [to commit sin].”

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: Abu Nu‘aym narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, from Salama b. Kuhayl, from Abu Salama, from Abu Hurayra (ؓ), who said: A man was owed a camel of a certain age (sinn) by the Prophet (ﷺ), and he came demanding it. Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: 'Abd al-Samad b. 'Abd al-Warith narrated to us; he said: Shu'ba narrated to us; he said: Abu Bishr narrated to us; he said: I heard Abu l-Mutawakkil narrating from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, ؓ, who said: Some of the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ stopped at one of the Arab tribes, and they did not host them or offer them hospitality. He said: Then the chief of that tribe fell ill, and they came to us and said: “Do you have a remedy?” We said: “Yes, but you did not host us or show us hospitality, so we will not do it until you set a payment for us.” So they set for them a flock of sheep for that, and one of our men began to recite the Fatiha of the Book over him. When they came to the Prophet ﷺ, we mentioned that to him. He said: “How did you know that it is a ruqya?” and he did not mention any prohibition from him. He said: “Eat, and give me a share with you from the payment.”
