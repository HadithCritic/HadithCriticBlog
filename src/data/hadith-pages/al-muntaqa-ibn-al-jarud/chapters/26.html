Entry74/ 15
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 32
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: ‘Abd al-Razzaq informed us, saying: Ma‘mar informed us, from al-Zuhri, from ‘Ata’ b. Yazid al-Laythi, from Humran b. Aban, who said: I saw ‘Uthman (ؓ) perform ablution: he poured water over his hands three times and washed them; then he rinsed his mouth and snuffed water into his nose and blew it out three times; then he washed his face three times; then he washed his right hand up to the elbow three times, then the left likewise; [then he wiped his head; then he washed his right foot up to the two ankles three times, then the left likewise]. Then he said: “I saw the Messenger of God (ﷺ) perform ablution like this ablution of mine.” Then he said: “Whoever performs ablution like this ablution of mine, then prays two rak‘as in which he does not speak to himself, his previous sins will be forgiven.”

Entry75/ 15
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 33
English

Ishaq b. Mansur narrated to us: ‘Abd al-Rahman b. Mahdi informed us, saying: Za’ida b. Qudama narrated to us, from Khalid b. ‘Alqama al-Hamdani, from ‘Abd Khayr, who said: ‘Ali (ؓ) entered al-Rahba after he had prayed Fajr and sat down in al-Rahba. Then he said to a young servant of his: “Bring me water for purification.” The servant brought him a vessel with water in it, and a basin. ‘Abd Khayr said: And we were sitting, watching him. He took the vessel in his right hand and tilted it over his left hand, then washed his palms; then he took the vessel in his right hand and poured over his left hand, then washed his palms; then he took the vessel in his right hand and poured over his left hand, then washed his palms — he did it three times. ‘Abd Khayr said: Throughout all of that he did not put his hand into the vessel until he had washed it three times. Then he put his right hand into the vessel, filled his mouth, rinsed, snuffed water into his nose and blew it out with his left hand, three times; then he washed his face three times; then he washed his right hand three times up to the elbow; then he washed his left hand three times up to the elbow; then he put his right hand into the vessel until the water covered it, then lifted it with what it held of water and wiped it with his left hand; then he wiped his head with both hands, once; then he put his right hand into the vessel, then poured over his right foot and washed it three times with his left hand; then he put his right hand into the vessel and filled it with water and drank from it. Then he said: “This is the purification (tuhur) of the Prophet of God (ﷺ); whoever would like to see the purification of the Prophet of God (ﷺ), then this is his purification.”
