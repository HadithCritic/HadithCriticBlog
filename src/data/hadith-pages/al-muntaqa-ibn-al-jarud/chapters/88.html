Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 325
English

Yusuf b. Musa and Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; they both said: Qabisa narrated to us; he said: Sufyan narrated to us, from 'Umar b. Muhammad, from al-Qasim, from Salim, from Ibn 'Umar, ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: Let none of you eat with his left hand, nor drink with his left hand, for Satan eats with his left hand and drinks with his left hand.

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا يوسف بن موسى ومحمد بن يحيى ، قالا : ثنا قبيصة ، قال : ثنا سفيان ، عن عمر بن محمد ، عن القاسم ، عن سالم ، عن ابن عمر رضي الله عنهما ، قال : قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم :

Matn

لا يأكل أحدكم بشماله ، ولا يشرب بشماله ؛ فإن الشيطان يأكل بشماله ، ويشرب بشماله

Entry937/ 27
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 325
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Isma'il b. Abi Uways narrated to us; he said: My brother narrated to me, from Sulayman b. Bilal, from 'Umar b. Muhammad, that Abu Bakr b. 'Ubayd God b. 'Abd Allah informed him that Salim b. 'Abd Allah informed him that 'Abd Allah b. 'Umar, ؓ, informed him that the Prophet ﷺ said this report. Abu Muhammad said: I heard Muhammad b. Yahya say: al-Qasim, in our view, is Abu Bakr b. 'Ubayd God, God willing.

العربية

حدثنا محمد بن يحيى ، قال : ثنا إسماعيل بن أبي أويس ، قال : حدثني أخي ، عن سليمان بن بلال ، عن عمر بن محمد أن أبا بكر بن عبيد الله بن عبد الله ، أخبره أن سالم بن عبد الله أخبره ، أن عبد الله بن عمر رضي الله عنهما أخبره أن النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم قال بهذا الخبر . قال أبو محمد : سمعت محمد بن يحيى يقول : القاسم عندنا هو أبو بكر بن عبيد الله إن شاء الله .

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 328
English

Ziyad b. Ayyub narrated to us; he said: Hushaym narrated to us; he said: Abu l-Zubayr informed us, from Jabir, ؓ, who said: The Prophet ﷺ sent me with Abu 'Ubayda on a raiding party, and our provisions ran out. We passed by a fish that the sea had cast up, and we wanted to eat from it, but Abu 'Ubayda forbade us. Then he said: We are messengers of the Messenger of God ﷺ, and (we are) in the way of God, so eat. We ate from it for days. When we came to the Messenger of God ﷺ, we told him, and he said: If any of it remains with you, send it to us.

Isnad

Ziyad b. Ayyub narrated to us, saying: Hushaym narrated to us, saying: Abu Bishr informed us, from Sa‘id ibn Jubayr, from Ibn ‘Abbas (ؓ), that he was asked about eating lizards (dabb).
