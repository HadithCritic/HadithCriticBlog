Isnad

Abu Hashim Ziyad b. Ayyub narrated to us; he said: Waki' narrated to us, from al-A'mash, from 'Umara b. 'Umayr, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Yazid, from 'Abd Allah, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said to us: “O company of young men, whoever among you is able to marry, let him marry, for it lowers the gaze and protects chastity more, and whoever is not able, let him fast, for it is a protection for him.”

English
Isnad

Abu Zur‘a al-Razi narrated to us, saying: Sa‘id ibn ‘Amr narrated to us, saying: ‘Abthar informed us, from al-A‘mash, from Abu Ishaq, from Abu al-Ahwas, from ‘Abdullah (ؓ), who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God (ﷺ) taught us the tashahhud in prayer and the tashahhud for a need (haja). He mentioned the tashahhud in prayer and the tashahhud for a need, and said: “The tashahhud for a need is that he says: ‘Indeed, all praise belongs to God; we praise Him, seek His help, and ask His forgiveness; and we seek refuge in God from the evils of our own selves. Whomever God guides, none can lead astray, and whomever He leads astray, none can guide. And I bear witness that there is no god but God, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger.’ Then he recites three verses of the Qur’an: {Fear God as He should be feared, and do not die except as Muslims}. {And fear God, through whom you ask one another, and the ties of kinship; indeed God is ever watchful over you}. {O you who believe, fear God and speak a straight word}.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا أبو زرعة الرازي ، قال : ثنا سعيد بن عمرو ، قال : أنا عبثر ، عن الأعمش ، عن أبي إسحاق ، عن أبي الأحوص ، عن عبد الله رضي الله عنه قال :

Matn

علمنا رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم التشهد في الصلاة ، والتشهد في الحاجة ، فذكر التشهد في الصلاة والتشهد في الحاجة ، فقال : والتشهد في الحاجة أن يقول : إن الحمد لله نحمده ونستعينه ونستغفره ، ونعوذ بالله من شرور أنفسنا ، ومن يهده الله فلا مضل له ومن يضلل فلا هادي له ، وأشهد أن لا إله إلا الله ، وأشهد أن محمدا عبده ورسوله ، ثم يقرأ ثلاث آيات من القرآن : { اتقوا الله حق تقاته ولا تموتن إلا وأنتم مسلمون } . { واتقوا الله الذي تساءلون به والأرحام إن الله كان عليكم رقيبا } . { يا أيها الذين آمنوا اتقوا الله وقولوا قولا سديدا } .

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: al-Nufayli narrated to us, saying: Zuhayr narrated to us, saying: Hisham b. ‘Urwa narrated to us, from ‘Urwa, from Zaynab bint Abi Salama, from Umm Salama that Umm Habiba said:

Matn

“O Messenger of God, do you have any interest in my sister?” He said: “And what shall I do?” She said: “Marry her.” He said: “Your sister?” She said: “Yes.” He said: “Would you like that?” She said: “I am not one who has you to herself alone (lastu bi-mukhliya), and the one I most love to share with me in good is my sister!” He said: “She is not lawful for me.” She said: “By God, I was told that you are proposing to Durra — or Dhurra; the doubt is from Zuhayr.” He said: “The daughter of Umm Salama?” She said: “Yes.” He said: “By God, even if she were not my stepdaughter in my care, she would not be lawful for me; she is my foster brother’s daughter; Thuwayba nursed me and her father. So do not present your daughters or your sisters to me.”
