Entry723/ 6
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 246
English

Muhammad b. 'Abd Allah b. 'Abd al-Hakam informed us that Ibn Wahb informed them; he said: Malik b. Anas, 'Amr b. al-Harith, Sufyan b. Sa'id al-Thawri, and others informed me that Rabi'a b. Abi 'Abd al-Rahman narrated to them, from Yazid, the freedman of al-Munba'ith, from Zayd b. Khalid al-Juhani, who said: A man came to the Messenger of God ﷺ while I was with him, and asked about found property. He said: “Learn its container and its tie, then announce it for a year. If its owner comes, [give it to him]; otherwise it is yours to deal with.” He said: “And a stray sheep?” He said: “It is for you, or for your brother, or for the wolf.” He said: “And a stray camel?” He said: “It has its footwear and its water container; it goes to water and eats from the trees, until its owner finds it.”

Entry724/ 6
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 246
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Muhammad b. Yusuf narrated to us, from Sufyan (h). He said: And 'Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us; he said: Sufyan informed us, from Rabi'a b. Abi 'Abd al-Rahman, from Yazid, the freedman of al-Munba'ith, from Zayd b. Khalid al-Juhani, who said: A Bedouin asked the Prophet ﷺ about found property. He said: “Announce it for a year. If someone comes and tells you about its container and its tie, [give it to him]; otherwise make use of it.” He asked him about a stray camel, and his face reddened. He said: “What do you have to do with it? It has its footwear and its water container; it goes to water and eats from the trees. Leave it until its owner finds it.” He asked him about a stray sheep. He said: “It is for you, or for your brother, or for the wolf.”

العربية
Isnad

حدثنا محمد بن يحيى ، قال : ثنا محمد بن يوسف ، عن سفيان ( ح ) . قال : وحدثنا عبد الرزاق ، قال : أنا سفيان ، عن ربيعة بن أبي عبد الرحمن ، عن يزيد مولى المنبعث ، عن زيد بن خالد الجهني ، قال :

Matn

سأل أعرابي النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم عن اللقطة ، فقال : عرفها سنة ، فإن جاءك أحد يخبرك بعفاصها ووكائها ، وإلا فاستمتع بها . وسأله عن ضالة الإبل ؟ فتمعر وجهه ، وقال : ما لك ولها ؟ معها حذاؤها وسقاؤها ، ترد الماء وتأكل الشجر ، دعها حتى يلقاها ربها . وسأله عن ضالة الغنم ؟ قال : هي لك ، أو لأخيك ، أو للذئب

English

'Abd Allah b. Muhammad b. 'Amr al-Ghazzi narrated to us; he said: al-Firyabi narrated to us; he said: Sufyan narrated to us, from Salama b. Kuhayl, from Suwayd b. Ghafala, who said: I found a whip and picked it up, and Zayd b. Suhan and Salman b. Rabi'a criticized me for it. I said: “If I find its owner, I will hand it over to him, and if not, I will make use of it.” He said: I mentioned that to Ubayy b. Ka'b, and he said: “You did well, you did well. I found a purse and brought it to the Prophet ﷺ, and he said: ‘Announce it.’ I announced it for a year and found no one who recognized it. He said: ‘Note its number, its container, and its tie. If its owner comes, hand it over to him; otherwise make use of it.’”

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 248
English

Ibn 'Abd al-Hakam informed us that Ibn Wahb informed them; he said: 'Amr b. al-Harith and Hisham b. Sa'd informed me, from 'Amr b. Shu'ayb, from his father, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr b. al-'As, ؓ: that a man of Muzayna came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: “What is your view of what is found on a frequented road and in an inhabited village?” He said: “Announce it for a year. If its seeker comes, hand it over to him; otherwise it is yours to deal with, and if its claimant comes one day, pay it back to him. And what is found on a road that is not frequented, or in a village that is not inhabited, on that and on buried treasure a fifth is due.”

Isnad

Matn

أن رجلا من مزينة أتى إلى النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم قال : كيف ترى في ما يوجد في الطريق الميتاء ، وفي القرية المسكونة ؟ قال : عرفه سنة ، فإن جاء باغيه فادفعه إليه ، وإلا فشأنك بها ، وإن جاء طالبها يوما من الدهر فأدها إليه ، وما كان في الطريق غير الميتاء ، أو القرية غير المسكونة ، ففيه وفي الركاز الخمس

Entry728/ 6
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 248
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: Sa‘id ibn ‘Amir narrated to us, from Shu‘ba, from Khalid al-Hadhdha’, from Yazid b. ‘Abdullah b. al-Shikhkhir, from Mutarrif, from ‘Iyad b. Himar (ؓ): that the Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “Whoever finds a lost item, let him have one just man, or two just men, bear witness to it; let him not conceal it nor hide it. If its owner comes, he has a greater right to it; otherwise it is the property of God, which He gives to whomever He wills.”

العربية
Isnad

Matn

من التقط لقطة فليشهد ذا عدل ، أو ذوي عدل ، ولا يكتم ، ولا يغيب ، فإن جاء صاحبها فهو أحق بها ، وإلا فهو مال الله يؤتيه من يشاء .
