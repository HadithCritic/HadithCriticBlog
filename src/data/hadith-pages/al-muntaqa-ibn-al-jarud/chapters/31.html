Entry146/ 14
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 54
English

al-Hasan b. Muhammad al-Za'farani narrated to us; he said: Waki' b. al-Jarrah informed us; he said: al-A'mash informed us; he said: I heard Mujahid narrate from Tawus, from Ibn 'Abbas, ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ passed by two graves and said: “They are being punished, and not for something major: as for this one, he used to go about spreading slander; and as for this other, he did not protect himself from his urine.” Then he called for a fresh palm frond, split it in two, and planted one on this grave and one on that. Then he said: “Perhaps their punishment will be lightened for as long as these do not dry out.”

Isnad

Matn

مر رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم على قبرين ، فقال : إنهما ليعذبان ، وما يعذبان في كبير : أما هذا فكان يمشي بالنميمة ، وأما هذا الآخر فكان لا يستبرئ من بوله ، ثم دعا بعسيب رطب فشقه باثنين ، فغرس على هذا واحدا وعلى هذا واحدا ، ثم قال : لعله أن يخفف عنهما ما لم ييبسا

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 55
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Ya'la b. 'Ubayd informed us; he said: al-A'mash informed us, from Zayd b. Wahb, from 'Abd al-Rahman b. Hasana, who said: 'Amr b. al-'As and I were sitting when the Messenger of God ﷺ came out to us with a shield in his hand, and he urinated while sitting. We spoke among ourselves and said: “He urinates as a woman urinates.” He came to us and said: “Do you not know what befell the companion of the Children of Israel? When urine touched them, they would cut it off [with scissors]; he forbade them, and he was punished in his grave.”

Matn

كنت أنا وعمرو بن العاص جالسين ، فخرج علينا رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم وفي يده درقة ، فبال وهو جالس ، فتكلمنا بيننا ، فقلنا : يبول كما تبول المرأة ، فأتانا فقال : أوما تدرون ما لقي صاحب بني إسرائيل ؟ كان إذا أصابهم بول قرضوه فنهاهم ، فعذب في قبره

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 55
English

Bahr b. Nasr narrated to us, from Ibn Wahb, from Ibn Lahi'a, al-Layth b. Sa'd and 'Amr b. al-Harith, from Yazid b. Abi Habib, from Suwayd b. Qays, from Mu'awiya b. Hudayj, who said: I heard Mu'awiya b. Abi Sufyan, ؓ, say: I asked Umm Habiba—wife of the Prophet ﷺ, ؓ—whether the Messenger of God ﷺ would pray in the garment in which he had had intercourse with her. She said: “Yes, if he saw no filth on it.”

English

Mahmud b. Adam narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, from al-Zuhri, from Sa‘id, from Abu Hurayra: that a Bedouin entered the mosque and prayed, and when he finished he said: “O God, have mercy on me and Muhammad, and do not have mercy together with us on anyone else.” The Prophet (ﷺ) turned to him and said: “You have fenced off what is vast.” It was not long before he urinated in the mosque, and the people rushed at him, but he (the Prophet) stopped them and said: “Pour over it a bucketful (dhanub) or a pailful (sajl) of water” — meaning, over his urine — and he said: “You were sent only to make things easy; you were not sent to make things hard.”
