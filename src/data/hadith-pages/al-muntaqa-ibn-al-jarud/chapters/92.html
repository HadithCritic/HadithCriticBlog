Entry983/ 10
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 338
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Abu Nu'aym and Ya'la b. 'Ubayd narrated to us; they both said: Zakariyya narrated to us, from 'Amir, from 'Adi b. Hatim—Ibn Yahya said: and this is the hadith of Abu Nu'aym—who said: I asked the Messenger of God ﷺ about game killed with a mi'rad (a blunt-shafted arrow). He said: What it hits with its edge, eat; and what it hits with its side is mawqudha (beaten to death). He said: And I asked him about game caught by a dog. He said: What it holds for you, eat, for the dog's catching is its slaughter. But if you find with your dog another dog or other dogs, and you fear that it took the game along with it and killed it, do not eat, for you mentioned the name of God over your dog and did not mention it over another.

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 339
English

Sa‘id ibn Bahr al-Qarasisi narrated to us, saying: ‘Ubayda b. Humayd narrated to us, saying: Bayan Abu Bishr narrated to me, from ‘Amir al-Sha‘bi, who said: ‘Adi b. Hatim (ؓ) said: I asked the Messenger of God (ﷺ), saying: “O Messenger of God, we send out trained dogs, and they kill (the game).” The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “If they kill, then eat, unless it has eaten from it, or another dog besides it has joined it.”

Entry985/ 10
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 339
English

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Abu 'Asim narrated to us; he said: Haywa b. Sharih narrated to us; he said: Rabi'a b. Yazid narrated to us; he said: Abu Idris al-Khawlani narrated to us; he said: Abu Tha'laba al-Khushani, ؓ, narrated to us; he said: I came to the Messenger of God ﷺ and said: O Messenger of God, we are in a land of the People of the Book and we eat from their vessels; and we are in a land of game, where I shoot with my bow, and hunt with my trained dog and my dog that is not trained. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: If you are in a land of the People of the Book as you mention, do not eat from their vessels unless you find no alternative; if you find no alternative, wash them and then eat in them. And if you are in a land of game as you mention, whatever you hunt with your bow, mention the name of God and eat; whatever you hunt with your trained dog, mention the name of God and eat; and whatever you hunt with your dog that is not trained and you reach it in time to slaughter it, eat.

Matn

أتيت رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم فقلت : يا رسول الله إنا بأرض أهل كتاب فنأكل في آنيتهم ، وإنا بأرض صيد فأرمي بقوسي ، وأصيد بكلبي المعلم ، وكلبي الذي غير معلم ، فقال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : إن كنتم بأرض أهل كتاب كما ذكرت فلا تأكلوا في آنيتهم ، إلا أن لا تجدوا منها بدا ، فإن لم تجدوا منها بدا فاغسلوها ثم كلوا فيها ، وإن كنتم بأرض صيد كما ذكرت ، فما صدت بقوسك فاذكر اسم الله وكل ، وما صدت بكلبك المعلم فاذكر اسم الله وكل ، وما صدت بكلبك الذي غير معلم فأدركت ذكاته فكل

Muhammad b. 'Abd Allah b. 'Abd al-Hakam informed us that Ibn Wahb informed them; he said: Haywa b. Sharih informed me that he heard Rabi'a b. Yazid al-Dimashqi say: I heard Abu Idris al-Khawlani say that he heard Abu Tha'laba al-Khushani, ؓ, say: I came to the Messenger of God ﷺ—and he mentioned the story of the dog alone.
