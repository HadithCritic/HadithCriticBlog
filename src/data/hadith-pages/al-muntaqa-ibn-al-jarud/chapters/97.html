Entry1041/ 11
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 358
English

Hammad b. al-Hasan b. 'Anbasa al-Warraq narrated to us; he said: Makki—meaning Ibn Ibrahim—informed us; he said: 'Abd Allah b. Sa'id b. Abi Hind narrated to us, from Isma'il b. Abi Hakim, from Sa'id b. Marjana, who said: I heard Abu Hurayra, ؓ, say: The Messenger of God ﷺ said: Whoever frees a believing slave, God will free from the Fire a limb of his for each limb of the slave, even to the point that He frees the hand for the hand, the foot for the foot, and the private part for the private part.

العربية

Matn

من أعتق رقبة مؤمنة أعتق الله بكل إرب منه إربا من النار ، حتى أنه ليعتق باليد اليد ، وبالرجل الرجل ، وبالفرج الفرج

Entry1042/ 11
Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 358
English

‘Abdullah b. Hashim narrated to us, saying: Yahya — that is, Ibn Sa‘id — narrated to us, from Hisham, who said: My father informed me that Abu Murawwih al-Ghifari informed him that Abu Dharr (ؓ) informed him that he said: “O Messenger of God, which deed is best?” He said: “Faith in God and striving in His cause.” He said: “And which slaves are best to free?” He said: “The most costly in price and the most precious to their owners.” He said: I said: “What if I cannot do so?” He said: “You help someone in need (da’i‘), or do work for someone unskilled (akhraq).” He said: “What if I am too weak for that?” He said: “You refrain from doing evil, for that is a charity you give to yourself.”

Matn

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Hammad b. Mas'ada narrated to us, from Malik, from Nafi', from 'Abd Allah b.
