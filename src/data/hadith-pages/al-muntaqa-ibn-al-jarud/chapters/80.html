English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us; he said: Sulayman b. Harb narrated to us; he said: Hammad b. Zayd narrated to us, from Khalid al-Hadhdha', from al-Qasim b. Rabi'a, from 'Uqba b. Aws, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr, ؓ, who said: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

Behold, every claim of honor that was reckoned and asserted in the Jahiliyya, whether of blood or of wealth, is beneath my two feet, except the giving of water to the pilgrims and the custodianship of the House. Then he said: Behold, the blood-money for accidental killing, where it was with a whip or a staff, is one hundred camels, forty of which are pregnant, with their young in their bellies.

Matn

English
Isnad

Ibrahim b. 'Abd Allah al-Naysaburi narrated to us; he said: Yazid b. Harun informed us; he said: Muhammad b. Ishaq informed us, from al-Harith b. Fudayl, from Sufyan b. Abi l-'Awja' al-Sulami, from Abu Shurayh al-Khuza'i, who said: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say:

Matn

Whoever suffers bloodshed or khabl—and khabl is a wound—he has a choice among one of three; if he wants a fourth, restrain him by the hands: to take retaliation, or to pardon, or to take the blood-money. If he takes any of these, and then transgresses after that, then he will have the Fire, abiding therein forever.

Muntaqā Ibn al-Jārūd · p. 289
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. Yahya narrated to us, saying: al-Humaydi narrated to us, saying: Sufyan narrated to us, saying: ‘Amr b. Dinar informed us, saying:

Matn

I heard Mujahid say: I heard Ibn ‘Abbas (ؓ) say: Retaliation (qisas) existed among the Children of Israel, and there was no blood-money (diya) among them. So God said to this community: {O you who believe, retaliation is prescribed for you in the case of the slain: the free for the free, the slave for the slave, the female for the female; but whoever is pardoned something by his brother…}. Ibn ‘Abbas (ؓ) said: Pardon means that he accepts the blood-money in a case of deliberate killing; {…then a following up with what is fair, and payment to him in good grace}. He said: On this one is to follow up with what is fair, and on that one to pay in good grace. {That is a lightening from your Lord} of what was prescribed for those before you; {and whoever transgresses after that, for him is a painful punishment}.

English
Isnad

Bahr b. Nasr narrated to us, saying: Ibn Wahb narrated to us, saying: Yunus informed me, from Ibn Shihab, from Sa‘id ibn al-Musayyab and Abu Salama b. ‘Abd al-Rahman, that Abu Hurayra (ؓ) said:

Matn

Two women of Hudhayl fought, and one of them threw a stone at the other and killed her and what was in her womb. They brought their dispute to the Messenger of God (ﷺ), and the Messenger of God (ﷺ) ruled that the blood-money for her fetus was a ghurra — a slave, male or female — and he ruled that the blood-money for the woman was to be paid by her ‘aqila (paternal kinsmen), and her offspring and those with them inherited from her. Hamal b. al-Nabigha al-Hudhali said: “O Messenger of God, how can I be made to pay for one who neither drank nor ate, nor spoke nor cried out? A case like that is nullified.” The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “This is only one of the brothers of the soothsayers” — because of his rhymed speech.

Abu Sa‘id al-Ashajj narrated to us, saying: al-Muharibi narrated to us, saying: Muhammad b. Ishaq narrated to us, from Yazid b. ‘Abdullah b. Qusayt, from Ibn Abi Hadrad al-Aslami, from his father (ؓ), who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God (ﷺ) sent us out on a raiding party, and in that party were Abu Qatada al-Ansari and Muhallim b. Jaththama b. Qays, and I was among them. While we were so, ‘Amir b. al-Adbat al-Ashja‘i passed by us and greeted us with the greeting of Islam, and we held back from him. Then Muhallim b. Jaththama attacked him and killed him, and took from him a camel of his and some fresh produce (rutb) of lin [sic; probably “a skin of milk,” watb min laban] that he had with him. When we came to the Messenger of God (ﷺ), the Qur’an was revealed concerning us: {O you who believe, when you go forth in the cause of God, make things clear (verify)…} to the end of the verse.

Isnad

al-Muharibi said: Ibn Ishaq said: Muhammad b. Ja‘far b. al-Zubayr narrated to me, saying: I heard Ziyad b. Dumra b. Sa‘d al-Sulami narrate to ‘Urwa b. al-Zubayr, saying: My father and my grandfather narrated to me

Matn

— and they had both witnessed Hunayn with the Messenger of God (ﷺ) — they said: The Messenger of God (ﷺ) prayed Zuhr, then sat in the shade of a tree. Al-Aqra‘ b. Habis and ‘Uyayna b. Badr stood up before him, ‘Uyayna demanding blood-vengeance for the man of Ashja‘ and al-Aqra‘ defending (the killer). They disputed at length in front of the Messenger of God (ﷺ), and the Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “Rather, accept the blood-money: fifty on our journey and fifty when we return.” The Messenger of God (ﷺ) kept working on them until they accepted the blood-money. When they accepted the blood-money, they said: “Where is your companion, that the Messenger of God (ﷺ) may ask forgiveness for him?” Then a tall man wearing a hulla (a two-piece garment), in which he had prepared himself to be killed, stood up until he sat down before the Messenger of God (ﷺ), and the Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “What is your name?” He said: “I am Muhallim b. Jaththama.” The Messenger of God (ﷺ) said: “O God, do not forgive Muhallim b. Jaththama!” So he rose from before the Messenger of God (ﷺ), catching his tears with the edge of his cloak.

Isnad

Matn

صلى رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم الظهر ، ثم جلس إلى ظل شجرة ، فقام إليه الأقرع بن حابس وعيينة بن بدر . عيينة يطلب بدم الأشجعي والأقرع يدفع عنه ، فاختصما بين يدي رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم طويلا ، فقال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : بل تقبلون الدية خمسين في سفرنا وخمسين إذا رجعنا . فلم يزل بهم رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم حتى قبلوا الدية ، فلما قبلوا الدية ، قالوا : أين صاحبكم فيستغفر له رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ؟ فقام رجل طويل عليه حلة قد تهيأ فيها للقتل ، حتى جلس بين يدي رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فقال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : ما اسمك ؟ قال : أنا محلم بن جثامة فقال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم : اللهم لا تغفر لمحلم بن جثامة ! فقام من بين يدي رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم ، وهو يتلقى دمعه بفضل ردائه
