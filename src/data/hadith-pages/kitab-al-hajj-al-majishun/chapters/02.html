'Abd al-'Aziz narrated to us, saying:

Matn

God, majestic be His praise, said: {The pilgrimage is in well-known months. Whoever undertakes the pilgrimage in them, then there is to be no sexual relations, no wickedness, and no quarrelling during the pilgrimage. Whatever good you do, God knows it. And take provision, for the best provision is God-consciousness (taqwa); and fear Me, O people of understanding.} And God said: {And proclaim the pilgrimage among the people; they will come to you on foot and on every lean mount, coming from every deep mountain pass.} And He said: {And complete the pilgrimage and the 'umra for God. But if you are prevented…} And He said: {And pilgrimage to the House is a duty owed to God by the people — for whoever is able to find a way to it.} And “ability,” according to what has reached us, is a mount and provisions.

The muhrim (the person in ihram) must not hunt, nor order hunting, nor point out game, nor kill any animal except what the Messenger of God ﷺ permitted; for it has reached us that he said: Five vicious creatures: there is no blame on the muhrim for killing them: the scorpion, the mouse, the biting dog (al-kalb al-'aqur), the crow, the kite, and the snake; one doubts about killing them. As for the biting dog, its interpretation in our view is: every predatory animal that attacks people — the lion, the leopard, the wolf, and the like; and among the things that indicated this to us is that a dog that is not biting carries no blame for the muhrim in killing it, because it is tame and domestic — so how much more the biting among them; so we understood that he meant only what attacks among wild animals, and that is similar to the vicious creatures. والناس يأخذون حصاهم من المزدلفة ويعدون حصىً كحصي الخذف أو أكبر منه سبع حصايات لكل إنسان، فإذا تبين الفجر صلى بالناس الصبح، ثم وقفوا ووقف الإمام على قزح، وهو المشعر الحرام. ووقف عليه رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم، ثم قال: هذا الموقف، والمزدلفة كلها موقفٌ، وارتفعوا عن بطن محسرٍ. ثم يدفع الإمام إذا أسفر ويتبين مسيره قبل مراهقة الشمس، ثم السير تلك الغداة كسيرهم في الليلة قبلها. وكان ابن عمر يضرب راحلته إذا هبط بطن محسرٍ، وليس ذلك [.. ..] (1) .
