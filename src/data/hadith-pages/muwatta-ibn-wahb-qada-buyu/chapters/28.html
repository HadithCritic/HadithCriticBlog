English
Isnad

Talha b. Abi Sa'id informed me that Bukayr b. al-Ashajj narrated to him

Matn

that 'Umar b. al-Khattab used to hold liable the craftsmen who are in the market and have set themselves up for people, for what is handed to them.

أَنَّ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ كَانَ يُضَمِّنُ الصُّنَّاعَ الَّذِينَ فِي السُّوقِ وَانْتَصَبُوا لِلنَّاسِ مَا دَفَعَ إِلَيْهِمْ.

Entry251/ 23
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 62
English
Isnad

And Malik said:

Matn

As for one hired from among the craftsmen, such as tailoring, dyeing, carpentry, laundering and the like of all kinds of work, when spoilage enters it, or contravention of the instruction of its owner, or destruction: as for one of these hired for a specified piece of work with no fixed term, which the worker takes to his house to do, whoever spoils any of that, or destroys it, or contravenes what he was ordered, must pay for what he spoiled or destroyed or contravened. But as for one hired from among them for named days to work in the house of the owner who hired him, there is no liability on him for whatever spoilage enters in it; it is only his effort and the work of his hands, unless he deliberately spoils something, in which case he is liable.

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ مَالِكٌ:

Matn

أَمَّا مَنِ اسْتُؤْجِرَ مِنْ أَهْلِ الصِّنَاعَاتِ مِثْلَ الْخِيَاطَةِ وَالصِّبَاغَةِ وَالنِّجَارَةِ وَالْغَسِيلِ وَأَشْبَاهِ ذَلِكَ مِنَ الأَعْمَالِ كُلِّهَا إِذَا دَخَلَهَا فَسَادٌ أَوْ خِلافٌ لأَمْرِ صَاحِبِهِ أَوْ هَلاكٌ؛ أَمَّا مَنِ اسْتُؤْجِرَ مِنْ هَؤُلاءِ عَلَى عَمَلٍ يُسَمَّى بِعَيْنِهِ لَيْسَ لَهُ أَجَلٌ، وَيَذْهَبُ بِهِ الْعَامِلُ إِلَى مَنْزِلِهِ يَعْمَلُهُ؛ فَإِنَّ عَلَى مَنْ أَفْسَدَ مِنْ ذَلِكَ شَيْئًا أَوْ أَهْلَكَهُ أَوْ خَالَفَ مَا أَمَرَهُ بِهِ، غُرِّمَ مَا أَفْسَدَ أَوْ أَهْلَكَ أَوْ خَالَفَ مَا أُمِرَ بِهِ. وَأَمَّا مَنِ اسْتُؤْجِرَ مِنْهُمْ أَيَّامًا مُسَمَّاةً يَعْمَلُ فِي مَنْزِلِ صَاحِبِهِ الَّذِي اسْتَأْجَرَهُ، فَمَا دَخَلَ فِيهِ مِنْ فَسَادٍ، فَلا غُرْمَ عَلَيْهِ، إِنَّمَا جُهْدُهُ وَعَمَلُ يَدَيْهِ، إِلا أَنْ يَعْتَمِدَ فَسَادَ شَيْءٍ، فَيُضَمَّنُهُ.

Entry252/ 23
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 62
English
Isnad

And Malik said:

Matn

Craftsmen were made liable for what was handed to them to work on only because of the need for their work, and not by way of choosing them and entrusting them; if it were left to their trustworthiness, people's property would be destroyed and lost in their hands and they would dare to take it; and if people left them, they would find no one to seek redress from, and no one else to turn to, and no one does those works but them; so they were made liable for that in the public interest. Similar to that, as a benefit for the public, is what the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “A townsman shall not sell for a nomad, and do not go out to meet the goods until they reach the markets.” When he saw that this serves the public, he commanded that.

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ مَالِكٌ:

Matn

إِنَّمَا ضُمِّنَ الصُّنَّاعُ مَا دُفِعَ إِلَيْهِمْ مِمَّا يَسْتَعْمِلُونَ عَلَى وَجْهِ الْحَاجَةِ إِلَى أَعْمَالِهِمْ، وَلَيْسَ ذَلِكَ عَلَى وَجْهِ الاخْتِيَارِ لَهُمْ وَالأَمَانَةِ، وَلَوْ كَانَ ذَلِكَ إِلَى أَمَانَتِهِمْ لَهَلَكَتْ أَمْوَالُ النَّاسِ وَضَاعَتْ قِبَلَهُمْ وَاجْتَرَءُوا عَلَى أَخْذِهَا؛ وَإِنْ تَرَكُوهُمْ لَمْ يَجِدُوا مُسْتَعْتَبًا، وَلَمْ يَجِدُوا غَيْرَهُمْ، وَلا أَحَدَ يَعْمَلُ تِلْكَ الأَعْمَالِ غَيْرُهُمْ، فَضُمِّنُوا ذَلِكَ لِمَصْلَحَةِ النَّاسِ. وَمَا يُشْبِهُ ذَلِكَ مِنْ مَنْفَعَةِ الْعَامَةِ مَا قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ: «لا يَبِعْ حَاضِرٌ لِبَادٍ، وَلا تَلَقَّوُا السِّلَعَ حَتَّى يُهْبَطَ بِهَا الأَسْوَاقَ» . فَلَمَّا رَأَى أَنَّ ذَلِكَ يُصْلِحُ الْعَامَّةَ أَمَرَ فِيهِ بِذَلِكَ.

Entry253/ 23
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 62
English
Isnad

And Malik said

Matn

regarding a launderer to whom a garment is handed, and who [mistakenly] gives it to a man, who wears it: He said: The wearer is not liable for anything, and the launderer is liable to the owner of the garment. That is because he wore the garment that was handed to him without knowing that it was not his; but if he wore it knowing that it was not his garment, he is liable for it.

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ مَالِكٌ

Matn

فِي الْغَسَّالِ يُدْفَعُ إِلَيْهِ الثَّوْبُ فَيَخْطُرُ بِهِ إِلَى رَجُلٍ، فَيَلْبَسُهُ الَّذِي أَعْطَاهُ إِيَّاهُ؛ قَالَ: لا يُغَرَّمُ الَّذِي لَبِسَهُ شَيْئًا، وَيُغَرَّمُ الْغَسَّالُ لِصَاحِبِ الثَّوْبِ. وَذَلِكَ أَنَّهُ لَبِسَ الثَّوْبَ الَّذِي دَفَعَهُ إِلَيْهِ عَلَى غَيْرِ مَعْرِفَةٍ أَنَّهُ لَيْسَ لَهُ، فَإِنْ لَبِسَهُ وَهُوَ يَعْرِفُ أَنَّهُ لَيْسَ ثَوْبَهُ، فَهُوَ لَهُ ضَامِنٌ.

English
Isnad

And Malik said

Matn

regarding a man who hands a tailor a garment to sew, and fire strikes the tailor's house, and the man's garment is seen burning in it: He said: The tailor is liable, unless it was a flood or a lightning bolt. And he said the same regarding a pledge.

Isnad

وَقَالَ مَالِكٌ

Matn

فِي الرَّجُلِ يَدْفَعُ إِلَى الْخَيَّاطِ ثَوْبًا يَخِيطُهُ، فَيُصِيبُ بَيْتَ الْخَيَّاطِ حَرِيقٌ، فَيُرَى ثَوْبُ الرَّجُلِ فِيهِ يَحْتَرِقُ؛ قَالَ: عَلَى الْخَيَّاطِ الضَّمَانُ، إِلا أَنْ يَكُونَ سَيْلٌ أَوْ صَاعِقَةٌ. وَقَالَ فِي الرَّهْنِ مِثْلَهُ.

Entry257/ 23
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 62
English
Isnad

Malik said:

Matn

Craftsmen are liable for what is handed to them, whether for a wage or without a wage; for if a man brought a valuable gem of his to a goldsmith to fix something in it for him, for no payment, and he said: “It was lost from me,” he is made to pay for it.

العربية
Isnad

English
Isnad

And Malik said

Matn

regarding a man who handed yarn to a weaver, saying: “Make me from this yarn a cloth of eight by five [cubits],” and he made him one of seven by four. He said: The weaver swears by God, than whom there is no god, that he made it only as he claimed; if he swears, the statement is his, and if he refuses to swear, he is liable for what he spoiled. The difference between the weaver and the builder in this is that the weaver, goldsmith, tailor and their like, when the goods are handed to him, have taken possession of them; so if the owner of the garment died and his creditors came and said: “We take the garment, and we have an equal share in it with the creditors,” that would not be theirs, and he has a better claim to it than they, by his possession and holding of it; nothing takes it from his hand until he takes his right. The builder, however, has taken possession of nothing and holds nothing, which is why the oath is on the one who employed him.

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 62
English
Isnad

Yunus informed me, from Ibn Shihab, that he said

Matn

regarding a man who hired a worker to carry something for him, and he carried a vessel or container for him, and the vessel fell from him or the container slipped away, and what was in it was lost. He said: I do not think he owes any compensation unless he did that deliberately.

العربية
Isnad

وَأَخْبَرَنِي يُونُسُ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ أَنَّهُ قَالَ

Matn

فِي رَجُلٍ اسْتَأْجَرَ أَجِيرًا يَحْمِلُ لَهُ شَيْئًا، فَحَمَلَ لَهُ إِنَاءً أَوْ وِعَاءً فَخَرَّ مِنْهُ الإِنَاءُ أَوِ انْفَلَتَ الْوِعَاءُ، فَذَهَبَ مَا فِيهِ؛ قَالَ: لا أَرَى عَلَيْهِ غُرْمًا إِلا أَنْ يَكُونَ تَعَمَّدَ ذَلِكَ.

Entry268/ 23
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 62
English
Isnad

And Malik b. Anas said to me

Matn

regarding a man who loaded something on an animal of his for hire, and one of the ropes broke, so the thing he had loaded fell and broke; or the animal lay down and it broke, or it jostled something. He said: He is liable if it is known that he was careless in tying it or abused the animal until it jostled, or it is known that his animal was one that lies down; but if none of that is known, he does not pay.

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ لِي مَالِكُ بْنُ أَنَسٍ

Matn

فِي رَجُلٍ حَمَلَ عَلَى دَابَّةٍ لَهُ شَيْئًا بِكِرَاءٍ فَانْقَطَعَ حَبْلٌ مِنْ أَحْبُلِهِ، فَسَقَطَ ذَلِكَ الشَّيْءُ الَّذِي حُمِلَ فَانْكَسَرَ، أَوْ رَبَضَتِ الدَّابَّةُ فَانْكَسَرَ أَوْ زَاحَمَتْ شَيْئًا؛ قَالَ: يُضَمَّنُ إِنْ كَانَ يَعْرِفُ أَنَّهُ غَرَّرَ فِي رِبَاطِهِ أَوْ خَرَقَ بِالدَّابَّةِ حَتَّى زَاحَمَتْ، أَوْ كَانَ يَعْرِفُ أَنَّ دَابَّتَهُ كَانَتْ رَبُوضًا؛ وَإِنْ لَمْ يُعْرَفْ مِنْ ذَلِكَ شَيْءٌ لَمْ يُغَرَّمْ.

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 63
English
Isnad

Sulayman b. Bilal informed me, from Rabi'a b. Abi 'Abd al-Rahman, who said: I heard a man say to Sa'id b. al-Musayyab:

Matn

Mu'awiya b. 'Abd Allah b. Ja'far hired a slave boy and lowered him into a well of his, and the boy fell in it and the slave died. The slave's master litigated against him before 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz. Sa'id said to him: What did 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz rule on it? He said: He made him pay it. Ibn al-Musayyab said: Did the boy do that kind of work? He said: No, he did not do it. Ibn al-Musayyab said: 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz was right. He said: But the Messenger of God ﷺ said: “Liability is not like the eye; make it lighter.”

العربية
Isnad

وَأَخْبَرَنِي سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ بِلالٍ، عَنْ رَبِيعَةَ بْنِ أَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَجُلا قَالَ لِسَعِيدِ بْنِ الْمُسَيِّبِ:

Matn

اسْتَأْجَرَ مُعَاوِيَةُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ جَعْفَرٍ غُلامًا فَأَنْزَلَهُ فِي بِئْرٍ لَهُ فَخَرَّ الْغُلامُ فِيهَا فَمَاتَ الْعَبْدُ، فَخَاصَمَهُ سَيِّدُ الْعَبْدِ إِلَى عُمَرَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ، فَقَالَ لَهُ سَعِيدٌ: فَمَاذَا قَضَى بِهِ عُمَرُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ؟ فَقَالَ: أَغْرَمَهُ إِيَّاهُ؛ قَالَ ابْنُ الْمُسَيِّبِ: هَلْ كَانَ الْغُلامُ يَعْمَلُ ذَلِكَ الْعَمَلَ؟ فَقَالَ: لا، لَمْ يَكُنْ يَعْمَلُهُ، فَقَالَ ابْنُ الْمُسَيِّبِ: قَدْ أَصَابَ عُمَرُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الْعَزِيزِ؛ قَالَ: وَلَكِنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: «لَيْسَ الضَّمَانُ كَالْعَيْنِ خَفِّفُوا»
