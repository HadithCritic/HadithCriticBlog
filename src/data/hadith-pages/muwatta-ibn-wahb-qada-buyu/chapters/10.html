English
Isnad

Al-Layth b. Sa'd, Malik b. Anas and 'Amr b. al-Harith informed me, from Yahya b. Sa'id, from Abu Bakr b. Hazm, that 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz narrated to him that Abu Bakr b. 'Abd al-Rahman narrated to him that he heard Abu Hurayra say: The Messenger of God ﷺ said:

Matn

“Any man who becomes insolvent, and a man finds his property itself, he has a better right to it than anyone else.”

Entry116/ 31
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 24-25
English
Isnad

Yunus informed me, from Ibn Shihab, who said: Abu Bakr b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. al-Harith b. Hisham informed me

Matn

that the Messenger of God ﷺ ruled concerning goods that a man buys and then becomes insolvent while they are still in his possession, their owner not having been paid anything of their price, that they are returned to their owner; but if he had been paid anything of their price, he ranks equally with the creditors. Abu Bakr said: And the Messenger of God ﷺ ruled that whoever dies while he has in his possession a man's goods, the very goods, of whose price [the owner] has received nothing, the owner of the goods ranks equally with the creditors in them. And Malik informed me, from Ibn Shihab, from Abu Bakr, from the Prophet, the like of that.

العربية
Isnad

وَأَخْبَرَنِي يُونُسُ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ الْحَارِثِ بْنِ هِشَامٍ،

Matn

أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَضَى فِي السِّلْعَةِ يَبْتَاعُهَا الرَّجُلُ فَيُفْلِسُ وَهِيَ عِنْدَهُ بِعَيْنِهَا، لَمْ يَقْضِ صَاحِبُهَا مِنْ ثَمَنِهَا شَيْئًا، أَنْ يَرُدَّ إِلَى صَاحِبِهَا، فَإِنْ كَانَ قَدْ قَضَى مِنْ ثَمَنِهَا شَيْئًا فَهُوَ أُسْوَةُ الْغُرَمَاءِ قَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ: وَقَضَى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ أَنَّهُ مَنْ تُوُفِّيَ وَعِنْدَهُ سِلْعَةُ رَجُلٍ بِعَيْنِهَا لَمْ يَقْبِضْ مِنْ ثَمَنِهَا شَيْئًا، فَصَاحِبُ السِّلْعَةِ أُسْوَةُ الْغُرَمَاءِ فِيهَا. وَأَخْبَرَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ أَبِي بَكْرٍ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ نَحْوَ ذَلِكَ

Entry119/ 31
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 25
English
Isnad

Yunus informed me, from Ibn Shihab, that he said:

Matn

If [the buyer] paid him part of the price of what he sold, and then the buyer became insolvent, I hold that the creditors share with him proportionally, because of the statement of the Messenger of God ﷺ on this. So that is among the creditors, who share in it according to their portions; but if he had not received anything of its price until [the buyer] became insolvent, the item itself belongs to the one who sold it, and none of the creditors shares it with him.

العربية
Isnad

وَأَخْبَرَنِي يُونُسُ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، أَنَّهُ قَالَ:

Matn

إِنْ كَانَ أَدَّى إِلَيْهِ بَعْضَ ثَمَنِ مَا بَاعَهُ، ثُمَّ أَفْلَسَ الْمُشْتَرِي، فَأَرَى أَنْ يُقَاسِمَهُ الْغُرَمَاءُ بِالْحِصَّةِ لِقَوْلِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ فِي ذَلِكَ. فَذَلِكَ بَيْنَ الْغُرَمَاءِ يَتَأَسَّوْنَ فِيهِ بِحِصَصِهِمْ؛ وَإِنْ لَمْ يَكُنْ قَبَضَ مِنْ ثَمَنِهَا شَيْئًا حَتَّى أَفْلَسَ فَهِيَ لِلَّذِي بَاعَهَا بِعَيْنِهَا، لا يَشْرَكُهُ فِيهَا أَحَدٌ مِنَ الْغُرَمَاءِ.

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 25
English
Isnad

Ibn Sam'an informed me; he said: I heard those of our scholars whom I met say:

Matn

Whoever sells goods to a man, and the buyer becomes insolvent, the owner of the goods has a better right to them if he finds the very goods, unless [the creditors] pay the full price of his goods; he is not entitled to the increase.

العربية
Isnad

وَأَخْبَرَنِي ابْنُ سَمْعَانَ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ مَنْ أَدْرَكْتُ مِنْ عُلَمَائِنَا يَقُولُونَ:

Matn

مَنْ بَاعَ سِلْعَةً مِنْ رَجُلٍ، فَأَفْلَسَ الْمُبْتَاعُ فَصَاحِبُ السِّلْعَةِ أَحَقُّ بِهَا إِذَا وَجَدَهَا بِعَيْنِهَا، إِلا أَنْ يَقْضِيَ ثَمَنَ سِلْعَتِهِ كَامِلا، لَيْسَ لَهُ النَّمَاءُ.

Entry122/ 31
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 25
English
Isnad

And Malik said to me:

Matn

As for goods that were bought and in which the buyer made no change, except that those goods have sold well and risen in price, so that their owner wants them back and the creditors prefer to keep them: the creditors are given the choice between giving the owner of the goods the price due to him, without reducing it at all, and handing the goods over to him. And if the goods have fallen in price, the seller has the choice: if he wishes, he takes his goods and has no further claim beyond that; and if he prefers to be a creditor among the creditors, sharing pro rata in his right and not taking his goods, that is his right.

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ لِي مَالِكٌ:

Matn

أَمَّا مَا ابْتِيعَ مِنَ السِّلَعِ الَّتِي لَمْ يُحْدِثْ فِيهَا الْمُشْتَرِي شَيْئًا إِلا أَنَّ تِلْكَ السِّلْعَةَ نَفِقَتْ وَارْتَفَعَ ثَمَنُهَا، فَصَاحِبُهَا يَرْغَبُ فِيهَا، وَالْغُرَمَاءُ يَرَوْنُ إِمْسَاكَهَا؛ فَإِنَّ الْغُرَمَاءَ يُخَيَّرُونَ بَيْنَ أَنْ يُعْطُوا رَبَّ السِّلْعَةِ الثَّمَنَ الَّذِي لَهُ، وَلا يَنْقُصُونَهُ شَيْئًا، وَبَيْنَ أَنْ يُسَلِّمُوا إِلَيْهِ السِّلْعَةَ؛ وَإِنْ كَانَتِ السِّلْعَةُ قَدْ نَقَصَ ثَمَنُهَا، فَالَّذِي بَاعَهَا بِالْخِيَارِ، إِنْ شَاءَ أَنْ يَأْخُذَ سِلْعَتَهُ، وَلا تِبَاعَةَ لَهُ فِيمَا سِوَى ذَلِكَ؛ وَإِنْ أَحَبَّ أَنْ يَكُونَ غَرِيمًا مِنَ الْغُرَمَاءِ يُحَاصُّ بِحَقِّهِ، وَلا يَأْخُذُ سِلْعَتَهُ، فَذَلِكَ لَهُ.

Isnad

And Malik b. Anas said to me

Matn

regarding a man who buys goods, merchandise, or yarn one day, and then [the one who bought that merchandise or plot] haggles over it, and then does some work on it, building the plot into a house or weaving the yarn into a garment, and then the buyer of that merchandise becomes insolvent, and the owner of the plot says: “I will take the plot and the buildings on it.” Malik said: The whole house is appraised, the plot, the building and what was improved in it; then one looks at what the plot's price is of that and what the price of the building is, and then they are partners in that: the owner of the plot according to his share, and the creditors have the share of the building. If the value of all that is one thousand five hundred, and the plot is worth five hundred and the building one thousand, the owner of the plot gets a third and the creditors two thirds. Likewise yarn when it has been woven, and other things when something like this enters them; and thus is the practice regarding it.

Matn

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 29
English
Isnad

Yunus b. Yazid informed me, from Rabi'a b. Abi 'Abd al-Rahman, that he said:

Matn

When a man becomes insolvent and his creditors share his property, then whoever deals with him after that deals with him only regarding property other than that of the creditors who declared him insolvent; they dealt with him on his account (dhimma), in respect of what God provides and gains for him in the future. If he becomes destitute a second time, those who dealt with him after his first destitution have a better right to his property, and they share it among themselves apart from the first creditors, unless it is blood money for an injury to him or an inheritance he inherits. As for every enterprise he operated, or anything by which provisions returned to him, it belongs to those who dealt with him after his destitution, because that is exclusively theirs, since their property went out into it; for he could not have attained that among people except through the dealings of those who dealt with him and the lending of those who lent to him, and his seeking provision from his Lord through business and trade. As for those who declared their debtor insolvent, their rights enter into any surplus he has in his hands after the rights of the others have been paid.

العربية
Isnad

English
Isnad

And I heard Malik say

Matn

regarding a man who owes a debt and whose property is surrendered to his creditors: if money comes back to him afterward, they take it from him until they receive their rights in full. If no money returns to him until others lend to him, and he becomes insolvent again with their money and they act against him, the first creditors do not enter in against these for what they lent him until they receive their rights in full, because what is in his hands is the property of those who lent to him. But if the later creditors do not act against him until a windfall enters to him by inheritance, or an eye of his is put out and he is awarded its blood money, then the first and the later creditors share in what he has of that inheritance or blood money.

العربية
Isnad

وَسَمِعْتُ مَالِكًا يَقُولُ

Matn
