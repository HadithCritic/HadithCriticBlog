Entry291/ 22
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 65
English
Isnad

And Malik said:

Matn

The rule among us regarding a man who hires a specific animal to a known town for a named hire, and then goes beyond that place, and the animal perishes or survives: He said: The animal is at its owner's risk, and the hire is his, until the hirer reaches the town to which he hired; then if the hirer exceeds, he becomes liable from the time he exceeded, until he returns to its owner. If it survives, the owner of the animal gets the first hire that he stipulated with him, and hire for what he exceeded is assessed against the hirer by its value on the day he exceeded, not looking to the first hire, nor to the day it is assessed against him. If the animal perished in the stretch beyond, the owner of the animal takes the first hire he stipulated with him, and has the choice: if he wishes he takes the price of the animal and has no hire for the excess, or he gets the hire for the excess for his animal and does not take the price of the animal.

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ مَالِكٌ:

Matn

الأَمْرُ عِنْدَنَا فِي الرَّجُلِ يَتَكَارَى الدَّابَّةَ بِعَيْنِهَا إِلَى بَلَدٍ مَعْلُومٍ بِكِرَاءٍ مُسَمًّى، ثُمَّ يَتَعَدَّى ذَلِكَ الْمَكَانَ، فَتَتْلَفُ الدَّابَّةُ أَوْ تَسْلَمُ؛ قَالَ: الدَّابَّةُ مِنْ صَاحِبِهَا، وَالْكِرَاءُ لَهُ حَتَّى يَبْلُغَ الْمُتَكَارِي الْبَلَدَ الَّذِي تَكَارَى إِلَيْهِ؛ ثُمَّ إِنْ تَعَدَّى الْمُتَكَارِي فَقَدْ ضُمِّنَ مِنْ حِينِ تَعَدَّى، حَتَّى يَرْجِعَ إِلَى صَاحِبِهِ، فَإِنْ سَلِمَتْ كَانَتْ لِصَاحِبِ الدَّابَّةِ الْكِرَاءُ الأَوَّلُ الَّذِي شَارَطَهُ عَلَيْهِ، وَأُقِيمَ عَلَى الْمُتَكَارِي كِرَاءَ مَا تَعَدَّى بِقِيمَتِهِ يَوْمَ تَعَدَّى، لا يَنْظُرُ إِلَى الْكِرَاءِ الأَوَّلِ وَلا يَوْمَ أُقِيمَ عَلَيْهِ؛ وَإِنْ تَلِفَتِ الدَّابَّةُ فِيمَا تَعَدَّى بِهَا أَخَذَ صَاحِبُ الدَّابَّةِ الْكِرَاءَ الأَوَّلَ الَّذِي شَارَطَهُ عَلَيْهِ، وَكَانَ بِالْخِيَارِ، وَإِنْ شَاءَ يَأْخُذُ ثَمَنَ الدَّابَّةِ وَلا كِرَاءَ لَهُ فِي التَّعَدِّي، أَوْ يَكُونُ لَهُ كِرَاءُ مَا تَعَدَّى بِدَابَّتِهِ، وَلا يَأْخُذُ ثَمَنَ الدَّابَّةِ.

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 65
English
Isnad

Malik said:

Matn

And if he hired it only for going and returning, and exceeded when he reached the town to which he hired, then the owner of the animal gets only half of the first hire; for the hire is half for the outward trip and half for the return, so the transgressor exceeded with the animal when only half the hire was due from him. And if the animal had died when he reached the town to which he hired it, the hirer would not be liable and the lessor would have only half the hire. If the hirer exceeded the place to which he hired it, the owner of the animal has the choice: if he wishes to hold the hirer liable for his animal as of the day he exceeded with it, he holds him liable by its value on the day he exceeded with it, and he gets the hire up to the place from which he exceeded; and if the owner of the animal prefers to take its hire for the hirer's excess and take back his animal, that is his right. So it is among us in the case of those who exceed or contravene what they took the animal for.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ مَالِكٌ:

Matn

وَإِنْ كَانَ إِنَّمَا تَكَارَاهَا ذَاهِبًا وَرَاجِعًا، ثُمَّ تَعَدَّى حِينَ بَلَغَ الْبَلَدَ الَّذِي تَكَارَى إِلَيْهِ، فَإِنَّمَا لِرَبِّ الدَّابَّةِ نِصْفُ الْكِرَاءِ الأَوَّلِ؛ وَذَلِكَ أَنَّ الْكِرَاءَ نِصْفَهُ فِي الْبَدْأَةِ، وَنِصْفَهُ فِي الرَّجْعَةِ، فَيَتَعَدَّى الْمُتَعَدِّي بِالدَّابَّةِ وَلَمْ يَجِبْ عَلَيْهِ إِلا نِصْفَ الْكِرَاءِ. وَلَوْ أَنَّ الدَّابَّةَ هَلَكَتْ حِينَ بَلَغَ بِهَا الْبَلَدَ الَّذِي تَكَارَى إِلَيْهِ، لَمْ يَكُنْ عَلَى الْمُسْتَكْرِي ضَمَانٌ وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لِلْمَكْرِيِّ إِلا نِصْفُ الْكِرَاءِ. فَإِنْ تَعَدَّى الْمُتَكَارِي الْمَكَانَ الَّذِي تَكَارَى إِلَيْهِ، فَرَبُّ الدَّابَّةِ بِالْخِيَارِ؛ إِنْ أَحَبَّ أَنْ يُضَمِّنَ دَابَّتَهُ الْمُتَكَارِيَ يَوْمَ تَعَدَّى بِهَا ضَمَّنَهُ إِيَّاهُ بِقِيمَتِهَا يَوْمَ تَعَدَّى بِهَا، وَلَهُ الْكِرَاءُ إِلَى الْمَكَانِ الَّذِي تَعَدَّى مِنْهُ، وَإِنْ أَحَبَّ صَاحِبُ الدَّابَّةِ أَنْ يَأْخُذَ كِرَاءَهَا بِتَعَدِّي الْمُسْتَكْرِي وَيَأْخُذَ دَابَّتَهُ، فَذَلِكَ لَهُ. وَكَذَلِكَ الأَمْرُ عِنْدَنَا فِي أَهْلِ التَّعَدِّي وَالْخِلافِ لِمَا أَخَذُوا عَلَيْهِ الدَّابَّةَ.

Entry295/ 22
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 65
English
Isnad

And Malik b. Anas said to me

Matn

regarding one who hires an animal to load a named weight on it to a known place, and adds to the weight more than was stipulated against him: He is liable for the animal from the day he loaded it, and if the animal perishes its owner has the choice: if he wishes, the price of the animal, with no hire; or if he wishes, the first hire that was stipulated against him, plus the hire for what he added in his excess. Malik said: The same applies to a slave when he hires him for one task and then exceeds and employs him in another task.

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ لِي مَالِكُ بْنُ أَنَسٍ

Matn

فِي الَّذِي يَتَكَارَى الدَّابَّةَ يَحْمِلُ عَلَيْهَا وَزْنًا مُسَمًّى إِلَى مَكَانٍ مَعْلُومٍ فَيَزِيدُ عَلَيْهَا فِي الْوَزْنِ أَكْثَرَ مِمَّا شَرَطَ عَلَيْهِ؛ إِنَّهُ يُضَمَّنُ الدَّابَّةَ مِنْ يَوْمِ حَمَلَ، وَإِنْ تَلِفَتِ الدَّابَّةُ كَانَ صَاحِبُهَا بِالْخِيَارِ، إِنْ شَاءَ ثَمَنَ الدَّابَّةِ، وَلا كِرَاءَ لَهُ، وَإِنْ شَاءَ فَالْكِرَاءُ الأَوَّلُ الَّذِي شَرَطَ عَلَيْهِ، وَكِرَاءُ مَا زَادَ عَلَيْهِ فِي تَعَدِّيهِ. قَالَ مَالِكٌ: وَكَذَلِكَ الْعَبْدُ إِذَا اسْتَأْجَرَهُ فِي عَمَلٍ، ثُمَّ تَعَدَّى فَاسْتَعْمَلَهُ عَمَلا غَيْرَهُ.

Entry296/ 22
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 65
English
Isnad

Muhammad b. 'Amr informed me, from Ibn Jurayj, from 'Ata' b. Abi Rabah. A man said to him:

Matn

“I went a little beyond the place I hired to, a mile or less, and [the animal] died.” He said: You pay. I said to 'Ata': “I added a little to the load I had stipulated, and it died.” He said: You pay. I said: “And if I hired it out to someone else without the order of the animal's owner, and it carried the like of my stipulation and did not exceed?” He said: You do not pay.

العربية
Isnad

وَأَخْبَرَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عَمْرٍو، عَنِ ابْنِ جُرَيْجٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءِ بْنِ أَبِي رَبَاحٍ، قَالَ لَهُ رَجُلٌ:

Matn

زِدْتُ عَلَى الْمَكَانِ الَّذِي اسْتَكْرَيْتَ قَلِيلا مَيْلا، أَوْ أَدْنَى، فَمَاتَ؛ قَالَ: تُغَرَّمُ. قُلْتُ لِعَطَاءٍ: فَزِدْتُ عَلَى الْحَمْلِ الَّذِي اشْتَرَطْتُ قَلِيلا، فَمَاتَ؛ قَالَ: تُغَرَّمُ؛ قُلْتُ: فَأَكْرَيْتُهُ مِنْ غَيْرِي بِغَيْرِ أَمْرِ سَيِّدِ الظَّهْرِ، فَحَمَلَ مِثْلَ شَرْطِي وَلَمْ يَتَعَدَّ؛ قَالَ: لا تُغَرَّمْ.

Entry298/ 22
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 65
English
Isnad

And Malik b. Anas said to me

Matn

regarding the transgressor who returns the animal in better condition than it was, and invites [the owner] to take the hire: He said: The owner of the animal has the choice, even if it came back in better condition than it was, because he kept it from things [such as sales] on account of demand that existed before his arrival with it, and the like; unless the animal's absence and detention was brief, and the animal did not change in that, and the markets did not turn.

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ لِي مَالِكُ بْنُ أَنَسٍ

Matn

فِي الْمُتَعَدِّي يَأْتِي بِالدَّابَّةِ أَحْسَنَ حَالا مِمَّا كَانَتْ، وَيَدْعُو إِلَى الْكِرَاءِ؛ قَالَ: صَاحِبُ الدَّابَّةِ مُخَيَّرٌ، وَإِنْ جَاءَتْ عَلَى أَحْسَنَ مِمَّا كَانَتْ، لأَنَّهُ قَدْ حَبَسَهَا عَنْ أَشْيَاءَ عَنْ نِفَاقٍ كَانَ قَبْلَ قُدُومِهِ بِهَا، وَأَشْبَاهِ ذَلِكَ؛ إِلا أَنْ تَكُونَ غَيْبَةُ الدَّابَّةِ وَحَبْسُهَا كَانَ يَسِيرًا لَمْ تَتَغَيَّرِ الدَّابَّةُ فِي ذَلِكَ، وَلَمْ تَحُلَّ الأَسْوَاقَ.

Matn

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 65
English
Isnad

And Malik wrote to me:

Matn

If a man hired camels from Egypt, and when he arrived at Medina the owner of the camels said: “I hired them to you only to Medina,” and the owner of the goods said: “I hired from you only to Mecca.” He said: The hire is examined and the hire valued: if what he gave him is the hire to Medina, the owner of the mounts is made to swear that he hired only to Medina; then only the hire of Medina is incumbent on him, and he does not take them beyond Medina. But if the hire, in people's view, is more than the hire to Medina, the owner of the mounts swears that he hired only to Medina, then he returns the surplus to the one who hired from him and goes off with his mounts, and does not take them beyond Medina; and if he refuses to swear, the owner of the goods swears that his hire was to Mecca, and goes on with his camels to Mecca.

Isnad

Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 65
English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: And I heard him say

Matn

regarding one who hires [mounts] for the pilgrimage, and then the hirer dies: The hire is binding on him; if his people wish to hire out those camels for the same as he hired them for, that is theirs to do; and if they find no one, they have nothing against the hirer. He said: And if the camels died and the hirer had not died, the lessor must carry him to the pilgrimage. Malik said: And the hire stands; it is said to his guardian: Hire out your ward's mount, or ride on it in his place, if he is of like lightness and condition, and he may hire it out to one like him if he wishes, and the lessor has no argument in that. Malik said: And a hired worker who is hired, the matter stands, the hire binds him and binds his employer in every case.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ ابْنُ وَهْبٍ: وَسَمِعْتُهُ يَقُولُ

Matn

فِي الَّذِي يَتَكَارَى لِلْحَجِّ، ثُمَّ يَمُوتُ الْمُتَكَارِي؛ إِنَّ الْكِرَاءَ لازِمٌ لَهُ، فَإِنْ أَحَبَّ أَهْلُهُ أَنْ يُكْرُوا تِلْكَ الإِبِلَ بِمِثْلِ مَا تَكَارَوْا كَانَ ذَلِكَ لَهُمْ؛ وَإِنْ لَمْ يَجِدُوا أَحَدًا فَلَيْسَ لَهُمْ عَلَى الْمُتَكَارِي شَيْءٌ. قَالَ: وَلَوْ مَاتَتِ الإِبِلُ وَلَمْ يَمُتِ الْمُتَكَارِي كَانَ عَلَى الْمَكْرِيِّ أَنْ يَحْمِلَهُ إِلَى الْحَجِّ. قَالَ مَالِكٌ: وَالْكِرَاءُ ثَابِتٌ، يُقَالُ لِوَلِيِّهِ: أَكْرِي مَرْكِبَ وَلِيِّكَ، أَوِ ارْكَبْ فِيهِ مَكَانَهُ، إِذَا كَانَ مِثْلَهُ فِي الْخِفَّةِ وَالْحَالِ وَيُكْرِيهِ مَنْ مِثْلُهُ إِنْ شَاءَ، وَلَيْسَ لِلْمَكْرِيِّ فِي ذَاكَ حُجَّةً. قَالَ مَالِكٌ: وَالأَجِيرُ يَسْتَأْجِرُ ثَابِتَ الأَمْرِ تَلْزَمُهُ الإِجَارَةُ، وَتَلْزَمُ صَاحِبَهُ عَلَى كُلِّ حَالٍ.

Entry308/ 22
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 65
English
Isnad

And Malik said

Matn

regarding one who hired a worker or hired an animal and paid the hire or wage, and the lessor became insolvent: The hirer has priority over the animal and the slave in his hand until he receives his right in full. Likewise, if a man hired himself out to a man to work for him in an orchard or crop, and worked in it until, at the time of his finishing, the owner of the property became insolvent without having paid him his wage, he has priority over that which he was working on until he receives his wage.

العربية
Isnad

وَقَالَ مَالِكٌ

Matn

فِيمَنِ اسْتَأْجَرَ أَجِيرًا أَوْ تَكَارَى دَابَّةً، فَنَقَدَ الْكِرَاءَ أَوِ الإِجَارَةَ، فَفَلسَ الْمَكْرِيُّ؛ إِنَّ صَاحِبَ الْكِرَاءِ أَوْلَى بِالدَّابَّةِ وَبِالْعَبْدِ الَّذِي بِيَدِهِ حَتَّى يَسْتَوْفِيَ حَقَّهُ. وَكَذَلِكَ لَوْ أَنَّ رَجُلا آجَرَ نَفْسَهُ مِنْ رَجُلٍ يَعْمَلُ لَهُ فِي جِنَانٍ أَوْ زَرْعٍ، فَعَمِلَ فِيهِ حَتَّى إِذَا كَانَ عِنْدَ فَرَاغِهِ فَلسَ صَاحِبُ الأَصْلِ وَلَمْ يَكُنْ دَفَعَ إِلَيْهِ أَجْرَهُ، كَانَ أَوْلَى بِذَلِكَ الَّذِي كَانَ يَعْمَلُهُ حَتَّى يَسْتَوْفِيَ أَجْرَهُ.

Matn

regarding a slave whom a man hires, or an animal he hires or buys, and then the seller or lessor becomes insolvent before the hirer or buyer takes possession of what he hired or bought. He said: I consider him to have a better claim to it than the creditors.

Entry311/ 22
Muwaṭṭaʾ Ibn Wahb: Sales · p. 65
English
Isnad

Ibn Wahb said: And there has reached me from Rabi'a b. Abi 'Abd al-Rahman that he said

Matn

regarding a man who hired an animal and then claimed that it had perished and that he hired another animal to complete his journey, and so accused the owner of the animal that was his, who denied that his animal had perished, with no witness between them to what they disputed. Rabi'a said: It is a trust of the hirer with whom the animal was, that it did perish; except that the owner of the animal, if he wishes to verify, has the oath of the one with whom the animal perished, and then he is cleared of it, and for him is reckoned the hire for as far as his animal went in proportion to what he hired it for, so a reckoning of that is taken for him, and what remains is deducted from the hirer in proportion to what remained of his journey. As for the new lessor, the first lessor is cleared of him; the stranded man hired only for himself, and between them is the reckoning for the distance the animal reached, and for what remained of the journey, as against what the hirer held in trust, on the basis of the first hire.

العربية
Isnad

قَالَ ابْنُ وَهْبٍ: وَبَلَغَنِي عَنْ رَبِيعَةَ بْنِ أَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ أَنَّهُ قَالَ

Matn

فِي رَجُلٍ اسْتَكْرَى دَابَّةً، ثُمَّ ادَّعَى أَنَّهَا هَلَكَتْ وَأَنَّهُ اسْتَكْرَى دَابَّةً لإِتْمَامِ سَفَرِهِ، فَخَوَّنَ صَاحِبُ الدَّابَّةِ الَّتِي كَانَتْ لَهُ وَأَنْكَرَ أَنْ تَكُونَ دَابَّتُهُ هَلَكَتْ وَلَمْ يَكُنْ بَيْنَهُمَا فِيمَا اخْتَلَفَا فِيهِ شَاهِدٌ؛ قَالَ رَبِيعَةُ: هِيَ أَمَانَةُ الْمُسْتَكْرِي الَّذِي كَانَتْ مَعَهُ الدَّابَّةُ لَقَدْ هَلَكَتْ؛ إِلا أَنَّ صَاحِبَ الدَّابَّةِ إِنْ أَحَبَّ أَنْ يَسْتَبْرِئَ، لَهُ يَمِينُ الَّذِي هَلَكَتْ مَعَهُ الدَّابَّةَ، ثُمَّ يَتَبَرَّأُ مِنْهَا وَيَحْسِبُ لَهُ كِرَاءَ مَا بَلَغَتْ دَابَّتُهُ مِنْ حِسَابِ مَا أَكْرَاهُ، فَيُؤْخَذُ لَهُ حِسَابُ ذَلِكَ، وَيُوضَعُ عَنِ الْمُسْتَكْرِي مَا بَقِيَ بِحِسَابِ مَا بَقِيَ مِنْ سَفَرِهِ. وَأَمَّا الْكَرِيُّ الَّذِي اسْتُحْدِثَ فَالْكَرِيُّ الأَوَّلُ مِنْهُ بَرِيءٌ، وَإِنَّمَا اسْتَكْرَى الْمُنْقَطِعُ بِهِ لِنَفْسِهِ وَبَيْنَهُمَا الْحِسَابُ فِيمَا بَلَغَتِ الدَّابَّةُ، وَفِيمَا بَقِيَ مِنَ السَّفَرِ عَلَى مَا غَابَ عَلَيْهِ الْمُسْتَكْرِي مِنَ الأَمَانَةِ مِنْ حِسَابِ الْكِرَاءِ الأَوَّلِ.
