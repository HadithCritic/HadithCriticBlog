Musnad Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ · Vol. 1, p. 30
English
Isnad

Ahmad narrated to us: 'Abd al-Rahman b. Mahdi narrated to us, from Sufyan, from Sa'd b. Ibrahim, from 'Amir b. Sa'd, from his father, who said:

Matn

The Prophet ﷺ came to visit him while he was in Mecca, and he disliked dying in the land from which he had emigrated. The Prophet ﷺ said: "May God have mercy on Sa'd b. 'Afra'! May God have mercy on Sa'd b. 'Afra'!" He said: And he had only one daughter. He said: "O Messenger of God, shall I bequeath all of my wealth?" He said: "No." He said: "Then half?" He said: "No." He said: "Then a third?" He said: "A third; and a third is much. That you leave your heirs well off is better than that you leave them dependent, begging from people with their hands. And whatever you spend is charity, even the morsel you raise to your wife's mouth. And perhaps God will raise you up, so that people benefit from you and others are harmed by you."

Musnad Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ · Vol. 1, p. 32
English
Isnad

Ahmad narrated to us: Abu Dawud, the master of the shawls (sahib al-Tayalisa), narrated to us: Ibrahim b. Sa'd narrated to us, from al-Zuhri, from 'Amir b. Sa'd b. Abi Waqqas, from his father, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ called for me during the Farewell Pilgrimage, during an illness in which I had come near to death. I said: "O Messenger of God, the pain has reached me as you see, and I am a man of much wealth, and only one daughter of mine inherits from me. Shall I give all my wealth in charity?" He said: "No." I said: "Then half?" He said: "No." I said: "Then a third?" He said: "A third; and a third is much. That you leave your heirs well off is better than that you leave them dependent, begging from people. You will never spend any expenditure seeking the face of God without being rewarded for it, even the morsel you raise to your wife's mouth." I said: "O Messenger of God, shall I be left behind after my companions?" He said: "You will not be left behind after me and do any deed seeking the face of God without increasing thereby in elevation and rank. And perhaps you will be left behind after me, so that a people benefit from you and others are harmed by you. O God, complete for my Companions their emigration, and do not turn them back on their heels. But the poor Sa'd b. Khawla" – the Messenger of God ﷺ grieved for him because he died in Mecca.

Musnad Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ · Vol. 1, p. 40
English
Isnad

Ahmad narrated to us: Yazid b. Harun narrated to us: Ibn Abi Dhi'b informed us, from al-Zuhri, from 'Amir b. Sa'd, from his father:

Matn

that a group came to the Prophet ﷺ and asked him, and he gave them [something], except for one man among them. Sa'd said: I said: "O Messenger of God, you gave them and left out so-and-so. By God, I consider him a believer." The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Or a Muslim." Sa'd said: "By God, I consider him a believer." The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Or a Muslim." Sa'd said that three times, and the Messenger of God ﷺ said: "Or a Muslim." Then the Prophet ﷺ said, the third time: "By God, I give a man a gift while another is dearer to me than he, out of fear that God will cast him face-first into the Fire of Hell."

He was brought with sore eyes; he spat in his eye, handed him the banner, and God granted victory at his hands. And when this verse was revealed: {Let us call our sons and your sons} (Q 3:61), the Messenger of God ﷺ called 'Ali, Fatima, Hasan and Husayn and said: 'O God, these are my family.'"

Ahmad narrated to us: 'Abd Allah b. Abi Musa narrated to me: 'Abd Allah b. Wahb narrated to us: Makhrama b. Bukayr informed me, from his father, from 'Amir b. Sa'd b. Abi Waqqas, who said: I heard Sa'd and other people from the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ say:

Matn

There were two brothers in the time of the Messenger of God ﷺ one of whom was better than the other. The better of the two died, and then the other lived forty nights after him and died. The superiority of the first over the other was mentioned to the Messenger of God ﷺ and he said: "Did he not pray?" They said: "Yes, O Messenger of God; there was no fault in him." The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "How do you know what his prayer has achieved for him?" Then he said: "The likeness of prayer is that of a deep, sweet river at a man's door, into which he plunges five times each day. What do you think that leaves of his filth? You do not know what his prayer has achieved for him."
