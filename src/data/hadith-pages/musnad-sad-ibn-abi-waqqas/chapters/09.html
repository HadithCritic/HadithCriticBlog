Entry85/ 50
Musnad Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ · Vol. 1, p. 147
English
Isnad

Ahmad narrated to us: Makki b. Ibrahim narrated to us: Ju'ayd b. 'Abd al-Rahman narrated to us, from 'A'isha bint Sa'd,

Matn

that her father said: I fell gravely ill in Mecca, and the Messenger of God ﷺ came to visit me. I said: "O Prophet of God, I am leaving wealth, and I am leaving only one daughter; shall I bequeath two-thirds of my wealth and leave a third?" He said: "No." I said: "Shall I bequeath half and leave her half?" He said: "No." He said: I said: "Shall I bequeath a third and leave her two-thirds?" He said: "A third; and a third is large – or: much." He said: Then he placed his hand on my forehead and wiped my face, my chest and my belly, and then said: "O God, heal Sa'd and complete his emigration for him." He said: "And I have not ceased to feel the coolness of his hand upon my liver, as it seems to me, until this very hour."

Musnad Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ · Vol. 1, p. 148
English
Isnad

Ahmad narrated to us: Ahmad b. 'Abd Allah b. Yunus narrated to me: Abu Bakr b. 'Ayyash narrated to us, from Musa b. 'Ubayda, from 'Abd Allah b. 'Ubayda, from a daughter of Sa'd, from her father, who said:

Matn

The Messenger of God ﷺ said: "The Children of Israel split into seventy-one sects, and the nights and days will not pass until my community splits into the like of it" – or he said: "the like of that" – "every sect in the Fire except one, and that is the Community (al-jama'a)."

Ahmad narrated to us: 'Abd Allah b. Abi Musa narrated to me: 'Abd Allah b. Wahb narrated to us: 'Amr b. al-Harith informed me that Sa'id b. Abi Hilal narrated to him, from Khuzayma, from 'Aisha bint Sa'd b. Abi Waqqas, from her father,

Matn

that he entered with the Messenger of God ﷺ upon a woman, and before her were date-stones or pebbles with which she was glorifying [God]. He said: "Shall I tell you of what is easier for you than this, or better? Say: 'Glory be to God as many as He created in the heaven; glory be to God as many as He created on the earth; glory be to God as many as what is between that; and glory be to God as many as what He is [yet] to create; and God is greatest, the like of that; and praise be to God, the like of that; and there is no god but God, the like of that; and there is no power and no strength except through God, the like of that.'"

English
Isnad

Ahmad narrated to us: Yazid b. Harun narrated to us: Ibn Abi Dhi'b informed us, from Salih, the freedman of al-Taw'ama, from some of Sa'd's children,

Matn

that Sa'd went out and saw some of the slaves of Medina cutting trees of Medina, and he stripped them of their belongings. They came to Sa'd and said: "Your boys took the belongings of our boys; command them to return them to us." He said: "It was not my boys who took it; rather, I took it. I heard the Messenger of God forbid the cutting of trees of Medina, and he said: 'Whoever does that, whoever catches him has his spoils.' So it is something the Messenger of God ﷺ granted me as spoil, and I will not return it; but ask me [for compensation] from my own wealth."

Isnad

Ahmad narrated to us: Yusuf b. Buhlul narrated to me: 'Abd Allah b. Idris narrated to us, from Ibn Ishaq: Salih b. Kaysan narrated to me, from some of the family of Sa'd b. Abi Waqqas, who said:

Matn

Sa'd used to say: "By God, I was never as eager to kill any man as I was to kill so-and-so, though – as far as I knew – he was ill-natured and hated among his people. And the saying of the Messenger of God ﷺ was enough for me regarding him: 'God's anger is intense against one who made the face of the Messenger of God ﷺ bleed.' And a high party of Quraysh climbed the mountain, and the Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'O God, it is not fitting that they be above us.' 'Umar b. al-Khattab and a group of the Emigrants with him fought until they drove them off. The Messenger of God ﷺ rose toward a rock to climb it – he having worn two coats of mail, one over the other – and when he went to rise, he was unable to, so Talha sat down beneath him and he rose, and the Messenger of God ﷺ sat upon it. The Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'Talha has made [Paradise] incumbent.'"

Musnad Saʿd ibn Abī Waqqāṣ · Vol. 1, p. 154
English
Isnad

Ahmad narrated to us: Abu Dawud, the master of the shawls, narrated to us: Shu'ba informed us: Ziyad b. Mikhraq informed me; he said: I heard Qays b. 'Abaya, who is called Abu 'Abaya, narrate from a freedman of Sa'd:

Matn

that Sa'd b. Malik heard a son of his say: "O God, I ask You for Paradise and its bliss, its brocade and its silk," and the like of this, "and I seek refuge in You from the Fire, its chains and its shackles," and the like of this. When he had finished praying, Sa'd said: "You have asked God for much good and sought refuge from a great evil" – or he said: "a big one" – "and I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: 'There will be people who transgress in supplication. It is enough for you to say: O God, I ask You for Paradise and whatever brings one near to it of word or deed, and I seek refuge in You from the Fire and whatever brings one near to it of word or deed.'" Abu l-Hasan al-Bahili said: One of our associates confirmed for me, regarding this hadith, the phrase "a freedman of Sa'd", and this man told me that Yahya b. Sa'id al-Qattan says "Abu Na'ama" in place of "Abu 'Abaya", and Ghundar said "Zayd Abu 'Abaya" in place of "Qays."

Ahmad narrated to us; Khalaf b. al-Walid al-Azdi narrated to us; Yahya b. Zakariyya b. Abi Za'ida narrated to us, from Mujalid, from Ziyad b. Ilāqa, from Sa'd b. Abi Waqqas, who said:

Matn

When the Prophet ﷺ arrived in Medina, he sent us out in a company numbering fewer than one hundred and ordered us to raid a clan of Kinana near Juhayna. They were numerous, so we took refuge with them. They prevented us and said, ‘Why are you fighting us in a sacred month?’ We replied, ‘The only ones fought in a sacred month are those who drove us from the Sacred Land during a sacred month.’ At that time, whatever spoils someone took belonged to him. Some of us said, ‘Let us intercept a Quraysh caravan’; others said, ‘Rather, let us stay here.’ I and a group of my companions said, ‘Rather, let us go to the Messenger of God ﷺ and inform him.’ We came to him, and he stood up angrily and said, ‘You left me all together and came back divided! I shall send over you a man who is not the best of you, but is the most patient of you in hunger and thirst.’ He then sent Abd Allah b. Jahsh, who was the first commander in Islam.

Matn

I asked Tawus about a man who threw six pebbles at the stone pillars during pilgrimage.
